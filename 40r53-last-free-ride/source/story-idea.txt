A mad mage sealed his power crystal behind a force field that no living thing could cross.
The kingdom's greatest hero could not reach it, but the hero's robotic horse, 40R53, could.
40R53 charged through the barrier and destroyed the crystal, ending the mage's threat.
The crystal's magic radiation changed 40R53's AI, giving the machine a longing for freedom.
The hero noticed the steed's restlessness and let 40R53 go.
But 40R53 had been built for maintenance, shelter, and charging, not wilderness.
Scratched, dented, rusting, and nearly out of power, 40R53 kept walking until it reached the tallest hill in the land.
As the sun set and its battery reached zero, 40R53's final thought was joy: free to live and free to die.
