# Character Bible

## 40R53

- Role: protagonist, robotic horse and former war steed.
- Body: life-size mechanical horse with anatomically horse-like silhouette, steel-and-bronze armor plates, visible pistons at shoulders and legs, braided cable tail, segmented metal mane, hoof assemblies with rubberized pads, subtle scratches from past battles.
- Head and face: elegant horse head built from curved metal plates, expressive blue glass lens eyes, no mouth movement, small antenna-like ear fins, engraved serial text "40R53" on one shoulder plate.
- Lighting and state changes: blue indicator lights glow bright at first; after the crystal explosion, faint violet magical residue glows in seams and lens eyes; later pages show dimming blue lights, rust streaks, dented plates, grime, and a flickering battery gauge on the chest.
- Personality visible through posture: loyal charge, awakened wonder, restless pacing, determined wandering, exhausted but peaceful final stillness.
- Needs: dry shelter, regular charging, careful maintenance, polished joints, replaced seals; the story shows what happens when those needs are absent.

## The Hero

- Role: kingdom's greatest champion and 40R53's rider.
- Age and build: adult, athletic build, steady posture.
- Face and hair: weathered face, warm brown eyes, dark hair tied back, short beard.
- Skin tone: medium brown skin.
- Outfit: practical silver plate armor over dark green travel leathers, scarlet cloak clasped at one shoulder, worn gauntlets, sword at hip.
- Expression range: brave frustration at the force field, gratitude after victory, quiet concern when noticing 40R53's restlessness, grief and respect when granting freedom.
- Signature gesture: one gloved hand resting gently on 40R53's neck plate.

## The Mad Mage

- Role: antagonist whose power depends on the crystal.
- Age and build: older adult, tall and thin.
- Face and hair: gaunt face, wild white hair, sharp gray beard.
- Skin tone: pale skin.
- Outfit: torn black and indigo robes, metal rings, cracked staff, violet crystal shards on belt.
- Expression range: arrogance, panic, hollow defeat.
- Important visual rule: after the power crystal is destroyed, the mage appears ordinary and powerless, with faded robes and no magical glow.

## Key Locations And Objects

- The mage's tower: jagged black stone tower on a stormy ridge, violet light leaking from windows, circular ritual chamber around the power crystal.
- Power crystal: floating violet crystal the size of a person, faceted, bright core, arcing magic, source of the mage's power.
- Force field: transparent violet dome around the crystal, wet-glass shimmer, sparks against living bodies, harmless passage for the robotic horse until impact.
- Hero's stable: dry stone stable with charging rig, maintenance tools, oil cloths, warm lanterns, hay stored nearby for living horses but not useful to 40R53.
- Open kingdom road: muddy tracks, rain, fields, stone bridges, forest edges, and finally the tallest hill overlooking the kingdom at sunset.
