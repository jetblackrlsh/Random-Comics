# Page Script

Global style for every page: full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, complete page with multiple panels or a splash panel, photoreal documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism look, natural imperfect framing, believable real-world lighting, practical horror creature realism, clean rectangular caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no blank text boxes. All readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art. This edition has 8 pages: cover plus 7 story pages.

## Page 01 - Cover

Story role: Signal the horror-comedy retail premise and Evan's rise and fall as monster king.

Layout: Dramatic realistic photo-comic cover splash. Evan Rook stands in an endless fluorescent storage aisle wearing his dusty slate blue manager shirt, lanyard, key ring, and an improvised crown made from shelf labels and barcode scanners. Marcy-Monster crouches at his side with fleshy headphone ears and a single glowing smartphone eye. Behind them, distorted mall-worker monsters fill the shelves, and a nearly human Manager-Monster reaches from darkness.

Caption boxes/text inside art: Title text: "MANAGER OF MONSTERS". Small caption box: "Behind one unlabeled door, every workday became a kingdom."

## Page 02 - Door Not On The Map

Story role: Establish Evan's ordinary job, the hidden door, and his decision to investigate.

Layout: Five panels. Panel 1: Evan closes a mall store at night under dull fluorescent lights, checking a clipboard and key ring. Panel 2: he studies a laminated store map beside stockroom shelves. Panel 3: an unlabeled gray service door appears behind a rolling rack where no door should be. Panel 4: close-up of Evan's hand on the door handle, name badge and keys visible. Panel 5: he steps into a plain storage room of cardboard boxes and metal shelves.

Caption boxes inside art: "Evan Rook knew every shortcut in the store." "The new door was not on the map." "It opened like any other problem he was expected to solve."

## Page 03 - Inventory Gets Strange

Story role: Show the room shifting from mundane storage to impossible territory and Evan losing time.

Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: Evan searches ordinary shelves for useful supplies. Panel 2: boxes contain impossible retail objects: mannequins with employee badges, wet clocks, packages labeled with dates from the future but no readable brand text. Panel 3: the aisle stretches much farther than the store could contain. Panel 4: Evan turns past shelf corners without noticing. Panel 5: close-up of his watch showing hours have passed. Panel 6: wide shot from behind Evan, the entrance door gone, shelves vanishing into darkness.

Caption boxes inside art: "At first, it was only overstock." "Then the inventory forgot what stores were for." "Hours passed before Evan noticed the door was gone."

## Page 04 - The First Growl

Story role: Introduce Marcy-Monster and Evan's first use of workplace memory as control.

Layout: Five panels. Panel 1: Evan freezes as something moves behind a shelf. Panel 2: Marcy-Monster unfolds from the aisle, fleshy headphones pulsing, single smartphone eye glowing. Panel 3: Evan backs away in terror, recognizing pieces of his coworker. Panel 4: he points with manager authority while Marcy-Monster looms. Panel 5: Marcy-Monster stops, grumbling, now crouched beside him like a resentful pet.

Caption boxes inside art: "He called into the shelves." "The answer was a growl with Marcy's posture." "Evan remembered the one threat she respected: a double shift." "The monster obeyed."

## Page 05 - Kingdom By Policy

Story role: Escalate Evan's control over other familiar monsters and define the rule of the world.

Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: Customer-Monster lurches forward, receipt tongues whipping. Panel 2: Evan brandishes a return-policy clipboard and the creature lowers its many hands. Panel 3: Security-Monster blocks an aisle with a CCTV face. Panel 4: Evan threatens a write-up and the security creature steps aside. Panel 5: Supervisor-Monster towers over him with red-pen fingers. Panel 6: Evan walks between kneeling monsters with Marcy-Monster at his side.

Caption boxes inside art: "Every creature was someone he almost remembered." "Every memory was a leash." "Return policy. Write-up. Double shift." "The storage room bowed to ordinary words."

## Page 06 - King Of The Aisles

Story role: Show Evan's transformation from survivor to ruler as ordinary memories begin to fade.

Layout: Five panels. Panel 1: Evan sits on a throne of stacked pallets and broken registers, wearing the improvised retail crown. Panel 2: monsters sort bizarre objects in endless aisles under his command. Panel 3: close-up of Evan staring at his old name badge as the name feels meaningless. Panel 4: his memory of the mall storefront appears as a faint blurred panel within the page, dissolving into fluorescent haze. Panel 5: King Evan raises his key ring while monsters gather around him.

Caption boxes inside art: "Control became easier than escape." "The store, the mall, the map, even his own last name thinned away." "The room gave him a crown for forgetting."

## Page 07 - The One He Cannot Name

Story role: Bring in Manager-Monster and reveal that Evan no longer recognizes the mundane self that could have saved him.

Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: a hush falls over the monster court in the storage aisles. Panel 2: Manager-Monster steps from behind a shelf, nearly human, wearing Evan's old slate blue shirt, lanyard, and tired employee-service smile. Panel 3: King Evan looks confused and offended, not recognizing the face. Panel 4: Marcy-Monster and the other creatures watch, uncertain. Panel 5: Manager-Monster reaches with long shadow arms. Panel 6: the old name badge glints on Manager-Monster's chest while Evan cannot read it.

Caption boxes inside art: "Then the room made a monster from Evan himself." "It looked too much like the life he had misplaced." "Without the memory, he had no command for it."

## Page 08 - Normal Behind The Door

Story role: Resolve the story with Evan's fall and the hidden world restoring its own order.

Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: the improvised barcode crown lies tipped on the concrete floor beside Evan's key ring. Panel 2: Marcy-Monster turns away from the abandoned crown toward Manager-Monster. Panel 3: the Familiar Monsters gather behind Manager-Monster like a new workplace order. Panel 4: close-up of Evan's key ring and lanyard left among torn shelf labels. Panel 5: the monsters calmly resume sorting impossible objects as if nothing unusual happened. Panel 6: far away in darkness, an unlabeled gray door waits at the end of an aisle no map can find.

Caption boxes inside art: "The king was gone like a bad shift ending." "His subjects remembered what he forgot: he was not one of them." "Behind the unlabeled door, the strange world returned to normal."
