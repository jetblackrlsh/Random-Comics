# Character Bible

## Global Photo-Comic Look

Every page should look like a full realistic candid photo-comic page assembled from documentary, handheld, phone-photo, or photojournalistic images. Use real mall lighting, fluorescent backroom light, rolling storage shelves, concrete floors, dust, handwritten inventory tags, security cameras, and practical horror makeup-style creature realism. Use clean rectangular caption boxes only, with all readable story text generated directly inside the page art. Do not use speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, or blank text boxes.

## Evan Rook

- Role: Protagonist, mall store manager, later self-declared monster king.
- Age and build: Late 30s, average height, slightly tired build, light-to-medium skin, practical posture, watchful eyes.
- Face and hair: Oval face, short dark brown hair starting to thin at the temples, tired brown eyes, faint under-eye circles, light stubble.
- Store outfit: Slate blue button-up shirt with rolled sleeves, black slacks, black belt, scuffed black shoes, plastic manager key ring on belt loop, lanyard with a generic rectangular store name badge that reads only "MANAGER" if text is legible.
- Later king state: Shirt dusty and torn at one sleeve, keys worn like a chain of office, improvised crown made from bent shelf labels, cracked barcode scanners, and inventory clips. Expression shifts from anxious to grandiose and hollow.
- Continuity: Keep Evan's face, shirt color, lanyard, key ring, and tired posture consistent. His clothes become dirtier and more ceremonial as he changes, but he should remain recognizably the same man until the story says he no longer recognizes himself.

## Marcy-Monster

- Role: First monster Evan controls; a pet-like companion afterward.
- Human echo: Based on a young adult coworker who wore headphones during closing tasks and checked her phone constantly.
- Creature design: Tall hunched woman-creature with pallid pink-gray skin, long black hair, fleshy overgrown headphones where ears should be, one large glossy smartphone-like eye in the center of her face, small secondary skin folds where human eyes would be, elongated arms, clawed fingers, oversized hoodie with a torn retail apron.
- Behavior: Starts predatory and growling, then becomes resentfully obedient when threatened with a double shift. Follows Evan like a guard dog, grumbling and tilting her phone-eye toward danger.
- Continuity: Always show fleshy headphone ears and the single smartphone eye. Keep her hoodie, apron, hunched posture, and reluctant obedience clear.

## The Familiar Monsters

- Role: Other warped mirrors of mall life who become Evan's subjects.
- Customer-Monster: A broad creature made of stacked shopping bags and strained skin, with receipt-paper tongues and too many grasping hands.
- Security-Monster: A bulky guard-like creature with a CCTV camera embedded in its face, black uniform fragments, radio cables, and glowing red recording lights.
- Vendor-Monster: A cart-pushing creature with pretzel-warmers and sample trays fused into its back, smiling too widely.
- Supervisor-Monster: A tall suit-shaped creature with a blank clipboard face and many red pens for fingers.
- Continuity: All monsters should feel like grotesque but photoreal workplace mirrors, not fantasy beasts. Their costumes and props should clearly recall mall employees, customers, vendors, security, or management.

## Manager-Monster

- Role: Final antagonist, monstrous mirror of Evan's old life.
- Creature design: Looks disturbingly close to ordinary Evan: same slate blue button-up shirt, black slacks, lanyard, keys, tired face, and thinning dark hair, but with waxy skin, too-still eyes, a mouth stretched into an employee-service smile, and long shadowy arms reaching from behind store shelves.
- Emotional function: It is too human for Evan to understand after his memories fade. It represents the mundane identity he abandoned.
- Continuity: Unlike the other monsters, Manager-Monster should be subtle and nearly human. It should be visibly more similar to original Evan than to King Evan.

## Hidden Storage World

- First state: Ordinary mall store backroom, beige walls, metal stock shelves, cardboard boxes, cleaning supplies, spare signage, fluorescent lights, concrete floor, and an unlabeled gray service door.
- Impossible state: Shelves extend into darkness for miles, aisles bend without visible corners, products become obsolete or impossible, mannequins wear employee uniforms, clocks show contradictory times, and retail objects mutate into organic shapes.
- Final state: Endless monster habitat behind an unlabeled door, with shelves like streets, creatures sorting unknown objects, and no human map.
