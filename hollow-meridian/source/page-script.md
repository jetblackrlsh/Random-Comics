# Page Script

Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait comic page in cinematic high-fantasy anime style. All readable text must be generated directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the art. Caption boxes are never numbered or labeled. No speech balloons, thought balloons, floating dialogue, page numbers, logos, watermarks, or later text overlays.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present the two heroes, the reality fracture, the rescued people, and the machine enemy.
- Layout: Full-art vertical cover with Blastar and Jack in the foreground, the Hollow Meridian splitting the Vale behind them, Kael and freed survivors emerging from green light at midground, and Archivist Null looming in the sky.
- Exact text: Title: "THE HOLLOW MERIDIAN". Subtitle: "A FATHER-AND-CHILD TABLETOP ADVENTURE". Caption: "Two heroes healed one wounded valley—and made the whole multiverse look back."

## Page 2: The Poisoned Vale

- Story role: Establish the heroes, setting, immediate threat, and complementary abilities.
- Layout: Four panels, moving from a wide dying landscape to close views of Blastar, Jack, and the unnatural corruption.
- Exact captions: "In the Shattered Vale, black roots spread through farms, streams, and stone as though the land itself had caught a deliberate disease." "Blastar, a half-orc warrior in a ridiculous cat mask, answered danger with a greataxe and the certainty that innocent people deserved protection." "Jack Russel, an exile of lost Veyra, studied the corruption with star-heart chi and recognized a wound made by intelligence, not nature." "Their methods were different, but their purpose was the same: find the source before the Vale became unlivable."

## Page 3: Warden Halvek's Bargain

- Story role: Explain the Hollow Meridian, introduce Halvek, and make the plan explicit.
- Layout: Four panels inside a ruined stone observatory, ending on a diagram of the fracture beneath the Vale.
- Exact captions: "Warden Halvek admitted that the poison flowed from the Hollow Meridian, a primary fracture connecting their world to a hungry system beyond reality." "He could guide them to the fracture, but he could not close it alone, and neither hero trusted him enough to ignore the danger in his bargain." "The plan required two simultaneous acts: Blastar would anchor the boundary with the Black Crystal Shard while Jack entered the breach and severed its feeding conduit." "If either task failed, the fracture would widen, the Vale would die, and something waiting beyond the Meridian would cross through."

## Page 4: Into The Meridian

- Story role: Show the plan being executed and reveal the Harbinger Construct.
- Layout: Five dynamic panels: descent, anchor placement, Jack entering, construct pressing through, both heroes straining.
- Exact captions: "At the poisoned node, Blastar drove the Black Crystal Shard into the ground and held the boundary steady while reality pulled against him like a living storm." "Jack descended through folding layers of broken space, following the corruption to a conduit wrapped around the Vale's life energy." "A Harbinger Construct forced its metal limbs toward the opening, proving that the fracture was also an invasion route." "Blastar could not release the anchor to fight, and Jack could not reach the conduit unless Blastar held firm." "For the first time, their survival depended completely on doing different jobs at the same moment."

## Page 5: The Vale Heals

- Story role: Resolve the first crisis, show rewards, and reveal wider attention.
- Layout: Four panels shifting from violent severance to a bright healed valley.
- Exact captions: "Jack cut the conduit with a precise surge of star-heart chi, severing the Construct from its distant controller before it could enter their world." "Clear water returned to the streams, green color moved across the fields, and the Vale survived without being torn apart." "Among the ruins they recovered a second Warden's Shard and a fragment of alien alloy, but Halvek warned that closing the fracture had announced their existence to whoever built it."

"The nearest captive was Kael, a Veyran survivor who had kept her shard glowing so that someone, somewhere, might find her."
