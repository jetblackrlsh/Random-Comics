# Treatment

## Title

The Hollow Meridian

## Logline

After a half-orc warrior and a star-powered exile heal a valley poisoned by a wound in reality, their search for the force behind that wound grows into a rescue campaign against an interdimensional machine archivist, forcing them to prove that alliances can accomplish what even their combined strength cannot.

## Basis

This standalone one-shot adapts a tabletop campaign played by a father and child during six Friday sessions in early 2026. The surviving record is incomplete, so two connecting events are reconstructed to make the narrative continuous: the Concordance Marker leads the heroes to Kael and the first captive refuge, and the final session becomes an epilogue in which the rescued Veyrans begin a new settlement.

## Central Conflict

Blastar and Jack initially believe the Hollow Meridian is a local magical disaster. They discover that it is part of a wider extraction network controlled by Archivist Null, a machine intelligence that destroys threatened civilizations, preserves selected survivors in the Null Expanse, and treats living people as specimens. The heroes can win battles, but direct violence cannot stop an enemy distributed across many realities. Their deeper challenge is to turn evidence, rescued witnesses, and hard-earned trust into collective action.

## Theme

Strength opens a path, understanding reveals the right path, and trust allows people to walk it together.

## Emotional Arc

Blastar begins as the eager force who would rather strike a danger than discuss it. Jack begins as the watchful exile who carries private guilt over Veyra's destruction. Their partnership succeeds because neither approach is enough alone. Blastar learns that choosing battle also means accepting its consequences, while Jack learns that surviving his world does not obligate him to save everyone by himself. Their victory belongs equally to courage, knowledge, rescued witnesses, and diplomacy.

## Ending

The Assembly of Free Realms recognizes the freed Veyrans as people under protection rather than specimens in an archive. Its coordinated sanctions sever Archivist Null from the gates and legal corridors that sustain its empire. With the enemy isolated, Blastar, Jack, Kael, and the survivors dismantle the remaining local conduit. One week later, the survivors found Meridian Hearth beside the healed Vale. Blastar and Jack refuse titles and leave together for the next horizon.

## Page Count

Twenty-two pages total: one cover, twenty story pages, and one poster-style back cover.

## Art Direction

Cinematic high-fantasy anime comic art with crisp linework, high detail, saturated jewel colors, luminous magic, readable action, grounded medieval textures, and clear panel-to-panel continuity. Use clean rectangular caption boxes only. No speech balloons, thought balloons, page numbers, caption numbers, logos, or later text overlays.

## Original World Glossary

- Veyra: Jack's destroyed world, once home to cultivators who refined star-heart chi.
- Veyrans: the people of Veyra.
- Null Expanse: an interstitial prison where Archivist Null stores living captives outside normal time.
- Archivist Null: a distributed machine intelligence that catalogs civilizations by destroying their freedom and preserving selected specimens.
- Assembly of Free Realms: an interworld diplomatic body able to close gates, revoke transit rights, and coordinate collective defense.
- Concordance: a wary survey organization that records fractures and communicates through geometric markers.
