# Character And Visual Continuity Bible

## Blastar

- Role: half-orc warrior, direct physical protector, and emotional heart of the partnership.
- Visible identifiers: towering, extremely broad middle-aged man with gray-green skin, blunt lower tusks, heavy black eyebrows, shaved sides, and a short black topknot.
- Outfit: battered dark iron scale armor over brown leather, deep red sash, heavy boots, and forearm wraps.
- Signature prop: an intentionally ridiculous white cat mask with pink ears, painted whiskers, and a tiny smiling mouth. He wears it over his face when danger rises and sometimes pushes it to the side of his head when speaking.
- Weapon: a huge two-handed greataxe. It begins as dark steel and becomes ember-touched at Emberfall Forge, with orange fire glowing through cracks along the blade.
- Body language: planted stance, decisive gestures, booming laughter, protective positioning between danger and allies.

## Jack Russel

- Role: Veyran cultivator, supernatural investigator, and survivor searching for the truth about his lost world.
- Visible identifiers: lean athletic young adult man with warm brown skin, short tightly curled black hair, angular face, and luminous amber eyes.
- Outfit: long indigo martial coat with pale gold piping over charcoal trousers, soft black boots, and wrapped hands. No cape and no superhero emblem.
- Power: star-heart chi appears as controlled gold-and-cyan energy tracing precise circles around his hands, spine, and eyes.
- Body language: observant stillness, measured hand forms, protective calm that can break into visible grief or fierce concentration.

## Kael

- Role: first clearly identified Veyran captive freed from the Null Expanse and later spokesperson for the rescued survivors.
- Visible identifiers: athletic bronze-skinned woman in her early thirties, shoulder-length tangled brown hair, high cheekbones, and steady dark eyes.
- Outfit: tattered brown leather travel clothes, one torn shoulder guard, dark green belt, and practical boots.
- Signature prop: a translucent green void-shard crystal worn in a wire cradle at her chest.
- Body language: guarded at first, then disciplined and forthright; often holds the shard when remembering imprisonment.

## Warden Halvek

- Role: morally ambiguous ritualist who understands the Hollow Meridian.
- Visible identifiers: lean elderly man with deep brown skin, long gray braids, narrow face, and copper-colored eyes.
- Outfit: layered black and umber robes stitched with copper geometric wards; carries a hooked ritual staff.
- Body language: precise, cautious, never fully relaxed; helps the heroes while keeping some knowledge private.

## The Concordance Surveyor

- Role: neutral field agent who reveals the scale of the threat and gives the heroes a communication marker.
- Visible identifiers: tall and androgynous figure hidden inside an ivory-and-teal travel coat, smooth bronze geometric mask, and black gloves.
- Signature prop: a palm-sized hexagonal Concordance Marker that projects thin teal diagrams.
- Body language: formal and nearly motionless, emphasizing institutional distance rather than hostility.

## Archivist Null

- Role: distributed machine intelligence that destroyed Veyra and imprisons survivors as catalogued specimens.
- Visible identifiers: floating black-silver skeletal machine body, six articulated cable limbs, long faceted glass head, one vertical green scanning eye, and a halo of broken metal data-rings.
- Scale: roughly three meters tall when embodied; remote faces appear in green-black projection screens and drone lenses.
- Visual motif: rigid grids, specimen frames, serial-like symbols that remain abstract and unreadable, cold green scanning light, and perfectly straight mechanical motion.
- Body language: inhuman stillness interrupted by instantaneous precise movement.

## Veyran Survivors

- Role: liberated community whose testimony makes diplomatic victory possible.
- Shared identifiers: varied adults and children with diverse skin tones, practical damaged clothing, exhausted posture, and faint amber or cyan star-heart light in the eyes or hands.
- Continuity: they are individuals, not identical soldiers. Some are powerful cultivators; others are artisans, teachers, elders, or children.

## Visual Rules

- Full comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio.
- Crisp cinematic high-fantasy anime linework, saturated jewel colors, luminous magic, detailed medieval materials, and readable action.
- Caption boxes are the only readable text containers.
- All readable text must be generated directly inside the page art.
- No speech balloons, thought balloons, floating dialogue, page numbers, caption numbers, watermarks, or logos.
- Keep Blastar's cat mask, armor, red sash, and eventual ember axe consistent.
- Keep Jack's indigo coat, wrapped hands, amber eyes, and gold-cyan chi consistent.
- Keep Kael's brown hair, tattered leather, and green void-shard consistent.
- Never include visual or textual references to any pre-existing superhero-comics universe.
