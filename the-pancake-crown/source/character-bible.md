# Character And Reference Bible

## Jet Black / Nicholas Alexander Benson

- Role: protagonist; hungry traveler; quick improviser; eventual leader of the goblin bandits.
- Identity reference: `../../series/probably-dreaming/reference-images/jet-black-character.png`.
- Visible identity: African-American man in his late twenties with warm dark-brown skin, brown eyes, very long black dreadlocks extending well past his shoulders, a full dense black beard and mustache, and a friendly expressive face.
- Build and posture: five feet ten inches, slim-to-average athletic build; relaxed curiosity at first, then increasingly weak and low to the ground as the poison advances.
- Outfit: open black zip-up hoodie over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants, white sneakers, and a small black cross-body belt bag. Preserve the reference exactly. Do not treat clothing text as his name.
- Emotional range: hungry curiosity, delighted amazement, betrayed alarm, desperate concentration, pained cunning, fierce resolve, exhausted disbelief, amused acceptance.
- Persistent continuity: purple-green shadows beneath the eyes and a faint violet cast to the lips appear after eating the pancakes and worsen after the herbs. Mud, torn fabric at one knee, and leaf litter accumulate after the fall. All poison symptoms vanish after the antidote on page 7, while the mud and torn knee remain.

## Gribnash

- Role: goblin bandit leader; guardian of the antidote; poisoned rival; defeated former chief.
- Appearance: compact muscular adult goblin about four feet tall, moss-green skin, long pointed ears with notched edges, amber eyes, heavy brow, broad flattened nose, one intact lower tusk and one broken lower tusk, bald crown with a short black topknot, and rough scarred hands.
- Outfit: dark crimson patchwork leather jerkin, black shoulder guards, brown belt with small stolen pouches, charcoal trousers, wrapped calves, and battered brown boots. A tiny cobalt-blue glass antidote vial hangs at his belt before Jet steals it.
- Emotional range: smug theatrical confidence, shocked outrage under flying pancakes, pleading sickness, greedy hope, betrayal, exhausted ferocity, stunned defeat.
- Persistent continuity: golden pancake batter and syrup coat the left side of his face and crimson jerkin after page 3. The same purple-green poison shadows later appear beneath his eyes. Mud covers him during the crawl and wrestle.

## Goblin Bandits

- Role: ensemble obstacle and final followers.
- Appearance: six to ten adult goblins with varied olive, fern-green, and gray-green skin; long pointed ears; practical patched leather and scavenged forest gear; small clubs, nets, rope, wooden bows, and cooking tools. Distinct but visually unified by black feathers tied to their clothes.
- Behavior: gleeful ambushers, frantic pursuers, confused spectators during the double poisoning, then ceremonially serious when strength changes leadership.
- Final prop: a crude crown woven from thornless twigs, black feathers, red cord, and one small lacquered golden pancake medallion.

## The Golden Pancakes And Poison

- The pancakes are thick, flawless, golden, steaming, and glossy with syrup, arranged on an ornate ceramic plate at the center of the impossible banquet.
- Poison is not gore. Show it through purple-green vapor at the food, pallor, sweat, unfocused eyes, trembling hands, and faint sickly color shadows around the affected characters.
- Pancake batter and syrup visibly remain on Gribnash after Jet throws the plate.

## The Antidote

- A thumb-sized cobalt-blue glass vial with a silver stopper, filled with luminous turquoise liquid.
- Jet steals the vial on page 3. It flies from his hand on page 4 and cracks against a broad waxy leaf, leaving a small glowing turquoise puddle cupped on the leaf and damp moss beneath it.
- The puddle remains in the same small clearing through pages 5-7. There is only enough liquid for one person.

## The False Herbs

- Broad serrated forest leaves with pale lavender veins and clusters of tiny white berries.
- They look plausibly medicinal but release a faint purple-green vapor when crushed.
- Jet chews them on page 4. He gives a handful to Gribnash on page 5.

## The Forest Banquet And Clearing

- Ancient temperate forest with mossy roots, ferns, towering trunks, shafts of warm daylight, and a narrow dirt path.
- The banquet occupies a long rustic table inexplicably laid beneath the trees with pancakes, roasted vegetables, breads, fruit, pastries, pies, cheeses, and silver serving dishes.
- Concealed rope nets, goblin footprints, and black feathers reveal that the beautiful feast is bait.
- The chase ends at a muddy fern-ringed clearing where the glowing antidote puddle rests on a broad leaf beside a twisted root.
