Create a standalone Fortune's Thread one-shot based on this campaign:

Jet wanders into a forest in search of food and discovers a fantastic banquet. Golden pancakes turn out to be poisoned bait in a goblin-bandit trap. Jet throws the pancakes into the goblin leader's face, steals the antidote, escapes, trips, and spills it. He mistakes wild herbs for a cure and makes the poisoning worse as the goblins catch him. The goblin leader is also dying from the poisoned pancakes and begs for the antidote, so Jet tricks him into eating the same toxic herbs. Both collapse and crawl toward the spilled antidote. After the leader reaches it first, Jet wrestles him away, licks the antidote from the forest floor, and recovers. Impressed by his victory, the goblin bandits anoint Jet as their new leader.

Fortune results reflected in the session: 4 extraordinary success for finding the banquet; 1 disaster for eating the poisoned pancakes; 3 success for stealing the antidote; 2 partial success for escaping but dropping it; 1 disaster for the poisonous herbs; 3 success for tricking the leader; 2 partial success for crawling toward the antidote but being beaten to it; 4 extraordinary success for winning the wrestle, being cured, and becoming goblin leader.
