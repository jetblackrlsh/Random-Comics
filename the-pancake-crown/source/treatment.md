# Treatment

## Title

Fortune's Thread: The Pancake Crown

## Format

Standalone one-shot, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Publication Date

2026-08-10

## Logline

When a starving Jet accepts a miraculous forest banquet and discovers that its golden pancakes are poisoned goblin bait, every attempt to survive makes the trap more absurd—until one desperate wrestling match wins him the cure and an unexpected goblin crown.

## Central Conflict

Jet needs to survive a fast-acting poison while goblin bandits pursue him and their equally poisoned leader fights for the only antidote. The conflict escalates through permanent Fortune's Thread consequences: apparent salvation becomes danger, clever victories create new problems, and even the final cure must be won from the mud.

## Fortune's Thread Logic

Every uncertain action receives a genuine result from one to four: 1 brings disaster, 2 brings partial success, 3 brings success, and 4 brings extraordinary success. The story preserves the campaign's sequence of consequences: 4, 1, 3, 2, 1, 3, 2, 4.

## Theme

Improvisation can turn disaster into opportunity, but every shortcut has consequences. Jet survives not through perfect planning, but through persistence, audacity, and a willingness to keep acting when every option has become ridiculous.

## Ending

Jet defeats the goblin leader in the mud and licks the spilled antidote from a broad leaf. The poison recedes. Expecting revenge, he instead watches the goblin bandits kneel and crown him with a crude circlet decorated with a tiny golden pancake. The final image transforms the forest trap into an accidental coronation: Jet entered hungry and leaves as the Goblin Pancake King.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins hungry and hopeful, relaxes into wonder at the impossible feast, and plunges into alarm when the trap is revealed. His quick thinking becomes mounting desperation as the cure is dropped and the false herbs worsen the poison. At his lowest point he and his enemy crawl side by side toward survival. Jet's decisive final effort restores his life, then flips terror into stunned, exhausted amusement when his attackers acclaim him.

## Cast

- Gribnash: goblin bandit leader, proud keeper of the antidote, then Jet's poisoned rival in the race across the forest floor.
- The goblin bandits: a scrappy woodland gang who obey whoever proves strongest enough to lead them.

## Art Direction

Full realistic candid photo-comic pages with the established Fortune's Thread visual language: photoreal documentary panels, imperfect handheld framing, cinematic natural forest light, readable parchment-colored rectangular caption boxes, and tangible fantasy creatures. Use rich emerald foliage, warm gold food, sickly violet-green poison effects, muddy earth, and crimson goblin leather. No gore. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
