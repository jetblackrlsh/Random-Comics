# Character And Reference Bible

## Visual Style

Realistic candid photo-comic reconstruction, like documentary phone photos, trail-camera stills, and handheld field-investigation photography arranged into comic pages. Natural darkness, grain, imperfect framing, motion blur, and realistic glare are acceptable. Keep the story grounded and factual, not glossy science fiction.

## Recurring Human Figures

### The Informant

Redacted adult male resident, late 40s to early 60s, average build, weathered face partially obscured by darkness, gray work jacket, dark knit cap or hood during night scenes, practical boots, tired but focused expression. His signature props are trail cameras, a small gamma meter, a phone with corrupted GPS traces, and notebooks with redacted labels.

### Neighbor Witness

Adult neighbor, late 40s to late 60s, bundled in an outdoor coat, cautious posture at a property line or porch, face never emphasized. Appears briefly as corroboration, not as a main character.

### Special Agent One

FBI field agent, 30s to 40s, dark field jacket, collared shirt, short hair, serious expression, notebook and pen. Keep agency markings subtle or absent; no large logos.

### Special Agent Two

FBI field agent, 30s to 50s, dark field jacket, utility flashlight, phone camera, careful technical posture. Keep clothing consistent with Special Agent One but visually distinct through stance and flashlight.

## Recurring Locations And Objects

### Secluded Northeastern Pond

Cold wooded property at night, dark water, western treeline, bare branches or dense evergreen silhouettes, damp ground, faint porch light in distance, no city skyline. The far end of the pond is the key stage for the white-over-red light sequence.

### Sensor Array

Trail cameras strapped to trees, small radiation meter showing a spike, phone/GPS screen with glitchy path traces, notebooks with black redaction bars, simple tripods or stakes. These should look like civilian field equipment, not a futuristic lab.

### The Orbs

Small luminous spheres or points of light with realistic glare and bloom: white, blue-white, and red. They hover near treetops, below the horizon, within trees, close to water, and sometimes appear as broad plumes or flashes. They should not look like spaceships, robots, monsters, or aircraft.
