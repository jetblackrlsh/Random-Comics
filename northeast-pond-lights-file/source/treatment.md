# Treatment

## Title

The Pond Lights File

## Logline

After years of private sightings over a secluded Northeastern pond, a credible informant's sensor logs and two FBI agents' own observations turn distant glowing orbs into an unresolved government record.

## Central Conflict

The informant needs the record to capture what keeps happening around his home, while the FBI agents must decide whether ordinary explanations can account for the lights they witness with their own eyes.

## Theme

Some evidence does not resolve a mystery; it makes the mystery harder to dismiss.

## Ending

The June 12, 2026 public release leaves the case unresolved, but the spectacular part remains: the file does not only describe a civilian report. It records agents seeing the lights themselves.

## Emotional Arc

The comic starts with private alarm and doubt around a repeated phenomenon. It escalates through instruments, neighbor corroboration, and the agents' visit. It ends with public documentation: blurry photos, cautious language, and a case that remains open-ended because the witnessed behavior still does not fit the easy answers.

## Cast

- The informant: redacted adult male resident in the Northeastern United States, careful, sleep-deprived, methodical, motivated by documentation rather than fame.
- Neighbor witness: nearby resident who independently sees the orbs, practical and uneasy.
- Special Agent One: FBI field agent in a dark field jacket, skeptical, observant, uses a notebook and phone camera.
- Special Agent Two: FBI field agent in a dark field jacket, calm, technical, watches instruments and GPS logs.
- The pond lights: luminous white, blue-white, and red orb-like lights near treelines and water; silent, erratic, sometimes hovering or repeating an impossible leftward motion.

## Page Count

Eight pages total: cover plus seven story pages.
