# Page Script

## Page 1 - Cover

Story role: Present the case as a government-file reconstruction.

Layout: One large night image of a dark pond and treeline, with a small bright white orb over the water and a red light lower in the distance. Add a subtle file-document border motif and black redaction bars as visual texture.

Caption/title text to generate in the page art:
- Title: "THE POND LIGHTS FILE"
- Caption: "A documented UAP case from the June 12, 2026 release."
- Caption: "The location stayed redacted. The lights did not stay private."

## Page 2 - The Resident Reports

Story role: Establish the informant and the repeated sightings near his home.

Layout: Four panels. Panel 1: rural house near woods at dusk. Panel 2: informant watching the treeline from a porch. Panel 3: white orb hovering near trees. Panel 4: small light close to pond water.

Caption-box text:
- Panel 1: "For years, a resident in the Northeastern United States reported lights near his secluded pond."
- Panel 2: "The file says some objects came within about 200 yards of his home."
- Panel 3: "They hovered, moved, appeared above the horizon, and dropped into the trees."
- Panel 4: "At times, they appeared close to the ground, in the waterline, or just above the pond."

## Page 3 - Instruments In The Dark

Story role: Show that the informant tried to collect data, not just tell a story.

Layout: Three large panels. Panel 1: trail cameras and sensors strapped to trees. Panel 2: gamma meter and notebook showing a spike. Panel 3: phone/GPS display glitching while a distant orb glows beyond the glass reflection.

Caption-box text:
- Panel 1: "He set up trail cameras and sensors across the property."
- Panel 2: "According to the file, gamma radiation spikes appeared during some sightings."
- Panel 3: "He also reported unusual effects on electronics and GPS data."

## Page 4 - Corroboration

Story role: Bring in the neighbor and shift the case from private obsession to corroborated report.

Layout: Four panels. Panel 1: informant showing a neighbor a shaky phone clip. Panel 2: neighbor standing at a fence line, seeing red points over dark water. Panel 3: redacted FBI document page. Panel 4: distant orbs appearing to split or multiply.

Caption-box text:
- Panel 1: "The report did not rely on one set of eyes alone."
- Panel 2: "A neighbor also reported seeing the orbs."
- Panel 3: "The names stayed hidden behind redactions."
- Panel 4: "But the behavior described in the file was specific: hovering, pulsing, vanishing, and returning."

## Page 5 - The Agents Arrive

Story role: Show the November 2024 FBI visit and the first agent-observed white light.

Layout: Four panels. Panel 1: two agents arriving in darkness with flashlights. Panel 2: agents facing the western treeline. Panel 3: white light dancing side to side. Panel 4: the light gone, agents looking at each other.

Caption-box text:
- Panel 1: "In November 2024, two FBI agents visited the property."
- Panel 2: "They watched the same dark treeline the resident had watched."
- Panel 3: "Their report describes a white light that moved side to side for less than ten seconds."
- Panel 4: "Then it disappeared."

## Page 6 - Treeline Pulses And Plumes

Story role: Escalate through multiple phenomena witnessed by the agents.

Layout: Four panels. Panel 1: blue-white pulses at top of treeline. Panel 2: broad pale plume forming in dark air. Panel 3: red flash moving erratically. Panel 4: agents trying to photograph, resulting image blurry.

Caption-box text:
- Panel 1: "More lights appeared at the western treeline."
- Panel 2: "Some pulsed with a white-blue hue before they vanished."
- Panel 3: "The agents also reported broad plumes of light followed by red or white flashes."
- Panel 4: "The photographs were blurry, but the observation was written down."

## Page 7 - The Pond Sequence

Story role: Recreate the most spectacular repeated motion both agents saw.

Layout: Three wide panels stacked vertically. Panel 1: bright white light above bright red light at far end of pond, treetop height. Panel 2: same lights streak quickly from right to left above the water. Panel 3: lights reappear at the starting point and repeat, agents silhouetted in foreground.

Caption-box text:
- Panel 1: "At the far end of the pond, a bright white light hovered above a bright red light."
- Panel 2: "Both agents saw it move quickly from right to left and disappear."
- Panel 3: "Then it reappeared where it had started and repeated the same motion."

## Page 8 - Release 03

Story role: Resolve with the June 12, 2026 declassification and the official unresolved status.

Layout: Four panels. Panel 1: documents stamped as declassified, with redactions. Panel 2: cropped grainy orb video still on a monitor. Panel 3: agents' notes beside drone/aircraft explanation checklist marked improbable. Panel 4: quiet pond at night, one small white point reflected in water.

Caption-box text:
- Panel 1: "On June 12, 2026, the files entered the public record."
- Panel 2: "The release did not prove what the lights were."
- Panel 3: "It showed why obvious explanations were not enough for the witnesses on site."
- Panel 4: "The spectacular part was simple: the mystery had witnesses, instruments, and a file."
