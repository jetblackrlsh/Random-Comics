# Character And Reference Bible

## The Solernoid

- Role: protagonist; last child of Solernoid; adopted son of Brightwalk; solar-powered Hardlight ally.
- Sex and age: male; shown as an infant, a curious boy around ten, a lean adolescent around sixteen, and primarily as an adult around thirty.
- Adult build and posture: tall, powerful athletic build, broad shoulders, grounded heroic posture that becomes visibly burdened after the Network falls.
- Face and skin: warm deep-brown skin; strong oval face; high cheekbones; straight nose; expressive dark brows; clean-shaven.
- Hair: short black locs swept back from the forehead; as a boy and teenager, shorter compact locs.
- Eyes: dark brown normally; brilliant amber-gold when charged with sunlight.
- Adult outfit: fitted charcoal sleeveless battle coat over black protective layers, dark trousers and boots, bronze forearm bands, and thin golden Hardlight circuit seams. No lettering or logos. During the Network assault the coat becomes torn and dust-covered.
- Power motif: warm golden-white radiance beneath the skin; blazing amber eyes; solar energy beams from both eyes; huge leaps; immense strength; glow visibly dims after major energy expenditure.
- Emotional range: adopted-child curiosity, delighted belonging, disciplined courage, defensive certainty, rage, horror at consequences, guilt, and quiet love.
- Continuity: his adult appearance remains constant from his first patrol through the Network battle and return to Brightwalk. After the enormous discharge, his eyes become dark, his skin stops glowing, and his clothing stays torn through the return-home page.

## Silverfang

- Role: female daywalker; elite Hardlight warrior; the Solernoid's closest comrade, protector, moral anchor, and eventual partner.
- Age: late twenties to early thirties during the adult story.
- Build and posture: athletic, agile, slightly shorter than the Solernoid, confident forward stance, often positioned protectively beside him when he is depleted.
- Face and skin: medium-brown skin, heart-shaped face, sharp cheekbones, golden-brown eyes, subtle visible upper fangs.
- Hair: long silver-white box braids gathered into a high warrior tail.
- Outfit: matte black fitted armor with pale gray shoulder pieces, white cloth sash, dark boots, and gold-trimmed wrist projector. No lettering or logos.
- Weapon motif: golden Hardlight spear and crescent shield; sunlight does not harm her.
- Emotional range: fierce focus, dry warmth, concern, grief, moral clarity, intimate reassurance.
- Continuity: appears first among the daywalker heroes, then on patrols and in battle. Keep the same armor until the epilogue, where she wears a simple charcoal-and-white civilian coat while retaining her braids and subtle fangs.

## The Vampire Lord

- Role: ruler of the nightward vampire village; gives the infant to Brightwalk; sponsors the Hardlight revolution.
- Apparent age and build: appears mid-fifties, tall and slender, controlled aristocratic bearing.
- Face and skin: very pale gray skin, narrow face, deep-set crimson eyes, pronounced but elegant fangs.
- Hair: long straight white hair.
- Outfit: high-collared black coat with dark crimson lining and small bronze solar devices at the belt; later wears a black solar cloak edged with thin gold light.
- Emotional range: suspicion, scientific fascination, pragmatism, guarded approval, strategic delight.

## Brightwalk Parents

- Role: human adoptive parents who give the Solernoid an ordinary home.
- Mother: middle-aged Black woman with dark-brown skin, round face, shoulder-length natural curls, layered rust-and-cream twilight clothing.
- Father: middle-aged Black man with deep-brown skin, close-cropped hair and short beard, sturdy build, weathered olive work coat.
- Emotional motif: protective tenderness and complete acceptance without answers.

## Hardlight Vampires

- Appearance: diverse adult vampires with pale gray or cool-brown skin, crimson or dark eyes, subtle fangs, black layered clothing, and hooded solar cloaks traced by narrow gold light.
- Technology: compact bronze-black projectors create solid-looking translucent gold weapons, shields, restraints, and cages. Their technology is crude, handmade, and visibly derived from an incomprehensibly smoother alien pod.
- Behavior: protectors in Brightwalk; stealth raiders around the Network.

## Daywalkers

- Appearance: human-vampire hybrids of varied skin tones with subtle fangs, reflective amber or crimson eyes, and practical mixed human-vampire clothing.
- Hardlight force: elite fighters use direct golden weapons without solar cloaks. Silverfang is their clearest recurring visual anchor.

## Phototrophs

- Appearance: biologically realistic humanoid fungal people, generally six to seven feet tall, with fibrous barklike flesh, cap or frond crowns, gill structures, rootlike fingers, and green-gold bioluminescent veins.
- Variants: massive armored bracket-fungus soldiers, agile translucent slime-mold forms, and cordyceps mind-controllers with branching crown filaments.
- Culture: sunlit fungal architecture grown from living trunks, woven mycelial surfaces, spore lanterns, and no written labels in visible scenes.

## Humushes And Firefoxes

- Humushes: human-phototroph hybrids with mostly human faces and proportions, subtle fibrous skin patterns, small cap-like growths or luminous freckles, and partial solar nourishment.
- Firefoxes: selected humush warriors descended from bioluminescent foxfire phototrophs. Mostly human faces, copper-to-brown skin, ember-orange fungal ridges along temples and shoulders, blue-green bioluminescent veins, bright orange-white palms and eyes, fitted dark-green organic armor. They are not fox people and have no animal ears or tails.
- Lead visual anchor: one young adult woman with copper-brown skin, shaved sides with a black braided crest, orange fungal ridges at both temples, luminous turquoise veins, and determined amber eyes.

## The Solernoid Escape Pod

- Design: smooth teardrop-shaped craft of pearl-white metal and seamless amber glass, small enough for one infant, with elegant golden solar channels and no readable controls or lettering.
- Power state: painfully brilliant at the crash; gradually dim; later dismantled only partially, remaining far more advanced than Hardlight devices.

## The Spectrum

- Sunward face: unbearable gold-white daylight, towering fungal jungles, enormous caps and spore clouds.
- Nightward face: permanent starry darkness, black-violet rock, cold mist, vampire stone settlements lit by fire and restrained gold Hardlight.
- Twilight band: permanent amber-blue horizon, human farms, limited livestock, fungal forest edge, villages built from wood, stone, bone, and cultivated mycelium.

## Major Locations

- Brightwalk: crowded twilight human settlement beside harvested fungal jungle; warm lanterns, rough timber and stone homes, walls reinforced with Hardlight defenses.
- Vampire village: gothic black-stone settlement beneath stars; laboratories are workshops of salvaged metal, glass, bone, and the alien pod.
- The Network: integrated human-phototroph city grown around colossal luminous fungal trunks. Underground, braided glowing mycelial cables span cavernous chambers.

## Visual Rules

- Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page.
- Use natural documentary camera energy, believable materials, and photoreal science-fantasy effects.
- Use clean rectangular caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, logos, or watermarks.
- All required readable title and story text must be generated directly inside the page art.
- Keep character faces, clothing, biological traits, technology, and location palettes consistent.
