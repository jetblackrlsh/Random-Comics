# Character Bible

## The Traveler

- Role: protagonist and moral center.
- Visual identifiers: Black woman in her late twenties with deep brown skin, an athletic medium build, an oval face, amber-brown eyes, and long black locs tied into a high ponytail with a brass clasp. She wears a teal hooded travel coat over a mustard-yellow tunic, dark fitted trousers, brown knee-high leather boots, fingerless gloves, and a small cross-body leather satchel. She begins with two gold coins and later carries a radiant golden axe.
- Expression and posture: curious, quick-witted, physically confident, sometimes impulsive; becomes fiercely protective of the bear.

## The Bear

- Role: gentle supporting hero seeking honey, friendship, and belonging.
- Visual identifiers: enormous natural quadrupedal cinnamon-brown bear with a cream muzzle and chest patch, expressive amber eyes, rounded ears, heavy paws, and a forest-green knitted scarf. He is never humanoid and never wears other clothing.
- Expression and posture: cautious around humans, earnest, dryly funny, visibly powerful but nonthreatening; repeatedly throws himself into danger to protect the traveler.

## The Shopkeeper

- Role: fearful merchant whose moral choice resolves the tragedy.
- Visual identifiers: short elderly white man with a round face, bald crown, fluffy white side hair and mustache, round brass spectacles, red shirt, moss-green waistcoat, brown apron, dark trousers, and soft leather shoes.
- Expression and posture: nervous around the bear, stubborn about prices, then ashamed and brave when the bear's heart appears in his shop.

## The River Spirit

- Role: whimsical guardian who gives the unpredictable golden axe.
- Visual identifiers: translucent aqua-blue woman with long flowing hair made of water, luminous pale-blue eyes, and a layered white-and-cyan gown formed from river foam and reflections.
- Expression and posture: serene, mischievous, absent-minded, uncomfortable with fancy possessions.

## The Bog Witch

- Role: antagonist who stole the hives and transformed forest residents into frogs.
- Visual identifiers: tall middle-aged witch with muted olive skin, sharp cheekbones, yellow-green eyes, tangled white hair resembling marsh grass, a ragged swamp-green robe, dark boots, bone jewelry, and a glowing modern smartphone.
- Expression and posture: entitled, suspicious, theatrical, and eager to weaponize outside authority while denying her own wrongdoing.

## Jerry

- Role: the bear's lost mahjong partner, restored on the final page.
- Visual identifiers: slightly smaller dark-brown natural quadrupedal bear with a tan muzzle, kind dark eyes, and a burgundy knitted scarf. Before restoration he is one ordinary green frog among many.

## The Golden Axe

- Visual identifiers: bright polished golden double-headed axe with a dark walnut handle, luminous cyan runes along both blades, and subtle spatial distortion bending light around its edges.
- Continuity: first appears as a rusted iron axe, becomes silver, then gold. Its confirmed power is teleportation. It responds most reliably to emotional intention and unfinished obligation.

## Global Page Rules

Every final page is a complete 4:5 portrait anime comic page. All readable story text is generated directly inside roomy rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Caption boxes contain only the exact reader-facing sentences: no panel labels, caption labels, caption numbers, page numbers, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, subtitles, logos, watermarks, or prompt scaffolding. Preserve approximately six percent clear outer margin around every complete caption border and every letter.
