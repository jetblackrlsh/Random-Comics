# Treatment

## Title

The Golden Axe and the Bear Without a Job

## Publication Date

2026-08-22

## Page Count

8 pages: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

A lost traveler joins a gentle talking bear to recover honey and restore his frog-transformed friend, but an unpredictable golden axe sends their fairy-tale quest into a modern disaster that can only be repaired when a fearful shopkeeper finally values compassion over payment.

## Central Conflict

The traveler must help the bear recover the bog witch's stolen beehives and restore Jerry, but the group lacks enough gold to buy a known magic item. Their reliance on an unexplained teleporting axe repeatedly transforms attempted solutions into larger dangers.

## Theme

Fear makes strangers look like monsters, while compassion creates belonging. The golden axe ultimately follows emotional debts and carries a heart toward the person capable of answering them.

## Emotional Arc

The traveler begins impulsive and amused by the strange forest. She gradually recognizes that the bear's hunger hides loneliness and exclusion. After his repeated acts of protection culminate in a near-fatal sacrifice, she learns to direct magic through care rather than confidence. The shopkeeper likewise moves from fearful profiteering to generosity, giving the bear both life and a place to belong.

## Ending

The shopkeeper sacrifices his finest four-gold magic item to return the bear's accidentally teleported heart. The reunited allies force the bog witch to return the hives and reverse her frog transformations. Jerry becomes a bear again, the bear receives honest work at the newly renamed honey shop, and the traveler keeps the axe with a wiser understanding of its magic.

## Visual Approach

Bright, high-saturation fantasy anime comic art with crisp linework, expressive comedy, cinematic action, and strong visual continuity. Detailed rectangular narration boxes carry the complete cause-and-effect story. Speech bubbles and thought bubbles are never used.
