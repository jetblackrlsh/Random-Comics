The Golden Axe and the Bear Without a Job

Adapted from Jet and Ashley's improvised role-play session of August 21, 2026. The complete prose adaptation is preserved separately as The_Golden_Axe_and_the_Bear_Without_a_Job.md. This comic follows the adaptation's complete arc: a lost traveler meets a talking bear, recruits a fearful magic shopkeeper against a bog witch who stole the forest's beehives and transformed the bear's friend Jerry into a frog, receives a mysterious golden axe from a river spirit, escapes the witch by teleporting to Las Vegas, nearly loses the bear when the axe accidentally teleports his heart, and returns to resolve the quest after the shopkeeper sacrifices his withheld four-gold magic item to restore him.
