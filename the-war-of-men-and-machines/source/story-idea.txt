Create a Random Comics one-shot adaptation of Jet Black's 90-second science-fantasy short film, The War of Men & Machines.

Jax Blade and Kindle, a humanoid mechanical hare, were once each other's only family. A galaxy-wide war between humans and machines pressures Jax to side with humanity and turn against Kindle. Their laser-sword duel ends when Kindle reminds Jax that she loved him when no one else did. Jax rejects tribal loyalty and chooses her. Their reconciliation is immediately tested by a catastrophic meteor bombardment. Working together, they fuse the power of their orange and green blades and save the station, but Kindle overloads her reactor and dies in Jax's arms.

Preserve the short film's tragic emotional clarity, the exact progression from betrayal to reconciliation to cooperation to sacrifice, and its central belief that people must choose the relationships that truly sustain them rather than the biological categories imposed by war.
