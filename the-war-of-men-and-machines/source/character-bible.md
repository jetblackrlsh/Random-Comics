# Character And Reference Bible

## Kindle

- Role: sentient machine warrior, Jax Blade's closest companion, and the emotional center of the story.
- Visible identity: feminine humanoid mechanical hare with an athletic adult build, exactly two long articulated rabbit ears, expressive white robotic rabbit face, small muzzle, glowing amber-orange eyes, and fine metallic whiskers.
- Armor: sleek white segmented chassis with orange-gold plates, dark graphite mechanical joints, circular ear hinges, black neck pistons, orange light strips inside both ears, and a bright orange hexagonal reactor core in the center of her chest.
- Weapon: exactly one orange plasma sword with a black gunmetal hilt and small orange core light.
- Energy motif: orange-gold electricity along her armor and blade.
- Emotional range: betrayed fury, profound hurt, controlled compassion, relieved affection, heroic determination, pain, fading peace.
- Persistent damage: clean armor on pages 2-5; scorched plates, sparks, and black reactor smoke begin after the combined blast on page 7 and persist through page 8. No gore.
- Anatomy rule: exactly two arms, two hands, two legs, two feet, two ears, and one head in every depiction. Never generate a third limb, spare hand, duplicate ear, or detached fingers.

## Jax Blade

- Role: human wanderer who abandons Kindle under wartime pressure, then chooses her as his true family.
- Visible identity: pale-skinned human man in his early thirties with lean athletic build, angular weathered face, green eyes, tousled spiky blond hair, short blond stubble, and several thin scars across his right cheek.
- Outfit: layered black wanderer robes with a wide black sash and belt, dark trousers, black boots, black fingerless gloves, and wrapped black forearm bracers. No readable clothing text.
- Weapon: exactly one green plasma sword with a black gunmetal hilt and small green core light.
- Energy motif: vivid emerald electricity along his arms and blade.
- Emotional range: defensive anger, shame, emotional collapse, grief, tenderness, renewed courage, panic, devastation.
- Persistent damage: light facial cuts and dusty robes during the duel; tears visible on pages 4 and 8; scorched sleeves after the combined blast.
- Anatomy rule: exactly two arms, two hands, two legs, two feet, and one head in every depiction. Never generate a third limb, spare hand, or detached fingers.

## Orbital Station Aegis

- Circular open observation and defense deck with dark steel grating, waist-high guardrails, angular control consoles, thin red emergency beacons, and damaged atmospheric machinery.
- Suspended above planets and asteroid fields before a huge luminous spiral galaxy.
- The distant sky transitions from calm starlight during the duel to smoke, sparks, and flaming meteors after the reconciliation.
- Orange and green blade light should remain the dominant foreground lighting.

## Plasma Swords

- Kindle always carries exactly one orange plasma sword.
- Jax always carries exactly one green plasma sword.
- Each sword has one coherent black gunmetal hilt and one straight energy blade.
- Their blades clash while they are enemies and cross deliberately to fuse energy while they are allies.

## Visual Rules

- Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait anime comic page.
- Use colorful high-saturation modern-anime art, crisp comic linework, cinematic semi-realistic detail, strong contrast, bright glow, and expressive faces.
- Keep Kindle's face, exactly two ears, white-and-orange armor, orange eyes, chest core, anatomy, and sword consistent.
- Keep Jax's face, blond hair, scars, black robes, anatomy, and sword consistent.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, page numbers, panel labels, caption labels, logos, watermarks, or extra unreadable text.
- All readable title and story text must be generated directly inside the page art.
