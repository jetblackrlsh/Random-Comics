# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait anime comic page. All readable narration and rare spoken lines must be generated directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Use caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating subtitles, blank text boxes, later text overlay, page numbers, panel numbers, caption numbers, caption labels, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise an intimate human-machine tragedy framed as a spectacular cosmic duel.
- Layout: One dominant close confrontation based on the short film's signature shot.
- Exact readable title text: `THE WAR OF MEN & MACHINES`
- Visual beats: Kindle on the left and Jax on the right roar at one another across two vertical blades, orange and green. Their weapons split a meteor-streaked spiral galaxy into two colors. Both characters grip exactly one sword with anatomically coherent hands.

## Page 2: The Shattered Bond

- Story role: Establish the galaxy-wide war, Kindle and Jax's former bond, and Jax's betrayal.
- Layout: Four large panels in a clear top-to-bottom reading order.
- Exact caption text:
  - `Before the galaxy divided flesh from steel, Jax Blade and Kindle had survived the void as each other's only family.`
  - `Then the war demanded that every human and machine choose a side.`
  - `Jax chose humanity—not because humans had loved him, but because fear told him they were his own kind.`
  - `On Orbital Station Aegis, Kindle met that choice with a drawn blade and a broken heart.`
- Visual beats: warm memory of them repairing each other's wounds; propaganda-like fleets of humans and machines separating across space; ashamed Jax turning away from Kindle; present-day confrontation on the station with orange and green swords igniting.

## Page 3: Everything We Stood For

- Story role: Deliver the duel and expose the personal wound beneath the ideological conflict.
- Layout: Five dynamic panels with diagonal blade movement and a large emotional final panel.
- Exact caption text:
  - `Kindle attacked with betrayed fury. Jax answered every strike with defensive anger.`
  - `"You betrayed everything we stood for!"`
  - `"I am siding with my own kind—not with machines!"`
  - `Kindle's anger softened into something that hurt far more.`
  - `"I was always there when no one else loved you. Machines are your true family—not humans."`
- Visual beats: fast sword clash; Kindle pressing Jax backward; Jax shouting behind a green guard; their blades locked in a shower of sparks; close final panel of Kindle's orange eyes dimming from rage to wounded sorrow.

## Page 4: I Choose You

- Story role: Break Jax's false loyalty and complete the reconciliation.
- Layout: Four spacious emotional panels with the face-touching moment as the dominant panel.
- Exact caption text:
  - `The war disappeared beneath the truth: Kindle had patched his wounds, listened to his dreams, and stayed when every human left.`
  - `Jax deactivated his blade and fell to his knees beneath the weight of what he had done.`
  - `Kindle lowered her weapon, touched his tear-streaked face, and whispered, "In the war of men and machines, we just have to pick each other."`
  - `"I choose you."`
- Visual beats: memory fragments of Kindle caring for Jax; green blade shutting off as he kneels; large tender panel of Kindle cradling his face with both anatomically correct hands; their foreheads close as he chooses her.

## Page 5: The Heavens Fall

- Story role: Shatter the reconciliation with an external catastrophe and transform their choice into action.
- Layout: Four panels that expand from intimacy into vast cosmic danger.
- Exact caption text:
  - `Their reconciliation lasted only seconds before Orbital Station Aegis screamed beneath a meteor bombardment.`
  - `The first impacts tore through the defense deck and turned the stars into fire.`
  - `Kindle and Jax rose back-to-back. This time, their blades faced the same enemy.`
  - `"We cannot give up on each other. Together, we will find a way out."`
- Visual beats: alarm lights interrupting their embrace; meteors striking the outer station; them rising through smoke; final heroic back-to-back pose with exactly one orange and one green sword aimed toward the sky.

## Page 6: United Power

- Story role: Present the impossible odds and the decisive proof that cooperation makes survival possible.
- Layout: Five escalating panels ending in a near-full-width energy blast.
- Exact caption text:
  - `The swarm was too vast for either warrior to stop alone.`
  - `Kindle drove her reactor beyond every safe limit. Jax poured his strength into her calculation.`
  - `"This is only possible when man and machine work together."`
  - `Orange and green became one power.`
  - `Their united blast reached the heavens—and the meteor swarm vanished in light.`
- Visual beats: overwhelming meteor field; Kindle's chest core brightening dangerously; Jax and Kindle raising their blades together; crossed swords forming an orange-green singularity; huge web of electricity vaporizing the meteors above the station.

## Page 7: The Price Of Unity

- Story role: Turn victory into tragedy by revealing the cost of Kindle's choice.
- Layout: Four cinematic panels, slowing sharply after the opening blast.
- Exact caption text:
  - `For one breathless moment, the station was safe.`
  - `Then a heavy metallic body struck the deck.`
  - `Kindle had carried more of the united power than her reactor could survive.`
  - `Jax dropped his sword and caught her as the orange light in her eyes began to fail.`
- Visual beats: clear sky and fading energy web; Kindle collapsing with scorched armor and black reactor smoke; horrified Jax turning; Jax kneeling and gathering Kindle's damaged body into his arms.

## Page 8: The Only Family He Had Known

- Story role: Resolve the story with Kindle's death, Jax's grief, and the meaning of their final choice.
- Layout: Four quiet panels with the final panel occupying the lower half of the page.
- Exact caption text:
  - `Kindle's reactor dimmed to a dying ember. Jax could do nothing but hold her.`
  - `The gentle vibration that had comforted him through countless lonely nights slowed—and stopped.`
  - `Together, they had saved the station and proved that man and machine were stronger as one.`
  - `Jax remained among the ruins, holding the only family he had ever known—the tragic victor of a war where everyone lost.`
- Visual beats: close view of Kindle's flickering amber eyes; Jax pressing his forehead to hers while tears fall; her eye-light going dark as the cleared galaxy returns behind them; final wide image of Jax alone on the ruined deck cradling Kindle beneath the saved stars. End with complete visual finality and no resurrection.
