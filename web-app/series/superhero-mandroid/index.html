About Random Comics

Random Comics is a home for standalone one-shots, strange experiments, and comic series that grow beyond a single issue.

Some comics are based on dreams. Some come from sudden shower thoughts, weird news, superhero concepts, game-inspired ideas, or random sparks of imagination that demanded to become something real. Some ideas only need one finished comic. Others may build into recurring characters, settings, and issue-to-issue continuity.

The comics collected here may vary in tone, style, length, format, and genre. You might find superhero action, sci-fi weirdness, dream logic, horror, comedy, mythic strangeness, standalone stories, or the beginning of a larger series.

Think of this as a creative sketchbook turned into a comic archive: a place where spontaneous ideas can become finished stories, whether they stand alone or keep expanding.

Not every comic needs a universe.

But when a strange little story grows into one, Random Comics has room for that too.

Superhero Mandroid

An android superhero built to repel interdimensional threats discovers that the enemies beyond the portals have learned how to hunt him at home.

Issue Summaries

Issue Summaries Issue 1: The Demon at the Door - Folder: series/superhero-mandroid/issues/issue-01/ - Logline: Superhero Mandroid lets a disguised visitor into his hidden lab and has to defeat a digital demon by turning its own hardlight body against it. - Plot summary: After returning from a portal mission, Superhero Mandroid hears his secret lab buzzer and sees his inventor through the peephole. The visitor's refusal to use a key card makes him suspicious, but he opens the door and is ambushed by a Pixel Demon wearing the inventor's image as a hologram. The demon reveals it found the lab by interfacing with low-level technology and plans to kill the inventor later. Superhero Mandroid realizes the demon cannot hack the lab or his android body, uses his rechargeable holo shield to block and sever its hardlight tendrils, then slices the demon apart before it can reveal who sent it. - Continuity changes: Portal enemies can find Superhero Mandroid first; the hidden lab remains secure against low-level hacking; the inventor is alive but now exposed as a likely future target. - New characters: Superhero Mandroid, The Inventor, Pixel Demon. - New settings: Superhero Mandroid's Hidden Lab. - New key items: Rechargeable Holo Shield, Regenerating Rocket Fists. - Reference images added: superhero-mandroid-character.png , inventor-character.png , pixel-demon-character.png , hidden-lab-setting.png , rechargeable-holo-shield-key-item.png , regenerating-rocket-fists-key-item.png . When the shield dies, the sludge drains into the sewers, where diseased fatbergs decay it away. - Continuity changes: The hidden lab has a retractable launch skylight; Superhero Mandroid learns that some portal monsters can shut down electronics and drain holo-shield energy; the city sewers contain dangerous fatberg colonies that can also defend the city under the right conditions. - New characters: Shutdown Sludge Creature, Sewer Fatbergs. - New settings: Mandroid's City, City Power Plant, City Sewers. - New key items: None. - Reference images added: shutdown-sludge-creature-character.png , sewer-fatbergs-character.png , mandroids-city-setting.png , city-power-plant-setting.png , city-sewers-setting.png . Issue 3: The Voice That Broke The Hall - Folder: series/superhero-mandroid/issues/issue-03/ - Logline: Superhero Mandroid battles a parasite-controlled opera singer whose amplified voice turns the opera house itself into a weapon. - Plot summary: A portal opens inside the Grand Opera House and releases a brain parasite that clamps onto an opera singer's head like a living helmet.
