About Random Comics

Random Comics is a home for standalone one-shots, strange experiments, and comic series that grow beyond a single issue.

Some comics are based on dreams. Some come from sudden shower thoughts, weird news, superhero concepts, game-inspired ideas, or random sparks of imagination that demanded to become something real. Some ideas only need one finished comic. Others may build into recurring characters, settings, and issue-to-issue continuity.

The comics collected here may vary in tone, style, length, format, and genre. You might find superhero action, sci-fi weirdness, dream logic, horror, comedy, mythic strangeness, standalone stories, or the beginning of a larger series.

Think of this as a creative sketchbook turned into a comic archive: a place where spontaneous ideas can become finished stories, whether they stand alone or keep expanding.

Not every comic needs a universe.

But when a strange little story grows into one, Random Comics has room for that too.

Dream Anime Chronicles

Dream Anime Chronicles follows the many AI-born Dream Anime characters of Nicholas Alexander Benson's dream mythology as they prove who they are through luminous anime battles, strange worlds, and power-defining choices.

Characters

Character Descriptions Character: Armor On - First appears: Issue 1, "Armor Against the Eye" - Role: protagonist, astral warrior, armor collector, traveler through virtual worlds. - Visual identifiers: Bald faceless humanoid made of glowing blue energy, smooth featureless head, intense cyan inner light, silver armor chest plate, incomplete modular armor set that can gain new pieces, luminous blue aura, confident forward-leaning battle posture, gestures that shift between proud challenge and anxious vigilance. - Continuity notes: Armor On flies through virtual realms, opens portals between worlds, senses when the hidden watcher is near, and can absorb armor pieces after defeating warriors. Their pursuit of the ultimate armor is fueled by pride, collector obsession, and fear of the unknown entity that watches them. - Reference image: ../reference-images/armor-on-character.png Character: The Watching Eye - First appears: Issue 1, "Armor Against the Eye" - Role: hidden observer, long-term threat, source of Armor On's fear. - Visual identifiers: Huge unblinking eye partly hidden in darkness, gooey black-violet surface, wet feathered edges, pale gold iris, impossible scale, tendrils of shadow and drifting feathers, usually seen through reflections, portals, or the sky between virtual worlds. - Continuity notes: Armor On can sense the entity watching but cannot locate or confront it directly. The Eye's presence pushes Armor On to seek stronger armor, but it may also be testing whether fear controls them. - Reference image: ../reference-images/watching-eye-character.png Character: Mirror Champion - First appears: Issue 1, "Armor Against the Eye" - Role: obstacle warrior guarding the Prism Plate in the Mirror Bastion. - Visual identifiers: Tall armored anime warrior with faceless mirrored helmet, crystalline shoulder guards, reflective silver-and-rainbow armor, prism lance, cape made of angular glass shards, calm defensive stance. - Continuity notes: The Mirror Champion is defeated by Armor On in Issue 1 and loses the Prism Plate, but survives the duel. The character may recur as a rival or witness to Armor On's growth. - Reference image: ../reference-images/mirror-champion-character.png Character: Captain Lady Jane - First appears: Issue 2, "The Honor of Falling" - Role: protagonist, superhero in training, armored heir of an undefeated warrior family. - Visual identifiers: Young adult anime woman with black hair in a high ponytail, black sunglasses, polished silver enchanted knight armor, white cape, athletic build, confident grin, aggressive forward-leaning battle poses, and a family crest glowing at the armor chest. - Continuity notes: Jane's enchanted family armor grants flight, enhanced strength, and durability. She begins obsessed with preserving her family's undefeated reputation, but Issue 2 teaches her that honor means protecting people even when doing so requires being knocked down in public. - Reference image: ../reference-images/captain-lady-jane-character.png

Settings

Setting Descriptions Setting: Fragmented Digital Sky - First appears: Issue 1, "Armor Against the Eye" - Visual identifiers: Vast saturated blue and violet sky filled with floating islands, glowing circuitry in the clouds, pixel-like star fields, luminous portal rings, drifting ruins, impossible horizon lines, and bright anime energy trails. - Continuity notes: Armor On travels through layered virtual worlds connected by this sky. It should feel like a dreamlike network of arenas, realms, and floating pathways. - Reference image: ../reference-images/fragmented-digital-sky-setting.png Setting: Mirror Bastion - First appears: Issue 1, "Armor Against the Eye" - Visual identifiers: Floating crystalline fortress suspended in the Fragmented Digital Sky, mirror walls, rainbow prism bridges, reflective battle platforms, glowing blue data runes, and fractured reflections that show different possible versions of a warrior. - Continuity notes: The Mirror Bastion houses the Prism Plate and tests challengers by reflecting the fear behind their ambition. - Reference image: ../reference-images/mirror-bastion-setting.png Setting: High Harbor City - First appears: Issue 2, "The Honor of Falling" - Visual identifiers: Bright modern anime coastal city with tall glass towers, harbor bridges, rooftop gardens, blue ocean light, heroic skyline, public plazas, and silver knight-family banners on memorial architecture. - Continuity notes: High Harbor City relies on Captain Lady Jane's family legacy for protection, but its people need Jane's responsibility more than her perfect record. - Reference image: ../reference-images/high-harbor-city-setting.png

Key Items

It becomes a visible new layer over their silver chest plate by the end of Issue 1. - Reference image: ../reference-images/prism-plate-key-item.png Key Item: Armor On's Incomplete Armor Set - First appears: Issue 1, "Armor Against the Eye" - Visual identifiers: Modular silver armor over a glowing blue-energy body, initially only a chest plate with partial shoulder and forearm hints, later gaining the Prism Plate as a rainbow-silver overlay. - Continuity notes: Armor On believes completing this armor set will make them invincible enough to survive the hidden watcher. Each new armor piece should feel like both a trophy and a shield against fear. - Reference image: ../reference-images/armor-on-character.png Key Item: Jane's Enchanted Family Armor - First appears: Issue 2, "The Honor of Falling" - Visual identifiers: Polished silver knight armor shaped for aerial superhero action, white cape, black sunglasses as part of Jane's heroic look, inherited crest at the chest, luminous silver-blue seams, and visible scratches or cracks after heavy impacts. - Continuity notes: The armor has passed through Captain Lady Jane's family for generations and grants flight, enhanced strength, and durability. In Issue 2 Jane discovers that it can ground a supernatural attack when she accepts the impact to protect civilians. - Reference image: ../reference-images/captain-lady-jane-character.png Key Item: Gravity Gallows - First appears: Issue 2, "The Honor of Falling" - Visual identifiers: Supernatural black-violet gallows-shaped gravity sigil, chains of dark light, floating stone fragments, clock-bell silhouettes, heavy downward distortion rings, and impact shadows that drag the sky toward the ground. - Continuity notes: The Gravity Gallows attacks High Harbor City by turning boastful pride into literal weight. Jane destroys its manifestation by grounding it through the harbor leyline anchor and her family armor. - Reference image: ../reference-images/gravity-gallows-key-item.png

Issue Summaries

Issue Summaries Issue 1: Armor Against the Eye - Folder: series/dream-anime-chronicles/issues/issue-01/ - Logline: Armor On invades the Mirror Bastion to claim the Prism Plate, but only wins after realizing the armor they need most is the power to see fear clearly. - Plot summary: Armor On races through the Fragmented Digital Sky toward the Mirror Bastion, convinced the Prism Plate will bring them closer to an invincible armor set. The Mirror Champion defeats their first attempts by reflecting Armor On's pride and fear back at them. When the Watching Eye appears in every mirrored surface, Armor On stops chasing raw protection and uses astral perception to identify the real opening, winning the duel and bonding the Prism Plate to their armor. - Continuity changes: Armor On gains the Prism Plate and learns that astral perception is not only a warning system but a strategic power. The Watching Eye remains unresolved and aware of Armor On's growth. - New characters: Armor On, The Watching Eye, Mirror Champion. - New settings: Fragmented Digital Sky, Mirror Bastion. - New key items: Prism Plate, Armor On's Incomplete Armor Set. - Reference images added: armor-on-character.png , watching-eye-character.png , mirror-champion-character.png , fragmented-digital-sky-setting.png , mirror-bastion-setting.png , prism-plate-key-item.png . Issue 2: The Honor of Falling - Folder: series/dream-anime-chronicles/issues/issue-02/ - Logline: Captain Lady Jane faces a gravity-warping siege that turns her own arrogance against her, forcing the undefeated heir to discover that honor means standing back up for the city. - Plot summary: Captain Lady Jane patrols High Harbor City as the latest heir to an undefeated line of armored superheroes. When the Gravity Gallows descends over the harbor, Jane tries to prove the family legend through brute strength and reckless flight, but the Gallows turns every boast into crushing weight. After nearly failing the civilians behind her, Jane remembers that her mother taught her to defend the weak, not preserve an image. She deliberately takes the fall at the harbor leyline anchor, grounds the Gallows through her cracked enchanted armor, and rises as a stronger protector. - Continuity changes: Captain Lady Jane enters the series with a clearer understanding that her family armor's honor is protective rather than performative. High Harbor City, Jane's enchanted family armor, and the Gravity Gallows are introduced. - New characters: Captain Lady Jane. - New settings: High Harbor City. - New key items: Jane's Enchanted Family Armor, Gravity Gallows. - Reference images added: captain-lady-jane-character.png , high-harbor-city-setting.png , gravity-gallows-key-item.png .
