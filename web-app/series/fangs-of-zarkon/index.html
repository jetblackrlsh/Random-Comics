About Random Comics

Random Comics is a home for standalone one-shots, strange experiments, and comic series that grow beyond a single issue.

Some comics are based on dreams. Some come from sudden shower thoughts, weird news, superhero concepts, game-inspired ideas, or random sparks of imagination that demanded to become something real. Some ideas only need one finished comic. Others may build into recurring characters, settings, and issue-to-issue continuity.

The comics collected here may vary in tone, style, length, format, and genre. You might find superhero action, sci-fi weirdness, dream logic, horror, comedy, mythic strangeness, standalone stories, or the beginning of a larger series.

Think of this as a creative sketchbook turned into a comic archive: a place where spontaneous ideas can become finished stories, whether they stand alone or keep expanding.

Not every comic needs a universe.

But when a strange little story grows into one, Random Comics has room for that too.

Fangs of Zarkon

After a duel for an empty demon throne leaves one prince dead and one emperor self-exiled, Damon Zarkon rules a broken empire while searching for a way to save the father who cannot forgive himself.

Characters

Character Descriptions Character: Damon Zarkon - First appears: Issue 1, "The Throne Can Wait" - Role: protagonist, surviving prince, new emperor. - Visual identifiers: late 20s, stern angular face, medium tan skin, long black hair tied back with loose strands, pale gray eyes that glow white when his path-sight activates, athletic swordsman's build, dark charcoal samurai armor with bone-white trim and a weathered crimson sash, controlled posture, grief held behind discipline, carries the Pathfang blade. - Continuity notes: Damon kills Jaco in the succession duel, rescues Zarkon from banishment, then becomes emperor when Zarkon self-exiles. His central ongoing goal is finding his father again. - Reference image: ../reference-images/damon-zarkon-character.png Character: Jaco Zarkon - First appears: Issue 1, "The Throne Can Wait" - Role: antagonist, Damon brother, dead prince. - Visual identifiers: late 20s to early 30s, sharp cheekbones, medium tan skin, black hair in a high warrior knot, narrow dark eyes, black samurai armor with red lacquer plates and silver fang motifs, predatory smirk, aggressive stance, carries the Reality Splitter blade. - Continuity notes: Jaco banished Zarkon before Issue 1 and intended to take the throne. Damon kills him during the duel after blinding him with dust. - Reference image: ../reference-images/jaco-zarkon-character.png Character: Demon Emperor Zarkon - First appears: Issue 1, "The Throne Can Wait" - Role: Damon and Jaco's father, former demon emperor, absent ruler. - Visual identifiers: towering horned demon emperor, weathered red-brown skin, heavy brow, bone crown fused with dark metal, long gray-black hair, black imperial armor with gold and bone inlays, clawed hands, scarred face, terrifying presence softened by paternal grief. - Continuity notes: Zarkon was banished by Jaco before Issue 1. Damon rescues him, but the sight of Jaco dead breaks him. Zarkon takes the Reality Splitter and exiles himself again. - Reference image: ../reference-images/zarkon-character.png

Settings

Key Items

Damon briefly uses it to bring Zarkon back, but Zarkon takes it and uses it for self-exile. - Reference image: ../reference-images/reality-splitter-key-item.png

Issue Summaries

Issue Summaries Issue 1: The Throne Can Wait - Folder: series/fangs-of-zarkon/issues/issue-01/ - Logline: Damon defeats his brother Jaco for the empty demon throne, chooses to rescue their father instead of ruling, and becomes emperor only after Zarkon exiles himself in grief. - Plot summary: Damon and Jaco meet in Zarkon's moonlit throne court after Jaco has banished their father. Jaco attacks with the Reality Splitter's portal slashes, while Damon survives through path-sight and the movement pull of his Pathfang blade. Jaco blinds Damon with reflected moonlight, but Damon senses the portal slash by instinct, then blinds Jaco with dust and kills him by sound. Damon uses the Reality Splitter to retrieve Zarkon, but Zarkon sees Jaco's corpse, realizes his sons destroyed each other in the shadow of his throne, and exiles himself again. Damon is left emperor with a broken heart and a vow to find his father. - Continuity changes: Damon becomes emperor of the Demon Empire; Jaco dies; Zarkon returns briefly, takes the Reality Splitter, and self-exiles; Damon begins his search for Zarkon. - New characters: Damon Zarkon, Jaco Zarkon, Demon Emperor Zarkon. - New settings: Demon Emperor's Throne Court. - New key items: Pathfang Blade, Reality Splitter. - Reference images added: damon-zarkon-character.png , jaco-zarkon-character.png , zarkon-character.png , demon-emperors-throne-court-setting.png , pathfang-blade-key-item.png , reality-splitter-key-item.png .
