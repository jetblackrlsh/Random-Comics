About Random Comics

Random Comics is a home for standalone one-shots, strange experiments, and comic series that grow beyond a single issue.

Some comics are based on dreams. Some come from sudden shower thoughts, weird news, superhero concepts, game-inspired ideas, or random sparks of imagination that demanded to become something real. Some ideas only need one finished comic. Others may build into recurring characters, settings, and issue-to-issue continuity.

The comics collected here may vary in tone, style, length, format, and genre. You might find superhero action, sci-fi weirdness, dream logic, horror, comedy, mythic strangeness, standalone stories, or the beginning of a larger series.

Think of this as a creative sketchbook turned into a comic archive: a place where spontaneous ideas can become finished stories, whether they stand alone or keep expanding.

Not every comic needs a universe.

But when a strange little story grows into one, Random Comics has room for that too.

Sugarblade & Starfang

Two retired legendary warriors try to live quietly on Candy Island, but the enemies they made across alien wars, demon realms, and sugar kingdoms keep forcing them to defend the peace they already earned.

Characters

Character Descriptions Character: Veyro Starfang - First appears: Issue 1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem" - Role: co-protagonist, retired psychic alien warrior. - Visual identifiers: tall lean alien man with pale lavender-gray skin, angular cheekbones, narrow silver eyes, swept-back midnight-blue crest hair, subtle star-speckled markings at the temples, and small fang-like lower canines. He wears a sleeveless charcoal-blue retirement tunic, wrapped forearms, loose dark trousers, rope belt, and beach sandals. His posture is stern and tactical even while relaxing, with violet-blue psychic energy around his hands when powers manifest. - Continuity notes: Veyro once invaded Demon World to stop demonic chaos from reaching his home planet. He now seeks meditation, fishing, and restraint, but his battle instincts remain terrifying. - Reference image: ../reference-images/veyro-starfang-character.png Character: Ronin Mochizuki - First appears: Issue 1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem" - Role: co-protagonist, retired samurai and gardener. - Visual identifiers: Japanese man in his late 40s to early 50s with warm tan skin, calm dark eyes, weathered face, black hair tied in a neat topknot with gray at the temples, short trimmed beard, and composed posture. He wears a faded indigo kimono jacket over practical garden clothes, a pale sash, straw sandals, and keeps the sheathed sword Moonbite at his side only when danger demands it. - Continuity notes: Ronin destroyed Lord Saccharon's first candy golem army and retired to Candy Island to drink tea, tend a mochi garden, and stop living by the blade. - Reference image: ../reference-images/ronin-mochizuki-character.png Character: Malzareth the Demon Wizard - First appears: Issue 1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem" - Role: antagonist, Veyro's arch-enemy. - Visual identifiers: gaunt demon sorcerer with ember-red skin, long black horns, sunken gold eyes, sharp cheekbones, black-violet robes, bone charms, clawed hands, and a dramatic hunched stance. His magic appears as black smoke, violet flame, and red ritual cracks in the ground. - Continuity notes: Malzareth sees Veyro as an invader and tyrant for Veyro's occupation of Demon World. He uses revenge plots, monster creation, possession magic, and dark contracts. - Reference image: ../reference-images/malzareth-demon-wizard-character.png Character: Lord Saccharon the Candymancer - First appears: Issue 1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem" - Role: antagonist, Ronin's arch-enemy. - Visual identifiers: flamboyant human candy sorcerer in his 50s with powdered white swept hair, curled mustache, sharp theatrical eyes, rose-pink velvet coat, gold trim, striped waistcoat, candy-cane staff, lacquered boots, and jewel-like sugar rings on his fingers. He poses like a stage magician and surrounds himself with spun sugar ribbons and hard-candy sparks. - Continuity notes: Saccharon built the first candy golem empire before Ronin destroyed it. He believes candy is the supreme magical art and treats every defeat as aesthetic sabotage. - Reference image: ../reference-images/lord-saccharon-candymancer-character.png Character: Dark Magic Candy Golem - First appears: Issue 1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem" - Role: regenerating monster, combined creation of Malzareth and Saccharon. - Visual identifiers: towering humanoid monster made of cracked black sugar, red licorice tendons, molten caramel seams, gumdrop growths, hard-candy armor plates, demon sigils, and glowing violet-red eyes. Broken pieces pull themselves back together unless bitten and eaten. - Continuity notes: Created by Malzareth and Saccharon as a revenge weapon. Its regeneration fails only where the heroes physically eat it. - Reference image: ../reference-images/dark-magic-candy-golem-character.png

Settings

Setting Descriptions Setting: Candy Island - First appears: Issue 1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem" - Visual identifiers: tropical island paradise with white sugar beaches, turquoise syrup lagoons, caramel rivers, peppermint palm forests, chocolate cliffs, gumdrop mountains, mochi garden terraces, candy-colored birds, and humid golden sunlight. It should feel like a real photographed island where edible magical geology has replaced ordinary sand, stone, and plants. - Continuity notes: Veyro and Ronin retire here because it seems peaceful and remote. Its hidden magical resources attract enemies from both heroes' pasts. - Reference image: ../reference-images/candy-island-setting.png Setting: Mochi Garden Tea Hut - First appears: Issue 1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem" - Visual identifiers: small quiet tea hut beside Ronin's mochi garden, with low wooden porch, paper screens, stone path, mochi plants like soft white rounded shrubs, tea tray, garden shears, raked sugar gravel, and a view toward caramel water and peppermint palms. - Continuity notes: Ronin's preferred retirement space. Veyro sometimes appears nearby while pretending he is not visiting for tea. - Reference image: ../reference-images/mochi-garden-tea-hut-setting.png

Key Items

Issue Summaries

Issue Summaries Issue 1: The Dark Magic Candy Golem - Folder: series/sugarblade-starfang/issues/issue-01/ - Logline: Veyro Starfang and Ronin Mochizuki try to begin peaceful retirements on Candy Island, but their arch-enemies combine demon magic and candymancy into a regenerating candy golem that can only be defeated by being eaten. - Plot summary: Veyro and Ronin separately settle into quiet routines on Candy Island, hoping to avoid war, missions, and legend. Malzareth the Demon Wizard and Lord Saccharon the Candymancer discover their location and form a spiteful alliance. Their Dark Magic Candy Golem survives psychic blasts, sword cuts, and the heroes' combined ultimate attack by reforming from cursed sugar and demon magic. Ronin bites the monster out of exhausted hunger, and the wound does not regenerate. Veyro recognizes the weakness, joins the feast, and the two warriors devour the monster until their horrified enemies flee. - Continuity changes: Veyro and Ronin meet as neighbors and reluctant allies. Malzareth and Saccharon learn Candy Island can be found. The heroes' retirement is already compromised, though they refuse to call it hero work. - New characters: Veyro Starfang, Ronin Mochizuki, Malzareth the Demon Wizard, Lord Saccharon the Candymancer, Dark Magic Candy Golem. - New settings: Candy Island, Mochi Garden Tea Hut. - New key items: Moonbite, Veyro's Fishing Rod. - Reference images added: veyro-starfang-character.png , ronin-mochizuki-character.png , malzareth-demon-wizard-character.png , lord-saccharon-candymancer-character.png , dark-magic-candy-golem-character.png , candy-island-setting.png , mochi-garden-tea-hut-setting.png , moonbite-key-item.png , veyros-fishing-rod-key-item.png .
