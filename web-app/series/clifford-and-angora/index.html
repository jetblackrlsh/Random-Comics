About Random Comics

Random Comics is a home for standalone one-shots, strange experiments, and comic series that grow beyond a single issue.

Some comics are based on dreams. Some come from sudden shower thoughts, weird news, superhero concepts, game-inspired ideas, or random sparks of imagination that demanded to become something real. Some ideas only need one finished comic. Others may build into recurring characters, settings, and issue-to-issue continuity.

The comics collected here may vary in tone, style, length, format, and genre. You might find superhero action, sci-fi weirdness, dream logic, horror, comedy, mythic strangeness, standalone stories, or the beginning of a larger series.

Think of this as a creative sketchbook turned into a comic archive: a place where spontaneous ideas can become finished stories, whether they stand alone or keep expanding.

Not every comic needs a universe.

But when a strange little story grows into one, Random Comics has room for that too.

AI Limitations

All of the comics and stories on Random Comics are created utilizing AI as part of the creative process.

That process can introduce visual inconsistencies, mistakes, awkward details, and story clarity issues. Characters may not look perfectly identical from page to page. Objects may shift. Text may be imperfect. Some scenes may explain too much, too little, or not quite land the way a fully controlled traditional production would.

Random Comics does not try to hide that AI is part of how these comics are made. The archive keeps many of those mistakes, inconsistencies, and rough edges as part of the finished work.

Readers are invited to accept the AI nature of these comics and appreciate them for what they are: strange, experimental, imperfect stories made with a developing creative technology.

As AI tools improve, visual mistakes and story issues will likely decrease over time. For now, the limitations are part of the texture of the project.

Clifford and Angora

A towering goliath bard whose magical songs rarely behave as expected and a red-haired elf fighter whose psychic blade answers to her mind travel a high-fantasy realm, surviving strange quests through improvisation, mutual encouragement, and the strengths they recognize in each other.

Clifford and Angora travel east to face the rival in the artificer's place.

Settings

Setting Descriptions Setting: The Tavern - First appears: Issue 1, "Clifford and Angora's First Adventure" - Visual identifiers: Crowded timber-and-stone fantasy tavern with amber lanterns, communal tables, travelers, mugs, a busy bar, and a clear open floor where magical trouble can erupt. - Continuity notes: Clifford and Angora meet here. Clifford's summon brings twelve angry tiefling women into the room, and Angora's blazing psychic sword frightens them away. Setting: The Magical Library - First appears: Issue 1, "Clifford and Angora's First Adventure" - Visual identifiers: Vast stone library with Gothic blue windows, carved wooden shelves, floating books, violet-blue magical light, reading tables, and open illuminated volumes. - Continuity notes: Angora learns that a warrior's deepest power comes from the mind. Clifford finds the Tune of the Astral Blade here. Setting: The River And Woodland - First appears: Issue 1, "Clifford and Angora's First Adventure" - Visual identifiers: Broad rushing river bordered by green woodland, rocky banks, and a shallow stony margin. Natural daylight contrasts with vivid green-gold music magic and pink-violet psychic energy. - Continuity notes: Shark bandits ambush Clifford and Angora here. Angora's missed psychic blast temporarily opens a crater in the rushing current and frightens the bandits into retreat. Setting: The Potion Shop - First appears: Issue 2, "The Living Lute" - Visual identifiers: Narrow timber-and-stone apothecary crowded with glass bottles, dried herbs, copper tools, shelves, wooden cabinets, and a guarded secret collection behind the counter. - Continuity notes: A mysterious potion explodes during identification, and Angora's uncontrolled restoration attempt completes the shop's destruction. Tournament winnings later pay for a complete rebuild. Setting: The All-Star Arena - First appears: Issue 3, "The All-Star Arena" - Visual identifiers: Monumental open-air fantasy arena built from pale sandstone, dark bronze, and weathered banners, with a sand fighting floor, vaulted fighter gates, elevated royal galleries, and crowded terraces representing many kingdoms. - Continuity notes: The Tavern Champion wins an international tournament here. Its adjacent grounds later support Clifford and Angora's academy challenge. - Reference image: ../reference-images/gladiatorial-arena-setting.png Setting: Clifford And Angora's Warrior Academy - First appears: Issue 4, "The Academy Challenge" - Visual identifiers: Expansive stone training campus constructed around an arena, with open practice courtyards, wooden dummies and obstacle frames, barracks, workshops, a study hall, and banners from many fictional nations. Its mission is to train elite students who return home to teach their nations. Medieval masonry remains dominant, with tactile magitech added rather than modern technology. - Continuity notes: The Rival Artificer guards the stolen ancient Horde text here.
