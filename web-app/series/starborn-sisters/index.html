About Random Comics

Random Comics is a home for standalone one-shots, strange experiments, and comic series that grow beyond a single issue.

Some comics are based on dreams. Some come from sudden shower thoughts, weird news, superhero concepts, game-inspired ideas, or random sparks of imagination that demanded to become something real. Some ideas only need one finished comic. Others may build into recurring characters, settings, and issue-to-issue continuity.

The comics collected here may vary in tone, style, length, format, and genre. You might find superhero action, sci-fi weirdness, dream logic, horror, comedy, mythic strangeness, standalone stories, or the beginning of a larger series.

Think of this as a creative sketchbook turned into a comic archive: a place where spontaneous ideas can become finished stories, whether they stand alone or keep expanding.

Not every comic needs a universe.

But when a strange little story grows into one, Random Comics has room for that too.

Starborn Sisters

Superpowered alien girls from different planets attend an intergalactic college for aspiring superheroes, where they learn the cosmos, master their abilities, and band together against the Galactic Empire.

Issue Summaries

Issue Summaries Issue 1: The First Starfall - Folder: series/starborn-sisters/issues/issue-01/ - Logline: Before Starborn Academy exists, Queen Amazoness shelters an alien princess whose pursuit by the Galactic Empire turns Earth's hidden island into the first lesson of an interstellar heroic future. - Plot summary: Princess Veyla flees a Galactic Empire conquest in an escape pod that scans Earth for powerful allies and crashes through the Amazons' invisibility barrier. The Amazons surround the pod, but Queen Amazoness welcomes the frightened princess and learns that Commander Grax Vorn is hunting her. Veyla believes her plant powers are too gentle for battle, so Amazoness teaches her that Earth plants include thorns, poisons, and predators. When Grax breaches the barrier, Amazoness orders the Amazons to stand down and urges Veyla to use what she has learned. Veyla's poison flower fails because Grax does not breathe, and he knocks her back with a shockwave, ending the issue with Amazoness still confident that the princess can find another answer. - Continuity changes: Queen Amazoness makes first contact with alien life. Princess Veyla learns that studying another world can transform how a power is used. The Galactic Empire discovers that Earth has hidden defenders behind magical barriers. - New characters: Queen Amazoness, Princess Veyla, Commander Grax Vorn, the Amazons. - New settings: Amazon Island Shore, Starborn Academy. - New key items: Princess Veyla's Escape Pod, Amazon Island Invisibility Barrier, Galactic Empire Insignia. - Reference images added: queen-amazoness-character.png , princess-veyla-character.png , commander-grax-vorn-character.png , amazon-warriors-character.png , amazon-island-shore-setting.png , starborn-academy-setting.png , princess-veylas-escape-pod-key-item.png , amazon-island-invisibility-barrier-key-item.png , galactic-empire-insignia-key-item.png .
