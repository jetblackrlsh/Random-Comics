# Page Script

## Page 1: Cover

Story role: Establish the apparent master-and-gladiator relationship, the ruined modern arena, and the stormy dark urban fantasy tone.

Layout: One full-page cover composition closely based on the supplied reference. Nereza walks confidently through rain outside the arena holding a heavy chain attached to crouched, feral Mara. Mara grips her huge bloodied sword. The modern stadium has become a jagged infernal fortress with fire, rubble, lightning, and red floodlights.

Readable text to generate in the page art:

- Large title: "CHAIN OF EMBERS"
- Arena sign: "GLADIATOR ARENA"

No other readable text.

## Page 2: A Normal Thursday

Story role: Establish Mara as an ordinary compassionate modern woman, establish her relationship with Ben, and introduce Nereza separately before the disaster.

Layout: Three large rectangular panels in clear top-to-bottom order.

Caption-box text:

- "Mara Voss was a twenty-nine-year-old paramedic in Graybridge, a city where the worst Thursday-night emergency was usually traffic, not magic."
- "She worked late, called her younger brother Ben on the drive home, and believed every disaster had a cause that trained people could understand."
- "Far below another sky, Nereza of Vharos mapped cracks spreading through her dying demon realm and searched for a world where her civilians might survive."

Visual beats: Mara calmly treats a cyclist beside a normal ambulance; Mara smiles while calling Ben from the parked ambulance; Nereza studies glowing rift maps in a crumbling infernal observatory.

## Page 3: The Breach

Story role: Clearly explain how demons began appearing on Earth and why Nereza came.

Layout: Three large panels, moving from corporate optimism to catastrophic rupture.

Caption-box text:

- "Graybridge Power promised limitless clean energy by drawing heat through a machine called the Threshold Array, even after Nereza sent warnings through its first tiny rifts."
- "During the public activation, the Array tore thousands of doorways between Earth and Vharos. Predatory demons crossed, frightened refugees followed, and human weapons widened every wound they struck."
- "Nereza entered Earth to shut the machine down, but the blast sealed her behind. By sunrise, power, communications, and ordinary law were failing together."

Visual beats: The pristine Array activates before cameras; many rifts rupture across downtown as different demons and terrified families emerge; Nereza crosses toward the machine through chaos while the gateway collapses behind her.

## Page 4: The Arena State

Story role: Clearly explain why modern gladiator arenas exist and how emergency rule became a dystopia.

Layout: Three broad panels with one diagram-like visual of the ward pylons connected to a converted stadium, expressed as story art rather than an infographic.

Caption-box text:

- "Six months later, General Gideon Rusk controlled Graybridge. His ward pylons kept the largest rifts closed, but each pylon consumed the supernatural energy released by pain, fear, and demon blood."
- "Rusk converted stadiums into gladiator arenas because public combat produced that energy in one guarded place, while broadcasts turned terrified citizens into obedient spectators."
- "Food and shelter were tied to arena attendance. Prisoners, debtors, refugees, and captured demons became property, and the emergency system hardened into a profitable new government."

Visual beats: Rusk addresses frightened citizens beneath active ward pylons; the sports stadium is rebuilt as the infernal arena while conduits carry red energy into the city; ration lines watch arena screens as chained prisoners enter.

## Page 5: How Mara Became The Feral

Story role: Show how the compassionate paramedic was enslaved and forcibly transformed.

Layout: Three large panels with a strong before-and-after progression.

Caption-box text:

- "Before Rusk's rule, Mara had used her ambulance to evacuate strangers. When she stole medicine for a fever camp, the Authority called compassion theft and sentenced her to the arena."
- "To make a human strong enough to fight demons, arena surgeons injected Mara with distilled hell-beast marrow and burned a command sigil into her spine."
- "The treatment sharpened her senses, healed wounds, and flooded her body with murderous panic. Every forced victory made the quiet paramedic harder to reach, until crowds knew only the Feral."

Visual beats: Mara carries medicine from an ambulance toward sick civilians and is arrested; brutal but non-graphic transformation surgery; altered Mara wins in the arena, horrified by her own bloodied hands while spectators roar.

## Page 6: Why Nereza Needed A Gladiator

Story role: Explain Nereza's position, the arena's master law, and why she chooses Mara.

Layout: Three large panels.

Caption-box text:

- "Rusk kept Nereza alive because her knowledge stabilized the ward grid. He promised access to the Threshold Array if she served as a licensed demon handler."
- "Under arena law, a demon could avoid execution only by becoming the registered master of a human gladiator. Each victory bought the master another day and another key toward the central machine."
- "Nereza hated the law, but she needed the Array to close the rifts and save both stranded peoples. Then she saw Mara tearing at her chains between matches."

Visual beats: Nereza repairs a ward under guard; Rusk presents the ownership contract and chain; Nereza sees feral Mara in a rain-soaked holding yard and recognizes the magical sickness.

## Page 7: The Contract

Story role: Show exactly how they met, how Nereza became Mara's legal master, and how Mara knowingly chose the stabilizing bond.

Layout: Three intimate panels with roomy captions.

Caption-box text:

- "Nereza recognized the surgeons' mistake: the hell-beast marrow was burning through Mara's mind because no second living will was carrying away its excess rage."
- "She offered a binding contract in plain words. The arena would call Nereza master, but Mara could refuse any private command, and either woman could end the bond after they reached the Array."
- "Mara chose the link because she wanted her mind back. When Nereza took half the fire into herself, the Feral stopped screaming and remembered her own name."

Visual beats: Mara lunges but Nereza calmly sees the glowing marrow currents; Nereza kneels at a safe distance and offers an open hand beside the written contract; their hands meet, fire divides between them, and Mara's eyes return from red to gray-blue.

## Page 8: What Each Woman Gained

Story role: Develop the partnership and make its mutual benefit unmistakable.

Layout: Three large panels showing training, care, and a nonlethal victory.

Caption-box text:

- "The bond did not make Mara tame. It let her decide when to unleash the strength that had once controlled her, while Nereza absorbed the surge and guided her safely home."
- "Mara protected Nereza from guards who despised demons. Nereza treated Mara's wounds, taught her infernal law, and quietly found Ben alive in a worker camp."
- "They won seven matches without killing defeated gladiators. What appeared to the crowd as ownership became a partnership built on survival, honesty, and a shared plan to free everyone."

Visual beats: Controlled training with slack chain; Mara shields Nereza and Nereza tends Mara's wounds beside a small image of Ben's record; the pair stand over spared opponents as the crowd reacts.

## Page 9: Rusk's Lie

Story role: Reveal the hidden alternative to blood-powered wards and define the plan's practical stakes.

Layout: Three panels in a restricted ward chamber.

Caption-box text:

- "Inside a restricted ward chamber, Nereza finally proved Rusk's central lie: death was never required. A freely chosen bond between two living people could power a pylon more steadily than terror."
- "Rusk kept the arenas because forced combat gave him spectacle, money, and control. He planned to open one permanent rift and recruit an infernal army before the public learned the safer method."
- "Mara wanted to attack immediately, but Nereza knew the city would die if the wards failed. They would have to expose Rusk and replace his system at the same time."

Visual beats: Nereza and Mara secretly test their golden paired energy on a pylon; they discover Rusk's army plan on a control display; the two argue, then join hands over a map and choose a careful plan.

## Page 10: Choosing Each Other

Story role: Put their trust under pressure and set the championship plan.

Layout: Three large panels, ending with the two women as equals.

Caption-box text:

- "Rusk announced a championship in which Mara would fight a captive Vharos war-beast. Her victory would place Nereza beside the Array; her death would erase the only witness who understood it."
- "Nereza admitted that she had first used the partnership to reach the machine and save her own people. Mara answered that trust did not require a perfect beginning, only an honest choice now."
- "They chose each other without titles. Mara would spare the beast and turn the crowd, while Nereza would broadcast the truth and convert the ward grid during the confusion."

Visual beats: Rusk announces the chained war-beast on arena screens; Nereza confesses in their cell with the chain lying on the floor; Mara clasps her forearm and they finalize the plan as equals.

## Page 11: The Championship

Story role: Force both women to prove the partnership through costly choices.

Layout: Three dramatic arena panels.

Caption-box text:

- "Rusk activated Mara's command sigil before the match, forcing the Feral rage past anything Nereza had ever carried."
- "Nereza could have released the bond and lived. Instead she took the pain into her own body, giving Mara one clear moment to remember the patients she had sworn never to abandon."
- "Mara lowered her blade, broke the beast's muzzle, and stood beside it. When the arena saw mercy defeat the command sigil, fear changed into anger at the man who had built the cage."

Visual beats: Mara convulses as Rusk triggers the sigil; Nereza absorbs a river of red-black pain and nearly collapses; Mara frees the beast instead of killing it, and the crowd rises against Rusk.

## Page 12: No More Blood

Story role: Resolve the external conflict without implausibly sacrificing the city's protection.

Layout: Three large panels with the largest final panel showing many voluntary pairs powering the grid.

Caption-box text:

- "While guards faced the uprising, Nereza reached the Array and sent her evidence across every arena screen: the wards could be powered by willing pairs, and Rusk had known for months."
- "Mara tore the control baton from Rusk and forced him to unlock every cell. Gladiators and guards chose partners, joined hands, and fed the grid with shared strength instead of suffering."
- "The pylons held. Uncontrolled rifts closed. One stable gate remained for refugees and diplomats, and Rusk's empire ended without sacrificing the city he had used as an excuse."

Visual beats: Nereza broadcasts from the Array; Mara defeats and captures Rusk without killing him as cells open; golden-white energy flows from willing human-demon pairs through the city and seals the violent rifts.

## Page 13: The Chain They Chose

Story role: Show the lasting social and emotional outcome and give the partnership a forward-looking conclusion.

Layout: Three large panels, with the final panel echoing the cover in a transformed context.

Caption-box text:

- "Six months later, Graybridge's arena was a clinic, assembly hall, and gateway office. Mara trained former gladiators to control their altered bodies, while Ben worked beside her distributing medicine."
- "Nereza represented Vharos in the new council. She and Mara kept the old chain only when traveling through territories where a demon 'master' and her feared champion could pass checkpoints others could not."
- "To strangers they still looked like owner and weapon. To each other they were the woman who returned a name and the woman who made a future possible—and every locked arena ahead of them was a promise."

Visual beats: The arena floor functions as a bright clinic and civic center where Mara reunites with Ben; Nereza speaks at a mixed human-demon council; in rain beyond Graybridge, Nereza and Mara walk as equals toward another distant arena, the old chain loose and unattached between their hands.
