# Character And Reference Bible

## Visual Style

- Match the supplied cover's polished dark urban fantasy graphic-novel illustration: dramatic inked linework, realistic anatomy, textured digital painting, stormy cinematic lighting, ruined modern architecture, rain, fire, charcoal gray, ember orange, and warning-sign red.
- Full portrait comic pages with two or three large panels on lore-heavy pages and clean rectangular caption boxes integrated directly into the art.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, page numbers, caption labels, or panel labels.
- The supplied cover is the binding visual reference for Nereza, Mara, the arena district, color language, and overall rendering style.

## Mara Voss

- Human woman, age twenty-nine.
- Before transformation: athletic but ordinary paramedic build, fair skin, shoulder-length wavy blonde hair, clear gray-blue eyes, empathetic face, navy Graybridge EMS jacket over a pale uniform shirt, dark utility trousers, black work boots.
- After transformation: powerful wiry gladiator build; same recognizable face and blonde hair, now long, wild, and tangled; glowing red eyes during rage surges; sharpened canine teeth; scars on arms, cheek, and torso; black cropped gladiator armor, torn dark-brown skirt panels, spiked black bracers, heavy boots.
- Signature weapon: broad brutal asymmetrical sword with a jagged guard. It should appear only after she becomes a gladiator.
- Command sigil: burned black-red branching mark at the upper spine, glimpsed when relevant.
- Body language: grounded and helpful as a paramedic; crouched, predatory, and tense while the marrow controls her; increasingly upright and deliberate as Nereza helps her regain agency.
- Core personality: compassionate, practical, stubborn, protective, observant. Her feral behavior is an imposed medical-magical condition, not her true personality.
- Relationship: publicly Nereza's chained gladiator; privately Nereza's equal partner.

## Nereza of Vharos

- Demon woman, visually about thirty-five.
- Tall, poised, elegant, pink-red skin, angular beautiful face, pointed ears, narrow pale gold eyes, two long swept-back dark horns, living orange-yellow flame instead of hair.
- Outfit: fitted black leather long-sleeved catsuit with high structured collar, corseted waist, gloves, and heeled boots; thick metal coils at the wrists when she is under Authority control; later cleaner but otherwise consistent.
- Signature prop: a heavy black iron arena chain. In public she holds it like Mara's master; in private it remains slack or unfastened.
- Rift-cartographer tools: palm-sized brass-black compass disk engraved with glowing infernal geometry, and compact handwritten maps.
- Body language: composed, sardonic, and commanding in public; exhausted, precise, and unexpectedly gentle in private.
- Core personality: brilliant, proud, cautious, dryly funny, loyal once trust is earned. She came to Earth as a scientist and refugee advocate, not an invader.

## General Gideon Rusk

- Human man, late fifties, broad military build, close-cropped iron-gray hair, square face, cold blue eyes.
- Outfit: severe charcoal emergency-authority uniform with red piping, black armored greatcoat, polished boots, and a metal control baton.
- Public image: calm paternal protector on arena screens.
- Private behavior: calculating authoritarian who uses a real crisis to justify permanent enslavement and suppresses proof that voluntary bonds can power the wards.

## Ben Voss

- Mara's twenty-four-year-old younger brother.
- Slim build, fair skin, sandy-blonde hair, brown eyes, wire-frame glasses.
- Before the Breach: casual green jacket and jeans.
- After liberation: practical clinic vest and rolled sleeves while distributing medicine.
- Kind, technically minded, and devoted to Mara.

## Vharos War-Beast

- Huge quadrupedal demon with muscular charcoal hide, ember cracks, blunt horned head, pale eyes, and an iron muzzle.
- Frightening but visibly captive, wounded, and capable of calming when freed.
- It is not evil; it is another prisoner used by Rusk.

## Locations And Key Props

### Ordinary Graybridge

- Contemporary Midwestern city, wet streets, glass medical center, coffee shops, traffic, buses, apartments, practical urban lighting.

### Threshold Array

- Corporate clean-energy facility with circular steel machinery, blue-white electrical coils, observation glass, and later black-orange rift contamination.

### Gladiator Arena

- Former football stadium converted into a fortress of jagged black stone, scavenged steel, floodlights, red neon signage, cages, broadcast screens, and ward conduits.
- Exterior and weather match the supplied cover: heavy rain, ruined streets, fire, lightning, and modern infrastructure transformed by infernal growth.

### Ward Grid

- Tall pylons built from municipal electrical hardware and black infernal stone.
- Coercive mode glows blood red and orange.
- Voluntary paired mode glows steady gold-white.

## Continuity Rules

- Keep faces, horns, flame hair, hair color, armor, scars, weapon, and body proportions stable across pages.
- After page 5, Mara remains visibly altered even when calm; her red eyes dim to gray-blue when she has control.
- Nereza's living flame hair responds to strain: bright and tall when commanding, low and smoky when exhausted.
- The chain is taut only when the arena's public performance requires it. In private it is slack, open, or set aside.
- Injuries and torn clothing from the championship persist through the liberation and are healed or repaired only in the six-month epilogue.
