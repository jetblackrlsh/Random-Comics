Create a standalone thirteen-page dark urban fantasy dystopia comic based on a supplied cover showing a fire-haired demon woman leading a chained feral blonde gladiator through a ruined modern arena district.

The story begins in an ordinary modern city and clearly explains how a dimensional-energy disaster brought demons to Earth, how emergency ward infrastructure became a dictatorship built around gladiator arenas, why the demon woman came to Earth, how a normal paramedic was transformed into a feral warrior, how the two women met, and why their public master-and-gladiator relationship is privately a mutually beneficial partnership. The ending must dismantle the arena regime without implausibly removing the ward system that keeps civilians alive.
