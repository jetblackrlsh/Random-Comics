## Logline

When two masked thieves mutate into a giant and a living-metal controller, Pulsewing and SuperScope must learn to fight as one team before a mysterious chest destroys a city block or vanishes into criminal hands.

## Central Conflict

Pulsewing's immediate physical intervention and SuperScope's analytical improvisation initially operate in parallel, leaving openings for the mutants and risking civilians. Victory requires them to communicate, correct mistakes, and combine their powers.

## Theme

Real teamwork is not flawless individual heroism; it is noticing what the other person needs, accepting correction, and creating victories neither partner could achieve alone.

## Emotional Arc

Two confident independent heroes collide with a threat that exposes their limitations. They move from reactive solo choices to deliberate cooperation, ultimately saving the chest together and choosing to remain partners on the case.

## Ending

OSJ secures the chest and gives both heroes encrypted liaison badges. A scan connects the artifact to an old case, turning the closed battle into the first chapter of a larger mystery.
