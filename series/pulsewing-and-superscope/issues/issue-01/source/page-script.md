# Page Script

## Page 1 - Cover

- Layout: Full-bleed realistic superhero cover.
- Visual: Pulsewing flies toward the viewer carrying the mysterious chest while SuperScope swings below on a grapple line, aiming the cyan-lit Super Scope. The muscle mutant and metal mutant rise from the damaged street behind them. Violet and cyan light converge around the sealed chest.
- Required title text: `PULSEWING & SUPERSCOPE`
- Required subtitle text: `ISSUE #1: THE CHEST RUN`
- No other readable text, logos, bubbles, or page number.

## Page 2 - The Theft

- Layout: Four large panels in clear reading order.
- Captions:
  1. `A masked crew tried to steal a mysterious chest from a quiet city street.`
  2. `Pulsewing dropped from the skyline to stop their escape.`
  3. `SuperScope arrived seconds later, trusting his AI-enhanced scope to reveal what ordinary eyes could not.`
  4. `Neither hero knew the thieves were mutants—or that the chest could start a war.`
- Visual beats: thieves strain with chest; Pulsewing lands before truck; SuperScope grapples down; truck accelerates and tension spikes.

## Page 3 - Mutants Revealed

- Layout: Four dynamic panels.
- Captions:
  1. `Pulsewing broke the street to stop the truck.`
  2. `The abandoned thief transformed into a giant and punched SuperScope through a storefront.`
  3. `Then the driver pulled metal from the truck and wore it like living armor.`
  4. `Two heroes had become the only shield between two mutants and a store full of civilians.`
- Visual beats: kinetic asphalt ridge; truck impact; SuperScope crashes through glass; both mutants revealed with terrified civilians behind shelving.

## Page 4 - The Trap

- Layout: Four large panels with readable action.
- Captions:
  1. `Injured but conscious, SuperScope used his scanner and grapple to retrieve a first-aid kit.`
  2. `His heat ray melted the armor, but the metal controller turned the molten alloy into restraints.`
  3. `Pulsewing severed the mutant's control by flipping a parked car between them.`
  4. `When SuperScope warned that civilians were trapped inside, she corrected her mistake and cleared the danger.`
- Visual beats: bandaging; heat ray; molten bands; Pulsewing redirects car away from civilians and pulls SuperScope toward safety.

## Page 5 - Move the Package

- Layout: Four panels, increasingly vertical.
- Captions:
  1. `The thieves were not fighting for territory. They were fighting for the chest.`
  2. `Pulsewing carried it skyward, but its impossible weight dragged her down.`
  3. `SuperScope made the load manageable with an anti-gravity ray.`
  4. `Then he grappled both mutants together, buying his partner the seconds she needed.`
- Visual beats: heroes identify the target; Pulsewing strains in flight; cyan field surrounds chest; grapple cable binds both mutants.

## Page 6 - Both Hands

- Layout: Four fast action panels.
- Captions:
  1. `SuperScope exploited the giant's burned skin, weakened him with heat, and ended the fight with a sleep ray.`
  2. `He lifted the metal controller away from every surface his power could command.`
  3. `Pulsewing blasted the floating villain out of the sky.`
  4. `Only after firing with both hands did she realize the chest was no longer in either one.`
- Visual beats: giant shrinks unconscious; metal falls from floating villain; Pulsewing's two-handed violet blast; chest drops into open air.

## Page 7 - The Catch

- Layout: Four panels ending in a wide rescue panel.
- Captions:
  1. `The chest fell toward the street, carrying secrets no one understood.`
  2. `SuperScope slowed it with anti-gravity while Pulsewing dived.`
  3. `Together they caught what neither could save alone.`
  4. `The Officers of Superhuman Justice arrived to secure the mutants and contain the artifact.`
- Visual beats: falling chest; cyan beam and violet dive; controlled two-hero catch; armored silver-and-blue OSJ responders arrive.

## Page 8 - Partners on the Case

- Layout: Four calm but ominous panels.
- Captions:
  1. `The chest held no radiation, poison, or explosive—only one football-sized object behind an energy field no scanner could penetrate.`
  2. `Pulsewing and SuperScope surrendered it to OSJ on one condition: they would remain connected to the case.`
  3. `OSJ issued them encrypted liaison badges. Their first fight had made them partners.`
  4. `Then the scan found a match to an old case, and the unopened chest became the beginning of their next adventure.`
- Visual beats: scanner display facing OSJ officer; chest on anti-gravity pallet; heroes receive badges; final close view of chest glowing faintly as both heroes look toward it.

## Universal Page Constraints

- Full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait.
- Story text appears only inside clean rectangular caption boxes generated in the art.
- No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, caption labels, panel labels, watermarks, or extra readable text.
- No repeated caption wording on the same page.
- Keep every caption, title, letter, and complete text-box border inside a generous outer safe margin.
- Maintain the binding reference designs, ongoing damage, violet Pulsewing effects, cyan SuperScope effects, and realistic physical environments.
