## Issue-Only Antagonists

### Muscle Mutant

Masked adult man whose transformation produces an eight-foot, massively muscular body that tears his charcoal work clothes at the shoulders and arms. When defeated by the sleep ray, he shrinks back into an unconscious ordinary human. He suffers visible but non-graphic heat burns during the battle.

### Metal Mutant

Masked adult man in dark work clothes who draws metal from a delivery truck into a fluid, gunmetal armor shell. The armor can form molten tendrils while he maintains contact. Without physical contact, the metal falls away and becomes inert.

## Continuity Damage

- After page 3, SuperScope carries scratches, dust, a bandage at his ribs, and later scorch damage on his suit.
- After page 4, the street includes a damaged truck, broken asphalt, shattered storefront glass, and scorch marks.
- Pulsewing remains physically uninjured but becomes more deliberate after SuperScope warns her about civilians.
