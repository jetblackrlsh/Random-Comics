# Character Descriptions

## Character: Pulsewing

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Chest Run"
- Role: Co-protagonist; airborne kinetic-energy hero.
- Visual identifiers: Black woman in her late twenties; athletic build; warm deep-brown skin; oval face; focused dark eyes; short natural coils swept upward; fitted matte-black flight suit with violet armored panels, luminous violet lines along her forearms and torso, practical boots, and two large feather-shaped energy wings made of translucent violet light.
- Continuity notes: Flies, produces focused concussive blasts, creates powerful downdrafts, and can redirect heavy objects. Bold and protective, but willing to admit a tactical mistake and correct it immediately. Ends Issue #1 with an OSJ liaison badge and a new partnership with SuperScope.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/pulsewing-character.jpg`

## Character: SuperScope

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Chest Run"
- Role: Co-protagonist; analytical gadget-driven superhero.
- Visual identifiers: Black man in his late twenties; athletic average-height build; medium-deep brown skin; square face; close-cropped black hair; neat short beard; dark navy tactical suit with black armor panels, cyan piping, reinforced gloves and boots, a slim cyan-lit targeting eyepiece over the right eye, a silver grapple gauntlet on the left wrist, and the compact Super Scope device carried in both hands when firing.
- Continuity notes: Uses the AI-enhanced Super Scope to scan environments and opponents and project heat, anti-gravity, and sleep rays. Carries a grapple system and relies on improvisation rather than invulnerability. Injured and burned in Issue #1 but stays operational. Ends Issue #1 with an OSJ liaison badge and a new partnership with Pulsewing.
