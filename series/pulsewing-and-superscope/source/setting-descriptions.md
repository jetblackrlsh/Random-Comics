# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Chest Run Street

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Chest Run"
- Visual identifiers: Contemporary American downtown side street at evening; brick storefronts with broad glass windows; parked cars; asphalt road; practical streetlights; a loading area beside a small delivery truck; interior convenience-store shelving visible through one storefront.
- Continuity notes: The battle leaves broken asphalt, a damaged truck, a shattered storefront window, scorched pavement, and overturned street furniture. It is the public location where Pulsewing and SuperScope first fight as a team.
