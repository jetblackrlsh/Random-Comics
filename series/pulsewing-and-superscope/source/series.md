# Pulsewing & SuperScope

## Title

Pulsewing & SuperScope

## Logline

An instinctive airborne powerhouse and an analytical gadget hero become an unlikely partnership while investigating superhuman crimes, impossible artifacts, and the cold cases linking them.

## Premise

Pulsewing and SuperScope are two independent heroes whose contrasting methods work best together. Pulsewing acts quickly, protects people directly, and commands violet kinetic energy through her wings and hands. SuperScope studies the battlefield, improvises with an AI-enhanced multi-function scope, and turns information into tactical advantages. Their first shared case ends with the Officers of Superhuman Justice issuing them encrypted liaison badges and connecting a mysterious chest to an old investigation. Future issues follow the partners as they uncover the artifact's origin, learn to trust each other, and face threats neither could solve alone.

## Tone

Grounded superhero action with candid documentary energy, visible consequences, tactical improvisation, warmth between partners, and mysteries that expand without making the immediate story unclear.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic handheld photojournalism and emergency-phone-video framing.
- Violet kinetic light for Pulsewing; cyan scanning light for SuperScope.
- Silver-and-blue OSJ technology as a bridge between street-level heroism and larger investigations.
- Damage, dust, imperfect angles, and civilians in real urban environments keep powers physically believable.

## Continuity Notes

- Pulsewing and SuperScope begin Issue #1 as independent heroes and end it as OSJ-connected partners.
- The mysterious chest contains one football-sized object behind a dense energy field.
- OSJ's scan links the chest to an unresolved old case.
- This series is based on the collaborative multi-user shared hallucination session played by Nicholas Alexander Benson as SuperScope and Nova as Pulsewing.
