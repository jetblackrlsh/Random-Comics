# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The Chest Run

- Folder: `series/pulsewing-and-superscope/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Pulsewing and SuperScope must protect civilians, defeat two mutants, and keep an impossible chest from falling into criminal hands.
- Plot summary: Pulsewing interrupts two masked thieves loading a mysterious chest into a truck as SuperScope swings in to assist. The thieves reveal muscle-enhancing and metal-control mutations, injuring SuperScope and endangering civilians. The heroes recover by combining Pulsewing's kinetic flight with SuperScope's scanning, grappling, heat, sleep, and anti-gravity functions. They defeat both mutants and save the falling chest together. OSJ takes custody, gives the heroes liaison badges, and discovers that the chest matches an old case.
- Continuity changes: Pulsewing and SuperScope become partners and OSJ liaisons; the chest enters OSJ containment; an old cold case becomes their next lead.
- New characters: Pulsewing; SuperScope.
- New settings: Chest Run Street and its damaged storefront.
- New key items: Super Scope device; mysterious chest; OSJ encrypted liaison badges.
