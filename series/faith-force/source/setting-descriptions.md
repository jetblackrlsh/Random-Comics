# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Hellgate Foundry

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Portal That Would Not Close"
- Visual identifiers: abandoned riverside steel foundry converted into an occult temple, rusted catwalks, cracked concrete floors, broken skylights, hanging chains, old furnaces, black candles, chalk sigils, red emergency light, rain leaking through roof gaps, improvised altars made from industrial scrap.
- Continuity notes: The cult uses the foundry because its old furnaces and deep foundations make the ritual chamber feel like a wound already half-open. The issue leaves the foundry damaged, scorched, and contaminated by delayed portal energy.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/hellgate-foundry-setting.png`

## Setting: Ritual Chamber

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Portal That Would Not Close"
- Visual identifiers: circular underground furnace room beneath the Hellgate Foundry, vertical iron ribs, molten red light from cracked machinery, occult geometry painted across the floor, chains pulled toward the central portal, cultists in dark coats, smoke moving against gravity.
- Continuity notes: The portal is delayed here, not destroyed. The chamber remains the initial breach point that feeds later demonic magic into the world.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/ritual-chamber-setting.png`
