# Faith Force

## Logline

Earth's greatest mortal martial artists become celestial champions when their faith in each other ignites a golden aura powerful enough to delay Hell's return.

## Premise

Faith Force follows a small team of elite human fighters who begin as the world's greatest martial artists and become Earth's last line of defense against an escalating supernatural war. Their first victory interrupts a demonic cult's attempt to open a Hell portal, but it does not end the threat. The partially awakened portal continues to feed dark magic into the world, drawing demonic armor, rogue divine intervention, planetary life-force, and eventually the arrival of the Demon King.

The team's power begins with martial discipline and trust. Marcus Hale's unshakable belief in his friends manifests as the golden aura called Faith Force, a mortal echo of holy conviction that burns demonic matter on contact. As later threats surpass that first miracle, the team must learn to focus Faith Force, draw on Gaia Green planetary life-force, and ultimately survive contact with Divine Fire.

## Tone

High-octane supernatural martial arts action with sincere heroic stakes, cosmic escalation, and team-first emotion. Battles should feel explosive and physical, but the core of the series is unity: human fighters pushing past mortal limits because they refuse to abandon one another or their world.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Martial arts action should look grounded and physical before supernatural energy erupts: sweat, dust, torn fabric, close-range impacts, bruised knuckles, cracked concrete, and practical footwork.
- Faith Force appears as a warm golden aura around hands, shoulders, eyes, and strike paths, with contact sparks that burn demonic material.
- Demonic magic appears black-red, tar-like, smoky, armored, and insectile, often warping ordinary ritual objects into hostile shapes.
- Hell portal energy appears as a vertical wound in the air, red-black fire at the edges, ember-lit depth beyond, and stone or metal bending toward it.
- Gaia Green should later feel organic and planetary: green light through cracks, vines of energy, pollen-like motes, and seismic pressure.
- Divine Fire should later feel white-gold and dangerous: clean flame, sword-shaped light, wing-like flares, and holy heat.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Continuity Notes

- Faith Force is not tied to one formal religion; in issue #1 it comes from Marcus Hale's intense faith in his teammates and their shared purpose.
- The Hell portal is delayed, not closed, at the end of issue #1. Its slow opening is the engine for the next three issues.
- Cultists survive issue #1 and scatter, carrying knowledge of the interrupted ritual.
- The Demon King does not physically arrive in issue #1, but the portal gives a partial glimpse of the coming threat.
- Marcus is the first hero to manifest Faith Force, but his breakthrough spreads to Serena and Darius because his faith is specifically faith in them.
- Future escalation should preserve the team dynamic: Marcus may be the strongest, but the team wins because every member contributes pressure, courage, and focus.
