# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The Portal That Would Not Close

- Folder: `series/faith-force/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Earth's greatest martial artists interrupt a cult's Hell portal ritual when Marcus Hale's faith in his team ignites the golden aura of Faith Force.
- Plot summary: Marcus Hale, Serena Park, and Darius Cross infiltrate the Hellgate Foundry to stop a demonic cult from opening a portal beneath the city. They defeat the cult's warrior monks with superior martial skill, but the monks channel demon familiars and transform into imps whose dark magic overwhelms ordinary technique. Marcus refuses to let the team break, and his faith in Serena and Darius ignites a golden aura that spreads through them. Their Faith Force burns the imps on contact, lets them break into the ritual chamber, and scatters the cultists before the Demon King can arrive.
- Continuity changes: The Hell portal is delayed but not closed, Faith Force manifests for the first time, surviving cultists scatter, and the team commits to training for the Demon King's eventual return.
- New characters: Marcus Hale, Serena Park, Darius Cross, Hellgate Warrior Monks, Demon King silhouette.
- New settings: Hellgate Foundry, Ritual Chamber.
- New key items: Golden Faith Force Aura, Delayed Hell Portal.
