# Character Descriptions

## Character: Marcus Hale

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Portal That Would Not Close"
- Role: strongest hero, Faith Force catalyst, heavyweight martial artist, team center
- Visual identifiers: adult Black man in his late 30s, tall heavyweight martial artist build, close-cropped black hair, trimmed beard, calm intense eyes, broad nose, scar over left eyebrow, worn white sleeveless gi jacket over a charcoal compression shirt, black martial arts pants, taped wrists, simple red cord bracelet on right wrist.
- Continuity notes: Marcus is the first person to manifest the golden Faith Force aura. His power comes from absolute trust in his friends, not from arrogance. He fights with heavy close-range strikes, grappling, rooted stances, and protective positioning.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/marcus-hale-character.png`

## Character: Serena Park

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Portal That Would Not Close"
- Role: Faith Force member, acrobatic striker, tactical scout
- Visual identifiers: Korean American woman in her early 30s, lean athletic build, angular face, sharp dark eyes, black hair in a short practical undercut, red cropped training jacket over black body armor, gray tactical leggings, fingerless gloves, white ankle wraps, alert coiled posture.
- Continuity notes: Serena is the team's fastest fighter and the first to exploit openings Marcus creates. When Faith Force ignites, her aura trails through spinning kicks and wall-running attacks like gold lightning.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/serena-park-character.png`

## Character: Darius Cross

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Portal That Would Not Close"
- Role: Faith Force member, precision technician, defensive counterfighter
- Visual identifiers: adult man in his mid 30s with medium-brown skin, compact muscular build, shaved head, close-trimmed goatee, thoughtful eyes, navy sleeveless hoodie under a black reinforced vest, olive training pants, wrapped forearms, worn high-top training shoes, calm analytical expression.
- Continuity notes: Darius reads enemy movement and turns pressure into counters. When Faith Force ignites, his aura forms clean golden arcs around blocks, joint locks, and palm strikes.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/darius-cross-character.png`

## Character: Hellgate Warrior Monks

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Portal That Would Not Close"
- Role: cult enforcers, martial obstacle, demonic transformation threat
- Visual identifiers: shaved-headed warrior monks of varied genders and ethnicities, ash-gray ritual robes reinforced with black leather wraps, red cord belts, iron prayer beads, bare forearms marked with ember-like occult brands, disciplined stances, faces calm until demonic familiars surface.
- Continuity notes: The monks are strong human fighters until they channel demon familiars and twist into imp forms. The transformed imps have horn nubs, ember eyes, clawed fingers, leathery patches of skin, and black-red smoke around their limbs. The order is defeated in issue #1, but surviving cult knowledge may return.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/hellgate-warrior-monks-character.png`

## Character: Demon King

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Portal That Would Not Close" as a partial silhouette beyond the portal
- Role: looming series antagonist
- Visual identifiers: towering horned silhouette glimpsed through red-black portal fire, crown-like horns, broad armored shoulders, ember cracks across a massive chest, clawed hand pressing from the far side of reality.
- Continuity notes: The Demon King is not released in issue #1. The partial glimpse establishes the stakes and should remain mysterious until the portal fully opens in a later issue.
- Reference image: None yet; only a distorted portal silhouette appears in issue #1.

## Character: Armored Cultist

- First appears: Issue #2, "Armor Fed By Hell"
- Role: surviving Hellgate cult engineer, power-armored battlefield antagonist
- Visual identifiers: adult cultist hidden inside bulky blackened crimson demonic power armor, asymmetrical furnace-like chest core, horned helmet with narrow ember visor, clawed gauntlets, hydraulic limbs wrapped in occult chain, scorched cloak fragments, red-black portal smoke venting from armor seams.
- Continuity notes: The cultist built the armor from foundry scrap, stolen military plating, and demonic energy leaking from the delayed Hell portal. The armor grows stronger as the portal widens, forcing Marcus to learn how to focus Faith Force into a concentrated hand blast.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/armored-cultist-character.png`
