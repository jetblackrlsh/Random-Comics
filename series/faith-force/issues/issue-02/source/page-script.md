# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce the demonic power armor threat and Marcus's focused Faith Force blast.
- Layout: Single dramatic photo-comic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Faith Force". Smaller issue title text: "Armor Fed By Hell".
- Visual beats: The armored cultist towers in blackened crimson demonic power armor at the center of a shattered city boulevard, furnace chest core glowing red. Marcus stands in the foreground with golden energy compressed into one fist. Serena blurs across a broken vehicle with gold kick trails. Darius braces behind a gold-lit guard. The delayed Hell portal glows on the skyline behind smoke and ruined military vehicles.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: War On The Boulevard

- Story role: Establish that the Hell portal's leakage has turned a cult survivor into a military-scale threat.
- Layout: Four large panels with roomy caption boxes.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The portal we delayed did not sleep. It leaked power into the world one red pulse at a time." Panel 2 top caption: "A surviving cultist built a suit around that leak, turning prayer, scrap, and stolen armor into a walking furnace." Panel 3 top caption: "By noon, the world's armies had learned that bullets could not frighten a machine fed by Hell." Panel 4 top caption: "Faith Force arrived before the boulevard became a graveyard."
- Visual beats: Red-black portal glow over distant skyline; close-up of demonic armor boot stepping through smoke; military vehicles disabled without gore; Marcus, Serena, and Darius arrive glowing gold at the edge of Portal-Siege Boulevard.

## Page 3: Armor Against Armies

- Story role: Show the armored cultist overpowering conventional force.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels with compact captions.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The cultist did not move like a soldier. The armor moved like a siege engine." Panel 2 top caption: "Every step cracked asphalt." Panel 3 top caption: "Every blast pushed trained units back behind their own burning shields." Panel 4 top caption: "Serena saw the gaps first." Panel 5 top caption: "Darius saw the rhythm. Marcus saw the people trapped behind the line."
- Visual beats: Armored cultist advances through smoke; asphalt cracks under heavy boot; red energy pulse knocks back empty armored shields and vehicles, no bodies; Serena studies armor joints from a rooftop; Darius and Marcus shield evacuating civilians and soldiers.

## Page 4: Gold Hits First

- Story role: Let Faith Force initially gain the advantage with the golden aura.
- Layout: Four large panels with confident team action.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Against imps, Faith Force burned on contact. We believed the same light would open this armor." Panel 2 top caption: "Serena struck first, turning speed into gold." Panel 3 top caption: "Darius caught the counterblow and bent its force aside." Panel 4 top caption: "When Marcus hit the chest core, the armor finally staggered."
- Visual beats: Golden team aura flares; Serena kicks the armor's knee joint with gold sparks; Darius blocks a clawed gauntlet with golden arcs; Marcus lands a heavy gold-lit punch on the furnace chest core, forcing the armor backward.

## Page 5: Too Dense To Burn

- Story role: Reveal the armor strengthening over time as the portal expands.
- Layout: Four large panels with a reversal.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Then the skyline pulsed red, and the armor drank from the portal again." Panel 2 top caption: "The plates darkened. The runes thickened. The magic packed itself tighter than our aura could reach." Panel 3 top caption: "Serena's kick no longer burned through." Panel 4 top caption: "Darius named the problem before fear could name us beaten: density."
- Visual beats: Distant Hell portal brightens; armor chest core pulls red-black smoke into its conduits; Serena's golden kick skids off newly dark armor plate; Darius kneels beside Marcus and points to compressed red energy layers around the armor.

## Page 7: The Blast

- Story role: Pay off the focused Faith Force blast defeating the armor.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels with a decisive blast.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The cultist charged, certain the armor had outgrown us." Panel 2 top caption: "Serena clipped the knee." Panel 3 top caption: "Darius opened the guard." Panel 4 top caption: "Marcus released the first focused Faith Force blast." Panel 5 top caption: "The beam did what the aura could not. It pierced the dense demonic core."
- Visual beats: Armored cultist charges through smoke; Serena lands a low golden kick at the knee; Darius redirects the clawed arm outward; Marcus thrusts one hand forward and fires a concentrated golden beam; beam punches through the furnace chest core with bright non-gory explosion of sparks and red smoke.
