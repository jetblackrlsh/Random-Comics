# Treatment

## Title

Armor Fed By Hell

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

When a surviving cultist uses Hell-powered armor to overpower the world's armies, Marcus learns to focus Faith Force into a concentrated blast.

## Central Conflict

Faith Force must stop an armored cultist whose demonic power armor is fueled by the slowly opening Hell portal. The heroes begin with confidence because their golden aura burned the imps in issue #1, but the armor grows stronger every minute and becomes too dense for ordinary aura contact to penetrate.

## Theme

Power without focus spreads thin. Faith becomes decisive when the team learns to concentrate trust, discipline, and courage into one exact point.

## Ending

Marcus focuses his golden Faith Force aura into one hand and fires a concentrated blast through the armor's furnace-like chest core. The cultist falls, the armies survive, and the team wins the day, but the still-opening Hell portal proves that every victory is only buying time before the Demon King arrives.

## Emotional Arc

The team begins stronger and more unified after issue #1, but the power armor exposes the first limit of their miracle. Serena and Darius keep the armored cultist off balance long enough for Marcus to understand the problem. Marcus stops trying to hit harder and learns to focus deeper, turning Faith Force from a protective aura into a directed weapon.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, war report and battlefield arrival, power armor routing armies, Faith Force's initial advantage, armor strengthening beyond aura contact, Marcus studying the density problem, the focused Faith Force blast, and the aftermath that confirms the Hell portal is still opening.

## Caption Approach

Use clear rectangular caption boxes generated inside the page art. Captions should explain that the armor is not merely stronger, but more concentrated with demonic magic than the team's diffuse aura, making Marcus's focused blast feel like a tactical evolution rather than a random power-up.
