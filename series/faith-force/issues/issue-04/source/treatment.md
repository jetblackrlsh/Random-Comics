# Treatment

## Title

Guardian Angels Of Earth

## Issue Number

4

## Logline

The Demon King emerges immortal, and Marcus must combine Faith Force, Divine Fire, and Gaia Green to destroy him before Earth splits apart.

## Central Conflict

Faith Force finally faces the Demon King after three issues of delaying the portal. Their martial arts, golden aura, and focused blasts are no longer enough because the Demon King can defend, regenerate, and overwhelm them. The team must accept help from Seraphiel and draw from every power they have earned: Faith Force, Divine Fire, and Gaia Green.

## Theme

The greatest power is not purity, but unity: mortal faith, divine fire, and planetary life-force working together to protect one home.

## Ending

Marcus absorbs Gaia Green from the cracked Earth while carrying Divine Fire and his friends' Faith Force. His Triple Force ultimate aura destroys the Demon King. Seraphiel rewards Marcus, Serena, and Darius with wings and knights them as Earth's guardian angels.

## Emotional Arc

The team begins ready and brave, but the Demon King's immortality breaks their confidence. Seraphiel's return turns an old enemy into an ally, and Serena and Darius give Marcus their Faith Force because they trust him to carry them. Marcus's final transformation is not solitary power; it is the visible sum of everyone and everything fighting for Earth.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, the Hell portal opens, Faith Force's combined blast fails against regeneration, the team is defeated, Seraphiel returns with the Divine Fire Sword, Marcus fights in Faith Force Divine Fire, Earth cracks and Gaia Green completes the ultimate transformation, and the Demon King is destroyed before the heroes are knighted as guardian angels.

## Caption Approach

Use clear rectangular caption boxes generated inside the page art. Captions should keep the escalating power logic readable: Faith Force alone fails, Divine Fire lets Marcus contend, Gaia Green completes the transformation, and the final blast works because all three powers are unified.
