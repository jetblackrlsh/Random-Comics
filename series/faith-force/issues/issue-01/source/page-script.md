# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce the Faith Force team, the Hellgate Foundry, the golden aura, and the delayed portal threat.
- Layout: Single dramatic photo-comic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Faith Force". Smaller issue title text: "The Portal That Would Not Close".
- Visual beats: Marcus Hale stands center-front with taped fists and a faint golden aura; Serena Park crouches on a broken catwalk to the left with gold light tracing one leg; Darius Cross braces in a defensive palm-strike stance to the right. Behind them, warrior monks twist into imps around a red-black vertical portal in an abandoned steel foundry. The Demon King's horned silhouette is barely visible beyond the portal fire.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Foundry Before Dawn

- Story role: Establish the heroes' mission, team identities, and the portal stakes.
- Layout: Four large panels in clear reading order, with roomy caption boxes.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Before sunrise, the Hellgate Foundry started breathing red light through its broken windows." Panel 2 top caption: "Marcus Hale led us in because no army could arrive quietly enough to stop a ritual already this close." Panel 3 top caption: "Serena moved like a thrown blade. Darius counted guards, exits, and mistakes. I trusted both with my life." Panel 4 top caption: "Below the old furnaces, cultists were pulling a door to Hell open one prayer at a time."
- Visual beats: Exterior of rusted foundry in rainy predawn city; Marcus signals Serena and Darius forward; Serena climbs a wet wall while Darius studies guard patterns; final panel reveals red occult light rising from floor grates.

## Page 3: Monks At The Furnace Gate

- Story role: Show that the warrior monks are strong, but the heroes are the world's greatest martial artists.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels with compact captions.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The cult did not leave the gate unguarded." Panel 2 top caption: "Their warrior monks met us with discipline, iron beads, and bodies trained to break stone." Panel 3 top caption: "Against ordinary fighters, that would have been enough." Panel 4 top caption: "Against Serena, speed became a wall they could not read." Panel 5 top caption: "Against Marcus and Darius, every strike became proof that human mastery still mattered."
- Visual beats: Monks block the furnace hall; first clash of staffs, elbows, and kicks; Serena flips between two monks; Darius turns a staff strike into a joint lock; Marcus drives a monk backward through sparks without lethal gore.

## Page 4: Familiar Fire

- Story role: Escalate from martial arts fight to a magical enemy change as the defeated monks call on demon familiars.
- Layout: Four large panels with magical transformation shown through smoke and light.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "When the monks fell, they did not yield." Panel 2 top caption: "They whispered names hidden inside their own shadows, and the shadows answered." Panel 3 top caption: "Demon familiars rose through the glowing brands on their arms." Panel 4 top caption: "The monks stood again as imps, smaller than men but burning with magic no human training had prepared us to touch."
- Visual beats: Defeated monks kneel and clutch ember brands; black-red smoke pulls from prayer beads and brands; smoky familiar shapes circle around them like dark fire; final panel shows imp forms stepping out of smoke with horn nubs, red eyes, clawed hands, and robe remnants.

## Page 5: Technique Breaks

- Story role: Put the heroes at a low point as dark magic overpowers ordinary skill.
- Layout: Four large panels, heavy impact and fear.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Serena hit first, and her perfect kick passed through smoke that burned back." Panel 2 top caption: "Darius blocked claws that were not fully physical, and the spell broke through his guard." Panel 3 top caption: "I threw my strongest strike and felt the imp twist around the impact." Panel 4 top caption: "For the first time that night, being the best humans alive was not enough."
- Visual beats: Serena's kick meets smoky imp magic and throws sparks; Darius shields against claw-shaped shadow; Marcus punches an imp that bends around the blow; final panel: the three heroes stagger together as imps ring them with red-black fire.

## Page 6: Faith In The Team

- Story role: Marcus rallies the team and manifests Faith Force.
- Layout: Four large panels with a decisive emotional turn.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Fear asked us to scatter. I looked at Serena and Darius and found something stronger." Panel 2 top caption: "I had faith in their courage, their skill, and the promise that none of us would let the others fall." Panel 3 top caption: "That faith became heat, then light, then a golden force that answered every hand reaching for mine." Panel 4 top caption: "The imps recoiled because the power was not mine alone. It belonged to the team."
- Visual beats: Marcus kneels battered but focused; close view of Serena and Darius choosing to stand with him; golden aura ignites around Marcus's hands and spreads through clasped arms; final panel: all three glow gold as imps shrink away from the light.

## Page 7: Golden Contact

- Story role: Pay off Faith Force as the team defeats the imps and reaches the ritual chamber.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels with bright golden motion.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Faith Force did not make our training vanish. It made every lesson burn brighter." Panel 2 top caption: "Serena's speed became a golden arc." Panel 3 top caption: "Darius turned defense into light." Panel 4 top caption: "I drove the center open, and the imps burned wherever our strikes touched." Panel 5 top caption: "The furnace gate broke, and the ritual chamber finally saw us coming."
- Visual beats: Serena lands a glowing spinning kick; Darius redirects two imps with gold-lit forearms; Marcus shoulder-checks through smoky magic; imps burn into ash-like sparks without gore; final panel: the team smashes through iron doors into the red-lit ritual chamber.

## Page 8: Delayed, Not Closed

- Story role: Resolve issue #1 by interrupting the ritual while preserving the larger series threat.
- Layout: Four panels, final panel as a large ominous splash.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The cultists scattered when Faith Force hit the circle, but the portal had already learned the shape of our world." Panel 2 top caption: "We broke the symbols, shattered the anchors, and forced the red wound to shrink." Panel 3 top caption: "It did not close. Beyond the fire, something vast pressed one claw against the other side." Panel 4 top caption: "We had delayed the Demon King. Now we had to become strong enough to survive the day the delay ended."
- Visual beats: Cultists flee through smoke as the golden team bursts in; Serena and Darius break ritual anchors while Marcus drives golden aura into floor sigils; the portal contracts but shows a horned silhouette and claw beyond it; final splash: the three heroes stand exhausted before the unstable red-black tear, gold aura fading, Hellgate Foundry ruined around them.
