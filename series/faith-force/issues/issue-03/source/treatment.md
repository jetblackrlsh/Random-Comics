# Treatment

## Title

The God Tree Ultimatum

## Issue Number

3

## Logline

A rogue angel plants a world-killing God Tree to stop the Demon King, forcing Marcus to wield Earth's stolen life-force as Gaia Green energy.

## Central Conflict

Seraphiel believes sacrificing Earth is the only way to stop the Demon King from escaping the nearly open Hell portal. Faith Force must fight a being whose holy power is far beyond the mortal golden aura they have mastered so far, while the God Tree drains the planet faster every minute.

## Theme

Saving the universe by destroying a home is not salvation. True protection means defending life instead of choosing which innocent world should die.

## Ending

Marcus eats the Gaia Fruit and channels Earth's stolen life-force into a massive Gaia Green shockwave. The God Tree shatters, the stolen energy returns to the planet, and Seraphiel is launched away from Earth, but the Hell portal is now almost open.

## Emotional Arc

The team begins issue #3 as proven defenders who have defeated demonic sorcery and demonic technology. Seraphiel makes them feel small again by showing that literal divine might can overpower mortal faith. Marcus's desperate choice to eat the Gaia Fruit is not a power grab; it is an act of solidarity with the entire planet. By the end, Faith Force understands that the coming final battle will require more than golden aura.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Seraphiel arrives, the God Tree drains Earth, Faith Force rejects the sacrifice, Divine Fire overwhelms golden aura, Marcus discovers and eats the Gaia Fruit, Gaia Green destroys the tree and repels Seraphiel, and the team sees that the Hell portal is almost open.

## Caption Approach

Use clear rectangular caption boxes generated inside the page art. Captions should make Seraphiel's logic understandable without making it acceptable, and should distinguish golden Faith Force, Divine Fire, and Gaia Green as separate energies.
