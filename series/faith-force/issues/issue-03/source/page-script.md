# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Seraphiel, the God Tree, Gaia Green, and the near-open Hell portal.
- Layout: Single dramatic photo-comic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Faith Force". Smaller issue title text: "The God Tree Ultimatum".
- Visual beats: Seraphiel hovers above a colossal pale God Tree with six wings and a white-gold flaming sword. Marcus stands below with golden Faith Force turning emerald Gaia Green around his hands. Serena and Darius brace beside him amid roots splitting the earth. A red-black Hell portal glows almost open on the far horizon.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Heaven Notices Hell

- Story role: Establish that the Hell portal has attracted Seraphiel's attention.
- Layout: Four large panels with roomy caption boxes.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "After the armor fell, the Hell portal widened again." Panel 2 top caption: "Its energy climbed high enough for Heaven to notice." Panel 3 top caption: "Seraphiel descended before dawn, not as a blessing, but as a verdict." Panel 4 top caption: "The angel looked at Earth and saw a door that had to be destroyed before the Demon King could use it."
- Visual beats: Distant portal pulse over city skyline; light breaking through storm clouds; Seraphiel descends with six wings and Divine Fire Sword; Seraphiel looks toward Earth and the portal with sorrowful severity.

## Page 3: Roots Of Judgment

- Story role: Show the God Tree being planted and draining the planet.
- Layout: Four large panels with clear planetary stakes.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Seraphiel planted one seed in the wounded ground." Panel 2 top caption: "The God Tree grew like a commandment carved into the planet." Panel 3 top caption: "Its roots did not drink water. They drank life." Panel 4 top caption: "Grass grayed, stone cracked, and green light poured out of Earth toward a single growing fruit."
- Visual beats: Seraphiel presses a bright seed into cracked earth; colossal pale tree erupts upward; roots spread through fields, roads, and stone; green life-force streams flow toward one glowing Gaia Fruit.

## Page 4: No World For The Furnace

- Story role: Faith Force confronts Seraphiel and rejects the sacrifice.
- Layout: Four large panels, tense standoff.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "We found Seraphiel where the roots met the sky." Panel 2 top caption: "The angel did not deny the cost. Earth would die, and the portal would die with it." Panel 3 top caption: "To Seraphiel, one world was a price the universe could survive." Panel 4 top caption: "To us, one world was everyone we had sworn to protect."
- Visual beats: Faith Force arrives under immense God Tree roots; Seraphiel hovers calmly with sword lowered; drained landscape and portal visible in distance; Marcus, Serena, and Darius ignite golden aura and take fighting stances.

## Page 5: Divine Fire

- Story role: Show that Seraphiel's literal divine might overpowers golden Faith Force.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels with compact captions.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Golden Faith Force mirrored holy faith, but a mirror is not the sun." Panel 2 top caption: "Serena struck from above." Panel 3 top caption: "Darius caught the flame's edge and nearly lost his guard." Panel 4 top caption: "Marcus focused everything into one blast." Panel 5 top caption: "Seraphiel's Divine Fire cut through it like dawn through fog."
- Visual beats: Seraphiel raises white-gold flaming sword; Serena attacks from a root with gold kick trails; Darius blocks a flame wave with golden arcs; Marcus fires a focused golden blast; Divine Fire sword splits the blast without gore.

## Page 6: The Fruit Of A Dying World

- Story role: Marcus discovers and eats the Gaia Fruit as a desperate answer.
- Layout: Four large panels with emotional focus.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Darius saw the pattern first: the roots were feeding one fruit with the life of Earth." Panel 2 top caption: "If that power stayed in the tree, the planet would become an empty shell." Panel 3 top caption: "I reached for the fruit because there was no safe choice left." Panel 4 top caption: "The taste was soil, rain, forests, oceans, and every heartbeat the tree had stolen."
- Visual beats: Darius points to green energy streams converging; close-up of Gaia Fruit glowing emerald-gold; Marcus climbs or leaps through roots to seize the fruit; Marcus eats it as green light floods his body and mixes with gold aura.

## Page 7: Gaia Green

- Story role: Pay off Gaia Green as Marcus destroys the God Tree and repels Seraphiel.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels with a huge planetary shockwave.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Faith Force became Gaia Green." Panel 2 top caption: "The ground remembered its own strength." Panel 3 top caption: "The shockwave climbed the roots and broke the God Tree from within." Panel 4 top caption: "Seraphiel raised Divine Fire, but Earth answered louder." Panel 5 top caption: "The angel fell upward, launched beyond the clouds by the life they tried to steal."
- Visual beats: Marcus glows emerald-green and gold; green cracks race through ground; God Tree roots and trunk shatter into light; Seraphiel shields with flaming sword against green shockwave; Seraphiel is hurled skyward in a non-gory burst of green light.

## Page 8: Almost Open

- Story role: Resolve issue #3 while setting up the final Demon King arrival.
- Layout: Four panels, final panel as a large ominous portal splash.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The stolen life returned in waves." Panel 2 top caption: "Grass lifted. Trees breathed. The world survived the angel's mercy." Panel 3 top caption: "But the God Tree had taken time we did not have." Panel 4 top caption: "The Hell portal was almost open, and the Demon King was almost here."
- Visual beats: Green life-force flows back into soil and cities; the team stands exhausted as plants recover around their feet; empty sky where Seraphiel vanished and broken God Tree fragments dissolve; final splash shows Faith Force facing the distant nearly open Hell portal with the Demon King's silhouette clearer than before.
