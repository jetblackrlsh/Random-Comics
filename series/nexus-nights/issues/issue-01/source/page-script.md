# Page Script

## Page 1: Cover

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-01-cover.png`
- Story role: Introduce Sasha, Nexus City, Astral Command, and the dangerous promise of the random power ticket.
- Layout: Full-page cover composition with Sasha centered in a teleporter arrival haze, holding the glowing ticket. Behind her, Nexus City neon and an Astral Command tower silhouette overlap with a blue activation pod glow.
- Readable text to generate inside the art: Title text: "NEXUS NIGHTS". Subtitle text: "Tourist Processing". Small issue label: "Issue 1".
- Visual beats: Sasha looks thrilled but uneasy. Blue space-time ripples curl around her hands before she understands them.

## Page 2: Arrival

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-02.png`
- Story role: Establish Sasha, her goal, and Nexus City as a commercialized superhero tourism hub.
- Layout: Four roomy panels.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Sasha crossed realities for one night of borrowed power and paid adventure."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Nexus City sold miracles like movie tickets, and her random draw had only one name on it: Astral Command."
  - Panel 3 caption: "The station smelled like ozone, rain, and a thousand overlapping worlds."
  - Panel 4 caption: "She raised the ticket and asked the crowd, 'Is anyone here looking for Sasha?'"
- Visual beats: Teleporter hum, holographic ticket, power advertisements, Sasha scanning the escort queue.

## Page 3: Taylor

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-03.png`
- Story role: Introduce Taylor and puncture Sasha's tourist fantasy with real danger.
- Layout: Four panels with handheld station-corridor energy.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Taylor from Astral Command stepped forward like escorting tourists was a punishment."
  - Panel 2 caption: "He warned her that random draws did not come with parades, mercy, or refunds."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Three tourists had died that month trying to play hero. Dead people did not leave testimonials."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Sasha smiled anyway and told herself thrill rides were not meant to be safe."
- Visual beats: Taylor checks tablet, gives dismissive once-over, security scanner flashes green, Sasha follows him while trying to hide sudden nerves.

## Page 4: Intake

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-04.png`
- Story role: Move Sasha into Astral Command and show the organization as grand outside, bureaucratic inside.
- Layout: Five panels, exterior establishing shot followed by tight intake details.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Astral Command Tower rose over Nexus City with a galaxy and sword burning above its doors."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Inside, the lobby was plain enough to make cosmic heroism feel like a license renewal."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Checkpoint by checkpoint, Sasha surrendered her phone, wallet, shoes, and last easy exit."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Officer Mara Venn handed her a black processing jumpsuit and pointed toward the chamber."
  - Panel 5 caption: "The adventure was still happening. It just had more forms than advertised."
- Visual beats: tower exterior, bare lobby security, trays with belongings, Mara's severe expression, Sasha holding jumpsuit.

## Page 5: The Pod

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-05.png`
- Story role: Show Sasha choosing the activation pod and treating danger like spectacle one last time.
- Layout: Four panels in the activation chamber.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "The activation chamber waited in a blue circle of glass pods and nervous recruits."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Sasha joked that the plain jumpsuit already felt like a costume. Mara did not laugh."
  - Panel 3 caption: "A pod opened with a soft hiss, bright and clean as a promise nobody had read closely."
  - Panel 4 caption: "When the door sealed, the machine asked her to rate the pain."
- Visual beats: chamber ring, recruits in pods, Mara gesturing impatiently, Sasha inside pod as liquid metal starts moving.

## Page 6: Integration

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-06.png`
- Story role: Escalate the power activation from uncomfortable to terrifying.
- Layout: Five panels, claustrophobic pod views and one red emergency panel.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "At first the liquid metal only tingled, and Sasha called the pain a two."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Then every nerve learned a sharper number."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Across the chamber, another recruit slammed the glass and begged for the process to stop."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Mara called it normal integration stress. The red lights disagreed."
  - Panel 5 caption: "Sasha had come for excitement. Now she wondered if excitement could kill her."
- Visual beats: pain-scale hologram, Sasha gripping pod rail, other recruit emergency, medical personnel moving, red alarm reflections.

## Page 7: Hold On

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-07.png`
- Story role: Bring Sasha to the low point and survival choice.
- Layout: Four large panels, surreal and physical.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "At seventy-five percent, nausea folded her in half and the pod answered with cold needles."
  - Panel 2 caption: "At ninety percent, blue light poured through her veins like molten stars."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Somewhere beyond the speakers, a voice said, 'Hold on. Don't let go.'"
  - Panel 4 caption: "Sasha held on until the room vanished."
- Visual beats: containment tray, injector stings, blue energy veins, blurred Mara and pod lights, final near-black panel.

## Page 8: Manifestation

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-08.png`
- Story role: Resolve issue 1 with Sasha alive, changed, and ready for the next phase of the arc.
- Layout: Four panels in the recovery room.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Sasha woke on a white recovery cot with the taste of metal still in her mouth."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Her hands glowed blue, bending the air above her palms in soft impossible ripples."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Dr. Imani Vale called it Astral Manipulation: space-time distortion, portal potential, quantum field projection."
  - Panel 4 caption: "A custom suit waited nearby. The power had worked. The mission had not even started."
- Visual beats: recovery cot, glowing hands, holographic anatomy display, Dr. Vale with tablet, Astral Command suit on mannequin.
