## Sasha

Woman in her mid-20s with warm brown skin, expressive dark eyes, shoulder-length dark curly hair, athletic average build, curious open posture, and a nervous grin when pressure rises. She starts in practical travel clothes with a short jacket and small crossbody bag, changes into a plain black processing jumpsuit, then wakes near a sleek black and silver Astral Command suit. Her emotional baseline is excited, talkative, and movie-literate, but the activation process leaves her frightened and relieved.

## Taylor

Bored teenage Astral Command liaison, around 17-19, pale olive skin, narrow face, short messy black hair with a silver streak, tired gray eyes, slim build, skeptical slouch, black and silver liaison uniform, badge, and tablet. He is dismissive but not heartless.

## Officer Mara Venn

Stern processing officer in her late 30s with light brown skin, severe cheekbones, close-cropped dark hair, rigid posture, black and silver Astral Command uniform, white processing gloves, and clipped security badge. She is clinical, impatient, and annoyed by Sasha treating power activation like entertainment.

## Dr. Imani Vale

Astral Command recovery doctor in her early 30s with deep brown skin, round glasses, dark braided hair tied back, tired but kind eyes, white Astral Command lab coat over black scrubs, and a blue diagnostic tablet. She appears only on the final page of this issue, setting up the briefing in issue #2.

## Visual Continuity Rules

- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, subtitles, or floating dialogue.
- Keep Sasha's face, hair, skin tone, and body type consistent through all outfit changes.
- Astral Command uniforms use black, silver, white, and ethereal blue highlights.
- Sasha's power glow is ethereal blue and should distort nearby space rather than look like ordinary electricity.
- The issue should look like candid photos arranged as comic pages, not painted comic art.
