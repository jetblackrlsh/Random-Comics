# Treatment

## Title

Tourist Processing

## Issue Number

1

## Arc Placement

Issue 1 of 3 in the first Nexus Nights arc following Sasha and Astral Command.

## Logline

Sasha arrives in Nexus City expecting a thrilling one-night superhero adventure, but Astral Command's power activation process teaches her that paid hero tourism can be lethal before the mission even begins.

## Central Conflict

Sasha wants power, spectacle, and adventure, while Astral Command treats her like a disposable recruit entering a dangerous machine. Her optimism collides with the physical terror of power integration.

## Theme

Adventure is not less meaningful when it becomes frightening; courage begins when the thrill ride stops feeling safe.

## Ending

Sasha wakes in Astral Command recovery with blue astral energy glowing through her hands. Dr. Imani Vale confirms that Sasha survived integration and received Astral Manipulation. A black and silver suit waits nearby, but the final caption makes clear that Sasha's real mission has not started yet.

## Emotional Arc

Sasha begins excited and joking, expecting a curated adventure. Taylor's warnings make her nervous, but she keeps performing confidence. The activation pod strips away that performance as pain, nausea, and another recruit's emergency reveal the process can kill her. She refuses to let go, blacks out, and wakes changed: shaken, alive, and still holding onto hope.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, arrival in Nexus City, Taylor's escort and warning, Astral Command intake, activation chamber choice, painful integration, near-blackout, and recovery with Astral Manipulation.
