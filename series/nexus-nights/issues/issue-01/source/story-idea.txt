Sasha is a traveler from another world who buys a random one-night superhero experience in Nexus City. Her ticket assigns her to Astral Command, an organization that gives recruits one temporary power, one custom suit, and one real mission.

Issue #1 of the first three-issue arc follows Sasha from arrival through power activation. She arrives at the teleporter station, meets the bored liaison Taylor, learns that tourists sometimes die on first missions, reaches Astral Command Tower, surrenders her civilian belongings, changes into a processing jumpsuit, enters an activation pod, suffers through dangerous integration, and wakes in recovery with Astral Manipulation and a suit waiting for her.

The full first arc should later continue through a rushed power briefing and a mission against an interdimensional incursion. Future arcs can follow other characters, organizations, powers, missions, and consequences in Nexus City.
