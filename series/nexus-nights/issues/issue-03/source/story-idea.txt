Nexus Nights: Issue #3, "Keep It Steady"

Issue #3 closes the first Nexus Nights arc by following Sasha through the Central Business District incursion mission. She arrives with containment soldiers in the Astral Command transport, using the tactical visor and trying not to look directly at the entity. The scale is far beyond anything her movie references prepared her for, and her first attempt at Astral Manipulation is small, frightened, and almost ridiculous.

The breakthrough comes when Sasha notices the hand motions feel like hula dancing from her grandmother's backyard. She turns "small hand wiggles" into a steady rhythm, then overcorrects when Dr. Vale demands a larger field and nearly destabilizes the breach. Instead of panicking or performing heroism, Sasha learns to feel the flow, slow down when it hurts, speed up when it fades, and eventually move the rhythm from her arms into her mind.

By the end, Sasha stabilizes the incursion long enough for Astral Command to collapse it safely. Reality knits itself back together. She returns exhausted, respected by the soldiers, and emotionally changed: she came for a thrill ride, but the real reward was becoming a beacon of hope for people who needed one.
