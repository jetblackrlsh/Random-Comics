# Treatment

## Title

Keep It Steady

## Issue Number

3

## Arc Placement

Issue 3 of 3 in the first Nexus Nights arc following Sasha and Astral Command.

## Logline

Sasha faces the reality-eating incursion with almost no training and discovers that her borrowed power only works when she stops performing heroism and learns to hold steady.

## Central Conflict

Sasha wants to survive the final-boss scale of the mission and prove the night meant something, while the incursion punishes every overcorrection. Her danger is not cowardice; it is trying to force a cosmic problem into the shape of a movie moment before she understands control.

## Theme

Heroism is not the biggest gesture in the crisis. Sometimes it is the rhythm you keep when everyone else needs something stable to trust.

## Ending

Sasha stabilizes the incursion, lets Astral Command collapse it safely, and rides back through a city that is knitting itself together. A containment soldier offers her water and calls her rookie with real respect. Sasha is exhausted, but she understands that the best part of the night was not the costume, the power, or the danger. It was becoming hope for somebody else.

## Emotional Arc

Sasha begins Issue 3 frightened, undertrained, and still translating terror into jokes. Her first field works only because she grabs onto a familiar hula rhythm. When she pushes too hard, she nearly makes the breach worse and has to abandon the fantasy of dramatic heroics. She learns to listen, adjust, and focus. By the final pages, the joke has become discipline, and her optimism has matured into steadiness other people can rely on.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, arrival over the incursion zone, first hula-rhythm field, dangerous overcorrection, steady tempo, mental rhythm breakthrough, safe collapse of the incursion, and return as a survivor who has become a real source of hope.
