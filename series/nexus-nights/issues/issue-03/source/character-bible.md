## Style Rules

- The issue should look like candid photos arranged as comic pages, not painted comic art.
- Use clean rectangular caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
- All readable text must be generated directly inside the image art by image_gen.
- Keep Sasha's suit consistent: black and silver armor, blue seams, practical silhouette, subtle galaxy-sword chest emblem.
- The hula-rhythm breakthrough should read as grounded body language, not a magical dance performance. It is a calming rhythm Sasha uses to control quantum fields.
