# Page Script

## Page 1: Cover

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-01-cover.png`
- Story role: Present the arc finale as Sasha holding a cosmic disaster together through focus rather than force.
- Layout: Full-page cover composition.
- Readable text to generate inside the art: Title text: "NEXUS NIGHTS". Subtitle text: "Keep It Steady". Small issue label: "Issue 3".
- Visual beats: Sasha in her black and silver Astral Command suit stands on the open side ramp of the transport above the warped Central Business District. Her hands trace a calm hula-like rhythm, forming a blue containment field around a blue-black vortex. Soldiers brace behind her. The city bends, but Sasha looks focused instead of performative.

## Page 2: Target Zone

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-02.png`
- Story role: Establish the incursion as overwhelming and Sasha as undertrained but present.
- Layout: Four cinematic panels inside and outside the transport.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "The transport hovered over the Central Business District while reality folded beneath it in pieces."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Through the visor, missing towers left glowing echoes where offices and streets should have been."
  - Panel 3 caption: "The soldiers fired at shapes Sasha was not supposed to look at directly."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Her plan was embarrassingly small: close her eyes, breathe, and remember Vale's hand motion."
- Visual beats: transport over warped city, visor overlay, soldiers braced and firing, Sasha closing eyes with glowing hands.

## Page 3: Hula Logic

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-03.png`
- Story role: Turn the "small hand wiggles" joke into Sasha's first real control method.
- Layout: Four panels on the transport ramp and above the target zone.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "The motion felt less like science than the hula lessons Sasha had learned in her grandmother's backyard."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Slow hands. Steady breath. One tiny blue ripple became a field."
  - Panel 3 caption: "The incursion noticed."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Dr. Vale's voice cracked through the communicator: 'Larger field, Sasha. Now.'"
- Visual beats: Sasha's hula-like hand rhythm, small blue distortion expanding, soldiers watching uneasily, blue-black vortex reacting, communicator glow.

## Page 4: Too Much

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-04.png`
- Story role: Show Sasha overcorrecting and nearly making the crisis worse.
- Layout: Five action panels with unstable composition.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "It was working, so Sasha did the most dangerous tourist thing possible."
  - Panel 2 caption: "She cranked up the tempo."
  - Panel 3 caption: "The field ballooned, the transport lurched, and half-built pieces of reality floated loose."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Vale shouted that Sasha was tearing open a permanent rift."
  - Panel 5 caption: "For one sick second, Sasha understood that saving the world could still be the thing that broke it."
- Visual beats: Sasha moving too fast, blue field surging, gravity-defying debris, soldiers grabbing rails, Dr. Vale hologram alarm, rift widening.

## Page 5: The Flow

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-05.png`
- Story role: Mark Sasha's growth from force to control.
- Layout: Four large panels with calmer rhythm after chaos.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "She stopped chasing the movie moment and listened to the pain in her arms."
  - Panel 2 caption: "When the field burned, she slowed down. When it faded, she sped up."
  - Panel 3 caption: "The rhythm settled into something she could trust."
  - Panel 4 caption: "For the first time that night, Astral Command sounded hopeful."
- Visual beats: Sasha easing the motion, blue field smoothing, debris stabilizing, soldiers taking perimeter positions, communicator showing Dr. Vale relieved but tense.

## Page 6: Beacon

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-06.png`
- Story role: Show the deeper breakthrough: Sasha moves from physical performance to focused visualization and becomes hope for others.
- Layout: Four panels, intimate and cosmic.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Her arms trembled, but the power had never really belonged to her hands."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Sasha carried the hula rhythm into her mind and let her body rest."
  - Panel 3 caption: "The containment field held without the frantic performance."
  - Panel 4 caption: "She had come for a thrill ride. Instead, she became the bright point everyone else could steer by."
- Visual beats: close on exhausted Sasha, eyes closed; ghosted visual rhythm around her; blue containment field steady around vortex; soldiers and cleanup team moving in under the stable light.

## Page 7: Collapse

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-07.png`
- Story role: Resolve the action by safely collapsing the incursion.
- Layout: Four panels showing controlled shutdown.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "With the perimeter secure, Astral Command began collapsing the breach from the outside in."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Sasha lowered the tempo slowly, like the last notes of a song."
  - Panel 3 caption: "The blue-black hunger thinned into smoke, then into harmless light."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Reality remembered its shape."
- Visual beats: containment soldiers deploying equipment, Sasha seated or braced in transport with glowing hands, vortex shrinking, buildings and streets re-forming.

## Page 8: Rookie

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-08.png`
- Story role: Close the first arc with consequence, respect, and Sasha's changed understanding of heroism.
- Layout: Four panels, quiet aftermath.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "The transport carried Sasha away from a city still knitting itself back together."
  - Panel 2 caption: "A soldier offered her water and said, with real respect, 'Nice work back there, rookie.'"
  - Panel 3 caption: "She was too tired to make the perfect movie joke."
  - Panel 4 caption: "The power would end with the ticket. The memory of being hope would not."
- Visual beats: transport leaving repaired skyline, exhausted Sasha slumped inside, soldier offering water bottle, Sasha smiling weakly, final view of Nexus City dawn or softened night with reality stabilized.
