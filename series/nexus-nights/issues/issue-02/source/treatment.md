# Treatment

## Title

Small Hand Wiggles

## Issue Number

2

## Arc Placement

Issue 2 of 3 in the first Nexus Nights arc following Sasha and Astral Command.

## Logline

Sasha suits up for her one-night hero mission, receives a rushed lesson in Astral Manipulation, and is deployed toward a reality-eating incursion before training can become anything more than "small hand wiggles."

## Central Conflict

Sasha wants to keep the experience fun and understandable, while Dr. Imani Vale and Astral Command need her to treat unfamiliar power as lethal work. The briefing room becomes a collision between Sasha's movie-shaped optimism and the organization's exhausted emergency discipline.

## Theme

A joke can steady a frightened person, but courage requires hearing the warning underneath the joke.

## Ending

Sasha boards an Astral Command transport with containment soldiers, a tactical visor, a quantum destabilizer pistol, and almost no training. The Central Business District is already warping ahead of her, and the final page leaves her bracing for contact with the incursion.

## Emotional Arc

Sasha begins relieved and delighted to be alive, admiring the suit and trying to turn cosmic science into familiar pop-culture language. Dr. Vale's impatience and the alarm force Sasha to understand that lives are already being lost. By the end, she is still joking, but the jokes have become a way to hold herself steady while she walks into real danger.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, recovery and suit-up, power briefing, movie-reference translation, shortened training, emergency gear and mission stakes, hangar deployment, and transport approach toward the Central Business District incursion.
