# Page Script

## Page 1: Cover

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-01-cover.png`
- Story role: Sell the issue as the moment Sasha's power fantasy becomes an emergency deployment.
- Layout: Full-page cover composition.
- Readable text to generate inside the art: Title text: "NEXUS NIGHTS". Subtitle text: "Small Hand Wiggles". Small issue label: "Issue 2".
- Visual beats: Sasha in the black and silver Astral Command suit stands in a hangar doorway with blue energy rippling around her hands. Dr. Imani Vale is behind her with a tablet. A transport vessel waits beyond them. In the skyline outside, the Central Business District bends around a distant blue-black incursion.

## Page 2: Suit Up

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-02.png`
- Story role: Resume immediately after Issue 1 and show Sasha's relief at surviving.
- Layout: Four roomy panels in the recovery room.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Sasha woke with metal on her tongue, blue light in her hands, and the private miracle of still being alive."
  - Panel 2 caption: "The suit waited beside her cot, black and silver and less ridiculous than she had secretly hoped."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Dr. Imani Vale warned her that the armor stabilized the power core, not the person wearing it."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Sasha decided that sounded like future-Sasha's problem."
- Visual beats: recovery cot, Sasha looking at glowing hands, suit on mannequin, Dr. Vale helping her stand, suit seals activating around Sasha.

## Page 3: Not A God

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-03.png`
- Story role: Explain Astral Manipulation in clear story terms and preserve Sasha's comic coping style.
- Layout: Four panels moving from recovery corridor to briefing room.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Dr. Vale called the power quantum field projection. Sasha asked if that meant she was basically a god."
  - Panel 2 caption: "The answer was no. Very no."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Space and time, Vale explained, could be bent like fabric if Sasha kept the distortion small."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Sasha heard 'portals' and immediately felt better."
- Visual beats: Dr. Vale with tablet and holographic body scan, Sasha flexing suited hands, a small blue ripple forming, stark briefing room with swirling diagrams.

## Page 4: Movie Logic

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-04.png`
- Story role: Brief the incursion and show Dr. Vale's patience cracking.
- Layout: Four large briefing-room panels with holographic city maps.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "The mission was not a monster to punch. It was an incursion eating chunks of the Central Business District."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Sasha translated that into Ghostbusters, Doctor Strange, and every movie where someone sensible should have stayed home."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Vale let one tired breath escape before explaining that real people were already dying."
  - Panel 4 caption: "The job was simple in the way falling off a tower was simple: stabilize the field and push the breach back."
- Visual beats: holographic vortex, city blocks flickering out, Dr. Vale pinching bridge of nose, Sasha pointing at map with worried enthusiasm.

## Page 5: Ten Minutes

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-05.png`
- Story role: Start training and cut it short with the alarm.
- Layout: Five panels, controlled training area shifting into crisis.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Training began with one instruction: make a small controlled field."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Vale moved her hand once, and the air folded like heat above pavement."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Sasha copied the motion and made a nervous blue wobble."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Then every alarm in the briefing room decided ten minutes was generous."
  - Panel 5 caption: "Training was over. The mission had arrived early."
- Visual beats: Dr. Vale demonstration ripple, Sasha imitating with awkward hand movement, blue field wobble, red alarms, soldiers entering.

## Page 6: Field Kit

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-06.png`
- Story role: Arm Sasha and make the stakes personal.
- Layout: Four panels with tight documentary urgency.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Vale clipped a field communicator to Sasha's wrist and brought up the spreading target zone."
  - Panel 2 caption: "She pressed a quantum destabilizer pistol into Sasha's hand in case the power failed."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Three containment teams were already silent. The city was not waiting for Sasha to feel ready."
  - Panel 4 caption: "Vale's final order was not tactical. It was human: 'Try not to die.'"
- Visual beats: smartwatch-like communicator, heavy sleek pistol, holographic map of Central Business District, Dr. Vale gripping Sasha's arm, Sasha's fear visible under forced confidence.

## Page 7: Hangar

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-07.png`
- Story role: Move from briefing to deployment and introduce the tactical visor and transport.
- Layout: Four panels in a reinforced hangar.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "Containment soldiers escorted Sasha through corridors built for disasters no brochure mentioned."
  - Panel 2 caption: "The hangar opened around a hovering Astral Command transport, its blue lines pulsing like a heartbeat."
  - Panel 3 caption: "A soldier handed her a tactical visor and warned her not to look directly at the entity."
  - Panel 4 caption: "That warning joined 'small hand wiggles' and 'special quantum gun' on Sasha's very short plan."
- Visual beats: soldiers in black-silver armor, hangar blast doors, transport vessel, tactical visor close-up, Sasha boarding.

## Page 8: Target Zone

- File: `assets/comic-pages/page-08.png`
- Story role: End with Sasha approaching the mission and set up Issue 3.
- Layout: Four cinematic panels inside and outside the transport.
- Caption boxes:
  - Panel 1 caption: "The transport lifted into a skyline that looked like someone had folded the city while it was still occupied."
  - Panel 2 caption: "Through the visor, the Central Business District became a storm of broken geometry and hungry blue-black light."
  - Panel 3 caption: "Sasha had wanted a first act. Nexus City had given her the final boss."
  - Panel 4 caption: "She closed her eyes, remembered the smallest motion Vale had shown her, and flew toward the breach."
- Visual beats: transport leaving hangar, warped city towers, visor overlay, soldiers braced, Sasha seated with eyes closed and glowing hands beginning a tiny ripple.
