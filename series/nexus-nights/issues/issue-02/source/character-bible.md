# Character Bible

## Sasha

- Source continuity: `series/nexus-nights/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/nexus-nights/reference-images/sasha-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: woman in her mid-20s with warm brown skin, expressive dark eyes, shoulder-length dark curly hair slightly frizzed by static, athletic average build, curious open posture, and nervous grin under pressure.
- Issue 2 state: starts weak on a recovery cot, then wears the sleek black and silver Astral Command suit with flexible armor panels, subtle galaxy-sword chest emblem, ethereal blue seams, and glowing forearm channels. Her hands can glow with soft blue Astral Manipulation ripples.
- Performance notes: Sasha jokes when frightened. She compares quantum field projection to portals, Ghostbusters, Doctor Strange, and superhero movies, but the emergency gradually sobers her.

## Dr. Imani Vale

- Source continuity: `series/nexus-nights/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/nexus-nights/reference-images/doctor-imani-vale-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: woman in her early 30s with deep brown skin, round glasses, dark braided hair tied back, calm but tired face, white Astral Command lab coat over black medical scrubs, and blue diagnostic tablet.
- Issue 2 state: begins as recovery doctor, becomes field-power instructor and mission handler. Her professional patience cracks when Sasha turns cosmic danger into movie analogies.

## Astral Command Containment Soldiers

- Source continuity: `series/nexus-nights/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/nexus-nights/reference-images/astral-command-containment-soldiers-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: compact team of adult tactical responders in matte black and silver armored Astral Command suits, sealed helmets or raised visors, blue energy trim, chest galaxy-sword emblem, compact rifles, utility harnesses, and disciplined formation posture.
- Issue 2 state: they escort Sasha from briefing to hangar and into the transport. They are professional, urgent, and uneasy about sending a tourist into a live incursion.
