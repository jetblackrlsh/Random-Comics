Nexus Nights: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"

Issue #2 of the first three-issue arc follows Sasha immediately after surviving Astral Command power activation. Dr. Imani Vale helps her into the Astral Command suit, explains Astral Manipulation in practical terms, and briefs her on an interdimensional incursion consuming the Central Business District. Sasha keeps translating cosmic physics into movie references and jokes because she is frightened but still determined to make the night mean something.

The training phase is cut short by alarms. Dr. Vale gives Sasha a field communicator, a quantum destabilizer pistol, and basic instructions: small controlled fields, do not overreach, and do not look directly at the entity. Astral Command containment soldiers escort Sasha through the hangar onto a transport vessel. As the ship lifts toward the warped skyline, Sasha realizes this is not a curated thrill ride; it is a real mission with real lives at stake.
