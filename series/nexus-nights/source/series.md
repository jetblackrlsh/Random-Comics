# Nexus Nights

## Logline

Travelers buy one-night superhero experiences in Nexus City, where every random power ticket comes with a costume, a real mission, and consequences too dangerous for the brochure.

## Premise

Nexus City is a neon crossroads of overlapping realities where power organizations sell temporary hero access to tourists, thrill seekers, stranded travelers, and desperate locals. A ticket assigns the buyer to a random organization, grants one temporary power, generates a custom suit, and sends them on one real mission before the night ends.

The first arc follows Sasha, a traveler from another world who draws Astral Command. She expects a thrilling power fantasy and discovers that the city treats heroism as a dangerous service industry. Her assigned power, Astral Manipulation, lets her distort local space-time, open controlled portals, and stabilize quantum fields, but the first mission points her at an incursion that is eating chunks of reality.

Future arcs can follow different travelers, organizations, powers, missions, and consequences across the same city.

## Tone

Bright cosmic superhero adventure with nervous comedy, dangerous bureaucracy, and sincere hope under the jokes. The story should feel like a candid photo-comic travel documentary that wandered into a high-stakes superhero disaster. Sasha jokes because she is excited and scared, but the danger should always feel real.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Nexus City mixes transit infrastructure, superhero commerce, corporate power branding, and visible reality glitches.
- Astral Command uses black, silver, white, and ethereal blue light, with a spiral galaxy around a sword as its emblem.
- Sasha's Astral Manipulation appears as soft blue space-time distortions, rippling air, floating dust, folded reflections, and portal-like arcs rather than simple lightning.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
- Treat in-world signs and holograms as sparse visual flavor unless the page prompt explicitly asks for readable text.

## Continuity Notes

- The first arc is planned as three issues following Sasha's first night with Astral Command.
- Issue #1 covers Sasha's arrival, Taylor's escort, Astral Command processing, power activation, and recovery.
- Issue #2 should cover the suit reveal, power briefing, shortened training, the alarm, and deployment toward the incursion.
- Issue #3 should cover the Central Business District mission, Sasha's hula-rhythm breakthrough, containment of the incursion, and her return as a survivor.
- Sasha is not from Nexus City and keeps comparing cosmic danger to movies from her own world.
- Astral Command allows tourists into real missions because the city's power economy is normalized, profitable, and morally compromised.
- Sasha's powers are temporary for the ticketed experience, but the emotional consequences and continuity of the night persist.
