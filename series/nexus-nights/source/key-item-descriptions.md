# Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: Random Power Ticket

- First appears: Issue #1, "Tourist Processing"
- Visual identifiers: translucent handheld holographic ticket, palm-sized, glowing cyan edge, shifting QR-like glyphs, and the readable assignment "ASTRAL COMMAND" when shown close.
- Continuity notes: The ticket assigns Sasha to Astral Command and authorizes one temporary power, one custom suit, and one real mission.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/random-power-ticket-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Astral Command Suit

- First appears: Issue #1, "Tourist Processing"
- Visual identifiers: sleek black and silver superhero suit with flexible armor panels, subtle galaxy-sword chest emblem, ethereal blue seams, glowing forearm channels, and a practical fitted silhouette rather than a flashy costume.
- Continuity notes: The suit links to Sasha's power core, stabilizes Astral Manipulation, and channels quantum field projection.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/astral-command-suit-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Activation Pod

- First appears: Issue #1, "Tourist Processing"
- Visual identifiers: transparent cylindrical glass-and-metal pod with blue internal light, liquid-metal tendrils, pain-scale hologram, medical tray, hidden injectors, and red emergency warning strips.
- Continuity notes: The pod processes the ticket, integrates Sasha's temporary power, and nearly overwhelms her before the manifestation succeeds.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/activation-pod-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Astral Manipulation

- First appears: Issue #1, "Tourist Processing"
- Visual identifiers: ethereal blue energy in Sasha's hands and suit seams, folded reflections in the air, small portal arcs, rippling space-time distortions, floating dust motes, and gravity-defying fragments.
- Continuity notes: Astral Manipulation is Sasha's temporary power. It can create localized space-time distortions, controlled portal effects, and quantum stabilization fields, but it depends on focus, rhythm, and energy reserves.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/astral-manipulation-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Field Communicator

- First appears: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"
- Visual identifiers: sleek black smartwatch-like wrist device with a thin silver frame, small blue holographic projection lens, Astral Command galaxy-sword icon, tactical readout glow, and flexible strap integrated over Sasha's suit sleeve.
- Continuity notes: Dr. Vale uses it to guide Sasha during the incursion mission.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/field-communicator-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Quantum Destabilizer Pistol

- First appears: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"
- Visual identifiers: compact heavy pistol with matte black grip, silver barrel housing, blue energy chamber, triangular warning glyphs, and practical military build rather than toy-like superhero styling.
- Continuity notes: Dr. Vale gives it to Sasha as a backup if Astral Manipulation fails near the incursion.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/quantum-destabilizer-pistol-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Tactical Visor

- First appears: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"
- Visual identifiers: slim wraparound transparent visor with black temple arms, blue quantum-overlay graphics, small Astral Command icon near one lens edge, and a reinforced nose bridge that fits over Sasha's face.
- Continuity notes: The visor helps Sasha see quantum distortions while warning her not to look directly at the entity.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/tactical-visor-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Astral Command Transport Vessel

- First appears: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"
- Visual identifiers: sleek hovering troop transport with black and silver hull, blue energy lines, compact side doors, angular nose, underside lift emitters, and Astral Command galaxy-sword insignia.
- Continuity notes: The transport carries Sasha and containment soldiers from Astral Command Tower toward the Central Business District incursion.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/astral-command-transport-vessel-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Interdimensional Incursion

- First appears: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"
- Visual identifiers: distant blue-black vortex with folded reflections, impossible geometry, missing chunks of real city structures, drifting debris, oily light distortions, and a visual warning that direct observation is dangerous.
- Continuity notes: The incursion consumes overlapping realities in the Central Business District. Sasha's mission is to stabilize and push it back.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/interdimensional-incursion-key-item.png`
