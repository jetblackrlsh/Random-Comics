# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Nexus City Teleporter Station

- First appears: Issue #1, "Tourist Processing"
- Visual identifiers: huge indoor arrival hall with circular teleporter pads, chrome rails, wet reflective floors, neon advertisements, holographic ticket kiosks, travelers from many realities, ozone haze, and layered city light visible beyond glass walls.
- Continuity notes: Sasha arrives here from another world and first realizes Nexus City sells power experiences like tourism.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/nexus-city-teleporter-station-setting.png`

## Setting: Astral Command Tower

- First appears: Issue #1, "Tourist Processing"
- Visual identifiers: massive skyscraper with black glass, silver ribs, blue energy lines, and a stylized spiral galaxy around a sword above the entrance. The outside is grand and cosmic, but the lobby is plain, bureaucratic, and underdecorated.
- Continuity notes: Astral Command operates from this tower. The mismatch between the exterior and lobby reflects the organization's dangerous, workmanlike attitude toward hero missions.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/astral-command-tower-setting.png`

## Setting: Power Activation Chamber

- First appears: Issue #1, "Tourist Processing"
- Visual identifiers: circular sterile chamber, raised blue-lit platform, ring of transparent glass-and-metal activation pods, ceiling cables, medical rails, holographic pain scales, and emergency lights ready to flash red.
- Continuity notes: Temporary powers and custom suits are assigned here. The chamber can save recruits or quietly process a disaster.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/power-activation-chamber-setting.png`

## Setting: Astral Command Recovery Room

- First appears: Issue #1, "Tourist Processing"
- Visual identifiers: stark white medical room, narrow recovery cots, wall-mounted diagnostic screens, blue holographic anatomy displays, sealed suit mannequins, and practical emergency equipment.
- Continuity notes: Sasha wakes here after integration with glowing hands and first learns the name of her power.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/astral-command-recovery-room-setting.png`

## Setting: Astral Command Briefing Room

- First appears: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"
- Visual identifiers: stark white tactical briefing room with glass walls, recessed blue lighting, a central holographic city table, wall displays showing vortex diagrams, emergency red alarm strips, and minimal furniture bolted to the floor.
- Continuity notes: Dr. Imani Vale explains Sasha's power and the incursion here before the alarm cuts training short.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/astral-command-briefing-room-setting.png`

## Setting: Astral Command Hangar

- First appears: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"
- Visual identifiers: reinforced high-ceiling hangar inside Astral Command Tower, dark metallic floor panels, blue runway lights, heavy blast doors opening toward Nexus City, containment soldiers moving in formation, and hovering transport vessels on standby.
- Continuity notes: Sasha boards the transport here for her first real mission.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/astral-command-hangar-setting.png`

## Setting: Central Business District Incursion Zone

- First appears: Issue #2, "Small Hand Wiggles"
- Visual identifiers: Nexus City financial towers and elevated walkways warped by blue-black reality fractures, missing chunks of buildings, floating debris, flickering windows, emergency haze, and a distant vortex bending the skyline.
- Continuity notes: The incursion consumes reality here during Issues #2-3. Sasha stabilizes the zone long enough for Astral Command to collapse the breach, leaving the district damaged but knitting itself back together.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/central-business-district-incursion-zone-setting.png`
