# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: Tourist Processing

- Folder: `series/nexus-nights/issues/issue-01/`
- Created: 2026-05-27
- Logline: Sasha arrives in Nexus City for a random one-night hero experience and survives Astral Command's dangerous power activation process.
- Plot summary: Sasha steps out of the teleporter station with a random power ticket and meets Taylor, a bored Astral Command liaison who warns her that tourists have died on first missions. He escorts her through Nexus City to Astral Command Tower, where she surrenders pieces of civilian identity, changes into a processing jumpsuit, and enters a power activation pod. The integration turns from exciting to terrifying as another recruit fails and Sasha nearly loses consciousness. She wakes in recovery with blue astral energy in her hands, a custom suit waiting nearby, and Dr. Imani Vale confirming that Sasha has received Astral Manipulation.
- Continuity changes: Sasha is now an Astral Command recruit for one temporary mission, bonded to Astral Manipulation and the Astral Command suit. Her expectations have shifted from simple thrill ride to dangerous hero work.
- New characters: Sasha, Taylor, Officer Mara Venn, Dr. Imani Vale.
- New settings: Nexus City Teleporter Station, Astral Command Tower, Power Activation Chamber, Astral Command Recovery Room.
- New key items: Random Power Ticket, Astral Command Suit, Activation Pod, Astral Manipulation.

## Issue #2: Small Hand Wiggles

- Folder: `series/nexus-nights/issues/issue-02/`
- Created: 2026-05-27
- Logline: Sasha suits up for her one-night hero mission, receives a rushed lesson in Astral Manipulation, and is deployed toward a reality-eating incursion before training can become anything more than "small hand wiggles."
- Plot summary: Sasha wakes in recovery relieved to be alive, puts on the Astral Command suit, and follows Dr. Imani Vale into a briefing room. Vale explains Astral Manipulation as quantum field projection, while Sasha translates the danger into portals, Ghostbusters, Doctor Strange, and other movie logic. The incursion in the Central Business District escalates before training can begin properly, so Vale gives Sasha a field communicator and quantum destabilizer pistol. Containment soldiers escort Sasha through the hangar, give her a tactical visor, and launch her toward the warped skyline in an Astral Command transport.
- Continuity changes: Sasha now wears the Astral Command suit in the field, carries mission gear, understands that the incursion is killing people, and is en route to her first real containment mission.
- New characters: Astral Command Containment Soldiers.
- New settings: Astral Command Briefing Room, Astral Command Hangar, Central Business District Incursion Zone.
- New key items: Field Communicator, Quantum Destabilizer Pistol, Tactical Visor, Astral Command Transport Vessel, Interdimensional Incursion.
