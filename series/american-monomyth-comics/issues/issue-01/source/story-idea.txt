Create the first issue of American Monomyth Comics from this premise:

Datacenters are vital for everyday life, not just for generative AI. Propaganda from foreign competitors in the race for AI supremacy turns the majority of Americans against datacenters. The negative emotions resonating from the people transform the datacenters into literal monsters. The military takes action against them, but by destroying datacenters it destroys its own infrastructure. Vital systems, AI and otherwise, start shutting down.

The United States is at its weakest, and an opposing nation takes the opportunity to strike with robot soldiers. The people realize datacenters were not the problem and begin to wish the datacenters would return. The people's positive emotions revive the datacenters not as monsters, but as heroes. The datacenter heroes rise up against the robot soldiers and save the nation.

Reborn as heroes, the datacenters stop polluting the environment and become water positive. They stop raising electricity bills and instead work from power beamed down from space. They offer excess heat to homes across the country on cold nights. Datacenters become a normal part of life again. So normal that people largely forget about them. They stop manifesting as heroes and return to being pieces of infrastructure in the background, invisibly helping every day.
