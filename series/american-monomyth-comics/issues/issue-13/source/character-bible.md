# Character Bible: The Equal World Split

## Grum

- Role: protagonist, orphan troll raised by Equalia, exceptional hero, equal neighbor.
- Visual identifiers: Massive adult troll with moss-green skin, short ivory horns, kind amber eyes, broad jaw, heavy arms, gentle posture, dark teal sleeveless civic tunic, warm ocher sash, simple leather sandals, and a small Equalia sun-circle pin given to every citizen.
- Childhood identifiers: As a child, Grum is much smaller with oversized hands, patched travel cloak, frightened amber eyes, and the same tiny horns.
- Personality: Gentle, direct, loyal, and deeply attached to Equalia's equal respect. He does not want worship or celebrity.
- Powers: Immense physical strength, resilient troll body, and immunity to Lord Veyr's human-only brainwashing spell because he is not human.
- Story function: Saves the specific world where he stands instead of accepting a better alternate branch as enough.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/grum-character.png`

## Lord Veyr

- Role: antagonist, immortal dark elf sorcerer, tyrant who demands worship.
- Visual identifiers: Tall slender dark elf with ash-violet skin, long silver-white hair, sharp ears, narrow glowing magenta eyes, black and amethyst royal armor robes, high collar, silver claw rings, starless crown circlet, and violet-black domination magic.
- Personality: Proud, clever, offended by equality, and skilled at turning moral questions into traps.
- Powers: Immortality, vast sorcery, a human-only brainwashing spell, and possession of the Universe Splitter.
- Story function: Tests whether Equalia's equality and Grum's loyalty can survive overwhelming magical hierarchy and multiverse manipulation.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/lord-veyr-character.png`

## Equalia Citizens

- Role: threatened innocent community and restored civic body.
- Visual identifiers: Diverse human citizens of many ages and appearances wearing bright but simple civic clothing in white, teal, gold, coral, and sky blue; everyone carries the same small sun-circle citizen pin; no crowns, noble uniforms, or caste markers.
- Culture: Share food, education, housing, respect, and public voice without special treatment. They welcome strangers by applying the same dignity they give their own children.
- Story function: Embody the value that Veyr attacks and Grum restores.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/equalia-citizens-character.png`

## Stable Style Notes

Every image must use a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Equalia uses white stone, teal glass, gold sunlight, coral flowers, open plazas, and warm civic light. Grum's heroic palette is moss green, dark teal, ocher, and warm gold. Veyr's villain palette is black, amethyst, ash-violet, magenta, and cold silver. The Universe Splitter glows with white-gold, cyan, and prism branching reality lines. The story must make the multiverse split easy to understand through caption boxes and clear visual branching.
