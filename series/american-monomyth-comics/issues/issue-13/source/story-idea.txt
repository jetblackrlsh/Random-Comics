Create the next issue of American Monomyth Comics from this premise:

On a new world, a utopia of humans treats everyone with equal respect and kindness, and no one receives special treatment. A young orphan troll enters the utopia, is welcomed with open arms, grows up there, and is treated just as well as everyone else.

A dark elf arrives and demands worship because he is immortal and magically powerful. The people refuse because equality is their civic principle. The dark elf brainwashes every human in the city, but his magic only works on humans, so the troll confronts him and orders him to end the spell.

The troll is stronger, but the dark elf is clever. He uses a Universe Splitter device to divide reality into a world where he surrenders and a world where he refuses. Their world appears to be the one where he refuses. The dark elf tells the troll to be happy that a better universe exists somewhere else.

The troll realizes that an alternate rescued world is not enough while his own world is still enslaved. He takes the Universe Splitter and divides reality again: one world where he leaves in shame, and one where he uppercuts the dark elf to the other side of the planet. Their world becomes the one where the troll fights. The dark elf is thrown so far away that his brainwashing spell breaks.

The people return to normal and ask what happened in the universe where the troll left in shame. The troll says he probably went beyond the city to request help from a hero's guild, because the lesson of the Universe Splitter is that there is always a way to save the day.

Life returns to normal. The troll is not celebrated as a hero, and no monuments are built in his name. He is treated like everyone else, exactly as he wants.
