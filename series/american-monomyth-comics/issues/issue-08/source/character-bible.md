# Character Bible

## Visual Style

All issue art uses a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Use crisp line art, radiant mythic skies, luminous water, iconic angel silhouettes, expressive talking animals, bright divine energy, and clear readable action. Avoid muddy realism, dull medieval painting, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, real-world religious iconography that imitates a specific church, and watermarks.

All readable story text must appear inside rectangular caption boxes generated directly inside the image art. Do not use speech balloons, thought balloons, manga bubble tails, or unboxed spoken or thought text.

## Seraphiel

- Role: protagonist, exceptional hero, outsider angel, future guardian angel of humanity.
- First appears: Issue #8, "The Angel Who Remembered Eden".
- Visual identifiers: tall androgynous angel with warm brown skin, luminous silver eyes, long dark curls threaded with starlight, six white-and-gold wings, pearl-and-gold celestial armor over a white robe, bare glowing hands, blue-gold halo ring, and later delicate radiant gills along the neck after eating the Tree of Life fruit.
- Power language: blue-gold mercy light, falling-star trails, protective wing shields, Tree of Life glow, underwater halos, and quiet defiance rather than conquest.
- Emotional arc: remembers Eden, defies Heaven's silence, saves the garden, refuses paradise, and accepts exile as a guardian angel for humanity.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/seraphiel-character.png`

## God as Divine Wrath

- Role: mythic antagonist force.
- First appears: Issue #8, "The Angel Who Remembered Eden".
- Visual identifiers: not shown as a normal human body; represented by colossal storm-cloud radiance, golden-white lightning, vast hands of light in the sky, unreadable eye-like sunbursts, and flood clouds that feel conscious and overwhelming.
- Continuity notes: God's rage at humanity consumes the world in the Great Flood and forgets Eden, forcing Seraphiel to choose mercy against divine wrath.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/divine-wrath-character.png`

## Eden Gate Angels

- Role: ordinary institution, devoted guardians, saved community.
- First appears: Issue #8, "The Angel Who Remembered Eden".
- Visual identifiers: disciplined angels in white-and-scarlet gate armor, two or four wings each, bronze-gold helmets, solemn faces, flaming swords with orange-white fire, and after transformation luminous gills and water-glow halos.
- Continuity notes: Guard Eden from humanity after Adam and Eve's exile. They enjoy their work, fail against the flood, help Seraphiel distribute fruit, survive as immortal water-adapted angels, and return to guarding Eden.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/eden-gate-angels-character.png`

## Eden's Talking Animals

- Role: innocent threatened community.
- First appears: Issue #8, "The Angel Who Remembered Eden".
- Visual identifiers: expressive paradise animals including lions, lambs, peacocks, foxes, deer, doves, serpents, rabbits, and turtles with gentle intelligent faces, tiny garlands, jewel-bright eyes, and after transformation delicate luminous gills and faint halo markings.
- Continuity notes: Live peacefully in Eden without sinful humans, nearly drown during the flood, eat the Tree of Life fruit, become immortal, repair Eden after the waters pass, and keep paradise alive.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/eden-talking-animals-character.png`

## Adam and Eve

- First appears: Issue #8, "The Angel Who Remembered Eden"
- Role: prologue figures whose exile defines Eden's sealed status.
- Continuity notes: Cast out of Eden before the main story begins. They appear only as distant prologue silhouettes beyond the gate.
