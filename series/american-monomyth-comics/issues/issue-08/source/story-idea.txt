Adam and Eve are cast out of the Garden of Eden. The east gate is guarded by angels with flaming swords, while Eden itself remains a paradise of talking animals, the Tree of Life, and the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil.

Generations later, God becomes enraged by humanity beyond Eden and floods the world, forgetting the innocent garden and the angels posted there. Eden's guards cannot abandon their post, cannot stop the waters, and lose their flaming swords when the flood puts them out.

Seraphiel, an angel from Heaven, remembers what God forgot. She descends without permission, enters Eden, and uses the Tree of Life to save the guard angels and as many talking animals as she can. The fruit makes them immortal and adapts them to survive underwater with luminous gills.

When the waters pass, Eden is restored and the flaming swords reignite. The grateful angels invite Seraphiel to remain in paradise, but she refuses. She defied God's will by coming to Earth and changing Eden forever, so she accepts exile. Eden's angels keep guarding Eden from humanity; Seraphiel becomes a guardian angel for humanity itself.
