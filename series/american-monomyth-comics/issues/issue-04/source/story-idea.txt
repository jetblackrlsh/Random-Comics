Create the next issue of American Monomyth Comics based on the following premise:
The utopia is thriving and all is right with the world.
While rats and mice exist, they are largely taken out by the utopia's powerful pesticides.
But the few rats that survived those pesticides have rapidly evolved into poison immune super-beasts.
They are larger, stronger, and smarter than their predecessors.
They have become so large that they can burrow through the walls of buildings.
They have started attacking restaurants and overrunning the streets.
There are too many of them for the police to take on.
Bullets seem to bounce off of their thick fur.
They spread disease wherever they go, making innocent people ill.
A wandering adventurer enters the city and sees that the people need her help.
She uses her skills as an artificer to create an animal translation device that allows her to talk to the rats.
She then connects her device to the city's emergency systems and broadcasts a message across the entire city that sends the rats into a panic.
The rats beg for the broadcast to stop.
They grab shiny things like gold and amulets and bring them to her.
They offer for her to take the treasures and leave them be.
But she refuses to accept their bribes and cranks up the volume of the broadcast.
Overwhelmed by the broadcast, the rats scatter out of the utopia and vow never to return.
The adventurer knows their journey isn't over, as the rats that scattered will likely plague other cities in the future.
The adventurer journeys off, while the utopia starts to turn back to normal.
Infrastructure is repaired, the sick recover, and monuments are put up to celebrate the hero that saved them.
But peace and time lead to the utopia eventually forgetting about the hero entirely.
