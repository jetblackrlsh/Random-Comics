# Character Bible

## Visual Style

All issue art uses a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Use crisp line art, strong superhero anatomy, dramatic perspective, expressive anime faces, dynamic motion, luminous utopian city light, rat-swarm danger, and clear readable action. Avoid photorealistic documentary styling, muddy colors, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, real-world logos, and real-world political symbols.

All readable story text must appear inside rectangular caption boxes generated directly inside the image art. Do not use speech balloons, thought balloons, manga bubble tails, or unboxed spoken or thought text.

## Aria Flint

- Role: protagonist, exceptional hero, wandering artificer-adventurer.
- First appears: Issue #4, "The Pestbright Piper".
- Visual identifiers: athletic young adult woman with warm brown skin, short wind-swept black hair with copper streaks, amber eyes, confident focused expression, teal travel cloak with brass clasps, fitted ivory-and-cobalt adventurer armor, brown utility belts, fingerless gloves, knee boots, small tool satchel, and glowing brass-and-crystal devices clipped to her gear.
- Power language: inventive artificer glow, teal-gold circuit sigils, brass mechanisms, crystal lenses, emergency-speaker light rings, and precise hand-built technology rather than raw super strength.
- Emotional arc: arrives as a free wanderer, listens to the monster society before defeating it, refuses a corrupt bargain, saves Edenbright, and leaves because the scattered danger is still moving.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/aria-flint-character.png`

## Pestborn Brood

- Role: collective antagonist, poison-immune super-rats.
- First appears: Issue #4, "The Pestbright Piper".
- Visual identifiers: enormous intelligent rats the size of large dogs or bigger, thick bristling charcoal-gray and oil-black fur that deflects bullets, glowing sickly green pesticide scars, oversized claws and teeth, bright cunning yellow eyes, armored hunched bodies, tunnel-digging forearms, and swarm formations that look organized rather than mindless.
- Power language: toxic green disease trails, debris sprays from burrowing, ricocheting bullet sparks against fur, swarming motion, and frantic panic when Aria's broadcast overwhelms them.
- Emotional arc: survive Edenbright's pesticides, evolve into organized raiders, spread disease and demand food, plead when translated language becomes unbearable, attempt bribery with stolen treasure, and scatter into exile while vowing never to return.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/pestborn-brood-character.png`

## Edenbright City

- Role: threatened utopian community and restored society.
- First appears: Issue #3, "The Last Hour of Y-Man".
- Visual identifiers: luminous clean city of white glass towers, vertical sky gardens, blue transit ribbons, solar crowns, free clinics, school terraces, public art plazas, soft gold civic lights, transparent council halls, flowering memorial parks, and elegant restaurants with bright communal dining rooms.
- Continuity notes: Designed for cooperation and peace, not war. After surviving Z-Man's conquest, Edenbright has returned to normal so completely that it trusts its civic systems again.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/edenbright-city-setting.png`

## Resonance Translator

- Role: Aria's animal translation device and decisive heroic tool.
- First appears: Issue #4, "The Pestbright Piper".
- Visual identifiers: hand-built brass-and-crystal shoulder device with a circular gramophone-like emitter, teal glowing circuit rings, amber lenses, silver tuning forks, small glyph dials, cable leads that connect to civic emergency systems, and visible sound-wave halos.
- Continuity notes: Built from Aria's artificer kit and Edenbright emergency components. It translates animal thought into meaning and can broadcast that meaning through citywide speakers at overwhelming intensity.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/resonance-translator-key-item.png`
