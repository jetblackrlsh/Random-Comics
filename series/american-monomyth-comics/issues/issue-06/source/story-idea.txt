Create the next issue of American Monomyth Comics from this premise:

The story begins in a peaceful utopia of humanoid rabbits in feudal Japan during a candle lighting ceremony. A young rabbit girl trips and drops her candle on the steps of a sacred temple. The temple existed to contain dangerous spirits behind a powerful seal. The seal is burned away by the flames and spirits leap out to attack the town. The town's rabbit monks leap into action with prayer beads, but the spirits effortlessly slice through the beads with their claws. The rabbit guards try to fight the spirits with their spears, but the spears do nothing to the spirits' incorporeal forms. A wandering rabbit samurai dressed in red dragon armor arrives and realizes he is needed. He calmly approaches the spirits who mock him as he draws his sword. He calls upon the aid of the dragon god whose light radiates through the blade. The mocking spirits are filled with fear as the blade glows with a golden sheen. Expertly the samurai slices through the spirits with incredible acrobatic grace. The town is saved and builds a statue in the hero's honor. The hero refuses to stay as they know they are needed in other towns across the land. Generations pass, the statue eventually crumbles, the hero is forgotten, but peace is maintained in the utopia.
