# Character Bible

## Visual Style

All issue art uses a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Use crisp line art, heroic silhouettes, dramatic feudal-Japan architecture, expressive humanoid rabbit faces, glowing candlelight, golden dragon power, smoky spirit claws, vivid festival lanterns, and clear readable action.

All readable story text must appear inside rectangular caption boxes generated directly inside the image art. Do not use speech balloons, thought balloons, manga bubble tails, floating dialogue, or unboxed spoken or thought text.

## Akaryu

- Role: protagonist and exceptional hero.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: tall athletic humanoid rabbit samurai with white fur, calm amber eyes, long swept-back rabbit ears, layered red lacquer dragon armor, gold horned dragon crest, crimson haori panels, black hakama, dragon-scale shoulder guards, and a golden-sheathed katana.
- Power language: calm stillness before motion, golden dragon-light flowing through the katana, acrobatic arcs, red armor silhouettes, and warm protective light that cuts only spirits.
- Emotional arc: arrives as a necessary outsider, saves the village without blame, accepts gratitude, then leaves because other towns need him more than one statue does.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/akaryu-character.png`

## Kiko

- Role: young candle bearer whose accident releases the spirits.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: small humanoid rabbit girl with cream fur, round dark eyes, short festival kimono in pale pink and moon-white, red sash, flower hairpin near one ear, and a small ceremonial candle cup.
- Continuity notes: Trips during the candle lighting ceremony, accidentally burns the Spirit Seal, then is forgiven after the village survives. She represents innocence rather than blame.

## Ashen Claw Spirits

- Role: collective antagonist and mythic evil.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: smoky black-and-indigo spirits with rabbit-skull hints, long white clawed hands, ember eyes, torn mist bodies, ash trails, and sharp crescent silhouettes. They are incorporeal until struck by dragon light.
- Power language: claw slashes that sever prayer beads, bodies that spears pass through, smoke trails, cold violet fear halos, and mocking crescent grins that turn fearful when golden blade-light appears.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/ashen-claw-spirits-character.png`

## Candle-Keeper Monks

- Role: ordinary religious institution that tries and fails to stop the spirits.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: adult humanoid rabbit monks in saffron, ivory, and indigo robes, wooden sandals, long prayer beads, shaved or tied fur patterns, and lantern charms.
- Continuity notes: Their beads preserve ordinary peace but cannot bind the freed Ashen Claw Spirits once the seal burns.

## Moon Gate Guards

- Role: ordinary civic defenders that try and fail to stop the spirits.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: disciplined humanoid rabbit guards in blue-gray lamellar armor, crescent helmets shaped around rabbit ears, long yari spears, and protective gate banners.
- Continuity notes: Brave and organized, but their physical spears cannot harm incorporeal spirits.

## Dragon God

- Role: sacred power source aiding Akaryu.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: enormous luminous golden dragon spirit with antler-like horns, pearl whiskers, sunlit scales, warm eyes, and a body made of clouds, flame, and shrine light.
- Power language: golden radiance descending into the Dragonlit Katana, dragon-shaped light trails, seal-restoring halos, and sacred warmth that makes spirits recoil.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/dragon-god-character.png`

## Tsukihana Village

- Role: threatened utopian community.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: peaceful feudal-Japan village of humanoid rabbits with lantern streets, rice terraces, moon bridges, wooden homes, tile roofs, koi ponds, cherry blossoms, candle ceremony banners, and a sacred temple above stone steps.
- Continuity notes: Survives the spirit attack, honors Akaryu with a statue, then remains peaceful for generations until the statue crumbles and the hero is forgotten.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/tsukihana-village-setting.png`

## Moon Lantern Temple

- Role: sacred temple and prison for the spirits.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: red-and-white wooden temple above broad stone steps, moon lanterns, gold roof ornaments, carved rabbit guardians, candle terraces, and a glowing circular Spirit Seal burned into the upper stair landing.
- Continuity notes: Built to contain the Ashen Claw Spirits. Its Spirit Seal burns during Kiko's accident and is restored by Dragon God light.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/moon-lantern-temple-setting.png`

## Spirit Seal

- Role: protective sacred seal.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: circular gold-and-white seal of brushstroke rings, moon petals, dragon scale marks, and rabbit temple glyphs set into stone at Moon Lantern Temple.
- Continuity notes: Holds the spirits behind the temple threshold until candle fire burns it away. Restored by Akaryu's dragon-lit blade.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/spirit-seal-key-item.png`

## Dragonlit Katana

- Role: Akaryu's spirit-cutting weapon.
- First appears: Issue #6, "The Dragon Blade of Moon Lantern Temple".
- Visual identifiers: elegant katana with red lacquer scabbard, gold dragon guard, white grip wrap, moon-shaped pommel charm, and a blade that becomes golden when the Dragon God answers.
- Continuity notes: Can cut incorporeal spirits only when used in calm sacred service, not anger or vanity.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/dragonlit-katana-key-item.png`
