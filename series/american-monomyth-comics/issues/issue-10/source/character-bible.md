# Character Bible: The Ice Walls of Everbite

## Rimecable

- Role: protagonist, exceptional hero, barbarian ice spirit from a future time and colder climate.
- Visual identifiers: Tall imposing spirit-warrior in futuristic white power armor, neon white visor on a full helmet, spiked icy shoulder plates, glowing cyan chest rune, tattered translucent white-blue cape covered in glowing frosty blue runes, long black cable dreadlocks tipped with icy fragments, heavy boots, frost vapor around the armor joints, and a powerful double-headed crystalline ice axe.
- Personality: Calm, severe, protective, and thoughtful. Rimecable is a barbarian in presence and courage but refuses pointless violence.
- Powers: Electrical stun lashes from the cable dreadlocks, mystical frost from the ice axe, time-spirit arrival and withdrawal, permanent ice-wall creation that holds in any climate.
- Story function: Stops the dragons without killing them, restores the island's balance, and leaves before gratitude can become dependence.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/rimecable-character.png`
- User-supplied visual input: `reference-inputs/rimecable-user-reference.png`

## Solekin Islanders

- Role: threatened innocent community.
- Visual identifiers: Small humans with large strong bare feet, warm brown island skin tones, expressive faces, woven leaf-and-shell clothing, waist pouches, scavenging baskets, shell tools, painted foot charms, and communal villages built around low platforms sized for their bodies and feet.
- Culture: They do not hunt or gather. They collect safe leftovers from komodo dragon meals, clean the meal grounds, sing thanks, and treat the dragons as dangerous providers.
- Story function: Their helplessness makes the crisis urgent because their survival depends on the dragons surviving too.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/solekin-and-komodo-dragons-character.png`

## Everbite Komodo Dragons

- Role: powerful provider species and crisis source.
- Visual identifiers: Huge komodo dragons with dark green, charcoal, bronze, and ocher scales; heavy claws; thick tails; muscular necks; forked tongues; scarred hides; and amber eyes. They are animalistic, not villainous, with territorial poses and feeding-ground behavior.
- Continuity notes: Their leftover meals sustain the Solekin. Overpopulation causes territorial fights that nearly wipe them out until Rimecable divides their ranges with ice walls.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/solekin-and-komodo-dragons-character.png`

## Solekin Elders and Mapkeepers

- Role: ordinary institutions and supporting collective.
- Visual identifiers: Older Solekin with shell necklaces, painted walking staffs, woven map cloths, drum crews, territory stones, and solemn footstep markings.
- Story function: They try old customs, offerings, drums, and maps to calm the dragons, but lack the strength and combat experience needed to intervene.

## Everbite Children

- Role: vulnerable civilians and future memory of the island.
- Visual identifiers: Tiny Solekin children with oversized bare feet, bright shell anklets, woven tunics, curious eyes, and small baskets.
- Story function: They show what will be lost if the dragons die and later show that peace lasted long enough for the hero to be forgotten.

## Stable Style Notes

Every image must use a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Rimecable's power color is blue-white frost with white electrical sparks. Match the user-supplied Rimecable visual input for the white armored silhouette, cyan visor, black cable dreadlocks, torn icy cape, rune glow, and double-headed ice axe. The Solekin palette is warm tropical green, coral, shell white, tan, and leaf gold. The dragons use earthy reptile colors contrasted with blue-white ice walls.
