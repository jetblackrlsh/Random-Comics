Create the next American Monomyth issue from this premise:

On a hidden island utopia live a race of small humans with large feet. They do not hunt or gather. They simply scavenge what is left of the meals of komodo dragons. The two species live in harmony on the island. But as the komodo dragon population continued to rise, territorial conflicts became more common. The komodo dragons began wrestling and biting each other. The fights happened so often that they were starting to wipe each other out. The people of the island lacked the strength or combat experience needed to intervene. If the komodo dragons went extinct, so too would the people.

Just as all hope seemed lost, the prayers of the people called upon a spirit from a future time and a colder climate. The barbarian ice spirit arrived in futuristic white power armor with a tattered white cape covered in glowing frosty blue runes, a neon white visor on the helmet, long black cable dreadlocks, and a powerful ice axe. The barbarian saw the violence and knew that more violence would only make things worse. Using electricity from the cable dreadlocks, the barbarian stunned the komodo dragons. Using frost from the axe, the barbarian split the island with walls of ice to divide the territories evenly. The cold-blooded nature of the komodo dragons repelled them from the ice walls. The mystical ice would continue to hold regardless of weather.

With the conflicts broken up, the komodo dragon population stabilized. The people of the island celebrated the barbarian and requested for the hero to stay. The barbarian refused, as their spirit was called to other lands and other times. The barbarian spirit vanished. Peace returned to the utopia and with time the people forgot about the barbarian that saved them. The ice walls were left as a forgotten monument to a hero lost to time.
