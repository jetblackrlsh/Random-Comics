# The Ice Walls of Everbite

## Issue Number

10

## Logline

When Everbite Island's harmony between the small large-footed Solekin people and their komodo dragons collapses into territorial fighting that could kill both species, future-born ice spirit Rimecable uses stunning lightning and permanent frost walls to stop the violence without becoming another predator.

## Core Premise

Everbite Island is a hidden tropical utopia where the Solekin, a race of small humans with large feet, survive by respectfully scavenging the leftovers of giant komodo dragons. The dragons hunt, the Solekin clean the remains, and both species have lived in balance for generations. The Solekin do not hunt or gather because the dragons' meals provide everything they need.

That balance fails when the dragon population rises. More dragons need more territory, and their disputes turn into constant wrestling, biting, and bloodshed. The Solekin cannot outmuscle the dragons, and any direct attack would break the harmony that keeps them alive. If the dragons wipe each other out, the Solekin will lose the source of their food and their way of life.

The Solekin's prayers call Rimecable, a barbarian ice spirit from a future time and colder climate. Rimecable arrives in futuristic white power armor, a tattered white cape marked by glowing frosty blue runes, a neon white visor, long black cable dreadlocks, and a powerful ice axe. Rimecable understands that the island does not need a stronger fighter. It needs a divider who can stop fights without destroying either side.

## Threatened Community

The Solekin and komodo dragons are a strange but innocent shared community. The dragons are not villains; they are powerful animals whose growing numbers create a territorial problem. The Solekin are gentle scavengers who depend on dragon leftovers and know the dragons as neighbors, providers, and dangerous partners.

## Mythic Evil

The mythic threat is escalating territorial violence. It is not an evil creature but a natural harmony breaking under pressure. Dragons wrestle, bite, bleed, and drive one another from feeding grounds until the island's life cycle begins collapsing.

## Why Institutions Fail

The Solekin elders, mapkeepers, drummers, and feast stewards try to calm the dragons with offerings, paths, warning drums, and old territory stones. None of it works because the dragons are too large, too many, too angry, and too cold-blooded to reason through Solekin ceremony.

## Hero's Exceptional Quality

Rimecable combines future power armor, barbarian courage, spirit form, electrical cable dreadlocks, and mystical frost. More importantly, Rimecable sees that violence would make the crisis worse. The hero can use force as restraint instead of domination.

## Redemptive Action

Rimecable stuns the fighting dragons with controlled electrical lashes from the cable dreadlocks, then uses the ice axe to raise permanent walls of mystical frost across the island. The walls divide territory evenly, guide the dragons away from each other, and repel them because cold-blooded bodies instinctively avoid the supernatural chill.

## Restoration

The dragons stop fighting because each territory has space, food paths, and cooling boundaries they refuse to cross. The dragon population stabilizes. The Solekin can safely scavenge leftovers again without watching their providers destroy one another.

## Final Separation

The Solekin ask Rimecable to stay and become the island's guardian, but the hero refuses. The spirit is called to other lands and other times where violence needs a colder answer. Rimecable vanishes, and generations later the Solekin remember the ice walls as natural landmarks while forgetting the hero who made them.

## Theme

Real rescue sometimes means stopping violence without choosing an enemy to destroy.

## Moral Tension

The dragons' violence is deadly, but the dragons are also the Solekin's providers. Rimecable must protect the victims without turning the providers into monsters or teaching the island that only greater violence can keep peace.

## Hero Desire

Rimecable wants threatened worlds to survive without becoming dependent on the hero's power.

## Hero Burden

Rimecable belongs to no warm home, no single time, and no community for long. The spirit can freeze a boundary into history, but cannot stay inside the peace it creates.

## Final Withdrawal Image

Rimecable dissolves into a blue-white time frost above the island. Far below, Solekin children play beside ancient ice walls that still glow, not knowing they are touching the monument of a forgotten savior.

## 10-Step American Monomyth Outline

### Step 01: The Edenic Community

Pages 2-3 show Everbite Island as a lush hidden utopia where small large-footed Solekin live peacefully by collecting safe leftovers after komodo dragon meals. The dragons provide, the Solekin clean and honor the remains, and the whole island seems balanced.

### Step 02: Evil Threatens the Community

Pages 4-5 show the dragon population growing too large for the old territories. Dragons wrestle, bite, and wound one another in repeated conflicts that turn the food cycle into a death spiral.

### Step 03: Ordinary Institutions Fail

Pages 6-7 show Solekin elders, mapkeepers, drummers, and feast stewards trying old customs to calm the dragons. Their drums, offerings, markers, and courage fail because they cannot physically stop the giant reptiles.

### Step 04: The Community Becomes Helpless

Pages 8-9 show starving Solekin families watching wounded dragons collapse and abandoned meal sites rot untouched. The people understand that if the dragons vanish, their own utopia will vanish with them.

### Step 05: The Exceptional Hero Appears

Pages 10-11 reveal Rimecable arriving through a blue-white time storm in futuristic white power armor, a rune-covered white cape, a neon visor, black cable dreadlocks, and a glowing ice axe. Rimecable studies the battle and sees that more violence would only deepen the disaster.

### Step 06: The Hero Resists Ordinary Limitations

Pages 12-13 show Rimecable refusing the Solekin request to kill the worst dragons. Instead, the hero enters the fighting ground and uses controlled electrical lashes from the cable dreadlocks to stun dragons long enough to stop the immediate bloodshed.

### Step 07: Redemptive Force Confronts Evil

Pages 14-15 show Rimecable driving the ice axe into the island and raising mystical frost walls that split the territory evenly. The cold-blooded dragons recoil from the ice, and the conflict breaks without slaughter.

### Step 08: The Community Is Restored

Pages 16-17 show each dragon territory stabilizing behind the ice boundaries. The dragons hunt without constant fights, the Solekin scavenge safely again, and the island's cycle recovers.

### Step 09: The Hero Does Not Fully Join the Community

Pages 18-19 show the Solekin celebrating Rimecable, decorating the ice walls, and asking the hero to stay. Rimecable stands apart because the call of other times is already pulling the spirit away.

### Step 10: The Hero Withdraws or Waits

Pages 20-21 show Rimecable vanishing into time frost and the long future of Everbite Island. Generations later, peace is normal, the hero's name is forgotten, and the ice walls remain as a monument no one fully understands.

## Back Cover Concept

The back cover frames Rimecable towering above Everbite Island, one cable dreadlock sparking white lightning and the ice axe raising blue-white walls between komodo territories. Small poster fragments show the Solekin scavenging peacefully, dragons recoiling from cold boundaries, the celebration, and future children playing by the forgotten ice. Integrated poster caption text: "HE DID NOT KILL THE DRAGONS." "HE DIVIDED THE VIOLENCE." "THE WALLS REMEMBER."
