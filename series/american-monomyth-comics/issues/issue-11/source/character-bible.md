# Character Bible: The World Inside the Chaos Crystal

## Shadow Zone Hero

- Role: protagonist, exceptional hero, outsider rescuer.
- Visual identifiers: Athletic masked superhero in matte black and deep indigo armor with silver edge lines, a crescent-shaped shadow emblem on the chest, glowing violet-blue eyes behind a smooth mask, a short torn dark cape, fingerless gauntlets trailing black smoke, and blue-violet portal energy around the boots and hands.
- Personality: Direct, morally certain, brave, and willing to be unknown. The hero speaks in rallying declarations and acts quickly, but learns that obvious teleport tricks can fail.
- Powers: Slips into the Shadow Zone to teleport, reappears from black-blue portals, senses lives hidden across dimensional boundaries, and can use the Shadow Zone as an escape route.
- Story function: Acts from outside the crystal-world, names the moral crime, calls on Gaia, and leaves without recognition from the world saved.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/shadow-zone-hero-character.png`

## Techadon

- Role: antagonist, behemoth thief from beyond the crystal-world's reality.
- Visual identifiers: Tall sharp-featured humanoid behemoth in a torn charcoal hooded cloak, angular predatory face, pale gray skin, long black hair, clawed gauntlets, jagged bronze-black armor under the cloak, heat-cracked orange crystal veins across the chest and arms, and a cruel smirk lit by the Chaos Crystal.
- Personality: Mocking, nihilistic, theatrical, and predatory. Techadon treats worlds as disposable because their destruction makes him feel powerful.
- Powers: Uses the Chaos Crystal to augment speed, heat blasts, strength, and corruption tendrils. Predicts simple teleport attacks while empowered.
- Story function: Personifies intentional planetary murder disguised as cosmic indifference.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/techadon-character.png`

## Gaia and Natural Law

- Role: redemptive force and living super-organism of nature.
- Visual identifiers: Colossal green-gold feminine planetary presence formed from roots, mountains, storm clouds, river light, leaves, vines, animal silhouettes, crystal shards, and a calm severe face that can appear in clouds, fissures, and the Chaos Crystal's glow.
- Continuity notes: Gaia answers when Shadow Zone Hero names intentional planetary killing as a crime against Natural Law. Gaia is not a normal person in the chamber; she manifests through the living world, the crystal, and the stone around Techadon.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/gaia-natural-law-character.png`

## Chaos Crystal Utopians

- Role: innocent threatened community.
- Visual identifiers: Graceful humanoid citizens with luminous skin tones in pearl, blue, gold, and rose; crystalline hair ornaments; flowing robes; leaf-and-gem jewelry; peaceful expressive faces; and small animal companions made of light and crystal.
- Culture: They live on the planet inside the Chaos Crystal in balance with rivers, forests, floating cities, crystal gardens, and animal spirits. Their world is peaceful enough that they do not understand the outer chamber until the draining begins.
- Story function: They are the saved community that never learns Shadow Zone Hero's name.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/chaos-crystal-utopians-character.png`

## Crystal-World Guardians and Healers

- Role: ordinary institutions of the crystal-world.
- Visual identifiers: Crystal-robed healers, geomancers, civic engineers, river-song elders, and guardian sentries with glowing staffs, prism shields, botanical crowns, and worried but disciplined expressions.
- Story function: They try to repair the rupture and protect citizens, but they cannot reach Techadon because he attacks from outside their reality.

## Hero Hospital Staff

- Role: aftermath caretakers and ordinary outside-world support.
- Visual identifiers: Calm superhero medics in clean white-and-teal uniforms with red cross-like fictional star emblems, glowing diagnostic tablets, bandage tools, and gentle efficient posture.
- Story function: Treat Shadow Zone Hero's shoulder after the battle, emphasizing that the hero's public reward is medical care away from the saved world, not celebration by that world.

## Stable Style Notes

Every image must use a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Shadow Zone Hero's power color is black-blue shadow with violet-blue portal glow. Techadon's corrupted crystal power is orange-red, black, and sickly magenta. The Chaos Crystal and the utopia inside it use clear cyan, emerald, pearl, gold, and rose light. Gaia's power is green-gold with roots, leaves, stone, river light, and living storm energy. The dank chamber is dark stone, but the art should remain vivid and readable, not muddy.
