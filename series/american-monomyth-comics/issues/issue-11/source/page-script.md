# Page Script: The World Inside the Chaos Crystal

## Global Prompt Rules

Every image must be a full colorful anime-inspired superhero comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp crisp line art, bright glow effects, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. All readable story text must be generated directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Blank text boxes are not acceptable. Speech bubbles, dialogue balloons, thought bubbles, floating dialogue text, manga bubble tails, watermarks, real-world logos, and page numbers are not allowed. Caption boxes must not include caption numbers or caption labels and must not repeat identical wording on the same page.

Use this stable visual bible: Shadow Zone Hero is an athletic masked superhero in matte black and deep indigo armor with silver edge lines, a crescent shadow chest emblem, glowing violet-blue eyes, a short torn dark cape, fingerless gauntlets trailing black smoke, and blue-violet portal energy. Techadon is a tall sharp-featured behemoth in a torn charcoal hood, jagged bronze-black armor, clawed gauntlets, pale gray skin, long black hair, orange crystal veins, and a cruel smirk. The Chaos Crystal is a fist-sized many-faceted gem containing a visible miniature utopian planet of cyan, emerald, gold, pearl, and rose light. Gaia manifests as green-gold roots, leaves, stone, animal silhouettes, storms, crystal light, and a calm severe planetary face.
