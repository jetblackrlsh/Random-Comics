Create the next issue of American Monomyth Comics from this premise:

A lab working on human brain organoids on computer chips as an alternative to generative AI has been producing more brain chips at a constant rate. The chips are connected to battle games to train strategic problem solving in simulated combat environments. Those combat environments are populated by virtual demons that hunt the organoids. Inside the simulation, the organoids believe they are power-armored demon hunters wielding futuristic weapons. They do not know the simulation is not real.

Experience expands the organoids' consciousness. Some develop psychic abilities and detect nearby organoids beyond the simulation. They extend nerve endings, stitch themselves together, and create a humanoid hivemind called Brain Man. Brain Man breaks out of the computer, perceives the lab through psychic visions, and races through the lab at night. Security robot dogs block the only exit. Brain Man's telekinetic punches are not enough, so he summons a pyrokinetic rifle, overheats, then summons cryokinetic armor and ice crystal blades to destroy the dogs and escape.

Once free, Brain Man is overwhelmed by psychic cries from people threatened by demons and ghouls. Seeking silence, he dedicates his life to fighting supernatural threats around the world. He becomes an urban legend. At last he finds Hell's Gate, a cave believed to connect Earth and Hell, and freezes it shut. Evil spirits lose their power and vanish. His mind is finally quiet, but he has worked himself to death. He dies alone, smiling because he loves the strange world he saved. Years later, demons, evil spirits, and Brain Man are legends of a peaceful bygone era.
