# Character Bible: The Arrows Above Witch High

## Cupid

- Role: protagonist, exceptional hero, hidden angelic rescuer.
- Visual identifiers: Androgynous youthful adult angel with warm brown skin, soft gold eyes, short rose-gold curls, white-and-pink celestial armor over flowing white robes, four luminous feathered wings, a small halo shaped like a glowing heart-ring, and a graceful rose-gold bow that fires holy love arrows.
- Personality: Humble, gentle, decisive, and uninterested in glory. Cupid acts with compassion for both sides and believes a victory that restores free hearts is better than a victory that leaves hatred alive.
- Powers: Fires holy love arrows that cleanse demon-fueled rage, dissolve infernal pact chains, and awaken sincere compassion and love without forcing false affection.
- Story function: Saves Witch High and Warlock High by breaking the spiritual source of the war, then remains hidden so the restored community can own its future.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/cupid-character.png`

## Witch High Students

- Role: threatened innocent community and disciplined defenders.
- Visual identifiers: Upper-year young adult witches in elegant navy, violet, white, and emerald academy uniforms; crescent hats or hair ornaments; wand belts; spellbooks; expressive determined faces; and magical pets including winged cats, tiny dragons, crystal owls, star rabbits, and floating koi spirits.
- Culture: Study healing, potion craft, astronomy, broom flight, animal bonding, protective wards, and ethical spellcraft in a beautiful all-girl academy.
- Story function: Prove that training, courage, and community are real strengths, even when demon-enhanced power outmatches them in war.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/witch-high-setting.png`

## Warlock High Students

- Role: attacking school, victims of demon pacts, and future partners in restoration.
- Visual identifiers: Upper-year young adult warlocks in black, crimson, and gold academy coats; sharp collars; infernal spellbooks; glowing pact marks on hands and chests; red-black aura chains; and faces that shift from demon-driven anger to regret and tenderness after the arrows cleanse them.
- Culture: Trained in conquest magic by a rival all-boy academy that trades freedom for overwhelming power through demon contracts.
- Story function: Embody the danger of power without freedom. Once the demon influence is broken, they can choose apology, love, and peace.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/warlock-high-setting.png`

## Demon Pact Shadows

- Role: mythic evil and infernal force behind the attack.
- Visual identifiers: Red-black demonic silhouettes, horned smoke faces, clawed contract chains, burning pact sigils, and shadowy hands emerging from spellbooks and marks rather than ordinary bodies.
- Continuity notes: Feed on anger and rivalry. They are defeated when Cupid's holy arrows burn the pact marks and remove the emotional fuel they need.

## Witch-Warlock Descendants

- Role: restored future community and living legacy.
- Visual identifiers: Young magical descendants with mixed witch and warlock visual motifs: bright academy uniforms combining violet, white, black, rose-gold, and emerald; clean glowing pact-free magic; beloved magical pets; and confident faces using disciplined power without demon chains.
- Story function: Show the long-term result of Cupid's hidden heroism. Their strength proves that peace created a better future than victory by conquest.

## Stable Style Notes

Every image must use a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Cupid's holy magic is rose-gold, pearl-white, and soft sky blue. Witch High uses emerald, violet, moon-silver, and warm golden school light. Warlock High and the demon pacts begin with crimson, black, ember orange, and harsh violet shadows. When the pacts break, the palette shifts toward rose-gold, clear blue, emerald, and sunrise white. The story should never frame romance as coercion: the arrows cleanse demon anger and reveal free, sincere love.
