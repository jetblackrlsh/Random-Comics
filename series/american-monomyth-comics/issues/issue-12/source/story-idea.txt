Create the next issue of American Monomyth Comics from this premise:

Witch High was a utopian all-girl school of witchcraft where upper-year young adult witches studied in harmony with magical pets. Warlock High, an all-boy school whose upper-year young adult warlocks drew power from demon pacts, attacked Witch High. The witches were better trained and more experienced, but the warlocks' demon magic made them far more powerful in an all-out magical war.

When the witches were about to lose, Cupid the angel descended from Heaven and fired magical love arrows into the battlefield. The holy arrows overpowered the demon pacts, cleared the warlocks' demon-fueled anger, and filled both witches and warlocks with honest love instead of hatred. The war ended because every witch and every warlock found their other half, rebuilt peace, and eventually raised children stronger than either school had been alone.

Cupid stayed hidden among the clouds. Cupid did not live for glory. Seeing generations of happy couples and stronger descendants was enough reward. Cupid's heroic legacy lived in the joy and peace they created, not in public legends about the angel who saved them.
