# Page Script: The Arrows Above Witch High

## Global Prompt Rules

Every image must be a full colorful anime-inspired superhero comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp crisp line art, bright glow effects, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. All readable story text must be generated directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Blank text boxes are not acceptable. Speech bubbles, dialogue balloons, thought bubbles, floating dialogue text, manga bubble tails, watermarks, real-world logos, and page numbers are not allowed. Caption boxes must not include caption numbers or caption labels and must not repeat identical wording on the same page.

Use this stable visual bible: Cupid is an androgynous youthful adult angel with warm brown skin, soft gold eyes, short rose-gold curls, white-and-pink celestial armor, flowing white robes, four luminous wings, a small heart-ring halo, and a rose-gold bow that fires holy love arrows. Witch High is a luminous all-girl academy for upper-year young adult witches, with moon-silver towers, emerald gardens, violet banners, floating classrooms, potion greenhouses, and magical pets. Warlock High is a storm-black all-boy academy for upper-year young adult warlocks, with crimson windows, infernal sigils, demon-chain bridges, and red-black pact magic. Demon pact shadows are horned smoke faces, clawed contract chains, and burning pact marks. Cupid's holy magic is rose-gold, pearl-white, and sky blue.
