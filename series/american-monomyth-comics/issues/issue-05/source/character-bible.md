# Character Bible

## Visual Style

All issue art uses a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Use crisp line art, heroic silhouettes, dramatic mountain scale, expressive anime faces, glowing lightning, vivid flowers, impossible rivers, and clear readable action.

All readable story text must appear inside rectangular caption boxes generated directly inside the image art. Do not use speech balloons, thought balloons, manga bubble tails, floating dialogue, or unboxed spoken or thought text.

## Purple Climber

- Role: central threatened redeemer, elite troll climber, feared future goddess.
- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod".
- Visual identifiers: athletic female troll with shaggy violet-purple fur, bell-shaped head, short curved ivory horns, glowing red eyes, climbing wraps, gold climbing clasps, purple lightning streaks around hands and feet, proud posture that softens after the journey.
- Power language: unnatural speed shown as purple lightning trails, afterimages on cliffs, and electric arcs around climbing holds.
- Emotional arc: begins greedy and afraid of sharing, follows the hero because she wants a greater prize, admits her parents died from giving too much away, learns balance, wins the Master Rod, and uses it generously.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/purple-climber-character.png`

## The Wandering Hero

- Role: protagonist and exceptional hero.
- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod".
- Visual identifiers: androgynous human-like traveler with calm eyes, weathered white-and-teal cloak, simple travel armor, brown boots, walking staff without magic, no crown, no emblem of government, and a steady compassionate expression.
- Power language: quiet golden path-light, clear eye contact, open hands, and stillness beside Purple's lightning.
- Emotional arc: enters Troll Land, deceives Purple to prevent disaster, learns the wound beneath her greed, teaches balance, brings her home, then leaves before gratitude can turn into dependence.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/wandering-hero-character.png`

## The Master

- Role: mythic troll god and keeper of the Master Rod.
- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod".
- Visual identifiers: giant ghostly white-furred troll deity with a bell-shaped head, enormous curved horns, calm red star-like eyes, flowing spectral fur, mountain-sized silhouette, and a solemn presence above Mount Master.
- Power language: white mist, blue-white divine lightning, hovering storm rings, and the Master Rod descending from his hands.
- Continuity notes: Promised that a race to the top of Mount Master would decide the next wielder of the Master Rod.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/the-master-character.png`

## Seven Elite Climbers

- Role: supporting collective of top troll climbers.
- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod".
- Visual identifiers: seven athletic shaggy trolls with bell-shaped heads, horns, and red eyes, each marked by a different fur color and climbing gear; Purple Climber is the fastest and most feared member.
- Continuity notes: Their tradition says the Master Rod race must be open to the best climbers, even when Purple's magic gives her an unfair advantage.

## Troll Land

- Role: threatened utopian community.
- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod".
- Visual identifiers: impossible bright paradise of green grass, pink flowers, rivers flowing uphill in glittering arcs, villages of horned shaggy trolls, and huge mountains under a clean blue sky.
- Continuity notes: Troll Land remains peaceful after Purple becomes a generous rod-bearer and after future generations inherit the Master Rod.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/troll-land-setting.png`

## Mount Master

- Role: sacred race site and home of The Master.
- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod".
- Visual identifiers: the largest mountain in Troll Land, impossibly tall with bright cliffs, flowered lower slopes, glowing storm crown, uphill rivers climbing its sides, and a divine white summit where The Master appears.
- Continuity notes: The Master Rod descends from its summit when the promised race begins.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/mount-master-setting.png`

## Master Rod

- Role: mythic key item and divine power source.
- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod".
- Visual identifiers: tall white-and-gold lightning staff with a glowing blue-white core, forked crown, storm rings, troll-horn motifs, and controlled lightning that can lift, heal, and protect.
- Continuity notes: Held by The Master, claimed by Purple Climber, then passed peacefully to later trolls across generations.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/master-rod-key-item.png`
