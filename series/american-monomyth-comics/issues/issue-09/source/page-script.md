## Global Prompt Rules

Every image must be a full colorful anime-inspired superhero comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp crisp line art, bright glow effects, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. All readable story text must be generated directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Blank text boxes are not acceptable. Speech bubbles, dialogue balloons, thought bubbles, floating dialogue text, manga bubble tails, watermarks, and real-world logos are not allowed.

Use the supplied visual reference direction for the issue: Vinaura has sleek black-and-silver cyber-fairy armor with hot magenta and emerald glow, translucent fairy wings, pink-green hair, white winglike ear fins, and ribboning thorn-vines of magenta and green energy. Rotspire is a towering black-green living-pollution abomination with toxic glowing green orbs, rotten root-like tendrils, fungus growths, sludge, corroded pipe spines, and a skull-like polluted face.
