Create the next issue of American Monomyth Comics from this premise:

Rotspire was an abomination born from sewer pollution, and he overran the city with sewer mutants like giant rats and alligators.
Unlike the other sewer mutants, Rotspire was literal living pollution, and he possessed the ability to telepathically control every creature twisted by the sewer's poison.
The cyberpunk city was called Cyberdon, a futuristic utopia that unknowingly polluted the world around it.
Rotspire drew power from the continual flow of pollution pouring into the sewer.
The hero was named Vinaura, and she possessed the power to control and manipulate vines.
She was a cyber fairy from the wilderness who lived as a wandering hero.
Vinaura saw Rotspire's rampage and watched Cyberdon's futuristic weapons fail to harm him.
Just when it seemed like all hope was lost, Vinaura ventured into the sewer in search of the source of the pollution flow.
She fought her way through sewer mutants and found an inflow pipe gushing toxic waste.
She used her powers to summon a thick clog of vines and sealed the pipe shut.
With the continual flow of toxic waste that powered Rotspire gone, Rotspire was ultimately defeated.
Vinaura informed the people of Cyberdon about the sewer pipe, and the people quickly rushed to build a proper filtration system to stop any future pollution.
The people created a monument in Vinaura's honor and requested that she stay with them.
While Vinaura possessed advanced technology built into her armor, she had always felt a deeper connection to nature.
She returned to the wilderness beyond Cyberdon to continue wandering in search of those in need.
Peace in Cyberdon lasted for generations, and over time the people eventually forgot about Vinaura.
Forgetting the hero was the cost of peace.
