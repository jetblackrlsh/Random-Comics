# Character Bible: The Vines Beneath Cyberdon

All issue art uses a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Use crisp line art, heroic silhouettes, neon cyberpunk city light, emerald vine magic, sickly toxic-green villain glow, dynamic sewer action, expressive anime faces, and very clear readable action. Avoid muddy realism, dull gray sludge pages, horror gore, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, real-world logos, and real-world political symbols.

## Vinaura

- Role: protagonist, exceptional hero, wandering cyber fairy.
- Visual identifiers: Athletic young adult cyber fairy with warm tan skin, sharp emerald eyes, long flowing hair streaked green and pink, translucent circuit-veined fairy wings, sleek black-and-silver cyber armor with bright magenta and emerald glowing panels, winglike white ear fins, luminous vine filigree on gauntlets and boots, and a fierce protective expression.
- Powers: Controls vines, roots, tendrils, leaves, and living green growth. Her armor can scan toxins, filter air, project wing light, and resist poison for a limited time.
- Palette: Emerald green, hot magenta, silver, white light, black armor panels, and living vine glow.
- Continuity: Saves Cyberdon by sealing the toxic inflow pipe, refuses to become the city's permanent guardian, and returns to the wilderness.
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/vinaura-character.png`

## Rotspire

- Role: antagonist, living pollution abomination.
- Visual identifiers: Towering humanoid mass of black-green sludge and rotten root-like pollution, corroded pipe spines, toxic foam shoulders, fungus-like growths, cracked concrete armor plates, glowing acid-green waste orbs, sewer-grate chest cavity, dripping clawed hands, long tendrils, and a skull-like face suggested by molten waste light rather than normal skin.
- Powers: Grows stronger from flowing toxic waste, reforms when damaged, and telepathically controls sewer creatures twisted by poison.
- Palette: Black sludge, acid green, rust orange, oil-slick rainbow highlights, warning red glints.
- Continuity: Born from Cyberdon's hidden sewer pollution and defeated when Vinaura seals the pipe that feeds him.
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/rotspire-and-sewer-mutants-character.png`

## Sewer Mutants

- Role: collective antagonist army and victims of pollution.
- Visual identifiers: Giant rats with bristling oily fur and glowing green scars, huge sewer alligators with armored toxic scales, warped fish and insects in the background, all with acid-green eyes while Rotspire controls them.
- Continuity: Twisted by sewer poison and telepathically commanded by Rotspire. Once the toxic flow stops, surviving mutants lose his control and some can be healed.
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/rotspire-and-sewer-mutants-character.png`

## People of Cyberdon

- Role: threatened community and restored civic body.
- Visual identifiers: Diverse futuristic civilians, transit workers, schoolchildren, nurses, engineers, city guards, sanitation crews, and builders in bright practical cyberpunk clothing with cyan, yellow, magenta, and white accents.
- Continuity: They unknowingly benefit from a hidden waste system. After Vinaura reveals the toxic inflow pipe, they build a visible filtration system and maintain peace for generations.

## Cyberdon

- Role: threatened utopian city setting.
- Visual identifiers: Futuristic cyberpunk city with clean glass towers, neon sky rails, vertical gardens, luminous clinics, schools, public plazas, holographic civic signs with fictional symbols, and hidden storm drains leading to old toxic sewers.
- Palette: Cyan, magenta, white, gold, emerald garden lights, with toxic green creeping in from below.
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/cyberdon-setting.png`

## Toxic Inflow Pipe

- Role: key item and source of the crisis.
- Visual identifiers: Massive rusted sewer pipe pouring neon green toxic waste, warning stripes, cracked concrete walls, corroded bolts, chemical mist, and later a dense braided plug of living emerald vines sealing the opening.
- Continuity: Feeds Rotspire until Vinaura seals it. Its exposure forces Cyberdon to build a proper transparent filtration system.
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/toxic-inflow-pipe-key-item.png`
