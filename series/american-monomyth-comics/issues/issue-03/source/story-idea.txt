Create the next issue of American Monomyth Comics based on this premise:

A utopian city is threatened by a gang leader who uses Super Steroid Z to give himself superhuman strength. Calling himself Z-Man, he takes over the city. Z-Man's incredible strength makes him completely immune to bullets and turns him into a mighty one-man army.

Y-Man, a stranger to the city, is horrified by the destruction he sees around him. When he learns that Z-Man is taking Super Steroid Z, he feels personally responsible for the chaos. This is because Y-Man created the original Super Steroid Y, which he used to use to grant himself similar abilities in order to protect the innocent.

But Super Steroid Y was extremely addictive and only lasted for an hour at a time. Y-Man has not used Super Steroid Y in years in an effort to become sober and fight his addiction. He knows that unless he takes Super Steroid Y now, he will have no chance of beating Z-Man.

Y-Man takes Super Steroid Y, which restores his youth and provides him with even greater strength than Z-Man. Y-Man overpowers Z-Man and prepares to destroy Z-Man's supply of Super Steroid Z.

Z-Man pleads with Y-Man to join forces with him, so that the two can combine both Super Steroids into a godlike formula they could use to rule the entire world. Y-Man refuses, knowing the destructive power of addiction all too well. He destroys the supply of Super Steroid Z and the last of his own supply of Super Steroid Y.

Without their Super Steroids, both men lose their power. Y-Man regresses to his natural elderly age. His heart cannot handle the transition like it used to, and he dies. Seeing Y-Man die scares Z-Man straight, causing him to swear to never do drugs again.

The utopian city is restored as it celebrates and mourns the loss of its hero. Eventually peace leads to Y-Man's memory fading away into obscurity.
