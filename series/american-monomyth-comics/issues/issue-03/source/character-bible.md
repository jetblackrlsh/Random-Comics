# Character Bible

## Visual Style

All issue art uses a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Use crisp line art, strong superhero anatomy, dramatic perspective, expressive anime faces, dynamic motion, glowing chemical power, radiant utopian city light, and clear readable action. Avoid photorealistic documentary styling, muddy colors, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, real-world logos, and real-world political symbols.

All readable story text must appear inside rectangular caption boxes generated directly inside the image art. Do not use speech balloons, thought balloons, manga bubble tails, or unboxed spoken or thought text.

## Y-Man

- Role: protagonist, exceptional hero, elderly former superhero, recovering addict, creator of Super Steroid Y.
- First appears: Issue #3, "The Last Hour of Y-Man".
- Elderly visual identifiers: slender elderly man with deep-set kind eyes, silver-white swept-back hair, lined face, neat gray travel coat, white shirt, dark trousers, worn leather gloves, and a small hidden belt case for the final blue-gold vial.
- Restored heroic visual identifiers: youthful tall muscular superhero with the same silver-white hair now bright and windblown, blue-and-gold bodysuit with a clean white Y-shaped chest emblem, golden-blue aura, luminous veins like lightning under skin, protective open-handed fighting posture, and grief under the heroic confidence.
- Power language: golden-blue strength aura, hourglass-like light motes, heavy impact rings, restoration glow that looks miraculous but unstable.
- Emotional arc: wants sobriety and anonymity, accepts one final dose to stop the harm descended from his invention, refuses Z-Man's offer of godlike power, destroys both formulas, and dies as an elderly man after saving Edenbright.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/y-man-character.png`

## Z-Man

- Role: antagonist, gang leader, chemically empowered tyrant, cautionary mirror of Y-Man.
- First appears: Issue #3, "The Last Hour of Y-Man".
- Visual identifiers: huge hyper-muscular gang leader with jagged black hair, red chemically glowing eyes, cracked red-orange vein patterns across swollen arms and neck, black armored street coat torn open over a red Z-shaped chest harness, heavy boots, spiked belt, and a predatory grin that collapses into fear after withdrawal.
- Power language: red-orange steroid aura, shockwave fists, bullet-flattening skin, concrete-cracking stomp waves, brute-force motion.
- Emotional arc: uses Super Steroid Z to conquer Edenbright, tempts Y-Man with a combined god-form formula, loses his strength, sees Y-Man die, and swears to abandon drugs after understanding the cost.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/z-man-character.png`

## Edenbright City

- Role: threatened utopian community and restored society.
- First appears: Issue #3, "The Last Hour of Y-Man".
- Visual identifiers: luminous clean city of white glass towers, vertical sky gardens, blue transit ribbons, solar crowns, free clinics, school terraces, public art plazas, soft gold civic lights, transparent council halls, and flowering memorial parks.
- Continuity notes: Designed for cooperation and peace, not war. Z-Man's conquest scars civic plazas and public systems, then Y-Man's sacrifice lets the city rebuild so fully that his memory eventually fades into quiet peace.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/edenbright-city-setting.png`

## Super Steroid Y

- Role: Y-Man's original addictive power serum and final heroic temptation.
- First appears: Issue #3, "The Last Hour of Y-Man".
- Visual identifiers: small glass vial of luminous blue-gold serum with a white Y insignia, hourglass-shaped inner glow, polished metal cap, and warning bands; when active, it creates golden-blue strength aura and temporary youth.
- Continuity notes: Grants greater strength than Super Steroid Z for exactly one hour, restores Y-Man's youth temporarily, is extremely addictive, and is destroyed by Y-Man after his final use.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/super-steroid-y-key-item.png`

## Super Steroid Z

- Role: Z-Man's addictive mass-produced power serum and the threat source.
- First appears: Issue #3, "The Last Hour of Y-Man".
- Visual identifiers: crates of red-orange vials with black Z markings, harsh hazard lights, thick glowing fluid, cracked injection ampoules, and chemical smoke; when active, it creates red-orange muscle growth, bullet immunity, and violent strength.
- Continuity notes: Derived from the formula lineage of Super Steroid Y, used by Z-Man to become a one-man army, and destroyed by Y-Man before it can be combined with Super Steroid Y.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/super-steroid-z-key-item.png`
