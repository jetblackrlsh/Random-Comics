# The Last Hour of Y-Man

## Issue Number

3

## Title

The Last Hour of Y-Man

## Logline

When a steroid-fueled gang emperor conquers a utopian city, an elderly former hero risks one final relapse into his own addictive power serum to save the innocent, destroy both formulas, and die before the city can truly know him.

## Theme

Power that depends on addiction cannot save a soul; the truest heroism is destroying the thing that makes heroism easy when it would also make tyranny possible.

## Moral Tension

Y-Man knows that taking Super Steroid Y will make him powerful enough to stop Z-Man, but it also reopens the addiction that nearly consumed his life. The issue asks whether a person in recovery can ethically use the source of their ruin to save others, and whether victory still matters if no one remembers the cost.

## Hero Desire

Y-Man wants to remain sober, anonymous, and free from the chemical identity that once made him famous and hollow.

## Hero Burden

Y-Man created the formula lineage that led to Super Steroid Z. He believes Z-Man's conquest is partly his responsibility, and he can only stop it by touching the substance he has spent years refusing.

## Threatened Community

Edenbright City is a clean, luminous, cooperative utopia of sky gardens, public transit towers, art plazas, schools, clinics, and glass civic halls. It is worth saving because its peace is ordinary, shared, and vulnerable.

## Mythic Evil

Z-Man turns chemical strength into political domination. His body becomes a one-man army, but his deeper threat is the belief that craving, conquest, and escalation should rule everyone.

## Why Institutions Fail

Edenbright's police, medics, civic drones, council guards, and emergency shields are designed for a peaceful society. They can protect people from normal disasters, but bullets, barricades, and policy fail against a man whose chemically swollen body ignores every conventional response.

## Hero's Exceptional Quality

Y-Man has already survived the temptation that defines the villain. He understands Super Steroid power from the inside, knows its time limit, and has the moral strength to destroy both Z and Y when Z-Man offers godlike rule.

## Redemptive Action

Y-Man wins by refusing escalation. He uses one last hour of strength to overpower Z-Man, then destroys every remaining vial of Super Steroid Z and the final vial of Super Steroid Y instead of combining them.

## Restoration

Without the drug supply, Z-Man loses his power and is emotionally broken by witnessing Y-Man's death. Edenbright rebuilds, celebrates, mourns, and eventually returns to such deep peace that Y-Man's name fades from everyday memory.

## Why the Hero Cannot Join

Y-Man dies from the physical shock of losing the formula's temporary youth and strength. Even his memory cannot fully stay inside the restored utopia because peace gradually erases the crisis that made his sacrifice visible.

## Final Withdrawal Image

A small statue of Y-Man stands half-hidden by flowering vines in a quiet Edenbright garden, while children play nearby without knowing his name.

## 10-Step American Monomyth Outline

### Pages 2-3: The Edenic Community

Edenbright City shines as a utopian civic paradise of sky gardens, transit ribbons, free clinics, and public plazas. Page 3 focuses on ordinary people whose safety is so complete that heroism seems unnecessary, until a faint red chemical glow appears in the city's underlevels.

### Pages 4-5: Evil Threatens the Community

A gang leader injects Super Steroid Z and transforms into Z-Man, an enormous red-veined tyrant. He storms from the undercity into Edenbright's civic plaza, smashes public defenses, and declares himself ruler through pure strength.

### Pages 6-7: Ordinary Institutions Fail

Police, shield drones, medics, and council security try to contain Z-Man. Bullets flatten against his skin, barricades bend, and emergency systems meant for a gentle city collapse because Z-Man is a chemically empowered one-man army.

### Pages 8-9: The Community Becomes Helpless

Families, transit riders, clinic patients, and schoolchildren shelter beneath cracked glass while Z-Man's gang takes food, medicine, and power hubs. An elderly traveler named Y-Man sees the devastation and recognizes Super Steroid Z as a descendant of his own Super Steroid Y.

### Pages 10-11: The Exceptional Hero Appears

Y-Man reveals his past: he once used Super Steroid Y to protect the innocent, but the one-hour miracle became an addiction that almost destroyed him. He has stayed sober for years, yet now accepts that Edenbright has no other defender.

### Pages 12-13: The Hero Resists Ordinary Limitations

Y-Man takes his last vial of Super Steroid Y. The formula restores his youth, ignites golden-blue power, and gives him strength beyond Z-Man's, but the clock begins immediately and every heartbeat threatens his recovery and his life.

### Pages 14-15: Redemptive Force Confronts Evil

Young Y-Man confronts Z-Man in a titanic battle across Edenbright's towers and plazas. Y-Man overpowers him, reaches the Super Steroid Z supply, and faces Z-Man's offer to combine Y and Z into a godlike world-ruling formula.

### Pages 16-17: The Community Is Restored

Y-Man refuses the offer and destroys Super Steroid Z along with the final Super Steroid Y vial. Z-Man loses his strength. Y-Man regresses to elderly age and dies from the transition, frightening Z-Man into a vow of sobriety.

### Pages 18-19: The Hero Does Not Fully Join the Community

Edenbright mourns Y-Man as its unknown savior, while Z-Man, powerless and shaken, surrenders. The city rebuilds with gardens, clinics, schools, and transparent memorial ceremonies, but Y-Man is absent from the world he restored.

### Pages 20-21: The Hero Withdraws or Waits

Years pass. Peace deepens until fewer people remember the crisis clearly. Y-Man's memorial becomes small and overgrown, not because the city is ungrateful, but because his sacrifice succeeded so completely that fear no longer organizes their lives.

### Page 22: Poster-Style Back Cover Summary

The back cover frames Y-Man and Z-Man as mirrored chemical titans around shattered vials, Edenbright rebuilt behind them, and an overgrown memorial below. The poster's central moral is that the last dose saved the city because Y-Man chose to end the formula instead of worship it.
