# Character Bible

## Visual Style

All issue art uses a colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired American superhero comic aesthetic. Use crisp line art, heroic silhouettes, candy-bright environments, expressive food-being faces, sparkling sugar surfaces, fruit-colored hero energy, oversized ant danger, speed lines from roller skates, and clear readable action.

All readable story text must appear inside rectangular caption boxes generated directly inside the image art. Do not use speech balloons, thought balloons, manga bubble tails, floating dialogue, or unboxed spoken or thought text.

## Orange

- Role: protagonist and exceptional hero.
- First appears: Issue #7, "The Orange Hero of Candy World".
- Visual identifiers: athletic anthropomorphic orange fruit hero with bright orange peel texture, expressive heroic eyes, white gloves, green-and-white sweatbands on wrists and forehead, compact muscular limbs, blue-and-white roller skates with fruit-leaf emblems, and a confident protective grin.
- Power language: citrus-gold strength aura, green healthy-energy streaks, roller-skate speed trails, balanced athletic poses, lifting heavy debris, and controlled movement that protects civilians.
- Emotional arc: arrives as a joyful disciplined outsider, saves Candy World without mocking it, inspires healthier habits, then leaves before praise turns into dependence.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/orange-character.png`

## Candy World Denizens

- Role: threatened innocent community.
- First appears: Issue #7, "The Orange Hero of Candy World".
- Visual identifiers: friendly gingerbread men with icing faces, translucent gummy bears in jewel colors, chocolate bunnies with candy-button clothes, frosting hats, sprinkle accessories, soft round shapes, and expressive frightened or joyful faces.
- Continuity notes: Begin kind and harmonious but sugar-dependent, weak, and easily exhausted. After Orange saves them, they add healthy food and daily exercise to their culture and become stronger over generations.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/candy-world-denizens-character.png`

## Giant Sugar Ants

- Role: collective antagonist and mythic invasion.
- First appears: Issue #7, "The Orange Hero of Candy World".
- Visual identifiers: enormous armored sugar ants with glossy red-brown shells, crystal-sugar mandibles, bright hungry eyes, segmented bodies as large as houses, clawed legs gripping candy streets, and sugar grains stuck to their armor.
- Power language: lifting giant sugar cubes and candy towers ten times their size, ripping open gingerbread walls, swarming along glowing sugar trails, and shaking the ground under candy citizens.
- Continuity notes: Invade Candy World to eat its sugar. Orange defeats them by using strength, speed, and discipline to lure and trap them away from civilians.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/giant-sugar-ants-character.png`

## Candy World

- Role: threatened utopian setting.
- First appears: Issue #7, "The Orange Hero of Candy World".
- Visual identifiers: gingerbread houses, frosting roads, gumdrop gardens, sugar fountains, chocolate bridges, lollipop lampposts, candy-cane arches, bright skies, and cheerful candy citizens living together peacefully.
- Continuity notes: Nearly collapses because sugar abundance has made its citizens weak. After Orange saves it, Candy World becomes stronger through healthier food, exercise paths, fruit gardens, and balanced traditions.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/candy-world-setting.png`

## Fruit World

- Role: Orange's healthy homeland and contrast to Candy World.
- First appears: Issue #7, "The Orange Hero of Candy World".
- Visual identifiers: bright orchards, fruit-shaped homes, vegetable gardens, training paths, smoothie waterfalls, clean sunlight, athletic fruit citizens, and green-and-gold healthy energy.
- Continuity notes: Orange's daily discipline and healthy diet come from Fruit World culture.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/fruit-world-setting.png`

## Orange's Roller Skates

- Role: key item and movement tool.
- First appears: Issue #7, "The Orange Hero of Candy World".
- Visual identifiers: blue-and-white heroic roller skates with citrus-green wheels, leaf emblems, polished silver axles, and glowing speed trails.
- Continuity notes: Let Orange evade the giant sugar ants, circle the swarm, and lure them into the rock-candy canyon without being caught.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/oranges-roller-skates-key-item.png`
