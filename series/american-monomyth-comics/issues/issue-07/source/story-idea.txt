Create the next issue of American Monomyth Comics from this premise:

Candy World is a utopia where gingerbread men, gummy bears, and chocolate bunnies live together in harmony.
Everyone in Candy World eats sugar, which makes them lazy and weak.
Giant sugar ants invade and start to eat all of the sugar.
The Candy World denizens try to fight back, but they lack the strength needed.
Ants can lift things ten times their size.
Their destructive power tears through gingerbread houses with ease.
Just when all hope seems lost, an Orange from Fruit World arrives.
Orange eats only healthy food, which makes him incredibly strong.
Orange exercises everyday and always wears sweatbands.
Orange rolls around on roller skates, allowing him to easily evade the attacks of the giant sugar ants.
With his incredible strength, Orange takes down the giant sugar ants and saves Candy World.
The candy beings of Candy World are inspired by Orange and decide to start eating healthier.
They honor Orange with a monument, but he does not stay to bask in the limelight.
Orange skates off on his next adventure to protect those in need.
Over generations the Candy World became a stronger and healthier place, but with peace the memory of Orange the Hero eventually faded away.
