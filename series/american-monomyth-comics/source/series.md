# American Monomyth Comics

## Logline

Self-contained 22-page anime-inspired superhero comics built around the American Monomyth: a community is threatened, ordinary systems fail, an exceptional hero restores order, and the hero remains apart.

## Premise

American Monomyth Comics is an anthology-style series of complete superhero stories. Every issue uses a fixed 22-page structure: a full-art cover, 20 story pages paced as two pages for each of the 10 American Monomyth beats, and a poster-style back cover that summarizes the whole issue.

Each issue should be readable in isolation. Issues may share continuity, recurring characters, crossover elements, or long-running consequences, but a reader should not need earlier issues to understand the central community, threat, hero, conflict, resolution, and final separation of the current issue.

## Tone

Mythic superhero action, bright anime emotion, dramatic rescue stakes, iconic power fantasy, and bittersweet separation. The stories can be sincere, critical, hopeful, tragic, rebellious, or unsettling, but each issue should make the hero's exceptional role and final distance from the restored community emotionally clear.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Colorful, high-saturation, high-detail, sharp, bright-glow, modern anime-inspired superhero comic aesthetic.
- American superhero comic energy: strong anatomy, heroic posing, dramatic perspective, bold silhouettes, iconic costumes, and epic scale.
- Modern anime dynamism: expressive faces, speed lines, impact frames, emotional close-ups, stylized energy effects, and cinematic action angles.
- Bright digital color: vivid power palettes, glowing shields, portals, auras, energy blasts, technology, and sunrise or city-light restoration imagery.
- Clear action and readable panel flow even during large battles.
- Full-art covers should be pure artistic statements and should not require title text.
- Back covers should be poster-style summaries with any tagline, theme, or recap text generated directly inside the image.

## Continuity Notes

- The 22-page American Monomyth page structure is mandatory for every issue.
- The cover does not count as one of the 10 story steps.
- The back cover is a creative poster-summary page, not an extra story beat.
- Series reference images for this series use the same bright anime-inspired superhero style as the issues, not the repo's default realistic candid photo-comic style.
- Every issue should stand alone, even when it uses returning heroes, villains, settings, artifacts, or crossover continuity.
