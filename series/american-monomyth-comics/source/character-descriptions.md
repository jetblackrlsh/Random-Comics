# Character Descriptions

## Character: Datacenter Giants

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Invisible Giants"
- Role: collective protagonist and exceptional hero.
- Visual identifiers: In heroic form, luminous humanoid server titans with clean white and cobalt armor, transparent coolant rivers through their torsos, green water-positive halos, solar-gold shoulder plates, blue-white data hearts, fiber-optic capes, and calm protective expressions. In monstrous form, massive datacenter beasts with cooling-tower horns, cable tendrils, cracked concrete armor, vent mouths, red warning eyes, leaking coolant, and jagged server-rack ribs.
- Continuity notes: Public fear first mutates datacenters into monsters. Public gratitude and understanding revive them as heroes who save the nation, repair their environmental and energy harms, then withdraw back into ordinary infrastructure.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/datacenter-giants-character.png`

## Character: Blackwire Legion

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Invisible Giants"
- Role: antagonist robot army launched by an unnamed rival power.
- Visual identifiers: Matte black humanoid robot soldiers with angular red sensor slits, segmented armor, drone backpacks, cable-whip rifles, triangular fictional invasion emblems with no real-world national symbols, and synchronized formation movement.
- Continuity notes: The legion strikes when the United States is weakened by datacenter destruction and infrastructure collapse. The Datacenter Giants disable them through restored networks, precision power, and infrastructure protection.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/blackwire-legion-character.png`

## Character: Civic Chorus

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Invisible Giants"
- Role: collective supporting character representing everyday people across the country.
- Visual identifiers: Diverse civilians, nurses, teachers, farmers, firefighters, students, small-business owners, elderly residents, children, and families shown in homes, hospitals, streets, farms, and public shelters.
- Continuity notes: Their fear helps create the datacenter monsters; their regret, gratitude, and hope revive the Datacenter Giants as heroes.
