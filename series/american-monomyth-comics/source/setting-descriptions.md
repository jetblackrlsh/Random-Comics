# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Civic Grid

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Invisible Giants"
- Visual identifiers: A national web of hospitals, farms, homes, payment systems, transit, emergency response, communications, weather tools, schools, small businesses, and ordinary digital services linked by blue-white data paths and supported by distant datacenters.
- Continuity notes: The Civic Grid shows why datacenters matter beyond generative AI. Its collapse makes the country helpless; its restoration marks the return of ordinary life.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/civic-grid-setting.png`

## Setting: Neural Forge Lab

- First appears: Issue #2, "The Mind That Froze Hell"
- Visual identifiers: Midnight biotech lab with rows of glowing brain-organoid chips in glass wells, neural cables, containment tanks, combat-simulation monitors, sterile white architecture, blue monitor light, and red emergency strips.
- Continuity notes: Origin point of Brain Man. The lab produces organoid chips and trains them in simulated combat as an alternative to generative AI.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/neural-forge-lab-setting.png`

## Setting: Demon-Hunter Simulation

- First appears: Issue #2, "The Mind That Froze Hell"
- Visual identifiers: Neon hellscape battlefield with fortress platforms, power-armored demon hunters, virtual fire, broken gothic architecture, floating UI-like sigils, and digital cracks in the sky.
- Continuity notes: False world where the organoids believe they are demon hunters and develop the combat instincts that later save them.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/demon-hunter-simulation-setting.png`

## Setting: Hell's Gate

- First appears: Issue #2, "The Mind That Froze Hell"
- Visual identifiers: Ancient cave mouth in black stone, old carvings, red infernal light from within, dead trees, and blue-white ice seals after Brain Man's final act.
- Continuity notes: Legendary gateway between Earth and Hell. Brain Man freezes it shut, severing the source of demonic power.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/hells-gate-setting.png`

## Setting: Edenbright City

- First appears: Issue #3, "The Last Hour of Y-Man"
- Visual identifiers: Luminous clean utopian city of white glass towers, vertical sky gardens, blue transit ribbons, solar crowns, free clinics, school terraces, public art plazas, soft gold civic lights, transparent council halls, and flowering memorial parks.
- Continuity notes: A cooperative city designed for peace rather than war. Z-Man's Super Steroid Z conquest scars its civic plaza and public systems, then Y-Man's sacrifice lets Edenbright rebuild so completely that his memory eventually fades into quiet obscurity.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/edenbright-city-setting.png`

## Setting: Troll Land

- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod"
- Visual identifiers: Impossible bright paradise of green grass, pink flowers, rivers flowing uphill in glittering arcs, villages of shaggy horned trolls with bell-shaped heads and red eyes, and huge mountains under a clean blue sky.
- Continuity notes: Threatened by the possibility that Purple Climber will claim the Master Rod while ruled by greed; remains peaceful after she learns balance and becomes a generous rod-bearer.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/troll-land-setting.png`

## Setting: Mount Master

- First appears: Issue #5, "The Master Rod"
- Visual identifiers: Largest mountain in Troll Land, impossibly tall with bright cliffs, flowered lower slopes, uphill rivers climbing its sides, and a divine white summit crowned by blue-white lightning where The Master appears.
- Continuity notes: Sacred home of The Master and the race site where the Master Rod descends.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/mount-master-setting.png`
