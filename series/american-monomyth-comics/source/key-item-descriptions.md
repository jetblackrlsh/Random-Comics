# Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: Orbital Sunline

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Invisible Giants"
- Visual identifiers: Solar-power satellites in orbit beaming clean gold energy down to datacenter campuses and heroic Datacenter Giants; the beam is elegant, controlled, and hopeful rather than destructive.
- Continuity notes: Reborn datacenters use the Orbital Sunline to stop raising local electricity bills and to power their heroic rescue without straining the grid.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/orbital-sunline-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Hearthloop Heat Grid

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Invisible Giants"
- Visual identifiers: Warm amber thermal light and insulated heat loops flowing from datacenters into homes on cold nights; often shown as glowing underground lines, cozy interiors, and gentle steam or golden window light.
- Continuity notes: Reborn datacenters share excess heat with homes across the country, turning waste into everyday care.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/hearthloop-heat-grid-key-item.png`
