# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The Invisible Giants

- Folder: `series/american-monomyth-comics/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: When weaponized public rage turns America's datacenters into monsters and their destruction collapses the systems everyone depends on, a national change of heart revives them as quiet heroes who save the country, repair their harms, and return to invisible service.
- Plot summary: Everyday life depends on datacenters for hospitals, farms, homes, emergency response, payments, communications, and more. Foreign-origin propaganda turns public feeling against them until negative emotion mutates datacenter campuses into monsters. The military destroys the monsters but also destroys the infrastructure underneath ordinary life. As systems fail, the Blackwire Legion attacks the weakened nation. People realize datacenters were not the true enemy, and their gratitude revives the ruins as heroic Datacenter Giants. The giants restore networks, defeat the robots, become water positive, draw power from the Orbital Sunline, share excess heat through the Hearthloop Heat Grid, and finally withdraw back into ordinary background infrastructure.
- Continuity changes: Establishes American Monomyth Comics as a 22-page anime-inspired superhero anthology. Establishes the Datacenter Giants, Blackwire Legion, Civic Chorus, Civic Grid, Orbital Sunline, and Hearthloop Heat Grid.
- New characters: Datacenter Giants; Blackwire Legion; Civic Chorus.
- New settings: Civic Grid.
- New key items: Orbital Sunline; Hearthloop Heat Grid.
