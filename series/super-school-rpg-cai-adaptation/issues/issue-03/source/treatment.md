# Treatment

## Title

Shadowgarde Field Trip

## Issue Number

3

## Logline

After the Simulation Dome names Jack, Aurora, and Blaze the Reality Breakers, Professor Lightman sends them on their first real Field Trip mission to stop a gravity crisis in Shadowgarde City.

## Central Conflict

The Reality Breakers want adventures with real meaning, but their first chance outside training immediately tests whether they can protect people, respect hero ethics, and work as a team when the danger is no longer simulated.

## Theme

Heroism is measured by character under pressure, not just power under controlled conditions.

## Ending

The team arrives in Shadowgarde City and sees the gravity crisis firsthand. A torn chunk of street rises toward them before they can finish spreading out, forcing their first true mission to begin with immediate action and no simulation safety net.

## Emotional Arc

Jack starts excited by the idea of meaningful, higher-stakes missions. Blaze begins as the practical upperclassman who understands the ranking system, while Aurora balances playfulness with moral clarity. Professor Lightman reframes their victory as evidence of judgment, not just power. By the end, the trio has accepted that becoming respected heroes means facing real consequences together.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, the Reality Breakers team name, explanation of Field Trips and rankings, Jack's desire for higher stakes, Lightman's moral-test warning, the Shadowgarde assignment, the teleportation chamber commitment, and the arrival in Shadowgarde City as the gravity crisis begins.
