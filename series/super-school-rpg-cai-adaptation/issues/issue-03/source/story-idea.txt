Issue Three: Shadowgarde Field Trip

Story Page 1
The simulation dome falls silent as the shattered remains of the Lava Leviathan scatter harmlessly across the floor. A calm system voice announces the team's victory and officially records the trio under a new name: the Reality Breakers. Jack Blue rises with Blaze Carter and Miss Aurora, realizing that this training exercise has done more than test their powers. It has proven that their abilities complement each other in a way that makes them stronger as a unit. With the battle over, Jack turns his attention away from the simulation and toward the bigger question of what comes next.

Story Page 2
Jack asks Blaze whether students at Power Academy ever get to go on real missions instead of staying inside training exercises. Blaze explains that the school offers special assignments called Field Trips, which send promising students into real situations where lives and consequences are at stake. He makes it clear that these missions are not handed out casually, because only the highest ranked students usually earn the chance to go. According to Blaze, students climb the hero ranking system by performing well in combat simulations and by proving themselves in moral tests. Miss Aurora adds that every mission, every classroom decision, and every public action can shape a student's reputation as a future hero.

Story Page 3
Jack admits that the ranking system sounds difficult, but that challenge only makes it more appealing to him. He explains that he wants the Reality Breakers to rise through the ranks because real missions would give their adventures greater meaning and higher stakes. Jack also reflects that the Lava Leviathan battle felt like a strong second issue in his growing comic-inspired view of their lives, but it was still protected by the safety of a simulation. Before the conversation can go any further, the dome doors open and Professor Lightman steps inside with a datapad in his hand. His expression shows that he has been watching not just their victory, but the choices that led to it.

Story Page 4
Professor Lightman congratulates the team on their performance and tells them that their teamwork was more impressive than he expected. He confirms Jack's suspicions by explaining that hero rankings are shaped by two things above all else: performance in simulations and conduct in moral tests. A student can be powerful and still fail if they show poor judgment, selfishness, or a willingness to sacrifice innocent people. Because of that, the professor says, the path to becoming a respected hero requires both strength and character. Then he offers the Reality Breakers something unusual for students at their level: a real Field Trip mission.

Story Page 5
As the team follows Professor Lightman through the academy halls, he reveals the details of their assignment. Their destination is Shadowgarde, the dark sister academy of Power Academy, located in the towering city that shares its name. There, a rogue Shadowgarde student with unstable gravity powers has begun endangering civilians and tearing apart part of the city. Professor Lightman calls the mission low-stakes by hero standards, but only because the threat comes from a student rather than a veteran supervillain. He quickly strips away any comfort in those words by warning them that the danger is still real, and that failure could get innocent people killed.

Story Page 6
Jack realizes immediately that this mission is more than a chance to see the outside world. It is a living moral test designed to measure whether the Reality Breakers can neutralize a threat without abandoning their ethics. He accepts the mission without hesitation, but only after making clear that he wants Blaze and Aurora beside him when they face the crisis. Miss Aurora's playful confidence fades into focused determination, while Blaze shifts into planning mode the moment the stakes become real. Together, the three students step into the academy's teleportation chamber as the first true mission of the Reality Breakers begins.

Story Page 7
A burst of blue light carries the team across the world and drops them into the heart of Shadowgarde City. The skyline is filled with obsidian towers, distorted air, floating debris, and terrified civilians clinging to unstable ground as gravity bends unnaturally around them. In the distance, a lone rogue student hovers above the chaos, wrapped in dark energy and clearly responsible for the destruction below. Blaze orders the team to move fast, Aurora prepares to shield civilians, and Jack studies the battlefield as he prepares to counter the enemy's warped power. Before the Reality Breakers can fully spread out, a massive chunk of pavement tears free from the street and hurtles straight toward them, beginning their first real mission with no room for hesitation.
