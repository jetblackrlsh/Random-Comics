# Treatment

## Title

Reality Breakers Recap

## Issue Number

5

## Logline

Jack Blue breaks the fourth wall to give readers a complete catch-up issue covering the Reality Breakers' origin, powers, personalities, motivations, major victories, and current story status.

## Central Conflict

The issue is not a new external battle; it is a clarity issue. Jack must organize four issues of events into a readable recap that explains who the Reality Breakers are, why their choices matter, and why their first real mission changed the series.

## Theme

Understanding the story means understanding both the powers and the moral choices behind them.

## Ending

Jack leaves the reader fully caught up: the Reality Breakers have gone from classroom strangers to a named team with real field experience, and their current story rests on the aftermath of Shadowgarde City's gravity crisis.

## Emotional Arc

Jack starts as a playful fourth-wall narrator inviting late readers into the story. The recap moves from wonder and humor to tactical teamwork and moral stakes. By the final page, the tone becomes focused: the reader now understands that the team is no longer only impressive but responsible.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Jack's fourth-wall catch-up invitation, Issue #1 classroom debut, cast and power guide, Lava Leviathan teamwork recap, rankings and Field Trip setup, Shadowgarde mission opening, moral victory recap, and current-status conclusion.
