# Character Bible

Use these entries for every page prompt. They cite the series continuity files and reference images for recurring elements.

## Jack Blue

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/jack-blue-character.png`
- Stable identifiers: late-teen student with medium brown skin, calm oval face, short deep-blue-black hair, clear dark eyes with faint violet reflections, slim athletic build, navy school blazer over a white shirt, cosmic-purple tie, dark trousers.
- Recap role: fourth-wall narrator who looks toward the reader, gestures to floating recap panels, and explains events with calm clarity.
- Power visuals: purple cosmic energy, starfield shimmer, cosmic slingshot, wings, shields, and floating recap pages.

## Miss Aurora

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/miss-aurora-character.png`
- Stable identifiers: late-teen girl with fair warm skin, bright blue eyes, long wavy silver-blonde hair, thoughtful expressive face, academy blazer with pale blue accents, star-shaped hair clip.
- Recap role: warm, curious teammate whose playful confidence becomes protective courage.
- Power visuals: golden sunlight shields, radiant domes, Solar Sphere Strike, bright compassionate posture.

## Blaze Carter

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/blaze-carter-character.png`
- Stable identifiers: late-teen upperclassman with warm tan skin, sharp cheekbones, amber eyes, short spiky black hair tipped with ember-red highlights, athletic build, black-and-crimson academy combat jacket with heatproof panels, fingerless gloves, scorched boots.
- Recap role: cocky energy manipulator who grows into a practical teammate.
- Power visuals: red flame aura, heat absorption streams, guarded stance beside Aurora.

## Professor Lightman

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/professor-lightman-character.png`
- Stable identifiers: middle-aged teacher with light brown skin, tidy silver hair, trimmed beard, warm sharp eyes, fitted charcoal teaching suit with gold piping, luminous lightning-bolt lapel pin.
- Recap role: mentor who explains that hero rankings require both power and character.

## Rogue Shadowgarde Gravity Student

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/rogue-shadowgarde-gravity-student-character.png`
