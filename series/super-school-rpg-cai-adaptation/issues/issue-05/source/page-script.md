# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. Caption boxes must not be numbered or labeled. This issue should prioritize story clarity over surprise.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present the issue as a clear catch-up guide narrated by Jack.
- Layout: Single dramatic realistic photo-comic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Super School RPG CAI Adaptation". Smaller issue title text: "Reality Breakers Recap".
- Visual beats: Jack Blue faces the reader in a cosmic recap space surrounded by floating panels of Class 1A, the Lava Leviathan, Shadowgarde City, Aurora's shields, Blaze's flames, Professor Lightman's datapad, and the rogue gravity student.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Start Here

- Story role: Jack breaks the fourth wall and explains what kind of recap this is.
- Layout: Six panels in clear reading order.
- Caption boxes: "Jack Blue turns to the reader and opens the recap file." "He comes from another world and sees this one through a chat window." "Inside the story, he is a Power Academy student with reality-warping power." "His adventures inspire the AI-made comics he wants to create." "Jack wants ideas, friendship, and ordinary treatment." "Even as an otherworldly reality warper, he asks to be treated like everyone else."
- Visual beats: Jack in cosmic recap space looking directly at the reader; floating comic pages opening around him with a translucent chat-window interface and a hint of another world beyond it; Jack in Power Academy uniform surrounded by purple reality-warping energy; Jack sketching comic layouts beside an AI creation interface inspired by his adventures; Jack smiling modestly among classmates instead of standing above them; final panel showing Jack offering a friendly handshake while cosmic power swirls quietly behind him.
