# Treatment

## Title

Gravity Redemption

## Issue Number

4

## Logline

The Reality Breakers survive their first moments in Shadowgarde City by protecting civilians and persuading a rogue gravity student to stand down before Shadowgarde's own responders arrive.

## Central Conflict

The team must stop a dangerous gravity crisis without becoming reckless themselves. Jack sees that the rogue student's instability is tied to wounded pride and isolation, so the mission becomes a test of whether the Reality Breakers can save people while also refusing to reduce the antagonist to a target.

## Theme

Real heroism means de-escalating when force would be easier, especially when innocent people are watching the choice.

## Ending

The rogue gravity student lowers the debris and agrees to stand down. Shadowgarde enforcement arrives too late to take credit, while the Reality Breakers recognize that their first true mission has made them more than simulation students.

## Emotional Arc

Jack begins by answering immediate danger with creative power, then shifts into moral analysis. Aurora moves from shield duty into compassionate persuasion, while Blaze stays ready to fight but respects the de-escalation plan. The rogue moves from defiant instability to wounded honesty, and the team ends with a victory defined by restraint.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, cosmic slingshot response, civilian shielding, Jack's first question, the shield-cracking crisis, Jack's ranking-system insight, the rogue's emotional surrender, and the delayed enforcement squad arriving after the Reality Breakers pass the real test.
