# Character Bible

Use these entries for every page prompt. They cite the series continuity files and reference images for recurring elements.

## Jack Blue

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/jack-blue-character.png`
- Stable identifiers: late-teen student with medium brown skin, calm oval face, short deep-blue-black hair, clear dark eyes with faint violet reflections, slim athletic build, navy school blazer over a white shirt, cosmic-purple tie, dark trousers, relaxed posture.
- Power visuals: brilliant cosmic purple energy, starfield shimmer, glowing mist, rainbow geometry, rippling shields, and large construct shapes while his expression stays focused and calm.

## Miss Aurora

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/miss-aurora-character.png`

## Blaze Carter

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/blaze-carter-character.png`
- Stable identifiers: late-teen upperclassman with warm tan skin, sharp cheekbones, amber eyes, short spiky black hair tipped with ember-red highlights, athletic build, black-and-crimson academy combat jacket with heatproof panels, fingerless gloves, scorched boots, cocky squared stance.
- Power visuals: red flame aura around arms and shoulders, superheated punches, then heat absorption shown as orange energy streaming away from the Lava Leviathan into his hands.

## Lava Leviathan Simulation

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/lava-leviathan-simulation-character.png`
- Stable identifiers: towering molten-rock creature with broad cracked obsidian shoulders, glowing lava seams, hornlike slag ridges, furnace-orange eyes, massive clawed arms, dripping magma plates, and smoke venting from its back.
- State changes: starts fluid and molten, splashes and reforms after fire attacks, then becomes blackened brittle obsidian as heat is drained away.
