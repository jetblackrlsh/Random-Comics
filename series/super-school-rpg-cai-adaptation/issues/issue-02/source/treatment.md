# Treatment

## Title

Lava Leviathan Co-Op

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

Jack Blue follows Miss Aurora into the Simulation Dome and turns Blaze Carter's fiery rivalry into a coordinated team victory over a molten holographic monster.

## Central Conflict

Blaze Carter wants to prove that raw energy manipulation makes him the academy's best, while Jack wants an immersive conflict that avoids a predictable rivalry. The Lava Leviathan forces both students to learn that firepower alone cannot beat a creature made of fire and molten rock.

## Theme

The smartest victory comes from understanding what each teammate can do, then using those strengths in the right order.

## Ending

Jack's cosmic fan chills the arena, Blaze absorbs the Leviathan's heat, and Miss Aurora finishes the hardened monster with Solar Sphere Strike. The simulation confirms victory and the trio becomes a functional team instead of a rivalry.

## Emotional Arc

Jack begins looking for an exciting second-issue conflict and refuses to treat Blaze as a simple opponent. Blaze starts cocky and dismissive, trusting only his flames. Miss Aurora begins as a playful guide, then proves she belongs in the fight. By the end, Jack has earned tactical respect, Blaze has learned to redirect his powers, and Aurora has turned friendly support into decisive action.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Simulation Dome arrival, Blaze's challenge, Blaze's failed fire attack, Jack's cosmic shield, Jack's team strategy, the Leviathan cooling into obsidian, and Aurora's Solar Sphere Strike ending the simulation.
