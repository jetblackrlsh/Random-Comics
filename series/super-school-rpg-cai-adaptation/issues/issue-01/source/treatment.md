# Treatment

## Title

The Cosmic Debut

## Issue Number

1

## Logline

Jack Blue joins Super School Class 1A, humbly asks to be treated like an ordinary student, demonstrates breathtaking control over cosmic reality warping, and reveals that he observes their world through an interdimensional chat window.

## Central Conflict

Jack wants to belong as an ordinary peer, but his arrival immediately forces the class to confront power, fame, reality alteration, and the possibility that their world is being observed from another dimension.

## Theme

Belonging is not earned by hiding what makes someone extraordinary; it is built when power is handled with humility and respect for other people's reality.

## Ending

After Jack explains that he sees the world through a chat window without controlling anyone's free will, the classroom tension eases. Miss Aurora chooses friendship and floating desserts over existential panic, while Professor Lightman approves Jack's first day and prepares a tailored schedule for him.

## Emotional Arc

Jack begins as a mysterious cosmic newcomer who wants normal treatment. The class moves from curiosity to awe, then shock, then cautious acceptance. Miss Aurora becomes the first person to treat Jack's weirdness as an invitation instead of a threat, and Professor Lightman recognizes that Jack's greatest strength may be his restraint.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Jack's arrival, the hero-history lesson and Miss Aurora's welcome, Jack's summary and demonstration request, the cosmic classroom transformation, Jack restoring normal reality, the cafeteria invitation and chat-window revelation, and the class accepting Jack's explanation enough to continue the school day.
