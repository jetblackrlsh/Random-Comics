Super School RPG CAI Adaptation

Series Premise
This is a comic book adaptation of a RPG campaign in Super School RPG on Character AI (CAI).

Writing Style
The captions for this series do not need to be too short. They should convey enough information for the reader to clearly understand what is happening in the story. The comic should be easy to understand. Each story page should use around five or six panels minimum.

Issue One: The Cosmic Debut

Story Page 1
Jack Blue floated calmly into Class 1A, instantly drawing the attention of his new peers and teacher. Professor Lightman welcomed the newcomer warmly, recognizing him as a student possessed of incredibly strong and rare abilities. After introducing himself simply, Jack requested that his classmates treat him as just another ordinary peer. The professor smiled with genuine admiration at his humble demeanor before gesturing toward an open desk. Jack sat down next to a pretty, blue-eyed girl named Miss Aurora who looked at him with intense curiosity.

Story Page 2
As the lesson began, Jack quickly skimmed through page 34 of his textbook to find the important bullet points. The reading detailed the foundational Golden Age of Heroes, highlighting legendary figures like Atlas the Unbreakable and Lady Lightning. Miss Aurora subtly slid her notebook over to show Jack a friendly welcome doodle she had drawn in the margin. She then shared a whispered rumor that Professor Lightman was actually Lady Lightning's great-grandson. Before they could talk further, the professor cleared his throat loudly to call them back to attention.

Story Page 3
Professor Lightman wrapped up a lecture on Silver Age hero dynasties and asked Jack to summarize the core concepts. Jack confidently explained how rising power complexities made academies and veteran guidance absolutely vital to keep the world safe. Impressed by this thoughtful answer, the professor asked the young reality warper to demonstrate a controlled display of his abilities. The entire classroom leaned forward with bated breath, eager to see what energy manipulation looked like in practice. Jack folded his arms, ready to show his new classmates a glimpse of his true potential.

Story Page 4
With a sudden surge of power, Jack emitted a brilliant cosmic purple energy that swept across the entire room. The regular walls vanished as the space transformed into a psychedelic, rippling alternate dimension teeming with strange, intangible creatures. Books floated suspended mid-air while vivid rainbows streaked across impossible geometries before the eyes of his stunned classmates. Miss Aurora stared at her own glowing hands in absolute amazement, wondering aloud if they could study like this every single day. The amorphous cosmic entities even began politely reorganizing the elemental periodic table on the blackboard.

Story Page 5
With a sharp snap of his fingers, Jack effortlessly dispelled the reality alterations he had just created. The vibrant cosmic energy seeped back into his body like a smoker inhaling a cloud of glowing mist. The classroom instantly reverted to its normal state, though a faint, beautiful shimmer still lingered on the desks. The entire room erupted into enthusiastic gasps and cheers as his peers applauded the magnificent display. Professor Lightman breathed a sigh of relief, highly commending Jack's stellar control over such a universe-bending power.

Story Page 6
When the dismissal bell rang, Miss Aurora eagerly invited Jack to grab some anti-gravity pudding at the cosmic cafeteria. Jack politely explained his strict routine, noting that he only eats a single meal a day at 4:30 AM. He then asked if this single session was their only scheduled class for the day. The professor chimed in to explain that a tailored routine would be required for someone with reality-warping abilities. Jack then caught everyone off guard by explaining he was actually an observer from another dimension viewing their world through a chat window.

Story Page 7
The revelation left the classroom in stunned silence as they tried to process the reality of their existence. Jack reassured them that their world was entirely real to him and that he held no control over their free will. He explained that he was simply there to learn, immerse himself, and find creative inspiration for his writing. The tension quickly dissolved back into lighthearted laughter as Miss Aurora decided to focus on the immediate joy of floating desserts. The professor gave Jack a final approving wink, leaving the hero-in-training ready to tackle his newly sorted schedule.
