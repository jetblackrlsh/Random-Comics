# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. Story pages should use around five or six panels minimum so the action is easy to follow.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Jack Blue, Class 1A, Miss Aurora, Professor Lightman, and Jack's cosmic reality-warping debut.
- Layout: Single dramatic realistic photo-comic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Super School RPG CAI Adaptation". Smaller issue title text: "The Cosmic Debut".
- Visual beats: Jack Blue floats calmly in the center of a bright superhero classroom, one hand releasing cosmic purple energy that turns the walls into a star-rainbow dimension. Miss Aurora watches from a nearby desk with bright blue-eyed curiosity, Professor Lightman stands by a holographic blackboard with controlled admiration, and stunned classmates lean forward around them.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Arrival In Class 1A

- Story role: Jack enters, Professor Lightman welcomes him, Jack requests ordinary treatment, and Miss Aurora becomes his first curious seatmate.
- Layout: Six panels in clear reading order.
- Caption boxes: "Jack Blue floated calmly into Class 1A, and every desk turned toward him." "Professor Lightman welcomed the newcomer as a rare and powerful student." "Jack introduced himself simply." "He asked everyone to treat him like any other classmate." "The professor smiled at that humility and pointed to an open desk." "Jack sat beside Miss Aurora, whose blue eyes studied him with open curiosity."
- Visual beats: Jack floating through the doorway; students turning in surprise; Professor Lightman greeting him warmly; Jack standing with hands open in a humble introduction; Lightman gesturing to an empty desk; Jack sitting beside Miss Aurora while she looks intensely curious.

## Page 3: Page 34

- Story role: Establish the hero-history lesson, the Golden Age material, Miss Aurora's welcome doodle, the Lady Lightning rumor, and the professor calling them back.
- Layout: Six panels, classroom rhythm with readable textbook and notebook details.
- Caption boxes: "The lesson began on page 34." "Jack skimmed for the bullet points about the Golden Age of Heroes." "Atlas the Unbreakable and Lady Lightning stood at the center of the reading." "Miss Aurora slid over a notebook with a small welcome doodle in the margin." "She whispered that Professor Lightman might be Lady Lightning's great-grandson." "Before Jack could answer, the professor cleared his throat and reclaimed the room."
- Visual beats: Jack opening a hero-history textbook to page 34; close view of textbook bullet points and historical hero photos; Miss Aurora doodling a friendly star-and-lightning welcome; she slides the notebook toward Jack; the two whisper quietly; Professor Lightman clears his throat at the board while classmates refocus.

## Page 4: The Demonstration Request

- Story role: Jack summarizes the Silver Age lesson and is asked to demonstrate controlled energy manipulation.
- Layout: Six panels, escalating anticipation.
- Caption boxes: "Professor Lightman ended the Silver Age lecture with one question for Jack." "Jack explained that stronger powers made academies and veteran guidance essential." "The answer was calm, practical, and exactly right." "The professor asked for a controlled display of energy manipulation." "Class 1A leaned forward in complete silence." "Jack folded his arms, ready to show only as much power as the room could bear."
- Visual beats: Lightman standing beside a holographic timeline of hero dynasties; Jack answering from his desk; classmates impressed; Lightman inviting Jack to demonstrate; Miss Aurora and other students leaning forward; Jack rising or floating slightly with arms folded and faint purple light around him.

## Page 5: Reality Turns Cosmic

- Story role: Jack transforms the classroom into a psychedelic alternate dimension full of cosmic energy, floating books, impossible geometry, and helpful entities.
- Layout: Six spectacle panels that still read clearly.
- Caption boxes: "Cosmic purple energy swept across the room." "The walls vanished into a rippling alternate dimension." "Books floated above the desks while rainbow geometry bent the air." "Strange intangible creatures drifted through Class 1A without touching anyone." "Miss Aurora stared at her glowing hands and wondered if every class could be like this." "One polite entity began reorganizing the periodic table on the blackboard."
- Visual beats: Jack releasing brilliant purple power; classroom walls dissolving into stars and impossible geometry; books floating around desks; amorphous translucent entities drifting harmlessly; Miss Aurora amazed by glowing hands; entity rearranging periodic-table tiles on the holographic blackboard while classmates gape.

## Page 6: Back To Normal

- Story role: Jack dispels the reality alteration, the class applauds, and Professor Lightman praises his control.
- Layout: Six panels with a clean reversal from spectacle to classroom.
- Caption boxes: "Jack snapped his fingers." "The altered dimension folded back into him like glowing mist." "Class 1A returned to normal in an instant." "Only a faint shimmer remained across the desks." "The room burst into cheers and astonished applause." "Professor Lightman praised Jack's stellar control over a universe-bending gift."
- Visual beats: Close view of Jack snapping his fingers; purple cosmic mist streaming back into his body like inhaled glowing cloud; classroom restoring tile by tile; desks shimmering faintly; students cheering and clapping; Professor Lightman relieved and impressed.

## Page 7: The Chat Window

- Story role: After dismissal, Miss Aurora invites Jack to the cosmic cafeteria, Jack explains his strict routine and class schedule question, and then reveals his interdimensional observer status.
- Layout: Six panels with social comedy turning into surprise.
- Caption boxes: "The dismissal bell finally rang." "Miss Aurora invited Jack to try anti-gravity pudding at the cosmic cafeteria." "Jack politely said he only ate one meal a day at 4:30 AM." "He asked whether this had been their only scheduled class." "Professor Lightman explained that Jack needed a tailored routine." "Then Jack added that he was an observer from another dimension watching through a chat window."
- Visual beats: Bell ringing above the classroom door; Miss Aurora animatedly pointing toward floating cafeteria dessert poster or pudding cup; Jack politely explaining with awkward calm; Jack asking about the schedule; Lightman answering from his desk with planning tablet; a translucent interdimensional chat window flickering beside Jack as the class freezes.

## Page 8: Real To Him

- Story role: The class processes the revelation, Jack reassures them, Miss Aurora restores the mood, and Jack is ready for his new schedule.
- Layout: Six panels ending with calm forward momentum.
- Caption boxes: "For a moment, Class 1A could only stare." "Jack promised that their world was real to him." "He said he had no control over their free will." "He had come to learn, immerse himself, and find inspiration for his writing." "Miss Aurora decided floating desserts were easier to understand." "Professor Lightman gave Jack an approving wink as the new schedule began."
- Visual beats: Silent classroom staring at Jack and the floating chat window; Jack speaking gently with open hands; classmates relaxing as the chat window dims; Jack looking sincere beside his desk; Miss Aurora laughing lightly and holding or gesturing toward a hovering pudding cup; Professor Lightman winking approval while Jack stands ready with a freshly projected tailored schedule.
