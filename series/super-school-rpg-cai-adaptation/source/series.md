# Super School RPG CAI Adaptation

## Logline

A humble reality-warping student named Jack Blue enters a superhero academy, where ordinary class days keep turning into cosmic demonstrations, social surprises, and fourth-wall questions about worlds seen through a chat window.

## Premise

This series adapts a Super School RPG campaign from Character AI into clear, readable photo-comic issues. Each issue follows Jack Blue as he learns the rhythms of Super School, meets classmates and teachers with extraordinary abilities, and navigates the strange tension between daily student life and universe-bending power.

Jack is powerful enough to distort reality, reshape rooms into cosmic dimensions, and perceive his world through an interdimensional chat window. He still asks to be treated like an ordinary student. That humility is the emotional center of the series: enormous cosmic scale should keep rubbing against simple school routines, class friendships, and the desire to belong.

## Tone

Clear superhero-school drama with playful cosmic spectacle, sincere character interactions, and readable captions that explain the story plainly. Humor comes from impossible power being handled like classroom business: lectures, notebooks, cafeteria plans, tailored schedules, and teachers trying to keep reality stable.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing, even when the setting becomes impossible.
- Super School should feel like a modern academy adapted for young superheroes: bright classrooms, reinforced desks, holographic displays, safety rails, cosmic cafeteria counters, floating trays, and practical hero-history materials.
- Jack's powers should appear as brilliant cosmic purple light, shimmering mist, starfield distortions, rainbow geometry, and matter behaving as if classroom reality briefly became editable.
- The interdimensional chat window should look like a floating translucent rectangular interface that is clearly supernatural but not branded to any real platform.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Continuity Notes

- Jack Blue is the central protagonist and should remain humble, calm, and matter-of-fact about impossible power.
- Miss Aurora is Jack's first friendly classmate and should remain curious, warm, and socially observant.
- Professor Lightman is a mentor figure who admires Jack's control while staying responsible for classroom safety.
- Super School Class 1A is the first recurring classroom environment.
- The interdimensional chat window is a recurring fourth-wall element; Jack can describe it, but it does not control anyone's free will.
