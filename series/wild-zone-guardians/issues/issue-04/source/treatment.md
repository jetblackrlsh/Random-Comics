# Treatment

## Title

False Hunger

## Issue Number

4

## Logline

Cornered by a starving corrupted bandit who could kill them with one punch, the Guardians must save her without letting compassion become hesitation.

## Central Conflict

The Chronicler wants to preserve the infected bandit's life because she is still a person, not a monster. Kaelen, Vessara, and Quasar agree in principle, but every nonlethal option is risky: the bandit is too strong to restrain safely, too corrupted for casual purification, too unstable for a blind teleport, and too close for extended debate. The practical conflict is a shrinking battlefield. The team keeps backing away while trying to build a rescue plan, until the bandit corners them and forces action.

## Theme

Mercy is not passive. Sometimes saving someone requires faster, riskier courage than defeating them.

## Ending

The Chronicler teleports the team behind the bandit to escape a lethal strike, then teleports the stabilized bandit to the gate guards, who are ready with containment rather than weapons. The bandit survives the immediate encounter, but Kaelen's void-tech suit is damaged by the overextension. The victory is real, but fragile: the next problem is reaching a sanctuary shelter before the damaged suit leaves the team exposed.

## Emotional Arc

The Chronicler begins horrified by the idea of hurting an infected person and frustrated by the lack of safe options. As the bandit advances, compassion is tested by fear. The crystal-wall punch proves that hesitation could get everyone killed. The Chronicler's arc is learning that mercy can require decisive tactical action: move the team, trust Vessara, trust Kaelen's stabilizing field, wait for Quasar's confirmation, then commit to the rescue teleport.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, the bandit advancing from Issue #3's cliffhanger, the first retreat and moral dilemma, Quasar's blocked contact with the gate, the team being forced backward into a crystal choke point, the wall-shattering punch, the catalyst teleport and Kaelen stabilization, and the rescue teleport that saves the bandit while damaging Kaelen's suit.

## Caption Approach

Use fewer, larger panels than Issue #3 and keep the captions tense. The reader should feel the distance shrinking. Captions should emphasize geography, time pressure, and choices closing off: one step closer, less room behind the team, weaker signal, less time before Kaelen's suit overloads. The bandit should not speak much, but when she does, her words should be painful and human.
