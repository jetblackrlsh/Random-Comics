# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

Use tense story-clarity captions and 3-4 large panels per page. The encounter should feel like a slow pressure trap: the bandit advances, the team retreats, options close, and the rescue plan has to work before one punch ends the fight.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Sell the corrupted bandit encounter as tragic, physical, and dangerous.
- Layout: Single dramatic photo-comic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Wild Zone Guardians". Smaller issue title text: "False Hunger".
- Visual beats: The corrupted four-armed bandit dominates the foreground, towering and massively muscular, one fist embedded in a shattered crystal wall. The Guardians are backed into broken crystal terrain behind her: the Chronicler holds the teleporter with one hand and points with the other like a finger gun, Vessara throws glowing catalyst vials into the air with all four arms, Kaelen projects a strained blue containment field from a flickering suit, and Quasar displays an emergency beacon.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The Person Inside The Threat

- Story role: Resume from Issue #3 and make the bandit pitiable before the danger takes over.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "She came toward us slowly, as if every step had to fight the body carrying it." Panel 2 top caption: "The corruption burned around her mouth, but her eyes still knew fear." Panel 3 top caption: "Residue was not food. The hunger only told her it was." Panel 4 top caption: "That made her harder to fight, not easier."
- Visual beats: The bandit lurches from jagged rocks, huge and shaking. Her four muscular arms twitch with uneven corrupted spasms. The Chronicler sees fear in her eyes. Vessara and Kaelen hold defensive positions without attacking. Quasar scans her mouth, neural pathways, and muscle instability.

## Page 3: Giving Ground

- Story role: Establish the slow retreat and the moral problem.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "We backed away because killing her was the wrong answer." Panel 2 top caption: "We backed away because letting her touch us might be the last mistake we ever made." Panel 3 top caption: "Kaelen said standard purification would not reach the neural damage in time." Panel 4 top caption: "Vessara said close contact might get one of us broken before it got her saved."
- Visual beats: The team retreats down a narrowing path between crystal formations. The bandit follows, not fast, but unstoppable. Kaelen's blue containment lines flicker defensively. Vessara keeps catalyst vials ready but out of reach. The Chronicler glances between the teleporter and the bandit, conflicted.

## Page 4: No Clean Signal

- Story role: Make the rescue plan harder by blocking immediate guard coordination.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Teleporting her to the gate without warning could save us and get her killed." Panel 2 top caption: "Quasar reached for the subnet, but the local corruption turned signal into static." Panel 3 top caption: "The bandit heard none of our calculations. She only heard the hunger." Panel 4 top caption: "Every second we spent choosing mercy gave her another step."
- Visual beats: Quasar projects a broken connection toward the western gate, heavy static cutting through the display. The Chronicler refuses a blind teleport. The bandit reaches toward residue with four trembling hands. Kaelen checks distance markers while Vessara watches the narrowing path behind the team.

## Page 5: The Wall Breaks

- Story role: Prove the bandit's physical strength and force the team into immediate action.
- Layout: Three panels, final panel large.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The path ended in a wall of natural crystal." Panel 2 top caption: "For one breath, I thought the wall might protect us." Panel 3 top caption: "Then she missed us by inches and punched the wall into knives of light."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Guardians realizing they are cornered by a crystal wall. Panel 2 shows the bandit raising one massive corrupted arm, tears and hunger on her face. Panel 3 shows her punch obliterating the crystal wall beside them, shards exploding, dust and light everywhere, proving that one hit would end someone.

## Page 6: Catalyst Teleport

- Story role: Turn the pressure into coordinated nonlethal action.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "We were out of room, so the plan became smaller than a sentence." Panel 2 top caption: "Vessara threw the catalysts where I could reach them without letting her get close." Panel 3 top caption: "I teleported the vials into the space above the bandit." Panel 4 top caption: "Purifying light rained down, and the corruption screamed through her muscles."
- Visual beats: Vessara launches amber, violet, and obsidian catalyst vials high into the air with all four arms. The Chronicler triggers the personal teleporter with a white-violet shimmer. The vials reappear above the bandit and burst into purifying rain. The bandit convulses, still upright, monstrous strength barely slowed.

## Page 7: Thirty Seconds

- Story role: Show Kaelen overextending the suit while Quasar gets the needed confirmation.
- Layout: Four panels, with the final panel tense and close.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The catalysts gave Kaelen an opening, not a solution." Panel 2 top caption: "Blue containment lines wrapped the bandit's corrupted field and shook like wire under too much weight." Panel 3 top caption: "Quasar finally punched a warning through the static: containment team standing by." Panel 4 top caption: "Kaelen gave me thirty seconds before the suit overloaded."
- Visual beats: Kaelen projects a strained blue void-tech stabilization field around the bandit. The suit's circuitry flares, cracks of light stuttering across the armor. Quasar's emergency beacon connects to distant gate guard silhouettes and containment shapes on a holographic display. The Chronicler prepares the teleporter, face tight with fear.

## Page 8: Mercy At The Last Second

- Story role: Resolve the encounter by saving the bandit and leave the next operational problem.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "First, I moved the team out of the corner." Panel 2 top caption: "Her strike destroyed the place where we had been standing." Panel 3 top caption: "Then I sent her to the gate, where the guards were ready to contain instead of execute." Panel 4 top caption: "We saved her life. Kaelen's suit paid the price."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a white-violet teleport shimmer moving the Guardians behind the bandit. Panel 2 shows the bandit's punch smashing the cornered position into crystal debris. Panel 3 shows the bandit vanishing in a teleport ripple while Quasar displays gate guards securing a containment unit. Panel 4 shows Kaelen's suit dim and damaged, blue circuitry flickering out, while the exhausted team stands in cracked crystal dust and looks toward the dangerous route ahead.
