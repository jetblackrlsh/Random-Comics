## Core Cast

### The Chronicler

Adult human off-world comic writer in dusty gray-green rune-woven survival clothing with faint glowing seams, compact satchel, wrist interface, and small chest-carried personal teleporter. In this issue the Chronicler is frightened but morally focused, refusing to kill the bandit while also learning that mercy demands fast action.

### Quasar

Floating blue-white holographic AI companion with orb-like core, data rings, maps, scanning displays, and tactical overlays. Quasar tries to contact the gate guards through corrupted signal interference, tracks the shrinking distance between the bandit and the team, and confirms containment readiness before the final teleport.

### Kaelen

Tall athletic humanoid in matte-black void-tech armor with glowing blue circuitry, transparent-visor helmet, blue-lit eyes, and containment canisters. Kaelen is disciplined and protective, but the suit is still stressed from the previous cleansing. Overextending a stabilization field around the bandit damages the suit and leaves Kaelen vulnerable by the end.

### Vessara

Slender four-armed humanoid with iridescent scaled skin, four expressive eyes, taloned hands, field wraps, and belts of glowing amber, violet, and obsidian catalyst vials. Vessara recognizes that the bandit is too unstable for close contact, then commits to a risky catalyst throw that depends on the Chronicler's teleportation.

### Corrupted Four-Armed Bandit

Very tall four-armed Wild Zone humanoid with a massive muscular build, broad shoulders, heavy jaw, close-cropped dark hair, torn scavenger armor, crude belt pouches, cracked gauntlets, and green-violet corruption veins around her mouth, eyes, neck, and swollen arms. She moves slowly and unevenly, with flashes of fear and awareness breaking through violent false hunger. Her strength must feel overwhelming: one missed punch can obliterate a crystal wall.

## Continuity Notes For Issue #4

- The issue begins immediately after Issue #3's cliffhanger.
- The bandit must remain both pitiable and menacing.
- The team's retreat is not only about contamination. They are also backing away because the bandit is physically powerful enough to kill them with one hit.
- The final teleport should save the bandit rather than defeat her.
- Kaelen's damaged suit should motivate the next move toward a safe maintenance point.
