## Core Cast

### The Chronicler

Adult human off-world comic writer in gray-green rune-woven survival clothing with faint glowing seams, compact satchel, wrist interface, and small chest-carried personal teleporter. In this issue they are dusty from the first cleansing, carrying the emotional aftershock of becoming useful in combat, and asking practical questions about recovery, repair, reporting, and whether Guardian work is sustainable.

### Quasar

Floating blue-white holographic AI companion with orb-like core, data rings, maps, scanning displays, and comic layout projections. Quasar compiles battle data, archives corruption patterns, scans biological contamination, and tries to explain Wild Zone systems without flattening the human stakes.

### Kaelen

Tall athletic humanoid in matte-black void-tech armor with glowing blue circuitry, transparent-visor helmet, blue-lit eyes, and containment canisters. Kaelen is tired after the first cleansing but disciplined, explaining Arcanum Guard reporting protocols and the limits of void-tech containment. Their posture should show controlled fatigue rather than collapse.

### Vessara

Slender four-armed humanoid with iridescent scaled skin, four expressive eyes, taloned hands, field wraps, and belts of glowing amber, violet, and obsidian catalyst vials. Vessara is dry, observant, and more openly bitter about what corruption does to living ecosystems and intelligent victims.

### Corrupted Four-Armed Bandit

Very tall four-armed Wild Zone humanoid with a massive muscular build, broad shoulders, heavy jaw, shaved or close-cropped dark hair, torn scavenger armor, crude belt pouches, and cracked gauntlets. Green-violet corruption veins spread around her mouth, eyes, neck, and swollen arms. Her expression should combine hunger, fear, confusion, and sudden violent focus. She is not a beast; she is an intelligent person being driven by false hunger. She should look pitiable and menacing at the same time, strong enough that a missed punch can shatter crystal.

## Continuity Notes For Issue #3

- The issue begins immediately after Issue #2 at the cleansed active anomaly site.
- The Chronicler's Tele-Shot success should be acknowledged, but Issue #3 is about consequences rather than celebration.
- Kaelen and Vessara should not sound invincible. Their fatigue is a major part of the issue's stakes.
- Corruption in plants, beasts, and intelligent beings should build toward the corrupted bandit cliffhanger.
- The corrupted bandit should not be defeated or saved in this issue. Issue #3 ends before the full encounter begins.
