Series continuity: Issue #2 ended with the Wild Zone Guardians cleansing their first active anomaly together. The Chronicler proved useful with the Tele-Shot, Quasar uploaded the battle through the Wild Zone subnet, and Kaelen and Vessara accepted the Chronicler as more than a passive observer.

Issue #3 premise: Continue immediately after the first cleansing. The team has won, but the end of a mission is not restful. Kaelen and Vessara explain that the Guardians must report fresh corruption patterns to the Arcanum Guard, document the location for Wild Zone subnet archives, and gather purified residue for funding. The Chronicler pushes on the practical questions: why return so often, how much rest do Guardians actually get, and how sustainable is this work?

The issue should reveal that Kaelen and Vessara have been operating for seven local solar cycles with only a few full rest periods, relying on ration packs, meditation, and harsh vitality stimulants. Use the aftermath to explain how corruption moves through the ecosystem: corrupted food can mutate beasts, plant life can become predatory, and intelligent species who consume corrupted matter suffer neural deterioration, false hunger, delirium, and violent loss of control.

The issue ends as a cliffhanger, not a resolved fight. Just as the Chronicler asks whether intelligent victims can be saved, a corrupted four-armed bandit emerges from the rocks. She is pitiable because she is starving, frightened, and partly aware of what is happening to her. She is also menacing because she is huge, massively muscular, mentally unstable, and strong enough that one corrupted punch could kill a Guardian. The final page should make clear that Issue #4 will be the slow pressure encounter where the team backs away until they are cornered and must save her without letting her reach them.
