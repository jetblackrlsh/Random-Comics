# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

Use story-clarity captions with mostly 3-4 large panels. The issue should feel like a post-battle debrief that slowly becomes dread. Keep the team visibly tired. End with the corrupted bandit as a cliffhanger, not a resolved action scene.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Sell the issue as aftermath turning into a human horror cliffhanger.
- Layout: Single dramatic photo-comic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Wild Zone Guardians". Smaller issue title text: "No Time To Rest".
- Visual beats: The Chronicler, Kaelen, Vessara, and Quasar stand in a cleansed crystal basin under sickly green-violet sky light. The team looks exhausted rather than triumphant. In the foreground shadow, a huge corrupted four-armed bandit reaches toward a pouch of glowing residue, her massive fists and corruption-veined arms dominating the lower frame. Kaelen's blue void-tech lines are dim, Vessara clutches catalyst vials with all four hands, and Quasar projects a warning scan.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The Victory Did Not End The Work

- Story role: Continue directly after Issue #2 and show that the post-battle moment is not peaceful.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The anomaly was gone, but the work did not end when the light faded." Panel 2 top caption: "Kaelen archived the containment data before the ground finished cooling." Panel 3 top caption: "Vessara gathered residue samples like each glowing shard was both payment and evidence." Panel 4 top caption: "I had expected a victory breath. The Guardians were already preparing the report."
- Visual beats: The cleansed active anomaly basin glows softly under cracked crystals. The Chronicler lowers the hardlight rifle, dusty and alert. Kaelen kneels beside blue diagnostic geometry from the void-tech suit. Vessara carefully bottles purified residue with two hands while holding catalyst vials in the other two. Quasar projects a battle report and hidden subnet map fragments.

## Page 3: Fresh Intelligence

- Story role: Explain why the Guardians go back and forth so often and connect field work to city politics.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Most prospectors waited until their packs were heavy before returning to the city-state." Panel 2 top caption: "Guardians carried something more valuable than residue: fresh patterns of corruption." Panel 3 top caption: "The Arcanum Guard paid for reports fast enough to adjust a defense grid before the next wound opened." Panel 4 top caption: "That meant the team lived between two dangers: the Wild Zones in front of them and the city narrative behind them."
- Visual beats: Quasar displays a route back to the western perimeter gate and a red-marked Arcanum Guard report packet. Distant city spires shimmer behind the gate on the horizon. Kaelen secures containment canisters while looking toward the city. Vessara studies ominous corruption glow farther west, skeptical and tense. The Chronicler records notes, clearly trying to understand the system.

## Page 4: Seven Solar Cycles

- Story role: Establish exhaustion as a serious operational risk.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "A local solar cycle was twenty-eight hours. Kaelen and Vessara had been working through seven of them." Panel 2 top caption: "In that time, they had earned only three full rest periods." Panel 3 top caption: "Quasar translated the log into a number I could feel: four hours of deep sleep every three cycles." Panel 4 top caption: "The Wild Zones did not only corrupt the land. They trained heroes to ignore their own breaking points."
- Visual beats: Quasar projects a Guardian activity log with sleep blocks as tiny blue slivers. Kaelen stands very still while the suit's blue circuitry flickers at low power. Vessara sits briefly on a crystal ledge, all four arms moving through a tired equipment check. The Chronicler looks disturbed, no longer treating the team as genre heroes who bounce back between scenes.

## Page 5: Stimulants And Rations

- Story role: Show how the Guardians sustain themselves and why that is a fragile workaround.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The city supplied vitality stimulants to field operatives, sharp little miracles with sharp little costs." Panel 2 top caption: "Wild Zone food could carry trace corruption, so most meals came from ration packs sealed behind official runes." Panel 3 top caption: "Meditation, timing, chemistry, discipline: none of it changed the truth that rest was being replaced, not restored." Panel 4 top caption: "A hero team could survive that way for a while. A world should not require them to."
- Visual beats: Close-up of small stimulant ampoules and sealed ration packs on a flat stone, photographed like evidence. Kaelen refuses or hesitates over a stimulant while running suit diagnostics. Vessara opens a ration pack beside a patch of beautiful but suspicious glowing fungi. The Chronicler watches quietly as Quasar scans the food and marks contamination warnings around nearby plants.

## Page 6: What Corruption Eats

- Story role: Explain corrupted flora, fauna, and the anomaly ecosystem.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Corruption did not care where life began. Root, fang, flower, wing: all of it could be rewritten." Panel 2 top caption: "Beasts that fed on corrupted flora grew stronger, angrier, and easier for anomalies to pull into violence." Panel 3 top caption: "Plants changed differently. Vines learned to move. Flowers learned to trap. Hunger became architecture." Panel 4 top caption: "Every mutation fed the network, and the network fed the next mutation."
- Visual beats: Vessara points out claw marks beside corrupted plant growth. Quasar projects a food-chain diagram without making it feel like a classroom. A distant warped beast silhouette drinks from an iridescent pool. Nearby predatory vines curl around small bones and glowing crystal roots. Kaelen watches the horizon with containment gear ready.

## Page 7: False Hunger

- Story role: Establish what happens when intelligent beings consume corrupted matter and foreshadow the bandit.
- Layout: Three large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "Then I asked what happened when the victim was intelligent enough to know they were changing." Panel 2 top caption: "Kaelen said higher minds gave corruption more pathways to fracture." Panel 3 top caption: "Vessara called the worst symptom false hunger: a starving command from a body that could no longer understand food."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Chronicler asking the question in a tense group arrangement, not casual conversation. Panel 2 shows Quasar's blue medical-style scan of a humanoid nervous system being overtaken by green-violet fractures, with no gore. Panel 3 shows Vessara's grave expression and four still hands as the wind carries a faint sound from behind jagged rocks. Kaelen turns sharply toward the sound.

## Page 8: Residue For The Hunger

- Story role: Cliffhanger introduction of the corrupted bandit and the next issue's physical stakes.
- Layout: Three panels, with the final panel large and oppressive.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The answer walked out of the rocks before anyone could soften it." Panel 2 top caption: "She was not a beast. She was a person, huge and shaking, with corruption burning around her mouth." Panel 3 top caption: "She reached for our residue and whispered, 'Need it... for the hunger.'"
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a shadow moving between jagged crystals behind the team. Panel 2 reveals the corrupted four-armed bandit: very tall, massively muscular, torn scavenger armor, corrupted veins around mouth and arms, terrified eyes fighting violent focus. Panel 3 shows her taking one heavy step forward, four huge fists flexing, a nearby crystal wall cracked by her accidental touch, while the Guardians instinctively back away into tighter ground. End before any plan or rescue attempt.
