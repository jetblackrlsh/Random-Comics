## Core Cast

### The Chronicler

Adult human off-world comic writer with practical modern clothes at first, later gray-green rune-woven survival clothing with faint glowing seams. Carries a compact satchel, wrist interface, and small chest-carried personal teleporter. Curious, genre-aware, verbally observant, and still learning the difference between chasing conflict and bearing witness.

### Quasar

Floating blue-white holographic AI companion with an orb-like core, subtle face cues, translucent data rings, projected maps, scanning displays, and reference thumbnails. Calm, analytical, protective, and visibly involved in comic creation.

### Kaelen

Tall athletic humanoid in matte-black void-tech armor with glowing blue circuitry and a sleek helmet with transparent visor. Sharp angular face and blue-lit eyes show through the visor. Precise, disciplined, haunted by past failure, and oriented toward containment.

### Vessara

Slender four-armed humanoid with iridescent scaled skin, four expressive eyes, taloned hands, practical field wraps, and belts of glowing amber, violet, and obsidian vials. Suspicious of city authority, dryly amused by the Chronicler, and emotionally tied to the Wild Zones.

### Arcanum Guard

Institutional silver-armored officials with featureless blue-glowing helms, synchronized movements, and brass-quartz scanners. They should feel more like a system than like individual villains in this issue.

## Continuity Notes For Issue #1

- The Chronicler starts in ordinary off-world travel clothes and changes into rune-woven survival clothing before leaving the city.
- The teleporter should be visible enough for the Spire scanner to identify it.
- Quasar should appear beside the Chronicler in most pages, recording or projecting information.
- Kaelen and Vessara only appear after the Chronicler reaches the Wild Zone departure area. They should feel like experienced heroes already in motion.
- This issue ends before the first anomaly battle.
