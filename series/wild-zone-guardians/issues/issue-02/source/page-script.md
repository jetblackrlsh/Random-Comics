# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

Use story-clarity captions as the default. Prefer 3-4 large panels per story page with roomy caption boxes so the reader understands the Wild Zone rules, the team roles, and the Chronicler's tactical contribution.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Sell the first big Wild Zone battle and introduce Tele-Shot as the issue's visual hook.
- Layout: Single dramatic photo-comic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Wild Zone Guardians". Smaller issue title text: "The First Cleansing".
- Visual beats: The Chronicler fires a blue-white hardlight rifle while Quasar hovers behind them projecting a teleportation target reticle. The hardlight bolt bends through a violet-white teleport shimmer toward the jagged crystal cluster on the back of a massive corrupted crystal beast. Kaelen holds back a green-violet anomaly with blue containment geometry while Vessara flings glowing catalyst arcs with all four arms. The active anomaly site glows behind them.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Past The Gate

- Story role: Continue directly from issue #1 and establish the Wild Zones as dangerous, beautiful, and outside city control.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The city ended behind us, but its damage continued under our feet." Panel 2 top caption: "Kaelen moved like every stone might hide a consequence. Vessara moved like the land was speaking and only they could hear it." Panel 3 top caption: "Quasar scanned the air for two things at once: corrupted energy and the hidden subnet that might carry our comic beyond city filters." Panel 4 top caption: "I had crossed the gate as a chronicler. The Wild Zones immediately asked whether I could be useful as more than a witness."
- Visual beats: The team walks away from the western perimeter gate into cracked Wild Zone terrain. The city dome is distant behind them. Kaelen leads with blue circuitry glowing; Vessara studies rocks and iridescent pools; Quasar projects maps and signal traces; the Chronicler records reference images while keeping the teleporter visible but inactive.

## Page 3: The Anomaly Feeds

- Story role: Explain what the anomaly is doing and why the team must intervene.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The first anomaly did not look alive, but it fed like a living thing." Panel 2 top caption: "Crystal deposits around it pulsed in time with the green-violet core, giving the corruption a battery it had no right to possess." Panel 3 top caption: "Kaelen called it containment work: compress the unstable energy before it spread into the ground." Panel 4 top caption: "Vessara called it a wound: stabilize the fever, then dissolve what the city had left behind."
- Visual beats: The active anomaly site fills a cracked basin with jagged mineral clusters and hovering green-violet corruption. Quasar overlays energy-flow arrows from crystals into the anomaly. Kaelen kneels to anchor blue containment geometry. Vessara uncorks amber and violet catalyst vials, ready to purify.

## Page 4: Not Just Light

- Story role: Turn the cleansing from abstract energy work into a monster battle.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "At first, I worried the comic would fail the action test. The battle looked like light fighting brighter light." Panel 2 top caption: "Then the anomaly answered my complaint with a roar." Panel 3 top caption: "A beast came out of the crystal field, not born from the corruption but rewritten by it." Panel 4 top caption: "The lesson was immediate: the Wild Zones did not separate environmental damage from physical danger."
- Visual beats: The Chronicler watches from a rocky vantage point, Quasar recording. Kaelen's blue containment geometry strains against the anomaly. A massive corrupted crystal beast erupts from behind jagged mineral clusters, warped flesh and crystal spine glowing. The beast charges, tendrils cracking the ground as Vessara pivots defensively.

## Page 5: The Rifle Quasar Made

- Story role: Give the Chronicler a local, lore-consistent way to participate in the battle.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "I thought about teleporting in a weapon from home, but Quasar rejected the idea before I finished asking." Panel 2 top caption: "Foreign weapons could destabilize the anomaly. A local solution had to use local energy." Panel 3 top caption: "Quasar drew power through the rune-weave, shaped it with hardlight, and placed a rifle in my hands." Panel 4 top caption: "It was not a hero's weapon from another world. It was a tool made by this battlefield, for this battlefield."
- Visual beats: The Chronicler ducks behind jagged rock as the beast's eye flare burns overhead. Quasar projects warnings about foreign energy signatures. Blue-white filaments run from the Chronicler's rune-woven sleeves into a forming hardlight rifle. Final panel: the Chronicler grips the semi-transparent rifle, frightened but focused, while Quasar projects cover and target overlays.

## Page 6: Finding The Beast's Weakness

- Story role: Show the Chronicler using observation, not raw power, to become useful.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "My first shot struck an eye and taught me the wrong lesson: hurting the beast could release more corruption." Panel 2 top caption: "Kaelen and Vessara saw what I missed. The beast was not powered by rage. It was powered by the crystal cluster growing from its back." Panel 3 top caption: "That changed the story shape. We did not need the biggest blast. We needed the right angle." Panel 4 top caption: "That was when the teleporter stopped being only my escape route and became part of the team's strategy."
- Visual beats: A hardlight shot hits one glowing eye and the beast recoils, releasing a dangerous corruption flare. Kaelen shields the flare with blue containment geometry while Vessara stabilizes the air with violet catalyst light. Quasar highlights the jagged crystal cluster on the beast's back. The Chronicler studies the angle from behind cover, one hand near the teleporter.

## Page 7: Tele-Shot

- Story role: Deliver the issue's decisive action sequence.
- Layout: Four panels, with the final panel large.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "I fired away from the beast, straight into open air where the shot could not hurt anyone." Panel 2 top caption: "Then I teleported the bolt, not myself, folding the path into an angle the beast could not defend." Panel 3 top caption: "The shot appeared behind it and struck the crystal cluster exactly where Quasar marked the weak point." Panel 4 top caption: "The crystals shattered, and for the first time that day, the anomaly stopped feeding faster than the Guardians could cleanse."
- Visual beats: The Chronicler fires the hardlight rifle upward from behind cover. A white-violet teleport shimmer catches the blue bolt midair. The bolt reappears behind the beast and pierces the crystal cluster on its back. Final large panel: the cluster explodes into glittering shards while Kaelen and Vessara move in perfect synchronization toward the weakened anomaly.

## Page 8: First Upload

- Story role: Resolve the battle and show why the Chronicler's comic-making matters.
- Layout: Four panels ending on a resolved team image.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "With the beast weakened, Kaelen compressed the anomaly into a shape the land could survive." Panel 2 top caption: "Vessara's catalysts did the gentler work: neutralize, stabilize, dissolve, and leave the soil with less pain than before." Panel 3 top caption: "The corruption field became a signal amplifier, and Quasar finally found the hidden subnet." Panel 4 top caption: "Our first battle became our first upload. I had wanted an action sequence. The Wild Zones gave me evidence."
- Visual beats: Kaelen contains the collapsing anomaly in blue void-tech geometry. Close-up on Vessara's catalyst vials releasing amber, violet, and obsidian light into the soil, with Vessara only partially visible to avoid a full-body anatomy pose. Quasar projects comic pages as data threads ride green-violet static into the hidden subnet. Final panel: the Chronicler lowers the hardlight rifle while Kaelen and Vessara stand nearby as distant readable silhouettes, the cleansed ground glowing softly under their feet.
