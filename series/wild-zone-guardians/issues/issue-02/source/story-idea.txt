Series continuity: Issue #1 ended with The Chronicler, Quasar, Kaelen, and Vessara stepping through the western perimeter gate into the Wild Zones. The Chronicler came to this world to make comics about real heroes, but issue #1 taught them that documenting the Guardians could expose city-state failures and propaganda.

Issue #2 premise: Continue immediately after issue #1. The team travels from the gate toward an active anomaly site in the Wild Zones. Kaelen and Vessara explain the process of cleansing a corruption field: Kaelen contains and compresses unstable energy with void-tech, while Vessara neutralizes and stabilizes it with catalysts. The anomaly feeds on mineral clusters and amplifies the hidden Wild Zone subnet, giving Quasar a possible upload path. Before the cleansing can finish, a corrupted crystal beast attacks. Quasar forms a hardlight rifle from the interaction between the rune-woven survival gear and Quasar's projection system. The Chronicler invents Tele-Shot by firing upward and teleporting the bolt into the beast's crystal growths, giving the Guardians the opening needed to cleanse the anomaly and upload the first battle sequence.

Keep the Chronicler's comic-writer role central. They should analyze the battle as both a storyteller and a field teammate. The issue should not turn the Chronicler into the only hero; Kaelen and Vessara still perform the actual cleansing, while the Chronicler helps by solving the beast problem.
