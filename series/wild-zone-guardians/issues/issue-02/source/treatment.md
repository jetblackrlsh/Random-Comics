# Treatment

## Title

The First Cleansing

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

The Chronicler follows Kaelen and Vessara to their first active anomaly site, where a corrupted crystal beast forces them to become more than an observer by inventing the Tele-Shot.

## Central Conflict

The Guardians need to cleanse an unstable anomaly before it feeds on nearby crystal deposits and spreads, while the Chronicler wants to document the mission without becoming a liability. The conflict turns physical when the anomaly draws a corrupted beast into the fight and the Chronicler must decide whether chronicling from safety is enough.

## Theme

Witnessing heroism is powerful, but joining the work changes what the witness is responsible for.

## Ending

The Chronicler uses Quasar's hardlight rifle and their teleporter to redirect a shot into the beast's crystal cluster, giving Kaelen and Vessara the opening they need to cleanse the anomaly. Quasar uploads the battle through the Wild Zone subnet, and Kaelen recognizes the Chronicler as an active field asset.

## Emotional Arc

The Chronicler begins eager but cautious, framing the mission as the first great action sequence for the comic. Watching Kaelen and Vessara work shows that cleansing is more complex than spectacle. When a corrupted beast attacks, the Chronicler's fear becomes useful analysis, then action. By the final page, the Chronicler is still a writer, but no longer only a witness.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, the team crossing into the Wild Zones, first anomaly scan, Kaelen and Vessara beginning containment, corrupted beast attack, Quasar creating the hardlight rifle, Tele-Shot breaking the beast's crystal cluster, and the Guardians cleansing the anomaly while Quasar uploads the first battle comic through the subnet.

## Caption Approach

Use longer, descriptive story-clarity captions and mostly 3-4 large panels per page. The issue must clearly explain how anomalies feed on crystal deposits, why corrupted beasts matter, how Kaelen and Vessara's roles differ, how the hardlight rifle forms, and why Tele-Shot makes the Chronicler useful without replacing the Guardians.
