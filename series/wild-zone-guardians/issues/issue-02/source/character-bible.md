## Core Cast

### The Chronicler

Adult human off-world comic writer in gray-green rune-woven survival clothing with faint glowing seams, compact satchel, wrist interface, and small chest-carried personal teleporter. In this issue they begin as a field observer, then become an active teammate by combining writerly observation, Quasar's tactical overlays, the hardlight rifle, and the teleporter.

### Quasar

Floating blue-white holographic AI companion with orb-like core, data rings, maps, scanning displays, and comic layout projections. Quasar records the battle, searches for subnet access, and creates the hardlight rifle by channeling energy through the rune-woven survival gear.

### Kaelen

Tall athletic humanoid in matte-black void-tech armor with glowing blue circuitry, transparent-visor helmet, blue-lit eyes, and containment canisters. Kaelen's job in the battle is containment: compressing anomaly energy and preventing the corruption from spreading.

### Vessara

Slender four-armed humanoid with iridescent scaled skin, four expressive eyes, taloned hands, field wraps, and belts of glowing amber, violet, and obsidian catalyst vials. Vessara's job in the battle is purification: neutralizing, stabilizing, and dissolving corruption after Kaelen contains it.

### Corrupted Crystal Beast

Massive four-legged predator twisted by anomaly energy, with warped flesh, jagged crystal growths, glowing corrupted eyes, and green-violet tendrils. The beast is not the anomaly itself; it is a living creature made dangerous by the anomaly's corruption field.

## Continuity Notes For Issue #2

- The issue begins immediately after the team leaves the city through the western perimeter gate.
- The Chronicler should still think and speak like a comic writer, but the captions should show growing responsibility, not detached excitement.
- Quasar's upload attempt should be tied to the anomaly's signal amplification.
- The hardlight rifle is made locally from Quasar plus rune-weave, not imported from another world.
- Tele-Shot is the Chronicler's new tactic: fire a hardlight bolt, then teleport the bolt to a better angle.
- Kaelen and Vessara cleanse the anomaly; the Chronicler helps by disabling the corrupted beast.
