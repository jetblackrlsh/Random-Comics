# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Pop, the sailor-world rescue, the giant crow, the witch, and the absurd hyperreality spinach solution.
- Layout: Single dramatic photo-comic cover composition with action-comedy energy.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Pop The Imp". Smaller issue title text: "The Spinach Sailor Rescue".
- Visual beats: Pop The Imp grins in the foreground, purple and potbellied in neon green shorts, one clawed hand holding open a glowing book. Behind him, Spinach Sailor flexes cosmic starlight muscles while bending cage bars near a stormy sea cave. A giant black crow flaps overhead carrying the cackling Cave Witch, and the curly red-haired girlfriend reaches from the cage. A huge green can labeled "HYPERREALITY SPINACH" glows between Pop and the sailor.
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Bored In Hyperreality

- Story role: Establish Pop's lonely boredom, the hyperreality library, the world-book choice, and the cry for help that finally excites him.
- Layout: Six panels in clear reading order.
- Caption boxes: "Hyperreality had everything except company." "Pop had already been bored for a thousand Tuesdays." "Every book held a world." "One cover promised Spinach Sailor." "The harbor looked painfully normal." "Then someone screamed."
- Visual beats: Pop sprawls upside down in empty hyperreality, bored and alone. He flips through floating shelves of glowing world-books. He pulls out the book titled "Spinach Sailor." Pop dives through the book pages like a portal. He lands on a mundane old harbor and slumps in disappointment. Final panel: Pop hears a cry for help, launches upward grinning from ear to ear.

## Page 3: The Crow And The Sailor

- Story role: Reveal the abduction, introduce Spinach Sailor, and show the spinach-powered transformation.
- Layout: Six panels, fast action escalation.
- Caption boxes: "A giant crow had found its prize." "Pop smelled adventure." "He also hated adventuring alone." "The local hero arrived running." "One can changed everything." "Spinach Sailor became the rescue."
- Visual beats: Giant black crow snatches the curly red-haired girlfriend from the dock with talons while she struggles. Pop hovers nearby, thrilled and worried. Spinach Sailor charges down the dock in navy coat and white cap. Close panel of him ripping open a normal green spinach can, not hyperreality spinach and not glowing. Spinach Sailor eats the normal spinach. Final panel: his wiry body inflates into a muscle-bound superhuman as boards crack under his boots.

## Page 4: Bad Timing

- Story role: Pop asks Spinach Sailor to adventure with him, gets mistaken for a familiar, and accidentally lets the crow escape.
- Layout: Six panels with comedic dodging and a final failure beat.
- Caption boxes: "Pop chose the worst possible introduction." "Spinach Sailor assumed Pop must be another one of the witch's familiars." "Pop dodged every punch." "He promised he was new in town." "The witch meant nothing to him yet." "The crow used the argument well."
- Visual beats: Pop flies in front of Spinach Sailor with an eager handshake. Spinach Sailor angrily accuses him and swings a huge fist. Pop teleports around him in playful purple blurs. Pop explains he does not know anything about a witch. Both freeze and gasp. Final panel: the giant crow flies away over the water with the red-haired girlfriend caught in its talons.

## Page 5: The Failed Leap

- Story role: Spinach Sailor blames Pop, tries a heroic leap, loses strength, and falls into the sea.
- Layout: Six action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Spinach Sailor had no patience left." "He jumped before Pop could apologize." "For one glorious second, strength was enough." "Then the spinach ran out." "The crow swatted him aside." "The sea caught what pride dropped."
- Visual beats: Spinach Sailor scolds Pop on the dock, pointing toward the escaping crow. He crouches with huge muscles and launches into the sky. He almost reaches the crow and girlfriend. His muscles suddenly deflate midair, coat sagging. The giant crow slaps him with one wing. Final panel: Spinach Sailor plummets into the gray-blue harbor water with a huge splash.

## Page 6: Boat With A Plan

- Story role: Pop rescues Spinach Sailor, becomes transportation, and reaches the witch's cave where the hostage is caged.
- Layout: Six panels, rescue to chase to villain reveal.
- Caption boxes: "Pop finally helped without arguing." "He became the strangest fishing boat afloat." "Spinach Sailor accepted the ride." "Fishing boat became speed boat." "The cave waited beyond the rocks." "The witch set the bait in a cage."
- Visual beats: Pop transforms into a living purple fishing boat with his imp face on the bow and neon green trim. He hooks Spinach Sailor from the water with a fishing line. Spinach Sailor coughs water and thanks him, pointing after the crow; in this panel he has exactly two human arms total, with one hand pointing and one hand holding onto the boat, and no hand on his mouth. Pop transforms into a sleek purple speed boat. Spinach Sailor clutches his sailor cap as they race across choppy water. Final panel: at a black sea cave, the Cave Witch emerges while the giant crow drops the red-haired girlfriend into an iron cage beside her.

## Page 7: No Spinach Left

- Story role: The witch makes her ultimatum, Spinach Sailor refuses, and Pop must find an inspired solution.
- Layout: Six panels with threat, search, confusion, and decision.
- Caption boxes: "The witch offered a rotten bargain." "Marry her, or lose the woman he loved." "Spinach Sailor refused without blinking." "His pockets answered badly." "No cans. No strength." "Pop hated obvious miracles."
- Visual beats: The Cave Witch points her crooked staff at the caged red-haired girlfriend while the giant crow looms behind her. Spinach Sailor stands on Pop-as-speed-boat and refuses angrily. He reaches into coat pockets. Close panel: empty pockets, no spinach cans. Spinach Sailor asks Pop to use his crazy powers. The witch looks baffled because Spinach Sailor is talking to a boat. Final panel: Pop's boat eyes brighten with a strange new idea.

## Page 8: Hyperreality Spinach

- Story role: Pop becomes a giant can of spinach, empowers Spinach Sailor, resolves the rescue, and exits toward the next adventure.
- Layout: Six panels ending with Pop's next leap.
- Caption boxes: "Pop became the can." "Spinach Sailor hated understanding that." "The spinach went in anyway." "Hyperreality made muscles cosmic." "The witch chose cowardice." "Pop left before boredom caught him."
- Visual beats: First panel shows only Pop's giant green can form with his grinning imp face on the label and readable text "HYPERREALITY SPINACH"; do not show Pop still as a speedboat in this panel. Spinach Sailor recoils, realizing Pop is the spinach. Pop pours glowing green spinach into Spinach Sailor's mouth anyway. Spinach Sailor grows cosmic starlight muscles, sparkling with purple-green power. The Cave Witch flees on the giant crow in terror. Spinach Sailor bends open the cage bars and embraces his red-haired girlfriend. Final panel: Pop waves and leaps through a glowing book-shaped portal toward another wild cosmic adventure.
