# Character Bible

Use these descriptions in every page prompt where the character appears. The issue uses realistic candid photo-comic styling, so even impossible characters should look physically present in photographed environments.

## Pop The Imp

- Series source: `series/pop-the-imp/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/pop-the-imp/reference-images/pop-the-imp-character.png`
- Stable look: short potbellied purple imp, diamond-shaped head, small dark horns, pointed ears, huge bright eyes, wide sharp grin, thin expressive tail, clawed hands and feet, neon green shorts, rubbery cartoonish proportions rendered with photoreal texture.
- Personality in poses: impatient, hyperactive, lonely, theatrical, delighted by danger, always leaning into the next event.
- Power continuity: can teleport, fly, transform into vehicles or objects, and jump between book-worlds, but he avoids obvious direct fixes unless inspired.

## Spinach Sailor

- Stable look: wiry middle-aged sailor with weathered tan skin, squinting eyes, anchor tattoos on both forearms, battered navy pea coat, striped shirt, white sailor cap, black boots, stubbled jaw, and exaggerated forearms.
- Spinach-powered look: body inflates into a muscle-bound superhuman with the same cap, coat, striped shirt, tattoos, and boots; muscles look comically large but photoreal.
- Hyperreality spinach look: muscles become cosmic and sparkling, with starlight veins and glowing forearms while the sailor remains recognizably himself.
- Personality in poses: protective, stubborn, quick to swing at threats, embarrassed by help but loyal once convinced.

## Curly Red-Haired Girlfriend

- Stable look: adult woman with long curly red hair, fair skin, green eyes, blue vintage coastal dress, practical flats, expressive face, and defiant posture.
- Continuity: abducted in a giant crow's talons on Pages 2-3, caged at the witch's cave on Pages 5-7, rescued by Spinach Sailor on Page 8.

## Cave Witch

- Stable look: elderly villainous witch with sharp cheekbones, gray tangled hair, black sea-cave cloak, crooked walking staff, shell jewelry, long fingers, and a cruel grin.
- Personality in poses: theatrical, possessive, threatening, cowardly once Spinach Sailor gains cosmic strength.

## Giant Crow

- Stable look: enormous black crow large enough to carry a person, glossy feathers, dagger beak, yellow eyes, huge talons, and sea-spray-wet wings.
- Continuity: abducts the girlfriend, knocks Spinach Sailor from the sky, carries the witch during her escape.
