# Treatment

## Title

The Spinach Sailor Rescue

## Issue Number

1

## Logline

Pop The Imp jumps into the book "Spinach Sailor" and turns himself into the one thing his reluctant sailor partner needs to rescue a kidnapped girlfriend: hyperreality spinach.

## Central Conflict

Pop wants an adventure and a companion, while Spinach Sailor wants to save his girlfriend without trusting the strange purple imp who appears at the worst possible moment. Their mission is endangered because Spinach Sailor's strength is temporary and Pop refuses to solve the rescue with boring omnipotence.

## Theme

Adventure is more satisfying when power makes room for someone else to be heroic.

## Ending

Pop becomes a giant can of hyperreality spinach and pours himself into Spinach Sailor, giving the sailor cosmic muscles that scare the witch away and let him bend open his girlfriend's cage. Pop celebrates the rescue by leaping out toward the next adventure before anyone can make him stay still.

## Emotional Arc

Pop begins bored and lonely, chasing any spark of novelty. Spinach Sailor begins suspicious and irritated, seeing Pop as another magical obstacle. After Pop saves him from drowning and carries him to the cave, Spinach Sailor accepts Pop's help. Pop finally finds a fun solution that complements Spinach Sailor's heroism instead of replacing it.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Pop choosing the book, the giant-crow abduction and spinach transformation, Pop distracting Spinach Sailor, the failed leap and sea rescue, the speed-boat chase and witch's ultimatum, the missing spinach problem, and Pop's hyperreality spinach solution.
