# Character Descriptions

## Character: Pop The Imp

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Role: protagonist, hyperreal adventurer, lonely reality editor
- Visual identifiers: short potbellied purple imp with a diamond-shaped head, small dark horns, pointed ears, wide bright eyes, huge sharp grin, thin expressive tail, clawed hands and feet, rubbery cartoonish proportions rendered with photoreal skin texture, and neon green shorts.
- Continuity notes: Pop is more real than the worlds he enters. He can reshape reality, transform his body, alter objects, teleport, and jump between fiction and hyperreality, but he refuses boring obvious solutions and needs inspiration for his powers to fire creatively.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/pop-the-imp-character.png`

## Character: Spinach Sailor

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Role: temporary adventure partner, sailor hero of the Spinach Sailor world
- Visual identifiers: wiry middle-aged sailor with weathered tan skin, squinting eyes, anchor tattoos on both forearms, a battered navy pea coat over a striped shirt, white sailor cap, black boots, stubbled jaw, and comically swollen forearms. After eating spinach, his whole body inflates into a muscle-bound superhuman while keeping the same outfit and cap.
- Continuity notes: Spinach Sailor's strength depends on cans of spinach and fades fast. He is protective, stubborn, suspicious of magical creatures, and devoted to his red-haired girlfriend.
- Reference image: None; introduced as an issue-specific partner.

## Character: Curly Red-Haired Girlfriend

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Role: captured civilian, Spinach Sailor's girlfriend
- Visual identifiers: beautiful adult woman with long curly red hair, fair skin, green eyes, blue vintage coastal dress, practical flats, and expressive defiance even while trapped.
- Continuity notes: She is kidnapped by the witch's giant crow and held as leverage, then rescued when Spinach Sailor bends open the cage.
- Reference image: None; introduced as issue-specific supporting cast.

## Character: Cave Witch

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Role: antagonist of the Spinach Sailor world
- Visual identifiers: elderly villainous witch with sharp cheekbones, gray tangled hair, black sea-cave cloak, crooked walking staff, shell jewelry, and a cruel triumphant smile.
- Continuity notes: She uses a giant crow as a familiar and tries to force Spinach Sailor to marry her by threatening his girlfriend.
- Reference image: None; introduced as issue-specific antagonist.
