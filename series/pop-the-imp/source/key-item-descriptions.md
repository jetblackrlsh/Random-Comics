# Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: World-Books

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Visual identifiers: old hardbound books with luminous page edges, covers that show shifting scenes from their contained worlds, impossible depth inside the pages, and colored light spilling from the paper.
- Continuity notes: Each world-book contains a complete world or story. Pop can open one and physically hop inside, treating the book as both map and doorway.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/world-books-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Spinach Cans

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Visual identifiers: small green metal cans of spinach, dented and sea-worn, carried in Spinach Sailor's coat pockets.
- Continuity notes: Eating spinach temporarily transforms Spinach Sailor into a muscle-bound superhuman. In Issue #1, Pop improvises by becoming a giant can of hyperreality spinach after the normal cans run out.
- Reference image: None; introduced as issue-specific item.
