# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The Spinach Sailor Rescue

- Folder: `series/pop-the-imp/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Pop The Imp enters a seemingly mundane sailor story and teams with Spinach Sailor to rescue a kidnapped red-haired woman from a witch and her giant crow.
- Plot summary: Bored in hyperreality, Pop chooses the world-book called "Spinach Sailor" and nearly leaves when the harbor seems dull. A cry for help leads him to a giant crow abducting Spinach Sailor's girlfriend. Pop distracts Spinach Sailor while asking for a buddy adventure, causing the crow to escape. After Spinach Sailor's spinach strength wears off mid-leap, Pop rescues him from the water, transforms into a speed boat, and carries him to the witch's cave. When Spinach Sailor runs out of spinach, Pop becomes a giant can of hyperreality spinach, empowering Spinach Sailor with cosmic muscles so he can bend open the cage while the witch flees.
- Continuity changes: Pop's standard adventure pattern is established: boredom in hyperreality, choosing a world-book, finding trouble, recruiting a reluctant partner, avoiding the easy omnipotent fix, and using an inspired transformation that complements the partner.
- New characters: Pop The Imp, Spinach Sailor, Curly Red-Haired Girlfriend, Cave Witch.
- New settings: Hyperreality Library, Spinach Sailor Harbor World, Witch's Sea Cave.
- New key items: World-Books, Spinach Cans.
