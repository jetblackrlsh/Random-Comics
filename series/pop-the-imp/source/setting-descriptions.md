# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Hyperreality Library

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Visual identifiers: infinite white-and-black nonspace with impossible perspective, floating wooden bookshelves, books arranged in spirals and vertical rivers, clean empty floors that reflect nothing correctly, and portals of paper light opening in midair.
- Continuity notes: Pop uses this lonely library to browse worlds and choose each adventure. The books are not symbolic to him; they are physical entrances into realities and stories.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/hyperreality-library-setting.png`

## Setting: Spinach Sailor Harbor World

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Visual identifiers: mundane old-fashioned coastal harbor with wooden docks, gray-blue water, fishing shacks, rope coils, gulls, weathered boats, and a rocky cliff line that hides the witch's cave.
- Continuity notes: Pop initially finds this world too ordinary, then discovers its adventure when a giant crow abducts the red-haired girlfriend.
- Reference image: None; introduced as issue-specific adventure location.

## Setting: Witch's Sea Cave

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Spinach Sailor Rescue"
- Visual identifiers: jagged cave mouth in black coastal rock, slick tide pools, iron cage beside the entrance, driftwood charms, crow feathers, greenish cave glow, and crashing surf below.
- Continuity notes: The witch uses the cave as her lair and bargaining stage. It is reached by Pop and Spinach Sailor after a speed-boat chase.
- Reference image: None; introduced as issue-specific adventure location.
