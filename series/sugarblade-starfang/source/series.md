# Sugarblade & Starfang

## Logline

Two retired legendary warriors try to live quietly on Candy Island, but the enemies they made across alien wars, demon realms, and sugar kingdoms keep forcing them to defend the peace they already earned.

## Premise

Veyro Starfang is a psychic alien warrior who once invaded Demon World to stop demonic chaos from spilling onto his home planet. Ronin Mochizuki is a master samurai who ended a global candy golem invasion led by a wicked sugar sorcerer. Both heroes have survived their great wars, defeated their arch-enemies, and grown tired of being treated as living legends.

They retire to Candy Island, a tropical paradise of sugar beaches, caramel rivers, peppermint forests, chocolate cliffs, syrup lagoons, and gumdrop mountains. Veyro wants to meditate, fish, and avoid interdimensional politics. Ronin wants to drink tea, tend his mochi garden, and keep his sword sheathed.

Their pasts refuse to stay gone. Demon lords, candymancers, galactic bounty hunters, rogue samurai, cursed chefs, dessert pirates, and living desserts keep finding the island, drawn by revenge or by the island's hidden magical resources. Veyro and Ronin insist they are not forming a team and are not returning to hero work. They just happen to keep saving Candy Island before teatime.

## Tone

Action comedy with retired-hero melancholy, tropical candy fantasy, deadpan warrior banter, absurdly intense battles over quiet domestic routines, and sincere questions about whether earned peace can survive unfinished history.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Candy Island should look like a believable tropical island transformed by edible geology: sugar sand, caramel water, peppermint groves, chocolate rock, gumdrop hills, mochi gardens, and bright humid sunlight.
- Veyro's psychic power appears as violet-blue lensing, floating debris, pressure waves, and glowing hand auras integrated into real-camera action.
- Ronin's swordwork is quiet and precise: white-blue cutting arcs, falling candy fragments, calm posture, and Moonbite's crescent guard visible when drawn.
- Malzareth's demon magic uses black-violet smoke, ember cracks, horned shadow shapes, and ritual geometry.
- Saccharon's candymancy uses jewel-bright sugar constructs, spun sugar ribbons, hard-candy weapons, molten caramel, and theatrical gestures.
- The series balances epic combat with mundane retirement details: tea cups, fishing gear, garden tools, sandals, beach towels, and half-finished chores.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Continuity Notes

- Veyro and Ronin have only recently arrived on Candy Island at the start of Issue #1.
- Neither hero wants to be publicly treated as active again, but both are unwilling to let innocent islanders suffer.
- Veyro still carries guilt from ruling through conquest and now tries to practice restraint, though his idea of restraint is severe.
- Ronin has vowed not to draw Moonbite unless peace truly requires it.
- Malzareth believes Veyro's past invasion of Demon World makes revenge righteous.
- Lord Saccharon believes candy magic is the supreme art and treats Ronin's victory over his first candy empire as a personal insult.
- Candy Island's hidden magical resources are not fully understood in Issue #1, but they attract hostile forces.
