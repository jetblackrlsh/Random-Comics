# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The Dark Magic Candy Golem

- Folder: `series/sugarblade-starfang/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Veyro Starfang and Ronin Mochizuki try to begin peaceful retirements on Candy Island, but their arch-enemies combine demon magic and candymancy into a regenerating candy golem that can only be defeated by being eaten.
- Plot summary: Veyro and Ronin separately settle into quiet routines on Candy Island, hoping to avoid war, missions, and legend. Malzareth the Demon Wizard and Lord Saccharon the Candymancer discover their location and form a spiteful alliance. Their Dark Magic Candy Golem survives psychic blasts, sword cuts, and the heroes' combined ultimate attack by reforming from cursed sugar and demon magic. Ronin bites the monster out of exhausted hunger, and the wound does not regenerate. Veyro recognizes the weakness, joins the feast, and the two warriors devour the monster until their horrified enemies flee.
- Continuity changes: Veyro and Ronin meet as neighbors and reluctant allies. Malzareth and Saccharon learn Candy Island can be found. The heroes' retirement is already compromised, though they refuse to call it hero work.
- New characters: Veyro Starfang, Ronin Mochizuki, Malzareth the Demon Wizard, Lord Saccharon the Candymancer, Dark Magic Candy Golem.
- New settings: Candy Island, Mochi Garden Tea Hut.
- New key items: Moonbite, Veyro's Fishing Rod.
