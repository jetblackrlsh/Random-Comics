# Character Descriptions

## Character: Veyro Starfang

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Role: co-protagonist, retired psychic alien warrior.
- Visual identifiers: tall lean alien man with pale lavender-gray skin, angular cheekbones, narrow silver eyes, swept-back midnight-blue crest hair, subtle star-speckled markings at the temples, and small fang-like lower canines. He wears a sleeveless charcoal-blue retirement tunic, wrapped forearms, loose dark trousers, rope belt, and beach sandals. His posture is stern and tactical even while relaxing, with violet-blue psychic energy around his hands when powers manifest.
- Continuity notes: Veyro once invaded Demon World to stop demonic chaos from reaching his home planet. He now seeks meditation, fishing, and restraint, but his battle instincts remain terrifying.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/veyro-starfang-character.png`

## Character: Ronin Mochizuki

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Role: co-protagonist, retired samurai and gardener.
- Visual identifiers: Japanese man in his late 40s to early 50s with warm tan skin, calm dark eyes, weathered face, black hair tied in a neat topknot with gray at the temples, short trimmed beard, and composed posture. He wears a faded indigo kimono jacket over practical garden clothes, a pale sash, straw sandals, and keeps the sheathed sword Moonbite at his side only when danger demands it.
- Continuity notes: Ronin destroyed Lord Saccharon's first candy golem army and retired to Candy Island to drink tea, tend a mochi garden, and stop living by the blade.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/ronin-mochizuki-character.png`

## Character: Malzareth the Demon Wizard

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Role: antagonist, Veyro's arch-enemy.
- Visual identifiers: gaunt demon sorcerer with ember-red skin, long black horns, sunken gold eyes, sharp cheekbones, black-violet robes, bone charms, clawed hands, and a dramatic hunched stance. His magic appears as black smoke, violet flame, and red ritual cracks in the ground.
- Continuity notes: Malzareth sees Veyro as an invader and tyrant for Veyro's occupation of Demon World. He uses revenge plots, monster creation, possession magic, and dark contracts.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/malzareth-demon-wizard-character.png`

## Character: Lord Saccharon the Candymancer

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Role: antagonist, Ronin's arch-enemy.
- Visual identifiers: flamboyant human candy sorcerer in his 50s with powdered white swept hair, curled mustache, sharp theatrical eyes, rose-pink velvet coat, gold trim, striped waistcoat, candy-cane staff, lacquered boots, and jewel-like sugar rings on his fingers. He poses like a stage magician and surrounds himself with spun sugar ribbons and hard-candy sparks.
- Continuity notes: Saccharon built the first candy golem empire before Ronin destroyed it. He believes candy is the supreme magical art and treats every defeat as aesthetic sabotage.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/lord-saccharon-candymancer-character.png`

## Character: Dark Magic Candy Golem

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Role: regenerating monster, combined creation of Malzareth and Saccharon.
- Visual identifiers: towering humanoid monster made of cracked black sugar, red licorice tendons, molten caramel seams, gumdrop growths, hard-candy armor plates, demon sigils, and glowing violet-red eyes. Broken pieces pull themselves back together unless bitten and eaten.
- Continuity notes: Created by Malzareth and Saccharon as a revenge weapon. Its regeneration fails only where the heroes physically eat it.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/dark-magic-candy-golem-character.png`
