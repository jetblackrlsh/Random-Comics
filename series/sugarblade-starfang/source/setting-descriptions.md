# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Candy Island

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Visual identifiers: tropical island paradise with white sugar beaches, turquoise syrup lagoons, caramel rivers, peppermint palm forests, chocolate cliffs, gumdrop mountains, mochi garden terraces, candy-colored birds, and humid golden sunlight. It should feel like a real photographed island where edible magical geology has replaced ordinary sand, stone, and plants.
- Continuity notes: Veyro and Ronin retire here because it seems peaceful and remote. Its hidden magical resources attract enemies from both heroes' pasts.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/candy-island-setting.png`

## Setting: Mochi Garden Tea Hut

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Visual identifiers: small quiet tea hut beside Ronin's mochi garden, with low wooden porch, paper screens, stone path, mochi plants like soft white rounded shrubs, tea tray, garden shears, raked sugar gravel, and a view toward caramel water and peppermint palms.
- Continuity notes: Ronin's preferred retirement space. Veyro sometimes appears nearby while pretending he is not visiting for tea.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/mochi-garden-tea-hut-setting.png`
