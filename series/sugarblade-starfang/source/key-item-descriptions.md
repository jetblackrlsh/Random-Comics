# Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: Moonbite

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Visual identifiers: elegant katana with a dark lacquer scabbard, moon-white blade, crescent-shaped silver guard, blue silk wrap, small bite-shaped notch near the guard, and faint cold light along the edge when focused against magic.
- Continuity notes: Ronin's legendary sword can cut enchanted sugar, cursed armor, and spiritual energy when he focuses properly. Ronin prefers not to draw it unless peace truly requires it.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/moonbite-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Veyro's Fishing Rod

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"
- Visual identifiers: plain bamboo-like fishing rod modified with alien metal rings, wrapped blue cord, small star-shaped charm, and a syrup-stained reel. It looks humble beside Veyro's psychic power.
- Continuity notes: Veyro carries it for retirement fishing and uses it as a visual symbol of refusing old wars, even when enemies interrupt him.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/veyros-fishing-rod-key-item.png`
