# Character Bible

All pages use realistic candid photo-comic style, documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism feel, natural imperfect framing, believable real-world lighting, photoreal action VFX, clean rectangular caption boxes, and no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Veyro Starfang

Tall lean alien man with pale lavender-gray skin, angular cheekbones, narrow silver eyes, swept-back midnight-blue crest hair, subtle star-speckled markings at the temples, small fang-like lower canines, and a severe tactical stare. He wears a sleeveless charcoal-blue retirement tunic, wrapped forearms, loose dark trousers, rope belt, and beach sandals. His psychic powers manifest as violet-blue hand auras, floating debris, lensing ripples, and pressure waves. He is stern and accidentally funny because he treats fishing, tea, and snacks with battlefield intensity.

Reference image: `../../../reference-images/veyro-starfang-character.png`

## Ronin Mochizuki

Japanese man in his late 40s to early 50s with warm tan skin, calm dark eyes, weathered face, black hair tied in a neat topknot with gray at the temples, short trimmed beard, and relaxed disciplined posture. He wears a faded indigo kimono jacket over practical garden clothes, pale sash, and straw sandals. Moonbite rests in a dark lacquer scabbard at his side only when danger demands it. He moves with quiet precision and keeps his expression mild even during absurd danger.

Reference image: `../../../reference-images/ronin-mochizuki-character.png`

## Malzareth the Demon Wizard

Gaunt demon sorcerer with ember-red skin, long black horns, sunken gold eyes, sharp cheekbones, black-violet robes, bone charms, clawed hands, and theatrical revenge posture. His magic appears as black smoke, violet flame, and red ritual cracks.

Reference image: `../../../reference-images/malzareth-demon-wizard-character.png`

## Lord Saccharon the Candymancer

Flamboyant human candy sorcerer in his 50s with powdered white swept hair, curled mustache, sharp theatrical eyes, rose-pink velvet coat with gold trim, striped waistcoat, lacquered boots, candy-cane staff, and jewel-like sugar rings. He poses like a stage magician and attacks with spun sugar ribbons, hard-candy sparks, molten caramel, and candy weapons.

Reference image: `../../../reference-images/lord-saccharon-candymancer-character.png`

## Dark Magic Candy Golem

Towering humanoid monster made of cracked black sugar, red licorice tendons, molten caramel seams, gumdrop growths, hard-candy armor plates, demon sigils, and glowing violet-red eyes. Broken pieces pull themselves back together unless bitten and eaten.

Reference image: `../../../reference-images/dark-magic-candy-golem-character.png`

## Candy Island

Tropical island paradise with white sugar beaches, turquoise syrup lagoons, caramel rivers, peppermint palm forests, chocolate cliffs, gumdrop mountains, mochi garden terraces, candy-colored birds, and humid golden sunlight. It must feel like a real island photographed in the world, with magical edible materials replacing normal landscape.

Reference image: `../../../reference-images/candy-island-setting.png`

## Mochi Garden Tea Hut

Small quiet tea hut beside Ronin's mochi garden, with low wooden porch, paper screens, stone path, mochi plants like soft white rounded shrubs, tea tray, garden shears, raked sugar gravel, and a view toward caramel water and peppermint palms.

Reference image: `../../../reference-images/mochi-garden-tea-hut-setting.png`

## Moonbite

Elegant katana with dark lacquer scabbard, moon-white blade, crescent-shaped silver guard, blue silk wrap, small bite-shaped notch near the guard, and faint cold light along the edge when focused against magic.

Reference image: `../../../reference-images/moonbite-key-item.png`

Plain bamboo-like fishing rod modified with alien metal rings, wrapped blue cord, small star-shaped charm, and a syrup-stained reel.
