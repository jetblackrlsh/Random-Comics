Series Title: Sugarblade & Starfang

Series premise: After saving their respective worlds, Veyro Starfang and Ronin Mochizuki retire to Candy Island, a tropical paradise of sugar beaches, caramel rivers, peppermint forests, chocolate cliffs, and gumdrop mountains. Veyro is a psychic alien warrior who once invaded Demon World to stop demonic chaos from spilling onto his home planet. Ronin is a master samurai who ended a global candy golem invasion led by a wicked sugar sorcerer. Both heroes are tired of destiny, war, and being treated like living legends.

Issue #1 premise: "The Dark Magic Candy Golem"

Veyro and Ronin have both recently arrived on Candy Island, each hoping for a quiet retirement. Their peace is shattered when Malzareth the Demon Wizard, Veyro's bitter arch-enemy, and Lord Saccharon the Candymancer, Ronin's flamboyant arch-enemy, discover their location. Malzareth and Saccharon combine demon magic and candymancy to create the Dark Magic Candy Golem, a regenerating monster made from cursed sugar, licorice tendons, molten caramel, and demon magic.

Veyro's psychic blasts and Ronin's sword strikes tear the golem apart, but it reforms. Even their combined ultimate technique fails. Exhausted, Ronin takes a desperate bite out of the monster. The bite does not regenerate. Veyro notices, joins in, and the retired warriors defeat the ultimate candy monster by eating it piece by piece. Malzareth and Saccharon flee, swearing revenge. Veyro and Ronin save Candy Island, but their quiet retirement is already doomed.
