# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce the retired-hero duo, Candy Island, the villains, and the absurd monster threat.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with photo-comic action-comedy energy.
- Visual beats: Veyro stands on a sugar beach with violet-blue psychic energy around one hand and a bent fishing rod in the other. Ronin stands beside him with Moonbite half drawn, tea cup still in his off hand. Behind them, the Dark Magic Candy Golem rises from caramel surf while Malzareth and Saccharon glare from opposite sides of a stormy candy-colored sky. Candy Island's peppermint palms, chocolate cliffs, and gumdrop mountains frame the scene.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Sugarblade & Starfang". Smaller issue title text: "The Dark Magic Candy Golem".
- Prompt notes: Realistic photo-comic cover, readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Retirement Plans

- Story role: Establish Veyro and Ronin as exhausted legends trying to live simply.
- Layout: Four panels, quiet comedic setup across Candy Island.
- Caption boxes: "Candy Island promised peace." "Veyro came to forget invasions." "Ronin came to forget wars." "Neither had asked for a neighbor with a legend."
- Visual beats: Veyro sits cross-legged beside a syrup lagoon, fishing with severe concentration while peppermint palms sway. Ronin trims soft mochi shrubs beside the tea hut. Veyro notices Ronin's sword with suspicion. Ronin notices Veyro levitating the fish by accident. They nod politely without smiling.

## Page 3: Old Enemies Land

- Story role: Bring both villains to Candy Island and establish their alliance.
- Layout: Five panels with split villain entrances and one shared pact.
- Caption boxes: "Retirement is hard to hide." "Malzareth followed old psychic scars." "Saccharon followed ruined sugar spells." "Revenge made them polite." "Polite did not last."
- Visual beats: Malzareth steps from a black-violet demon portal onto a sugar beach, angry and smoking. Saccharon arrives by caramel boat with spun sugar sails and candy sparks. They argue under chocolate cliffs while pointing toward the tea hut and lagoon. Their magic reluctantly interlocks over the sand. Final panel: demon sigils and candy glyphs form a shared circle.

## Page 4: The Golem Rises

- Story role: Create the Dark Magic Candy Golem and threaten the island.
- Layout: Six panels escalating from ritual to attack.
- Caption boxes: "Cursed sugar took shape." "Licorice became tendons." "Caramel became blood." "Demon magic became hunger." "Candy Island shook." "The first peaceful afternoon ended early."
- Visual beats: Black sugar pours into the ritual circle. Red licorice twists into muscle. Molten caramel fills cracks. Violet-red demon eyes ignite. The Dark Magic Candy Golem rises taller than peppermint palms. It smashes through a caramel bridge while islanders flee and candy birds scatter.

## Page 5: Separate Solutions

- Story role: Show Veyro and Ronin fighting separately and failing.
- Layout: Six action panels alternating Veyro and Ronin.
- Caption boxes: "Veyro chose restraint." "His restraint broke three cliffs." "Ronin drew Moonbite once." "One became many." "Legend still worked." "The monster worked faster."
- Visual beats: Veyro fires a violet-blue psychic blast that tears the golem's arm away. The arm splashes into cursed sugar and crawls back. Ronin slices the golem cleanly through with Moonbite's moon-white arc. The halves melt into caramel, reconnect, and punch both heroes into sugar sand. Veyro and Ronin exchange a grim look.

## Page 6: Ultimate Technique

- Story role: Escalate to cooperation and make the failure feel decisive.
- Layout: Five panels leading to one large failed finishing move.
- Caption boxes: "They were not a team." "They coordinated anyway." "Psychic force met Moonbite." "For one breath, legends agreed." "The golem disagreed."
- Visual beats: Veyro levitates broken candy boulders into place. Ronin plants his feet and raises Moonbite. Veyro channels violet-blue psychic energy through Moonbite's moon-white blade. The combined slash explodes through the golem in a brilliant diagonal blast. Large final panel: the scattered pieces reform behind the exhausted heroes, larger and angrier than before.

## Page 7: The Bite

- Story role: Reveal the comic solution through Ronin's desperate snack.
- Layout: Six panels shifting from exhaustion to discovery.
- Caption boxes: "Ronin had skipped lunch." "Desperation has strange manners." "He bit the monster." "The wound stayed gone." "Veyro saw the tactic." "Then he improved it badly."
- Visual beats: Ronin sits half-buried in sugar sand, hungry and annoyed, as the golem's caramel arm pins him nearby. He bites a chunk from the golem's wrist. Everyone freezes: Ronin, Veyro, Malzareth, Saccharon, and the golem. Close panel of the bite mark failing to regenerate. Veyro's silver eyes widen. Final panel: Veyro tackles the golem with terrifying tactical seriousness and bites into its shoulder.

## Page 8: Before Teatime

- Story role: Resolve the battle and launch the ongoing retirement problem.
- Layout: Four panels ending in a quiet final beat.
- Caption boxes: "The ultimate monster had one weakness." "It was dessert." "The villains fled hungry for revenge." "Veyro and Ronin saved Candy Island before teatime. They denied this meant anything."
- Visual beats: Veyro and Ronin eat through the golem piece by piece while it shrinks, still arranged as action-comedy photo-comic panels rather than gore. Malzareth and Saccharon recoil in horror and flee through separate portals, swearing revenge without speech bubbles. Islanders peek from behind peppermint trees as caramel rain settles. Final wide panel: Veyro and Ronin sit at the tea hut, sticky and exhausted, with tea and fishing rod between them, refusing to look like a team.
