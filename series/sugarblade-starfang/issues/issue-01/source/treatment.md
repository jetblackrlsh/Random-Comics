# Treatment

## Title

The Dark Magic Candy Golem

## Issue Number

1

## Logline

Two retired legendary warriors arrive on Candy Island for peace, only to defeat their enemies' regenerating candy golem by discovering the one thing it cannot recover from: being eaten.

## Central Conflict

Veyro and Ronin want retirement without missions, teams, or violence, but Malzareth and Saccharon force them to decide whether refusing hero work means ignoring danger or defending the peace they came to protect.

## Theme

Retirement from war does not mean indifference to the peace that war was supposed to protect.

## Ending

The Dark Magic Candy Golem is devoured piece by piece, leaving Malzareth and Saccharon horrified enough to flee. Veyro and Ronin return to tea and fishing with sticky hands, both denying that they have formed a team while Candy Island quietly cheers.

## Emotional Arc

Veyro begins rigidly committed to solitude and restraint. Ronin begins calmly committed to never drawing Moonbite again. The golem forces both men past their preferred retirement rituals into reluctant cooperation. By the end, they have not accepted hero work, but they recognize in each other the same exhausted duty to protect a hard-won peace.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, peaceful arrivals, enemy alliance, golem creation, failed separate attacks, failed combined technique, the bite discovery, and the edible victory that ruins their quiet retirement.
