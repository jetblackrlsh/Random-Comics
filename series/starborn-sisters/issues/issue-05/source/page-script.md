# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present the parallel revelations: the Universal Nexus as the academy's birthplace and the crown's weight on Amazon Island.
- Layout: Dramatic split cover composition with the Universal Nexus above and Amazon Island below.
- Caption boxes: None.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Starborn Sisters". Smaller issue title text: "The Place Between Worlds".
- Visual beats: Upper half shows Queen Amazoness and Princess Veyla standing on a floating green landmass in the Universal Nexus, wormhole tunnels encircling them, vines pulling fragments of technology and stone toward a glowing academy foundation. Lower half shows Princess Amazoness helping Lysandra up from the sand while Lysandra's sword lies dropped nearby. The Crown of the Amazons glows between them like a heavy promise.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo, no caption boxes.

## Page 2: The Center

- Story role: Establish the Universal Nexus and Amazoness's awe.
- Layout: Four large panels, space spectacle and cockpit reaction.
- Caption boxes: Panel 1 top caption: "The wormhole released them into the center of everything." Panel 2 top caption: "Tunnels of light surrounded the Nexus, each one a road to a different piece of the universe." Panel 3 top caption: "Organic islands, broken machines, crystal fields, and impossible soil drifted together in silence." Panel 4 top caption: "Queen Amazoness had ruled a hidden island for ages, but she had never seen a place this open."

Panel 3: floating materials from across the universe: plants, metal, crystal, stone, ancient machinery. Panel 4: Amazoness stunned inside the cockpit, Veyla calmer at controls.

Panel 3 top caption: "Amazoness looked at the floating island and asked where those students would sleep, train, or belong." Panel 4 top caption: "Veyla smiled and told her to watch carefully." Panel 5 top caption: "Then roots reached into the impossible debris, and the first foundation of Starborn Academy began to gather."
- Visual beats: Panel 1: Amazoness watching many civilian ships pass through distant wormholes. Panel 2: Veyla points to diverse ships crossing the Nexus.
