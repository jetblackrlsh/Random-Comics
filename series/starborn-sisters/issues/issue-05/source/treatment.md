# Treatment

## Title

The Place Between Worlds

## Issue Number

5

## Logline

Queen Amazoness reaches the Universal Nexus and questions how a floating wilderness can become a school, while Princess Amazoness turns Lysandra's failed challenge into the first test of a more open crown.

## Central Conflict

Amazoness and Lysandra are both forced to reconsider what a throne can become. Amazoness must understand why an unruled place between worlds is safer than a capital city, while Lysandra must accept that a home can become a prison when leadership refuses to let the world in.

## Theme

Freedom and duty can exist together: a crown may be a burden without being a punishment, and a borderless school may protect the galaxy better than any fortress.

## Ending

Veyla proves the Universal Nexus can become the academy by using plant life to draw scattered technology and floating landmasses into a first living foundation. On Amazon Island, Princess Amazoness accepts the crown's burden but asks Lysandra to become her advisor, turning an attempted murder into a test of trust and change.

## Emotional Arc

Queen Amazoness begins awestruck and skeptical, unable to see why the Universal Nexus matters beyond spectacle. Veyla guides her toward the political truth: the academy must begin where no empire can claim a throne. Lysandra begins collapsed under the shame of realizing Princess Amazoness may be right. Princess Amazoness moves from defensive anger into deliberate leadership, accepting a crown she does not want because she knows the island must open.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, arrival at the Universal Nexus, Lysandra confronted by the idea of home as prison, landing on a living landmass, Princess Amazoness naming the crown's burden, Veyla explaining the academy and galactic politics, Princess Amazoness asking Lysandra to advise her, and Veyla building the first foundation of Starborn Academy.
