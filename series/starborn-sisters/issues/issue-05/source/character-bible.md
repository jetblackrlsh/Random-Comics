# Character Bible

All pages use realistic candid photo-comic style, documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism feel, natural imperfect framing, believable real-world lighting, photoreal action VFX, clean rectangular caption boxes, and no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Queen Amazoness

Powerful athletic woman in her 30s with warm brown skin, dark curly hair braided into a crown, steady dark eyes, gold circlet or Crown of the Amazons, bronze-and-white Amazon battle armor, red cloak, engraved bracers, and a short sword at her side. In this issue she is awestruck, skeptical, and learning to think like a founder instead of only a queen.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/queen-amazoness-character.png`

## Princess Veyla

Gentle alien woman in her early 20s with luminous teal skin, soft silver-white hair in loose waves, large violet eyes, faint bioluminescent freckles, leaf-vein markings along her hands and neck, flowing pale green and pearl diplomatic gown, delicate vine jewelry. She is confident in the Nexus, pilots the repaired pod, and uses plant powers to pull organic and technological fragments into the academy's first foundation.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-veyla-character.png`

## Princess Amazoness

Young adult Amazon woman by appearance with deep brown skin, bright observant dark eyes, long black curls tied back with a gold training band, athletic build, white-and-gold traveling tunic, bronze bracers, light red cloak, satchel of scrolls and star charts, and a travel-worn short sword. She does not want the crown, but she accepts her birthright because she knows the island must open to the world.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-amazoness-character.png`

## High Advisor Lysandra

Older Amazon warrior woman in her late 40s by appearance with olive-brown skin, stern gray eyes, silver-streaked black hair in a tight braided bun, scar across one cheek, dark bronze command armor, deep crimson sash, and engraved sword. In this issue she is shaken, ashamed, and beginning to understand that strength alone cannot answer the island's future.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/high-advisor-lysandra-character.png`

## Amazon Island Shore

Hidden island shoreline with turquoise water, white sand, black volcanic rocks, lush palms, flowering vines, bronze watchtowers, ancient marble stairs, a faint shimmering invisibility barrier over the ocean, and Amazon onlookers watching the aftermath of the duel.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-island-shore-setting.png`

## Universal Nexus

Vast center-of-the-universe region surrounded by luminous wormhole tunnels, floating landmasses, living alien plants, mineral islands, ship wreckage, ancient machinery, ice, crystal, metal, soil, coral-like organic growth, and materials from worlds across the cosmos. It feels beautiful, strange, unclaimed, and accessible from every direction.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/universal-nexus-setting.png`

## Repaired Two-Seat Escape Pod

Larger rebuilt version of Princess Veyla's pearlescent teardrop-shaped escape pod, reinforced with Amazon Island mystical metals: bronze-gold ribbing, engraved wave-and-star motifs, translucent green alien controls, repaired clear canopy, vine-like alien circuitry interwoven with glowing Amazon metal seams, compact two-seat cockpit, and a hull that catches both starlight and magical gold reflections.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/repaired-two-seat-escape-pod-key-item.png`

## Crown of the Amazons

Ancient gold circlet with bronze laurel points, small red gemstones, engraved wave-and-star motifs, and subtle magical glow. In this issue it is the emotional weight between Princess Amazoness and Lysandra, not a trophy to be won.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/crown-of-the-amazons-key-item.png`
