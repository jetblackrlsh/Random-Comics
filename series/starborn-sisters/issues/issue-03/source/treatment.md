# Treatment

## Title

The Teacher's Crown

## Issue Number

3

## Logline

Princess Veyla asks Queen Amazoness to teach the galaxy instead of waging war, forcing Amazoness to choose a future beyond the island and provoking her advisor's warrior-first challenge to the crown.

## Central Conflict

Veyla sees that Amazon teaching can help powerful women across the universe defend their worlds, while High Advisor Lysandra sees Amazon identity and leadership as something proven by combat.

## Theme

A culture built for battle can become something larger when it understands that teaching is also a form of defense.

## Ending

Lysandra storms away after Amazoness rejects the idea of fighting for rulership. Amazoness tells Veyla that her daughter, Princess Amazoness, left the island to study the wider world, and that offering her the crown will finally bring her home while Amazoness begins her own adventure beyond the island.

## Emotional Arc

Veyla begins as a victorious outsider asking whether one island's knowledge can become a galactic answer. Amazoness begins still thinking of the Amazons as warriors with limited reach, but she accepts that her deepest gift is teaching. Lysandra begins as a loyal advisor and ends as a wounded traditionalist who cannot accept a crown passed through trust rather than conquest.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Veyla's proposal, Amazoness accepting the teacher role, the crown announcement, Princess Amazoness defended as heir, Lysandra's challenge, Amazoness rejecting rule by combat, and the final reveal that the new queen is away learning about the world beyond the island.
