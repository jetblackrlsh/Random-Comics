# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present the ideological clash over whether Amazons are only warriors or teachers for the galaxy.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image, no caption boxes.
- Visual beats: Queen Amazoness stands on the Amazon shore holding the glowing Crown of the Amazons in one hand and lowering her spear with the other. Princess Veyla stands beside her, pointing toward a holographic star map rising from the damaged escape pod. High Advisor Lysandra stands opposite them with sword drawn, angry and wounded. In a faint destiny-like vision above the island, Princess Amazoness appears as a young traveling Amazon with scrolls and star charts, walking beyond the shoreline.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Starborn Sisters". Smaller issue title text: "The Teacher's Crown".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo, no caption boxes.

## Page 2: Not a War

- Story role: Continue from Issue #2 and introduce Veyla's argument that the galaxy needs teachers, not another army.
- Layout: Four panels, aftermath on the shore.
- Caption boxes: "The warrior had fallen, but his empire still stood." "Amazoness could promise courage, not an intergalactic war." "Veyla answered that the universe did not need another war." "It needed women who knew what their own power could become."
- Visual beats: Grax remains unconscious under Amazon guard near the escape pod. Amazoness speaks gravely to Veyla while Amazons listen. Veyla points to the thorned vine and drifting seeds as proof of learning. A holographic star map rises from the pod showing many threatened worlds.

## Page 3: More Than Warriors

- Story role: Show Veyla naming Amazoness as a teacher and connecting her own victory to shared knowledge.
- Layout: Five panels with close emotional beats.
- Caption boxes: "Amazoness said the Amazons were warriors." "Veyla saw the spear, but also the lesson behind it." "On her world, flowers had never warned her about thorns." "One island's knowledge had changed what her power meant." "So she asked Amazoness to teach the universe what she had taught one princess."
- Visual beats: Amazoness gestures to armed Amazons and the shoreline defenses. Veyla touches a harmless flower, then a thorned vine, comparing both with wonder. Close panel of Amazoness realizing the words land. Amazons exchange nervous looks. Veyla offers Amazoness a glowing resource crystal or star-map key from the pod.

## Page 4: The Crown Will Pass

- Story role: Amazoness accepts quickly and shocks the Amazons by announcing succession.
- Layout: Four panels with crowd reaction.
- Caption boxes: "Amazoness agreed before fear could make a throne of hesitation." "The Amazons understood the mercy of the choice." "They feared the cost of it." "Their queen answered with a different shock: she would not split her attention. She would pass down the crown."
- Visual beats: Amazoness clasps Veyla's hand in agreement. Amazons murmur with worried faces. Amazoness removes or touches the glowing Crown of the Amazons. Wide panel of shocked Amazons as the sun catches the crown.

## Page 5: The Daughter Beyond the Island

- Story role: Introduce Princess Amazoness as heir and challenge the Amazons' assumptions about age and ability.
- Layout: Five panels, including a memory/vision inset.
- Caption boxes: "Lysandra named the oldest fear: the princess was only a child." "Amazoness named the older truth: a few thousand years can still earn a crown." "She looked to Veyla, nearly the same age by the measure that mattered." "If one young princess could save her world, why could another not rule an island?" "Some Amazons believed. Some were not ready."
- Visual beats: Lysandra steps forward, stern, questioning Amazoness. Amazoness stands firm with the crown. Veyla listens, surprised by the comparison. A memory-like inset shows Princess Amazoness traveling beyond the island with scrolls and star charts. Amazons look divided.

## Page 6: The Advisor's Challenge

- Story role: Turn the disagreement into open conflict with Lysandra's demand to fight for the crown.
- Layout: Five panels, rising tension.
- Caption boxes: "Lysandra had stood beside the queen for ages." "Loyalty had hardened into entitlement." "Her sword left its sheath." "She begged for one trial: let her fight the daughter for the crown." "For a moment, the old way sounded almost reasonable."
- Visual beats: Lysandra's angry face, scar visible. Her hand tightens on her engraved sword. The sword flashes free in the sun. Amazons recoil as Lysandra points the blade toward the imagined heir's place. Veyla watches with alarm while Amazoness's expression hardens.

## Page 7: Not the Empire

- Story role: Amazoness rejects rule by combat and redefines the Amazon calling as teaching.
- Layout: Four large panels, verbal confrontation.
- Caption boxes: "Amazoness asked if Lysandra could hear herself." "A crown won by force was the empire's lesson, not theirs." "Lysandra screamed that Amazons were warriors." "Amazoness looked from Veyla to her own people and chose the harder truth: Amazons were teachers."
- Visual beats: Amazoness steps toward Lysandra, stopping the challenge with presence rather than a weapon. Close panel of the Galactic Empire insignia on Grax's armor near them, visually echoing rule by strength. Lysandra screams with sword raised. Amazoness lowers her spear and points toward the Amazons as students and guardians, not conquerors.

## Page 8: Homeward Crown, Outward Road

- Story role: End with Lysandra leaving and Amazoness choosing the road beyond the island.
- Layout: Five panels, emotional cliffhanger.
- Caption boxes: "Lysandra drove her sword into the sand." "Then she vanished into the island's green shadows." "Veyla asked whether the island could survive without its queen." "Amazoness trusted the daughter who had gone to learn the world beyond it." "The crown would bring Princess Amazoness home. For the first time, Amazoness would be the one to leave."
- Visual beats: Lysandra stabs her sword into the sand in fury. She launches into the brush with superhuman acrobatic skill, disappearing among palms. Veyla quietly asks Amazoness if things will be okay. Amazoness smiles with faith, holding the glowing crown. Final wide panel: Amazoness and Veyla face the ocean and star map together while the empty path into the jungle hints at Princess Amazoness's return and Lysandra's unresolved anger.
