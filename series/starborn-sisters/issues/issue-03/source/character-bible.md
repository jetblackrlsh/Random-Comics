# Character Bible

All pages use realistic candid photo-comic style, documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism feel, natural imperfect framing, believable real-world lighting, photoreal action VFX, clean rectangular caption boxes, and no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Queen Amazoness

Powerful athletic woman in her 30s with warm brown skin, dark curly hair braided into a crown, steady dark eyes, gold circlet or Crown of the Amazons, bronze-and-white Amazon battle armor, red cloak, engraved bracers, spear or short sword, calm commanding posture. In this issue she accepts that the Amazons' true calling can be teaching and plans to pass the crown to her daughter.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/queen-amazoness-character.png`

## Princess Veyla

Gentle alien woman in her early 20s with luminous teal skin, soft silver-white hair in loose waves, large violet eyes, faint bioluminescent freckles, leaf-vein markings along her hands and neck, flowing pale green and pearl diplomatic gown, delicate vine jewelry, exhausted but hopeful after defeating Grax. Her eyes glow soft green when she controls plants.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-veyla-character.png`

## High Advisor Lysandra

Older Amazon warrior woman in her late 40s by appearance with olive-brown skin, stern gray eyes, silver-streaked black hair in a tight braided bun, scar across one cheek, dark bronze command armor, deep crimson sash, engraved sword, rigid military posture. She is proud, wounded, and furious when Amazoness chooses Princess Amazoness as successor.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/high-advisor-lysandra-character.png`

## Princess Amazoness

Young adult Amazon woman by appearance with deep brown skin, bright observant dark eyes, long black curls tied back with a gold training band, athletic build, white-and-gold traveling tunic, bronze bracers, light red cloak, satchel of scrolls and star charts, curious open posture. She is Queen Amazoness's daughter, a few thousand years old, away from the island learning about the world beyond it.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-amazoness-character.png`

## The Amazons

Diverse women warriors with bronze armor, white linen tunics, red or gold sashes, braided or tied-back hair, magical spears, shields, bows, and alert defensive formations. In this issue they are nervous about Amazoness splitting her attention and shocked by her abdication plan.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-warriors-character.png`

## Amazon Island Shore

Hidden island shoreline with turquoise water, white sand, black volcanic rocks, lush palms, flowering vines, bronze watchtowers, ancient marble stairs, a faint shimmering invisibility barrier over the ocean, and the damaged escape pod nearby.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-island-shore-setting.png`

## Crown of the Amazons

Ancient gold circlet with bronze laurel points, small red gemstones, engraved wave-and-star motifs, and subtle magical glow. It represents stewardship and teaching, not conquest.
