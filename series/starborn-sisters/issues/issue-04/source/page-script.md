# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present the split A/B plot: Queen Amazoness and Veyla facing space hazards while Princess Amazoness clashes with Lysandra on the island.
- Layout: Dramatic split cover composition with diagonal divide between deep space and Amazon Island shore.
- Caption boxes: None.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Starborn Sisters". Smaller issue title text: "Through the Broken Belt".
- Visual beats: Upper half shows the repaired two-seat escape pod banking through jagged asteroids toward a luminous wormhole, with Queen Amazoness visible through the canopy and Princess Veyla at the controls. Lower half shows Princess Amazoness and High Advisor Lysandra crossing swords on a rocking boat at the invisibility barrier, shockwave rippling across turquoise water. The glowing Crown of the Amazons appears small and ghostlike between the two halves.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo, no caption boxes.
