# Treatment

## Title

Through the Broken Belt

## Issue Number

4

## Logline

Queen Amazoness and Princess Veyla survive an asteroid field by joining tactical knowledge with alien piloting, while Princess Amazoness reaches home only to be attacked by Lysandra for a crown she does not even want.

## Central Conflict

Amazoness has left the island to teach the galaxy, but space travel strips away her usual warrior options. At the same time, Princess Amazoness arrives to inherit a throne and is forced into a duel that proves the island still mistakes rulership for combat.

## Theme

Power becomes wiser when it accepts limits: a warrior can guide without swinging a sword, a pilot can trust another mind, and a future queen can challenge a trapped culture by refusing the crown's assumptions.

## Ending

Amazoness and Veyla pass safely through the asteroid belt and enter the wormhole because they combine Amazon tactical discipline with alien flight skill. On the shore below, Princess Amazoness reveals she does not want the crown because the island feels like a fishbowl; the Amazons study the world but rarely experience it.

## Emotional Arc

Queen Amazoness begins proud and worried as she sees her daughter return just as she leaves. In space, panic and frustration force her to discover a new role: not the warrior who strikes, but the strategist who sees. Veyla begins as the only one who can operate the pod and ends grateful for Amazoness's battle-trained perception. Princess Amazoness begins as a returning daughter and becomes an unwilling heir who names the cost of isolation. Lysandra begins certain that only battle can decide the throne and ends shaken because the supposed rival does not want the prize.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, the barrier crossing and launch, Lysandra's boat attack, the asteroid field blocking the wormhole, Amazoness forced to think instead of strike, Princess Amazoness rejecting the crown's assumptions, Amazoness and Veyla navigating as one, and the split ending of wormhole success beside an unresolved island duel.
