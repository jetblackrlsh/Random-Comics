# Character Bible

All pages use realistic candid photo-comic style, documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism feel, natural imperfect framing, believable real-world lighting, photoreal action VFX, clean rectangular caption boxes, and no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Queen Amazoness

Powerful athletic woman in her 30s with warm brown skin, dark curly hair braided into a crown, steady dark eyes, gold circlet or Crown of the Amazons, bronze-and-white Amazon battle armor, red cloak, engraved bracers, short sword at her side, calm commanding posture that cracks into worry when she watches her daughter from the pod. In this issue she cannot use physical force in space, so her tactical mind becomes the weapon.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/queen-amazoness-character.png`

## Princess Veyla

Gentle alien woman in her early 20s with luminous teal skin, soft silver-white hair in loose waves, large violet eyes, faint bioluminescent freckles, leaf-vein markings along her hands and neck, flowing pale green and pearl diplomatic gown, delicate vine jewelry. She is newly confident after defeating Grax, focused at the repaired escape pod controls, and amazed by Amazoness's battlefield perception.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-veyla-character.png`

## Princess Amazoness

Young adult Amazon woman by appearance with deep brown skin, bright observant dark eyes, long black curls tied back with a gold training band, athletic build, white-and-gold traveling tunic, bronze bracers, light red cloak, satchel of scrolls and star charts, and a travel-worn short sword. She returns by small boat through the invisibility barrier and fights with skill while insisting she does not want to be queen.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-amazoness-character.png`

## High Advisor Lysandra

Older Amazon warrior woman in her late 40s by appearance with olive-brown skin, stern gray eyes, silver-streaked black hair in a tight braided bun, scar across one cheek, dark bronze command armor, deep crimson sash, engraved sword, rigid military posture. She is furious that Princess Amazoness was chosen as heir and attacks before the princess can step onto shore.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/high-advisor-lysandra-character.png`

## Amazon Island Shore

Hidden island shoreline with turquoise water, white sand, black volcanic rocks, lush palms, flowering vines, bronze watchtowers, ancient marble stairs, a faint shimmering invisibility barrier over the ocean, and Amazon onlookers gathering near the landing beach.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-island-shore-setting.png`

## Amazon Island Invisibility Barrier

Translucent curtain of bent sunlight over the ocean, subtle gold motes, mirrored waves, distorted horizon line, and magical ripples when Princess Amazoness's boat passes through it.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-island-invisibility-barrier-key-item.png`

## Repaired Two-Seat Escape Pod

Larger rebuilt version of Princess Veyla's pearlescent teardrop-shaped escape pod, now reinforced with Amazon Island mystical metals: bronze-gold ribbing, engraved wave-and-star motifs, translucent green alien controls, repaired clear canopy, vine-like alien circuitry interwoven with glowing Amazon metal seams, compact two-seat cockpit, and a hull that catches both starlight and magical gold reflections.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/repaired-two-seat-escape-pod-key-item.png`

## Crown of the Amazons

Ancient gold circlet with bronze laurel points, small red gemstones, engraved wave-and-star motifs, and subtle magical glow. It is mostly symbolic in this issue: the absent prize that causes Lysandra's attack and Princess Amazoness's refusal.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/crown-of-the-amazons-key-item.png`
