# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Starborn Sisters as a cosmic superhero origin and frame Issue #1 as Amazoness's first alien encounter.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with realistic photo-comic superhero energy.
- Visual beats: Queen Amazoness stands on the hidden Amazon shore with spear lowered but ready, red cloak in sea wind. Princess Veyla kneels beside her scorched pearlescent escape pod, eyes glowing green as small flowers grow through sand. Commander Grax Vorn hovers above the ocean outside a shimmering gold invisibility barrier, black armor and red insignia lit by cosmic fire. A faint ghostly silhouette of future Starborn Academy appears high in the wormhole-filled sky as a destiny image, not a physical building in the scene.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Starborn Sisters". Smaller issue title text: "The First Starfall".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: A World Falls

- Story role: Establish the Galactic Empire's conquest and Veyla's desperate escape toward Earth.
- Layout: Four large panels, cinematic pursuit from planet to space to Earth.
- Caption boxes: "Before Starborn Academy had a name, another world was almost lost." "Princess Veyla fled with the last hope her people could spare." "The Galactic Empire sent Commander Grax Vorn to drag that hope back." "Her pod searched the blue planet for power, and found one impossible island."
- Visual beats: A distant alien city burns under dark imperial ships. Veyla straps into the pearlescent escape pod with tears and determination. Grax launches after her through orbit, thruster boots flaring red. The pod's green scanner projects a map of Earth with a bright pulse over the hidden Amazon island.

## Page 3: Through the Hidden Sea

- Story role: Show the escape pod breaching the invisibility barrier and crashing onto Amazon Island.
- Layout: Five panels with fast descent and sudden first-contact tension.
- Caption boxes: "The pod did not slow for the ocean." "It passed through sunlight that had been taught to lie." "The island appeared where waves should have been." "The crash shook marble, palms, and magic." "The Amazons came armed."
- Visual beats: The pod streaks toward empty ocean while Veyla panics behind the canopy. The hull ripples through a golden invisible curtain. Amazon Island suddenly appears below: shoreline, towers, marble stairs. The pod crashes into white sand and throws up seawater and steam. Amazons surround it with glowing spears and shields.

## Page 4: All Women Are Welcome

- Story role: Establish Amazoness's compassion, Veyla's beauty and fear, and the first moment of trust.
- Layout: Four large panels with room for expressions and captions.
- Caption boxes: "The stranger stepped from the broken star-shell." "She was not an invader." "She was a queen with nowhere left to run." "Amazoness told her the oldest law of the island: all women in fear may stand under Amazon protection."
- Visual beats: Veyla exits the cracked escape pod, luminous teal skin and silver hair visible in harsh beach light. Amazons lower their weapons in awe but remain alert. Queen Amazoness steps through the formation, calm and regal. Amazoness offers her hand while Veyla trembles and points toward the sky.

## Page 5: What She Feared

- Story role: Reveal Grax searching above the barrier and explain the Galactic Empire.
- Layout: Five panels moving from hidden sky threat to intimate explanation.
- Caption boxes: "Veyla was not afraid of the Amazons." "Above the waves, her hunter searched blind." "Grax served an empire that called conquest proof of worth." "Planets joined by force, or broke under it." "Amazoness listened, and measured the shape of a larger war."
- Visual beats: The Amazons look up from behind palm shadows. Grax flies over the ocean beyond the shimmering barrier, unable to see the island. Close shot of Veyla explaining through fear, hands shaking with green light. A small hologram from the pod shows imperial ships over alien worlds. Amazoness watches the sky with a sharpened, thoughtful expression.

## Page 6: More Than Flowers

- Story role: Teach Veyla that studying other worlds can expand her power and plant knowledge.
- Layout: Four wide panels with thoughtful mentor energy and vivid botanical examples.
- Caption boxes: "Amazoness asked what power Veyla carried." "Veyla grew beauty from barren sand." "On her world, plants had always been gentle." "Amazoness taught her Earth's lesson: thorns protect, poisons warn, and some flowers eat what threatens them."
- Visual beats: Veyla's violet eyes glow green as small flowers bloom along the shoreline. Veyla looks ashamed, holding a harmless blossom. Amazoness kneels and shows thorny vines, poison-colored leaves, and a carnivorous flower growing near rocks. Veyla stares in wonder while Amazoness gestures beyond the island, explaining that hidden people still study the wider world.

## Page 7: The Barrier Breaks

- Story role: Force Veyla to apply the lesson when Grax finally finds the island.
- Layout: Five action panels, escalating from breach to first attack.
- Caption boxes: "The lesson ended when the hunter found the lie in the light." "The Amazons raised their weapons." "Amazoness ordered them to stand down." "Veyla stood shaking between a conquered world and a hidden one." "Then she chose to grow something dangerous."
- Visual beats: Grax punches through the golden invisibility barrier, sunlight cracking around his armor. Amazons form ranks with spears and bows. Amazoness lifts one hand to hold them back. Veyla steps forward in front of the pod, eyes blazing green. A giant strange flower erupts from the sand between Veyla and Grax as he charges at incredible speed.

## Page 8: Poison Is Not Enough

- Story role: End on the specified cliffhanger: Veyla's first adapted tactic fails, but Amazoness still believes she can win.
- Layout: Six panels ending with a wide cliffhanger beat.
- Caption boxes: "The flower opened like a shield." "Its gas rolled over the shore." "Grax walked through it without breathing." "His hands struck the air like thunder." "Veyla fell, and caught herself with a vine." "Amazoness did not move. Her eyes said the lesson was not over."
- Visual beats: Grax collides with the giant flower bulb and it bursts poisonous green vapor. Amazons cover their mouths and retreat. Grax emerges unharmed from the gas, armor wet with poison mist. He claps his gauntlets and sends a visible shockwave across the sand. Veyla is blasted backward but summons a vine that catches her above the rocks. Final wide panel: Veyla hangs from the vine facing Grax's next charge, Amazons worried behind her, Amazoness calm and confident in the foreground.
