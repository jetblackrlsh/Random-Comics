# Treatment

## Title

The First Starfall

## Issue Number

1

## Logline

Before Starborn Academy exists, Queen Amazoness shelters an alien princess whose pursuit by the Galactic Empire turns Earth's hidden island into the first lesson of an interstellar heroic future.

## Central Conflict

Princess Veyla believes her plant powers are too gentle to stop Commander Grax Vorn, while Queen Amazoness believes power becomes stronger when someone studies beyond the limits of their own world.

## Theme

No world becomes strong by hiding from every other world; knowledge shared across borders can turn a gentle gift into a heroic defense.

## Ending

The issue ends on a deliberate cliffhanger. Veyla's first Earth-inspired tactic, a poisonous flower, fails because Grax's species does not breathe. Grax hurls her backward with a shockwave, but Veyla catches herself with a vine while Amazoness refuses to intervene, confident that Veyla can still adapt.

## Emotional Arc

Veyla begins as a terrified refugee who sees her powers as beautiful but useless in war. Amazoness begins as a hidden queen defending her people from the unknown. Their meeting shifts both women: Veyla learns that her power contains possibilities she never imagined, and Amazoness discovers that Earth's hidden knowledge may matter to worlds beyond Earth.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Galactic Empire pursuit, crash through the barrier, first contact with the Amazons, Veyla's fear and gentle power, Amazoness's lesson about Earth plants, Grax breaching the barrier, and Veyla's failed first tactic ending on the cliffhanger.
