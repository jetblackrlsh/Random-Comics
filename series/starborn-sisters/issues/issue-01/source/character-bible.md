# Character Bible

All pages use realistic candid photo-comic style, documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism feel, natural imperfect framing, believable real-world lighting, photoreal action VFX, clean rectangular caption boxes, and no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Queen Amazoness

Powerful athletic woman in her 30s with warm brown skin, dark curly hair braided into a crown, steady dark eyes, gold circlet, bronze-and-white Amazon battle armor, red cloak, engraved bracers, spear or short sword, calm commanding posture. She is protective, curious, and controlled under pressure.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/queen-amazoness-character.png`

## Princess Veyla

Gentle alien woman in her early 20s with luminous teal skin, soft silver-white hair in loose waves, large violet eyes, faint bioluminescent freckles, leaf-vein markings along her hands and neck, flowing pale green and pearl diplomatic gown, delicate vine jewelry, frightened but compassionate expression. Her eyes glow soft green when she controls plants. Her plants can grow instantly from sand, rock cracks, or soil.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-veyla-character.png`

## Commander Grax Vorn

Towering alien warrior with charcoal-gray armored hide, tusk-like cheek ridges, glowing ember eyes, bald ridged skull, heavy black imperial armor, red Galactic Empire chest insignia, thruster boots, scarred gauntlets, predatory forward-leaning posture. He can fly, survive without air, move at high speed, and create shockwaves with his clapping hands.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/commander-grax-vorn-character.png`

## The Amazons

Diverse women warriors with bronze armor, white linen tunics, red or gold sashes, braided or tied-back hair, magical spears, shields, bows, and alert defensive formations. They are wary of outsiders but welcome frightened women under Amazoness's law.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-warriors-character.png`

## Amazon Island Shore

Hidden island shoreline with turquoise water, white sand, black volcanic rocks, lush palms, flowering vines, bronze watchtowers, ancient marble stairs, and a faint shimmering invisibility barrier over the ocean.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-island-shore-setting.png`

## Princess Veyla's Escape Pod

Pearlescent teardrop-shaped escape pod with green sensor lights, cracked transparent canopy, vine-like alien circuitry, scorched hull seams, emergency beacon glow, and sand-and-seawater damage after impact.

## Amazon Island Invisibility Barrier

Translucent curtain of bent sunlight over the ocean, subtle gold motes, mirrored waves, distorted horizon line, and magical ripples when spacecraft or warriors pass through it.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-island-invisibility-barrier-key-item.png`

## Galactic Empire Insignia

Red angular star-claw emblem stamped into dark metal armor, banners, ship plating, and weapons; harsh geometric lines that suggest conquest and hierarchy.
