# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Issue #2 as the action continuation of Veyla versus Grax on Amazon Island.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with realistic photo-comic superhero action energy.
- Visual beats: Princess Veyla hangs from a giant vine turned into a dandelion crown, surrounded by enormous floating white seeds. Commander Grax Vorn rockets upward through the seed cloud with bleeding thorn-cut hands and blurred double-image motion. Queen Amazoness stands below on the beach, spear lowered, watching with fierce confidence while Amazons look worried. The damaged escape pod and shimmering barrier sit in the background.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Starborn Sisters". Smaller issue title text: "Seeds Above the Shore".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Thorns in the Hands

- Story role: Resume the cliffhanger and show Veyla adapting instantly after the poison gas failed.
- Layout: Five panels, action rising from the vine to Grax's hands.
- Caption boxes: "The last lesson had not failed." "Grax seized the vine that saved her." "Veyla answered before fear could answer for her." "Thorns opened under his grip." "The empire's warrior bled, and laughed at the pain."
- Visual beats: Veyla hangs from the thick vine above black rocks. Grax grabs the vine with both armored hands and starts to pull. Veyla's eyes glow green as sharp thorns erupt around his grip. Close panel of blood on Grax's palms and thorns. Grax snarls with contempt, still pulling.

## Page 3: Fight or Flight

- Story role: Let the Amazons doubt Veyla while Amazoness explains why she still believes in her.
- Layout: Four large panels with action and mentor commentary.
- Caption boxes: "The Amazons begged their queen to end it." "They saw a refugee, not a warrior." "Amazoness saw the truth moving through her." "Veyla had fled through the stars. Now she was fighting with everything she had."
- Visual beats: Amazons plead with Amazoness while pointing toward Veyla. Amazoness stands unmoved with spear lowered. Grax plants his feet in the sand and rips the vine's roots from the ground. Veyla clings to the wildly whipping vine above the shoreline.

## Page 4: Seeds in the Air

- Story role: Transform the uprooted vine into a new survival tactic.
- Layout: Five panels, kinetic motion leading to airborne concealment.
- Caption boxes: "Grax turned the vine into a weapon." "Veyla turned the weapon into a way out." "The vine flowered where no flower should survive." "Seeds broke loose like white lanterns." "When Grax looked up, his prisoner was gone."
- Visual beats: Grax whips the giant vine in a violent arc, smashing sand and surf. Veyla hangs on for dear life near the top. Her green power spreads through the vine tip. The vine tip blooms into a huge dandelion head. Giant white dandelion seeds scatter across the sky as Veyla vanishes among them.

## Page 5: Too Many Targets

- Story role: Show Veyla using the seed cloud and Amazoness guiding the warriors' attention upward.
- Layout: Five panels, vertical sky search.
- Caption boxes: "Grax searched the ground for a fallen enemy." "Amazoness pointed higher." "The sky had become a field." "Grax rose into it, hunting one seed among hundreds." "Veyla held still and let the wind carry her."
- Visual beats: Grax scans the beach and broken vine roots. Amazoness points up toward the sky. Wide panel of enormous dandelion seeds drifting down over the Amazon shore. Grax flies through the seed cloud, knocking seeds aside. Veyla hides by gripping one floating seed stem, gown and hair moving in the wind.

## Page 6: Poison in the Blood

- Story role: Reveal that the thorn cuts were the real attack while Grax's senses fail.
- Layout: Six panels with disorientation and escalating aerial danger.
- Caption boxes: "The gas had failed because Grax did not breathe." "The thorns had only needed skin." "Poison moved where pride refused to look." "His vision blurred." "The sky split into doubles." "Then he saw her, or thought he did."
- Visual beats: Close panel of Grax's bleeding hands under armor seams, faint green poison veins spreading. Grax wavers midair, suddenly unsteady. His vision distorts the seeds into doubles. He shakes his head in confusion. He spots what looks like Veyla on a floating seed. Double-image panel shows one real Veyla hidden farther back and one illusion-like blur ahead.

## Page 7: The Empty Strike

- Story role: Pay off the poison-and-seed tactic with Grax's defeat.
- Layout: Five action panels ending in the crash.
- Caption boxes: "Grax chose speed over thought." "He struck the wrong sky." "The image broke apart into drifting seeds." "The poison finished what the thorns began." "The empire fell onto Amazon sand."
- Visual beats: Grax darts through the sky at the false Veyla with red thrusters blazing. His body passes through a cluster of dandelion seeds instead of Veyla. The seeds scatter and reveal no one there. Grax loses consciousness in midair. He crashes hard into the shore, throwing up sand near the damaged escape pod as Amazons rush in.

## Page 8: The Next Idea

- Story role: Explain Veyla's strategy, celebrate the victory, and end on the galaxy-saving cliffhanger.
- Layout: Four panels, aftermath with clear emotional turn.
- Caption boxes: "The Amazons surrounded the fallen warrior." "Veyla descended on a single seed." "She had not beaten him with flowers. She had beaten him with study." "Then she looked to Amazoness and saw a future larger than one island: to save the galaxy, she would need help."
- Visual beats: Amazons surround unconscious Grax with weapons ready, confused by his defeat. Veyla descends gently on a giant dandelion seed, exhausted but alive. Veyla shows the poisonous thorn scratches on Grax's hand while Amazons realize the strategy and begin cheering. Final panel: Veyla faces Queen Amazoness on the shore with the seed cloud above them and says through a caption that she has an idea to save the galaxy from the Galactic Empire, but she needs Amazoness's help.
