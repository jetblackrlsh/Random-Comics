# Character Bible

All pages use realistic candid photo-comic style, documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism feel, natural imperfect framing, believable real-world lighting, photoreal action VFX, clean rectangular caption boxes, and no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Queen Amazoness

Powerful athletic woman in her 30s with warm brown skin, dark curly hair braided into a crown, steady dark eyes, gold circlet, bronze-and-white Amazon battle armor, red cloak, engraved bracers, spear or short sword, calm commanding posture. In this issue she refuses to interrupt Veyla's fight and watches with mentor confidence.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/queen-amazoness-character.png`

## Princess Veyla

Gentle alien woman in her early 20s with luminous teal skin, soft silver-white hair in loose waves, large violet eyes, faint bioluminescent freckles, leaf-vein markings along her hands and neck, flowing pale green and pearl diplomatic gown, delicate vine jewelry, frightened but increasingly determined expression. Her eyes glow soft green when she controls plants. In this issue she uses thorned vines, poisonous thorns, giant dandelion growth, airborne seeds, and vine support.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-veyla-character.png`

## Commander Grax Vorn

Towering alien warrior with charcoal-gray armored hide, tusk-like cheek ridges, glowing ember eyes, bald ridged skull, heavy black imperial armor, red Galactic Empire chest insignia, thruster boots, scarred gauntlets, predatory forward-leaning posture. He can fly, survive without air, move at high speed, and create shockwaves. In this issue his hands bleed from poisonous thorns, his vision blurs, and he crashes unconscious on the shore.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/commander-grax-vorn-character.png`

## The Amazons

Diverse women warriors with bronze armor, white linen tunics, red or gold sashes, braided or tied-back hair, magical spears, shields, bows, and alert defensive formations. They worry that Veyla lacks battle training, then cheer once they understand her strategy.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-warriors-character.png`

## Amazon Island Shore

Hidden island shoreline with turquoise water, white sand, black volcanic rocks, lush palms, flowering vines, bronze watchtowers, ancient marble stairs, and a faint shimmering invisibility barrier over the ocean. In this issue the beach is churned by uprooted vines, drifting giant dandelion seeds, poison vapor residue, and Grax's crash impact.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/amazon-island-shore-setting.png`

## Princess Veyla's Escape Pod

Pearlescent teardrop-shaped escape pod with green sensor lights, cracked transparent canopy, vine-like alien circuitry, scorched hull seams, emergency beacon glow, and sand-and-seawater damage after impact. It remains on the shore as the battle's starting point.

Series reference image: `../../../reference-images/princess-veylas-escape-pod-key-item.png`

## Amazon Island Invisibility Barrier

Translucent curtain of bent sunlight over the ocean, subtle gold motes, mirrored waves, distorted horizon line, and magical ripples where it was breached.
