# Treatment

## Title

Seeds Above the Shore

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

Princess Veyla turns Queen Amazoness's lesson into a sky battle, using thorns, seeds, and poison in the blood to defeat Commander Grax Vorn without becoming the kind of conqueror he serves.

## Central Conflict

Commander Grax Vorn believes only overwhelming strength matters, while Veyla must prove that adaptation, patience, and knowledge can defeat brute force.

## Theme

Fighting does not require abandoning gentleness; it requires understanding how a gift can protect life when flight is no longer enough.

## Ending

Veyla defeats Grax by poisoning the thorns that cut his hands, waiting while dandelion seeds hide her position, and letting the bloodstream poison blur his vision until he crashes unconscious on the shore. The Amazons celebrate her victory, but Veyla ends the issue by telling Queen Amazoness she has an idea to save the galaxy from the Galactic Empire and needs Amazoness's help.

## Emotional Arc

Veyla begins the issue clinging to a vine after her first tactic failed. The Amazons doubt she can fight, but Amazoness recognizes that Veyla has already moved from flight to resistance. Veyla stops trying to overpower Grax directly and instead wins by turning every gentle-seeming plant feature into part of a strategy. By the end, she is no longer only a frightened refugee; she is a leader imagining a coalition.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Grax grabbing the vine, Amazoness defending Veyla's fight, the uprooted vine becoming a dandelion, Veyla hiding among airborne seeds, poison spreading through Grax's bloodstream, the illusion crash, and Veyla revealing the strategy before asking Amazoness to help save the galaxy.
