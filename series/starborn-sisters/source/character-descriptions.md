# Character Descriptions

## Character: Queen Amazoness

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Starfall"
- Role: protagonist, queen of the Amazons, future founder of Starborn Academy.
- Visual identifiers: powerful athletic woman in her 30s with warm brown skin, dark curly hair braided into a crown, steady dark eyes, gold circlet, bronze-and-white Amazon battle armor, red cloak, engraved bracers, spear or short sword, calm commanding posture.
- Continuity notes: Begins Issue #1 as Earth's hidden Amazon queen. Her first alien encounter inspires her belief that worlds become stronger by studying one another.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/queen-amazoness-character.png`

## Character: Princess Veyla

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Starfall"
- Role: alien princess, refugee leader, plant-powered future ally.
- Visual identifiers: gentle alien woman in her early 20s with luminous teal skin, soft silver-white hair in loose waves, large violet eyes, faint bioluminescent freckles, leaf-vein markings along her hands and neck, flowing pale green and pearl diplomatic gown, delicate vine jewelry, frightened but compassionate expression.
- Continuity notes: Leader from a conquered world fleeing the Galactic Empire. She controls plants but initially believes her power is not suited for combat until Amazoness teaches her to understand unfamiliar forms of life.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/princess-veyla-character.png`

## Character: Commander Grax Vorn

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Starfall"
- Role: antagonist, Galactic Empire warrior.
- Visual identifiers: towering alien warrior with charcoal-gray armored hide, tusk-like cheek ridges, glowing ember eyes, bald ridged skull, heavy black imperial armor, red chest insignia, thruster boots, scarred gauntlets, predatory forward-leaning posture.
- Continuity notes: Member of the Galactic Empire adapted for space travel and able to survive without breathing air. He hunts Princess Veyla to force submission after her planet's fall.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/commander-grax-vorn-character.png`

## Character: The Amazons

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Starfall"
- Role: supporting warriors and citizens of Amazon Island.
- Visual identifiers: diverse women warriors with bronze armor, white linen tunics, red or gold sashes, braided or tied-back hair, magical spears, shields, bows, and alert defensive formations.
- Continuity notes: They welcome frightened women to their island but defend the barrier and their queen without hesitation.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/amazon-warriors-character.png`
