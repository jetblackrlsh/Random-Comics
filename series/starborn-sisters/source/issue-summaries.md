# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The First Starfall

- Folder: `series/starborn-sisters/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Before Starborn Academy exists, Queen Amazoness shelters an alien princess whose pursuit by the Galactic Empire turns Earth's hidden island into the first lesson of an interstellar heroic future.
- Plot summary: Princess Veyla flees a Galactic Empire conquest in an escape pod that scans Earth for powerful allies and crashes through the Amazons' invisibility barrier. The Amazons surround the pod, but Queen Amazoness welcomes the frightened princess and learns that Commander Grax Vorn is hunting her. Veyla believes her plant powers are too gentle for battle, so Amazoness teaches her that Earth plants include thorns, poisons, and predators. When Grax breaches the barrier, Amazoness orders the Amazons to stand down and urges Veyla to use what she has learned. Veyla's poison flower fails because Grax does not breathe, and he knocks her back with a shockwave, ending the issue with Amazoness still confident that the princess can find another answer.
- Continuity changes: Queen Amazoness makes first contact with alien life. Princess Veyla learns that studying another world can transform how a power is used. The Galactic Empire discovers that Earth has hidden defenders behind magical barriers.
- New characters: Queen Amazoness, Princess Veyla, Commander Grax Vorn, the Amazons.
- New settings: Amazon Island Shore, Starborn Academy.
- New key items: Princess Veyla's Escape Pod, Amazon Island Invisibility Barrier, Galactic Empire Insignia.

## Issue #2: Seeds Above the Shore

- Folder: `series/starborn-sisters/issues/issue-02/`
- Logline: Princess Veyla turns Queen Amazoness's lesson into a sky battle, using thorns, seeds, and poison in the blood to defeat Commander Grax Vorn without becoming the kind of conqueror he serves.
- Plot summary: Continuing directly from Issue #1, Grax grabs the vine that caught Veyla and tries to uproot it. Veyla grows thorns into his hands, but Grax ignores the wounds and tears the vine from the shore. While the Amazons plead for Amazoness to intervene, Amazoness recognizes that Veyla has stopped fleeing and is fighting with everything she has. Veyla transforms the vine's crown into a giant dandelion, scatters enormous floating seeds, and hides among them as Grax searches the sky. The thorn cuts were poisonous, and once the poison reaches Grax's bloodstream, his vision blurs until he attacks an illusion-like seed cluster, passes out, and crashes unconscious onto the shore. Veyla reveals her strategy, earns the Amazons' cheers, and tells Amazoness she has an idea to save the galaxy from the Galactic Empire, but she needs Amazoness's help.
- Continuity changes: Veyla wins her first direct battle against a Galactic Empire warrior by adapting Earth plant knowledge. The Amazons witness that alien powers can become stronger through shared learning. Veyla proposes a larger plan that points toward the future Universal Alliance of Good and Starborn Academy.
- New characters: None.
- New settings: None.
- New key items: None.
- Reference images added: None.

## Issue #3: The Teacher's Crown

- Folder: `series/starborn-sisters/issues/issue-03/`
- Logline: Princess Veyla asks Queen Amazoness to teach the galaxy instead of waging war, forcing Amazoness to choose a future beyond the island and provoking her advisor's warrior-first challenge to the crown.
- Plot summary: Continuing from Veyla's proposal in Issue #2, Queen Amazoness says the Amazons cannot wage an intergalactic war, but Veyla argues that the universe needs teachers more than another army. Veyla explains that Amazoness's knowledge of Earth's plants transformed her powers, and that powerful alien women across the galaxy could defend their worlds if given the same kind of teaching. Amazoness agrees and announces she will pass the crown to her daughter, Princess Amazoness, rather than split her attention between the island and the universe. High Advisor Lysandra objects that the princess is too young and demands the chance to fight her for the crown. Amazoness rejects the challenge as no different from the Galactic Empire's rule by strength and declares that the true calling of the Amazons is to teach. Lysandra storms away into the island, while Amazoness tells Veyla that her daughter left to learn the world beyond the island and that the crown will finally bring her home.
- Continuity changes: Amazoness commits to an interstellar teaching mission that points directly toward Starborn Academy. Princess Amazoness is named as heir to the Amazon crown. High Advisor Lysandra breaks with Amazoness over warrior tradition versus teaching.
- New characters: Princess Amazoness, High Advisor Lysandra.
- New settings: None.
- New key items: Crown of the Amazons.
- Reference images added: `princess-amazoness-character.png`, `high-advisor-lysandra-character.png`, `crown-of-the-amazons-key-item.png`.
