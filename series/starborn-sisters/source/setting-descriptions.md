# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Amazon Island Shore

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Starfall"
- Visual identifiers: hidden island shoreline with turquoise water, white sand, black volcanic rocks, lush palms, flowering vines, bronze watchtowers, ancient marble stairs, and a faint shimmering invisibility barrier over the ocean.
- Continuity notes: The island is hidden from the outside world by magic. Princess Veyla's escape pod breaches the barrier and crashes on the shore, creating Earth's first direct contact with the Galactic Empire conflict.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/amazon-island-shore-setting.png`

## Setting: Starborn Academy

- First appears: Future series setting; established in series premise.
- Visual identifiers: massive space-station campus at a wormhole nexus, panoramic windows showing colorful cosmic tunnels, dorm modules, classrooms, cafeteria rings, training chambers, holographic simulation arenas, and students from many alien species moving through bright practical corridors.
- Continuity notes: Founded by Queen Amazoness after the events begun in Issue #1. It becomes the central school for aspiring heroines across the universe.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/starborn-academy-setting.png`

## Setting: Universal Nexus

- First appears: Issue #5, "The Place Between Worlds"
- Visual identifiers: vast center-of-the-universe region surrounded by luminous wormhole tunnels, floating landmasses, living alien plants, mineral islands, ship wreckage, ancient machinery, ice, crystal, metal, soil, coral-like organic growth, and materials from worlds across the cosmos.
- Continuity notes: The most accessible location in the universe because ships from across the cosmos pass through its wormhole routes. It is not a planet with a ruler to overthrow, making it a strategically neutral birthplace for Starborn Academy.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/universal-nexus-setting.png`
