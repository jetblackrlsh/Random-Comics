# Starborn Sisters

## Creators

- Ashley Barry: Series Concept Creator
- Nicholas Alexander Benson: Lead Writer / AI Artist

## Logline

Superpowered alien girls from different planets attend an intergalactic college for aspiring superheroes, where they learn the cosmos, master their abilities, and band together against the Galactic Empire.

## Premise

Starborn Academy is an all-girls school for women across the universe who possess superpowers. The academy is a massive space station located in a nexus of wormholes at the center of the universe, making travel possible for students from distant worlds. Its dorms, cafeterias, classrooms, medical bays, observation decks, training arenas, and holographic simulation chambers prepare the next generation of heroines to protect the universe.

The academy was founded after Queen Amazoness of Earth first encountered alien life on the hidden island of the Amazons. That encounter taught her that isolated worlds could survive by studying one another, sharing strengths, and training across cultures instead of waiting for conquest to reach their shores.

The main external threat is the Galactic Empire, an imperialist alliance of powerful alien warriors and monsters who believe only the strongest should rule. They conquer planets by force, test young heroines as possible recruits, and try to break anyone who refuses to serve them.

## Tone

Science-fiction superhero action-adventure with anthology flexibility, interstellar school drama, heroic mentorship, cosmic wonder, team-building, and battles that test whether power should be used to dominate or protect.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Starborn Academy should feel like a real lived-in space station campus: bright dorm corridors, panoramic wormhole windows, cafeteria noise, practical uniforms, training gear, holographic arenas, and students from many worlds.
- Amazon Island should look like a believable hidden Mediterranean island with ancient stone halls, lush shoreline plants, bronze weapons, and magic that bends light around the coast.
- Galactic Empire technology and armor should look brutal, conquest-driven, and physically heavy: dark metals, red warning lights, monster-scale silhouettes, scars, trophies, and imperial insignia.
- Superpowers should feel photoreal and physically present in the scene: glowing eyes, wind from flight, bending vines, impact waves, holograms, plasma, cosmic light, and environmental damage.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Continuity Notes

- Issue #1 takes place before Starborn Academy exists.
- Queen Amazoness begins as the ruler of the hidden Amazons, not yet the founder of an intergalactic school.
- Princess Veyla's arrival is Queen Amazoness's first confirmed encounter with alien life.
- The lesson of Issue #1 is the seed of Starborn Academy: isolated worlds can grow stronger by studying one another and sharing knowledge.
- The Galactic Empire is not primarily trying to destroy powerful young women; it wants to recruit, dominate, or break them into service.
- The island's magical invisibility barrier can hide the Amazons from ordinary sight, but it is not absolute against advanced pursuit or persistent tracking.
- Issue #3 clarifies that Amazon culture is not only martial. The Amazons preserve knowledge, teach strategy, and can reshape the galaxy through education rather than conquest.
- Queen Amazoness intends to pass the crown to her daughter, Princess Amazoness, so she can help build a wider interstellar teaching mission.
- High Advisor Lysandra represents an older warrior-first tradition that believes authority must be earned through battle.
