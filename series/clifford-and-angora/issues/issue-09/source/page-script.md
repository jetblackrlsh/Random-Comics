# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. The immense many-winged Changeling shields fallen Angora while Clifford plays beneath it. The Dwarf Rogue runs from the fortress carrying the Horde Chronicle, the Winged Cauldron collides with blue-lit drones, and flying Dragonborn darken the moon.
- Readable title text: "THE ANGEL'S TEST"
- Visual beats: Photoreal live-action fantasy, pale-gold wings, violet sword, green-gold music, blue machinery, and overwhelming airborne enemies.

## Page 2: The Heist Begins

- Layout: Four parallel-action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford filled the night with magical music and drew the Dragonborn's attention toward himself." "Beneath the distraction, the dwarf rogue slipped around the Rival Artificer and entered the castle library." "The Dragonborn watched Clifford." "The fortress watched the thief."
- Visual beats: Clifford performs; rogue crosses shadowed bridge; Dragonborn focuses; blue lenses turn toward rogue.

## Page 3: Detected And Defeated

- Layout: Four escalating panels.
- Caption boxes: "Flying sentry drones discovered the rogue before he could reach the chronicle." "Outside, Angora tried to advance against the robot army while her strengthened sword still burned." "Her body failed even though the weapon did not." "She collapsed before the machines, limp and unable to protect herself."
- Visual beats: Drones swarm library; rogue dodges; Angora staggers; ground-level collapse beneath robots.

## Page 4: The Child's True Form

- Layout: Three enormous reveal panels.
- Caption boxes: "The sleeping child unfolded into an immense fey being surrounded by wings and countless eyes." "Its presence brought Angora peace, and its feathers formed a living shield around her." "The changeling revealed that mercy toward its helpless form had been a test - and Clifford and Angora had passed."
- Visual beats: Transformation; fey cradles Angora; serene blue-violet eyes and protective wings.

## Page 5: The Fortress Falls Silent

- Layout: Four spectacular action panels.
- Caption boxes: "The angelic fey swept its enormous wings across the battlefield." "A colossal gust smashed the robot army onto the stone." "For one glorious instant, the fortress defenses appeared broken." "Then an army of flying Dragonborn rose over the horizon to defend and avenge its leader."
- Visual beats: Wing sweep; robots tumble; party looks hopeful; airborne army silhouette fills sky.

## Page 6: The Chronicle Recovered

- Layout: Five fast interior panels.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford sent the Winged Cauldron into the library to clear the rogue's path." "The cauldron opened its top and scooped the flying sentries inside instead of fighting them one by one." "The dwarf disappeared between the shelves." "At last, he found the ancient Horde chronicle."
- Visual beats: Cauldron flies; drones swallowed; rogue runs; locked book; rogue lifts chronicle.

## Page 7: Salvation From The Sky

- Layout: Four urgent escape panels.
- Caption boxes: "The changeling gathered the exhausted party inside its tremendous wings and told them to trust it." "A Pegasus descended through the mountain wind." "Clifford, Angora, the rogue, the child, and the cauldron mounted with the recovered chronicle." "They fled the eastern fortress before the gathering army could surround them."
- Visual beats: Protective wing enclosure; Pegasus arrival; crowded mounting; flight past black towers.

## Page 8: The Horde Follows

- Layout: Three wide aerial panels ending in dread.
- Caption boxes: "For a moment, the stolen book and open sky made the mission feel complete." "Then the fugitives looked behind them." "The Dragonborn could fly, and the entire Horde was following them home."
- Visual beats: Pegasus above mountains; relieved riders turn; vast formation of winged Dragonborn in pursuit.
