At the eastern fortress, Clifford distracts the Dragonborn Rival Artificer with magical music while the Dwarf Rogue enters the library. Flying sentry drones detect the thief. Outside, the weakened Angora collapses before the robot army. The protected changeling becomes an immense angelic fey with many wings and eyes, explains that the party passed a test of compassion, shields Angora, and destroys the robot formation with a colossal gust.

An army of flying Dragonborn appears over the horizon. Clifford sends the Winged Cauldron into the library, where it scoops the sentry drones into its open top. The rogue recovers the ancient Horde chronicle. The changeling gathers the party, a Pegasus descends, and everyone escapes the fortress with the book. Their relief ends when they discover the Dragonborn army can fly and is pursuing them home.
