## Recurring Identities

Use the exact established Clifford, Angora, Dwarf Rogue, Winged Cauldron, Rival Artificer, castle, and machine designs from the cited references. Angora remains pale, red-haired, and armored even while visibly drained. The Dwarf Rogue remains short, purple-clad, braided, and equipped with crescent dagger, crossbow, Displacer Beast cloak, and golden winged shoes.

## Changeling's Fey Form

Immense supernatural guardian with a serene androgynous face, pale-gold skin, recognizable blue-violet eyes, many enormous white-and-gold feathered wings, numerous luminous eyes within the wings, and an uncanny biblical-angel silhouette. Its scale should shelter Angora and later gather the party.

## Dragonborn Horde

Flying Dragonborn warriors with varied orange, bronze, rust, and ember scales, many bearing blue markings that echo their leader. They wear worn purple, black, and brass armor, carry medieval weapons, and breathe orange fire. They are a disciplined people following their wizard-artificer leader, not mindless monsters.

## Horde Chronicle

Large ancient dark-leather book with brass corners, thick uneven parchment, a circular interlocking-dragon emblem, dim blue runes, and a heavy mechanical clasp opened by the rogue.
