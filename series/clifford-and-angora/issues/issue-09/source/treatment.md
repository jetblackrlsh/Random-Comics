# Treatment

## Title

The Angel's Test

## Issue Number

9

## Publication Date

2026-08-08

## Format

Series issue #9, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

When the fortress heist collapses and Angora falls, the demonic child reveals its angelic true form, repays the heroes' compassion, and helps them escape with the Horde chronicle before a flying army pursues them home.

## Central Conflict

The party must retrieve the chronicle while robot defenses attack from within, the Rival Artificer attacks from outside, and Angora's strength disappears.

## Theme

Compassion can create help that force could never command.

## Ending

The party escapes on a Pegasus with the chronicle, only to discover that the Dragonborn Horde can fly and is following them toward the academy.

## Emotional Arc

Clifford's careful heist turns into open disaster. The changeling transforms fear into gratitude by revealing that the child was testing their mercy. Victory seems possible once the chronicle is recovered, but escape carries the threat directly toward everything the heroes built.

## Cast

- Clifford, Angora, the Dwarf Rogue, and the Winged Cauldron Companion.
- The Changeling in its immense angelic fey form.
- The Rival Artificer and the Dragonborn Horde.
- The Pegasus.

## Art Direction

Moonlit fortress photojournalism with tactile brass-and-black robots, blue sentry lights, green-gold music, violet sword light, and a vast pale-gold many-winged fey presence. The escape shifts into stormy aerial fantasy. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, page numbers, caption labels, or repeated same-page wording.
