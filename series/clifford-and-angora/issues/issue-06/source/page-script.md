# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. Clifford and Angora defend the Warrior Academy as dozens of pink young kobolds burst from the cracked arena floor. The Rival Headmaster rallies students from both schools, the Swamp Witch stands beside the shattered egg, and the armed Tiefling Artificer appears as a distant horned silhouette.
- Readable title text: "THE KOBOLD UPRISING"
- Visual beats: Photoreal live-action fantasy; academy banners, orange fire, violet sword light, green-gold music, flying debris, and a mysterious arrival beyond the chaos.

## Page 2: The First Test Of The Alliance

- Layout: Four broad documentary panels.
- Caption boxes: "The alliance between the two warrior academies was new, fragile, and essential to surviving the returning Horde." "Students who had once trained as rivals now gathered around a mysterious egg supplied by the Swamp Witch." "Angora watched from the front rank while Clifford stood beside the Rival Headmaster." "Before the creature hatched, Clifford asked what kind of enemy could threaten every kingdom at once."
- Visual beats: Mixed student ranks; egg in packed earth; Angora and Clifford established; headmaster turns gravely toward Clifford.

## Page 3: The Shape Of The Horde

- Layout: Three large ominous panels with the cracking egg present throughout.
- Caption boxes: "The headmaster explained that the Horde attacked beneath the cover of night and moved with the speed of a living swarm." "Its victims became blind and confused, conquered before they could understand what surrounded them." "An ancient magical text might contain the world's defense - but a violent crack ended the warning before she could explain more."
- Visual beats: Shadowy historical vision; frightened students; egg shell splits open under gathering weapons.

## Page 4: Small Creature, Real Fire

- Layout: Four fast action panels.
- Caption boxes: "A pink young kobold tumbled from the shell, so small that several students laughed." "The creature answered their amusement with a roar and a blast of fire." "One eager fighter charged alone, determined to prove that he needed no help." "He escaped the kobold's claws only by leaping over them, then crashed helplessly onto his back."
- Visual beats: Cute first reveal; violent fire; reckless charge; dusty fall beneath clawed feet.

## Page 5: Courage Needs A Partner

- Layout: Four emotional action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford played a heroic chord and sang until the fallen student's fear became determination." "The fighter stood again, but the sweat on his face revealed that courage had not erased the danger." "Angora called a second student forward because the Horde would never be defeated through individual glory." "The two young warriors lowered their weapons, studied the kobold, and chose to move as one."
- Visual beats: Green-gold music; student rises; Angora points another fighter into arena; pair coordinate.

## Page 6: The Lesson Works

- Layout: Five brisk combat panels ending with a larger victory panel.
- Caption boxes: "One student drew the kobold's fire while the other attacked from its unguarded side." "Whenever the creature turned, they exchanged positions and forced it to divide its attention." "Their coordination exhausted the young kobold without killing it." "Clifford praised the victory as proof that teamwork was the academy's greatest weapon against the Horde."
- Visual beats: Alternating attack angles; fire narrowly avoided; kobold collapses alive; students and schools celebrate together.

## Page 7: Eggs Everywhere

- Layout: Four panels that turn celebration into dread.
- Caption boxes: "Hoping the creatures might someday aid their defense, Clifford asked the Swamp Witch where she had found the egg." "The witch stared at him and explained that young kobold eggs were buried throughout the entire area." "The ground shifted beneath every student in the arena." "Dozens of cracks spread across the training field like lightning."
- Visual beats: Clifford gestures at defeated kobold; witch's baffled answer; boots lose balance; overhead view of fractures.

## Page 8: The Academy Erupts

- Layout: Three large escalating panels with a full-width final panel.
- Caption boxes: "Pink claws, black-armored tails, and hyena-like heads erupted from beneath the academy as the colony came to defend its fallen child." "The kobolds breathed fire and built traps from broken shells, loose equipment, and anything their clever hands could seize." "As the training exercise became an invasion, a horned stranger carrying an enormous golden weapon approached from the horizon."
- Visual beats: Mass emergence; fire and improvised traps overwhelm students; final distant Tiefling Artificer silhouette framed beyond Clifford and Angora's defensive line.
