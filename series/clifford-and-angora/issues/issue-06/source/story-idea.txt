The alliance between Clifford and Angora's academy and the Rival Headmaster's school begins its first shared training exercise around a mysterious egg supplied by the Swamp Witch. While the egg cracks, Clifford asks what the returning Horde truly is. The headmaster explains that it attacks at night with the speed and confusion of a swarm, conquering victims before they can understand what surrounds them.

A small pink young kobold hatches. Its hyena-like head, black volcanic natural armor, clawed feet, tail, and loose cream trousers initially seem almost cute, but it breathes fire and attacks with startling speed. One student tries to fight alone, falls, and nearly loses courage. Clifford inspires him through heroic music, while Angora calls a second student into the arena. By switching positions, distracting the kobold, and striking together, the pair defeat it without killing it. Clifford praises cooperation as the academy's true preparation for the Horde.

When Clifford asks the Swamp Witch where she found the egg, she reveals that young kobold eggs lie throughout the area. The ground fractures everywhere. Dozens of young kobolds emerge to defend their fallen companion, breathing fire and building crude traps. As the academy becomes an invasion zone, a horned stranger carrying an enormous golden arcane weapon approaches on the horizon.
