# Treatment

## Title

The Kobold Uprising

## Issue Number

6

## Publication Date

2026-08-04

## Format

Series issue #6, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

When the allied academies test their students against a newly hatched kobold, Clifford and Angora prove that teamwork can overcome fear - only to discover that an entire underground colony has come to defend the defeated creature.

## Central Conflict

The academy must transform former rivals into a cooperative fighting force before the Horde arrives. A frightened student initially treats the kobold challenge as a chance for individual glory, but Clifford's encouragement and Angora's tactical judgment teach him to depend upon a partner. Their successful lesson accidentally provokes a much larger danger beneath the school.

## Theme

Courage becomes reliable only when people learn to support one another.

## Ending

The students defeat one young kobold through teamwork, but dozens more erupt from beneath the arena. While the academy struggles against the invasion, a heavily armed horned stranger approaches from the horizon.

## Emotional Arc

Clifford begins by seeking a clear understanding of the Horde. The headmaster's warning makes the threat frighteningly real, but the students' cooperative victory gives everyone proof that the new alliance can work. That hope becomes immediate chaos when the kobold colony rises, forcing the academy to apply its lesson on a much larger scale.

## Cast

- Clifford, Angora, the Swamp Witch, and the Rival Headmaster.
- Students from both newly allied warrior academies.
- A young pink kobold and the underground kobold colony.
- The Tiefling Artificer, seen only as a mysterious approaching figure.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid photo-comic at the established Warrior Academy. Natural daylight and grounded training photography give way to orange fire, cracked earth, black volcanic kobold armor, and frantic documentary action. The final page should contrast the chaotic foreground invasion with the clean silhouette of the armed stranger in the distance. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, page numbers, caption labels, or repeated same-page wording.
