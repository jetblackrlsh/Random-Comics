## Recurring Identities

Clifford remains the immense Black goliath bard with deep brown skin, high bundled locs, full beard, symmetrical white tribal paint, layered green-and-purple gold-trimmed bardic clothing, and the living gold-filigreed lute. Angora remains the freckled fair-skinned elf with green eyes, vivid red high ponytail, long pointed ears, spiked gunmetal armor, green accents, blue cape, round wooden shield with psychic sheen, and translucent pink-violet energy sword. The Swamp Witch remains the weathered silver-haired elder in moss and peat robes with reed charms and a crooked staff. The Rival Headmaster remains the stern dark-skinned elf scholar-warrior with a black-and-silver braided crown, midnight-blue robes over silver scale armor, white mantle, and rune-etched spear.

## Young Kobolds

Small child-height reptilian humanoids with bright pink skin, hyena-like heads, rounded black noses, large alert ears, clawed feet, long tails, and irregular plates of black volcanic-rock natural armor across the crown, shoulders, spine, forearms, and tail. They wear loose cream-colored trousers. Their orange fire breath is dangerous, and groups rapidly build crude traps from shell, wood, rope, and battlefield debris.

## The Tiefling Artificer

Only a distant silhouette appears in this issue: an androgynous adult tiefling with pinkish-purple skin, two large swept horns, flowing white hair with a bold black streak, travel-workshop clothing, and an enormous triangular-barreled golden arcane weapon containing a blue energy core.

## Visual Rules

Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Maintain the binding identities, props, and academy architecture. All readable text appears directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes generated in the page art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, page numbers, panel labels, caption numbers, caption labels, or repeated same-page wording.
