While the Tiefling Artificer confronts their former master, Clifford and the weakened Rival Headmaster open the stolen chronicle. It contains an ancient spell that can banish the Dragonborn Horde back to its home dimension, but the academy needs a spellcaster capable of speaking its forgotten language. Angora sends a frightened adult academy courier to bring the Swamp Witch.

The Swamp Witch arrives like mist, opens the chronicle, and speaks the ancient words. Calm spreads through the Dragonborn. They descend, sleep, and vanish, leaving grass and flowers where an army had fallen. The academy survives because students, teachers, former rivals, magical companions, artificers, a rogue, a shapeshifter, scholars, and a witch each contributed something no single champion could provide. The Swamp Witch warns that banishment is not destruction and that others may try to reopen the dimensional door.
