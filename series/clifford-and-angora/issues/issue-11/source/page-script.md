# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. The Swamp Witch floats above the open Horde Chronicle as peaceful turquoise-and-amber magic spreads across sleeping Dragonborn. Clifford and Angora stand beside the exhausted academy alliance while flowers rise through the empty battlefield.
- Readable title text: "THE HORDE BANISHED"
- Visual beats: Photoreal live-action fantasy, ancient spell light, restful fallen warriors, burning academy behind calm grass and flowers.

## Page 2: The Chronicle's Secret

- Layout: Four investigative panels amid battle.
- Caption boxes: "While master and former student fought, Clifford escaped the front line with the weakened Rival Headmaster." "They opened the stolen chronicle as the academy collapsed around them." "Its oldest pages contained a spell capable of returning the Dragonborn Horde to another dimension." "The enemy did not need to be defeated one warrior at a time if the forgotten words could still be spoken."
- Visual beats: Clifford supports headmaster; book opened; runes glow; dimensional diagram revealed.

## Page 3: The Magic They Needed

- Layout: Four urgent panels.
- Caption boxes: "The ancient language demanded power and knowledge beyond the academy's exhausted defenders." "Angora knew one spellcaster who had survived more magical mistakes than anyone else." "She sent one of the academy's fastest students toward the village." "The frightened courier ran while fire and machines shook the road behind them."
- Visual beats: Angora reads page; realizes answer; gives task to an adult student; courier runs through the siege road.

## Page 4: Courage Finds The Witch

- Layout: Four emotional travel panels.
- Caption boxes: "The young warrior reached the village trembling and breathless." "The Swamp Witch listened as the student explained that the Horde had reached the academy." "There was barely time to describe the stolen book or the ancient spell." "The witch understood before the explanation was finished."
- Visual beats: Student arrives; witch doorway; urgent gestures; witch grips staff with calm purpose.

## Page 5: The Forgotten Words

- Layout: Three grand battlefield panels.
- Caption boxes: "The Swamp Witch returned like mist moving across the land." "The chronicle was placed into her weathered hands while robots, Dragonborn, and defenders fought around her." "She opened the book and began speaking words so old that ordinary memory had lost their meaning."
- Visual beats: Witch floats toward academy; receives chronicle; ancient runes and turquoise-amber mist rise.

## Page 6: Peace Across The Horde

- Layout: Four increasingly quiet panels.
- Caption boxes: "The spell did not strike the Dragonborn with violence." "A strange calm spread through the army and extinguished its rage." "Wings slowed, weapons lowered, and fire disappeared from open mouths." "One after another, the Horde descended and entered an impossibly deep sleep."
- Visual beats: Spell mist; Dragonborn calm; land gently; rows sleeping beneath wings.

## Page 7: Where An Army Had Fallen

- Layout: Three wide transformation panels.
- Caption boxes: "The ancient words carried the sleeping Dragonborn beyond the world they had invaded." "Warriors vanished until only grass remained beneath the quiet sky." "Flowers moved gently where an army had existed moments before."
- Visual beats: Bodies dissolve into peaceful light; empty field expands; close flowers and stunned survivors.

## Page 8: Together, They Were Enough

- Layout: Four reflective panels ending on the witch's warning.
- Caption boxes: "The academy survived because students, teachers, former rivals, machines, a rogue, a shapeshifter, scholars, and a witch each supplied something no champion possessed alone." "The lesson that began with two students cooperating against one kobold had become the principle that saved their world." "No one person was enough. Together, they were." "The Swamp Witch warned that banishment was not destruction - and somewhere between worlds, the door could still be opened."
- Visual beats: Ensemble across damaged academy; students help each other; Clifford and Angora together; witch faces a faint dimensional crack beyond the flowered field.
