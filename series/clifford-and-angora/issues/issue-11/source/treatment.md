# Treatment

## Title

The Horde Banished

## Issue Number

11

## Publication Date

2026-08-08

## Format

Series issue #11, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

As the academy falls, Clifford discovers a banishment spell inside the stolen chronicle and entrusts the forgotten words to the Swamp Witch, whose peaceful magic sends the Dragonborn Horde home.

## Central Conflict

No army at the academy can defeat the Dragonborn individually, so the heroes must understand and perform an ancient dimensional spell before the defenders collapse.

## Theme

No single champion is enough, but a community can supply every missing strength.

## Ending

The Horde vanishes and the academy survives, but the Swamp Witch warns that banishment did not destroy the dimensional doorway or those who may seek to reopen it.

## Emotional Arc

The battle's desperation gives way to intellectual hope when the chronicle is opened. A frightened academy courier proves courageous, the unpredictable witch becomes the calmest person on the battlefield, and victory arrives as quiet absence rather than greater violence. Relief ends with a promise of future danger.

## Cast

- Clifford, Angora, and the Rival Headmaster.
- The Swamp Witch and a frightened adult academy courier.
- The Tiefling Artificer and the Rival Artificer.
- The Dwarf Rogue, changeling, Winged Cauldron, academy defenders, and Dragonborn Horde.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid siege photography gradually becoming still and luminous. The ancient spell should appear as misty turquoise, amber, and pale white light that calms rather than attacks. The final flowered field contrasts with the burning academy and includes the surviving alliance.
