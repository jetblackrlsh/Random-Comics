## Recurring Identities

Maintain every established character, costume, item, machine, and magic color from the cited references. The Swamp Witch remains an elderly silver-haired woman in moss-green and peat-brown robes with reed charms, bone beads, herb-stained hands, and a crooked staff; her magic appears as smoky turquoise and amber light. The Rival Headmaster remains the dark-skinned elf scholar-warrior with black-and-silver braided crown, blue robes, silver armor, white mantle, and rune spear.

## Young Academy Protege

An unnamed adult academy courier in her mid-twenties with an athletic build, brown skin, dark hair tied back, and a blue academy tunic over practical leather training armor. She is visibly frightened but completes the mission to summon the witch.
