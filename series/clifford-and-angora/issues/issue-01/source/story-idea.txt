Clifford and Angora's First Adventure

The adventure began inside a lively tavern filled with travelers, adventurers, and the constant murmur of conversation. Clifford, a towering goliath bard, sat among the patrons with his magical lute close at hand. Although his immense size might have suggested that he was a formidable warrior, Clifford considered himself more of a musician than a fighter.

The tavern door opened, and an elven fighter named Angora entered. She carried a wooden shield and a remarkable psychic sword capable of glowing with supernatural energy. After noticing one another, Clifford and Angora introduced themselves. Angora explained that she had come looking for a worthy opponent—someone powerful enough to test her mettle as a fighter.

Clifford admitted that he was not much of a combatant, but offered to play a magical song and summon a creature for Angora to battle. The creature appeared, but behaved like a confused and friendly visitor. Angora asked it to be her friend. Offended by the suggestion that it had no friends, the creature summoned twelve aggressive tiefling women who were ready for a brawl.

Clifford tried to weaken the tieflings with what he believed was a holy song. It did not hurt them; it made them furious because it was a reversed, insulting version of one of their sacred songs. While they protested Clifford's performance, Angora raised her psychic sword. Its brilliant supernatural light frightened the tieflings into fleeing.

Clifford thanked Angora, admitted he needed to learn more about magical music, and proposed visiting a magical library. Angora agreed. There, Angora found a text teaching that the greatest source of a warrior's power was the mind rather than the body. Clifford found the Tune of the Astral Blade, supposedly able to enhance a magical sword.

After their research, they went to a nearby river to rest. Humanoid shark bandits ambushed them and tried to steal Angora's glowing psychic sword. Clifford played the Tune of the Astral Blade, but Angora's sword flickered and weakened. His second attempt failed too, although the terrible performance annoyed the sharks enough to make them focus on him.

Remembering the library's lesson, Angora concentrated upon the psychic power flowing through her sword and launched a mental energy blast. It missed every bandit and struck the river, causing a tremendous explosion that scattered water through the air and temporarily dispersed part of the river. Terrified, the shark bandits fled.

Clifford apologized and said he needed music lessons. Angora replied that the music was in his heart. Clifford answered that Angora was the best fighter because her power was in her mind. Their first adventure ended with two unlikely companions recognizing the strength within one another.
