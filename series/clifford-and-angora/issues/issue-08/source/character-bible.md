## Recurring Identities

Clifford remains the immense Black goliath bard with deep brown skin, high bundled locs, full beard, symmetrical white tribal paint, layered green-and-purple gold-trimmed clothing, and the living gold-filigreed lute. Angora remains the freckled fair-skinned elf with green eyes, vivid red ponytail, pointed ears, spiked gunmetal armor, green accents, blue cape, round wooden shield, and translucent pink-violet psychic sword. The Winged Cauldron remains blackened bronze with a golden face, small gold wings, mechanical feet, and blue inner glow.

## Dwarf Rogue

Short Black dwarf man with brown eyes, compact strong build, braided full beard, light-purple headband, dark-purple hooded tunic, blue-and-yellow underlayers, lighter-purple gloves, a Displacer Beast cloak with two predatory tendril-like extensions, worn golden winged shoes, silver crescent hook dagger with gold inlay, and wooden crossbow.

## Changeling

The same supernatural being has three forms: a helpless human infant wrapped in worn cream cloth; a lean charcoal-skinned horned demon-child with clawed hands and ember eyes; and an immense pale-gold fey guardian surrounded by many feathered wings and numerous luminous eyes. Preserve recognizable blue-violet eye color across all forms.

## Rival Artificer

Relatively short but imposing Dragonborn wizard-artificer with orange scales crossed by vivid blue stripes, a jagged horned head, glowing blue eyes, black claws, elaborate purple-and-gold robes, purple cape, necklace with one blue and one red stone, crystal staff with a circular coiling crown, and a strange brown living pouch marked with blue-purple and red coloration.
