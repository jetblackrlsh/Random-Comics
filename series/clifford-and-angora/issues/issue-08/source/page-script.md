# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. Clifford shields a sleeping baby while weakened Angora braces behind her violet sword. The purple-clad Dwarf Rogue aims his crossbow at the child's looming demonic shadow, and the orange-and-blue Dragonborn Rival Artificer watches from the moonlit fortress above.
- Readable title text: "THE DEMON CHILD"
- Visual beats: Photoreal live-action fantasy, compassionate heroes under threat, purple cloak motion, green-gold music, violet energy, and cold blue fortress lights.

## Page 2: A Thief For An Impossible Castle

- Layout: Four documentary road panels.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford and Angora followed the Winged Cauldron east toward a fortress guarded by magical machines." "On the road they met a dwarf rogue whose cloak, dagger, and winged shoes marked him as an expert infiltrator." "Clifford offered the wealth of their international academy if the rogue could steal the Horde chronicle." "The dwarf knew their reputation, accepted the contract, and turned a dangerous journey into a covert expedition."
- Visual beats: Eastern road; rogue reveal; Clifford explains castle; handshake beneath hovering cauldron.

## Page 3: The Child On The Road

- Layout: Four escalating panels.
- Caption boxes: "The travelers found a baby crying alone on the eastern road." "Angora approached with compassion and tried to help." "Before she reached it, the infant transformed into a horned demon." "Startled by the transformation, the party retreated to decide what the creature truly was."
- Visual beats: Baby in cloth; compassionate approach; startling transformation; cautious retreat.

## Page 4: Strength Abandons Angora

- Layout: Four tense action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Angora turned to fight, but an unnatural weakness made her powerful body tremble." "The exhaustion had begun when they accepted responsibility for the strange child." "Clifford played the Tune of the Astral Blade, sending green-gold music into her violet weapon." "The sword blazed with strength while Angora herself remained frighteningly weak."
- Visual beats: Angora falters; demon advances; Clifford plays; sword brightens while fighter sinks to one knee.

## Page 5: The Rogue's Hidden Shot

- Layout: Four quick panels ending in stunned silence.
- Caption boxes: "While the strange magic held the heroes' attention, the dwarf vanished beneath his Displacer Beast cloak." "He reached an unseen firing position and studied the spell from a safer angle." "One precise shot broke the transformation spell." "As the magic faded, the creature's true form began to return."
- Visual beats: Cloak blur; crossbow close-up; bolt breaks the spell sigil; magic fades.

## Page 6: Mercy After Fear

- Layout: Four emotional panels.
- Caption boxes: "The broken spell revealed a sleeping baby where the strange magic had been." "His mystical lullaby did not create loyalty, but it placed the child into peaceful sleep." "The travelers could not explain whether they carried a baby, demon, changeling, or test." "They knew only that the sleeping creature was helpless, and they chose to protect it."
- Visual beats: Clifford kneels; gentle green-gold music; sleeping baby wrapped safely; party resumes journey.

## Page 7: The Rival Revealed

- Layout: Three large night panels.
- Caption boxes: "The eastern road ended beneath a mechanized castle carved into a black mountain." "A hooded figure waited outside and announced that Clifford and Angora had been expected." "Moonlight revealed an orange-and-blue Dragonborn wizard-artificer with glowing eyes, crystal staff, and the confidence of someone who claimed to know everything."
- Visual beats: Castle exterior; shadowed figure; full Dragonborn reveal with staff, necklace, and living pouch.

## Page 8: An Army Between Them And The Book

- Layout: Four ominous panels ending on the heroes' plan.
- Caption boxes: "Robot soldiers emerged across every bridge and terrace behind the Dragonborn." "The stolen chronicle waited somewhere inside a library watched by machines." "Clifford told the rogue to enter unseen while his music held the Rival Artificer's attention." "Against an enemy who seemed to know every secret, their only hope was the ally no one could see."
- Visual beats: Robot army; library window; Clifford whispers plan; rogue disappears as Clifford raises lute.
