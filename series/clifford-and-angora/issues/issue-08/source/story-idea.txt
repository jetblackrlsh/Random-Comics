Clifford and Angora travel east with the Winged Cauldron Companion to recover the stolen Horde chronicle. On the road they hire a skilled dwarf rogue to infiltrate the Rival Artificer's mechanized castle. Soon afterward they find an abandoned baby that transforms into a demon. Angora becomes mysteriously weak, Clifford's Tune of the Astral Blade strengthens only her sword, and the hidden rogue breaks the transformation spell with a precise crossbow shot. The creature becomes a helpless baby again. Clifford chooses mercy, puts it to sleep with a lullaby, and carries it onward.

The party reaches the mountain fortress at night. The Rival Artificer is revealed as an orange-and-blue Dragonborn wizard-artificer with glowing blue eyes, purple-and-gold robes, a crystal staff, magical stones, and a living pouch. The Dragonborn claims to know everything and waits before a robot army, forcing Clifford and Angora to trust their new rogue and attempt the heist.
