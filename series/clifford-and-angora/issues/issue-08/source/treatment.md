# Treatment

## Title

The Demon Child

## Issue Number

8

## Publication Date

2026-08-08

## Format

Series issue #8, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

On the road to the mechanized fortress, Clifford and Angora hire a dwarf thief and spare a demonic shapeshifting child before finally confronting the Dragonborn rival who stole the Horde chronicle.

## Central Conflict

Clifford and Angora need a thief capable of entering an impossible fortress, but their compassion is tested when a seemingly helpless child reveals dangerous magic and drains Angora's strength.

## Theme

Mercy matters most when offering it feels risky.

## Ending

The party reaches the eastern fortress and discovers that the Rival Artificer is a powerful Dragonborn wizard commanding an army of machines.

## Emotional Arc

The journey begins as a practical mission to hire a criminal specialist. Fear and confusion replace confidence when the child transforms and Angora weakens. Clifford's decision to protect the sleeping creature proves that the party's values survive danger, but the Dragonborn's reveal makes clear that compassion alone will not open the fortress.

## Cast

- Clifford, Angora, and the Winged Cauldron Companion.
- The Dwarf Rogue, a stealth expert hired to steal the chronicle.
- The Changeling, appearing first as a baby and then as a demon.
- The Rival Artificer, revealed as a Dragonborn wizard-artificer.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid photo-comic moving from an overcast eastern road into moonlit black-stone magitech. Purple rogue clothing, green-gold bardic magic, violet psychic light, and blue fortress machinery provide distinct visual threads.
