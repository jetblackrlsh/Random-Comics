# Treatment

## Title

The All-Star Arena

## Issue Number

3

## Publication Date

2026-08-02

## Format

Series issue #3, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

After magical mistakes ruin a potion shop and cripple their first gladiator, Angora must recover a forgotten Elvish spell before an arena full of kings witnesses their enterprise collapse.

## Central Conflict

The companions have staked their debts and reputation upon one champion, but every attempt to help him makes him weaker. Angora must turn incomplete childhood knowledge into a precise act of ancient magic while Clifford uses the backward version of his own failed song to remove part of the curse.

## Theme

Past failures become useful when they are understood and deliberately reversed.

## Ending

Their restored champion wins the international tournament, the potion-shop debt is paid, and Angora proposes turning their accidental success into a permanent academy for warriors.

## Emotional Arc

Angora begins overconfident in the strange power granted by the potion and is humbled when she worsens the destruction. At the arena, the trio's combined failure appears to doom their champion. Clifford and Angora then apply what they learned in earlier disasters: he reverses harmful music, and she trusts the ancient strength stored in her mind. Their victory gives Angora the confidence to imagine a larger purpose.

## Cast

- Clifford, Angora, the Swamp Witch, and the Tavern Champion.
- Rigarchu: arrogant foreign gladiator who humiliates their weakened champion.
- The potion-shop keeper.
- Foreign rulers, military envoys, nobles, arena officials, and competing gladiators.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid photo-comic. Begin with chaotic turquoise-violet potion magic in a cramped shop, then expand into a monumental sandstone-and-bronze arena crowded with international fantasy cultures. Ancient Elvish strength magic is radiant white-gold threaded with Angora's violet psychic energy. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
