The mysterious potion explodes inside the shop and fills Clifford, Angora, the Swamp Witch, and the keeper with uncontrolled magic. Angora believes she can restore the damage, but instead destroys what remains. Clifford promises the furious keeper a share of the winnings from their newly represented champion, and the keeper reluctantly accompanies them to the arena.

Foreign kings, military envoys, and nobles have gathered for an all-star gladiatorial event. Angora, Clifford, and the witch try to strengthen their champion, but the combined spells weaken him. A foreign gladiator named Rigarchu kicks the trembling champion behind the knee and humiliates him before the contest even begins. Clifford's reversed tune drains away some of the harmful magic, but not enough.

Angora remembers an ancient Elvish word for strength from a crumbling book in the magical library. When she speaks it through her psychic sword, a radiant aura heals the champion, grants him greater strength, and surrounds him with protection. He enters the arena and defeats every opponent. The companions repay the potion keeper and become wealthier than they ever imagined. Angora proposes using the fortune to establish a school that will train the world's greatest warriors.
