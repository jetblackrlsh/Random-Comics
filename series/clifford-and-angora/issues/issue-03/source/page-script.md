# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. Angora raises her violet sword over the restored Tavern Champion as radiant ancient armor forms around him. Clifford plays beside the living lute, the Swamp Witch casts behind them, and Rigarchu smirks from the vast arena entrance.
- Readable title text: "THE ALL-STAR ARENA"
- Visual beats: International arena crowd, kings above, white-gold and violet magic, photoreal live-action fantasy.

## Page 2: The Potion's Answer

- Layout: Four chaotic shop panels.
- Caption boxes: "The mysterious potion exploded before the witch could identify it." "Its magic knocked everyone down and shattered shelves, bottles, and equipment throughout the shop." "Feeling impossible power inside her, Angora tried to restore the ruined business." "Her uncontrolled spell destroyed everything that the explosion had spared."
- Visual beats: Blast; debris; energized Angora rises; second destructive wave collapses shop around horrified keeper.

## Page 3: A Promise Paid By Victory

- Layout: Four panels expanding from shop rubble to arena scale.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford promised the furious keeper a share of their champion's winnings until every repair was paid." "The keeper reluctantly joined their strange company and followed them to the arena." "There they found kings, nobles, and military envoys gathered from across the world." "Their tavern fighter would face the greatest living champions in an all-star tournament."
- Visual beats: Handshake in wreckage; procession; first arena reveal; champion sees elite opponents.

## Page 4: Help That Crippled

- Layout: Four tense panels.
- Caption boxes: "Angora shared psychic strength, Clifford played an empowering song, and the witch cast protection over their champion." "The three spells collided and weakened him instead. His muscles failed, and the protective magic became a crushing weight." "A foreign gladiator kicked him behind the knee before the match even began." "Standing over the fallen fighter, the attacker proudly introduced himself as Rigarchu."
- Visual beats: Three magics converge; champion trembles; cheap kick; Rigarchu poses while rulers watch.

## Page 5: The Forgotten Word

- Layout: Four panels moving from frantic music to memory.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford played his empowering tune backward, drawing away part of the harmful enchantment." "The champion remained too weak to fight, and arena officials were already calling his name." "Angora remembered a crumbling library book written in the ancient Elvish spoken by her grandfather." "On its final page, one forgotten symbol had meant strength."
- Visual beats: Backward green-gold ribbons pull curse; champion still kneels; blue library memory; close ancient glyph reflected in Angora's eyes.

## Page 6: Ancient Strength Awakened

- Layout: Three large transformation panels.
- Caption boxes: "Angora raised her psychic sword and spoke the ancient words." "Radiant Elvish magic healed every injury, filled the champion with greater strength, and formed a barrier around his body." "Restored beyond his former power, their fighter entered the arena beneath the astonished gaze of the world."
- Visual beats: Invocation; white-gold armor and violet arcs form; champion strides through gate.

## Page 7: Champion Of Champions

- Layout: Five brisk action panels with one dominant victory panel.
- Caption boxes: "Their champion overwhelmed every opponent placed before him." "The ancient barrier turned aside blades, hammers, and even Rigarchu's fastest attacks." "By the final contest, the tavern fighter stood undefeated before the assembled rulers." "Clifford and Angora had transformed a reckless wager into an international victory."
- Visual beats: Varied combat montage; Rigarchu defeated fairly; champion crowned; companions celebrate.

## Page 8: A Greater Opportunity

- Layout: Four panels ending on Angora's vision.
- Caption boxes: "The enormous prize paid for the potion shop's complete reconstruction and still left the companions wealthy." "Only hours earlier, they had been unable to afford stew. Now they represented the world's most celebrated gladiator." "Angora proposed using their fortune to recruit and train warriors from every kingdom." "Their unlikely adventure had given birth to an academy."
- Visual beats: Keeper receives gold; treasure and stunned companions; Angora addresses rulers; imagined training grounds rise in sunrise light.
