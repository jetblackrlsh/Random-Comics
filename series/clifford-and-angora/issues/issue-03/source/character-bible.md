## Recurring Identities

Clifford remains the immense Black goliath with bundled locs, full beard, white tribal paint, green-and-purple gold-trimmed bardic garments, and warm demeanor. Angora remains the freckled fair-skinned elf with pointed ears, red high ponytail, spiked gunmetal armor, green accents, blue cape, violet sword, and round shield. The Swamp Witch remains the silver-haired elder in moss and peat robes. The Tavern Champion remains the shaved-headed, scarred, thick-bearded veteran in battered bronze-and-leather armor with a red sash.

## Rigarchu

Lean, athletic East Asian-coded human gladiator in his late twenties with sharp cheekbones, short black hair gathered into a topknot, confident smirk, lacquered crimson-and-black lamellar armor, gold lightning motifs, and flexible arena boots. His cheap attack is arrogant rather than lethal.

## Magic Continuity

Clifford's active music is green-gold. Harmful tangled enchantments appear muddy green, gray, and violet until the backward tune pulls them away. Angora's ancient word creates white-gold protection threaded by the established pink-violet psychic energy.
