# Treatment

## Title

The Academy Challenge

## Issue Number

4

## Publication Date

2026-08-02

## Format

Series issue #4, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

To fill their new academy, Clifford and Angora stake their champion against the finest warriors in the world—but Clifford's triumphant vision of peace becomes an apparent prophecy of destruction.

## Central Conflict

Clifford and Angora must prove that their unconventional experience can strengthen entire nations rather than merely produce one champion. Their public challenge succeeds so completely that suspicion replaces admiration, forcing them to explain their true cooperative purpose. Clifford then tries to make that purpose visible and accidentally shows the rulers their worst nightmare.

## Theme

Trust requires more than visible strength; a shared purpose must also survive fear and misunderstanding.

## Ending

Clifford's magical holograms show the rulers their kingdoms burning. The dignitaries interpret the images as a terrible omen and begin withdrawing support from the academy.

## Emotional Arc

Clifford begins determined to prove that a bard can train warriors and succeeds through the living lute. Angora turns that demonstration into a bold international challenge. Their academy rapidly fills, but triumph creates new doubts about their motives. Clifford's sincere attempt to reassure everyone produces the most frightening magical mistake yet.

## Cast

- Clifford, Angora, the living lute, the Tavern Champion, and the Swamp Witch.
- Foreign rulers, dignitaries, military envoys, and elite warriors from many fantasy cultures.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid photo-comic centered on the new warrior academy: stone courtyards, wooden training apparatus, banners from many nations, practical barracks, and an adjoining arena. Training combat should feel physical and documentary. Clifford's final holograms are green-gold projections corrupted into towering red-orange visions of burning capitals. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
