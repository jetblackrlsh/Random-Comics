Foreign rulers doubt what a bard could teach their greatest fighters. At dawn, Clifford releases his living lute and directs it to strike a training dummy with magical force. Angora challenges each ruler to send a champion against their Tavern Champion: any warrior who loses must remain at the new academy as a student.

Nearly every foreign champion loses. Elite fighters from distant kingdoms and unfamiliar traditions fill the academy while royal payments pour into the organization. Angora explains that they do not intend to steal the warriors; they will return each student to teach their homeland's armies everything learned at the school.

The rulers remain uncertain, so Clifford plays a grand song showing what their future could become. The magical holograms betray his hopeful lyrics. Instead of prosperous protected kingdoms, the rulers see their capitals burning, armies falling, and nations consumed by flame. Terrified dignitaries prepare to withdraw their warriors from the academy.
