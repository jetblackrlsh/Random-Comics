# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. Angora stands before a diverse formation of warriors while Clifford raises the living lute over a shattered training dummy. The Tavern Champion faces a line of foreign challengers. Above them, corrupted holograms reveal distant cities burning.
- Readable title text: "THE ACADEMY CHALLENGE"
- Visual beats: Heroic academy foreground with ominous red-orange prophecy overhead; photoreal live-action fantasy.

## Page 2: What Can A Bard Teach?

- Layout: Four dawn-lit training-yard panels.
- Caption boxes: "The rulers admired Angora's fighting skill but doubted what Clifford could teach the greatest warriors of their kingdoms." "At dawn, Clifford stood before a training dummy while every dignitary watched." "He released his living lute and guided it forward with a sharp magical chord." "The instrument struck with enough force to tear the reinforced dummy from its foundation."
- Visual beats: Skeptical rulers; Clifford readies lute; animated flight; explosive wooden impact.

## Page 3: Angora's Challenge

- Layout: Four clear negotiation panels.
- Caption boxes: "The demonstration proved that Clifford's music and living instrument could become weapons in their own right." "Angora invited every ruler to send one champion against their victorious tavern fighter." "If a foreign warrior won, nothing would change." "If that challenger lost, the fighter would remain at Clifford and Angora's academy as a student."
- Visual beats: Amazed envoys; Angora addresses semicircle; champion enters; rulers accept and summon fighters.

## Page 4: The First Students

- Layout: Five action-forward panels.
- Caption boxes: "Warriors from distant kingdoms entered the arena one after another." "Their weapons, armor, and traditions were different, but their opponent's ancient strength remained overwhelming." "Nearly every foreign champion was defeated." "Each honorable loss brought another extraordinary student into the new school."
- Visual beats: Diverse challengers; several distinct clashes; respectful defeat; first students line up.

## Page 5: A School Of The World

- Layout: Four broad academy panels.
- Caption boxes: "Soon the training yard held elite fighters from unfamiliar armies and faraway cultures." "Royal payments built barracks, libraries, practice halls, and workshops around the arena." "Clifford taught fighters to hear the rhythm of battle, while Angora trained them to focus power through the mind." "Their successful gladiator agency had become an international academy."

"Angora promised that every student would return home and teach everything learned at the academy." ## Page 7: A Song Of The Future

- Layout: Four panels with magical projections growing larger.
- Caption boxes: "The dignitaries remained uncertain, so Clifford tried to show them the peaceful future their cooperation could create." "He sang of prosperous kingdoms, powerful armies, and nations protected from every threat." "Green-gold holograms rose above the academy as rulers searched the visions for their homes." "The images did not match Clifford's hopeful song."
- Visual beats: Clifford begins; music ribbons; holographic capitals emerge; his smile fades.

## Page 8: Kingdoms In Flame

- Layout: Three enormous ominous panels.
- Caption boxes: "The rulers watched their capitals burn, their armies fall, and their towers collapse." "What Clifford intended as a promise appeared to be a terrible omen of destruction." "Terrified dignitaries ordered their warriors home—and the academy's new alliance began to break apart."
