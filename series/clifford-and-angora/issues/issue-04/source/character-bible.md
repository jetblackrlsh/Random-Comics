## Recurring Identities

Clifford remains the enormous Black goliath bard with bundled locs, beard, white tribal paint, layered green-and-purple clothing, and gold-trimmed living lute. Angora remains the red-ponytailed fair-skinned elf in spiked gunmetal armor, green accents, blue cape, wooden shield, and violet psychic sword. The Tavern Champion remains the shaved-headed, scarred, bearded veteran in battered bronze-and-leather armor with a red sash. The Swamp Witch remains the silver-haired elder in moss and peat robes.

## International Students And Rulers

Show a broad, respectful variety of adult fantasy peoples, body types, armor traditions, royal garments, weapons, and cultural silhouettes. They are visibly representatives of many nations rather than a uniform army. Avoid modern flags or real-world national emblems.

## Visual Rules

Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Maintain binding identities and setting geography. All readable text appears inside clean rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, page numbers, caption labels, or repeated same-page wording.
