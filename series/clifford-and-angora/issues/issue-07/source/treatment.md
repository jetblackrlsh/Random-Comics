# Treatment

## Title

The Artificer's Warning

## Issue Number

7

## Publication Date

2026-08-04

## Format

Series issue #7, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

A mysterious tiefling inventor saves the academy with an army of enchanted machines, strengthens Angora's failing psychic sword, and reveals that the only text capable of stopping the Horde was stolen by the rival they are too frightened to face again.

## Central Conflict

Clifford and Angora must decide whether the heavily armed stranger approaching during the kobold invasion is another threat or the ally their academy desperately needs. The artificer's machines prove their intentions, but their knowledge reveals that the sword, the stolen text, and the inventor's past are all connected to the coming Horde.

## Theme

Knowledge becomes courage when it is shared with people willing to continue where fear forced someone else to stop.

## Ending

The Rival Headmaster stays to defend and train the students. The Tiefling Artificer admits that fear prevents them from confronting their rival again, but entrusts their winged cauldron companion to Clifford and Angora. The heroes depart east to recover the stolen text.

## Emotional Arc

The academy first sees the artificer as a terrifying intruder. Relief replaces suspicion when their robot companions end the invasion, and curiosity turns the stranger into a new teacher. The artificer's confident mastery then gives way to vulnerability when their rival is mentioned. Clifford and Angora answer that fear by accepting the quest and carrying the artificer's hope forward.

## Cast

- Clifford and Angora.
- The Tiefling Artificer, a gifted but traumatized inventor and new academy teacher.
- The sentient Winged Cauldron Companion.
- The Rival Headmaster, the Swamp Witch, academy students, young kobolds, and enchanted Robot Companions.
- The Rival Artificer, discussed but not yet seen.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid photo-comic combining the established academy's practical stone-and-timber training grounds with tactile magitech: worn gold machinery, bright blue energy cores, animated tools, and protective force pulses. The sword enhancement should combine Clifford's green-gold music, Angora's violet psychic plasma, and the artificer's blue-gold engineering.
