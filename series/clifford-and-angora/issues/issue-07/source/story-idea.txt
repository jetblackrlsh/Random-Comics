During the kobold invasion, an androgynous tiefling artificer approaches the academy carrying an enormous golden arcane weapon. They have pinkish-purple skin, large horns, flowing white hair with a bold black streak, golden goggles, green-and-brown workshop clothing, and a triangular weapon barrel containing a bright blue energy orb. A sentient winged cauldron with a golden face floats beside them.

The artificer explains that the cauldron showed them the future and revealed that the academy needs help against the Horde. Clifford asks whether they have enough robot companions to stop the kobolds. An entire machine army appears. The clever kobolds try to dismantle the robots, but protective enchantments repel them until they flee underground.

Angora invites the artificer to teach at the academy. Clifford asks how to build a magical band of living instrument robots, and the artificer begins teaching him. Angora asks for help strengthening her violet psychic sword. The artificer speaks with the living magic in the weapon and discovers that it is weakening because it senses the Horde approaching. Clifford plays the Tune of the Astral Blade while the artificer uses golden mechanisms and blue energy to reinforce it. The sword flies into the sky and strikes the ground beside a mysterious glowing stone, which Angora stores in her knapsack.

The artificer reveals that the magical text capable of stopping the Horde was stolen by a rival who lives in a distant castle guarded by robot soldiers. The Rival Headmaster promises to remain at the academy and defend the students. Although the tiefling is too frightened to confront their rival again, the winged cauldron agrees to guide Clifford and Angora east. The heroes depart to recover the text.
