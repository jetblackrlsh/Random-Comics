## Recurring Identities

Clifford remains the immense Black goliath bard with deep brown skin, high bundled locs, full beard, symmetrical white tribal paint, layered green-and-purple gold-trimmed bardic clothing, and the living gold-filigreed lute. Angora remains the freckled fair-skinned elf with green eyes, vivid red high ponytail, long pointed ears, spiked gunmetal armor, green accents, blue cape, round wooden shield with psychic sheen, and translucent pink-violet energy sword. The Swamp Witch and Rival Headmaster retain their established references and clothing.

## The Tiefling Artificer

Androgynous adult tiefling with pinkish-purple skin, two large swept horns, angular friendly features, and flowing white hair marked by one bold black skunk-like streak. Golden workshop goggles rest on their forehead. They wear a forest-green shirt beneath layered brown utility belts, brown shoulder guards, a high brown leather collar, sturdy gloves, heavy boots, and a rugged brown split workshop apron. Their enormous gold-and-brass arcane cannon has a triangular barrel framing a bright blue energy orb.

## Winged Cauldron Companion

Waist-high blackened-bronze cauldron with a rounded golden mask-like face, two small articulated gold wings, side handles, stubby mechanical feet, and a warm blue magical glow rising from its open top. It communicates through expressive wing positions, chiming sounds, and tilting body language.

## Robot Companions

A varied family of small tactile magitech constructs built from worn brass, dark iron, wood, leather, and blue energy cores. The group includes winged scouts, two-legged tool carriers, wheeled shield machines, hopping animated tools, and compact multi-legged repair units. They look handcrafted and individual rather than mass-produced.

## Mysterious Glowing Stone

Palm-sized irregular charcoal-gray stone threaded with bright violet crystalline veins, a thin golden ring of artificer energy around its middle, and a faint blue-white inner pulse. It remains cool while hovering slightly above an open hand.

## Visual Rules

Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Maintain the binding identities and the tactile, worn physical construction of every magical machine. All readable text appears directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes generated in the page art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, page numbers, panel labels, caption numbers, caption labels, or repeated same-page wording.
