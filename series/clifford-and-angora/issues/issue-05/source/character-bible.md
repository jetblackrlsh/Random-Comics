## Recurring Identities

Clifford remains the immense Black goliath bard with high bundled locs, full beard, white tribal paint, green-and-purple bardic clothing, and the living gold-filigreed lute. Angora remains the freckled fair-skinned elf with a vivid red high ponytail, long pointed ears, spiked gunmetal armor, green accents, blue cape, wooden shield with psychic sheen, and violet energy sword. The Swamp Witch remains the weathered silver-haired elder in moss and peat robes with reed charms and a crooked staff.

## The Rival Headmaster

Dark-skinned elf woman in her late forties with a tall athletic build, long sharply pointed ears, stern amber eyes, high cheekbones, black-and-silver braided hair gathered into a crown, and a thin scar along her jaw. She wears elegant midnight-blue scholar's robes over articulated silver scale armor, a white mantle, leather scroll cases, and a straight ceremonial spear etched with ancient runes. Her posture is controlled and authoritative rather than openly aggressive.

## The Ancient Horde

An immense distant mass of varied armored silhouettes, giant siege beasts, crooked standards, and smoky torchlight gathering beyond dark mountains. Keep individual identities obscured so the horde remains a future mystery; no modern machinery or firearms.

## Visual Rules

Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Maintain binding identities and the established magical library's Gothic blue windows, carved shelves, floating books, and violet-blue illumination. All readable text appears inside clean rectangular caption boxes generated directly in the art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, page numbers, caption labels, or repeated same-page wording.
