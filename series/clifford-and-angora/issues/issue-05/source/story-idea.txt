Clifford's disastrous holograms convince foreign rulers that cooperation will destroy their nations. As they prepare to withdraw their warriors, Angora asks the Swamp Witch to attempt a difficult ancient spell remembered from the magical library. The witch places a vision directly in every ruler's mind. Each dignitary sees their homeland safe, their people thriving, and their armies protecting the innocent. The rulers restore their support for the academy.

Because ancient knowledge has repeatedly saved them, Clifford suggests returning to the magical library for lost techniques and histories. Before they reach its doors, the headmaster of a rival warrior school blocks their path. She is furious that royal families are considering moving their champions to Clifford and Angora's academy.

Angora refuses to answer rivalry with hostility. She invites the headmaster to join them as keeper of knowledge, preserving ancient texts and teaching forgotten warrior traditions. The rival reveals why her academy guarded those traditions: an ancient horde, unseen for more than a thousand years, is gathering beyond the known lands. No single warrior, school, or kingdom can stop it. Somewhere inside the magical library may be the knowledge their alliance needs to save the world.
