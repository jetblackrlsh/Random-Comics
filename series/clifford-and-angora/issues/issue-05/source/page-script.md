# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. Angora and Clifford stand with the Swamp Witch before the blue-lit magical library doors. The Rival Headmaster blocks the entrance with her ceremonial spear. Across the stormy sky behind the library, the enormous shadow of an armored horde gathers beyond black mountains.
- Readable title text: "THE RETURNING HORDE"
- Visual beats: Heroic foreground confrontation and apocalyptic distant scale; photoreal live-action fantasy.

## Page 2: An Alliance Breaking

- Layout: Four emotional panels under fading red hologram light.
- Caption boxes: "Foreign rulers began withdrawing their warriors after Clifford's magic showed every kingdom in flames." "Clifford could not explain why a hopeful song had created such terrifying images." "Angora remembered an ancient mind-vision spell from the magical library—one she had always feared to attempt." "She asked the Swamp Witch to place a truthful vision directly within the rulers' minds."
- Visual beats: Departing delegations; devastated Clifford; Angora recalls runes; witch accepts grave task.

## Page 3: The Future They Could Build

- Layout: Three large luminous panels.
- Caption boxes: "The witch drew upon ancient wisdom and connected every ruler to the hope behind the academy." "Each dignitary saw a different homeland standing strong, its people safe, and its soldiers defending the innocent." "The visions did not promise that danger would disappear. They showed that cooperation could help every nation survive it."
- Visual beats: White-gold spell links minds; varied peaceful homelands; rulers see prepared compassionate defenders.

## Page 4: United Again

- Layout: Four panels moving from reconciliation to departure.
- Caption boxes: "Fear released its hold, and the rulers pledged their support to Clifford and Angora once more." "The academy's students resumed training beneath the banners of many nations." "Ancient magic had repeatedly changed their fate, so Clifford proposed returning to the magical library." "There they might find forgotten techniques capable of guiding the school they had built."
- Visual beats: Renewed handclasps; multicultural training; Clifford points to old score; trio leaves academy toward library.

## Page 5: The Headmaster At The Door

- Layout: Four tense approach panels.
- Caption boxes: "Before they could enter the library, the headmaster of a rival warrior school blocked their path." "Royal families were considering moving their champions from her academy to Clifford and Angora's new institution." "She accused the pair of stealing students and ancient knowledge that her school had guarded for generations." "Angora lowered her sword and approached the rival as a colleague rather than an enemy."
- Visual beats: Library road; headmaster reveal; sharp confrontation; Angora de-escalates.

## Page 6: Keeper Of Knowledge

- Layout: Four measured negotiation panels.
- Caption boxes: "Angora argued that both schools wanted to prepare the greatest generation of fighters the world had ever known." "She invited the rival headmaster to preserve ancient texts and teach the forgotten ways of warriors at their shared academy." "No kingdom would own the knowledge, and no worthy student would be denied it." "The headmaster hesitated, because her academy's secrecy had never been motivated by pride alone."
- Visual beats: Angora's open hand; library shelves visible; imagined shared classroom; rival weighs secret.

## Page 7: A Thousand-Year Secret

- Layout: Four panels growing ominous.
- Caption boxes: "The rival revealed that an ancient threat was returning after more than a thousand years." "Beyond the known lands, a horde unlike anything in living memory was gathering." "No single warrior, school, or kingdom possessed the strength to stop it." "Their academy would need every fighter it could train and every alliance it could forge."
- Visual beats: Rival opens forbidden chronicle; historic battle images; distant horde; protagonists absorb stakes.

## Page 8: The Knowledge To Save The World

- Layout: Three vast concluding panels.
- Caption boxes: "The headmaster turned toward the magical library, where forgotten histories waited behind ancient doors." "Somewhere within those texts might be the only knowledge capable of defeating the returning horde." "Clifford and Angora had built a school by accident. Now it might become the world's last defense."
- Visual beats: Blue doors open; shelves extend impossibly deep; final exterior shows four allies entering while horde shadow spreads beyond mountains.
