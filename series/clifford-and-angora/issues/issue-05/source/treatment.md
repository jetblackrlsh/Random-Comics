# Treatment

## Title

The Returning Horde

## Issue Number

5

## Publication Date

2026-08-02

## Format

Series issue #5, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

After Clifford's false omen shatters the academy's alliance, Angora and the Swamp Witch restore the rulers' hope—only to learn that the destruction they feared may be approaching for real.

## Central Conflict

The academy cannot survive without trust between nations. Angora must risk an ancient mind-vision spell to replace panic with a truthful shared purpose, then persuade a rival headmaster that cooperation matters more than ownership of knowledge. The rival's secret transforms their institutional rivalry into a world-saving mission.

## Theme

Knowledge protects the future only when former rivals are willing to share it.

## Ending

The Rival Headmaster reveals that an ancient horde is gathering beyond the known lands and that the magical library may contain the only knowledge capable of preparing a united defense.

## Emotional Arc

Clifford begins devastated that his music has threatened everything they built. Angora answers fear with trust in the witch and in ancient knowledge. The restored alliance proves that their academy can unite nations, not merely train fighters. Angora then extends that same cooperative instinct to a rival, earning the warning that launches their next great adventure.

## Cast

- Clifford, Angora, the Swamp Witch, and representatives of the international academy.
- The Rival Headmaster: disciplined scholar-warrior who guards forgotten military knowledge and knows of the returning horde.
- Foreign rulers and students.
- The ancient horde, seen only as an ominous distant force.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid photo-comic. Begin beneath the red afterglow of Clifford's broken holograms, shift into intimate white-gold mind visions, then return to the established blue-violet Gothic magical library. The Rival Headmaster has a scholarly warrior silhouette that contrasts with Angora's aggressive spikes. The final horde vision should be vast, shadowed, and specific enough to establish danger without revealing every creature. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
