# Treatment

## Title

The Horde Comes Home

## Issue Number

10

## Publication Date

2026-08-08

## Format

Series issue #10, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

The heroes return with the Horde chronicle but accidentally lead the flying Dragonborn army to their academy, where the Tiefling Artificer must finally confront the former master who taught them everything.

## Central Conflict

The academy must survive the prophecy it was built to stop even though its warriors cannot defeat the Dragonborn Horde or the Rival Artificer's machines through strength alone.

## Theme

Preparation is tested when the feared future finally arrives.

## Ending

The Dragonborn reveals that they were once the Tiefling Artificer's master, transforming an international war into a deeply personal confrontation.

## Emotional Arc

Relief becomes guilt when Clifford realizes the rescue mission brought the Horde home. His students answer with courage but are overwhelmed. The Tiefling returns instead of running, only to face the person at the center of their fear.

## Cast

- Clifford, Angora, the Dwarf Rogue, the changeling, and the Winged Cauldron Companion.
- Academy students and the Rival Headmaster.
- The Tiefling Artificer and their flying cauldron army.
- The Dragonborn Rival Artificer, Dragonborn Horde, and robot soldiers.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid battlefield photojournalism across the established academy. Orange dragon fire, blue rival machinery, green-gold bardic light, violet psychic energy, and gold-and-blue flying cauldrons collide amid practical stone-and-timber architecture. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, page numbers, caption labels, or repeated same-page wording.
