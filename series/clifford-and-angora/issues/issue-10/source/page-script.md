# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. Clifford rallies academy warriors beneath the burning sky while the Pegasus lands with Angora and the chronicle. Flying Dragonborn and rival robots attack from above as the Tiefling Artificer arrives with an army of golden-winged cauldrons.
- Readable title text: "THE HORDE COMES HOME"
- Visual beats: Photoreal live-action siege, international academy banners, orange fire, violet sword light, green-gold music, and blue-gold machines.

## Page 2: The Enemy At Their Heels

- Layout: Four fast arrival panels.
- Caption boxes: "The Pegasus carried the fugitives and the stolen chronicle back toward Clifford and Angora's academy." "The Dragonborn Horde remained behind them through every mile of sky." "The heroes landed among the warriors they had trained." "The enemy descended before anyone could celebrate the mission's success."
- Visual beats: Aerial chase; academy below; hard landing; Dragonborn blot out sun.

## Page 3: A Prophecy Made Real

- Layout: Three large reflective panels.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford had once shown the world's rulers mistaken visions of their kingdoms burning." "The Rival Headmaster later warned that an ancient flying swarm would conquer through speed and confusion." "By seeking the knowledge needed to stop that future, Clifford and Angora had led the prophecy directly to their own gates."
- Visual beats: Memory of burning visions; headmaster's warning; present academy under airborne Horde.

## Page 4: Everything The Academy Represented

- Layout: Four rally panels.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford stood before students gathered from rival schools and distant nations." "He reminded them that cooperation was the reason their academy existed." "This was their chance to defend every kingdom represented within its walls." "Inspired warriors raised their weapons and charged together."
- Visual beats: Clifford with lute; diverse students; Angora lifts sword; mass charge.

## Page 5: Courage Was Not Enough

- Layout: Five chaotic combat panels.
- Caption boxes: "Dragonborn dived from the sky breathing fire." "Surviving machines attacked beside them while the Rival Artificer cast powerful spells." "The academy's fighters were brave, trained, and united." "They were still overwhelmed."
- Visual beats: Fire dive; robot clash; students protect each other; defenders driven back; academy burns.

## Page 6: The Teacher Returns

- Layout: Four heroic reveal panels.
- Caption boxes: "When hope nearly vanished, the Tiefling Artificer returned to the battlefield they had feared." "Their enormous cannon struck the Dragonborn rival and stunned them." "This time the inventor did not run." "They summoned an army of flying robot cauldrons to defend their students and friends."
- Visual beats: Tiefling entrance; blue cannon blast; determined face; portals release cauldron swarm.

## Page 7: Machines Against Machines

- Layout: Five kinetic aerial panels.
- Caption boxes: "Golden-winged cauldrons collided with the Rival Artificer's sentries above the academy." "Blue fire and brass fragments filled the air." "For one moment, the former coward's courage seemed capable of turning the battle." "Then the Dragonborn stood again and laughed."
- Visual beats: Cauldron dogfight; robots fall; Tiefling hopeful; Dragonborn rises from smoke.

## Page 8: The Master's Secret

- Layout: Three dramatic confrontation panels.
- Caption boxes: "The Dragonborn admitted that the Tiefling had grown powerful." "There was a reason the inventor understood machines, magic, and the enemy's every design." "The Rival Artificer had once been their master - the teacher who taught them everything."
- Visual beats: Dragonborn and Tiefling face each other; flashback silhouette of instruction; present master-student duel framed by burning academy.
