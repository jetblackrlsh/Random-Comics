The Pegasus carries Clifford, Angora, the Dwarf Rogue, the changeling, and the Winged Cauldron back to the warrior academy with the recovered chronicle. The flying Dragonborn Horde follows, turning Clifford's accidental visions and the Rival Headmaster's warning into a self-fulfilling prophecy. Clifford rallies the international students, but Dragonborn fire, powerful spells, and the Rival Artificer's surviving machines overwhelm the academy.

When defeat seems certain, the Tiefling Artificer returns. They blast the Rival Artificer with their enormous cannon and summon an army of flying robot cauldrons. Machines clash above the academy. The Dragonborn recovers, laughs, and reveals that the Tiefling became powerful because the Dragonborn taught them. The enemy is the Tiefling Artificer's former master.
