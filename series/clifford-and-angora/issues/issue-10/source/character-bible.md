## Recurring Identities

Maintain every established face, outfit, weapon, scale pattern, machine material, and magic color from the cited references. The Tiefling Artificer remains an androgynous pinkish-purple tiefling with swept horns, white hair with one black streak, golden goggles, green-and-brown workshop gear, and an enormous triangular gold-and-brass cannon with a blue core. The Rival Artificer remains orange-scaled with blue stripes, glowing blue eyes, purple-and-gold robes, crystal staff, red-and-blue necklace, and living pouch.

## Flying Cauldron Army

Dozens of smaller cousins of the established Winged Cauldron Companion: blackened-bronze bodies, expressive gold mask faces, articulated golden wings, blue inner flames, and varied handmade dents and repairs. They look like a family of individual enchanted machines rather than identical factory products.

## Visual Rules

Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. All readable text appears directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes generated in the page art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, page numbers, panel labels, caption numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, or repeated same-page wording.
