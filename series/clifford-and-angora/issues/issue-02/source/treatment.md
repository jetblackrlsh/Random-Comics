# Treatment

## Title

The Living Lute

## Issue Number

2

## Publication Date

2026-08-02

## Format

Series issue #2, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

When a swamp witch accidentally gives Clifford's lute a mind of its own, Clifford and Angora must heal their frightened new companion, escape a ruinous tavern debt, and survive one final magical mistake.

## Central Conflict

Clifford wants magical instruction, but the witch's unreliable spells create problems faster than the companions can solve them. The newly sentient lute is hurt and distrustful, Angora's impulsive wager multiplies their debt, and ordinary repairs only worsen the damage. Clifford must recognize that his music can heal and persuade even when it cannot reliably fight.

## Theme

Care, creativity, and persuasion can transform accidents into new possibilities.

## Ending

After repairing both the living lute and Angora's shield, the companions receive a mysterious potion. The witch's identification spell detonates the vial and engulfs the shop in magic.

## Emotional Arc

Clifford begins hopeful that the witch can perfect his instrument. He becomes horrified when the lute is injured, then discovers a gentler and more reliable use for his astral song. Angora's confidence collapses after the tavern champion defeats her, but Clifford turns that humiliation into a business opportunity without blaming her. Their recovered optimism leads directly into the potion-shop cliffhanger.

## Cast

- Clifford and Angora.
- The Swamp Witch: weathered, secretive spellcaster whose magic often backfires.
- The Tavern Champion: enormous veteran fighter who defeats Angora and becomes their first represented gladiator.
- The living magical lute: Clifford's familiar instrument, now animated, emotional, and capable of acting independently.
- Tavern patrons and the potion-shop keeper.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid photo-comic matching Issue #1. Warm village and tavern photography contrasts with violet psychic light and green-gold musical magic. The living lute must remain recognizably identical to Clifford's established ornate instrument while adding two small articulated wooden legs and expressive body language. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
