Clifford and Angora return toward their familiar tavern and meet an old witch on the village road. Clifford asks her to tune his magical lute. Her spell gives the instrument tiny wooden legs and a mind of its own. It runs away, Angora catches it, and the lute cracks in her grip. Now alive and frightened, it whimpers until Clifford plays the Tune of the Astral Blade. Angora's psychic sword channels violet energy through the music and heals the broken wood.

Inside the tavern, the companions buy stew and mead while questioning the guarded witch about the distant swamps. Angora accidentally orders more than she can afford. Clifford and his newly animated lute perform together, but their coins still do not cover the bill. Angora challenges the tavern's strongest fighter to a wager of one hundred flagons and loses almost instantly, leaving her shield shattered and the debt even larger.

Clifford avoids another fight and instead offers to represent the victorious champion in the gladiatorial arena. The fighter loves the idea and pays an advance that clears the debt. When the witch tries to repair Angora's shield, she breaks it into even smaller pieces. Clifford again channels the Tune of the Astral Blade and restores it with a faint psychic sheen.

At the potion shop, the proprietor rewards their growing magical reputation with a mysterious free vial. The witch tries to identify it. The potion explodes.
