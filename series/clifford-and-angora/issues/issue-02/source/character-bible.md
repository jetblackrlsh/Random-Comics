## Clifford

Black/African goliath bard in his early thirties, eight feet tall and immensely muscular, with deep brown skin, full black beard, very long black locs bundled high with gold cuffs, and symmetrical white tribal face and body paint. He wears layered green-and-royal-purple bardic garments with gold trim and carries the giant ornate lute. His scale is imposing but his expression and posture are warm.

## Angora

Fair-skinned athletic elf fighter in her mid-twenties with freckles, long pointed ears, green eyes, vivid deep-red high ponytail, spiked gunmetal armor, green accents, a blue cape, round wooden shield, and pink-violet psychic sword. Her stance is disciplined and direct.

## The Swamp Witch

Elderly human woman with a deeply lined face, sharp gray eyes, long tangled silver hair, layered moss-green and peat-brown robes, reed charms, bone beads, a crooked walking staff, and weathered hands stained by herbs. Her magic appears as smoky turquoise and amber light. She looks knowledgeable even when a spell goes wrong.

## The Tavern Champion

Massive middle-aged human pit fighter with deep brown weathered skin, shaved head, thick dark beard, scar over one eyebrow, battered bronze-and-leather arena armor, red sash, heavy boots, and a broad iron practice sword. He is physically overwhelming, proud, and delighted by Clifford's offer of fame.

## The Living Lute

The established giant ornate brown wooden lute with carved gold filigree, curled headstock, and golden strings, now supported by two small articulated wooden legs. It communicates through posture, hopping, trembling, and musical sounds rather than a face or written symbols.
