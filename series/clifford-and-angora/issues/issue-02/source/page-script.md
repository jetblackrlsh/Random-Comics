# Page Script

Every page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. Generate all readable title and story text directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the artwork. Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption labels, blank boxes, later overlays, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise magical comedy, the newly living lute, and the cost of another disastrous request for help.
- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover. Clifford reaches toward his ornate lute as it runs on tiny wooden legs through a lantern-lit village street. Angora pursues with her violet sword and wooden shield. The Swamp Witch raises both hands in alarm while the Tavern Champion looms in a warm tavern doorway behind them.
- Readable title text: "THE LIVING LUTE"
- Visual beats: Photoreal live-action fantasy, urgent movement, playful danger, Clifford and Angora matching their binding references.

## Page 2: The Witch's Adjustment

- Layout: Four large panels in top-to-bottom order.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford and Angora were almost back at the tavern when an old witch stepped into their path." "Still eager to improve his unreliable music, Clifford asked the experienced spellcaster to tune his magical lute." "The witch adjusted one peg and poured ancient power into the instrument." "Two tiny wooden legs sprouted beneath the lute—and it bounded down the village road."
- Visual beats: Pair walking together; Clifford respectfully presents lute; close hands at golden strings; startled lute leaps away as all three react.

## Page 3: A Frightened New Life

- Layout: Four action panels with the cracked lute dominant in the final panel.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford and Angora chased the runaway instrument through the crowded street." "Clifford nearly caught it, but the nimble lute twisted beyond his enormous hands." "Angora seized it at last. The struggling wood cracked loudly in her grip." "The lute whimpered. The spell had not merely animated an object—it had created a frightened living companion."
- Visual beats: Candid chase; lute dodges Clifford; Angora catches it; crack across body, golden strings slack, Angora remorseful.

## Page 4: The Tune That Healed

- Layout: Three large emotional panels.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford cradled the wounded lute and played the Tune of the Astral Blade as gently as he could." "Angora's violet sword answered the melody, sending psychic energy through the golden strings and into the broken wood." "The fracture vanished. Fully healed, the living lute trusted Clifford enough to leap joyfully into his arms."
- Visual beats: Close compassionate Clifford; Angora's blade channels violet energy along green-gold music ribbons; restored lute hops with expressive posture.

## Page 5: Music Cannot Pay Everything

- Layout: Four warm tavern panels.
- Caption boxes: "Over hot stew, the witch admitted that she came from the distant swamps—but guarded every other secret." "Angora ordered more food and mead before realizing that she could not afford the bill." "Clifford and his animated lute performed together, astonishing the patrons and filling the floor with coins." "The duet still fell short. Clifford and Angora remained in debt."
- Visual beats: Group at table; tavern keeper delivers order; joyful duo performance; counted coins beside unpaid bill.

## Page 6: One Hundred Flagons

- Layout: Four sharp combat panels.
- Caption boxes: "Angora challenged the tavern's strongest fighter: victory would earn one hundred flagons, but defeat would cost the same." "The massive champion accepted before Clifford could question the bargain." "One brutal strike knocked Angora to the floor and shattered her wooden shield in half." "Their debt had become enormous, and their strongest fighter had just been defeated."
- Visual beats: Angora points at champion; crowd forms ring; decisive impact; Angora amid shield pieces while champion stands victorious.

## Page 7: Clifford's Better Weapon

- Layout: Four panels shifting from humiliation to clever triumph.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford did not challenge the champion. He offered to become the fighter's agent instead." "He promised greater opponents, arena fame, and a percentage of every victory they could arrange." "The delighted champion paid an advance large enough to erase every tavern debt." "Then the witch tried to repair Angora's shield—and broke it into even smaller pieces."
- Visual beats: Clifford pitches calmly; champion imagines arena glory; coins clear bill; comic final image of shield fragments exploding around mortified witch.

## Page 8: One Mystery Too Many

- Layout: Four panels ending on a full-width magical blast.
- Caption boxes: "Clifford again played the astral tune, rebuilding Angora's shield with a protective psychic sheen." "At the potion shop, their growing reputation earned them a mysterious vial from the keeper's secret collection." "The keeper would only say that they would understand its purpose when they needed it." "The witch attempted to identify the potion. The vial exploded."
- Visual beats: Shield pieces knit together; shopkeeper presents vial; suspicious trio studies it; turquoise-violet explosion fills shop as cliffhanger.
