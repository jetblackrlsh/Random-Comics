# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: The Tavern

- First appears: Issue #1, "Clifford and Angora's First Adventure"
- Visual identifiers: Crowded timber-and-stone fantasy tavern with amber lanterns, communal tables, travelers, mugs, a busy bar, and a clear open floor where magical trouble can erupt.
- Continuity notes: Clifford and Angora meet here. Clifford's summon brings twelve angry tiefling women into the room, and Angora's blazing psychic sword frightens them away.

## Setting: The Magical Library

- First appears: Issue #1, "Clifford and Angora's First Adventure"
- Visual identifiers: Vast stone library with Gothic blue windows, carved wooden shelves, floating books, violet-blue magical light, reading tables, and open illuminated volumes.
- Continuity notes: Angora learns that a warrior's deepest power comes from the mind. Clifford finds the Tune of the Astral Blade here.

## Setting: The River And Woodland

- First appears: Issue #1, "Clifford and Angora's First Adventure"
- Visual identifiers: Broad rushing river bordered by green woodland, rocky banks, and a shallow stony margin. Natural daylight contrasts with vivid green-gold music magic and pink-violet psychic energy.
- Continuity notes: Shark bandits ambush Clifford and Angora here. Angora's missed psychic blast temporarily opens a crater in the rushing current and frightens the bandits into retreat.

## Setting: The Potion Shop

- First appears: Issue #2, "The Living Lute"
- Visual identifiers: Narrow timber-and-stone apothecary crowded with glass bottles, dried herbs, copper tools, shelves, wooden cabinets, and a guarded secret collection behind the counter.
- Continuity notes: A mysterious potion explodes during identification, and Angora's uncontrolled restoration attempt completes the shop's destruction. Tournament winnings later pay for a complete rebuild.

## Setting: The All-Star Arena

- First appears: Issue #3, "The All-Star Arena"
- Visual identifiers: Monumental open-air fantasy arena built from pale sandstone, dark bronze, and weathered banners, with a sand fighting floor, vaulted fighter gates, elevated royal galleries, and crowded terraces representing many kingdoms.
- Continuity notes: The Tavern Champion wins an international tournament here. Its adjacent grounds later support Clifford and Angora's academy challenge.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/gladiatorial-arena-setting.png`

## Setting: Clifford And Angora's Warrior Academy

- First appears: Issue #4, "The Academy Challenge"
- Visual identifiers: Expansive stone training campus constructed around an arena, with open practice courtyards, wooden dummies and obstacle frames, barracks, workshops, a study hall, and banners from many fictional nations. Architecture combines practical military design with warm communal spaces.
- Continuity notes: Founded with All-Star Arena winnings and royal payments. Its mission is to train elite students who return home to teach their nations. By Issue #5, it becomes the foundation of an international alliance against the returning horde.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/warrior-academy-setting.png`

## Setting: The Rival Artificer's Castle

- First appears: Issue #7, "The Artificer's Warning"
- Visual identifiers: Distant black-stone fortress built into a jagged eastern mountain, reinforced with worn brass machinery, blue-lit defensive conduits, rotating gearwork gates, narrow bridges, and patrol terraces occupied by robot soldiers. Medieval masonry remains dominant, with tactile magitech added rather than modern technology.
- Continuity notes: The Rival Artificer guards the stolen ancient Horde text here. Issue #7 reveals the castle through the Winged Cauldron Companion's vision, and Clifford and Angora begin traveling toward it.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/rival-artificer-castle-setting.png`
