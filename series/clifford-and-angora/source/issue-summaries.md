# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: Clifford and Angora's First Adventure

- Folder: `series/clifford-and-angora/issues/issue-01/`
- Creation date: 2026-08-01
- Logline: When Clifford's unreliable magical music repeatedly creates trouble, the gentle goliath bard and the battle-hungry elf Angora survive angry tieflings and shark bandits by discovering that their real strength lies in the heart and mind they encourage in each other.
- Plot summary: Clifford meets Angora in a lively tavern and offers to summon a creature worthy of testing her fighting skill. The harmless visitor takes offense at Angora's offer of friendship and summons twelve tiefling women, while Clifford accidentally insults them with a reversed sacred song. Angora frightens them away with her psychic sword, and the new companions visit a magical library to improve their abilities. At a nearby river, shark bandits ambush them for Angora's sword. Clifford's supposed strengthening tune weakens the blade, but his terrible second performance distracts the attackers long enough for Angora to focus her mental power. Her psychic blast misses the bandits and detonates in the river, terrifying them into retreat. Clifford and Angora end the adventure by affirming that his power comes from his heart and hers from her mind.
- Continuity changes: Clifford and Angora become traveling companions. Clifford accepts that he needs musical instruction, and Angora begins consciously drawing her fighting strength from mental focus.
- New characters: Clifford; Angora.
- New settings: The tavern; the magical library; the river and surrounding woodland.
- New key items: Clifford's magical lute; Angora's psychic sword; Angora's wooden shield.
- Reference images added: `clifford-character.jpg`; `angora-character.jpg`; `clifford-magical-lute.jpg`; `angora-psychic-sword-and-shield.jpg`.

## Issue #2: The Living Lute

- Folder: `series/clifford-and-angora/issues/issue-02/`
- Creation date: 2026-08-02
- Logline: When a swamp witch accidentally gives Clifford's lute a mind of its own, Clifford and Angora must heal their frightened new companion, escape a ruinous tavern debt, and survive one final magical mistake.
- Plot summary: A Swamp Witch tries to tune Clifford's lute and instead gives it wooden legs and a living mind. Angora accidentally cracks the fleeing instrument, but Clifford heals it by channeling the Tune of the Astral Blade through Angora's sword. In the tavern, their performance cannot cover an accidental debt, and Angora loses a reckless wager to the Tavern Champion, who shatters her shield. Clifford recruits the victor as their first represented gladiator and uses his advance to clear the bill. After Clifford repairs Angora's shield with psychic energy, the witch attempts to identify a mysterious potion and detonates the vial.
- Continuity changes: Clifford's lute becomes a sentient two-legged companion. The Tavern Champion becomes Clifford and Angora's first represented fighter. Angora's rebuilt shield gains a faint psychic sheen. The Swamp Witch joins the companions.
- New characters: The Swamp Witch; the Tavern Champion.
- New settings: The potion shop; the village road.
- New key items: The Living Magical Lute; the mysterious potion.
- Reference images added: `swamp-witch-character.png`; `tavern-champion-character.png`; `living-magical-lute.png`.

## Issue #3: The All-Star Arena

- Folder: `series/clifford-and-angora/issues/issue-03/`
- Creation date: 2026-08-02
- Logline: After magical mistakes ruin a potion shop and cripple their first gladiator, Angora must recover a forgotten Elvish spell before an arena full of kings witnesses their enterprise collapse.
- Plot summary: The mysterious potion destroys its shop, and Angora's uncontrolled attempt at restoration worsens the damage. Clifford promises the keeper a share of their champion's winnings. At the All-Star Arena, the companions' combined enhancements weaken the Tavern Champion, allowing Rigarchu to humiliate him. Clifford reverses part of the harmful magic, and Angora remembers an ancient Elvish word for strength. Her spell heals, empowers, and protects their fighter, who wins the entire tournament. The winnings rebuild the shop and fund Angora's proposal for an international warrior academy.
- Continuity changes: Angora gains access to an ancient Elvish strength spell. The Tavern Champion becomes an internationally celebrated gladiator. Clifford and Angora become wealthy and decide to found a warrior academy.
- New characters: Rigarchu; the potion-shop keeper.
- New settings: The All-Star Arena.
- New key items: Ancient Elvish strength magic.
- Reference images added: `gladiatorial-arena-setting.png`.

## Issue #4: The Academy Challenge

- Folder: `series/clifford-and-angora/issues/issue-04/`
- Creation date: 2026-08-02
- Logline: To fill their new academy, Clifford and Angora stake their champion against the finest warriors in the world—but Clifford's triumphant vision of peace becomes an apparent prophecy of destruction.
- Plot summary: Clifford proves that a bard can train warriors by directing his living lute through a reinforced dummy. Angora challenges foreign rulers to send their champions against the Tavern Champion, with defeated fighters remaining as students. Nearly every challenger loses, and royal support rapidly builds an international academy. Angora promises that students will return home to strengthen their own nations. Clifford tries to show the rulers that peaceful future through magical holograms, but the images instead depict their kingdoms burning. Terrified dignitaries begin withdrawing their warriors.
- Continuity changes: Clifford and Angora's warrior academy becomes a functioning international institution. Clifford learns to use the living lute as a direct combat weapon. The alliance enters crisis after the false omen.
- New characters: International rulers, envoys, and academy students.
- New settings: Clifford and Angora's Warrior Academy.
- New key items: None.
- Reference images added: `warrior-academy-setting.png`.

## Issue #5: The Returning Horde

- Folder: `series/clifford-and-angora/issues/issue-05/`
- Creation date: 2026-08-02
- Logline: After Clifford's false omen shatters the academy's alliance, Angora and the Swamp Witch restore the rulers' hope—only to learn that the destruction they feared may be approaching for real.
- Plot summary: Angora asks the Swamp Witch to replace Clifford's frightening holograms with truthful visions of safe homelands and cooperative defenders. The rulers restore their support, and the academy resumes training. Clifford suggests searching the magical library for lost knowledge, but a Rival Headmaster blocks their path and accuses them of stealing students and traditions. Angora invites her to join the academy as keeper of knowledge. The headmaster reveals that an ancient horde is returning after more than a thousand years and that the library may hold the only knowledge capable of saving the world.
- Continuity changes: The international alliance is restored. The Rival Headmaster becomes a potential ally. The academy's mission expands from training warriors to preparing a united defense against the returning horde.
- New characters: The Rival Headmaster; the ancient horde.
- New settings: None.
- New key items: The forbidden chronicle of the ancient horde.
- Reference images added: `rival-headmaster-character.png`.

## Issue #6: The Kobold Uprising

- Folder: `series/clifford-and-angora/issues/issue-06/`
- Creation date: 2026-08-04
- Logline: When the allied academies test their students against a newly hatched kobold, Clifford and Angora prove that teamwork can overcome fear - only to discover that an entire underground colony has come to defend the defeated creature.
- Plot summary: Clifford asks the Rival Headmaster to explain the returning Horde while students from both academies gather around an egg supplied by the Swamp Witch. A small pink kobold hatches, breathes fire, and knocks down a student who attacks alone. Clifford restores the fighter's courage through music, and Angora calls a second student forward. The pair defeat the kobold without killing it by exchanging positions and splitting its attention. Clifford celebrates their cooperation, but the Swamp Witch reveals that kobold eggs lie throughout the area. Dozens of young kobolds erupt beneath the academy to defend the fallen creature, and a heavily armed horned stranger approaches during the invasion.
- Continuity changes: The Rival Headmaster's school and Clifford and Angora's academy begin training as one alliance. Their students prove that coordinated teamwork can defeat a dangerous enemy. An underground kobold colony invades the academy.
- New characters: Young Kobolds; the Tiefling Artificer, seen at a distance.
- New settings: None.
- New key items: None.
- Reference images added: `young-kobold-character.png`; `tiefling-artificer-character.png`.

## Issue #7: The Artificer's Warning

- Folder: `series/clifford-and-angora/issues/issue-07/`
- Creation date: 2026-08-04
- Logline: A mysterious tiefling inventor saves the academy with an army of enchanted machines, strengthens Angora's failing psychic sword, and reveals that the only text capable of stopping the Horde was stolen by the rival they are too frightened to face again.
- Plot summary: The Tiefling Artificer arrives during the kobold invasion and explains that their Winged Cauldron Companion has shown them a future threatened by the Horde. They summon a large family of enchanted Robot Companions whose protective magic prevents the kobolds from dismantling them and drives the colony underground. Angora invites the inventor to teach at the academy, while Clifford begins learning how to create a band of living instrument machines. The artificer discovers that Angora's sword is weakening because it senses the Horde. Clifford's Tune of the Astral Blade and the artificer's blue-gold machinery strengthen it, producing a mysterious glowing stone. The artificer reveals that a feared rival stole the ancient Horde text and guards it within a mechanized castle. The Rival Headmaster remains to defend the academy, and the winged cauldron guides Clifford and Angora east.
- Continuity changes: The Tiefling Artificer joins the academy as a teacher. Clifford begins learning to build magical instrument companions. Angora's psychic sword is strengthened, and she gains the Mysterious Glowing Stone. Clifford and Angora depart to recover the stolen Horde text.
- New characters: The Winged Cauldron Companion; the Rival Artificer.
- New settings: The Rival Artificer's Castle.
- New key items: The Winged Cauldron Companion; the Artificer's Robot Companions; the Mysterious Glowing Stone.
- Reference images added: `winged-cauldron-companion.png`; `artificer-robot-companions.png`; `mysterious-glowing-stone.png`; `rival-artificer-castle-setting.png`.
