## Series

## Logline

A grieving engineer and an abandoned space-barbarian must see past one another's appearances when a warrior princess asks them to turn their shared connection to manatite into a new kind of knighthood.

## Central Conflict

Web Shooter and ROOF-AR both want to protect others, but trauma has taught each to distrust what the other represents. The Princess must show them that their different strengths and shared losses make cooperation possible.

## Theme

Family is not only what fate gives or violence takes away. It is also the responsibility people choose toward one another.

## Emotional Arc

Three isolated survivors begin in different corners of the galaxy. Web Shooter hides grief beneath control, ROOF-AR hides fear beneath anger, and the Princess hides urgency beneath serenity. Their collision is tense, but recognition replaces prejudice. By choosing a common purpose, they transform loneliness into the foundation of a team.

## Ending

Web Shooter holsters his weapons, ROOF-AR grounds his axe, and the Princess opens a route across the glowing star map. They do not erase their suspicion, but they choose to stand together at the Citadel windows as the first Manatite Knights are founded.
