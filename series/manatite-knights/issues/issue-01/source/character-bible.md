## Web Shooter

Use `../../../../source/character-descriptions.md` and `../../../../reference-images/web-shooter-character.webp`. Preserve his late-30s Black appearance, short twists, close beard, open face, matte red-and-cobalt-blue magnetic suit, belt cartridges, and twin sealant pistols. His movement is precise and careful, not conventionally military.

## ROOF-AR

Use `../../../../source/character-descriptions.md` and `../../../../reference-images/roof-ar-character.webp`. Preserve his eight-foot green canine body, amber eyes, notched ear, rough fur, barbarian armor, red cords, charcoal cloak, double-headed axe, and worn rifle. His anger should never erase his readable grief or protective instinct.
