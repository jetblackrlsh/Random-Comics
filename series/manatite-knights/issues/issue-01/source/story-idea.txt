Long before anyone called him Web Shooter, he was a celebrated aerospace engineer whose manatite sealant made routine interstellar travel safer. After criminals murdered his family, he adapted the invention into nonlethal web grenades and became a stealthy bounty hunter who protected other travelers. ROOF-AR began as an ordinary dog abandoned on a one-way space mission, transformed into a towering green humanoid canine by manatite radiation, and found a chosen family among nomadic alien space barbarians. When a superior enemy destroyed or captured that clan, he was forced to seek help. The Manatite Princess, heir and general of a crystal kingdom, understood that the same enemy would soon threaten her people. She summoned the two survivors because each possessed a profound manatite attunement and the character to use it responsibly. Their first meeting was hostile, but shared grief allowed them to recognize one another. Together with the Princess, they chose to become the first Manatite Knights and protect families across the galaxy.
