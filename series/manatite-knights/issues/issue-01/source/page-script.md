# Page Script

## Page 1 — Cover

Full-page cinematic group portrait inside the Manatite Citadel. The Princess stands centered with her red-blue shield raised. Web Shooter clings magnetically to a slanted metal wall above and left, pistols lowered but ready. ROOF-AR stands at right with his axe grounded. Blue-violet crystal light unifies them while their body language still shows tension. Readable title only: “MANATITE KNIGHTS” and subtitle “THE FIRST THREE.”

## Page 2 — The Engineer Who Could Not Save His Family

Three large panels. A famous aerospace engineer demonstrates manatite sealant closing a hull breach. Wealth and praise follow. Then a quiet memorial photograph and empty home show that criminals murdered his family despite all his success. Captions clearly establish the invention, loss, and change of purpose.

## Page 3 — The Hunter on the Ceiling

Four action panels aboard a pirate-held passenger ship. Web Shooter crawls silently upside down, studies enemy positions, fires expanding foam, and leaves pirates immobilized as a frightened civilian family emerges safely. Captions explain his mortality, method, and true reward.

## Page 4 — The Dog the Stars Remade

Four chronological panels. An ordinary dog is sealed into a one-way experimental spacecraft. Blue-violet radiation transforms him. Alien space barbarians discover the vessel and teach the now-humanoid ROOF-AR language, navigation, and combat. The final panel shows him welcomed at their fire-table. Captions make abandonment and chosen family explicit.

## Page 5 — Alone Again

Three large panels. A superior unseen enemy tears through the barbarian fleet. ROOF-AR fights but cannot prevent clan ships from being destroyed or seized. In the wreckage, he admits that strength alone cannot recover his family. Captions carry his fear of trusting strangers and his decision to seek help anyway.

## Page 6 — The Princess Who Was Ready

Four panels in the crystal kingdom. The Princess learns the wish-law by breathing over manatite powder, trains behind her holographic shield, commands soldiers, and studies projections of Web Shooter and ROOF-AR. Captions explain manatite's threefold role and why character matters more than raw power.

## Page 7 — The Summons

Four tense panels in the Citadel command chamber. Web Shooter keeps distance and watches ROOF-AR like a raider. ROOF-AR snarls at the humanlike hunter. The Princess places images of both lost families above the tactical table and offers them a purpose greater than revenge. Their expressions soften as each recognizes the other's grief.

## Page 8 — The First Three

Two broad panels. Web Shooter holsters his sealant pistols, ROOF-AR peacefully grounds his axe, and the Princess opens a blue-violet path across the star map. The final image shows the trio standing together at the Citadel windows, facing the peaceful worlds they have chosen to protect. Captions state that their different lives have become one purpose and that destiny did not choose them—they chose to answer.
