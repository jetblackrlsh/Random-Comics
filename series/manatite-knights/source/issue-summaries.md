# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The First Three

- Folder: `series/manatite-knights/issues/issue-01/`
- Publication date: 2026-08-11
- Logline: A grieving inventor, an abandoned canine barbarian, and a warrior princess overcome mutual suspicion to become the galaxy's first Manatite Knights.
- Plot summary: Web Shooter turns his manatite hull-sealant invention into a nonlethal weapon after criminals murder his family. ROOF-AR is abandoned in space, transformed by manatite radiation, adopted by alien barbarians, and left alone again when a superior enemy overwhelms his clan. The Manatite Princess, foreseeing a threat to her crystal kingdom, summons both survivors. Their distrust gives way to recognition of their shared loss, and they accept her offer to form a new order devoted to protecting other families.
- Continuity changes: Web Shooter and ROOF-AR agree to trust the Princess and one another. The Manatite Knights are formally founded with three members.
- New characters: Web Shooter; ROOF-AR; the Manatite Princess.
- New settings: Manatite Citadel Command Chamber; Nomad Barbarian Starship; Pirate Passenger Ship.
- New key items: Manatite; Web Sealant Pistols; ROOF-AR's Axe; Holographic Shield.
