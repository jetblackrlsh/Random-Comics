# Character Descriptions

## Character: Web Shooter

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Role: founding Manatite Knight; aerospace engineer turned nonlethal bounty hunter.
- Visual identifiers: Black man in his late 30s; lean engineer's build; deep brown skin; angular intelligent face; short neat twists; close-cropped beard; focused dark-brown eyes. Practical matte red-and-cobalt-blue magnetic infiltration suit with segmented plates, reinforced palms and soles, compact belt canisters, and an open face without a mask. Carries twin sealant pistols with translucent cartridges of pearlescent blue-white manatite foam.
- Continuity notes: Invented manatite emergency hull sealant and became wealthy before criminals murdered his family. Physically mortal and not a soldier; survives through stealth, patience, engineering, and immobilizing web grenades. His purpose is preventing other families from suffering his loss.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/web-shooter-character.webp`

## Character: ROOF-AR

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Role: founding Manatite Knight; transformed canine and space-barbarian warrior.
- Visual identifiers: eight-foot-tall green humanoid canine; powerful build; broad shepherd-mastiff muzzle; amber eyes; pointed ears with one notch; short rough moss-green fur with darker green along the spine; large clawed hands and digitigrade feet. Wears scavenged dark leather, blackened steel plates, red clan cords, an asymmetrical shoulder guard, and a ragged charcoal cloak. Carries a massive double-headed iron axe with a blue energy emitter and a worn laser rifle.
- Continuity notes: Launched as an ordinary dog on a mission with no return, transformed by manatite radiation, then adopted by nomadic alien space barbarians. Distrusts humanlike civilizations but reveres chosen family. His clan has been destroyed or captured by an enemy powerful enough to frighten him.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/roof-ar-character.webp`

## Character: The Manatite Princess

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Role: founding Manatite Knight; royal heir, general, scholar, and spiritual master of manatite.
- Visual identifiers: woman in her early 30s; warm medium-brown skin; calm oval face; luminous hazel-brown eyes; dark brown hair in two elegant symmetrical space buns. Wears a silver cosmic tiara with blue-violet crystals, layered white royal battle robes over pearl-gray armor, subtle red and cobalt-blue trim, and silver boots. Projects a translucent hexagonal red-and-blue holographic shield from her left forearm and carries manatite powder at her waist.
- Continuity notes: Raised in a kingdom built upon manatite. Combines meditative attunement with front-line military command. Recruits protectors for character as much as power and deliberately binds opposing strengths into a team.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/manatite-princess-character.webp`
