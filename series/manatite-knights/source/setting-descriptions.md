# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Manatite Citadel Command Chamber

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Visual identifiers: vast circular war room of pale gray stone and transparent blue-violet crystal; high arched ribs; angular windows opening to a starfield; black metal command table with restrained holograms; reflective white floor; red and cobalt-blue military lines; meditation alcoves beside tactical stations.
- Continuity notes: The Princess uses the chamber for both strategic planning and contemplative judgment. It becomes the place where the first Manatite Knights choose one another.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/manatite-citadel-setting.webp`

## Setting: Nomad Barbarian Starship

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Visual identifiers: battered iron spacecraft with round pressure doors, exposed pipes, smoke-hazed amber work lights, leather wraps, clan cords, weapon racks, and ancient carved metal fitted around modern starship systems.
- Continuity notes: ROOF-AR's first true home. Its loss or capture drives him to seek allies.

## Setting: Pirate Passenger Ship

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Visual identifiers: cramped gray industrial corridors, ribbed metal ceilings, cold strip lights, cargo alcoves, exposed vents, and emergency red illumination.
- Continuity notes: A representative hunting ground where Web Shooter uses ceiling-crawling stealth and nonlethal sealant weapons.
