# Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: Manatite

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Visual identifiers: rare translucent blue-violet crystal with silver veins and star-like internal lights; crushed powder sparkles with the same cool glow. Common forms include raw shards, ritual powder in a silver mortar, and sealed technological cartridges.
- Continuity notes: Currency, technology, and magic at once. An attuned person can scatter powdered manatite with their breath and make a wish; the amount consumed limits the scale of the reality alteration. It powers Web Shooter's sealant, caused ROOF-AR's transformation, and anchors the Princess's kingdom and abilities.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/manatite-key-item.webp`

## Key Item: Web Sealant Pistols

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Visual identifiers: twin compact industrial pistols with black metal frames, blue status lights, and translucent cartridges of pearlescent blue-white expanding foam.
- Continuity notes: Fire rapidly expanding manatite-infused sealant originally invented to close spacecraft hull breaches. Web Shooter uses controlled bursts and grenade capsules to immobilize targets without killing them.

## Key Item: ROOF-AR's Axe

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Visual identifiers: massive double-headed blackened iron axe, worn blade edges, red cord around the grip, and a compact blue energy emitter integrated into the haft.
- Continuity notes: Combines the ancient and futuristic weapon traditions of ROOF-AR's adopted clan.

## Key Item: Holographic Shield

- First appears: Issue #1, "The First Three"
- Visual identifiers: translucent elongated hexagonal shield projected from the Princess's left forearm, divided into elegant red and cobalt-blue energy fields with pale crystal edges.
- Continuity notes: A defensive focus for the Princess's military technology and manatite attunement.
