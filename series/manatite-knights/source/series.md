# Manatite Knights

## Logline

Three survivors whose lives were transformed by the reality-bending mineral manatite become the first knights of a new galactic order, protecting families and worlds that conventional armies cannot save.

## Premise

Manatite is simultaneously currency, technology, and magic. Those deeply attuned to it can accomplish wonders, but every wish, invention, and transformation creates responsibility. The Manatite Princess recruits rare protectors whose contact with the mineral has shaped both their abilities and their character. Web Shooter contributes stealth, engineering, and nonlethal ingenuity. ROOF-AR contributes overwhelming strength, barbarian courage, and fierce loyalty. The Princess contributes military leadership, spiritual balance, and the deepest known mastery of manatite.

## Tone

Earnest cosmic superhero adventure with intimate grief beneath spectacular science-fantasy imagery. The heroes are powerful but vulnerable, suspicious but compassionate, and defined by chosen responsibility rather than destiny.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic panels with documentary angles and believable on-location light.
- Blue-violet manatite glow with internal star-like points and silver mineral veins.
- Red and cobalt-blue accents visually connect the three knights despite their different cultures.
- Web Shooter scenes favor cramped industrial corridors, ceilings, negative space, and patient compositions.
- ROOF-AR scenes favor smoky iron spaces, rough textures, low angles, and kinetic framing.
- Princess scenes favor white stone, crystalline light, centered compositions, and quiet symmetry.

## Continuity Notes

- The first order consists of Web Shooter, ROOF-AR, and the Manatite Princess.
- Their bond is chosen, not foretold. Shared loss gives them empathy, but trust must continue to be earned.
- Manatite powder can grant wishes when scattered with an attuned person's breath. Greater alterations require more mineral.
- Web Shooter remains physically mortal; his advantage is preparation, stealth, and invention.
- ROOF-AR distrusts humanlike civilizations because humans abandoned him, but he fiercely values chosen family.
- The Princess is both heir and general. Her serenity is discipline, not passivity.
