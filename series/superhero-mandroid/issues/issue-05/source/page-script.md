# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Signal the portal standoff, Aurelia's sorrow, and the apocalyptic power she does not want.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image.
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid stands in a cracked city intersection at night, red suit and yellow cape lit by green portal light, one oversized green rocket fist wound back but not fired. Aurelia steps from the swirling portal in a white hooded dress, blonde hair visible, green diamond in her forehead, tears on her face, green mystical flames beginning around her hands. The portal silhouettes demonic shapes behind her without showing her parents.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "The Birthday Prophecy".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Waiting At The Portal

- Story role: Establish the unusual beat that Mandroid arrives before the threat and has to wait.
- Layout: Five panels moving from portal alarm to tense anticipation.
- Caption boxes: "Usually, Mandroid arrived after the monster." "This time, the portal opened first." "He stood ready in the green light." "Whatever came through would meet a rocket fist." "Then a human silhouette appeared."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a city street portal swirling open before any creature exits. Panel 2 shows Mandroid landing near emergency barriers and wet pavement. Panel 3 close on his green mechanical rocket fist winding back with thruster vents glowing. Panel 4 shows his suspicious face watching the portal. Panel 5 shows a slim hooded silhouette forming inside the portal light.

## Page 3: Not A Monster

- Story role: Subvert Mandroid's expectation with Aurelia's vulnerable arrival.
- Layout: Five panels with clear emotional contrast.
- Caption boxes: "The figure was not a demon brute." "She was young, human-looking, and crying." "A white hood framed blonde hair and a green diamond." "Mandroid kept his fist raised." "His duty said trap. Her tears said person."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Aurelia stepping out of the portal in a white hooded dress. Panel 2 close on her tear-streaked face, blonde hair, green eyes, and emerald diamond in her forehead. Panel 3 shows Mandroid stunned, rocket fist still cocked. Panel 4 shows Aurelia looking down, hands trembling. Panel 5 shows Mandroid lowering his stance, uncertain but attentive.

## Page 4: Birthday Tears

- Story role: Let Mandroid choose compassion and introduce the birthday confusion.
- Layout: Six panels with calm body language and short caption-dialogue.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid hated seeing anyone cry." "He knelt and asked what was wrong." "She said today was her eighteenth birthday." "That confused him more than the portal." "On Earth, birthdays meant celebration." "He wished her a happy birthday anyway."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid kneeling so his large green fists rest near the pavement instead of aiming at Aurelia. Panel 2 shows Aurelia wiping tears beneath the white hood. Panel 3 shows Mandroid's face softening with confusion. Panel 4 shows emergency lights and green portal glow around them. Panel 5 shows Aurelia beginning to scowl through tears. Panel 6 shows Mandroid cautiously offering kindness with open posture.

## Page 5: The Prophecy Wakes

- Story role: Reveal Aurelia's inherited apocalyptic mission and the scale of her power.
- Layout: Five large panels for readable lore and escalation.
- Caption boxes: "There was nothing happy about this birthday." "At eighteen, she had to fulfill her father's wishes." "The ground cracked as green light filled her eyes." "Mystic fire rose from her hands." "As the daughter of Satan and Lilith, she sadly declared the end of this world."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Aurelia scowling with tears still visible. Panel 2 shows the green forehead diamond flaring. Panel 3 shows pavement splitting under her feet, green light shining from cracks. Panel 4 shows her levitating as green flames wrap around both hands. Panel 5 wide shot shows Mandroid bracing under the portal glow while Aurelia hovers, powerful but sorrowful.

## Page 6: Not Human Either

- Story role: Show Mandroid defending himself while arguing that origin does not decide identity.
- Layout: Five panels balancing green fire shockwaves and shielded conversation.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid heard remorse under the power." "He had been built to smite her." "Instead, he said eighteen meant choosing an adult life." "She screamed that she was not from Earth." "Behind his shield, Mandroid answered: neither am I."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 close on Aurelia's glowing green eyes still leaking tears. Panel 2 shows green fire shockwaves rippling outward, bending signs and cracking asphalt. Panel 3 shows Mandroid raising the cyan-green hex-grid holo shield from his wrist. Panel 4 shows flames splashing against the shield while he holds steady. Panel 5 shows his oversized green rocket fists visible beside the shield, proving his own nonhuman body.

## Page 7: Another Path

- Story role: Let Aurelia admit she does not want to be her parents' weapon.
- Layout: Six panels with the action draining into confession.
- Caption boxes: "She said Earth would never accept her." "Mandroid raised his rocket fists without firing them." "Humanity accepted him as a hero." "The green fire faded." "Aurelia fell to her knees." "She had been raised as a weapon, but she had never wanted ruin."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Aurelia hovering lower, fear replacing anger. Panel 2 shows Mandroid lowering the holo shield but keeping his fists open and nonthreatening. Panel 3 shows green flames shrinking around Aurelia's hands. Panel 4 shows her collapsing to her knees on cracked pavement. Panel 5 close on her eyes returning from glowing green to normal tearful green. Panel 6 shows Mandroid kneeling across from her, patient and hopeful.

## Page 8: The Hero She Could Be

- Story role: Resolve the conflict with Aurelia choosing a heroic future.
- Layout: Five panels ending in an upward, hopeful final image.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid had shown her another path." "The portal began to close." "Aurelia thanked him through the last tears." "She rose into the sky and made a new vow." "One day, she would become the hero he saw in her."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Aurelia standing, small smile through tears, the green diamond calm. Panel 2 shows the portal folding shut behind her, demonic silhouettes disappearing. Panel 3 shows Mandroid looking up, awed rather than triumphant. Panel 4 shows Aurelia flying upward on green mystical light, white dress and hood trailing. Panel 5 final wide shot shows her as a bright green-white figure above the city skyline while Mandroid watches from the cracked street.
