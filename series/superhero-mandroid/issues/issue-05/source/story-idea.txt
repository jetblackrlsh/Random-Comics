Superhero Mandroid reaches a portal before the demon arrives and waits with his rocket fist wound back. Instead of a monster, a crying young woman in a white hooded dress steps through. She has blonde hair and a green diamond embedded in her forehead. Mandroid suspects a trick, but kneels to ask what is wrong.

The woman says it is her eighteenth birthday, which confuses Mandroid because birthdays on Earth are usually celebrations. She angrily explains that there is nothing happy about the day because she must fulfill her father's wishes and the prophecy attached to her birth. Her eyes glow green, the ground cracks, and green mystical flames gather in her hands as she sadly declares that as the daughter of Satan and Lilith she must begin the end of the world.

Mandroid hears the remorse in her voice and sees that she is still crying. He knows he was built to smite her, but he also knows this is not who she wants to be. He tells her that on Earth, turning eighteen means choosing how to live as an adult. She screams that she is not from Earth and not human, while uncontrolled green fire lashes out. Mandroid shields himself and answers that he is not human either, yet humanity accepts him as its hero.

The woman collapses, admitting she was raised to be her parents' weapon but never wanted that life. Mandroid's compassion shows her another path. As the portal closes, she flies into the sky and swears she will one day become the hero he saw in her.
