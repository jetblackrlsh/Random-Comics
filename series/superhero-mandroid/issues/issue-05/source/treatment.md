# Treatment

## Title

The Birthday Prophecy

## Issue Number

5

## Logline

Superhero Mandroid prepares to punch whatever emerges from a portal, but Aurelia, the crying daughter of Satan and Lilith, forces him to decide whether his duty is to smite a threat or help someone reject the prophecy built around her.

## Central Conflict

Mandroid must survive Aurelia's uncontrolled mystical power long enough to convince her that being born for ruin does not mean she has to choose ruin.

## Theme

Adulthood is not only inheritance; it is the first real chance to choose what kind of person to become.

## Ending

Aurelia rejects the apocalyptic prophecy her parents raised her to fulfill. The portal closes behind her, she rises into the sky, and she promises to become the hero Mandroid believed she could be.

## Emotional Arc

Mandroid begins suspicious and combat-ready because portals usually mean monsters. He becomes disarmed by Aurelia's grief, then frightened by the scale of her power, but he listens closely enough to understand that she is not attacking from malice. Aurelia begins resigned to an inherited role she hates, erupts in grief and power when challenged, then accepts that she can choose a different adult life. The issue ends with Mandroid awed rather than victorious.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Mandroid waiting at the portal, Aurelia's tearful arrival, birthday confusion, the prophecy awakening, shielded conversation through green fire, Aurelia's collapse and confession, and her promise to become a hero.
