Superhero Mandroid goes on a series of missions fighting giant robots, giant scorpions, massive drilling machines, and grotesque alien fish men invading through purple portals. On each of these missions, he finds himself in deep contemplation about his greater purpose. He was built to fight off these portal invaders and to protect the innocent. He loves making people smile and keeping people safe. And yet he used to wish that one day the portals would stop opening and that the world would forever be in peace.

But now he knows that he is a character in a comic book. If the portals were to ever stop appearing, his entire reality would be erased like a sketch on a page. He does not know how to feel about this realization. The one thing he thought he wanted, the portals to finally stop coming, he now knows he never wants. He at one point in time thought that when all the portals were gone that he and Doctor Dawes would just relax together and watch TV all day.

Those imaginings used to bring him a sense of comfort. They were a long term goal for him to aspire to achieve. A retirement plan for the world's greatest hero. Simple but peaceful. But he knows now that these daydreams are just fantasies.

He will never rest even after Doctor Dawes is long dead. Even after civilization shifts and changes toward an unimaginable future. Even if his artificial hair grays, his artificial skin wrinkles, and his rocket fists start to rust: He will continue to fight whatever monster he needs to in order to keep people safe. He and everyone he knows might be fictional, but that does not matter. A smile is real even if it is drawn.

He knows deep down inside that he is not just saving his world. There is someone looking down at him right now. Someone who is looking for escape, excitement, and joy. He smiles knowing that he is helping them smile with every flip of the page. That is what it means to be a real hero in a fictional world.
