# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue is a mainline Superhero Mandroid story after Issue #15, where Mandroid knows his reality is a living comic sustained by stories, portals, and the reader turning pages.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise an action-heavy but reflective issue about Mandroid accepting his heroic purpose inside a fictional world.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition with a subtle comic-page edge curling at one corner.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "A Smile Is Real". Smaller bottom caption: "A hero in a fictional world still saves someone."
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid hovers above Mandroid's City using only his oversized green rocket fists for flight, yellow cape whipping, red suit and yellow `SHM` insignia clear. Around him, several purple portals open at once: a giant robot arm reaches from one, a scorpion claw from another, a massive drill from a third, and wet fish-men silhouettes from a fourth. Mandroid looks toward the reader with a calm, complicated smile while his cyan-green holo shield glows in one hand.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The Work He Was Built For

- Story role: Establish Mandroid's ongoing mission and the contradiction created by Issue #15.
- Layout: Four large action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Superhero Mandroid was built for the moment a purple portal opened." "A giant robot stepped into the city, and he answered with the rocket fists Dr. Dawes had given him." "He loved the sound of people cheering when the danger fell." "He used to dream that one day the portals would stop forever."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a purple portal opening between city towers, reflected in glass windows and wet pavement. Panel 2 shows a towering alien robot stomping into the street while civilians flee. Panel 3 shows Mandroid flying by green rocket-fist exhaust only, firing one oversized mechanical fist into the robot's chest. Panel 4 shows the robot collapsing away from civilians as people smile and wave from behind emergency barriers; Mandroid looks proud but thoughtful.

## Page 3: The Wish That Became A Fear

- Story role: Show the scorpion mission and reveal that the old wish for peace now scares him.
- Layout: Four roomy panels, mixing rescue action with introspection.
- Caption boxes: "A giant scorpion came next, all black armor and violet venom." "Mandroid saved the family beneath its claws before the stinger found them." "Once, this victory would have felt like one step closer to peace." "Now he knew that a world with no portals might vanish like a sketch rubbed from a page."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a bus-sized scorpion erupting from a purple portal at an elevated train station. Panel 2 shows its serrated claws threatening a trapped family near a cracked support column. Panel 3 shows Mandroid throwing his cyan-green holo shield between the claw and the civilians while a rocket fist hooks the stinger aside. Panel 4 shows a subtle self-aware image: the defeated scorpion and station edge begin to resemble pencil sketch lines at the panel border while Mandroid stares at the fading edge with worry.

## Page 4: The Retirement That Cannot Happen

- Story role: Contrast a massive drilling-machine fight with Mandroid's old peaceful fantasy with Dawes.
- Layout: Five panels with one large central daydream panel.
- Caption boxes: "A drilling machine tore up through the avenue like the planet was something to mine." "Mandroid stopped it because innocent people needed the street beneath their feet." "But his mind drifted to the old promise he kept for himself." "When the last portal closed, he and Dr. Dawes would sit on the couch and watch television all day." "That future had once comforted him. Now it felt like a room drawn on paper."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a skyscraper-scale alien drilling machine bursting through asphalt, throwing cars and concrete aside without gore. Panel 2 shows Mandroid bracing against the rotating drill with his holo shield sparking. Panel 3, the largest panel, shows a warm quiet daydream: Mandroid and elderly Dr. Elias Dawes sit on a simple couch in the hidden lab lounge watching TV, Mandroid's giant green fists resting carefully on his knees, both peaceful. Panel 4 shows the imagined TV room edges turning into pencil lines and blank white space. Panel 5 returns to reality as Mandroid punches the drilling machine's control core, expression pained.

## Page 5: Longer Than Any Lifetime

- Story role: Push Mandroid into the frightening future implied by endless portals.
- Layout: Four large panels with time-shift imagery.
- Caption boxes: "Dr. Dawes was human. One day, even his brilliant hands would be gone." "Civilization would change into shapes Mandroid could not imagine." "His artificial hair might gray. His synthetic skin might wrinkle. His rocket fists might rust." "And still, somewhere, a portal would open."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid in the hidden lab watching Dr. Dawes at a workbench, the older inventor's hand resting affectionately on a diagnostic console. Panel 2 shows a far-future city skyline outside, stranger and taller, with unfamiliar transit lights and vertical gardens, while Mandroid stands alone. Panel 3 shows aged Mandroid: grayer artificial hair, subtle wrinkles in white synthetic skin, faded red suit, frayed yellow cape, and rust-specked green rocket fists. Panel 4 shows old Mandroid turning toward a fresh purple portal opening in the futuristic city, still ready despite worn parts.

## Page 6: Monsters Still Come

- Story role: Return to present action with grotesque fish men and show Mandroid choosing duty without bitterness.
- Layout: Six action-forward panels with compact captions.
- Caption boxes: "The next portal smelled of salt, rot, and another world." "Grotesque fish men poured into the floodlit street." "Mandroid did not know whether the fight was written for him or by him." "He only knew the people behind him were afraid." "His holo shield rose." "His rocket fists answered."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a wet purple portal opening beside a rain-slick underpass. Panel 2 shows fish-men invaders crawling out: gray-green scales, bulging eyes, gills, webbed claws, finned crests, barnacle armor, and dripping portal slime. Panel 3 shows Mandroid landing between them and a crowd of civilians, yellow cape wet and heavy. Panel 4 shows his cyan-green holo shield blocking tridents or claws. Panel 5 shows rocket fists launching with bright green exhaust, knocking fish-men back toward the portal without gore. Panel 6 shows Mandroid pushing the last invader through the purple light, expression steady.

## Page 7: The Drawn Smile

- Story role: Let Mandroid articulate the issue's moral through saved civilians and the unseen reader.
- Layout: Five panels, quieter after the fight.
- Caption boxes: "When the portal closed, the street did not feel less real." "The rescued child clung to her mother with a smile that no eraser could make meaningless." "Mandroid understood then that he was not only saving his world." "Someone was looking down at him, looking for escape, excitement, and joy." "A smile was real even if it was drawn."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the portal shrinking away as rain steams off damaged pavement. Panel 2 shows a rescued child and parent smiling through tears while emergency lights color the scene. Panel 3 shows Mandroid kneeling to return a dropped toy, his huge green fist careful and gentle. Panel 4 shows a subtle upward view from Mandroid's perspective: the panel border and page edge are visible, with a vague warm reader-shaped presence beyond it, not a literal monster or god. Panel 5 shows Mandroid smiling softly as green rocket-fist light reflects in his eyes.

## Page 8: Every Flip Of The Page

- Story role: Resolve Mandroid's purpose and give the issue an emotionally complete ending.
- Layout: Five panels ending on a direct heroic image.
- Caption boxes: "He no longer asked for a final portal." "He would fight after every easy dream was gone." "He would fight after time changed his city, his body, and every quiet plan he had made." "Because every flip of the page could give someone courage, laughter, or one more breath of joy." "That is what it means to be a real hero in a fictional world."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid standing among repaired streets at dawn, the city beginning to move again. Panel 2 shows faint montage ghosts around him: the robot, scorpion, drill, and fish-men portals fading like completed panels. Panel 3 shows an echo of old Mandroid and young Mandroid overlapping, both facing the same horizon. Panel 4 shows civilians smiling and waving from windows, sidewalks, and emergency barriers. Panel 5 final panel shows Superhero Mandroid launching upward on green rocket-fist exhaust only, red suit, yellow cape, yellow `SHM`, and cyan holo shield visible, smiling directly toward the reader as a new purple portal opens in the sky.
