# Character Bible

## Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin on his face, slicked-back brown artificial hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and wrist thruster vents. When flying, bright green-white exhaust comes only from the rocket-fist vents; his boots stay plain and inactive.
- Issue continuity: Mandroid knows from Issue #15 that his world is a living comic sustained by the Sketchmaker's imagination. He still loves making people smile and keeping people safe, but he is grieving the retirement fantasy he once imagined. Across this issue he changes from conflicted duty to a mature acceptance that fictional heroism can create real joy.

## Dr. Elias Dawes

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/inventor-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: older human man in his early 60s with light skin, gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, and a practical dark lab coat over a blue shirt. He often wears a key card on a lanyard near the lab.
- Issue continuity: Dawes appears in Mandroid's imagined peaceful future, relaxing on a couch with him after the portals stop. The image is tender but impossible because Mandroid now knows that a story without conflict could fade into erasure.

## The Sketchmaker

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/sketchmaker-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: adult male stick-figure person rendered as a living pencil sketch inside photoreal city environments, with a round simple head, black graphite line body, expressive dot eyes, and loose graphite smudges drifting from him when imagination falters.
- Issue continuity: The Sketchmaker does not appear as an active participant. His revelation from Issue #15 shapes Mandroid's internal conflict.

## One-Off Portal Threats

- Giant portal robot: towering industrial alien robot with scratched black steel armor, purple portal glow in its seams, massive hydraulic arms, and cratered footfalls. One-off threat for this issue.
- Giant portal scorpion: bus-sized armored scorpion with glossy black chitin, violet glowing stinger, serrated claws, and sand-scored plates. One-off threat for this issue.
- Massive drilling machine: skyscraper-scale interdimensional mining machine with rotating drill heads, bronze-black alien plating, violet exhaust, and conveyor teeth tearing pavement. One-off threat for this issue.
- Alien fish men: grotesque amphibious humanoid invaders with wet gray-green scales, bulging eyes, gill slits, finned crests, webbed claws, barnacle-like armor, and purple portal slime. One-off threat swarm for this issue.

## Issue-Specific Visual Motifs

- Purple portals casting violet light across real city streets, glass towers, smoke, and rain-wet pavement.
- Mandroid acting decisively in action panels while caption boxes reveal private contemplation.
- Comic-page self-awareness shown through subtle panel borders, pencil sketch edges, a giant unseen page-shadow, and final eye contact toward the reader, without breaking the realistic photo-comic look.
- Aging android imagery: graying artificial hair, wrinkled synthetic facial skin, scuffed red suit, faded yellow cape, and rusty green rocket fists.
