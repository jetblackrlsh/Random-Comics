# Character Bible

## Recurring Characters

### Paul Teethings, Protector Paul

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/paul-teethings-possessed-exorcist-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: gaunt light-skinned man in his late 30s with hollow brown eyes, short receding dark hair, trimmed beard, torn black priest clothing with white collar, charcoal-black cracked skin patches, ember-red throat and eyes, ragged demon wings, black clawed fingers, smoke-stained collar, worn wooden rosary, and brimstone glow around his mouth when breathing hellfire.
- Issue continuity: Paul flies beside the passenger jet as its guardian, burns Crossmarked jet packs with fire breath, then loses control when the demon inside him tries to escape into an Animax android body. After the scanner incident, Paul no longer hears the demon inside his head.

### Dr. Leona Vale, Transhuman Organizer

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/leona-vale-transhuman-organizer-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: Black woman in her early 40s with warm brown skin, close-cropped silver hair, calm dark eyes, angular cheekbones, tailored charcoal jacket, matte white smart-fabric shirt, copper-and-blue neural interface filaments at the temples, transparent augmented-reality lenses, slim carbon-fiber forearm braces, and a small glowing AI companion device clipped near her collarbone.
- Issue continuity: Leona travels to Bulgaria for the conference, studies digital immortality as an advocate and skeptic, recognizes the demon's attempted escape, and risks herself by stepping onto the stage to talk Paul's human side back into control.

### Dr. Elias Dawes

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/inventor-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: older light-skinned man in his early 60s with gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, practical dark lab coat over a blue shirt, and a key card lanyard.
- Issue continuity: Dawes is invited as a conference guest speaker. He is fascinated by Animax android bodies as engineering, then helps Doctor Moreau disable the scanner terminal during Paul's psychic struggle.

### Crossmarked Paramilitary Cell

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/crossmarked-paramilitary-cell-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: coordinated paramilitary attackers in matte black armor and heavy helmets with opaque visors, rough white cross graffiti, scripture-like scratches, hand-painted crusader symbols, reinforced gloves, tactical boots, compact jet packs, and angular laser rifles.
- Issue continuity: The cell escalates from batons to airborne attacks against transhuman leaders, trying to destroy the passenger flight before the Bulgaria conference.

### Biter / Petar Marinoff

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/biter-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: human-sized but powerfully built beetle-android body with glossy black chitinous armor, burnt-orange chest and abdominal segments, orange compound-lens eyes, segmented mechanical faceplate with small mandible structures, two black antennae curving back from the helmet, translucent amber beetle wings, compact flight thrusters, clawed black gloves and boots.
- Issue continuity: Biter arrives over the Black Sea and disables Crossmarked jet packs with a wide taser pulse. He is a fan of Protector Paul and is publicly introduced as Project Animax's successful Bulgarian pilot reborn in a beetle android body.

## New Recurring Elements

### Doctor Moreau

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/doctor-moreau-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: Bulgarian transhumanist scientist in his late 50s with pale skin, swept-back iron-gray hair, narrow hazel eyes, sharp cheekbones, clean-shaven face, tailored graphite suit under a white lab coat with amber circuit embroidery, black nitrile gloves, discreet neural-interface earpiece, and proud theatrical posture.
- Issue continuity: Head of Project Animax. He presents Biter as proof that animal-ability android bodies can preserve human minds, then panics when his scanner completes the demon upload before he can shut it down.

### Goat-Bot

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/goat-bot-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: humanoid goat android with black-and-bronze cybernetic armor, backward-curving metal horns, goat-like angular skull face with ember-red sensor eyes, cloven steel hooves, digitigrade legs, cable-like beard, segmented armored torso, clawed mechanical hands, and sulfur-red diagnostic light leaking through chest seams.
- Issue continuity: Goat-Bot is the escaped android body carrying the demon brain scan pulled from Protector Paul. It has Paul's former demonic malice without Paul's conscience and disappears before anyone can stop it.

## Issue-Specific Visual Motifs

- Flight attack: high-altitude passenger jet over the Black Sea, Protector Paul's ragged wings and fire breath, Crossmarked jet packs trailing smoke, Biter's amber wings cutting through clouds, electric taser arcs disabling attackers without gore.
- Project Animax: optimistic medical technology mixed with unsettling animal android bodies, amber-blue diagnostics, public stage confidence turning into panic.
- Possession crisis: Paul's ember eyes, scanner display implying two minds, red demonic light flowing toward the goat android pod, Vale's calm human presence cutting through the chaos.
