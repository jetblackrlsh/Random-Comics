# Page Script

All pages must be full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. All readable story text, title text, captions, and any short speech must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only. Do not use speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, blank text boxes, logos, watermarks, or later text overlays.

## Page 1: Cover

Story role: Sell the central crisis: Project Animax's scanner frees the demon from Protector Paul into Goat-Bot while Biter and Leona try to stop it.

Cover composition: Dramatic conference-stage cover with the Project Animax Scanner blazing amber-blue at center. Protector Paul, half-human and half-demonic, braces against the scanner while red demonic energy streams toward a humanoid goat android silhouette. Biter lunges through fire sparks from one side with amber wings open. Dr. Leona Vale reaches toward Paul from the audience edge. Doctor Moreau and Dr. Dawes are small figures at the terminal. Keep it photoreal and readable, not poster-painted.

Caption/title text to generate inside page art: title text "THE DEMON IN THE SCANNER"; small caption box "A second body can save a life, or set a demon free."

Continuity: Biter must match `biter-character.png`; Paul must match `paul-teethings-possessed-exorcist-character.png`; scanner should match `project-animax-scanner-key-item.png`; Goat-Bot silhouette should match `goat-bot-character.png`.
