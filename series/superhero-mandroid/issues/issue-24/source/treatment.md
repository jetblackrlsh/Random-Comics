# Treatment

## Title

The Demon in the Scanner

## Issue Number

24

## Continuity Label

Mainline. Protector Paul, Dr. Leona Vale, Dr. Elias Dawes, and the Crossmarked Paramilitary Cell return from earlier Superhero Mandroid issues. Biter crosses into this series using the provided reference image and his prior Project Animax background from Superhero Action Comics continuity.

## Logline

On a guarded flight to a Bulgarian transhumanism conference, Protector Paul and Biter stop a Crossmarked jet-pack attack, only for Project Animax's mind-transfer scanner to offer Paul's demon the escape it has been waiting for.

## Central Conflict

Protector Paul wants to serve as a guardian for transhumans without surrendering to the demon that powers him. Project Animax promises bodies beyond death, which inspires Dawes, fascinates Vale, and gives the demon a route out of Paul's soul. Biter believes Project Animax can save lives because it saved his own mind, but he must confront the danger of a machine that can separate and weaponize a mind before anyone understands the consequences.

## Theme

Immortality without consent is not liberation. A second body can be a rescue, a prison, or an escape route for something that should have stayed contained.

## Ending

Dawes and Moreau disable the scanner too late. Paul regains himself and is finally free from the demon's whisper, but the scanned demon has already fled in a humanoid-goat android body. Biter reboots after being burned, and Paul promises that the two of them will hunt down Goat-Bot together.

## Emotional Arc

Paul starts the issue trying to prove that the frightening power inside him can protect transhumans. Biter's admiration makes him feel unexpectedly seen, but the Project Animax scanner turns his worst fear into a public crisis. Vale's voice helps Paul remember who he is while the demon tries to abandon him. He ends shaken but clearer: the demon is no longer inside his head, yet the cost of freedom is a new enemy with his worst impulses.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, flight setup, aerial attack, Biter rescue and Bulgaria landing, Project Animax presentation, demon upload crisis, Vale's intervention and scanner shutdown, and the Goat-Bot escape aftermath.
