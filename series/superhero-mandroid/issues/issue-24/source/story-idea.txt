Create the next issue of Superhero Mandroid based on the following story idea:

Doctor Leona Vale travels to Bulgaria for a transhumanism conference. Doctor Dawes is onboard the same flight, as he has been invited as a guest speaker. Flying alongside the plane to protect it from anti-transhumanist threats is Protector Paul. The plane is attacked mid-flight by the Crossmarked Paramilitary Cell, now armed with jet packs and laser rifles. Protector Paul burns several jet packs with fire breath, causing attackers to crash into the water below, but there are too many for him to face alone.

Biter arrives and fires a taser pulse that disables the remaining jet packs. The plane lands safely in Bulgaria, where Protector Paul and Biter introduce themselves. Biter already knows Protector Paul and admires his work. Protector Paul has never heard of Biter.

At the conference, Biter is presented on stage beside the head of Project Animax, Doctor Moreau. Project Animax transfers human minds into android bodies with animal-like abilities, pursuing digital immortality and superhuman enhancement. Biter was once a Bulgarian air force pilot named Petar Marinoff until he died in a fatal crash. His brain was recovered, scanned, and placed in a beetle android body so he could fly with his own wings and be protected by beetle armor. He has secretly helped people across Bulgaria during a black-ops testing round, and now Project Animax is going public.

Doctor Dawes is intrigued by the android bodies. Doctor Vale is intrigued by digital immortality. The demon inside Protector Paul is intrigued by the possibility of escape. Protector Paul's demon side takes control and charges the Project Animax scanner. The scanner recognizes two minds inside him. The possessed Paul tells it to ignore the human half and upload the demon half into a humanoid-goat android.

Doctor Vale realizes what is happening and tells Biter to stop Protector Paul before it is too late. The only way to stop the upload is to reach the scanner terminal guarded by possessed Protector Paul. Biter lunges forward, but Protector Paul hits him with fire breath and overheats his systems. Doctor Vale jumps onto the stage and talks to Paul, partially waking his human side and creating a psychic tug of war. While Paul is distracted, Doctor Dawes and Doctor Moreau sneak to the terminal and disable the scanner.

Protector Paul regains control and no longer hears the demon whispering inside his head. Dawes and Moreau panic because the upload had already completed. The Goat-Bot, carrying the demon brain scan, has escaped. Biter reboots, and Protector Paul promises him that together they will stop his runaway demon.
