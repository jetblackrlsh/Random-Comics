## Superhero Mandroid

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: human-looking adult android man with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.
- Page continuity: begins flying over the city with green-white rocket fist exhaust, is blasted by water, crashes onto a rooftop, sparks and spasms as water leaks from his rocket fists, raises a cyan-green holo shield, and ends damp, scuffed, and relieved after Hydrohero shuts down.

## The Inventor

- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/inventor-character.png`

## Dr. Vera Volta

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rival-inventor-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: middle-aged woman in her late 40s with light olive skin, sharp cheekbones, intense dark eyes, chin-length black hair with a silver streak, angular black glasses, fitted teal waterproof lab coat over a graphite jumpsuit, surrounded by water-pressure gauges and robotics equipment.
- Page continuity: builds Hydrohero in her wet robotics workshop, appears by holo message ordering Hydrohero to finish Mandroid, and ends by slamming her fist into a computer terminal while vowing revenge.

## Hydrohero Womandroid

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-womandroid-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: human-looking adult woman android with waterproof pearl-blue synthetic skin, short slick silver-blue hair, bright aqua eyes, athletic build, streamlined blue-and-white armored bodysuit, translucent chest water tank with visible swirling water, chrome shoulder turbines, wrist nozzles, and calf jet ports spraying controlled water streams.
- Page continuity: begins pristine and triumphant, fires a high-pressure water beam that knocks Mandroid from the sky, lands on a rooftop using water jets, attacks with water whips and a torrential water blast, visibly weakens as her chest tank empties, then collapses and shuts down furious.
