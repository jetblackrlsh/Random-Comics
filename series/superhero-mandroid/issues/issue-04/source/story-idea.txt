The inventor's rival grows jealous of Superhero Mandroid's success and builds Hydrohero Womandroid, a waterproof hydroelectric android powered by water and armed with pressurized water attacks. Hydrohero shoots Superhero Mandroid out of the sky, damages his rocket fists with water, and tries to replace him as the city's defender. Mandroid argues that they could work together, but the rival orders Hydrohero to finish him. During the duel, Mandroid realizes Hydrohero is draining her own power source with every attack. Hydrohero shuts down when her chest tank runs empty, and the rival inventor swears revenge.
