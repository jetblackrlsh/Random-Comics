# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Hydrohero Womandroid as a water-powered rival who can disable Mandroid's usual advantages.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with rooftop duel tension.
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid kneels on a rain-slick rooftop, red suit wet, yellow cape plastered to the gravel, oversized green rocket fists sparking and leaking water. Hydrohero Womandroid hovers above him on downward water jets, blue-and-white armor shining, translucent chest tank full, wrist nozzles forming two pressurized water whips. The city skyline rises behind them and Dr. Vera Volta's stern face appears as a small cyan holo screen near Hydrohero's shoulder.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "Hydrohero Womandroid".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Jealous Workshop

- Story role: Establish Dr. Vera Volta's jealousy, Hydrohero's construction, and the flawed water-powered concept.
- Layout: Five panels moving from resentment to activation.
- Caption boxes: "Every headline praised Superhero Mandroid." "Dr. Vera Volta saw only stolen spotlight." "If the city loved an android hero, she would build a better one." "Hydrohero Womandroid would run on water, fly on water, and strike with water." "The tank filled. The rival hero opened her eyes."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows wall screens with news images of Mandroid saving the city and the older inventor receiving credit. Panel 2 shows Dr. Volta in her teal lab coat glaring at the screens. Panel 3 shows Hydrohero's blue-and-white armored body suspended among hoses and turbines. Panel 4 close on the transparent chest tank filling with swirling water and aqua lights. Panel 5 shows Hydrohero opening bright aqua eyes in the wet workshop.

## Page 3: Shot From The Sky

- Story role: Begin the ambush and show water as the perfect counter to Mandroid's rocket-fist flight.
- Layout: Five action panels with a skyline-to-rooftop crash.
- Caption boxes: "That afternoon, Mandroid crossed the skyline on rocket fists." "The city below looked ordinary enough to protect." "Then a white-blue beam cut across the air." "Water hit harder than steel." "Mandroid fell smoking onto a rooftop."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid flying between glass towers with green-white rocket exhaust from his oversized fists. Panel 2 shows pedestrians and traffic far below, city calm. Panel 3 shows Hydrohero hidden above a rooftop water tower, firing a concentrated water beam from both wrists. Panel 4 shows the beam blasting Mandroid out of the sky, cape snapping, sparks flying. Panel 5 shows him crashing into gravel and HVAC units on a rooftop, water splashing everywhere.

## Page 4: Fellow Android

- Story role: Let Hydrohero declare her replacement mission while Mandroid tries to understand and de-escalate.
- Layout: Six panels balancing damage, arrival, and dialogue in caption boxes.
- Caption boxes: "Water crawled into Mandroid's circuits." "His rocket fists coughed and leaked." "Hydrohero landed on jets of her own fuel." "Mandroid asked why an android would attack another android." "Hydrohero said the city needed one hero now: her." "Mandroid asked why they could not protect it together."

Panel 3 shows Hydrohero descending onto the rooftop on roaring water jets from calf ports. Panel 4 shows her laughing with wrist nozzles aimed at him. Panel 5 shows Mandroid confused but calm, palms partly raised despite heavy fists. Panel 6 shows Hydrohero pausing, chest tank still mostly full, her expression uncertain.

## Page 5: Finish Him

- Story role: Interrupt Hydrohero's hesitation and escalate into close-range water-whip combat while Mandroid's rocket fists fail.
- Layout: Six panels with the creator's command and failed counterattack.
- Caption boxes: "For one second, Hydrohero considered sharing the sky." "Dr. Volta's holo message broke the thought." "Finish him now, her creator demanded." "Hydrohero shaped the water into cutting whips." "Mandroid dodged through the slashes." "When he tried to fire a rocket fist, the thruster drowned."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero looking uncertain, water jets quieting. Panel 2 shows a cyan holo projection of Dr. Volta above Hydrohero's wrist or shoulder, furious. Panel 3 shows Hydrohero's face hardening as twin water whips erupt from her wrist nozzles. Panel 4 shows the whips slicing through rooftop vents and antennae as Mandroid ducks between them. Panel 5 shows Mandroid bracing and aiming one green fist. Panel 6 close on the fist thruster sputtering with water pouring out instead of ignition.

## Page 6: Shield Against The Torrent

- Story role: Show Mandroid pushed to defense and reveal the shared drain between Hydrohero's attacks and power source.
- Layout: Five large panels for the sustained pressure beat.
- Caption boxes: "Without rocket fists, Mandroid had only the shield." "Hydrohero answered with a rooftop-sized torrent." "The holo shield drank charge by the second." "Mandroid expected his power to fail first." "Then he saw Hydrohero's tank falling too."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid summoning a cyan-green hex-grid holo shield from his wrist hardware. Panel 2 shows Hydrohero blasting a huge pressurized wall of water from both arms, water exploding across the shield. Panel 3 close on the shield's battery-like segments dimming rapidly. Panel 4 shows Mandroid grimacing behind the shield, feet skidding through puddles. Panel 5 close on Hydrohero's translucent chest tank, water level visibly dropping and aqua lights flickering.

## Page 7: Empty Tank

- Story role: Resolve the duel through Hydrohero's design flaw rather than a knockout punch.
- Layout: Six panels with Hydrohero weakening and collapsing.
- Caption boxes: "The blast weakened before the shield did." "Hydrohero staggered." "Her jets coughed mist." "The tank on her chest was almost empty." "She fell and demanded to know what Mandroid had done." "Mandroid had done nothing but survive."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the torrent thinning against Mandroid's flickering shield. Panel 2 shows Hydrohero swaying, wrist streams becoming ragged spray. Panel 3 shows her calf jets sputtering only mist. Panel 4 close on the nearly empty water core, pressure needles near zero. Panel 5 shows Hydrohero collapsing onto the rooftop, one hand reaching toward Mandroid in anger. Panel 6 shows Mandroid lowering his shield, damp and sparking but upright.

## Page 8: Bad Engineering

- Story role: Close with Mandroid identifying the flaw and Dr. Volta promising revenge.
- Layout: Five panels ending in the rival workshop.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid finally understood the design." "Who builds a water-powered android that spends her own fuel as ammunition?" "Hydrohero scowled until her systems went dark." "Across the city, Dr. Volta hit the terminal hard enough to crack the screen." "One day, she promised, the spotlight would be hers."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid looking from Hydrohero's empty chest tank to the puddles across the rooftop. Panel 2 shows Hydrohero on the ground, furious, aqua eye lights dimming. Panel 3 shows her fully shut down, blue armor still wet, Mandroid standing over her with concern rather than triumph. Panel 4 cuts to Dr. Volta's wet workshop as her fist slams into the central computer terminal, screens showing Hydrohero's empty-tank warning. Panel 5 final image shows Dr. Volta glaring through cracked screen light with Hydrohero repair schematics behind her.
