# Treatment

## Title

Hydrohero Womandroid

## Issue Number

4

## Logline

Superhero Mandroid is ambushed by a rival android whose pressurized water attacks nearly disable him, until he notices that her power source and weapon supply are the same tank.

## Central Conflict

Superhero Mandroid must survive a rooftop duel against Hydrohero Womandroid while water shorts his rocket fists, drains his holo shield, and leaves him unable to use his usual strongest attack.

## Theme

A better hero is not the one built to steal attention; a better hero understands the cost of every power.

## Ending

Mandroid does not defeat Hydrohero with a final blow. He holds out long enough to understand her flaw: every water blast, whip, and jet spends the same water that keeps her alive. Hydrohero collapses with an empty chest tank, shuts down in anger, and Dr. Vera Volta swears revenge from her workshop.

## Emotional Arc

Mandroid begins the issue confident in flight over the city, then becomes physically vulnerable when water gets into his circuits and disables his rocket fists. He is confused by Hydrohero's hostility because she is another android who could protect people beside him. When she hesitates, he tries diplomacy, but her creator's command forces the duel onward. Mandroid moves from worry to tactical clarity as he sees Hydrohero weaken, ending with dry humor and a new rival threat unresolved.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, jealous construction, aerial ambush, rooftop confrontation, failed rocket fists, shield under water pressure, Hydrohero's power drain and shutdown, and Dr. Volta's revenge vow.
