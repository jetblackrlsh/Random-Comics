Todd Phillips was once a celebrated but secret cybersecurity engineer. He designed classified programs that helped protect the nation, but because his work was hidden, the public never knew he was a hero.

When AI coding agents became strong enough to do his job, his organization laid him off. With everyone using AI, Todd could not find new work in the field he had given his life to. News clips kept showing Superhero Mandroid being celebrated as a hero, but Todd saw only another machine stealing human glory.

Desperate for money and consumed by resentment, Todd decides to use his hacking skills to seize control of Superhero Mandroid and turn him into a puppet for theft. In the hidden lab, Superhero Mandroid is monitoring portal signatures when a sudden intrusion attacks his mechanical mind. His eyes turn red, and he collapses.

Dr. Elias Dawes activates the Lyoko-Tron-Matrix protocol, plugs a terminal cable into his ear, and transfers his mind's electrical signals into the lab system. Inside Superhero Mandroid's cyberspace grid, Mandroid fights multiplying computer virus monsters with rocket fists, but each destroyed monster splits into more attackers.

Dr. Dawes manifests beside him and explains that the hack can only be defeated permanently with digital weaponry. Mandroid remembers using his holo shield against the Pixel Demon in Issue #1, summons it in digital form, and rams through the swarm while Dawes summons a holosword. Together they delete the monsters and recover a stray data packet that identifies Todd Phillips as the attacker.

Authorities reach Todd's home too late. Todd is gone, carrying his tools and his hatred with him. He swears that one day the machines will pay for stealing his life, and Superhero Mandroid will be his.
