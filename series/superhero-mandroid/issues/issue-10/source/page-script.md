# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Todd's hack as an attack on Mandroid's mind and Dr. Dawes's emergency rescue.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with layered physical-lab and cyberspace imagery.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "The Puppet Signal".
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid kneels in the hidden lab with red glowing eyes, one oversized green mechanical fist gripping his head, yellow cape crumpled around him. Dr. Elias Dawes lunges toward a terminal with the black-and-silver mind-link cable in one hand. Behind Mandroid, the lab wall seems to tear open into a black-and-cyan cyberspace grid filled with red-black computer virus monsters. Todd Phillips appears as a cold blue monitor-lit silhouette in the upper corner, hunched over hacking screens.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The Forgotten Hero

- Story role: Establish Todd's service, secrecy, layoff, and resentment toward Mandroid.
- Layout: Four large panels inside Todd's apartment workroom and memory-like fragments.
- Caption boxes: "Todd Phillips had protected the nation from behind classified screens." "He was rich enough to be envied, but too secret to be thanked." "When AI coding agents learned his job, the organization forgot his name." "Every channel called Mandroid a hero, and Todd saw a machine wearing stolen glory."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows younger Todd at a secure government cybersecurity console, face lit by green code, national defense diagrams blurred enough to avoid readable secrets. Panel 2 shows present-day Todd's dusty commendations and expensive but lonely apartment objects. Panel 3 shows Todd receiving a layoff envelope beside monitors showing generic AI coding dashboards. Panel 4 shows present Todd in his cluttered home workstation glaring at news clips of Superhero Mandroid being celebrated by crowds.

## Page 3: The Intrusion

- Story role: Launch the hack and show Mandroid's body falling under Todd's control signal.
- Layout: Five panels cutting between Todd's home and Mandroid's hidden lab.
- Caption boxes: "No company wanted a human specialist where machines could write the defenses." "Todd stopped looking for work and started looking for a door into Mandroid." "In the hidden lab, Mandroid watched for portal signatures." "The attack did not come through a portal." "It arrived inside his mind."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Todd wiring hardware security keys, cracked laptops, and custom exploit devices at his home workstation. Panel 2 close on Todd's bitter face reflected in monitor code and a red target diagram of Mandroid. Panel 3 shows Superhero Mandroid in the hidden lab watching portal-monitoring screens, calm and alert. Panel 4 shows red corrupted data tendrils entering lab diagnostics from the screen side, no physical portal. Panel 5 shows Mandroid collapsing to his knees with red glowing eyes as Dr. Dawes rushes toward him.

## Page 4: Lyoko-Tron-Matrix

- Story role: Show Dr. Dawes taking the risk to enter Mandroid's mind.
- Layout: Five panels in the hidden lab with a transition into digital transfer.
- Caption boxes: "Dr. Dawes saw the red eyes and knew the body was not the battlefield." "He armed the only protocol dangerous enough to follow the signal." "The Lyoko-Tron-Matrix translated thought into current." "Dawes plugged the terminal cable into his own ear." "His mind crossed the wire before Mandroid's could be stolen."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Dr. Dawes kneeling beside Mandroid, alarmed by the red eyes and clenched green fists. Panel 2 shows Dawes slamming a palm onto a terminal labeled with cyan waveform lights rather than excessive readable text. Panel 3 close on the Lyoko-Tron-Matrix mind-link cable: black-and-silver braided cable, green LEDs, slim rounded ear plug. Panel 4 shows Dawes plugging the cable into his ear with a pained but determined expression. Panel 5 shows electrical blue-white signals streaming from Dawes through the terminal into a glowing outline of Mandroid's head.

## Page 5: Multiplying Monsters

- Story role: Put Mandroid in the cyberspace grid and reveal that ordinary force makes the hack worse.
- Layout: Five action panels inside the cyberspace grid.
- Caption boxes: "Inside Mandroid's mechanical mind, the lab became an endless grid of memory and code." "Todd's virus monsters swarmed every path." "Mandroid fired rocket fists by instinct." "Each shattered monster became two more bodies." "The harder he fought like a machine, the faster the hack spread."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid's digital avatar standing on a black-and-cyan grid beneath floating memory towers and green circuit rivers. Panel 2 shows red-black computer virus monsters flooding toward him from cracked red intrusion lines. Panel 3 shows Mandroid firing his oversized green rocket fists through the monsters with bright green exhaust trails. Panel 4 shows destroyed monsters splitting into red code fragments that reform into twice as many creatures. Panel 5 shows Mandroid surrounded, his yellow cape whipping in digital wind, red corruption crawling toward his `SHM` chest insignia.

## Page 6: Digital Weapons

- Story role: Bring Dr. Dawes into the grid and define the rule that wins the fight.
- Layout: Four large panels for clarity and exposition.
- Caption boxes: "Dr. Dawes arrived as a signal shaped like a man." "He told Mandroid the truth: this was a hack, not a demon portal." "Digital beings could be knocked apart by force, but deleted only by digital weaponry." "Mandroid remembered the holo shield that had cut a digital demon apart."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Dr. Dawes manifesting beside Mandroid as a translucent blue-white digital form wearing the same glasses and lab coat silhouette. Panel 2 shows Dawes pointing to red intrusion cracks that lead back toward Todd's hidden signal. Panel 3 shows virus monsters reforming behind them while Dawes summons a cyan-green holosword, its blade made of hardlight. Panel 4 shows Mandroid's wrist projector flaring as the rechargeable cyan-green holo shield forms in the grid, curved with a hex surface and bright rim.

## Page 7: Shield And Sword

- Story role: Show Mandroid and Dawes defeating the hack and recovering Todd's identity.
- Layout: Six action panels, fast but readable.
- Caption boxes: "This time, Mandroid did not punch the swarm." "He drove the holo shield through it." "Dawes cut the fragments before they could divide." "One by one, the monsters stopped coming back." "A stray data packet survived the last deletion." "The attack had a human name: Todd Phillips."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid charging behind the cyan-green hex-grid shield like a battering ram. Panel 2 shows virus monsters dissolving cleanly where the shield hits them. Panel 3 shows Dawes slicing red code fragments with the holosword. Panel 4 wide panel shows the cyberspace grid clearing as red cracks seal into green circuit lines. Panel 5 close on Dawes catching a glowing data packet, a small cube of corrupted red code. Panel 6 shows the packet opening into Todd Phillips's face and home workstation coordinates displayed as visual evidence rather than dense readable text.

## Page 8: The Man On The Run

- Story role: Resolve the immediate attack while leaving Todd as an active threat.
- Layout: Five panels moving from authorities to Todd's escape.
- Caption boxes: "The trace reached Todd's apartment before the authorities did." "They found the workstation hot, the commendations dusty, and the chair empty." "Todd had already packed the tools that still made him dangerous." "He had lost his job, his status, and now his hiding place." "He swore the machines would pay, and Mandroid would be his."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows police and federal cybercrime agents entering Todd's dim apartment workstation with flashlights and cautious body language. Panel 2 shows the empty chair, tangled cables, abandoned monitors, and face-down commendations. Panel 3 shows Todd in a rain-dark alley or transit station carrying his battered messenger bag of cables, hood up, looking back over his shoulder. Panel 4 close on Todd's bitter face lit by a phone screen showing Mandroid news coverage. Panel 5 final ominous panel shows Todd disappearing into the city crowd, one hand gripping a hardware key, while a red reflection of Mandroid's eyes glows faintly on the phone glass.
