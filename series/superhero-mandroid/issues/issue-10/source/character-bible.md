# Character Bible

## Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rechargeable-holo-shield-key-item.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/regenerating-rocket-fists-key-item.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.
- Issue continuity: Begins in the hidden lab monitoring portal signatures. During Todd's intrusion his eyes glow red and he collapses. In the cyberspace grid, his digital avatar keeps the same red suit, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` insignia, and green fists, but his edges glow with cyan diagnostic light. His rocket fists can scatter virus monsters but cannot delete them until he summons the digital form of his rechargeable holo shield.

## Dr. Elias Dawes, The Inventor

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/inventor-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: older human man in his early 60s with light skin, gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, and a practical dark lab coat over a blue shirt, with a key card on a lanyard in the lab.
- Issue continuity: Formerly known in source files as The Inventor. In this issue he is named Dr. Elias Dawes. He activates the Lyoko-Tron-Matrix protocol, risks plugging a terminal cable into his ear, and appears inside Mandroid's cyberspace grid as a translucent blue-white digital form with the same glasses, beard, and lab coat silhouette. He summons a cyan-green holosword to delete virus monsters.

## Todd Phillips

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/todd-phillips-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human man in his early 40s with light skin, tired gray-green eyes, short messy dark blond hair, patchy stubble, narrow face, stress lines around the eyes, rumpled navy hoodie under an old black blazer, faded jeans, worn sneakers, and a battered messenger bag full of cables.
- Issue continuity: Former classified cybersecurity engineer who resents AI coding agents, lost recognition, and Superhero Mandroid's public hero status. He launches the hack from a cluttered apartment workstation, escapes before authorities arrive, and becomes a future human antagonist.

## Computer Virus Monsters

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/computer-virus-monsters-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: swarming jagged humanoid cyber creatures made from red-black corrupted code, torn translucent data plates, glitching wireframe limbs, shard-like claws, flickering error-symbol eyes, and crawling static halos.
- Issue continuity: Todd's intrusion creates them inside Mandroid's mechanical mind. Ordinary rocket-fist impacts make them split into more monsters. Digital hardlight weapons delete them permanently.
