# Treatment

## Title

The Sponge Gate

## Issue Number

9

## Logline

Dr. Vera Volta sends Hydrohero Womandroid to study a portal for alien materials, but sponge goblins turn Hydrohero's water weapons into a threat Superhero Mandroid must solve by burning their strength away.

## Central Conflict

Dr. Vera Volta wants alien material and portal control data so she can finally build weapons worthy of defeating Superhero Mandroid's cosmic metal body. Hydrohero Womandroid wants to obey her creator but fears repeating the water-drain failure that almost shut her down. Superhero Mandroid enters the battle to protect Hydrohero from portal creatures, only to become the target of Hydrohero's orders and the sponge goblins' water-fed strength.

## Theme

The right upgrade can restore confidence, but obedience without judgment can turn a rescue into a disaster.

## Ending

Mandroid identifies that the sponge goblins grow stronger with every burst of water. He uses overclocked rocket-fist flames to evaporate their absorbed water, then knocks the weakened creatures unconscious with his holo shield before Hydrohero can recharge them. He cracks Hydrohero's water tank with a rocket fist, forcing her to retreat. Volta loses the rooftop fight but wins the data she wanted: enough portal readings to begin building her first controlled portal prototype.

## Emotional Arc

Hydrohero begins frightened by the memory of running out of water, regains confidence after Volta reveals the ambient-water refill upgrade, and then slips back into unquestioning obedience when Volta orders her to attack Mandroid. Mandroid begins as a rescuer, becomes a target, and wins by refusing to treat the fight as simple strength against strength. Volta ends the issue angrier at Hydrohero's gratitude than at the portal threat, proving that her ambition matters more than her creation's safety.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Volta's cosmic-metal discovery, Hydrohero's upgrade and mission, the portal ambush, Volta's order and the water-fed reversal, Mandroid's flame solution, the shield bash and cracked tank, and Volta's portal prototype reveal.
