Create the next issue of Superhero Mandroid based on this premise:

Doctor Vera Volta studies spectrographic scans of Superhero Mandroid captured through Hydrohero Womandroid's live camera feeds during their first encounter. The readings prove that Mandroid is built from cosmic metals, and Volta realizes she cannot defeat alien material with ordinary Earth elements. She decides she needs alien material of her own.

Because space travel is too expensive and unstable portals are too dangerous to enter blindly, Volta sends Hydrohero Womandroid to locate and analyze a portal from a safe distance. Hydrohero hesitates because she nearly ran out of water in her last battle, but Volta reveals an upgrade that lets her absorb ambient water molecules from the air and refill slowly over time. Hydrohero flies through the workshop skylight on water jets to find a portal.

Hydrohero locates an open portal and analyzes it from a nearby rooftop, but sponge-like goblins leap through and tackle her. They drain her water, growing larger and stronger. Superhero Mandroid's rocket fists knock them away, and Hydrohero thanks him. Volta sends an angry holo-message ordering Hydrohero to destroy the enemy instead of thanking him.

Hydrohero obeys and fires water jets into the fight, but the goblins absorb the water and become stronger. They overwhelm Mandroid until he notices the rule: water empowers them. He overclocks his rocket fists and releases a shockwave of engine flame, evaporating the water from their bodies until they shrink. Hydrohero tries to power them up again, but Mandroid bashes them unconscious with his holo shield, then fires a rocket fist into Hydrohero's water tank. The cracked tank forces her to retreat.

Back in Volta's lab, she analyzes the portal recording and smiles because she now has enough data to build her first portal prototype.
