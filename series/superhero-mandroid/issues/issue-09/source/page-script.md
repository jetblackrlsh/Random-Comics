# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Signal Hydrohero's mission turning into a water-fed portal fight.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image.
- Visual beats: On a rain-slick city rooftop at night, Superhero Mandroid braces with his cyan-green holo shield while two swollen sponge goblins loom over him, wet and hulking. Hydrohero Womandroid hovers behind them on blue-white water jets with her cracked-looking translucent chest water tank visible, conflicted and ready to fire. A violet-blue portal glows behind the goblins, and Dr. Vera Volta's angry hologram floats in the corner like a surveillance command feed.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "The Sponge Gate".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

Panel 4 shows her face lit by violet portal footage, calipers in hand, epiphany turning into a menacing smile.
