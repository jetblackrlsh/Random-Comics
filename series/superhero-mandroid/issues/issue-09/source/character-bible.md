# Character And Reference Bible

Use these continuity details in every generated page prompt where the element appears. The issue belongs to `series/superhero-mandroid/`, and the series reference images are under `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/`.

## Superhero Mandroid

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`
- Flying reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: human-looking adult android man with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents. He flies only with the flaming green jets of his rocket fists.
- Page continuity: enters as Hydrohero's rescuer, thanks her with confused restraint, gets overwhelmed by sponge goblins strengthened by her water jets, then overclocks his rocket fists for a fire shockwave before finishing the fight with a holo shield bash and a precision rocket punch.

## Dr. Vera Volta

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rival-inventor-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: middle-aged woman in her late 40s with light olive skin, sharp cheekbones, intense dark eyes, chin-length black hair with a silver streak, angular black glasses, and a fitted teal waterproof lab coat over a graphite jumpsuit. Her workshop is filled with brass gauges, pressure tanks, water turbines, portal scans, and angry Superhero Mandroid surveillance.
- Page continuity: studies cosmic-metal spectrographic readings, upgrades Hydrohero with ambient-water recovery, orders Hydrohero to stop thanking Mandroid, then uses the portal recording to begin her first portal prototype.

## Hydrohero Womandroid

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-womandroid-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: human-looking adult woman android with waterproof pearl-blue synthetic skin, short slick silver-blue hair, bright aqua eyes, athletic build, streamlined blue-and-white armored bodysuit, translucent chest water tank with visible swirling water, chrome shoulder turbines, wrist nozzles, and calf jet ports that spray controlled water streams.
- Page continuity: begins nervous because her water tank drained in Issue #4, regains confidence after the ambient-water upgrade, flies through the workshop skylight, gets drained by sponge goblins, thanks Mandroid for the rescue, obeys Volta by firing water at the fight, then retreats with her cracked chest water tank leaking.

## Sponge Goblins

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/sponge-goblins-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: two squat goblin-like portal creatures made of porous yellow-gray sponge material, huge dark wet eyes, jagged rubbery mouths, webbed claw hands, hunched backs, damp pitted skin full of water holes, and bodies that swell from knee-high scavengers into hulking muscular shapes as they absorb water. When burned dry, they shrink, wrinkle, and turn pale brittle beige.
- Page continuity: leap from the portal, drain Hydrohero's water, grow larger from her water jets, overpower Mandroid with heavy punches, then shrink after Mandroid's rocket-fist flame shockwave evaporates their stored water.

## Hydrohero Water Core

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/key-item-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-water-core-key-item.png`
