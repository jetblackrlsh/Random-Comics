## Recurring Character: Superhero Mandroid

Use the canonical Superhero Mandroid design for historical exhibits, memory images, and the final origin-loop image: human-looking adult male android with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green rocket fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and thruster vents. When flying, his only propulsion is green-white exhaust from the rocket fists.

For the far-future frozen museum form, keep the same identity but age and damage the artificial materials: wrinkled white synthetic skin, gray artificial hair, torn red suit, shredded yellow cape, rusted green metal rocket fists, locked joints, dim eyes, cracked chest insignia, and statue-still posture. He should look like a beloved preserved hero, not a corpse.

## Recurring Character: Dr. Elias Dawes

Only appears in memory or final historical echo: older human man in his early 60s with light skin, gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, practical dark lab coat over a blue shirt, and a key card on a lanyard. The issue states that he is gone by the possible future, leaving no one able to maintain Mandroid.

## New Character Group: Founding Super Champions of Mandroid

The founding team is a group of five young alien heroes from different worlds, all wearing red-and-yellow superhero suits inspired by Mandroid rather than exact copies.

- Nara Vex: tall blue-skinned crystalline alien teen with faceted cheekbones, translucent blue crystal hair fins, silver eyes, and hardlight-reflective forearms that can refract portal beams.
- Koro Thane: compact orange-scaled reptilian alien teen with a broad snout, bright green eyes, clawed hands, and a warm fearless grin; can grow dense armor plates and anchor larger teammates.
- Luma Pei: floating pale lavender jellyfish-like alien teen with a translucent bell-shaped upper body, soft glowing tendrils, and telekinetic bioluminescent fields.
- Brakko Morn: broad gray stone-skinned alien teen with moss-green markings, heavy shoulders, kind eyes, and seismic strength.
- Sella Nine: slender silver insectoid alien teen with four arms, black compound eyes, folded wing plates, and fast engineering instincts.

All five wear coordinated red body suits with yellow capes or cape-like panels, yellow chest insignias inspired by `SHM`, practical boots or alien equivalents, and small museum-issued utility belts. Their expressions should show nervous courage becoming organized leadership.

## New Setting: Superhero Mandroid Museum

Vast future memorial museum built around Mandroid's frozen body, combining glass, brushed metal, warm memorial lighting, alien-human civic architecture, suspended exhibit galleries, portal-war artifacts, murals of Mandroid's greatest adventures, holographic timelines, and a central rotunda where the rusted Mandroid stands on a simple platform. The museum should feel hopeful, public, and reverent even when damaged.

## New Setting: Super Champions Underground Headquarters

Large headquarters beneath the museum, built into reinforced subterranean chambers with red-and-yellow banners, training bays, alien medical pods, portal-warning screens, suit racks, tactical tables, launch elevators, memorial stonework from the museum foundation, and a secure dusty storage room where the failed time machine is later abandoned.

## New Key Item: Super Champions Time Machine

Improvised but advanced circular time machine built by young alien heroes to meet Superhero Mandroid in the past: waist-high bronze, red, yellow, and green ring frame, cracked crystal timing coils, alien circuitry, handwritten safety tags, small Mandroid-inspired `SHM` plates, unstable blue-green portal glow, and a control console with warning lights. It should look sincere, brilliant, and dangerously unfinished.
