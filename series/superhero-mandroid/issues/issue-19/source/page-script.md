# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue must be visibly marked as a possible future story.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Announce a possible future and introduce the Super Champions around Mandroid's frozen legacy.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition with readable title text.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "Possible Future: The Super Champions of Mandroid". Bottom caption: "After the greatest hero falls, many rise."
- Visual beats: In a future museum rotunda, rusted frozen Superhero Mandroid stands like a statue with wrinkled synthetic skin, gray artificial hair, shredded yellow cape, torn red suit, cracked `SHM` chest insignia, and rusted green rocket fists. Five young alien heroes in coordinated red-and-yellow Mandroid-inspired suits stand protectively before him while portals glow above the glass ceiling and a giant monster silhouette looms outside.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: A Possible Future

- Story role: Make the continuity label explicit and show Mandroid's body failing after Dawes is gone.
- Layout: Four large somber panels with roomy captions.
- Caption boxes: "This is a possible future." "Doctor Dawes was gone, and no one knew how to keep Mandroid alive forever." "His artificial skin wrinkled. His artificial hair turned gray. His suit tore. His green rocket fists rusted." "At last, his joints locked and his circuits fell silent."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a future archive placard and holographic timeline labeled as possible future. Panel 2 shows an empty old lab chair for Dawes beside maintenance tools no one understands. Panel 3 shows aged Mandroid walking through a ruined portal battlefield with torn suit and rusted fists. Panel 4 shows him frozen upright in a quiet civic chamber, eyes dim, people approaching with grief and reverence.

## Page 3: The Museum in the Dark Age

- Story role: Establish the museum, human-alien harmony, and the portal-threat dark age after Mandroid.
- Layout: Five panels moving from hope to threat.
- Caption boxes: "Humans and aliens built a museum around the hero who saved them." "Every wall remembered a battle he had won." "But portals did not mourn him." "Robots, monsters, and kaiju came without mercy." "The future needed more than one champion."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows human and alien families entering the Superhero Mandroid Museum together under warm lights. Panel 2 shows exhibits of his greatest adventures: rocket fists, holo shield, portal battles, Version One sketches, and a mural of Dawes. Panel 3 shows a portal opening over a future skyline. Panel 4 shows giant robots and grotesque monsters threatening mixed human-alien neighborhoods. Panel 5 shows the museum glowing as a beacon while smoke and portal lightning darken the city beyond.

## Page 4: The Monster at the Memorial

- Story role: Trigger the founding moment with a kaiju attack on the museum.
- Layout: Five action panels with clear museum geography.
- Caption boxes: "Then the dark age found the museum." "A kaiju tore through the memorial plaza." "The guards were brave, but bravery alone could not lift the stones." "Five young aliens had come only to honor Mandroid." "When the ceiling cracked above him, they stepped forward."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a huge grotesque kaiju smashing the museum plaza amid shattered glass and alarms. Panel 2 shows the monster clawing toward the central rotunda. Panel 3 shows guards and civilians evacuating humans and aliens together. Panel 4 shows the five founding alien teens in civilian travel clothes near the Mandroid statue, terrified but focused. Panel 5 shows them forming a line between the monster and frozen Mandroid as debris falls.

## Page 5: Anyone Can Become A Hero

- Story role: Show the alien teens defending the museum through kindness, cleverness, and alien biology.
- Layout: Five heroic panels with coordinated action.
- Caption boxes: "Nara bent portal light through crystal arms." "Koro anchored the broken wall with living armor." "Luma lifted children on gentle fields of light." "Brakko shook the plaza until the kaiju lost its footing, and Sella rewired the doors open." "They learned what Mandroid had been teaching for centuries: a kind heart and a clever mind can make anyone a hero."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Nara refracting a portal-energy blast away from the statue. Panel 2 shows Koro bracing a collapsing wall with orange armor plates. Panel 3 shows Luma floating rescued children and elders to safety. Panel 4 shows Brakko slamming stone hands into the floor while Sella's four arms repair emergency door circuits. Panel 5 shows the kaiju stumbling back as the five teens stand united before Mandroid's frozen form.

## Page 6: The Super Champions of Mandroid

- Story role: Show the team growing into an army and founding its headquarters under the museum.
- Layout: Five montage panels with hopeful escalation.
- Caption boxes: "Stories of the museum stand crossed the stars." "More young heroes came with stranger gifts and the same promise." "They wore red and yellow for the ancient hero who inspired them." "Under the museum, they built a headquarters for every world that needed courage." "They named themselves the Super Champions of Mandroid."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows alien news feeds and starship arrivals spreading the story. Panel 2 shows dozens of young heroes from many species training together. Panel 3 shows suit racks of red-and-yellow Mandroid-inspired uniforms with varied alien shapes. Panel 4 shows the underground headquarters with tactical screens, training bays, and memorial banners. Panel 5 shows the founding five, now suited and leading a large army of heroes beneath the museum.

## Page 7: The Thank You Machine

- Story role: Show the time-machine project and the decision not to tamper after the failed test.
- Layout: Five panels with a shift from excitement to caution.
- Caption boxes: "Some Champions wanted to thank Mandroid in person." "So they built a machine to reach the past." "They flipped the switch." "Nothing happened." "Wisdom won. Time was too dangerous to touch."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Sella and other Champions designing the circular time machine at a lab table with Mandroid museum records. Panel 2 shows the nearly finished machine glowing blue-green in the headquarters. Panel 3 shows the founding five activating it, excited and nervous. Panel 4 shows sparks, smoke, and dead warning lights while no visible portal opens. Panel 5 shows the leaders covering the machine and wheeling it toward a dusty storage room, choosing restraint.

## Page 8: The First Portals

- Story role: Reveal the malfunctioning loop and connect the possible future to Dr. Dawes and Version One.
- Layout: Five panels ending on a time-spanning cause-and-effect image.
- Caption boxes: "But the machine had not failed." "In the dark, it kept reaching." "It opened doors across dimensions, across space, across time." "Long ago, those doors sent invaders to Earth and forced Doctor Dawes to build Version One." "In this possible future, Mandroid inspired the Champions, and the Champions created the need for Mandroid."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the covered time machine forgotten in a dusty storage room, a tiny glow under the tarp. Panel 2 shows the ring quietly opening small unstable portals while no one watches. Panel 3 shows portals spilling robots, monsters, and strange dimensional creatures into different eras. Panel 4 shows young Dr. Dawes in the hidden lab watching early portal alarms beside the exposed green-metal Version One android. Panel 5 shows a layered time-loop composition: frozen future Mandroid, the Super Champions, malfunctioning time machine portals, young Dawes, and Version One all connected by blue-green arcs.
