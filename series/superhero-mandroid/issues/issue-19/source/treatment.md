# Treatment

## Title

Possible Future: The Super Champions of Mandroid

## Issue Number

19

## Continuity Label

Possible future. This issue is presented as one possible far-future branch of Superhero Mandroid continuity, not a guaranteed mainline ending. Its time-machine reveal offers a possible portal-origin loop that can coexist with Issue #15's Sketchmaker revelation as a future-born mechanism the story may be drawing through.

## Logline

In a possible future after Superhero Mandroid rusts into a museum statue, young alien heroes defend his memorial, build an army in his honor, and accidentally create the malfunctioning time machine that may have opened the first portals in his past.

## Central Conflict

Superhero Mandroid's long protection of Earth cannot outlast time, rust, and Dr. Dawes's death. After Mandroid freezes forever in the distant future, humans and aliens build a museum around him, but the world falls into a dark age of portal invaders without its single champion. The central conflict is whether a future raised on Mandroid's example can stop waiting for one hero and become many. When a kaiju attacks the museum, the founding alien super teens must prove that courage, kindness, and cleverness matter more than being built by Dr. Dawes.

## Theme

A hero's body can fail, but a heroic example can become a civilization's shared power.

## Ending

The Super Champions of Mandroid build a time machine to thank their inspiration, decide not to risk using it after a failed test, and abandon it in storage. The final reveal shows that the broken machine never stopped running: it opens portals across time, space, and dimensions, sending the first threats into the past and inspiring Dr. Dawes to build Version One. In this possible future, Mandroid's legacy creates the team, and the team's mistake creates the need for Mandroid.

## Emotional Arc

The issue begins with grief: Mandroid's skin wrinkles, hair grays, suit tears, rocket fists rust, and his circuits lock after Dr. Dawes is gone. The museum around his frozen body feels like a memorial to a better age. The alien super teens begin as scared admirers protecting what remains of their hero. By defending the museum together, they understand that Mandroid's example was never only about his machinery. Their grief becomes organization, their admiration becomes responsibility, and their final attempt to thank him becomes a tragic, awe-filled loop.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, the possible-future warning and Mandroid's rusting end, the museum and dark age, the kaiju attack on the museum, the alien super teens' first stand, the rise of the Super Champions of Mandroid, the time-machine experiment, the abandoned malfunction, and the closing causal loop to Dr. Dawes and Version One.

## New Continuity Elements

This possible-future issue introduces the Superhero Mandroid Museum, the Founding Super Champions of Mandroid, the Super Champions Underground Headquarters, and the Super Champions Time Machine as possible-future continuity elements. Because the issue is explicitly a possible future, these elements are available for future stories without forcing the current mainline to reach this ending.
