# Treatment

## Title

The Pigeon In The Backrooms

## Issue Number

14

## Continuity Label

Mainline. Dr. Volta weaponizes the portal prototype from Issue #9 by bolting it over Hydrohero Womandroid's cracked water tank, but the upgrade backfires and strands Hydrohero in the Backrooms until Detective Webwidow intervenes.

## Logline

When Hydrohero Womandroid gains a portal beam and accidentally banishes a city pigeon, Superhero Mandroid reflects the weapon back at her, sending Hydrohero into the Backrooms with the angry creature her shot transformed.

## Central Conflict

Dr. Vera Volta believes Hydrohero does not need to be replaced; she only needs help strong enough to outclass Superhero Mandroid. The finished portal prototype seems to solve two problems at once by sealing Hydrohero's cracked water tank and letting her fire dimensional portal beams. But the weapon is reckless. When Hydrohero misses Mandroid and sends a pigeon to another dimension, Mandroid understands the danger before she does. His defensive reflection saves him but strands Hydrohero in the Backrooms, where the transformed pigeon destroys her upgrade and leaves her leaking, scared, and dependent on the kind of hero she has refused to become.

## Theme

Power that opens doors without responsibility eventually leaves its user trapped on the wrong side.

## Ending

Detective Webwidow rescues Hydrohero from the Backrooms through the multiversal web, patches her leaking tank with webbing, and warns her to stop treating portals like toys. Hydrohero returns home alive but shaken. She has not become a hero yet, but for the first time she seriously considers that beating Mandroid is less important than becoming better than Volta's jealousy.

## Emotional Arc

Hydrohero begins the issue triumphant, convinced the portal retrofit finally makes her superior. After accidentally banishing a harmless city pigeon and being sent to the same dimension herself, she experiences the terror and helplessness her weapon inflicts. The Backrooms-mutated pigeon turns her own cruelty into a physical threat, and Webwidow's rescue forces her to confront the possibility that other versions of herself are capable of real heroism. She ends contemplative rather than repentant, with the first visible crack in Volta's control.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Volta's retrofit, Hydrohero's ambush and the vanished pigeon, Mandroid's reflected beam, Hydrohero stranded in the Backrooms, the mutated pigeon destroying the portal prototype and cornering her, Webwidow's rescue through the multiversal web, and Webwidow's warning as Hydrohero quietly rethinks her purpose.
