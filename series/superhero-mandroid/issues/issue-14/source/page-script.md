# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue is a mainline Superhero Mandroid story that sends Hydrohero Womandroid into the Backrooms.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise the central reversal: Hydrohero's new portal weapon turns a harmless pigeon into the thing hunting her.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "The Pigeon In The Backrooms". Smaller bottom caption: "Hydrohero opened the wrong door."
- Visual beats: Hydrohero Womandroid runs through a yellow Backrooms hallway, her cracked chest water tank leaking from behind a damaged brass-and-chrome portal device. A monstrous Backrooms-mutated pigeon looms behind her with spread wings and a cracked beak. In the foreground, Detective Webwidow's silver web line cuts across the scene, while a small inset reflection shows Superhero Mandroid raising his cyan-green holo shield in the city.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Volta's Better Answer

- Story role: Establish Volta's revised plan and the portal retrofit over Hydrohero's cracked tank.
- Layout: Four large panels in Dr. Volta's wet robotics workshop.
- Caption boxes: "Dr. Vera Volta had wanted portal metal for a better android." "Then Hydrohero almost beat Superhero Mandroid with a little help from sponge goblins." "Editor's Note: See Issue 9." "Volta decided her creation did not need replacing. She needed a weapon no rival could block."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Volta studying portal schematics and Superhero Mandroid footage in her teal-lit workshop. Panel 2 shows Hydrohero standing on a repair platform with the crack visible in her translucent chest water tank. Panel 3 shows Volta bolting the brass-and-chrome portal prototype over the crack as a sealed armor plate. Panel 4 shows Hydrohero's new chest-mounted portal unit glowing violet-blue while Volta smiles with jealous triumph.

## Page 3: The Missing Pigeon

- Story role: Show the ambush, the first portal beam, and Mandroid realizing the weapon's moral danger.
- Layout: Five action panels on a city street.
- Caption boxes: "Hydrohero found Mandroid on patrol before the portal alarms did." "He heard water jets and moved on instinct." "The beam missed the hero." "It did not miss the pigeon." "\"What did you do to it?\" Mandroid demanded."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Superhero Mandroid walking a city street, green rocket fists idle, yellow cape moving in traffic wind. Panel 2 shows Hydrohero bursting from above on water jets, chest portal unit charging violet-blue. Panel 3 shows Mandroid leaping sideways as a dimensional beam slices past him. Panel 4 shows an ordinary gray city pigeon being struck and vanishing in a clean violet flash, no gore. Panel 5 shows Mandroid shocked and angry while Hydrohero hovers mockingly, asking whether he is too scared to face her.

## Page 4: Reflected Doorway

- Story role: Turn Hydrohero's portal weapon back on her.
- Layout: Four large panels, clear tactical sequence.
- Caption boxes: "\"Stay still,\" Hydrohero said, \"and you can find out.\"" "She fired again." "Mandroid did not answer with a rocket fist." "He answered with a shield."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero aiming the chest-mounted portal device while water jets hold her in the air. Panel 2 shows the violet-blue beam racing toward Mandroid. Panel 3 shows Mandroid bracing behind his curved cyan-green holo shield, hex-grid surface bright and angled. Panel 4 shows the beam reflecting from the shield and striking Hydrohero in the chest unit, folding the city around her into a sudden portal flash.

## Page 5: Moisture In The Maze

- Story role: Place Hydrohero in the Backrooms and show why she can survive there at first.
- Layout: Four roomy panels with surreal hallway repetition.
- Caption boxes: "Hydrohero landed where the walls all looked the same." "The carpet was wet enough to feed her tank, so panic came slowly." "A mirror might let her shoot herself home." "Then the hallway answered with one distorted, furious coo."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero sprawled in the Backrooms, surrounded by endless yellow walls, buzzing fluorescent lights, damp stained carpet, and no windows. Panel 2 shows moisture drawing into her water tank while the portal prototype on her chest flickers. Panel 3 shows her searching hallway after hallway for a mirror, passing warped doorways and impossible angles. Panel 4 shows her freezing as a huge shadow with wing shapes grows on the wall behind her.

## Page 6: The Beak At The Door

- Story role: Reveal the mutated pigeon, destroy the portal prototype, and put Hydrohero at her low point.
- Layout: Five action panels.
- Caption boxes: "The pigeon had arrived first." "The Backrooms had made it bigger, angrier, and harder to ignore." "Hydrohero charged one last portal beam." "The beak hit first." "Water began to leak from the crack Mandroid had left behind."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 reveals the Backrooms-mutated pigeon: enormous, damp, furious, feathers tangled with carpet fibers. Panel 2 shows Hydrohero recoiling while trying to charge the violet-blue chest portal. Panel 3 shows the mutated pigeon lunging and pecking the brass-and-chrome portal prototype. Panel 4 shows the prototype shattering in sparks and violet fragments, exposing the cracked water tank underneath. Panel 5 shows Hydrohero running through the wet carpet hallways, one hand over the leaking tank, unable to spend water on jets while the mutant pigeon flies after her.

## Page 7: Web In The Walls

- Story role: Rescue Hydrohero and connect Webwidow's choice to Issue #11's alternate heroic Hydrohero.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: "The maze ended in a corner." "The pigeon did not." "Then a silver web yanked the beast backward." "\"I've seen worlds where you make a difference,\" Webwidow said. \"Try becoming that version before portals eat you alive.\""
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero cornered against a yellow wall, leaking water, face frightened as the mutated pigeon approaches. Panel 2 shows a silver web line wrapping the pigeon from behind and slamming its head into the wall, dazing it without gore. Panel 3 shows Detective Webwidow rushing through an opening in the multiversal web, fedora low, duster flying, wrist web shooter extended. Panel 4 shows Webwidow grabbing Hydrohero's hand and pulling her through a glowing web-shaped tear while the mutant pigeon recovers behind them.

## Page 8: Better Than The Weapon

- Story role: Close the multiversal escape, patch Hydrohero, and leave her in contemplation.
- Layout: Five panels ending on Hydrohero's unsettled face.
- Caption boxes: "The web closed before the Backrooms could follow." "Webwidow patched the cracked tank with silk stronger than Volta's pride." "\"Stop opening doors you do not understand. Start being a better hero.\"" "\"I saved you once. Next time, I may leave you to the maze.\"" "Hydrohero thanked him, and for once she had no boast ready."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Webwidow and Hydrohero tumbling back into Mandroid's City as the web-shaped opening seals, with the mutant pigeon blocked on the other side. Panel 2 shows Webwidow using sticky silver webbing to patch Hydrohero's cracked chest water tank. Panel 3 shows Webwidow pointing sternly before swinging away between buildings. Panel 4 shows Hydrohero standing alone, portal prototype gone, web patch visible, water level stabilizing. Panel 5 final panel is a close candid photo-comic panel of Hydrohero's face, bright aqua eyes thoughtful and shaken rather than triumphant, city lights reflected on wet pavement behind her.
