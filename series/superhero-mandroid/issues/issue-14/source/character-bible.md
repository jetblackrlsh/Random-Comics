# Character Bible

## Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rechargeable-holo-shield-key-item.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/regenerating-rocket-fists-key-item.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin on his face, slicked-back brown artificial hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.
- Issue continuity: Mandroid is on ordinary patrol when Hydrohero ambushes him. He reacts defensively when the portal beam vaporizes a pigeon from reality, then uses his cyan-green holo shield to reflect the next beam. He flies only by green-white exhaust from his green rocket fists, never by boot jets, wings, back thrusters, or a jet pack.

## Dr. Vera Volta

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rival-inventor-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: middle-aged woman in her late 40s with light olive skin, sharp cheekbones, intense dark eyes, chin-length black hair with a silver streak, angular black glasses, and a fitted teal waterproof lab coat over a graphite jumpsuit.
- Issue continuity: Volta abandons the idea of replacing Hydrohero after Issue #9. She bolts the finished portal prototype over Hydrohero's cracked water tank, treating the patch and weapon as proof that she is smarter than Dr. Elias Dawes.

## Hydrohero Womandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-womandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-water-core-key-item.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult woman android with waterproof pearl-blue synthetic skin, short slick silver-blue hair, bright aqua eyes, athletic build, streamlined blue-and-white armored bodysuit, translucent chest water tank with visible swirling water, chrome shoulder turbines, wrist nozzles, and calf jet ports that spray controlled water streams.
- Issue continuity: Her chest water tank remains cracked from Mandroid's rocket fist in Issue #9. Volta bolts the portal prototype over the tank as a repair plate and dimensional weapon. After the Backrooms-mutated pigeon destroys the prototype, the crack reopens and Hydrohero leaks water until Webwidow patches the tank with webbing.

## Detective Webwidow

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/detective-webwidow-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human man in his late 30s with light-to-medium skin, narrow angular face, tired sharp eyes, dark stubble, a dark spider-web tattoo spreading across one side of his face, weathered dark detective fedora, long charcoal-brown duster, practical trousers, worn boots, compact wrist web shooters, and a large sewn black widow spider emblem on the back of his duster.
- Issue continuity: Webwidow has seen many universes, including the alternate timeline in Issue #11 where Hydrohero became a true hero. He rescues this Hydrohero because he believes she has that same potential, but warns her that he will not keep saving her from reckless portal experiments.

## Backrooms-Mutated Pigeon

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/backrooms-mutated-pigeon-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: enormous distorted city pigeon grown to nearly person-sized, oily gray feathers clumped with damp yellow carpet fibers, swollen iridescent neck, huge black furious eyes, oversized cracked beak, talons scraping wet carpet, asymmetrical wings, and eerie fluorescent yellow reflections from Backrooms lighting.
- Issue continuity: It begins as an ordinary city pigeon accidentally hit by Hydrohero's portal beam. The Backrooms mutates it into a furious beast that destroys the portal prototype and chases Hydrohero until Webwidow knocks it away and closes the multiversal opening before it can follow.
