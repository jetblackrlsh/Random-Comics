# Page Script

## General Page Rules

- Full realistic candid photo-comic pages, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, or logos.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art by `image_gen`.
- Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Keep recurring character designs consistent with `source/character-bible.md` and the series reference images.
- This issue uses a deliberate 9-page exception: one cover plus eight story pages.

## Page 1: Cover

Story role: Establish Emperor Rex as a towering cybernetic dinosaur warlock threat to the Mandroid Men.

Cover composition: Dramatic city-broadcast invasion scene. Emperor Rex stands enormous in front of a purple portal with demonic shadow ninjas spreading around him. Superhero Mandroid lies partially powered down in the foreground with his red suit, yellow cape, `SHM` chest insignia, and green rocket fists visible. Roman, Master Liang Chen, Protector Paul, Detective Webwidow, and Aurelia appear as smaller action silhouettes moving into the crisis.

Readable cover text to generate in the art:
- Title: "Emperor Rex"
- Small caption: "Issue 23"

## Page 2: World Broadcast

Story role: Emperor Rex arrives and threatens the entire planet.

Panel layout: Three large panels.

Caption boxes:
- Panel 1: "A purple portal tore open above Mandroid's City, and Emperor Rex stepped out with a demon army."
- Panel 2: "His cybernetic eye seized every broadcast on Earth."
- Panel 3: "He demanded surrender, declaring that animals should rule all worlds and humanity should kneel."

Visual beats: Purple portal above a city plaza; cybernetic T-Rex warlock emerging with demonic shadow ninjas; televisions, phones, and giant city screens showing Rex's face; crowds reacting in panic.

## Page 3: The Smile Argument

Story role: Mandroid confronts Rex, and Rex attacks his human appearance before Mandroid defines his purpose.

Panel layout: Four panels.

Caption boxes:
- Panel 1: "Superhero Mandroid arrived first, wearing the human face and bright costume that made children cheer."
- Panel 2: "Rex's scanners saw through the skin and hair. The hero was entirely mechanical."
- Panel 3: "Rex asked why a machine would imitate the oppressive human race."
- Panel 4: "Mandroid answered that his face made people smile, and making people smile was what he lived for."

Visual beats: Mandroid lands using green rocket-fist jets; Rex scans him with red cybernetic eye light; close-up of Mandroid's calm synthetic face and green fists; Rex snarling while shadow ninjas wait behind him.

## Page 4: Older Than Animals

Story role: Mandroid turns Rex's ideology against him.

Panel layout: Four panels.

Caption boxes:
- Panel 1: "Rex said humanity came from animals and forgot its place."
- Panel 2: "Animals ruled Earth for millions of years before humans stood upright."
- Panel 3: "Mandroid asked why age gave animals the right to rule, when microscopic life came first."
- Panel 4: "For the first time, Emperor Rex looked less like a conqueror and more like a bully who had been corrected."

Visual beats: Rex looming and preaching; stylized holographic history from microbes to dinosaurs to humans projected by Rex's cybernetics; Mandroid calmly pointing out the flaw; Rex's expression turning furious.

## Page 5: Sleep Mode

Story role: Rex defeats Mandroid tactically by hacking him.

Panel layout: Four panels.

Caption boxes:
- Panel 1: "Mandroid still hoped diplomacy could defuse the invasion."
- Panel 2: "Rex laughed at mercy."
- Panel 3: "His cybernetic eye forced a command into Mandroid's systems."
- Panel 4: "Earth's greatest android hero dropped into sleep mode on live broadcast."

Visual beats: Mandroid raising a cautious open hand; Rex's cybernetic eye flaring red-purple code beams; Mandroid's eyes going dark as warning code glows across his face and chest insignia; Mandroid collapsing while shadow ninjas surge forward.

## Page 6: Non-Mechanical Members

Story role: GAL-XZ, Hydrohero, and Dr. Dawes realize android heroes cannot safely fight Rex.

Panel layout: Four panels.

Caption boxes:
- Panel 1: "In Dr. Dawes's hidden lab, GAL-XZ and Hydrohero watched Mandroid fall."
- Panel 2: "Dawes saw the pattern before panic could waste another second."
- Panel 3: "Rex's cybernetics could compromise android heroes."
- Panel 4: "The Mandroid Men needed their living, magical, and non-mechanical members in the field."

Visual beats: Lab monitors showing the live broadcast; GAL-XZ glowing green but held back; Hydrohero's water core swirling anxiously; Dawes pointing at diagnostic screens; dispatch displays highlighting Roman, Liang, Paul, Webwidow, and Aurelia.

## Page 7: Barrier Against Shadows

Story role: Roman, Master Liang Chen, Protector Paul, and Webwidow fight Rex's army but are nearly overwhelmed.

Panel layout: Five action panels.

Caption boxes:
- Panel 1: "Roman punched through the shadow ninjas with Atlantean strength."
- Panel 2: "Master Liang Chen covered him with golden-white chi."
- Panel 3: "The army kept coming."
- Panel 4: "Protector Paul raised a holy barrier, but it could only protect the fighters close beside him."
- Panel 5: "From a rooftop, Webwidow pinned Rex's armored tail before it crushed them."

Visual beats: Roman smashing smoky ninjas; Liang firing chi blasts; ninjas reforming in overwhelming numbers; Paul praying with rosary as a glowing holy barrier surrounds Roman and Liang; Webwidow firing silver webbing from a rooftop to pin Rex's tail to the pavement.

## Page 8: All Hope Fades

Story role: Rex overpowers the non-mechanical heroes, creating the low point.

Panel layout: Four panels.

Caption boxes:
- Panel 1: "Roman and Liang could match Rex's strength for moments, not forever."
- Panel 2: "The cybernetic eye that felled Mandroid became a laser cannon."
- Panel 3: "Roman fell, and Liang poured every breath of chi into a trembling shield."
- Panel 4: "Paul's barrier faded as the shadow ninjas closed in."

Visual beats: Roman and Liang striking Rex together; Rex blasting Roman with red-purple eye laser; Liang straining behind a golden chi barrier against the beam; Paul exhausted with dimming holy barrier while ninjas surround them.

## Page 9: Satan's Daughter

Story role: Aurelia turns the demon army, resolves the invasion, and leaves a revenge stinger.

Panel layout: Five panels.

Caption boxes:
- Panel 1: "Then the shadow ninjas bowed."
- Panel 2: "Aurelia flew above them, and the demons recognized Satan's daughter."
- Panel 3: "At her command, Rex's own army dragged him back through the portal."
- Panel 4: "When Mandroid rebooted, Aurelia warned that magic heroes cannot always arrive before wounds are made."
- Panel 5: "On his throne world, Emperor Rex strained against shadow chains and promised revenge."

Visual beats: Aurelia descending in green flame above bowing ninjas; ninjas tackling Rex and forcing him backward; Rex falling through the purple portal; Mandroid rebooting as Webwidow, Roman, Liang, Paul, and Aurelia regroup; final panel on Rex chained to his giant throne in the shadow throne world.
