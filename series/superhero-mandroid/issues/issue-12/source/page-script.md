# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue is a mainline origin flashback about the nameless Version One android becoming Superhero Mandroid.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Show the split between the feared green war-machine body and the beloved finished Superhero Mandroid identity.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition with a vertical before-and-after split.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "The Heart Beneath The Metal". Smaller bottom caption: "Before the suit, before the name, he was still a hero."
- Visual beats: Left side shows Version One as a fully exposed green cosmic-metal android with giant green rocket fists, no costume, no cape, no hair, no synthetic skin, standing in smoky portal light while frightened civilians recoil. Right side shows the finished Superhero Mandroid in white synthetic facial skin, slick brown hair, red suit, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and the same uncovered green rocket fists, with civilians smiling behind him. Dr. Elias Dawes stands between the two versions in the hidden lab, holding design sketches and looking emotionally shaken.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The Nameless Protector

- Story role: Establish that Version One is already effective and heroic in action, even before the world sees him that way.
- Layout: Four large panels with clear action beats.
- Caption boxes: "Before the name, there was Version One." "He was green cosmic metal from head to foot, with rocket fists built to answer portals fast." "Demons, aliens, and robots broke into the city." "Version One stopped them all."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Version One standing in the hidden lab launch shaft, a fully green metal android with giant fists and no costume, while Dawes monitors portal alarms. Panel 2 shows a purple portal tearing open over a city street. Panel 3 shows Version One flying only by green-white exhaust from his oversized rocket fists, no boot jets or back thrusters. Panel 4 shows him smashing a bulky alien robot away from civilians, shielding people with his body.

## Page 3: The Walking Tank

- Story role: Show the emotional cost of looking monstrous despite doing heroic work.
- Layout: Five panels, mixing rescue and public reaction.
- Caption boxes: "Every battle ended with the city alive." "Every rescue ended with people stepping back." "They did not see a protector." "They saw an intimidating, monstrous walking tank." "Version One learned that saving people did not always make them smile."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Version One holding up falling concrete after a portal fight while families crawl to safety. Panel 2 shows a child hiding behind a parent as Version One gently offers a huge green metal hand. Panel 3 shows news cameras filming him from far away, their lenses making him look larger and harsher. Panel 4 shows a crowd backing away while police and medics stare nervously. Panel 5 close on Version One's green metal face, eyes lowered with quiet hurt.

## Page 4: Function Was Not Enough

- Story role: Put Dawes's engineering worldview against Version One's emotional pain.
- Layout: Four roomy panels in the hidden lab.
- Caption boxes: "Dr. Elias Dawes measured success in threats neutralized and civilians protected." "He did not see a design problem." "\"They are alive,\" Dawes said. \"That is what matters.\"" "\"I know,\" Version One answered. \"But I hate being hated.\""
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Dawes reviewing mission footage and damage reports on lab monitors, satisfied but tired. Panel 2 shows Version One at a charging station, green metal body scuffed from combat, sitting instead of standing like a machine. Panel 3 shows Dawes turning in surprise as Version One looks toward footage of frightened civilians. Panel 4 shows a quiet two-shot: Dawes in practical dark lab coat, Version One huge and wounded in posture, the lab lights reflecting in both their eyes.

## Page 5: A Machine With A Heart

- Story role: Reveal Version One's simple desire and Dawes's realization.
- Layout: Four emotionally focused panels.
- Caption boxes: "\"I do not want statues,\" Version One said." "\"I do not want medals.\"" "\"I only want them to smile when they see me coming.\"" "That was when Dawes understood: he had not built a weapon. He had built a hero with a heart."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Version One speaking gently with his massive green fists folded carefully in his lap. Panel 2 shows a memory-like panel of civilians in earlier battles, fearful instead of grateful. Panel 3 close on Version One's metal face, subtle human sadness in glowing eyes. Panel 4 close on Dawes, glasses catching lab light, expression shifting from scientific surprise to humbled affection.

## Page 6: The Name

- Story role: Show Dawes choosing the hero identity and design logic.
- Layout: Five panels, design-room sequence.
- Caption boxes: "If Version One had the heart of a man, Dawes decided his name should say so." "\"Superhero Mandroid,\" Dawes wrote." "Red would complement the green metal." "Yellow would make the design bright and hopeful." "The world needed to see the hero, not just the hardware."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Dawes writing the name "Superhero Mandroid" on a workbench design sheet. Panel 2 shows fabric swatches: red suit material beside green metal samples. Panel 3 shows yellow cape fabric and a yellow `SHM` chest insignia under warm bench lights. Panel 4 shows Dawes sketching synthetic facial skin and brown artificial hair while Version One watches. Panel 5 shows Version One reflected in a dark monitor with the new design layered over him like a possible future.

## Page 7: The Face The World Could Read

- Story role: Complete the redesign while preserving the exposed rocket fists.
- Layout: Four large panels, surgical and costume transformation without gore.
- Caption boxes: "Dawes added white synthetic skin to the face." "He added brown artificial hair, shaped to move like a human hero's." "He left the green rocket fists uncovered." "They were too powerful, too honest, and too necessary to hide."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Dawes carefully fitting white synthetic facial skin panels onto Version One's green metal face in the hidden lab. Panel 2 shows artificial brown hair being aligned and styled while Version One watches himself in a mirror. Panel 3 shows the red unitard and yellow cape being fitted over the green metal torso, yellow `SHM` insignia centered on the chest. Panel 4 close on the huge green mechanical rocket fists staying exposed, thruster vents visible, with Dawes checking them and Version One flexing them carefully.

## Page 8: The Hero He Had Always Been

- Story role: Resolve the origin by showing the public finally recognizing Superhero Mandroid.
- Layout: Five panels with a triumphant final wide panel.
- Caption boxes: "The next portal opened before the new suit was even clean." "This time, the city saw red, yellow, and a human face above the green fists." "Mandroid defeated the robot as quickly as Version One ever had." "Then a child smiled and waved." "For the first time, Superhero Mandroid knew the world could see the hero he had always been."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a fresh portal opening over a city plaza as the newly named Superhero Mandroid launches from the hidden lab. Panel 2 shows him flying only by green-white exhaust from his uncovered rocket fists, red suit and yellow cape bright against the sky. Panel 3 shows him punching a portal robot away from civilians with one rocket fist while landing protectively in front of them. Panel 4 shows a child smiling and waving while adults cheer, no one recoiling. Panel 5 final wide panel shows Superhero Mandroid standing in sunlight, red suit, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, white synthetic face smiling gently, giant green fists uncovered at his sides, with Dawes watching from the crowd in quiet pride.
