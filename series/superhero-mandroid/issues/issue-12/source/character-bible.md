# Character Bible

## Superhero Mandroid Version One

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-version-one-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: nameless early form of Superhero Mandroid, adult male android silhouette, fully exposed green cosmic metal body from face to feet, heavy armor-like plating, dark seams, broad shoulders, athletic walking-tank build, oversized green mechanical rocket fists with knuckle plating and wrist thruster vents, no costume, no cape, no synthetic skin, no hair, no chest insignia. His face has humanoid features formed from green metal plates with glowing brown-green eyes, capable of sadness and gentle expressions despite the intimidating body.
- Issue continuity: This is the same being who later becomes Superhero Mandroid. He is not a separate character. He is excellent at fighting portal demons, aliens, and robots, but civilians see him as a frightening machine. His longing to make people smile makes Dr. Dawes realize that the android has a heart.

## Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/regenerating-rocket-fists-key-item.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin on his face, slicked-back brown artificial hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.
- Issue continuity: Dawes creates this public-facing design from Version One. The red suit complements the green metal, the yellow cape and emblem read as bright and hopeful, and the green rocket fists remain uncovered because they are Mandroid's signature strength and need exposed thruster vents to function.

## Dr. Elias Dawes, The Inventor

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/inventor-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: older human man in his early 60s with light skin, gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, and a practical dark lab coat over a blue shirt, with a key card on a lanyard in the lab.
- Issue continuity: In this origin flashback, Dawes initially thinks Version One's success in combat proves the design is complete. When Version One admits he hates being hated, Dawes understands that he has not built a simple machine. He gives the android a name, a face, and the visual language of a hero.
