# Treatment

## Title

The Heart Beneath The Metal

## Issue Number

12

## Continuity Label

Mainline origin flashback. This issue reveals how the nameless Version One android became the public hero known as Superhero Mandroid.

## Logline

Before he had a name, costume, or human face, Superhero Mandroid saved the city as a green metal war machine, until his sadness at being feared taught Dr. Dawes that he had built a hero with a heart.

## Central Conflict

Version One defeats every portal threat Dr. Dawes points him toward, but each victory makes the city more afraid of the nameless green android. Dr. Dawes believes function is enough: the machine wins fights, protects civilians, and survives. Version One wants something Dawes never programmed as a requirement. He wants the people he saves to smile when they see him. Dawes must accept that protection without connection is not enough for a true hero.

## Theme

A hero is not only a weapon that wins battles; a hero is someone people can recognize, trust, and love.

## Ending

Dawes names the android Superhero Mandroid, gives him white synthetic facial skin, brown artificial hair, and the red-and-yellow suit that frames his green metal fists as heroic instead of monstrous. When Mandroid saves civilians from one more portal robot, the crowd finally cheers, and he smiles because they can see the heart he had all along.

## Emotional Arc

Version One begins as an efficient but lonely protector who endures fear because he thinks it is the price of service. His pain surprises Dawes, who first treats the android as a successful machine and then recognizes shame, longing, and compassion in him. Dawes moves from engineering practicality to parental responsibility. The final redesign does not make Version One a hero; it helps the world recognize the hero he already was.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, nameless Version One saving the city, the public fearing him, Dawes dismissing the optics, Version One confessing that he only wants smiles, Dawes realizing the machine has a heart, the naming and redesign, and the public reveal where Superhero Mandroid is finally celebrated without hiding his green rocket fists.
