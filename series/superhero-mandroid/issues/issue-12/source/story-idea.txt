Create the latest issue of Superhero Mandroid based on the following idea:

Version one of Superhero Mandroid had no name. His entire body resembled the same green metal that obviously makes up his rocket fists. He wore no superhero costume and he seemed more machine than man. While he excelled at fighting the demons, aliens, and robots that burst free from random portals around the city: He was feared rather than celebrated. The people saw him as an intimidating, monstrous, walking tank.

Doctor Dawes didn't see much of a problem with this, Version One hated being hated, because all he wants in life is to make people smile. This surprised Doctor Dawes. He didn't know his machine had a heart. That's when Doctor Dawes realized that he had built a true hero rather than a simple machine. Doctor Dawes decides that if Version One is a hero with the heart of a man, then his name and his appearance should reflect this.

Doctor Dawes gives Version One the name "Superhero Mandroid". He creates the iconic red and yellow suit. Red, because it is a complimentary color to the green of the metal. Yellow, because it is bright and hopeful. Artificial hair and artificial skin are added to the face, but not the hands.

Superhero Mandroid's large rocket fists are what make him such a formidable hero. They would look wrong covered in artificial skin and they wouldn't function properly. When the world saw the new Superhero Mandroid, they could finally see him for the hero that he'd always been.
