# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue is a mainline Superhero Mandroid story centered on Paul Teethings becoming a protector for Mandroid City's transhuman community.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise Paul's transformation from feared monster into the transhuman community's protector.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "Guardian Demon". Smaller bottom caption: "Not a guardian angel. A guardian demon."
- Visual beats: Paul Teethings in torn priest clothing and full possessed form hovers inside the damaged transhuman hall, ragged wings spread protectively, ember-red eyes controlled, hellfire glowing behind his teeth. Behind him, frightened but hopeful transhumans with wearable tech, exoskeleton braces, and AI companion devices gather under flickering lights. In front of him, black-armored paramilitary attackers with rough white cross graffiti on their armor recoil from a wall of sulfur-yellow flame.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Beyond Human

- Story role: Establish Paul's conflicted joy in flight and his discovery that other people are choosing forms beyond ordinary humanity.
- Layout: Four large panels, moving from night sky to street-level observation.
- Caption boxes: "Paul Teethings hated the demon inside him." "But when his ragged wings caught the night air, he felt alive in a way his old human body never allowed." "Below him, people were changing by choice instead of possession." "AI companions, neural wearables, and exoskeletons were helping them call themselves transhuman."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Paul perched on a rooftop in Mandroid's City, torn clerical coat whipping in the wind, wings folded uneasily. Panel 2 shows him launching into flight with a reluctant expression of wonder. Panel 3 shows a city sidewalk below where augmented people use smart lenses, AI companion pins, powered leg braces, and neural devices in everyday life. Panel 4 shows Paul watching a discreet sign or phone notice for a transhuman meet-up, his ember eyes uncertain.

## Page 3: The Door Opens

- Story role: Show Paul's nervous arrival and the transhuman community's first reaction shifting from fear to curiosity.
- Layout: Four roomy panels inside the meet-up hall.
- Caption boxes: "Paul came to the meet-up afraid that even people beyond human would see only the demon." "At first, some of them did." "Then they saw his hands tremble, his wings stay folded, and his eyes ask permission instead of prey." "Their horror softened into amazement."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Transhuman Meet-Up Hall: converted warehouse community space with practical lights, folding chairs, repair tables, charging stations, posters about augmentation rights, and a mixed crowd of transhumans. Panel 2 shows Paul standing in the doorway in demonic form, smoke curling from his collar while several attendees step back. Panel 3 shows Leona Vale calmly raising a hand to steady the room while exoskeleton users and neural-augmented attendees watch. Panel 4 shows a closer moment of acceptance: attendees cautiously approach Paul, studying his wings and claws as controlled powers rather than a threat.

## Page 4: Monsters Before Heroes

- Story role: Let Leona connect Paul's fear of rejection to the history of Superhero Mandroid Version One and give Paul hope.
- Layout: Five panels, mostly conversation-through-caption with visual evidence.
- Caption boxes: "Dr. Leona Vale told Paul that many transhumans had been called monsters." "People feared freedom when it wore a body they did not understand." "She showed him old footage of Superhero Mandroid's Version One form." "The city had feared its greatest hero before it learned his heart." "For the first time since his possession, Paul's own heart felt hope."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Leona facing Paul with calm warmth, her neural filaments and collar AI device glowing softly. Panel 2 shows transhumans around them: a person with a nervous-system interface, another with powered arm braces, another with augmented-reality lenses. Panel 3 shows a projection or wall screen of Superhero Mandroid Version One as exposed green metal, intimidating but sad. Panel 4 shows a second image of the current beloved Superhero Mandroid in red suit and yellow cape, cheering civilians visible in the display. Panel 5 shows Paul quietly touched, wings lowering from defensive tension as Leona and the community accept him.

## Page 5: The Crossmarked Attack

- Story role: Break the peace with the paramilitary attack and establish the threat against transhumans.
- Layout: Five action panels with clear escalation.
- Caption boxes: "The doors burst inward before hope could settle." "Black armor filled the hall, scratched with white cross graffiti." "The attackers swung batons and called the augmented abominations." "Their leader claimed becoming more than human insulted divine order." "Leona sent the exoskeleton and nervous-system augmented forward while everyone else ran."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the hall doors crashing open under booted force. Panel 2 shows the Crossmarked Paramilitary Cell entering in matte black riot armor with opaque visors and rough white cross graffiti. Panel 3 shows attackers striking tables and shoving transhumans with batons, no gore. Panel 4 shows the armored leader pointing a baton while frightened augmented people scatter. Panel 5 shows Leona giving fast commands as exoskeleton users brace themselves and neural-augmented defenders move to block the attackers.

## Page 6: Demon In Control

- Story role: Show Paul's anger at the same religious rejection returning, then make his decisive choice to power up for protection.
- Layout: Four large panels for emotional clarity and impact.
- Caption boxes: "Paul knew that kind of judgment." "The same institution had stripped away his title and called him a deceiver." "The demon inside him begged to answer cruelty with cruelty." "Paul chose something harder: power without surrender."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Paul seeing frightened transhumans, his face flashing with recognition and pain. Panel 2 shows a brief visual echo of the cathedral tribunal in smoky reflection behind him, connecting to Issue #13 without changing location. Panel 3 shows his demon power swelling: ember-red throat, black claws visible, wings spreading, shadow smoke around his feet, but his posture remains controlled. Panel 4 shows Paul standing protectively before Leona and the transhuman community, wings forming a shield-like shape.

## Page 7: Wall Of Fire

- Story role: Deliver Paul's defensive action sequence: shadow speed, fire barrier, and disarming without surrendering to cruelty.
- Layout: Six action-forward panels with compact captions.
- Caption boxes: "Paul moved as a blur of shadow." "The armored line broke before him." "Fire rose as a barrier, not a sentence." "The next wave stumbled backward from the heat." "Wings carried Paul above the chaos." "Claws made for tearing chose to leave only dropped batons behind."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Paul streaking across the hall as a smoky blur. Panel 2 shows black-armored intruders recoiling and losing formation without anyone being struck. Panel 3 shows a sulfur-yellow wall of flame across the floor between attackers and civilians. Panel 4 shows paramilitary soldiers stumbling backward behind heat shimmer. Panel 5 shows Paul flying low over them on ragged wings. Panel 6 shows dropped batons on the floor while Paul lands protectively near Leona and the transhuman defenders as the attackers retreat.

## Page 8: Protector Paul

- Story role: Resolve with the attackers fleeing and the transhuman community recognizing Paul as their present-tense protector.
- Layout: Five panels ending on a symbolic hero image.
- Caption boxes: "The hall went quiet after the storm passed." "The people who had feared Paul now saw who he had chosen to protect." "Superhero Mandroid still showed the transhumans what acceptance might become someday." "Paul Teethings showed them who would stand guard until that day arrived." "Not a guardian angel. A guardian demon."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the damaged meet-up hall with fading smoke and warm light returning, no attackers visible. Panel 2 shows Paul humbled and controlled as Leona and the transhumans approach him with gratitude and awe instead of fear. Panel 3 shows a wall poster or screen of Superhero Mandroid in hopeful colors behind the group, tying the community's future to his public acceptance. Panel 4 shows Leona and augmented attendees gathering safely near Paul. Panel 5 final panel shows Paul silhouetted in the doorway of the damaged hall, wings forming a shield-like shape over the transhumans, ember eyes steady, caption naming him guardian demon.
