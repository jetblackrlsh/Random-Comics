# Character Bible

## Paul Teethings, Possessed Exorcist

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/paul-teethings-possessed-exorcist-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human man in his late 30s with light skin, hollow brown eyes, short dark hair receding at the temples, gaunt cheeks, trimmed beard, and an exhausted priestly posture. He wears torn black priest clothing with a white clerical collar, dark coat, plain black trousers, smoke-stained fabric, and a worn wooden rosary. His possessed form has charcoal-black cracked skin patches, ember-red glow in the throat and eyes, ragged demon wings, black clawed fingers, brimstone smoke, and sulfur-yellow firelight around his mouth when breathing hellfire.
- Issue continuity: Paul hates the demon inside him but loves the freedom of flight. He remains in control during this issue. He uses demonic wings, shadow-blur speed, claws, and hellfire to protect the transhuman community, not to take revenge. By the end, the community begins calling him Protector Paul.

## Dr. Leona Vale, Transhuman Organizer

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/leona-vale-transhuman-organizer-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: Black woman in her early 40s with warm brown skin, close-cropped silver hair, calm dark eyes, angular cheekbones, and a poised organizer's posture. She wears a tailored charcoal jacket over a matte white smart-fabric shirt, visible copper-and-blue neural interface filaments at the temples, transparent augmented-reality lenses, slim carbon-fiber forearm braces, and a small glowing AI companion device clipped near her collarbone.
- Issue continuity: Leona leads the transhuman meet-up with disciplined warmth. She believes people who go beyond human limits are often labeled monsters before society learns to understand them. She compares the community's future acceptance to how society learned to accept Superhero Mandroid after fearing his Version One design.

## Crossmarked Paramilitary Cell

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/crossmarked-paramilitary-cell-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: coordinated group of imposing paramilitary attackers in matte black riot armor, heavy helmets, opaque visors, reinforced gloves, tactical boots, and blunt metal batons. Their armor is defaced with rough white cross graffiti, scratched scripture-like marks, and hand-painted crusader-style symbols. They look organized, intimidating, and zealot-like without belonging to ordinary police or military.
- Issue continuity: The cell attacks the transhuman meet-up while claiming that attempts to become more than human insult divine order. They are frightened into retreat when Paul's demon form proves far more powerful than their armor and batons.

## Superhero Mandroid Version One

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-version-one-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: adult male android silhouette with an entirely exposed green cosmic-metal body from face to feet, heavy armor-like plating, dark seams, broad shoulders, athletic walking-tank build, oversized green mechanical rocket fists, no costume, no cape, no synthetic skin, no artificial hair, and no chest insignia. His face is humanoid but clearly metal, with glowing brown-green eyes.
- Issue continuity: Version One appears only as a projected image, poster, or memory reference during Leona's explanation. He represents the time before society learned to see Superhero Mandroid as a trusted hero.

## Current Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin on his face, slicked-back brown artificial hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists.
- Issue continuity: Current Superhero Mandroid appears as a hopeful public image on screens or posters, not as an active participant. He symbolizes the future acceptance Leona believes transhumans can earn.

## Issue-Specific Visual Motifs

- Transhuman augmentation: practical wearable technology, AI companion pins, neural temple filaments, augmented-reality glasses, carbon-fiber support braces, powered exoskeleton limbs, and visible medical-grade ports.
- Paul's controlled demon power: ember-red throat glow, ragged wings held protectively rather than predatory, smoky shadow blur, sulfur-yellow fire breath, and clawed hands disarming rather than mutilating attackers.
- Crossmarked attack imagery: black armor, rough white cross graffiti, batons, cracked floor tiles, panicked civilians, and flames used as barriers instead of gore.
