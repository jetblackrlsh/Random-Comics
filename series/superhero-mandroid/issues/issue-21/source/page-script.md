# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text, cover title text, labels, and any readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Announce Hydrohero's transformation from failing weapon to undersea ruler.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition with readable title text.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "God Queen of the Deep". Bottom caption: "Her creator wanted a weapon. The ocean offered a throne."
- Visual beats: Hydrohero Womandroid hovers underwater before the glowing towers of Atlantis, pearl-blue skin and blue-white armor bright against deep ocean light. Her cracked translucent chest water tank is wrapped in thinning silver web strands while fresh ocean water swirls inside. Roman, King of Atlantis, stands beside her with orange scales, green tunic, gold crown, and seaweed wings. Atlantean soldiers kneel or lower tridents in the background. Far above, a tiny polluted surface shoreline glows ominously green.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The Patch Evaporates

- Story role: Establish Hydrohero's failing tank, fear of Volta, and choice to run.
- Layout: Four roomy panels.
- Caption boxes: "Webwidow's web had saved Hydrohero Womandroid from the Backrooms." "Now the patch across her cracked water tank was evaporating." "Dr. Vera Volta could repair her, but repair would become another leash." "Hydrohero chose the ocean before her creator could choose for her."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero alone on a rooftop at night, cracked chest tank held by silver web strands. Panel 2 shows droplets leaking and webbing thinning as her tank level falls. Panel 3 shows a memory-like inset of Dr. Vera Volta in her teal lab coat reaching toward repair tools like restraints. Panel 4 shows Hydrohero blasting away from the city on water jets, scared but determined.

## Page 3: Cloud Refill

- Story role: Show Hydrohero nearly failing, then discovering she can sustain herself from the sky.
- Layout: Five panels with rising motion.
- Caption boxes: "Every second of flight spent the same water that kept her alive." "The ocean was close enough to see and too far to trust." "So Hydrohero climbed instead of falling." "Inside the clouds, the world itself poured into her empty core." "For the first time, survival did not belong to Volta."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero over dark coastline, tank dangerously low, water jets sputtering. Panel 2 shows her looking down at the distant ocean with panic in her bright aqua eyes. Panel 3 shows her angling upward into storm clouds on weak jets. Panel 4 shows mist and cloud water spiraling into the cracked tank through webbing. Panel 5 shows her tank refilled, eyes bright, diving like a blue comet toward the sea.

## Page 4: Intruder Beneath the Waves

- Story role: Discover Atlantis and prove Hydrohero's underwater power.
- Layout: Five action panels.
- Caption boxes: "The ocean did not drain her. It completed her." "A distant light shone where no surface diver had ever reached." "Hydrohero followed it into Atlantis." "The city's soldiers saw a surface machine and raised their tridents." "Underwater, her power had no ceiling."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero plunging into open ocean as water rushes into her tank. Panel 2 shows a glowing light far below, framed by abyssal darkness. Panel 3 reveals Atlantis with coral towers and shell-bronze domes. Panel 4 shows Atlantean fish-men soldiers surrounding her with tridents. Panel 5 shows Hydrohero blasting pressurized water beams and swirling jets, knocking soldiers back without blood or gore.

## Page 5: Roman's Welcome

- Story role: Introduce Roman, his apology, and Atlantis's reverence for Hydrohero.
- Layout: Four large panels, majestic and emotional.
- Caption boxes: "When the last trident fell, the ocean itself began to speak." "Roman, King of Atlantis, apologized through the vibrations of the sea." "He said no intruder could command water so completely." "To Atlantis, Hydrohero was not a pawn. She was a goddess."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows defeated soldiers recovering while Hydrohero takes a battle stance. Panel 2 shows Roman approaching calmly, slicked-back blonde hair with black highlights, green eyes, orange scaly skin, green royal tunic with gold trim, gold crown, pointed ears, and seaweed wings. Panel 3 shows visible vibration ripples carrying his message through the water, no speech bubble. Panel 4 shows Hydrohero stunned and honored as soldiers bow their heads.

## Page 6: Wonders and Wounds

- Story role: Tour Atlantis, reveal war preparations, and make Roman's grievance concrete.
- Layout: Four roomy panels with contrast between beauty and damage.
- Caption boxes: "Roman showed her magic that looked like science and science that moved like magic." "He also showed her soldiers drilling for war against the surface." "Then he showed her why Atlantis was angry." "Surface trash had turned whole neighborhoods into poisoned water."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero and Roman moving through luminous Atlantis plazas, coral towers, and science-magic pumps. Panel 2 shows fish-men soldiers performing disciplined trident drills in a royal training court. Panel 3 shows Roman leading Hydrohero into the polluted district. Panel 4 shows blackened water, chemical barrels, plastic trash, sick Atlantean citizens, and dead coral while Hydrohero looks horrified.

## Page 7: Both Hero and Goddess

- Story role: Hydrohero rejects both Volta's leash and Roman's war, choosing her own role.
- Layout: Four emotional panels ending with decisive action.
- Caption boxes: "The surface had called her villain and weapon." "Atlantis called her goddess, but even praise could become another command." "Hydrohero told Roman to call off the war." "She would be hero of the surface and goddess of the sea."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero looking upward toward a faint surface glow, torn between worlds. Panel 2 shows a symbolic split image: Volta's workshop on one side, Atlantis's throne on the other, both trying to define her. Panel 3 shows Hydrohero standing tall before Roman, cracked tank full, web patch still visible, water jets beginning to coil around her like a crown. Panel 4 shows Roman lowering his hand in agreement as Hydrohero unleashes controlled currents toward the polluted district.

## Page 8: The Surface Gets Its Mess Back

- Story role: Resolve Atlantis's crisis while setting up the radioactive-power consequence.
- Layout: Five panels ending with a cliffhanger image.
- Caption boxes: "Hydrohero pumped the poison out of Atlantis with limitless ocean power." "She carried the surface world's trash back to the surface world." "Atlantis breathed cleaner water, and its new God Queen stood beside Roman." "On the shore, people argued about the mess that had returned to them." "No one noticed the radioactive glow until the first impossible powers began."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero blasting massive currents through polluted Atlantis, pulling trash and chemicals away from homes. Panel 2 shows a towering water column erupting onto a surface beach, dumping barrels, plastic, sludge, and glowing green residue onto sand. Panel 3 shows Hydrohero and Roman overlooking cleaner Atlantis as citizens gather in cautious hope. Panel 4 shows angry beachgoers and emergency workers staring at the waste piles. Panel 5 shows green radioactive light touching a startled person and small shore animals as sparks, glowing eyes, and strange power effects begin.
