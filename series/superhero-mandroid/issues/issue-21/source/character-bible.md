## Recurring Character: Hydrohero Womandroid

Use the canonical Hydrohero Womandroid design: human-looking adult woman android with waterproof pearl-blue synthetic skin, short slick silver-blue hair, bright aqua eyes, athletic build, streamlined blue-and-white armored bodysuit, translucent chest water tank with visible swirling water, chrome shoulder turbines, wrist nozzles, and calf jet ports that spray controlled water streams. For this issue, her chest water tank is cracked and held together by thinning silver-white web strands from Webwidow; the patch looks fragile, damp, and slowly dissolving. Her early expression is afraid and defiant. Underwater she becomes stronger, calmer, and increasingly regal.

Hydrohero attacks and flies with pressurized water beams, water whips, wrist jets, and calf water jets. Her tank powers her body and weapons, so low water means shutdown. In clouds and ocean water she can refill and fight almost without limit.

## Recurring Character: Dr. Vera Volta

Dr. Vera Volta appears only as a feared possibility or memory image. She is a middle-aged woman with light olive skin, sharp cheekbones, intense dark eyes, chin-length black hair with a silver streak, angular black glasses, and a fitted teal waterproof lab coat over a graphite jumpsuit. Any image of Volta should feel like a controlling creator waiting to turn Hydrohero's repair into another command.

## Recurring Character: Detective Webwidow

Detective Webwidow does not appear in person, but his silver-white webbing is visible across Hydrohero's cracked tank. The web patch should read as temporary spider-hero technology: sticky, silvery, and evaporating under Hydrohero's water pressure.

## New Character: Roman, King of Atlantis

Roman is a regal adult Atlantean man with slicked-back blonde hair streaked with black highlights, green eyes, scaly orange skin, pointed elf-like ears, elegant green wings made of seaweed, a green royal tunic with golden metal trim, and a golden crown shaped like coral and wave crests. He communicates through the ocean by sending visible vibration ripples instead of ordinary speech bubbles. He begins majestic and apologetic, then reveals colder political anger when discussing the surface's pollution.

## New Character: Atlantean Fish-Men Soldiers

The Atlantean soldiers are a disciplined group of amphibious humanoid warriors with blue-green, teal, and orange-scaled skin, finned crests, webbed fingers, sharp wary eyes, plated shell-and-coral armor, kelp sashes, and long tridents made from bronze, bone, and polished sea stone. They are defensive guards, not villains. Their fear of the surface makes them attack first.

## New Setting: Atlantis, Lost City Beneath the Ocean

Atlantis is a vast hidden underwater city with glowing coral towers, shell-and-bronze domes, glasslike pressure chambers, luminous algae streets, ancient stone arches, science-magic pumps, pearl-lit plazas, and blue-green ocean light at impossible depth. It should feel wondrous, ancient, and alive.

## New Setting: Polluted Atlantis District

The polluted district is the damaged underside of Atlantis: blackened water, plastic trash, rusted barrels, oil sheen, broken nets, chemical drums, dead coral, corroded shell homes, dim emergency lamps, and sick Atlantean citizens. It should make Roman's anger understandable without making war feel justified.

## New Setting: Radioactive Waste Beach

The radioactive waste beach is an ordinary surface shoreline buried under what Hydrohero pumps out of Atlantis: trash heaps, leaking chemical barrels, glowing green radioactive residue, black sludge, emergency lights, wet sand, startled beachgoers, and early signs of strange powers in people and animals.
