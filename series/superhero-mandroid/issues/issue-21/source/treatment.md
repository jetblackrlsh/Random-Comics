# Treatment

## Title

God Queen of the Deep

## Issue Number

21

## Logline

With her cracked water tank failing and Dr. Vera Volta waiting to reclaim her, Hydrohero Womandroid dives into the ocean, discovers Atlantis, and chooses to become both a surface hero and the goddess of the sea.

## Central Conflict

Hydrohero Womandroid is dying because Webwidow's temporary web patch is evaporating from her cracked chest tank. Returning to Dr. Vera Volta would save her body but cost her freedom, since Volta would almost certainly repair her only to send her after Superhero Mandroid again. Hydrohero tries to save herself by flying toward the ocean, then finds Atlantis, a civilization that treats her power over water as divine. The acceptance tempts her away from the surface world, especially after Roman shows her how surface pollution has poisoned Atlanteans. Hydrohero must decide whether freedom means abandoning the surface, serving Atlantis's war, or defining her own kind of heroism.

## Theme

A created life becomes free when she chooses her purpose instead of merely changing masters.

## Ending

Hydrohero declares that she can be a hero of the surface and goddess of the sea. She convinces Roman to call off Atlantis's war, then uses limitless ocean power to pump trash and chemicals out of Atlantis and onto surface beaches. Atlantis is saved for now, and Hydrohero accepts her place as God Queen beside Roman. Unknown to her, radioactive waste on the shore begins changing people and animals into new powered beings.

## Emotional Arc

Hydrohero begins terrified of collapse and ashamed that survival might force her back to Dr. Vera Volta. The clouds and ocean teach her that she can sustain herself from the world instead of her creator. Atlantis's reverence gives her the acceptance she has never received on the surface, but Roman's war preparations test whether she will become another weapon in someone else's plan. By the end, Hydrohero chooses her own role, proud and self-directed, but her solution carries dangerous consequences she does not yet understand.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, failing web patch and refusal to return to Volta, desperate flight to the ocean through clouds, discovery of Atlantis and battle with soldiers, Roman's praise and tour, polluted Atlantis and the war plan, Hydrohero's choice to be both hero and goddess, the cleanup that saves Atlantis but contaminates the surface, and a final radioactive-power cliffhanger.

## New Continuity Elements

This issue introduces Roman, King of Atlantis; Atlantean fish-men soldiers; Atlantis as a hidden undersea civilization; the polluted Atlantis district; and the radioactive waste beach. It changes Hydrohero Womandroid's status from Volta's runaway weapon to the self-declared God Queen of Atlantis, establishes that she can indefinitely sustain herself through clouds and ocean water, and creates a new source of surface-level superpowers from radioactive pollution pushed out of Atlantis.
