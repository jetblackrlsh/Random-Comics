# Treatment

## Title

The Strongest Human

## Issue Number

7

## Logline

Superhero Mandroid investigates a supernatural energy spike in the park and discovers a human martial artist powerful enough to mistake pride for purpose.

## Central Conflict

Mandroid expects another disguised demon or portal breach, but the source of the energy is Master Liang Chen, a human martial artist whose chi has grown strong enough to lure the city's android protector into a forced duel. Mandroid wants to prevent collateral damage without rewarding senseless violence, while Liang wants proof that no one on Earth can challenge him except Mandroid.

## Theme

Strength without restraint becomes another kind of weakness.

## Ending

Liang's own missed chi blast destroys the park's largest tree, filling the air with splinters. Mandroid shields himself and civilians from the shrapnel while Liang tries to catch the fragments through sheer speed, injuring himself until he collapses. Mandroid carries him to the hospital and leaves unsettled by how powerful humans can become, and how far pride can push them.

## Emotional Arc

Mandroid begins suspicious, assuming supernatural energy means a demon in disguise. Liang reveals that the threat is not demonic but human arrogance sharpened into mystical force. Mandroid refuses to fight for spectacle, then grows annoyed when every attempt to disengage escalates the danger. His decisive choice is not a harder punch but a sideways evasion that lets Liang's own violence reveal its cost. By the end, Mandroid is compassionate toward the fallen fighter and newly humbled by human potential.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, energy spike, the park meditation, the sudden challenge, aerial escalation, redirected rocket fists, the sidestep and tree blast, and the hospital aftermath.
