# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

- Story role: Signal a human opponent whose chi feels like a portal threat.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image.
- Visual beats: In Mandroid City Central Park at dawn, Master Liang Chen stands calm in a charcoal tai chi jacket with golden-white chi coiling around his hands. Superhero Mandroid hovers opposite him on green rocket-fist exhaust with his yellow cape snapping, red suit and green fists clear, one eyebrow raised in suspicion. The huge old tree looms behind them.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "The Strongest Human".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.
