# Page Script

## General Page Rules

- Full realistic candid photo-comic pages, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, or logos.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art by `image_gen`.
- Use realistic documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Keep recurring character designs consistent with `source/character-bible.md` and the series reference images.

## Page 1: Cover

Story role: Establish the Mandroid Men as a new team forming against Blightlord in the Green Zones.

Cover composition: Large dramatic team shot on a glowing radioactive beach at dusk. Superhero Mandroid stands front-center with cyan-green holo shield raised and green rocket fists ready. Around him are Hydrohero Womandroid with water jets, Aurelia with green mystical flame, Detective Webwidow aiming web shooters, Roman with Atlantean strength pose, Master Liang Chen with chi-lit fists, GAL-XZ glowing with Norio energy, and Protector Paul with wings and rosary. Blightlord towers behind them as a pollution demon made of oil, radioactive sludge, and rusted barrels.

Readable cover text to generate in the art:
- Title: "Mandroid Men Origins"
- Small caption: "Issue 22"
