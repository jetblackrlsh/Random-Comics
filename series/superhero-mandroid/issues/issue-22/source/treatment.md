# Mandroid Men Origins

## Issue Number

22

## Logline

After radioactive waste creates the Green Zones, Superhero Mandroid and Earth's greatest heroes unite against Blightlord, the Pollution Demon, and become the Mandroid Men.

## Central Conflict

Superhero Mandroid faces a disaster he cannot solve with rocket fists: whole coastlines have become irradiated mutation zones after Hydrohero Womandroid's attempt to cleanse Atlantis. Dr. Elias Dawes can diagnose the problem but cannot cure fully grown mutants with current science, forcing Mandroid to rely on Aurelia's magic. Aurelia discovers that the Green Zones have empowered Blightlord, a pollution demon whose mystical connection to the poisoned land keeps the disaster alive.

## Theme

Heroism becomes bigger than one champion when the problem is bigger than one power set. The issue turns Mandroid's long history of individual protector-duty into the first shared mission of a true superhero team.

## Ending

Aurelia seals Blightlord after Mandroid, Webwidow, Roman, Master Liang Chen, GAL-XZ, Hydrohero, and Protector Paul each weaken the demon in a different way. The Green Zones begin healing, but not everything returns to normal. The remaining mutants and monsters give the newly formed Mandroid Men a standing mission.

## Emotional Arc

Mandroid begins frustrated that the Green Zones cannot be punched into safety. Aurelia begins afraid that her magic is not enough. The final battle proves that neither of them has to be enough alone. The team forms because each hero's limitation is covered by another hero's strength.

## Page Count

8 pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Continuity Notes

- Directly follows Issue #21's radioactive-waste beach ending.
- Introduces the Green Zones as quarantined coastal mutation areas caused by radioactive ocean pollution.
- Introduces Blightlord, the Pollution Demon, as the infernal force empowered by the Green Zones.
- Forms the Mandroid Men from Earth's greatest heroes: Superhero Mandroid, Hydrohero Womandroid, Aurelia, Detective Webwidow, Roman, Master Liang Chen, GAL-XZ, and Paul Teethings as Protector Paul.
- Leaves some mutants and monsters active after the Green Zones begin healing, giving the new team an ongoing peacekeeping role.
