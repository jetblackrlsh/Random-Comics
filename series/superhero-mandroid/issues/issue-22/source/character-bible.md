# Character Bible

## Recurring Characters

### Superhero Mandroid

- Source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: human-looking adult man with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized green mechanical rocket fists. Uses cyan-green hardlight holo shields. Flies only with green-white jets from his rocket fists.

### Dr. Elias Dawes

- Source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/inventor-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: older light-skinned man in his early 60s with gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, dark lab coat, blue shirt, and a key card lanyard.

### Hydrohero Womandroid

- Source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-womandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-water-core-key-item.png`
- Visible identifiers: human-looking adult woman android with waterproof pearl-blue synthetic skin, slick silver-blue hair, aqua eyes, streamlined blue-and-white armored bodysuit, translucent chest water tank, chrome shoulder turbines, wrist nozzles, and water-jet propulsion. In this issue she is treated as God Queen of Atlantis.

### Aurelia, Daughter of Satan and Lilith

- Source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/aurelia-daughter-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: young seemingly human woman with fair skin, shoulder-length blonde hair spilling from a white hood, green eyes, small emerald diamond in the center of her forehead, and a simple white hooded dress. Her eyes, hands, and body glow with green mystical flames when using magic.

### Master Liang Chen

- Source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`

### GAL-XZ

- Source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`

- Source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
