# Character Bible

## Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rechargeable-holo-shield-key-item.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/regenerating-rocket-fists-key-item.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin on his face, slicked-back brown artificial hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.
- Issue continuity: He begins the issue hoping the chaos might be the final portal and ends it wishing portals will continue because they mean the living story still has imagination. His right rocket fist is briefly erased by the Reality Drafting Compass and then redrawn from sketch lines into solid green metal. He flies only by green-white exhaust from his green rocket fists, never by boot jets, wings, back thrusters, or a jet pack.

## The Sketchmaker

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/sketchmaker-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: adult male stick-figure creator rendered as a living pencil sketch in a photoreal city, with a round simple head, black graphite line body, sketched hands, faint paper-fiber texture inside the lines, expressive dot eyes, a small worried mouth, and loose graphite smudges that drift from him when his confidence fails.
- Issue continuity: The Sketchmaker is not a conventional portal enemy. His imagination, focused through the Reality Drafting Compass, has been creating the portals, monsters, heroes, city, and even Mandroid's world. When inspiration fails, the compass defaults to erasing unfinished reality. Reading comics with Mandroid restores his creative confidence enough to redraw the city and continue the story.

## The Newly Drawn Woman

- Issue-only role: a temporary living person created by the Sketchmaker in Draw Mode and almost erased when the compass slips back into Erase Mode.
- Visual identifiers: adult woman forming from blue-white drafting lines into a believable real person, casual city clothes, startled expression, edges briefly visible as pencil strokes before becoming solid.
- Continuity notes: She demonstrates that the Reality Drafting Compass can create living people as well as erase them. She is restored with the city by the end but is not established as a recurring character.

## Issue-Specific Visual Motifs

- White void: a blank matte-white nothingness eating the edges of streets, buildings, vehicles, and people.
- Eraser shavings: giant curled gray-white eraser crumbs blowing through the city like storm debris.
- Draw Mode: blue-white drafting lines, glowing pencil strokes, clean construction marks, and living ink-like geometry that becomes solid reality.
- Erase Mode: gray-white static, dusty graphite fragments, softened edges, and people or buildings reverting into loose pencil marks before fading.
- Half-erased library: a damaged city library with shelves, books, walls, and ceiling corners partly solid and partly rough pencil sketches, used as the place where stories restore the Sketchmaker's inspiration.
