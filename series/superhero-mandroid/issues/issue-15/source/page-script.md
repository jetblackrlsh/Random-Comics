# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue is a mainline Superhero Mandroid story about the creator behind the portals and the imagination that keeps Mandroid's world alive.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise the impossible reveal that Mandroid has found the creator behind the portals while the city is being erased.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "The Last Portal". Smaller bottom caption: "The world was running out of ideas."
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid rockets through a city dissolving into white void, flying only by green-white exhaust from his oversized green rocket fists. Giant eraser shavings whip through the air. Below him, the pencil-drawn Sketchmaker stands beneath the glowing Reality Drafting Compass, whose circular dial splits between blue-white creation light and gray-white eraser static. Buildings behind them are half photoreal and half pencil sketch.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The City Comes Undone

- Story role: Establish the erasure disaster and Mandroid's hope that this could be the final portal.
- Layout: Four large panels across a collapsing city street and skyline.
- Caption boxes: "Giant eraser shavings blew through Mandroid's City." "People and buildings became sketches before the white void took them." "Superhero Mandroid had fought portals for so long that one thought still tempted him." "Maybe this was the last portal. Maybe one final victory could make the world safe."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a city avenue with curled eraser shavings tumbling like storm debris. Panel 2 shows building corners turning into graphite outlines and dissolving into blank white. Panel 3 shows civilians half-real and half-sketch fleeing while Mandroid shields them with one arm. Panel 4 shows Mandroid launching above the street on bright green rocket-fist exhaust toward the epicenter, exhausted but determined.

## Page 3: No Portal Today

- Story role: Reveal the Sketchmaker and the Reality Drafting Compass instead of a portal.
- Layout: Five panels moving from aerial search to uncanny discovery.
- Caption boxes: "At the center of the damage, there was no portal." "There was a man drawn in pencil." "Above him floated a compass that did not measure distance." "It measured whether imagination would build or erase." "\"Oh hey, hero,\" the stick figure said. \"I was hoping you'd come by.\""
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid hovering over the fading city, scanning for portal light. Panel 2 reveals the Sketchmaker standing alone in a circular patch of blank white pavement. Panel 3 is a clear view of the Reality Drafting Compass: floating circular impossible artist's compass, rotating inner dial, glowing sketch-line frame, half blue-white Draw Mode, half gray-white Erase Mode. Panel 4 shows Mandroid landing with rocket fists ready, confused by the absence of enemies. Panel 5 shows the Sketchmaker giving a weak nervous smile beneath the hovering compass.

## Page 4: Drawn, Then Almost Gone

- Story role: Demonstrate the compass creating life and immediately endangering it.
- Layout: Four roomy panels with clean cause and effect.
- Caption boxes: "The Sketchmaker lifted one graphite hand." "The compass clicked into Draw Mode." "A woman blinked into life one careful line at a time." "Then the dial slipped, and Erase Mode reached for her."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Sketchmaker raising a sketched hand toward the compass. Panel 2 shows blue-white drafting lines pouring from the compass like living ink. Panel 3 shows a newly drawn woman forming from pencil strokes into solid flesh and casual city clothes, startled and breathing. Panel 4 shows gray-white eraser static washing toward her as eraser shavings begin to fall, with Mandroid moving into action.

## Page 5: The Wrong Victory

- Story role: Mandroid attacks the compass, stops the erasure briefly, and learns the Sketchmaker created the portals.
- Layout: Five action-and-reveal panels.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid fired a rocket fist before the woman could fade." "The punch did not break the compass." "It knocked the dial back to Draw Mode." "\"No portals today,\" the Sketchmaker said. \"I can't make them when I'm not inspired.\"" "\"You made the portals?\" Mandroid asked."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid launching a green mechanical rocket fist at the compass, green thruster trail bright. Panel 2 shows the fist striking the rotating dial with sparks of pencil light, not damaging the device. Panel 3 shows gray static disappearing from the woman as the compass locks blue-white again. Panel 4 shows Mandroid standing between the woman and the Sketchmaker, shocked and angry. Panel 5 shows the Sketchmaker nodding proudly but sadly while the fading city and white void surround them.

## Page 6: Erased Fist, Redrawn Hand

- Story role: Make Mandroid feel the stakes physically and show creation is as real as destruction.
- Layout: Four large panels focused on Mandroid's body and the compass.
- Caption boxes: "\"I'm glad the portals could end,\" Mandroid said, \"but I cannot let you erase my world.\"" "The compass tried to erase him instead." "Mandroid's rocket fist vanished before it could return." "\"I'm trying to remember how to keep drawing it,\" the Sketchmaker said."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid bracing as gray-white Erase Mode spreads over the ground and turns pavement into loose sketch marks. Panel 2 shows him firing another rocket fist into the compass dial. Panel 3 shows the fist dissolving into graphite dust and blank space, leaving Mandroid staring at a missing green mechanical hand. Panel 4 shows blue-white Draw Mode wrapping his arm in construction lines as a replacement rocket fist forms from pencil strokes into solid green metal.

## Page 7: The Library Of Unfinished Worlds

- Story role: Explain the world rule and give Mandroid a nonviolent solution.
- Layout: Five panels moving from existential reveal to quiet action.
- Caption boxes: "The Sketchmaker finally told him the truth." "The portals, the monsters, the city, and Mandroid himself lived inside a story." "When imagination went empty, unfinished thoughts became eraser dust." "Mandroid found a half-erased library nearby." "Together, hero and maker read until the hand that drew the world stopped shaking."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Sketchmaker beneath the compass, small and frightened amid blank void and half-erased streets. Panel 2 shows ghostly blue-white sketches of past portals, monsters, and Mandroid orbiting the compass as evidence of imagination. Panel 3 shows the compass flickering dangerously between modes. Panel 4 shows Mandroid leading the Sketchmaker into a library whose walls, shelves, and books are half solid and half pencil outline. Panel 5 shows them sitting among damaged shelves reading comics, with the compass glowing steadier blue-white above the table.

## Page 8: Never The Last Portal

- Story role: Restore the city and resolve Mandroid's new understanding of his role.
- Layout: Five panels ending on a hopeful portal in the sky.
- Caption boxes: "The eraser shavings stopped falling." "People returned as sketches, then color, then living flesh." "The Sketchmaker drew windows, streets, homes, clouds, memories, and hope." "Then a new portal opened above the city." "This time, Mandroid wished the portals would never end."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Reality Drafting Compass shining in pure blue-white Draw Mode above the Sketchmaker. Panel 2 shows half-erased civilians becoming solid again from sketch lines. Panel 3 shows buildings redrawing themselves from the foundation upward while the white void retreats like fog. Panel 4 shows the restored city skyline with a fresh glowing portal opening in the sky. Panel 5 final panel shows Superhero Mandroid looking upward calmly, green rocket fists whole, yellow cape lifting in the wind, no longer exhausted but ready.
