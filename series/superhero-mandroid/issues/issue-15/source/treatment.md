# Treatment

## Title

The Last Portal

## Issue Number

15

## Continuity Label

Mainline. Superhero Mandroid discovers that the portals, the monsters, the city, and even his own heroic life are sustained by an anxious creator figure whose Reality Drafting Compass begins erasing the world whenever imagination runs dry.

## Logline

When Mandroid races toward what he thinks is the final portal, he finds the stick-figure creator whose fading imagination is erasing reality, forcing the hero to save the world by helping his maker remember why stories matter.

## Central Conflict

Superhero Mandroid has always hoped the portal wars could end after one last victory. When the city begins dissolving into pencil marks and white void, he assumes the final portal has arrived. Instead, the destruction comes from the Sketchmaker, a pencil-drawn creator figure whose Reality Drafting Compass turns inspiration into living reality and turns creative exhaustion into erasure. Mandroid must confront the impossible truth that the danger and the world he protects share the same source: imagination. He cannot punch the problem away without destroying the mechanism that can also restore his world.

## Theme

A hero's purpose is not to end every story, but to help the next good story survive.

## Ending

Mandroid leads the Sketchmaker into a half-erased library, where reading comics steadies the creator's imagination. The Reality Drafting Compass returns fully to Draw Mode, redrawing people, buildings, memories, and hope from sketch lines into life. When a new portal opens after the city is restored, Mandroid no longer wishes for the portals to end. He understands that danger, imagination, and heroism are linked, and he looks up wishing the stories will keep coming.

## Emotional Arc

Mandroid begins exhausted by endless portal battles and secretly relieved by the thought that this could be the final one. He becomes disturbed when he learns the portals are caused by the same imagination that sustains his world. Losing a rocket fist to erasure humbles him, and seeing it redrawn teaches him that creation and danger come from the same fragile source. By the end, he chooses responsibility over escape and embraces the continuing work of being a hero inside an unfinished living story.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, the white-void collapse, Mandroid finding the Sketchmaker and the Reality Drafting Compass, the attempted rescue of a newly drawn woman, Mandroid learning the Sketchmaker created the portals, the erasure of Mandroid's rocket fist and its restoration, the explanation that imagination sustains the world, the half-erased library restoring inspiration, and the rebuilt city with a new portal Mandroid now welcomes.
