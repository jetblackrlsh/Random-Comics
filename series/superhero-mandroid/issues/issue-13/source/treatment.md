# Treatment

## Title

The Brimstone Confession

## Issue Number

13

## Continuity Label

Mainline. This issue introduces Paul Teethings, a disgraced regional exorcist transformed by a portal demon who chooses a dangerous antihero mission instead of surrendering to possession.

## Logline

After the church silences an exorcist for calling portal invaders demons, betrayal leaves him vulnerable to possession, forcing Superhero Mandroid to stop his revenge before the fallen priest decides what kind of monster he will become.

## Central Conflict

Paul Teethings believes his duty requires truth, but his hierarchy values silence. When he publicly connects portal creatures to demonic possession, his superiors strip him of authority and brand him a deceiver. The humiliation blackens his faith just as a portal opens, merging him with a demon powerful enough to make his former tools useless. His rage sends him against the men who condemned him. Superhero Mandroid cannot solve the theological fight, but he can stop murder long enough for Paul to remember the servant of God he meant to be.

## Theme

Truth without humility can become rage, but betrayal does not have to decide what a person becomes.

## Ending

Superhero Mandroid blocks possessed Paul's attack on the praying exorcists. Paul escapes under a cloud of brimstone dust, then hides in an alley where revenge and duty fight inside him. Mandroid's example reminds him that a title does not create a hero. Paul swears to God that he will use his demonic gifts to protect people and speak the truth, even if the church that trained him no longer accepts him.

## Emotional Arc

Paul begins as a devout servant who thinks honesty and faith are inseparable. He becomes publicly condemned by the institution he served, falls into rage, and nearly becomes the monster his enemies named him. Superhero Mandroid's intervention does not redeem Paul by force; it gives him one clear image of heroic restraint. Paul ends wounded, altered, and unresolved, but he chooses service over revenge.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Paul's ordinary exorcist duty before the portals, the post-portal change and church silence, Paul's viral confession and punishment, his portal possession, his attack on the tribunal, Mandroid's intervention and Paul's escape, and Paul's alley vow to use hellfire for protection rather than revenge.
