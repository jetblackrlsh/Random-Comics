# Character Bible

## Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rechargeable-holo-shield-key-item.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/regenerating-rocket-fists-key-item.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin on his face, slicked-back brown artificial hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.
- Issue continuity: Mandroid is not part of the church dispute and does not claim to settle it. He intervenes only when Paul is about to maim or kill praying exorcists. He flies only by green-white exhaust from his green rocket fists, never by boot jets, wings, back thrusters, or a jet pack. His cyan-green holo shield blocks Paul's claw strike.

## Paul Teethings, Possessed Exorcist

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/paul-teethings-possessed-exorcist-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human man in his late 30s with light skin, hollow brown eyes, short dark hair receding at the temples, gaunt cheeks, trimmed beard, and an exhausted priestly posture. In ordinary form he wears a black clerical shirt with white collar, dark coat, plain black trousers, and a worn wooden rosary. After portal possession he keeps the torn priest clothing but gains charcoal-black cracked skin patches, ember-red glow in the throat and eyes, ragged demon wings, black clawed fingers, smoke-stained collar, and sulfur-yellow flame light around his mouth when breathing hellfire.
- Issue continuity: Paul knows formal exorcism methods because he spent his life training in them. His title is revoked after he publicly says portal invaders are demons. The demonic possession gives him flight, claws, brimstone dust breath, and hellfire breath, but prayer weakens him. By the end, he refuses to let the demon own him and vows to protect people with the gifts forced on him.

## Exorcist Superiors

- Series source: issue-specific group, no series reference image.
- Visual identifiers: senior clergy and regional exorcist officials in black cassocks, white collars, formal coats, and small cross pins; older men and women with stern faces, folded hands, prayer books, silver crosses, and holy water vials.
- Issue continuity: They silence Paul, revoke his title, and later survive his attack because their prayers weaken him long enough for Superhero Mandroid to intervene.
