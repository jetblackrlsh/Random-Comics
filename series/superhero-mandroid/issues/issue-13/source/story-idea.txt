Create the latest issue of Superhero Mandroid from this premise:

Regional exorcists belong to a hierarchy, and those above them can revoke a title when someone steps out of line. Paul Teethings dedicated his life to the church, trained in exorcism to free people from demons, and believed truth was part of that mission. Before the portals opened, possessions looked familiar: thrashing, voices, demonic tongues, holy water, crosses, and scripture. After the portals opened, the possessed began becoming literal monsters, but the church refused to acknowledge the change while Superhero Mandroid fought similar creatures on the news.

Paul goes on social media and says the quiet part out loud: the alien invaders coming through the portals are demons. His statement goes viral, then his superiors strip him of his title and label him a deceiver. The betrayal weakens his faith and leaves him vulnerable. A portal opens where he stands, merging him with a monster. In a demonic rage, he flies to attack his superiors, counters their exorcist tools, and burns them with hellfire breath. Their prayers begin to weaken him, but before he can claw them down, Superhero Mandroid blocks the attack with his holo shield. Paul does not want trouble with Mandroid, so he coughs out brimstone dust and escapes.

In a dark alley, the possessed exorcist is torn between revenge and duty. Seeing Superhero Mandroid reminds him of the person he tried to be. Title or no title, he refuses to let the demon own him. He swears to God that he will use his new gifts to protect people and spread the truth the church tried to suppress.
