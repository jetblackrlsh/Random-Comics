# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue is a mainline Superhero Mandroid story introducing Paul Teethings, the possessed exorcist.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce the fallen exorcist's split identity and Mandroid's role as the shield between revenge and restraint.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "The Brimstone Confession". Smaller bottom caption: "A silenced exorcist becomes the monster he warned about."
- Visual beats: Paul Teethings stands in torn priest clothing with ragged demon wings spread inside a smoky cathedral courtyard, one half of his face still human and wounded, the other cracked with ember-red possession. Superhero Mandroid lands in front of praying exorcists with a cyan-green holo shield raised, red suit and yellow cape bright against brimstone smoke. A violet portal glows behind Paul like a wound in the air.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Before The Portals

- Story role: Establish Paul as a sincere servant trained for familiar exorcisms before the world changed.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: "Before the portals, Father Paul Teethings knew what possession looked like." "There was thrashing, strange voices, and tongues that made the candles shake." "Scripture, a cross, and holy water could still bring a soul back." "Paul trained for that work because he wanted people free."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Paul in black clerical shirt and worn coat kneeling beside a frightened family in a modest bedroom. Panel 2 shows a possessed patient restrained by relatives, candles flickering but no gore. Panel 3 shows Paul's hand holding a wooden cross, open Bible, and vial of holy water. Panel 4 shows the exhausted patient calm again while Paul bows his head in relief.

## Page 3: The Possessions Changed

- Story role: Show the post-portal horror and the church's refusal to name it.
- Layout: Four roomy panels with documentary news and cathedral contrast.
- Caption boxes: "Then portals opened above the city." "The possessed stopped sounding like suffering people." "They started becoming literal monsters." "Everyone saw Superhero Mandroid fight the same beasts on the news, but the church offered only silence."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a violet-blue portal tearing open over Mandroid's City. Panel 2 shows Paul recoiling as a possessed human's silhouette stretches into a non-gory horned monster shape under portal light. Panel 3 shows a television news shot of Superhero Mandroid fighting a portal creature, with bystanders watching. Panel 4 shows Paul standing alone in a cathedral corridor while senior exorcists walk past him without answering.

## Page 4: The Quiet Part

- Story role: Turn Paul's conviction into public defiance and institutional punishment.
- Layout: Five panels mixing phone screens, social media, and tribunal space.
- Caption boxes: "\"Secrets are tools of the devil,\" Paul told himself." "So he said the quiet part out loud." "\"The invaders coming through the portals are demons.\"" "The message went viral before it vanished." "His superiors stripped his title and called him the deceiver."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 close on Paul recording a grim social media video in a bare rectory room, cross on the wall behind him. Panel 2 shows a phone screen with the short quote readable enough to imply the viral claim. Panel 3 shows blurred feeds and notifications multiplying around the image of Paul. Panel 4 shows stern exorcist superiors in the cathedral tribunal complex presenting a revocation document. Panel 5 shows Paul alone on the stone steps, collar loosened, holding the document with shaking hands.

## Page 5: The Portal In His Shadow

- Story role: Transform betrayal into vulnerability and possession.
- Layout: Four large panels, escalating from grief to body horror without gore.
- Caption boxes: "The betrayal blackened his heart and dulled his faith." "That was enough of an opening." "A portal split the air where he stood." "Something inside it merged with the man who had spent his life fighting demons."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Paul standing in an empty side courtyard at dusk, hunched under rain and cathedral shadows. Panel 2 shows violet portal light opening behind him at ground level. Panel 3 shows black smoke and ember-red energy wrapping his clerical coat, rosary, hands, and throat. Panel 4 shows Paul transformed: torn priest clothing, cracked charcoal skin patches, glowing ember eyes, ragged wings, black claws, and sulfur fire leaking from his mouth.

## Page 6: Hellfire Against The Hierarchy

- Story role: Show possessed Paul attacking his former superiors and countering the tools he once used.
- Layout: Five action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Rage gave the demon wings." "Paul knew every tool his superiors reached for." "Holy water became steam." "The cross could not stop claws that knew how it would be raised." "But prayer reached where tools could not."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows possessed Paul flying over the cathedral courtyard on ragged demon wings toward the tribunal officials. Panel 2 shows an exorcist raising a cross and holy water vial from the regional exorcist kit. Panel 3 shows Paul breathing yellow-orange hellfire that vaporizes the holy water midair and scorches the stone near their feet. Panel 4 shows him sweeping aside a silver cross with black claws. Panel 5 shows the superiors kneeling and praying together while Paul staggers, ember cracks dimming.

## Page 7: The Shield Between

- Story role: Bring Superhero Mandroid into the fight, saving the exorcists without destroying Paul.
- Layout: Four large panels with a clear intervention.
- Caption boxes: "Paul lunged to silence the prayers." "Superhero Mandroid arrived between rage and blood." "His holo shield caught the claws meant for the men who condemned Paul." "\"I have no quarrel with you,\" Paul forced through smoke. Then he vanished in brimstone."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Paul's claws arcing down toward the praying exorcists. Panel 2 shows Superhero Mandroid flying in only on green-white exhaust from his oversized rocket fists, yellow cape whipping, cyan-green holo shield already forming. Panel 3 shows the holo shield blocking Paul's claws in a burst of sparks and hardlight, Mandroid's face focused but not hateful. Panel 4 shows Paul coughing a thick sulfur-gray cloud of brimstone dust around the courtyard, hiding his escape while Mandroid shields the coughing exorcists.

## Page 8: Title Or No Title

- Story role: Resolve Paul's inner choice and set his new role.
- Layout: Five panels ending with a solemn vow.
- Caption boxes: "In a dark alley, the demon asked for revenge." "The servant Paul had been answered with duty." "Mandroid's shield had shown him another way to be strong." "\"Title or no title, I still belong to God.\"" "\"I will protect people. I will speak the truth. And this demon will not own me.\""
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Paul crouched in a narrow alley, wings folded, rain turning brimstone dust to black paste on the pavement. Panel 2 shows his reflection in a broken puddle: half priest, half demon. Panel 3 shows a memory-like sliver of Superhero Mandroid holding the holo shield in front of the praying exorcists. Panel 4 shows Paul gripping his rosary with clawed fingers, ember glow dimming as his human expression returns. Panel 5 final panel shows Paul standing under alley light, ragged wings tucked, hellfire glowing controlled behind his teeth, one hand on the rosary, looking toward the city with grim purpose.
