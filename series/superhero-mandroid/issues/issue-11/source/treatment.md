# Treatment

## Title

What If? Mandroid Failed

## Issue Number

11

## Continuity Label

Alternate Universe "What If" story. This issue is framed by Detective Webwidow as a multiverse tale and does not replace the main Superhero Mandroid continuity.

## Logline

In an alternate universe where Superhero Mandroid misses the fatberg documentary and fails against the shutdown sludge, Dr. Vera Volta builds a water-powered Hydrohero Womandroid who turns the sky itself into the city's salvation.

## Central Conflict

The city faces the same purple shutdown sludge creature from Issue #2, but one small difference changes everything: Superhero Mandroid never learns that fatbergs can destroy it. When he is consumed and the power plant falls, the city loses power, supplies, safety, and hope. Master Liang Chen proves that water can hurt the monster but dies before he can deliver enough of it. Dr. Vera Volta must stop treating heroism as a rivalry and build a replacement protector before the trapped city starves in the dark.

## Theme

Tiny differences can doom a world, but failure can also force a rival to become the hero the world still needs.

## Ending

Hydrohero Womandroid nearly fails when her water tank runs low, but she escapes into the clouds and discovers she can absorb atmospheric water. Recharged by the sky, she floods the sludge creature from above, restores the city power grid, and becomes the greatest hero of that alternate world.

## Emotional Arc

Webwidow begins as a wry multiverse narrator who understands that nearly identical worlds can break from one missing choice. Superhero Mandroid falls quickly, turning his usual tactical confidence into a tragic absence. Master Liang Chen moves from prideful challenge-seeking to desperate public defense, then dies proving the monster's weakness. Dr. Dawes is hollowed out by grief, while Dr. Volta turns rivalry into invention and accepts the burden of building a hero instead of merely defeating one.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: alternate-universe cover, Webwidow's multiverse framing, Mandroid's missed documentary and fatal failure at the power plant, Liang discovering water hurts the sludge, Liang's death and the city's sealed survival crisis, Volta finding Dawes broken and choosing to build, Hydrohero battling until her tank runs low, and Hydrohero recharging in the clouds to purge the sludge and become the AU city's hero.
