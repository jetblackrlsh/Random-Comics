# Character Bible

## Detective Webwidow

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/detective-webwidow-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human man in his late 30s with light-to-medium skin, narrow angular face, tired sharp eyes, dark stubble, dark spider-web tattoo spreading across one side of his face from temple to cheekbone, weathered dark detective fedora, long charcoal-brown duster, dark shirt and vest, practical trousers, worn boots, compact wrist web shooters, and a large black widow emblem with a red hourglass mark on the back of his duster.
- Issue continuity: Frames this as an alternate-universe "What If" story while swinging between glowing multiverse web strands. He is the narrator, not the main combatant.

## Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/regenerating-rocket-fists-key-item.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rechargeable-holo-shield-key-item.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents. He flies only with green-white rocket exhaust from the wrist/fist vents, never boots or back thrusters.
- Issue continuity: In this alternate universe, he patrols despite no portal alarm, misses the fatberg documentary that saved the main timeline, fails to solve the shutdown sludge rule, and is consumed by the creature before the power plant falls.

## Shutdown Sludge Creature

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/shutdown-sludge-creature-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: massive purple sludge monster with glossy tar-like body, rippling gelatinous folds, wet violet-black highlights, bulbous pseudopods, faint alien eyes or glowing cores under the surface, and a trail of dead electronics and darkened screens wherever it flows.
- Issue continuity: Same threat type as Issue #2, but in this alternate universe it consumes Superhero Mandroid, the power plant, Master Liang Chen, and the city perimeter. Water makes it recoil and eventually destroys it when Hydrohero Womandroid weaponizes the clouds.

## Master Liang Chen

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/master-liang-chen-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human man in his late 40s, East Asian, lean powerful build, shaved head with faint stubble, calm severe dark eyes, angular weathered face, faint scar through one eyebrow, plain charcoal tai chi jacket with frog closures, loose black training pants, flat black martial arts shoes, simple red cord bracelet, and golden-white chi aura around his hands, shoulders, feet, and strike paths.
- Issue continuity: In this alternate universe, his search for a challenge becomes an emergency defense of the city. He discovers that water hurts the sludge by bursting a hydrant with chi, then dies when a sludge tendril wraps around his head before he reaches another water source.

## Dr. Vera Volta

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rival-inventor-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: middle-aged woman in her late 40s with light olive skin, sharp cheekbones, intense dark eyes, chin-length black hair with a silver streak, angular black glasses, and a fitted teal waterproof lab coat over a graphite jumpsuit.
- Issue continuity: In this alternate universe, desperation overrides rivalry. She challenges Dr. Dawes's grief, builds a redesigned Hydrohero Womandroid without city power, and becomes the architect of the city's new hero.

## Dr. Elias Dawes

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/inventor-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: older human man in his early 60s with light skin, gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, practical dark lab coat over a blue shirt, and a key card on a lanyard when working near the lab.
- Issue continuity: In this alternate universe, Mandroid's death leaves him a broken hollow shell in the dark hidden lab. He cannot bring himself to build another Mandroid, but Volta turns his despair into a challenge.

## Hydrohero Womandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-womandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/hydrohero-water-core-key-item.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult woman android with waterproof pearl-blue synthetic skin, short slick silver-blue hair, bright aqua eyes, athletic build, streamlined blue-and-white armored bodysuit, translucent chest water tank with visible swirling water, chrome shoulder turbines, wrist nozzles, and calf jet ports that spray controlled water streams.
- Issue continuity: In this alternate universe, Volta redesigns Hydrohero to be powered by water and to weaponize water against the sludge. Her tank initially drains during combat, but she learns to recharge by absorbing water from clouds and becomes the city's greatest hero.
