# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue is an alternate-universe "What If" story narrated by Detective Webwidow.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Clearly label the issue as an alternate-universe "What If" story and preview the tragic citywide stakes plus Hydrohero's eventual rise.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition with layered multiverse-web framing.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "What If? Mandroid Failed". Smaller corner caption: "An Alternate Universe Story".
- Visual beats: Detective Webwidow swings across the top edge on silvery multiverse web strands, his fedora and duster visible against glowing portal-web light. Below him, Superhero Mandroid sinks into a towering purple sludge creature near the city power plant, one green rocket fist half-submerged and his yellow cape disappearing. In the stormy sky above the dark city, Hydrohero Womandroid rises with blue-white water light around her chest tank, foreshadowing the rescue. The city below is blacked out, with dead traffic lights and a dark power plant silhouette.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Webwidow's Multiverse Warning

- Story role: Frame the story as a "What If" tale and explain the small difference that changes the world.
- Layout: Four large panels moving through multiverse web space and into the alternate city.
- Caption boxes: "WHAT IF? AN ALTERNATE UNIVERSE STORY." "Editor's Note: Detective Webwidow first appeared in Issue 8." "Webwidow had swung through many worlds, and most were almost the same as ours." "In this world, one missed documentary meant Superhero Mandroid never learned the secret of the fatbergs."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Webwidow from the front in a simple two-handed swing pose through glowing strands like cosmic spiderwebs between floating city reflections: both gloved hands grip the same single webline above him, one left arm and one right arm only, no other visible arms or hands. Panel 2 shows Webwidow looking toward a translucent window of the city, badge wallet visible, with no city-name signage or labels. Panel 3 shows Superhero Mandroid patrolling the night sky even though lab screens show no portal alarm, flying only by green rocket-fist exhaust. Panel 4 shows a TV in the empty lab airing a fatberg documentary while Mandroid is absent, with a sewer diagram and an unreadable blurred host on-screen.

## Page 3: The Failed Answer

- Story role: Show the sludge reaching the power plant and Mandroid failing because he lacks the main-timeline clue.
- Layout: Five panels with escalating disaster.
- Caption boxes: "Editor's Note: The sludge demon first appeared in Issue 2." "The electricity-eating purple sludge rolled toward the city power plant." "Mandroid tried shields, fists, and every tactic he knew." "But without the fatberg clue, he never found the one answer that mattered." "The sludge consumed the hero, then the power plant, and the city went dark."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the massive purple sludge creature flowing down a city avenue toward the fenced power plant, electronics blacking out in its wake. Panel 2 shows Superhero Mandroid hovering above it, green rocket fists blazing, cyan-green holo shield raised. Panel 3 shows his rocket fist splashing uselessly into the sludge while the shield rim flickers and drains. Panel 4 shows sludge tendrils wrapping Mandroid's arms, chest, and cape as his eyes dim. Panel 5 shows the power plant swallowed, towers and city lights snapping off in a wave.

## Page 4: The Strongest Human Against Liquid Death

- Story role: Bring in Master Liang Chen, show brute force failing, and reveal water as the monster's weakness.
- Layout: Four large action panels for clarity.
- Caption boxes: "Editor's Note: Master Liang Chen first appeared in Issue 7." "Master Liang Chen came looking for the greatest challenge left in the powerless city." "His strongest punches and kicks passed through demonic liquid without stopping it." "Then his chi shattered a fire hydrant, and falling water made the monster recoil."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Liang walking into a dark street washed in purple emergency glow, hands shining with golden-white chi. Panel 2 shows him striking the sludge with a chi-charged kick that only splashes through its tar-like body. Panel 3 shows Liang firing a golden chi bolt at a red fire hydrant, bursting it into a tall white water jet. Panel 4 shows rain-like spray falling over the street as the purple sludge recoils, steam and violet-black ripples rising where water touches it.

## Page 5: No Rain Came

- Story role: Show Liang's temporary success, his death, and the city becoming a sealed survival trap.
- Layout: Five panels, somber and disaster-focused without gore.
- Caption boxes: "Liang shaped the hydrant spray with tai chi, turning water into a weapon." "It worked until the hydrant ran dry." "Before he reached another, the sludge closed over him." "The city waited for rain, but no rain came." "The creature sealed every road and swatted aircraft from the sky, leaving the city to starve in the dark."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Liang moving in tai chi stances, golden chi arcs guiding a sheet of water into the sludge. Panel 2 shows the hydrant sputtering dry while the monster regathers itself. Panel 3 shows a sludge tendril wrapped around Liang's head and shoulders as his golden aura fades, presented as tragic silhouette without gore. Panel 4 shows powerless hospital corridors, dark traffic intersections with crashed cars, and families rationing food by flashlight. Panel 5 shows the purple sludge forming a ring around the city perimeter while helicopters and a distant plane veer away from striking tendrils.

## Page 6: Two Geniuses In The Dark

- Story role: Move from disaster to invention as Dr. Volta confronts grieving Dr. Dawes.
- Layout: Four large panels in the hidden lab and improvised workshop space.
- Caption boxes: "Editor's Note: Dr. Vera Volta and Hydrohero Womandroid first appeared in Issue 4." "Desperate, Dr. Volta found Dr. Dawes in the dark lab where Mandroid would never return." "\"Build another android,\" she told him, because genius was useless if it only mourned." "\"There will never be another Mandroid,\" Dawes answered." "Volta heard grief, but she also heard a challenge."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Volta entering the powerless hidden lab with a handheld lantern, teal coat dusty, surrounded by dead portal monitors. Panel 2 shows Dawes sitting hollow-eyed beside Mandroid's empty charging station, glasses low, hands still. Panel 3 shows Volta leaning over him with fierce urgency while Dawes looks away in grief. Panel 4 shows Volta at a cluttered bench lit by battery lamps, sketching a water-powered android design beside Hydrohero parts and pressure gauges.

## Page 7: Hydrohero's Empty Tank

- Story role: Launch Hydrohero as the countermeasure and create the low point when the sludge notices her draining supply.
- Layout: Five action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Without electricity, Volta built a hero powered by water itself." "Hydrohero Womandroid struck the sludge with torrents, whips, and pressurized rain." "For the first time, the monster retreated from the city streets." "Then it saw her chest tank falling toward empty." "With the last of her water, Hydrohero fled upward as tendrils clawed at the sky."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero Womandroid stepping from an improvised launch bay, pearl-blue skin and blue-white armor gleaming, translucent chest tank full of swirling water. Panel 2 shows her blasting the sludge with wrist nozzles and water whips, blue water carving steaming paths through purple mass. Panel 3 shows civilians watching from dark windows as sludge pulls back from a street. Panel 4 close on Hydrohero's chest water tank nearly empty while a submerged alien eye focuses on it. Panel 5 shows Hydrohero rocketing upward on water jets, tendrils stretching after her between skyscrapers.

## Page 8: The Hero Of That World

- Story role: Resolve the "What If" by letting Hydrohero discover cloud recharge, purge the sludge, and become the AU hero.
- Layout: Five panels with a triumphant final splash.
- Caption boxes: "In the clouds, Hydrohero did not run out of power." "She drank the water from the sky." "Recharged by the storm above the stormless city, she returned as a living deluge." "The sludge washed away, the power grid woke, and the trapped city breathed again." "In that world, Superhero Mandroid was remembered as the hero who fell, and Hydrohero Womandroid became the hero who saved everyone else."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Hydrohero breaking through gray clouds, exhausted but still reaching upward. Panel 2 close on cloud mist spiraling into her chest water tank, aqua indicators refilling from empty to bright. Panel 3 shows her diving back over the city, surrounded by a vast controlled waterfall and rain curtain. Panel 4 shows the sludge dissolving under the deluge around the power plant as transformer lights flicker back on. Panel 5 final wide panel shows Hydrohero hovering above a restored city at dawn, citizens cheering below, Webwidow watching from a multiverse web strand at the page edge with a solemn nod.
