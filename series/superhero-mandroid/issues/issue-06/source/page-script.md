# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Signal the temptation, the fan-boy's pride, and the choice to reject demonic power.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image.
- Visual beats: Bolt stands in a narrow brick city alley at dusk wearing his homemade red-and-yellow Superhero Mandroid costume and green foam glove fists. A purple portal opens behind him with the Bargain Demon leaning out, holding a crushed silver can that glitters with purple sparks. Superhero Mandroid appears as a smaller background figure or reflected memory at the alley mouth, red suit, yellow cape, and real green mechanical fists showing the hero Bolt wants to emulate.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "No Powers Needed".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Not Your Sidekick

- Story role: Establish Bolt's desire and Mandroid's protective refusal.
- Layout: Five panels moving from fan excitement to wounded pride.
- Caption boxes: "Bolt had built the suit before he had built the strength." "He asked Superhero Mandroid for one thing: make me your sidekick." "Mandroid saw courage, but he also saw danger." "Demons were not playground villains." "Bolt heard only the word no."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Bolt proudly posing in his homemade costume on a city sidewalk near emergency barricades after a portal response. Panel 2 shows him looking up at Superhero Mandroid with pleading excitement. Panel 3 shows Mandroid calmly kneeling or bending closer, one real green mechanical hand open in a protective gesture. Panel 4 shows Mandroid gesturing toward distant portal scorch marks and cracked pavement, explaining the danger. Panel 5 shows Bolt's face hardening with embarrassment and anger.

## Page 3: The Can In The Portal

- Story role: Move Bolt from wounded pride into the alley where temptation can find him.
- Layout: Six panels with a sharp supernatural turn.
- Caption boxes: "He promised he would get powers one day." "Then he would be better than the hero who rejected him." "The alley gave him only echoes." "Bolt kicked a can because it was easier than admitting he was hurt." "A purple portal opened where no portal should be." "The can vanished first."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Bolt shouting at Mandroid, fists raised in childish fury. Panel 2 shows him storming away, yellow bedsheet cape twisting behind him. Panel 3 shows him alone in a narrow brick alley with dumpsters, puddles, and fire escapes. Panel 4 close on his sneaker kicking an ordinary dented soda can. Panel 5 shows a swirling purple portal tearing open at ankle height ahead of the can. Panel 6 shows the can flying into the portal while Bolt freezes.

## Page 4: Read Your Heart

- Story role: Introduce the demon and its offer of exactly what Bolt wants.
- Layout: Four larger panels for fear, performance, and temptation.
- Caption boxes: "The demon came out after the can." "Bolt was terrified, so he acted like the world's greatest hero." "The demon said it had read his heart." "It offered the can back with the power he wanted most."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Bargain Demon unfolding from the purple portal, too tall for the alley, smoky coat dragging through portal light. Panel 2 shows Bolt trembling but throwing a heroic pose with his green foam gloves up. Panel 3 close on the demon's sly yellow eyes and long fingers holding the now-sparking crushed can. Panel 4 shows Bolt staring at the glowing can, excitement breaking through his fear.

## Page 5: The Catch

- Story role: Make the bargain explicit and force Bolt to confront the target.
- Layout: Five panels focused on the offer, the catch, and Bolt's hesitation.
- Caption boxes: "Bolt asked the question every movie had taught him to ask." "The catch was simple." "Take the power, then use it to eliminate Superhero Mandroid." "Mandroid was still his hero." "The demon fed the wound in Bolt's pride."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Bolt cautiously pointing at the can, skeptical but tempted. Panel 2 shows the demon leaning close, the purple aura from the can lighting both faces. Panel 3 shows a ghostly portal-light vision of Superhero Mandroid as the target. Panel 4 shows Bolt conflicted, foam fists lowered, homemade cape drooping. Panel 5 shows the demon smiling as it reminds him that the world's greatest hero should prove it.

## Page 6: Not That Stupid

- Story role: Show Bolt rejecting the bargain at the last second and choosing loyalty over ego.
- Layout: Six panels with a clear reach-and-withdraw beat.
- Caption boxes: "His hand moved before his conscience caught it." "The power was one finger away." "Then Bolt remembered what demons wanted from Earth." "He pulled back." "He was angry, but he was not stupid." "The first punch surprised them both."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 close on Bolt's hand reaching toward the glowing can in the demon's claws. Panel 2 shows the can flaring purple with tiny sparks. Panel 3 shows Bolt's eyes widening as he realizes the demon wants Mandroid gone. Panel 4 shows him jerking his hand back. Panel 5 shows the demon mocking him, claws open. Panel 6 shows Bolt landing a sudden ordinary punch across the demon's jaw, foam glove crumpling on impact, both faces shocked.

## Page 7: Borrowed Move Names

- Story role: Turn Bolt's fan knowledge into courage and defeat the weak bargain demon.
- Layout: Six action panels with captioned move-name moments inside narration boxes.
- Caption boxes: "Bolt guessed the truth." "This was a deal demon, powerful only when someone accepted the deal." "So he walked forward." "Rocket punch was only an uppercut." "Holo shield bash was only an elbow." "But the demon still fell."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Bolt pointing at the demon with new confidence while the demon backs away. Panel 2 shows the demon recoiling as Bolt raises both fists. Panel 3 shows Bolt rushing forward on wet alley pavement. Panel 4 shows him uppercutting the demon while a caption names the borrowed move, not a speech bubble. Panel 5 shows him elbowing the demon in the face while purple sparks fly. Panel 6 shows the demon tumbling backward into the shrinking portal.

## Page 8: The Best Human He Could Be

- Story role: Resolve the temptation and redirect Bolt's ambition toward earned discipline.
- Layout: Five panels ending at the gym door.
- Caption boxes: "The portal closed, but the can stayed behind." "Purple power still sparkled inside the dents." "For one second, Bolt wondered if it was real." "Then he crushed the shortcut under his shoe." "He walked into the gym to become the best human he could be."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the alley after the demon is gone, portal closing to a thin purple line. Panel 2 close on the charged can glowing on wet pavement. Panel 3 shows Bolt kneeling near it, tempted but calmer. Panel 4 shows his sneaker crushing the can flat, purple sparks dying. Panel 5 final image shows Bolt entering a neighborhood gym under practical fluorescent light, cape loosened, foam gloves under one arm, posture determined.
