# Character And Reference Bible

Use these continuity details in every generated page prompt where the element appears. The issue belongs to `series/superhero-mandroid/`, and the series reference images are under `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/`.

## Superhero Mandroid

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: human-looking adult android man with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.
- Page continuity: appears only in the opening encounter and on the cover as the hero Bolt admires. He stays calm and protective while explaining that demon battles are unsafe for someone without powers.

## Bolt, Teenage Fan-Boy

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/bolt-fanboy-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: teenage boy about sixteen with medium brown skin, round face, expressive dark eyes, short curly black hair, slim build, and a homemade Superhero Mandroid costume: red hoodie and red sweatpants, yellow bedsheet cape tied at the neck, hand-painted yellow `SHM` cardboard chest badge, green foam boxing gloves taped with silver duct tape to imitate mechanical fists, scuffed sneakers, and nervous-but-defiant posture.
- Page continuity: begins eager and hurt in front of Mandroid, storms into a city alley with the homemade cape swinging, pretends to be brave in front of the demon, nearly reaches for the glowing can, then fights bare-handed while yelling Mandroid's move names. In the final page he removes or loosens the costume pieces and enters a neighborhood gym.

## Bargain Demon

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/bargain-demon-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: lean humanoid demon about seven feet tall with smoky purple-black skin, long angular face, narrow yellow eyes, small swept horns, sharp grin, long clawed fingers, a tattered dark coat like a street-corner con artist, purple ember cracks along the chest and hands, and wisps of portal smoke around its feet.
- Page continuity: emerges from a purple alley portal, reads Bolt's heart, offers back the portal-charged can, presses Bolt to eliminate Superhero Mandroid, then loses confidence when Bolt identifies it as a weak contract demon and starts punching.

## Mandroid's City

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/setting-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/mandroids-city-setting.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: dense modern city with glass towers, older brick roofs, rooftop HVAC units, street grids, emergency vehicles, crowd-filled sidewalks, power lines, and dramatic skyline views from above.
- Page continuity: the opening happens on a city sidewalk after a public portal response; the central confrontation happens in a narrow brick alley at dusk with wet pavement, dumpsters, trash cans, fire escapes, and purple portal light reflecting on the walls.

## Portal-Charged Can

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/key-item-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/portal-charged-can-key-item.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: crushed silver aluminum soda can with dents, torn red-and-white label fragments, dark scorch rings, tiny purple sparks, and a faint violet aura after passing through the demon portal.
- Page continuity: begins as ordinary alley litter kicked by Bolt, flies through the portal, returns in the demon's clawed hand as the offered bargain object, glows after the demon falls back through the portal, and ends flattened under Bolt's sneaker.

## Regenerating Rocket Fists

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/key-item-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/regenerating-rocket-fists-key-item.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: oversized green mechanical fists with heavy knuckle plating, dark seams, compact wrist thruster vents, internal pistons, and green-white rocket exhaust when launched.
- Page continuity: Mandroid's real fists appear in the opening. Bolt's homemade green foam boxing gloves are deliberately cheap imitations, and his later "rocket punch" is an ordinary uppercut, not an actual powered attack.

## Rechargeable Holo Shield

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/key-item-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/rechargeable-holo-shield-key-item.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: translucent cyan-green hardlight shield with curved hex-grid surface, bright rim, and pulsing battery-like segments projected from Superhero Mandroid's wrist hardware.
- Page continuity: Mandroid does not need to use the real shield in the issue; Bolt yells "holo shield bash" while delivering a normal elbow strike inspired by the move.
