# Treatment

## Title

No Powers Needed

## Issue Number

6

## Logline

A teenage Superhero Mandroid fan rejects a demon's offer of instant powers and discovers that the first step toward becoming a real hero is refusing the shortcut.

## Central Conflict

Bolt, a prideful teenage fan in a homemade Superhero Mandroid costume, wants powers badly enough to resent his hero. A bargain demon uses that resentment to tempt him with a portal-charged can, but accepting the gift would turn his dream into a weapon against Mandroid.

## Theme

Heroism is not proven by power or applause; it begins with the discipline to choose what is right when pride offers an easier path.

## Ending

Bolt crushes the glowing can instead of taking the demon's bargain. After defeating the demon with ordinary fists and borrowed courage, he walks into a gym determined to become the best human he can be.

## Emotional Arc

Bolt begins worshipful but entitled, wanting Mandroid to validate him as a sidekick. Rejection turns his admiration into wounded pride, and the demon gives that pride a supernatural opportunity. At the brink of accepting power, Bolt recognizes the trap, defends Mandroid, and defeats the demon by imitating the moves he once only shouted from the sidewalk. He ends humbler, still ambitious, but finally willing to earn strength instead of demanding it.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, the rejected sidekick request, the alley portal, the demon's offer, the demand to destroy Mandroid, Bolt's last-second refusal, the bare-handed fight, and the final choice to crush the can and train.
