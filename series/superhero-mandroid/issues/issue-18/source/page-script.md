# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. This issue is a mainline Superhero Mandroid story that introduces Ainrete as a magic-science world Mandroid can revisit through the Eagle Gem.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise a portal-fantasy team-up where Mandroid helps Calgoretos reclaim his name from Skullcerer.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition with readable title text.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "The Armorer of Ainrete". Smaller bottom caption: "A stolen staff. A fallen kingdom. A name reforged."
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid hovers using only green rocket-fist exhaust beside Calgoretos in battered dark green and bronze armor with dreadlocks, beard, skull emblems, huge gauntlets, and curved forearm blade. Behind them, the Sorceress of Good raises golden magic before Castle Ainrete while Skullcerer looms above with a green flaming skull staff and armored skeleton soldiers. A mystical Eagle gem glows in the foreground.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The Armorer Taken

- Story role: Establish Ainrete, Calgoretos's honorable craft, Skullcerer's conquest, and why the people blame Calgoretos.
- Layout: Four large lore panels with roomy captions.
- Caption boxes: "In Ainrete, ancient magic and advanced science lived in the same forge fire." "Calgoretos made armor for the king's soldiers, and his work held Skullcerer's undead army at the gates." "So Skullcerer chained the armorer and forced his hands to serve evil." "When the upgraded dead conquered Castle Ainrete, the people forgot the chains and remembered only the armor."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Calgoretos in a glowing forge hammering dark green bronze armor with rune circuits. Panel 2 shows king's soldiers in Calgoretos armor pushing back skeleton soldiers below Castle Ainrete. Panel 3 shows skeletal soldiers kidnapping Calgoretos, wrists chained, his expression furious. Panel 4 shows Skullcerer's armored undead taking the castle while frightened citizens point at Calgoretos as the blamed Armorer of Evil.

## Page 3: Not An Invader

- Story role: Bring Calgoretos to Earth and turn Mandroid's defensive misunderstanding into alliance.
- Layout: Five panels, from portal alarm to tense explanation.
- Caption boxes: "The Sorceress of Good sent Calgoretos through a desperate portal to find help beyond Ainrete." "On Earth, Superhero Mandroid saw armor, a blade, and an opening between worlds." "He stood between the stranger and his city." "Calgoretos did not raise his weapon." "He said Skullcerer had stolen a godlike power, and Earth would not stay safe if Ainrete fell forever."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Sorceress opening a golden portal in an underground rebel refuge. Panel 2 shows Calgoretos stumbling onto a Mandroid City rooftop through the portal. Panel 3 shows Mandroid landing in front of him with a cyan-green holo shield and green rocket fists ready. Panel 4 shows Calgoretos lowering his curved forearm blade and opening empty hands. Panel 5 shows Calgoretos explaining with grief while a small portal vision shows Skullcerer's death-magic spreading across Ainrete.

## Page 4: Armor For The Rebellion

- Story role: Return to Ainrete and show Calgoretos choosing to use his craft for the rebels again.
- Layout: Four panels with a mix of travel, preparation, and first battle.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid crossed worlds because protecting Earth sometimes meant protecting the door before it reached Earth." "Underground, the rebellion still breathed under stolen lanterns and broken banners." "Calgoretos reforged their armor, not for kings, not for fear, but for people who still hoped." "When Skullcerer's soldiers found them, the rebels stood taller than their graves."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid and Calgoretos emerging from a golden portal into Ainrete's underground rebel refuge beside the Sorceress of Good. Panel 2 shows exhausted rebels studying a glowing map of Castle Ainrete under stone arches and holographic runes. Panel 3 shows Calgoretos repairing and upgrading rebels' green-bronze armor at a field forge while Mandroid watches respectfully. Panel 4 shows upgraded rebels clashing with armored skeleton soldiers in a tunnel mouth, their rune armor holding.

## Page 5: The Staff No Shield Could Stop

- Story role: Escalate to Skullcerer's divine power and identify the staff as the tactical problem.
- Layout: Five battle panels.
- Caption boxes: "Calgoretos's armor broke the undead lines." "But no forge could plate a soul against Castle Ainrete's stolen divine power." "Skullcerer raised the mystic staff, and the battlefield knelt." "Mandroid's holo shield held for one heartbeat." "The Sorceress saw the truth: the staff was not a weapon. It was the handle of a stolen god."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows rebel soldiers driving back skeletons outside Castle Ainrete. Panel 2 shows Skullcerer floating on the castle steps, staff blazing sickly green and gold. Panel 3 shows divine magic blasting rebels aside without gore, armor glowing under unbearable force. Panel 4 shows Mandroid bracing behind his cyan-green holo shield as cracks of light spread across it. Panel 5 shows the Sorceress pointing urgently at the staff while Calgoretos realizes no one can reach it.

## Page 6: The Fist That Grabbed

- Story role: Show Mandroid's tactical solution: use a rocket fist to steal rather than destroy.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels with compact captions.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid stopped thinking like a wall." "He stopped thinking like a hammer." "He fired one rocket fist past Skullcerer's skull." "The fist opened. It grabbed the staff." "Then it came home."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid lowering his damaged holo shield and tracking the staff. Panel 2 shows Calgoretos and the Sorceress drawing Skullcerer's attention. Panel 3 shows Mandroid launching one oversized green mechanical rocket fist on a bright green exhaust trail, not aimed at Skullcerer's body. Panel 4 shows the fist opening its metal fingers and clamping around the staff in the same panel, yanking it free from Skullcerer's skeletal grasp. Panel 5 shows the rocket fist reconnecting to Mandroid's arm while holding the staff, with Skullcerer's green aura collapsing.

## Page 7: A Name Reforged

- Story role: Give Calgoretos the decisive victory and reverse Skullcerer's evil spells.
- Layout: Five large climactic panels.
- Caption boxes: "The Sorceress poured good magic into the armorer who had come back." "Mandroid channeled Ainrete's stolen power through the staff he had saved from evil hands." "Calgoretos's armor remembered what it was made to protect." "The Armorer of Evil ended at Skullcerer's feet." "The Armorer of Ainrete rose in his place."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Sorceress surrounding Calgoretos with warm golden magic. Panel 2 shows Mandroid carefully holding the staff as its gold divine power flows away from necromancy and into Calgoretos. Panel 3 shows Calgoretos glowing in his green-bronze armor, skull emblems transformed by clean light, gauntlets blazing. Panel 4 shows Calgoretos overpowering a weakened Skullcerer with his forearm blade and gauntlet, non-gory, shattering necromantic mist. Panel 5 shows skeleton servants collapsing into harmless bones while living people across Ainrete awaken from death-spells and the land brightens.

## Page 8: The Eagle Gem

- Story role: Resolve Ainrete's restoration, Calgoretos's redeemed reputation, and Mandroid's future connection to this world.
- Layout: Five panels ending on a portal farewell.
- Caption boxes: "Ainrete did not cheer because Calgoretos had never fallen." "They cheered because he came back and chose them again." "The Sorceress offered Mandroid a place among their champions." "He thought of Dr. Dawes, his city, and the world that still opened portals of its own." "So she gave him the Eagle Gem, and two worlds promised to call when courage was needed."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows restored Castle Ainrete at sunrise with citizens and rebels celebrating Calgoretos and the Sorceress. Panel 2 shows Calgoretos honored by people who once blamed him, his head bowed with humble relief. Panel 3 shows the Sorceress inviting Mandroid to stay while children and soldiers cheer. Panel 4 shows Mandroid gently refusing, holding the glowing Eagle gem, with a small memory-like image of Dr. Dawes and Mandroid's City behind him. Panel 5 shows Mandroid stepping through a golden portal back to Earth as Calgoretos and the Sorceress watch, the Eagle gem shining in his green mechanical palm.
