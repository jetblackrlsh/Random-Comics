# Treatment

## Title

The Armorer of Ainrete

## Issue Number

18

## Continuity Label

Mainline. This issue follows Superhero Mandroid's acceptance in Issue #17 that endless portals can still serve real heroic purpose, and it expands his multiverse connections by giving him a standing magical route to Ainrete.

## Logline

When the disgraced armorer Calgoretos arrives from the conquered magic-science world of Ainrete seeking help, Superhero Mandroid must steal a necromancer's divine staff so Calgoretos can reclaim his name and free his world.

## Central Conflict

Calgoretos once forged blessed armor for Ainrete's defenders, but Skullcerer kidnapped and enslaved him, forcing him to make stronger armor for an undead army. The conquered people now call him the Armorer of Evil, even though his hands were chained. Superhero Mandroid first mistakes him for another portal invader, then recognizes the larger danger: Skullcerer's stolen divine power could one day reach Earth. Together, Mandroid, Calgoretos, the Sorceress of Good, and the rebellion must win a war where ordinary strength, armor, and even hardlight shields cannot survive godlike magic.

## Theme

A forced mistake does not have to become a person's name forever; redemption means choosing what your gifts protect once you are free.

## Ending

Mandroid uses his rocket fist tactically, grabbing Skullcerer's mystic staff instead of striking the villain. With the staff's divine channel broken, the Sorceress of Good and Mandroid pour good magic and Ainrete power into Calgoretos, letting the armorer defeat Skullcerer in his own upgraded armor. Ainrete is restored, Calgoretos becomes a trusted defender again, and the Sorceress gives Mandroid a mystical Eagle gem so the worlds can call each other when the next crisis comes.

## Emotional Arc

Calgoretos begins ashamed and defensive, carrying the hatred of people who only saw the armor he was forced to build for evil. Mandroid begins guarded, assuming another portal arrival means another invasion. As Calgoretos explains the stakes, both heroes find common purpose: Mandroid understands that protecting Earth sometimes means protecting another world first, and Calgoretos learns that his craft can still belong to the innocent. By the end, Calgoretos accepts celebration without arrogance, and Mandroid turns down a tempting place among Ainrete's champions because his home and Dr. Dawes still need him.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Ainrete's fall and Calgoretos's enslavement, the Earth confrontation and plea for help, the return to the underground rebellion, the armored rebel assault, Skullcerer's overwhelming staff magic, Mandroid's rocket-fist staff theft, Calgoretos's empowered victory, and the restored-world farewell with the Eagle gem.

## New Continuity Elements

This issue introduces Calgoretos, Skullcerer, the Sorceress of Good, Ainrete, Castle Ainrete, Skullcerer's Mystic Staff, and the Eagle Gem as series continuity elements. It establishes that Superhero Mandroid can now contact and reach Ainrete through the Eagle Gem when future stories call for it.
