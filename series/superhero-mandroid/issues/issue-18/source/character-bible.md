# Character Bible

## Superhero Mandroid

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference images: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`, `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-flying-rocket-fists-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult android with white synthetic skin on his face, slicked-back brown artificial hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and wrist thruster vents. When flying, bright green-white exhaust comes only from the rocket-fist vents; his boots stay plain and inactive.
- Issue continuity: Mandroid has accepted that the portals can carry heroic purpose rather than only endless burden. He is still the protector of Earth, but this issue teaches him that stopping threats at the source sometimes means becoming an ally to another world.

## Dr. Elias Dawes

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/inventor-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: older human man in his early 60s with light skin, gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, and a practical dark lab coat over a blue shirt. He often wears a key card on a lanyard near the lab.
- Issue continuity: Dawes appears only as the emotional reason Mandroid cannot stay in Ainrete permanently.

## Calgoretos

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/calgoretos-armorer-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: strong Black adult male blacksmith-warrior with long black dreadlocks, full black beard, intense dark eyes, muscular build, soot-scarred hands, and battered dark green and bronze fantasy armor inspired by the user reference image. His armor has layered plates, bronze rivets, skull motifs on the breastplate and belt, huge segmented metal gauntlets, and one long curved forearm blade. His expression moves from shame and urgency to restored dignity.
- Issue continuity: Formerly celebrated as the Armorer of Ainrete, then kidnapped and forced to forge upgraded armor for Skullcerer. By the end he reclaims his name by using his craft and armor to defend Ainrete.

## Skullcerer

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/skullcerer-necromancer-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: undead skeletal necromancer with a tall bone-white skull, hollow green fire eyes, blackened jaw cracks, a ragged obsidian-purple cloak, tarnished bone-and-iron crown fragments, necromantic rune bands, armored skeleton hands, and a long mystic staff crowned with a skull and green divine fire. He is surrounded by death-mist and obedient armored skeleton soldiers.
- Issue continuity: Skullcerer conquers Ainrete after forcing Calgoretos to upgrade undead armor and stealing Castle Ainrete's divine power. Once Mandroid steals the staff, he loses the channel that makes him godlike.

## Sorceress of Good

- Series source: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/sorceress-of-good-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: wise adult woman sorceress with deep brown skin, bright gold eyes, long silver-white braids, practical white-and-gold travel robes reinforced with light leather, sunburst embroidery, crystal bracers, and warm golden magic around her hands. Her posture is calm, tactical, and compassionate even in battle.
- Issue continuity: Leader and spiritual guide of the Ainrete rebellion. She sends Calgoretos to Earth, identifies Skullcerer's staff as the key to his stolen power, channels good magic into Calgoretos, and gives Mandroid the Eagle Gem.

## Ainrete Rebellion Soldiers

- Series source: issue-specific group based on `series/superhero-mandroid/source/setting-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/ainrete-rebellion-soldiers-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: mixed group of exhausted human and humanoid fantasy-science rebels wearing Calgoretos-upgraded dark green and bronze armor, practical cloaks, glowing blue-gold rune seams, battered helmets, spears, shields, and short rifles or arcane-tech tools. Their armor looks hand-forged, repaired, and trusted.
- Issue continuity: The rebels escaped underground after Skullcerer's conquest. Calgoretos's repaired armor lets them overcome undead soldiers, but not Skullcerer's divine staff magic.

## Issue-Specific Visual Motifs

- Ainrete blends ancient magic and advanced science: stone castles with luminous conduits, forged armor with rune circuits, underground rebel lanterns beside holographic maps, and divine gold-green power contrasted against Skullcerer's sickly green necromancy.
- Calgoretos's armor should remain close to the attached reference image: dark green and bronze battered plates, skull emblems, huge gauntlets, dreadlocks, beard, and a curved forearm blade.
- Mandroid's technology should look foreign but compatible beside Ainrete magic: green rocket-fist exhaust, cyan-green holo shields, and red/yellow superhero colors cutting through medieval-futurist battlefields.
