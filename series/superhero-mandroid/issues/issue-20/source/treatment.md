# Treatment

## Title

Beyond Norio's Shadow

## Issue Number

20

## Logline

Demoted to Earth for failing to live up to her creator's legacy, the Space Police android GAL-XZ learns from Superhero Mandroid that inherited power can become her own heroic purpose.

## Central Conflict

GAL-XZ was built from a portion of Norio's energy to continue the work of one of the Space Police's greatest officers, but Norio's death has turned that gift into a burden. Space Police leadership and public rumor treat her as a failed replacement whose existence may have weakened Norio before his final battle. Her first Earth patrol becomes a crisis when a giant humanoid squid invades through a purple portal, speaks a language beyond her universal communicator, and overpowers her before she can channel Norio's green energy correctly. Superhero Mandroid's arrival gives her both a tactical model and an emotional mirror: another android protector who proves that a created life can choose what its design means.

## Theme

Legacy should guide a hero, not trap them into becoming a copy of someone else.

## Ending

After learning to use Norio's energy like an android power system instead of a borrowed mystic identity, GAL-XZ defeats portal monsters without needing Superhero Mandroid to rescue her. When the Space Police offer her Norio's vacant role, she refuses. Earth is no longer a punishment post. It is the place where she became her own hero.

## Emotional Arc

GAL-XZ begins ashamed, isolated, and desperate to prove that Norio did not waste his strength on her. Her first battle on Earth nearly confirms every accusation against her until Mandroid saves her and thanks her instead of blaming her. Their flight over the city reframes her existence: Norio did not build her in his own image because he wanted her to grow beyond his shadow. By the end, GAL-XZ changes from a failed successor into a self-defined defender who can honor Norio without surrendering her life to his memory.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, Norio's legacy and GAL-XZ's demotion, first Earth patrol and squid invasion, Mandroid's rescue and GAL-XZ copying rocket-fist flight, their combined victory, the city flight conversation, GAL-XZ training herself into independent control, and the Space Police promotion offer she refuses because Earth has become home.

## New Continuity Elements

This issue introduces GAL-XZ as a new android hero and potential recurring Earth ally, Norio as her deceased Space Police creator, the Space Police as an interstellar heroic authority, the Space Police outer patrol dock, GAL-XZ's Norio energy core, her universal communicator, and a giant portal squid invader. It establishes that Earth is outside normal Space Police priority, that Mandroid's portal experience can matter beyond Earth, and that GAL-XZ now chooses Earth by free will rather than demotion.
