# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text, cover title text, labels, and any readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Announce GAL-XZ and Mandroid as parallel android protectors.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover composition with readable title text.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Large issue title text: "Beyond Norio's Shadow". Bottom caption: "A fallen legend gave her power. Earth gave her a choice."
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid rockets over Mandroid's City using only green exhaust from his oversized green rocket fists while GAL-XZ flies beside him on radiant green hand-thrusters. A purple portal swirls behind them with a giant squid silhouette reaching through it. GAL-XZ's chrome skin, long curly blonde hair, glowing green eyes, emerald jumpsuit, green high heel combat boots, and chest energy core are clear. Mandroid's red suit, yellow cape, `SHM` chest insignia, and green rocket fists are clear.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.
