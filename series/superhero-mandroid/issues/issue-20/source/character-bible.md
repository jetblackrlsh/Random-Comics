## Recurring Character: Superhero Mandroid

Use the canonical mainline Superhero Mandroid design: human-looking adult male android with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents. When flying, his only propulsion is bright green-white exhaust from his rocket fists. He never uses rocket feet, boot jets, back thrusters, jet packs, wings, hoverboards, or any other flight device.

In this issue his role is mentor and tactical model. His expression should be calm, alert, and generous; he treats GAL-XZ as a fellow hero rather than a failed machine.

## New Character: GAL-XZ

GAL-XZ is a human-looking adult woman android with mirror-bright chrome skin, long curly blonde hair, glowing green eyes, an hourglass silhouette, a skintight emerald-green leather jumpsuit with subtle Space Police seam lines, green high heel combat boots, and a circular green energy core glowing under transparent armor at the upper chest. Her hands, eyes, and core flare with radiant green Norio energy when she channels power. Early pages should show shame, pressure, and panic; later pages should show growing confidence, precise control, and warmth when Mandroid treats her as wanted.

## New Character: Norio

Norio was one of the Space Police's mightiest alien officers and GAL-XZ's creator. He appears only in memory, monument, hologram, or symbolic panels. He is a tall noble alien man with deep indigo skin, bright green eyes, a mane of silver-white hair swept back like a comet tail, luminous green-gold energy veins across his forearms, and white-and-black Space Police armor with a star badge on the chest. His presence should feel heroic, compassionate, and impossible for GAL-XZ to measure herself against.

## New Character: Portal Squid Invader

The portal squid invader is a massive humanoid squid creature from a language and dimension the universal communicator cannot parse. It has a towering wet violet-gray body, broad humanoid torso, thick tentacle arms, smaller face tentacles, glossy black eyes, bioluminescent purple markings, and dripping alien slime. It should look physically powerful, strange, and dangerous without gore.

## New Setting: Space Police Outer Patrol Dock

The Space Police outer patrol dock is a practical orbital law-enforcement station on the fringe of defended space, with worn white-and-green alloy walls, hangar bays, alien officers from many species, holographic patrol maps, starfield windows, duty assignment screens, and memorial displays for fallen officers. It is impressive but bureaucratic, making GAL-XZ's demotion feel public and humiliating.

## New Key Item: Norio Energy Core

GAL-XZ's Norio energy core is a circular green power source set beneath transparent chest armor, made from a measured percentage of Norio's living energy. It glows like a battery with segmented charge rings, emits green sparks under stress, and throws warm green light across GAL-XZ's chrome body. It is not a magic wand; by the end GAL-XZ understands it as an android power system that can drive rocket-like hand thrust, shields, and impact force.

## New Key Item: GAL-XZ Universal Communicator

The universal communicator is a compact Space Police device worn on GAL-XZ's wrist and sometimes held near her mouth like a translator badge. It is chrome and emerald, with a small circular speaker, green waveform display, and alien glyph interface. It can translate known alien languages, but the portal squid's speech comes from outside this dimension and defeats it.
