## Detective Webwidow

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/detective-webwidow-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: human man in his late 30s with light-to-medium skin, narrow angular face, tired sharp eyes, dark stubble, dark spider-web tattoo across one side of his face from temple to cheekbone, weathered dark detective fedora, long charcoal-brown duster, dark shirt and vest, practical trousers, worn boots, compact wrist web shooters, and a large sewn black widow spider emblem on the back of his duster with a subtle red hourglass.
- Page continuity: swings out of a sky portal on silvery web lines, dodges Mandroid's opening rocket punch by releasing the web at the last second, tips his hat instead of fighting, shows his badge, explains the Super Spiders Of The Multiverse, then swings away laughing after webbing Mandroid's rocket fist engines.

- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/super-spiders-badge-key-item.png`

- Page continuity: appears in Detective Webwidow's hand when he proves he is not an enemy; close panels should make the badge organization readable enough for Mandroid to react.
