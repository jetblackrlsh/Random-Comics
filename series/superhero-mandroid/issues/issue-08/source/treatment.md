# Treatment

## Title

The Web Between Worlds

## Issue Number

8

## Logline

Superhero Mandroid meets a spider-powered multiverse detective who can help with portal threats but would rather make Mandroid prove he can catch him first.

## Central Conflict

Mandroid wants practical help closing the portals that keep ripping open around his world, while Detective Webwidow treats multiverse travel as a spider-hero specialty and treats Mandroid's reliability as a reason to relax. Their first meeting turns from assumed combat to philosophical disagreement, then into a rooftop chase that exposes how easily Webwidow can disable Mandroid's rocket fists without truly becoming his enemy.

## Theme

Being reliable can feel unfair, but it also gives other heroes room to rest and handle problems only they can reach.

## Ending

Detective Webwidow escapes after gumming up Mandroid's rocket fists with webbing. Mandroid climbs down a fire escape instead of flying home, irritated and reflective. By the time he reaches the street, he holds his head high: other heroes rely on him, he can rely on other heroes, and both truths can coexist with the fact that Detective Webwidow is still a bit of a jerk.

## Emotional Arc

Mandroid begins in immediate protector mode, assuming that anything dramatic exiting a portal may be dangerous. He shifts into confusion when the detective shows a badge, then frustration when the supposed ally admits he usually leaves local portal trouble to Mandroid. The chase gives Mandroid a physical reason to be annoyed, but the quiet walk afterward lets him reframe his duty as a form of earned trust rather than lonely obligation.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, portal arrival, rooftop dodge, badge reveal, multiverse-web explanation, the refusal to help, the chase and webbed rocket fists, and Mandroid's street-level reflection.
