A portal opens in the sky and a man with a detective hat, a spider-web tattooed on his face, and a large black widow symbol sewn into the back of his duster swings out on a web. Superhero Mandroid rockets at the spider-themed detective, but the detective dodges and lands on a rooftop. Mandroid lands ready to fight, and the detective tips his hat and says he is not an enemy.

The detective shows a badge for the Super Spiders Of The Multiverse. He explains that spider-powered heroes understand the multiverse because it behaves like a web, while portal monsters only tear holes in it. Mandroid asks if the detective has come to close the portals that keep opening around the world. The detective says no, because when he is home he relaxes and lets Mandroid handle it.

Mandroid is flabbergasted and frustrated. The detective agrees to lend a hand from time to time if Mandroid can catch him, then swings away. Mandroid tries to pursue with his rocket fists, but the detective gums up the fist engines with webbing and escapes laughing. Mandroid has to climb down the fire escape, thinking through what the detective said. He decides that it matters that other heroes can rely on him and that he can rely on other heroes, even if the detective is still a bit of a jerk.
