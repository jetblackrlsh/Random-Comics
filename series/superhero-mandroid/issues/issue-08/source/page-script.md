# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Signal a strange ally arriving through a sky portal.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image.
- Visual beats: Above Mandroid's City rooftops, a glowing portal hangs in the cloudy sky. Detective Webwidow swings out on a silvery webline, fedora low, face web tattoo clear, duster flaring to show the black widow emblem. Superhero Mandroid rockets up from below with oversized green fists and yellow cape, ready to intercept.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "The Web Between Worlds".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: The Portal Rider

- Story role: Establish Mandroid's protector response and the detective's impossible entrance.
- Layout: Five panels moving from city alarm to sky portal action.
- Caption boxes: "The portal opened above the city without warning." "Mandroid launched before the first sirens found it." "Something came through on a strand of silver web." "A detective hat. A spider-marked face." "Mandroid aimed a rocket fist at the unknown."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows citizens pointing up from a city street as a portal blooms in the sky. Panel 2 shows Mandroid blasting upward between rooftops on green rocket-fist exhaust. Panel 3 shows Detective Webwidow swinging out of the portal. Panel 4 close on his fedora, dark web tattoo, and duster whipping in the wind. Panel 5 shows Mandroid drawing back one oversized green fist for a midair punch.

## Page 3: Rooftop Dodge

- Story role: Turn the first attack into a near-fight and reveal the detective's composure.
- Layout: Six fast action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid threw the punch before the portal could throw a monster." "The detective let go." "Green metal crossed empty air." "The stranger landed on the roof like rain." "Mandroid landed ready." "The detective only tipped his hat."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid's green rocket fist surging forward. Panel 2 shows Detective Webwidow releasing the webline at the last instant. Panel 3 shows the rocket fist missing past his coat. Panel 4 shows Webwidow landing in a crouch on a wet rooftop near HVAC units. Panel 5 shows Mandroid landing opposite him with both fists raised. Panel 6 shows Webwidow tipping his fedora calmly and holding up one empty hand.

## Page 4: The Badge

- Story role: Reframe the detective from enemy to possible ally.
- Layout: Five panels with a readable badge reveal.
- Caption boxes: "He said he was not the enemy." "Mandroid did not lower his fists." "So the detective showed a badge." "Mandroid leaned closer." "\"Super Spiders Of The Multiverse\" did not clear things up."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Webwidow speaking through a caption box while Mandroid stays guarded. Panel 2 shows Mandroid narrowing his eyes and asking if the stranger is a cop. Panel 3 close on Webwidow opening the worn badge wallet. Panel 4 closer view of the bronze-and-black spider-web shield badge with readable organization text. Panel 5 shows Mandroid confused, one green fist still half-raised.

## Page 5: The Web Of Worlds

- Story role: Explain the Super Spiders and contrast web navigation with portal damage.
- Layout: Six conversational panels with surreal sky reflections.
- Caption boxes: "The detective said the group was spider heroes only." "The multiverse, he said, behaved like a web." "Spider powers knew how to ride the strands." "Mandroid listed demons, robots, and aliens without spider powers." "The detective pointed to the portal scar." "Those beasts tore holes instead."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Webwidow gesturing upward, silver web strands faintly reflected in the portal light. Panel 2 shows an abstract but photoreal overlay of glowing web-like reality strands across the sky. Panel 3 shows Mandroid gesturing in frustration toward the city where past portal scars glow faintly in windows. Panel 4 shows quick inset-style panels of demon, robot, and alien silhouettes at old portals without redesigning specific past enemies. Panel 5 shows Webwidow tracing a clean web strand with one finger. Panel 6 shows the portal edge ragged and torn like light ripping fabric.

## Page 6: Not Here To Help

- Story role: Make the central frustration explicit.
- Layout: Six panels escalating from hope to disbelief.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid asked if webs could close the portals." "The answer was no." "At home, the detective preferred to relax." "Mandroid stared at him." "Webwidow shrugged." "Mandroid was around to handle this world."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Mandroid pointing at the shrinking portal, hopeful but tense. Panel 2 shows Webwidow shaking his head with casual certainty. Panel 3 shows Webwidow lounging against a rooftop vent as if off-duty. Panel 4 close on Mandroid's flabbergasted face, white synthetic skin and human emotion clear. Panel 5 shows Webwidow shrugging with badge still tucked away. Panel 6 shows Mandroid stepping closer, frustrated, fists lowered but clenched.

## Page 7: Catch Me

- Story role: Turn the disagreement into a chase that Webwidow wins without a real fight.
- Layout: Six panels with rooftop motion and mechanical failure.
- Caption boxes: "The detective offered a deal." "If Mandroid could catch him, he would lend a hand." "Then the webline snapped upward." "Mandroid fired his rocket fists to follow." "Webwidow fired first." "The engines coughed under sticky webbing."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Webwidow smiling slightly and raising one wrist web shooter. Panel 2 shows him launching a webline toward a higher building. Panel 3 shows him swinging away over water towers and fire escapes. Panel 4 shows Mandroid trying to ignite both green rocket fists for pursuit. Panel 5 shows Webwidow twisting mid-swing and firing silvery webs backward. Panel 6 close on sticky webbing gumming up Mandroid's fist thruster vents, green exhaust sputtering and dying.

## Page 8: The Reliable One

- Story role: Resolve with Mandroid's annoyed but proud reflection.
- Layout: Five panels descending from rooftop to street.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid climbed down the fire escape." "He wondered how many heroes let him handle the portals." "Maybe reliability was not the same as loneliness." "Other heroes could rest because he stood guard." "That still did not make the detective less annoying."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Webwidow disappearing between buildings, laughing in the distance, while Mandroid stands with webbed rocket fists. Panel 2 shows Mandroid grunting and stepping onto a metal fire escape. Panel 3 shows him climbing down past apartment windows, thoughtful and irritated. Panel 4 shows him reaching the sidewalk as civilians hurry by under a normal sky. Panel 5 final image shows Mandroid walking down the street with head held high, cape settled, web residue still stuck to his green fists, proud but annoyed.
