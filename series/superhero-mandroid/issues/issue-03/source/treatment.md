# Treatment

## Title

The Voice That Broke The Hall

## Issue Number

3

## Logline

Superhero Mandroid battles a parasite-controlled opera singer whose amplified voice turns the opera house itself into a weapon.

## Central Conflict

Superhero Mandroid must save a crowded opera house without harming the singer, but the brain parasite amplifies her voice into sonic blasts strong enough to keep him from getting close.

## Theme

Protection means aiming at the thing doing the harm, not the person being used as its weapon.

## Ending

Superhero Mandroid stops trying to reach the parasite by walking through the sonic force. He fires a rocket fist through the blast, knocks the parasite from the singer's head, and frees her, but the mind-control threat leaves him worried about a new kind of interdimensional danger.

## Emotional Arc

Superhero Mandroid begins with direct urgency, breaking into the opera house to stop an attack. His confidence cracks as sonic blasts repeatedly throw him back and pin him down. He steadies himself behind his holo shield, realizes close-range heroics will fail, and chooses a precise ranged strike that saves the victim without treating her as the enemy.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, portal possession, theater entry, aerial sonic dodge, inventor warning and seating crater, shield advance, rocket-fist precision strike, and relieved aftermath with a new mind-control warning.
