# Treatment

## Title

Monsters Beneath Our Streets

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

When a purple shutdown sludge creature threatens the city power plant, Superhero Mandroid saves the city by redirecting the sky monster into the diseased fatbergs beneath the streets.

## Central Conflict

Superhero Mandroid must stop a portal monster that disables electronics on contact, which makes his usual direct attacks too dangerous and forces him to protect the power plant while finding an indirect biological solution.

## Theme

Saving a city sometimes means respecting the ugly systems beneath it, not only fighting the spectacular dangers above it.

## Ending

Superhero Mandroid's giant shield dies just as his rocket-fist moat opens into the sewers. The purple sludge drains below and is consumed by diseased fatbergs, leaving Mandroid horrified and grateful that the city's hidden monsters defeated the invader from the sky.

## Emotional Arc

Superhero Mandroid begins in urgent hero mode, racing from his lab toward the portal. His confidence falters when he realizes touching the sludge could shut him down. The oversized shield dam drains him faster than expected, turning the rescue into a countdown. He regains control by remembering something mundane and disgusting, then ends humbled by how much of the city is protected by people and systems he rarely sees.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, launch from the lab, portal sludge over the city, rooftop warning, power-plant shield dam, fatberg realization, rocket-fist moat, and sewer-monster resolution.
