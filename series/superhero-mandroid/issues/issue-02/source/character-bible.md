## Superhero Mandroid

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: human-looking adult android man with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.
- Page continuity: begins repaired after Issue #1, launches from the lab with rocket fists, becomes visibly strained while holding the giant holo-shield dam, and ends exhausted but intact.

## Shutdown Sludge Creature

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/shutdown-sludge-creature-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: massive purple sludge monster with glossy tar-like body, rippling gelatinous folds, wet violet-black highlights, bulbous pseudopods, faint alien eyes or glowing cores under the surface, and dead electronics or darkened screens wherever it flows.
- Page continuity: leaks from the sky portal, spreads through city streets, shuts down electronics on contact, drains the giant holo shield, then falls into the sewer moat and decays when surrounded by fatbergs.
