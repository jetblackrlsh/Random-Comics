# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce the sky portal, the purple shutdown sludge, the threatened power plant, and the sewer fatberg solution.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with superhero disaster tension.
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid hovers above the city on green-white rocket-fist thrust, red suit and yellow cape visible, SHM insignia clear. A purple portal tears open in the sky, leaking a huge glossy sludge creature toward the streets. Below, a city power plant glows with danger lights, civilians flee, and a cutaway-like sewer opening hints at pale diseased fatbergs waiting underground. A huge cyan-green holo shield flickers between the sludge and the power plant.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "Monsters Beneath Our Streets".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.
