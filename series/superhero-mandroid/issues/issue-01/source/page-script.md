# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Superhero Mandroid, the hidden lab, and the digital demon threat.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with realistic superhero photo-comic tension.
- Visual beats: Superhero Mandroid stands in the cracked doorway of his hidden lab, red unitard and yellow cape dusty, yellow `SHM` insignia visible, huge green mechanical fists raised. A translucent cyan-green holo shield arcs from one wrist. Behind him, the Pixel Demon rises as a glowing blue pixel humanoid with hardlight tendrils and blank cyan eyes. The heavy lab door, peephole, portal-monitor screens, and emergency red lighting frame the scene.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Mandroid". Smaller issue title text: "The Demon at the Door".
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated inside the cover image, no publisher logo.

## Page 2: Mission Return

- Story role: Establish the hero, his normal portal-defense mission, and the private safety of the hidden lab.
- Layout: Four panels with documentary action-to-quiet pacing.
- Caption boxes: "Portals opened. Superhero Mandroid answered." "Giant machines, mutant beasts, and alien demons all learned the same lesson." "After the last threat fell, he returned to the one place almost nobody knew." "Then the lab door buzzer chimed."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows a smoky portal site in the distance behind Superhero Mandroid, with a defeated robot shape and sparks near his green fists. Panel 2 shows him entering the hidden lab, cape dusty and shoulders tired. Panel 3 shows portal-monitor screens and diagnostic lights as he powers down. Panel 4 shows him freezing at the sharp buzz from the heavy security door.

## Page 3: The Visitor

- Story role: Build suspicion around the inventor's impossible visit.
- Layout: Five panels centered on the lab door and peephole.
- Caption boxes: "Very few people knew this door existed." "Fewer still would ever press the buzzer." "Through the peephole, Mandroid saw his inventor waiting outside." "The face was right. The timing was wrong." "The key card stayed unused."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 close on the buzzing door panel. Panel 2 shows Superhero Mandroid approaching with narrowed eyes, mechanical fist lowered but ready. Panel 3 peephole view of the inventor in a dark lab coat with glasses and key-card lanyard. Panel 4 shows Superhero Mandroid's skeptical expression. Panel 5 shows his hand opening the door while his other green fist remains tense.

## Page 4: False Inventor

- Story role: Reveal the deception and injure Superhero Mandroid.
- Layout: Six panels with a sudden violent reversal.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid asked why the key card stayed in his pocket." "The inventor smiled too wide." "Hardlight tendrils unfolded from a borrowed shadow." "The first strike hit before the question finished." "Steel cracked behind Mandroid's back." "The disguise burned away in blue pixels."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Superhero Mandroid facing the inventor at the threshold. Panel 2 close on the inventor's evil grin, wrong and predatory. Panel 3 shows glowing blue hardlight tendrils erupting from behind the disguise. Panel 4 shows a tendril whiplash smashing Superhero Mandroid backward. Panel 5 shows his body hitting the reinforced wall, tools and sparks flying. Panel 6 shows the inventor hologram dissolving into the Pixel Demon.

## Page 5: The Threat

- Story role: Clarify the inventor is alive while raising the threat against him.
- Layout: Four larger panels with room for clear captions.
- Caption boxes: "The Pixel Demon laughed at the simplest trick in the world." "Mandroid rose through pain and demanded the truth." "The inventor was away at a conference. Safe, for now." "The demon promised that safety would end when he came home."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Pixel Demon looming in the damaged lab doorway, blue pixel body and tendrils flickering. Panel 2 shows Superhero Mandroid pushing himself up from cracked wall debris, bruised and angry. Panel 3 shows the demon gesturing mockingly as a small holographic conference badge or travel projection flickers in its palm. Panel 4 shows Superhero Mandroid's face hardening as the demon threatens the inventor.

## Page 6: Pixel Evasion

- Story role: Show the demon's intangible digital form and technology-interface ability.
- Layout: Six action panels with clear cause and effect.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid charged before the demon could say more." "The rocket fist crossed the room like a missile." "The demon became a cloud of pixels." "It reformed laughing beside the monitors." "Low-level systems had told it where to look." "That was enough to make Mandroid open the door."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Superhero Mandroid lunging forward with a green mechanical fist primed. Panel 2 shows a rocket punch firing with green-white exhaust. Panel 3 shows the Pixel Demon breaking apart into an intangible blue pixel cloud as the fist passes through. Panel 4 shows the demon reforming beside portal-monitor screens, one claw touching the electronics. Panel 5 shows data reflections and lab-location traces in blue pixel light. Panel 6 shows Superhero Mandroid scowling as the fist regenerates or reconnects.

## Page 7: Hardlight Against Hardlight

- Story role: Turn the fight by revealing the demon's limits and the holo shield's matching hardlight nature.
- Layout: Six panels escalating from trap to counterattack.
- Caption boxes: "Mandroid charged again, this time smiling." "The demon shaped one tendril into a spear." "The holo shield snapped open before impact." "Hardlight met hardlight." "The shield cut through what the demon thought was invulnerable." "Low-level tricks could not hack the lab, and they could not hack him."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows Superhero Mandroid charging with a grim smirk. Panel 2 shows the Pixel Demon thrusting a blue hardlight tendril spear. Panel 3 shows the translucent cyan-green hex-grid shield appearing from Mandroid's wrist. Panel 4 shows the spear striking the shield with sparks and square pixel fragments. Panel 5 shows Mandroid slicing the tendril off with the shield edge. Panel 6 shows the demon recoiling in pain while Mandroid stands between it and the lab monitors.

## Page 8: No Answer

- Story role: Resolve the fight while leaving the larger threat unresolved.
- Layout: Five panels, decisive action followed by quiet aftermath.
- Caption boxes: "The demon screamed that it should be untouchable." "Mandroid understood the rule better than the invader did." "One hardlight edge split another hardlight body." "The Pixel Demon evaporated before naming what it came to steal." "Mandroid had won, but the portals were no longer waiting for him to arrive."
- Visual beats: Panel 1 shows the Pixel Demon staggering, missing a tendril, blue pixels shedding from its body. Panel 2 shows Superhero Mandroid raising the glowing holo shield like a blade. Panel 3 shows the shield slicing the demon in half, blue pixel fragments scattering without gore. Panel 4 shows the evaporating demon as Mandroid demands an answer, his expression frustrated. Panel 5 final quiet image: Superhero Mandroid alone in the damaged hidden lab, emergency lights glowing, looking toward the closed door and a monitor that shows the inventor's conference location marked safe.
