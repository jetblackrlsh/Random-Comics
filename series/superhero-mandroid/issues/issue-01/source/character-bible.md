## Superhero Mandroid

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: human-looking adult man with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents.

## Pixel Demon

- Source entry: `series/superhero-mandroid/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/superhero-mandroid/reference-images/pixel-demon-character.png`
- Stable visual identifiers: humanoid demon made of glowing blue pixels, fractured hardlight plates, flickering square particles, jagged horn-like pixel clusters, blank cyan eyes, clawed hands, and hologram tendrils emerging from its back.
- Page continuity: begins hidden under the inventor disguise; becomes fully visible after the tendril strike; can disperse into an intangible blue pixel cloud; loses a tendril on Page 7; is sliced and evaporating on Page 8.
