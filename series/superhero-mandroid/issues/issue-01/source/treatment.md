# Treatment

## Title

The Demon at the Door

## Issue Number

1

## Logline

Superhero Mandroid lets a disguised visitor into his hidden lab and has to defeat a digital demon by recognizing that the monster's hardlight body is vulnerable to his own hardlight shield.

## Central Conflict

Superhero Mandroid is built to intercept portal threats in the field, but the Pixel Demon changes the rules by finding his private lab, impersonating the inventor, and proving that interdimensional enemies can use deception instead of brute force.

## Theme

A protector who only watches the obvious battlefield can miss the quieter doorway where danger enters.

## Ending

Superhero Mandroid destroys the Pixel Demon before it reveals who sent it or what it wanted. The inventor is still safe at a conference, but the attack leaves Superhero Mandroid frustrated and newly aware that future enemies may target his home and creator.

## Emotional Arc

Superhero Mandroid begins tired but confident after another successful mission. The buzzer makes him suspicious, the disguised attack wounds both his body and his trust, and the threat against his inventor turns suspicion into anger. By the end, he regains control through tactical reasoning, but his victory feels incomplete because the demon reached him first.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, mission return, suspicious visitor, disguised ambush, demon threat, failed rocket-punch attack, hardlight shield reversal, and unresolved aftermath.
