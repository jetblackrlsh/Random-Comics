# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Superhero Mandroid's Hidden Lab

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Demon at the Door"
- Visual identifiers: concealed high-tech underground laboratory with reinforced steel walls, matte black equipment racks, portal-monitoring screens, green diagnostic lights, a heavy security door with peephole and buzzer panel, tool benches, charging equipment, and subtle damage from past missions. Lighting is practical and cool, with emergency red accents after attacks.
- Continuity notes: The lab is secret and hardened against low-level hacking. The Pixel Demon can discover its location through technology traces but cannot hack the door or Superhero Mandroid directly.
