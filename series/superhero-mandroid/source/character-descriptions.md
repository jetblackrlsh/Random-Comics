# Character Descriptions

## Character: Superhero Mandroid

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Demon at the Door"
- Role: protagonist, android superhero designed to protect the world from interdimensional threats.
- Visual identifiers: human-looking adult man with white synthetic skin, slicked-back brown hair, clean-shaven face, alert brown eyes, athletic build, red superhero unitard, yellow cape, yellow `SHM` chest insignia, and oversized mechanical green fists with panel seams, knuckle plating, and small thruster vents. His expression shifts from controlled suspicion to focused tactical anger.
- Continuity notes: Can summon a rechargeable cyan-green hardlight holo shield and fire regenerating rapid rocket punches. He is used to fighting at portal sites, not being found in his hidden lab.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/superhero-mandroid-character.png`

## Character: The Inventor

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Demon at the Door"
- Role: supporting character, creator of Superhero Mandroid.
- Visual identifiers: older human man in his early 60s with light skin, gray swept-back hair, tired blue eyes, narrow face, trimmed gray beard, wire-frame glasses, and a practical dark lab coat over a blue shirt. He wears a key card on a lanyard when working near the lab.
- Continuity notes: He is away at a conference during Issue #1, but his identity is used by the Pixel Demon as a holographic disguise. He is one of the few people who knows the hidden lab exists.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/inventor-character.png`

## Character: Pixel Demon

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Demon at the Door"
- Role: antagonist, digital demon and technology infiltrator.
- Visual identifiers: humanoid demon made of glowing blue pixels, fractured hardlight plates, flickering square particles, jagged horn-like pixel clusters, blank cyan eyes, clawed hands, and hologram tendrils that emerge from its back. Its body can break apart into an intangible cloud of blue pixels before reforming.
- Continuity notes: Can mimic a person with a hologram disguise, form hardlight tendrils, interface with low-level technology, and become temporarily intangible by dispersing into pixels. Its hardlight body can still be cut by Superhero Mandroid's holo shield.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/pixel-demon-character.png`
