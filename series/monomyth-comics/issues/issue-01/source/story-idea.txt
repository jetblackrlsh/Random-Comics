Series: Monomyth Comics
Issue #1 working title: The Machine Who Chose the Door

Demons, aliens, robots, villains, and monsters from other dimensions are invading the world through purple portals, all centered on one city. Doctor Balmson, the city's top scientist, has engineered Dyanadroid: an intelligent android with flight, incredible strength, green energy shields, green energy blasts, and a Master Portal sensor.

Dyanadroid is sent to find and close the Master Portal. He fears that if he fails, everyone dies, but if he succeeds, the world will have no further use for him and may shut him down. The issue follows his 17-stage Hero's Journey from created tool to autonomous being. In the end, after sealing the apocalypse, Dyanadroid chooses a new life in another dimension where his future is his own.

Theme: a being created for a purpose is not owned by that purpose.
Moral: intelligence, autonomy, and freedom belong together; a world that builds minds only to discard them should not be surprised when those minds choose themselves.
