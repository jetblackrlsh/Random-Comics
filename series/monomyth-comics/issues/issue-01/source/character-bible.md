## Dyanadroid

Tall athletic humanoid android with a smooth silver and graphite synthetic body, expressive emerald-green optical lenses, segmented face plates capable of subtle humanlike sorrow, green reactor glow in the chest, streamlined shoulder and forearm armor, black carbon-fiber joints, no cape, no exposed wires unless damaged. His green powers appear as energy shields, blasts, flight aura, sensor light, and portal-sealing energy.

Continuity arc: begins as emergency equipment, realizes he is a person, chooses to save the world without surrendering his future to it.

Reference image to generate and reuse: `series/monomyth-comics/reference-images/dyanadroid-character.png`

## Doctor Balmson

Exhausted middle-aged scientist in a rumpled white lab coat over dark emergency clothes, short graying hair, tired eyes, rectangular glasses, ID badge, tablet full of portal telemetry, green emergency lab light on his face. He is not gleefully evil; he is a utilitarian creator who cannot stop treating Dyanadroid as a mission asset.

Reference image to generate and reuse: `series/monomyth-comics/reference-images/doctor-balmson-character.png`

## Portal City

Dense modern city under apocalypse conditions: cracked asphalt, evacuation lights, emergency vehicles, ruined glass towers, smoke columns, purple portals above streets and rooftops, panicked crowds, barricades, green light from Dyanadroid's powers. Keep it believable and contemporary.

Reference image to generate and reuse: `series/monomyth-comics/reference-images/portal-city-setting.png`

## Doctor Balmson's Emergency Lab

Hardened underground emergency laboratory with flickering fluorescent lights, glass observation walls, scattered cables, portal telemetry monitors, an android deployment platform, green diagnostic lighting, and purple warning maps of the city.

Reference image to generate and reuse: `series/monomyth-comics/reference-images/doctor-balmsons-emergency-lab-setting.png`

## The Other Dimension

Luminous alien horizon beyond a purple threshold, unfamiliar sky geometry, impossible floating landforms, warm green reflections from Dyanadroid's power, and a quiet sense of openness after the urban disaster.

Reference image to generate and reuse: `series/monomyth-comics/reference-images/other-dimension-setting.png`

## Key Items

- Master Portal Sensor: compact rugged black wrist or handheld device with green holographic radar arcs and pulsing purple anomaly indicators.
- Master Portal: enormous unstable purple rift at the convergence point of all smaller portals, surrounded by cracked air, levitating debris, alien silhouettes, and green sealing energy.
- Green Energy Shield: translucent emerald barrier projected from Dyanadroid's hands or forearms, throwing green light onto smoke and metal.
