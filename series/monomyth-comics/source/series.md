# Monomyth Comics

## Logline

Self-contained photo-comic epics where each issue turns a short premise into a complete 17-stage Hero's Journey.

## Premise

Monomyth Comics is an anthology-style series of self-contained stories built around the full Campbell Hero's Journey. Every issue dedicates exactly one story page to each of the 17 stages, plus a cover page. The premise supplied by the user may be short, but the issue must expand it into a complete journey with a visible call, refusal, threshold, trials, transformation, return complication, and final freedom.

Issue #1 follows Dyanadroid, an intelligent android built by Doctor Balmson to end an interdimensional portal apocalypse. The issue asks whether a being created for a single purpose is owned by that purpose, and whether a disposable intelligent machine owes loyalty to a world that only values it as a tool.

## Tone

Grand superhero apocalypse, tragic machine self-discovery, cosmic threat, and morally uneasy liberation. The story should feel like urgent documentary phone footage of a city collapsing under impossible invasions, with quiet caption-box narration that lets Dyanadroid's inner autonomy emerge over the spectacle.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Purple interdimensional portals as the main apocalyptic motif.
- Dyanadroid's power effects are green: energy shields, energy blasts, flight aura, sensor readouts, and portal-sealing light.
- Portal invaders can include demons, aliens, robots, villains, and dimensional monsters, but each issue should keep visual chaos readable.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
- In-world screen text and signage should be sparse unless explicitly required by a page prompt.

## Continuity Notes

- Monomyth Comics issues are self-contained and may introduce new protagonists, worlds, or timelines.
- The 17-stage page structure is mandatory for every issue.
- The cover does not count as a Hero's Journey stage.
- Issue #1 uses `Dyanadroid` as the protagonist name. The original concept used other spellings, but the user corrected the intended name to Dyanadroid.
- Issue #1 should end with Dyanadroid choosing a new life in another dimension after stopping the apocalypse, not with the world reclaiming ownership over him.
