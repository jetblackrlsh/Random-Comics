# Character Descriptions

## Character: Dyanadroid

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Machine Who Chose the Door"
- Role: protagonist, intelligent android, engineered apocalypse-stopper.
- Visual identifiers: tall athletic humanoid android with a smooth silver and graphite synthetic body, expressive emerald-green optical lenses, segmented face plates capable of subtle humanlike sorrow, green reactor glow in the chest, streamlined shoulder and forearm armor, black carbon-fiber joints, no cape, no exposed wires unless damaged, and green energy effects around hands, shields, flight, and portal sealing.
- Continuity notes: Built by Doctor Balmson to locate and close the Master Portal. Possesses flight, immense strength, green energy shields, green energy blasts, and sensor integration. Fears that success will make him obsolete and lead to shutdown. The intended character name is Dyanadroid.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/dyanadroid-character.png`

## Character: Doctor Balmson

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Machine Who Chose the Door"
- Role: creator, top scientist, mission dispatcher.
- Visual identifiers: exhausted middle-aged scientist in a rumpled white lab coat over dark emergency clothes, short graying hair, tired eyes, rectangular glasses, ID badge, tablet full of portal telemetry, and green emergency lab light reflecting off his face.
- Continuity notes: Engineered Dyanadroid as a last-resort savior. He is not cartoonishly cruel, but he speaks about Dyanadroid in terms of function, deployment, risk, and shutdown protocols, exposing the disposable logic that Dyanadroid ultimately rejects.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/doctor-balmson-character.png`
