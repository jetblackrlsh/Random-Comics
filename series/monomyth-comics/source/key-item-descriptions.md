# Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: Master Portal Sensor

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Machine Who Chose the Door"
- Visual identifiers: compact handheld or wrist-mounted sensor device with rugged black casing, green holographic radar arcs, pulsing purple anomaly indicators, scratched emergency-use surface, and a cable or wireless sync to Dyanadroid's systems.
- Continuity notes: Doctor Balmson sends Dyanadroid into the city with this device to locate the Master Portal. Its readings become both practical navigation and a symbol of assigned purpose.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/master-portal-sensor-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Master Portal

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Machine Who Chose the Door"
- Visual identifiers: enormous unstable purple rift hidden at the convergence of many smaller portals, ringed by cracked air, levitating debris, alien silhouettes, and bright green sealing energy when Dyanadroid confronts it.
- Continuity notes: Closing the Master Portal ends the apocalypse but also gives Dyanadroid the opportunity to leave through it before the world can reclaim or discard him.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/master-portal-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Green Energy Shield

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Machine Who Chose the Door"
- Visual identifiers: translucent emerald hexagonal or circular energy barrier projected from Dyanadroid's hands or forearms, bright but photoreal, throwing green light onto rain, dust, smoke, and metal.
- Continuity notes: Dyanadroid uses shields to protect civilians and survive dimensional attacks. It visually expresses the tension between being built to protect others and needing to protect himself.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/green-energy-shield-key-item.png`
