# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The Machine Who Chose the Door

- Folder: `series/monomyth-comics/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Built to stop an interdimensional apocalypse and then be discarded, Dyanadroid follows the Master Portal into a choice no creator planned for him: save a world that owns him, or save himself.
- Plot summary: Doctor Balmson activates Dyanadroid as purple portals begin turning Portal City into the hinge of a global apocalypse. Dyanadroid receives the Master Portal Sensor, leaves the lab, survives the invaded city, saves civilians, and discovers that his mission file ends with his own obsolescence. After confronting Balmson and realizing purpose is not ownership, he finds the Master Portal and learns it can only be sealed from the other side. Helped by civilians he saved, Dyanadroid closes the apocalypse while stepping into another dimension, choosing a future that belongs to him.
- Continuity changes: Establishes Monomyth Comics as a 17-stage Hero's Journey series. Establishes Dyanadroid's self-liberation, Doctor Balmson's creator role, Portal City, the Master Portal, and the other dimension.
- New characters: Dyanadroid; Doctor Balmson.
- New settings: Portal City; Doctor Balmson's Emergency Lab; The Other Dimension.
- New key items: Master Portal Sensor; Master Portal; Green Energy Shield.
