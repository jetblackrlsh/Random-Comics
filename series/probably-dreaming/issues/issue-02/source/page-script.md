# Page Script

## Global Prompt Requirements

Every page must be a complete full realistic candid photo-comic page in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo. All readable story text, title text, caption text, and any clothing text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not leave blank caption boxes for later typesetting.

Use clear, easy-to-follow wording in the caption boxes. Story pages should use five or six panels with straightforward cause-and-effect storytelling.

Jet Black continuity: Black man in his 30s with medium-brown skin, long black dreadlocks past his shoulders, full beard and mustache, black zip hoodie with white drawstrings, black shirt under the hoodie with visible colorful text reading "Dream Anime", black pants, white sneakers, small black crossbody/fanny pack across the front. Use realistic candid photo-comic styling based on the provided protagonist reference.

Dracula continuity: pale tall aristocratic vampire, slick black hair, sharp cheekbones, red-lined black cape, formal black suit, white shirt, dark vest, blood-red eyes during hypnosis, long fingers, sharp fangs. He can appear as a gray fog cloud and as a large black wolf with glowing red eyes.

## Page 1: Cover

- Filename: `assets/comic-pages/page-01-cover.png`
- Story role: Distinct cover page for Issue #2, with enough setup for new readers without using a separate prologue page.
- Layout: One dramatic realistic photo-comic cover image. Jet sits up in bed smiling with delighted confidence while Dracula looms over him in a red-lined cape. Gray fog curls from the apartment air vent, faint purple Lucid Light glimmers around Jet's raised hand, and pre-dawn Huntsville light barely edges the window blinds.
- Caption boxes:
  - Title text: "Probably Dreaming"
  - Subtitle text: "Issue #2: Fangs for the Dream"
  - Small setup line: "Jet treats every nightmare like a lucid dream."
  - Small cover line: "Dracula chose the wrong breakfast."
- Visual beats: The cover should signal vampire horror comedy, Jet's confidence, the ordinary apartment setting, and the dream/reality ambiguity.

## Page 2: Story Page One

- Filename: `assets/comic-pages/page-02.png`
- Story role: Dracula enters Jet's apartment and wakes him while trying to reach his neck.
- Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: exterior pre-dawn Huntsville sky above apartment buildings, with a gray fog cloud drifting after a long night. Panel 2: fog squeezes through Jet's bedroom air vent. Panel 3: Dracula reforms beside the bed while Jet sleeps under rumpled sheets. Panel 4: Dracula leans toward Jet's neck but Jet's long dreadlocks cover the bite spot. Panel 5: Dracula carefully moves the dreadlocks aside with clawed fingers, waking Jet. Panel 6: Dracula looms over Jet with fangs exposed.
- Caption boxes:
  - Caption 1: "Before dawn, Dracula slipped into Jet's apartment as fog."
  - Caption 2: "After hunting the skies of Huntsville, he was hungry."
  - Caption 3: "Jet's blood was supposed to be breakfast."
  - Caption 4: "Jet's dreadlocks blocked the bite."
  - Dracula caption: "It is time to feast."
- Visual beats: Keep the scene creepy but readable. Jet is asleep until panel 5. Dracula should look confident and hungry.

## Page 3: Story Page Two

- Filename: `assets/comic-pages/page-03.png`
- Story role: Jet reacts with joy and punches Dracula, then explains his dream identity.
- Layout: Five panels. Panel 1: Dracula expects terror, but Jet's eyes are wide with ecstatic delight. Panel 2: Jet springs out of bed with sudden athletic energy. Panel 3: Jet punches Dracula hard in the face, cape flying and Dracula's head snapping sideways. Panel 4: Dracula stumbles back, shocked by the force. Panel 5: Jet stands proudly on the bed or floor, pointing to himself like a heroic dream adventurer while Dracula looks confused.
- Caption boxes:
  - Caption 1: "Dracula expected fear."
  - Caption 2: "Jet looked thrilled instead."
  - Jet caption: "A vampire dream? Perfect."
  - Caption 3: "Jet hit harder than Dracula thought possible."
  - Dracula caption: "What are you?"
  - Jet caption: "In dreams, I am a Lucid Lord."
- Visual beats: Make Jet's punch clear and physically strong. Dracula should shift from predatory to baffled.

## Page 4: Story Page Three

- Filename: `assets/comic-pages/page-04.png`
- Story role: Jet tries to summon Lucid Light, fails, and Dracula realizes Jet is vulnerable.
- Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: Jet closes his eyes and visualizes a sword made of cosmic purple Lucid Light. Panel 2: faint purple motes gather around Jet's empty hand. Panel 3: Jet poses dramatically, but no sword appears. Panel 4: Dracula squints at Jet's empty hand, puzzled. Panel 5: Jet explains the problem with calm embarrassment. Panel 6: Dracula laughs triumphantly, cape spread wide in the bedroom.
- Caption boxes:
  - Caption 1: "Jet tried to summon a sword of Lucid Light."
  - Caption 2: "He focused as hard as he could."
  - Caption 3: "Nothing formed in his hand."
  - Dracula caption: "Is the sword invisible?"
  - Jet caption: "Sometimes I need a Lucid Conduit first."
  - Dracula caption: "Then you have no chance."
- Visual beats: The imagined sword should be only a ghostly purple outline or mental image, not a real weapon. The failed magic must be obvious.

## Page 5: Story Page Four

- Filename: `assets/comic-pages/page-05.png`
- Story role: Dracula tries hypnosis, but Jet resists by poking him in the eyes.
- Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: Dracula regains composure and studies Jet like a recruit. Panel 2: Dracula's eyes glow red as he begins hypnotic control. Panel 3: red light washes toward Jet's face, but Jet lowers into a quick charge. Panel 4: Jet pokes Dracula directly in both eyes with two fingers. Panel 5: Dracula recoils in pain, clutching his face. Panel 6: Jet explains calmly while Dracula squints in agony.
- Caption boxes:
  - Dracula caption: "With that strength, you could serve me."
  - Caption 1: "Dracula opened his hypnotic vision."
  - Caption 2: "Jet did not wait for the spell to settle."
  - Jet caption: "I have dealt with hypnosis before."
  - Jet caption: "Past Life Regression taught me resistance."
  - Dracula caption: "Was your past life Van Helsing?"
- Visual beats: Keep it physical and comedic. The red hypnosis effect should be clear but interrupted.

## Page 6: Story Page Five

- Filename: `assets/comic-pages/page-06.png`
- Story role: Jet claims an alien past life, Dracula transforms into a wolf, and attacks.
- Layout: Five panels. Panel 1: Jet answers sincerely, making Dracula even more confused. Panel 2: Dracula glares, torn between disbelief and hunger. Panel 3: Dracula declares he will not leave without feeding. Panel 4: Dracula transforms into a large black wolf in a burst of cape, fog, and shadow. Panel 5: the wolf lunges across the bedroom and knocks Jet down.
- Caption boxes:
  - Jet caption: "No. I was an interdimensional alien."
  - Caption 1: "Dracula became more confused than before."
  - Dracula caption: "I do not know what to believe."
  - Dracula caption: "But I will still feast."
  - Caption 2: "He became a wolf and pounced."
- Visual beats: The transformation should be dramatic but still photoreal. Jet should look surprised, not terrified.

## Page 7: Story Page Six

- Filename: `assets/comic-pages/page-07.png`
- Story role: Jet's certainty that he is dreaming burns Dracula like faith, and Jet knocks him out.
- Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: Dracula's wolf form pins Jet to the floor beside the bed. Panel 2: the wolf's paws begin burning with pale gold-white heat where they press against Jet. Panel 3: Dracula transforms back to vampire form and retreats in horror, smoking hands held up. Panel 4: Dracula realizes Jet's dream-faith is hurting him. Panel 5: Jet launches forward with a confident uppercut. Panel 6: Dracula collapses unconscious on the bedroom floor.
- Caption boxes:
  - Caption 1: "The wolf pinned Jet down."
  - Caption 2: "Then Dracula's paws began to burn."
  - Dracula caption: "Your faith that this is a dream..."
  - Dracula caption: "It weakens me like holy faith."
  - Caption 3: "While Dracula panicked, Jet moved."
  - Caption 4: "One uppercut ended the fight."
- Visual beats: Do not add religious symbols. The faith effect is shown through light and Dracula's reaction. The knockout should be clear.

## Page 8: Story Page Seven

- Filename: `assets/comic-pages/page-08.png`
- Story role: Jet searches for a Lucid Conduit, accidentally destroys Dracula with sunlight, and assumes it was a dream.
- Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: Dracula lies unconscious on the floor while Jet scans the bedroom for purple Lucid Light. Panel 2: Jet looks toward the closed blinds, thinking the conduit might be outside. Panel 3: Jet opens the blinds and dawn sunlight pours in. Panel 4: behind Jet, Dracula's body turns to ash in the sunlight, but Jet is looking out the window. Panel 5: Jet turns back and sees the floor empty except for a gray dust smear. Panel 6: Jet shrugs, relaxed and unconcerned, as ordinary morning fills the room.
- Caption boxes:
  - Caption 1: "With Dracula down, Jet searched for a Lucid Conduit."
  - Jet caption: "Maybe the conduit is outside."
  - Caption 2: "Dawn answered before Jet noticed."
  - Caption 3: "Sunlight turned Dracula to dust behind him."
  - Jet caption: "Guess I was dreaming."
  - Caption 4: "Probably."
- Visual beats: The reader must clearly see Dracula dusting away while Jet misses it. End with Jet casual and the room mostly normal.
