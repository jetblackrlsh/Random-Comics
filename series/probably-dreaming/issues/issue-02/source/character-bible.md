## Jet Black

Black man in his 30s with medium-brown skin, long black dreadlocks past his shoulders, full beard and mustache, warm smile, slim-to-average build, relaxed posture, black zip hoodie with white drawstrings, black shirt under the hoodie with visible colorful text reading "Dream Anime", black pants, white sneakers, small black crossbody/fanny pack worn across the front.

In this issue Jet begins asleep in bed before dawn. His dreadlocks spread across his pillow and neck, accidentally blocking Dracula's bite. Once awake he is ecstatic rather than afraid, because he believes the vampire attack proves he is dreaming. His body language should be delighted, confident, and practical even during danger.

## Dracula

One-issue antagonist. A pale, tall, aristocratic vampire with slick black hair, sharp cheekbones, red-lined black cape, formal black suit, white shirt, dark vest, blood-red eyes when using hypnosis, long fingers, and sharp fangs. He is theatrical and predatory, but becomes increasingly confused by Jet's dream logic.

Dracula can enter as a gray fog cloud, transform into a large black wolf with glowing red eyes, use hypnotic red vision, and burn under dawn sunlight. He ends the issue destroyed by sunlight, so he is not recorded as a recurring continuity element.

## Settings

Emerald Ridge Apartment is Jet's lived-in home base. For this issue, focus on the bedroom before dawn: a modest bed, rumpled sheets, nearby nightstand, air vent, closed blinds, faint computer equipment or AI comic notes visible through the room, and ordinary apartment details. The final page uses the same room with warm dawn light pouring through the blinds.

Huntsville outside the window appears briefly as ordinary pre-dawn apartment complex scenery: low apartment buildings, parked cars, trees, and a pale sunrise.

## Powers And Effects

Lucid Light is cosmic purple energy with star-like motes, translucent ribbons, soft glow, lens distortions, and impossible reflections. In this issue Jet imagines a sword made of Lucid Light, but the sword never materializes. Show only a faint imagined purple outline or glimmer when the power fails.

A Lucid Conduit is discussed but not found. Jet searches for one after Dracula is knocked out.

Dracula's hypnosis appears as red light in his eyes and subtle red spiral reflections around Jet's face. Jet resists it by acting physically before it can settle.

Jet's dream-faith weakening Dracula should look like the paws of the wolf form burning with pale gold-white heat where they press against Jet, echoing the visual logic of religious faith without adding religious symbols.
