# Probably Dreaming: Fangs for the Dream

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

Dracula sneaks into Jet Black's apartment before dawn to drink his blood, but Jet treats the attack like a lucid dream challenge and accidentally defeats the vampire with dream-faith, practical violence, and morning sunlight.

## Central Conflict

Dracula wants to feed on Jet or turn him into a soldier, while Jet wants to prove he is a Lucid Lord and find a Lucid Conduit before the dream gets boring or dangerous.

## Theme

Belief changes how a person faces fear, and Jet's absolute faith that the nightmare is a dream becomes stronger than Dracula expects.

## Ending

Jet knocks Dracula out, searches for a Lucid Conduit, and opens the blinds. Dawn sunlight destroys Dracula behind him. Jet misses the evidence and decides the whole thing was probably a dream.

## Emotional Arc

Jet moves from groggy confusion, to delighted dream confidence, to slight embarrassment when Lucid Light fails, to practical resistance, to casual victory and denial. Dracula moves from predatory confidence, to confusion, to curiosity, to rage, to fear.
