# Treatment

## Title

The Last Bloodline

## Issue Number

8

## Logline

When an ageless sorcerer reaches Meridian to murder Radiant and steal the final share of their family magic, the heroine must accept that her origin is magical rather than alien and discover that chosen family matters more than blood.

## Central Conflict

Maelor believes family is a finite source of power that belongs to the last survivor. Lyra believes their shared blood is a reason to stop killing, while Caelum and her heroic friends prove through action that family can be created through loyalty rather than inherited through ancestry.

## Theme

Family is made by love, trust, and the decision to remain beside someone—not merely by blood or birthplace.

## Story-Clarity Approach

Every major cause and consequence is stated directly in caption boxes: why Maelor kills relatives, why Ardan changes and exiles Lyra, why Maelor can pursue her for millennia, why Meridian mistakes her for an alien, why Lyra and Caelum suppress their love, how the battle proves her magical ancestry, why Maelor still wants her dead, how her friends weaken him, why his power transfers to Lyra, and how both heroes reinterpret family afterward.

## Ending

After Lyra and Caelum kill Maelor together, the bloodline's complete magic enters Lyra. Both heroes grieve the biological homes they have now lost beyond recovery, but their friends help them understand that the bonds they built in Meridian were already family. Certain that they are not related, Lyra and Caelum finally share a quiet first kiss above the city.

## Emotional Arc

Lyra begins with an uncertain identity but a secure chosen community. Maelor's revelation gives her the truth she wanted in the cruelest possible form and forces her to destroy the last living person connected to her origin. Caelum begins by treating Lyra as the last surviving fragment of his planet and loses that hope. They end by choosing each other without using biology to justify their bond.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, ancient bloodline crime and Ardan's rescue, Lyra's modern life and forbidden love, Maelor's revelation, the heroes' defeat, the chosen-family counterattack, Maelor's death and the transfer of power, and an emotional resolution above Meridian.
