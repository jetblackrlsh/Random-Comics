# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Every readable word must be generated directly inside the page art. Use roomy rectangular caption boxes only: no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, blank text boxes, caption labels, caption numbers, panel labels, page numbers, watermarks, or later text overlay. The exact captions below are intentionally direct so no important cause, motivation, rule, action, or emotional consequence is left for the reader to infer.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Establish Lyra, Caelum, Maelor, Meridian, and the bloodline-versus-chosen-family conflict.
- Layout: Dramatic single-image cover with realistic photo-comic texture.
- Title text: "Superhero Action Comics" and "The Last Bloodline".
- Visual beats: Radiant and Caelum fly side by side above Meridian, visibly distinct but sharing parallel white-gold and blue-white power trails. Maelor towers behind them inside a red-violet dimensional tear, rune-covered hands reaching toward Lyra. The crystal vessel floats as a ghosted ancient image below Lyra, while Captain Strategist, Laser Light, and Geomancer stand together on a rooftop beneath the two flying heroes.
- Prompt notes: Generate only the two exact titles as readable cover text. No captions, logos, issue number, or extra text.

## Page 2: The Magic That Murder Left Behind

- Story role: Explain the bloodline rule, Ardan's fear, Lyra's transformation and exile, Ardan's defeat, Maelor's stolen power, agelessness, and long hunt.
- Layout: Four large panels with roomy caption boxes.
- Caption boxes: "Long ago beneath the sea, the sorcerer Maelor murdered his own relatives because each death divided the victim's magic among the surviving bloodline." "Ardan knew his evil brother would eventually target his infant granddaughter, Lyra, so he remade her with flight, immense strength, blazing eyes, freezing breath, and nearly indestructible flesh." "He sealed Lyra inside a crystal vessel and sent her thousands of years into the future, beyond Maelor's reach." "Maelor defeated Ardan, inherited most of his power, and discovered that one missing share still lived. Made ageless by stolen magic, he hunted Lyra across centuries, time, and strange dimensions."
- Visual beats: Maelor draining red-violet magic from fallen relatives in an ancient undersea hall; Ardan compassionately transforming infant Lyra in a pale turquoise spell; the crystal vessel streaking through a spiraling time corridor; Maelor standing over defeated Ardan as branching centuries and dimensions open around him.
- Continuity notes: Ardan and Maelor share olive skin and angular family features, but Ardan's magic is pale turquoise and Maelor's is red-violet. The infant is protected and unharmed.

## Page 3: The Family Meridian Gave Her

- Story role: Explain Lyra's arrival, lost memory, mistaken alien identity, training, heroic friendships, Caelum's emotional hope, and the love they refuse to pursue.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: "Lyra's vessel finally opened in modern Meridian, where Caelum—the world's greatest champion and the last known survivor of an alien planet—found her." "Because Lyra shared Caelum's powers and remembered almost nothing, everyone assumed she was another survivor of his species." "Caelum trained her until she mastered her gifts and became Radiant. Captain Strategist, Laser Light, and Geomancer accepted her as a friend without demanding proof of where she came from." "Lyra and Caelum fell in love, but they refused to act on it while they might be biological relatives. He treasured her as the only family he believed remained from his lost world."
- Visual beats: Caelum kneeling beside the newly opened vessel on a Meridian rooftop; the two heroes flying and using matched eye beams and freezing breath during training; Radiant laughing with Captain Strategist, Laser Light, and Geomancer after a rescue; Lyra and Caelum sharing a restrained longing look while deliberately standing apart above the city.

## Page 4: The Truth Attacks

- Story role: Reveal Lyra's magical ancestry and prove it through the heroes' different reactions to Maelor's spells.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: "Maelor finally tore into Meridian and declared that Lyra was not an alien, but the last heir of his magical bloodline." "Caelum rejected the claim and attacked to protect her." "Maelor's spell nearly stopped Caelum's alien heart; his body was dangerously vulnerable to magic." "The same spell hurt Lyra far less. When she felt Maelor's bloodline magic answer inside her, she knew his terrible claim was true."
- Visual beats: Maelor steps from a red-violet dimensional wound over Meridian; Caelum charges him; a curse collapses Caelum mid-flight with red-violet bands around his chest; Lyra catches Caelum while a similar spell splashes off her skin and lights a matching family rune inside her palm.

## Page 5: Why Blood Made Her a Target

- Story role: State Lyra's attempt at peace, Maelor's motive, the full range of Lyra's powers, and the heroes' defeat by centuries of preparation.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: "Lyra begged Maelor to stop. With every other relative dead, the two of them were the only family each had left." "Maelor answered that their shared blood was exactly why she had to die: her death would transfer the missing family power into him." "Lyra fought with blazing eyes, freezing breath, strength, flight, and nearly indestructible flesh." "But Maelor had prepared for centuries. His layered spells bound Lyra, crushed Caelum, and brought both heroes to defeat."
- Visual beats: Lyra lowers her hands and pleads while shielding injured Caelum; Maelor points toward the matching family rune within her; a fast cause-and-effect montage of Radiant blasting, freezing, flying, and punching through summoned obstacles; Maelor's layered wards redirect every attack and leave Lyra and Caelum pinned on a shattered civic terrace.

## Page 6: Chosen Family Arrives

- Story role: Make the friends' intervention clear, show how their different abilities work together, and create the final opening.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: "Before Maelor could kill Lyra, the friends she had made as Radiant arrived. They came because chosen family does not abandon its own." "Captain Strategist identified Maelor's linked wards and timed one precise combined attack between their pulses." "Geomancer trapped Maelor's feet and broke the ground sigils while Laser Light flooded his shadow shield until it shattered." "Their different abilities weakened Maelor's defenses and gave Lyra and Caelum one final opening."
- Visual beats: Captain Strategist, Laser Light, and Geomancer land between Maelor and the fallen heroes; Captain studies pulsing wards on his wrist comm and calls the timing with hand signals; stone clamps rise and crack the ground runes exactly as rainbow-white light pierces the shadow shield; Maelor stands exposed for the first time as Lyra and Caelum look up.

## Page 7: The Last Inheritance

- Story role: Resolve the battle, transfer the bloodline magic, and show both Lyra's and Caelum's grief.
- Layout: Four large panels.
- Caption boxes: "Lyra and Caelum rose together and struck Maelor before his wards could rebuild." "Maelor died, and the full power of the bloodline passed into Lyra, making Radiant stronger than ever." "Victory gave her no joy. She mourned the last living connection to the forgotten home Ardan had sacrificed everything to save her from." "Caelum grieved too. The truth proved that Lyra was not the surviving piece of his lost planet that he had hoped she was."
- Visual beats: Radiant and Caelum launch a synchronized final strike; Maelor disappears non-graphically into red-violet sparks and empty robes; the magic turns white-gold and enters Lyra, intensifying her aura and eyes; Lyra kneels alone beside the empty robes before the final panel separates her grief from Caelum's grief beneath the night sky.

## Page 8: Family Above Meridian

- Story role: Resolve the theme, remove the biological barrier to romance, and end on the requested first kiss.
- Layout: Four large panels with increasing openness and a wide final panel.
- Caption boxes: "In the quiet days that followed, Lyra understood that Captain Strategist, Laser Light, and Geomancer had already treated her as family without shared blood or birthplace." "Caelum understood the same truth: losing a biological connection did not erase the home they had built together." "Now that they knew they were not related, the fear that had kept their love buried was gone." "Above Meridian, Radiant and Caelum chose each other and shared their first quiet kiss."
- Visual beats: Lyra recuperates with all three friends beside her; Caelum joins the group and receives a hand on his shoulder; Lyra and Caelum rise into the calm sunset sky while the friends watch from a rooftop; wide final image of their gentle first kiss floating above Meridian's gold-lit towers, with no battle energy and no spectacle beyond the peaceful city.
