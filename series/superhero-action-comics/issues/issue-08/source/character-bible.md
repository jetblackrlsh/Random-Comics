# Character And Reference Bible

## Lyra / Radiant

- Series continuity: New recurring hero introduced in Issue #8, "The Last Bloodline."
- Role: protagonist, ancient magical heir mistaken for an alien survivor.
- Visual identifiers: Athletic woman in her mid-20s with warm olive-brown skin, expressive amber eyes that blaze white-gold when powered, long black curls with one natural silver-white streak at the right temple, strong oval face, indigo fitted flight suit with pearl-white armored shoulders and ribs, gold sun-disc chest emblem, pearl-white gauntlets and boots, short translucent pale-gold cape, and a soft white-gold aura in flight.
- Behavior: Compassionate, direct, courageous, and deeply loyal. Lyra wants the truth about her origin but refuses Maelor's belief that blood gives one relative ownership over another.
- Powers and limits: Flight, immense strength, blazing eye beams, freezing breath, nearly indestructible flesh, and bloodline magic that becomes vastly stronger after Maelor dies. Maelor's spells can hurt and bind her, but they injure her far less than they injure Caelum.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/lyra-radiant-character.png`

## Caelum

- Series continuity: New recurring hero introduced in Issue #8, "The Last Bloodline."
- Role: co-protagonist, world's greatest champion, last known survivor of an alien planet, Lyra's mentor and eventual romantic partner.
- Visual identifiers: Tall, extremely muscular Black man in his early 30s with deep brown skin, calm ice-blue eyes, close-cropped black hair with a silver sheen, broad square face, cobalt-blue fitted superhero suit with white armored ribs and shoulders, silver eight-point star chest emblem, silver gauntlets and boots, short white cape, and cool blue-white energy around his hands and feet in flight.
- Behavior: Protective, principled, and quietly lonely. He treasures Lyra as the family he thought survived his world and initially reacts to Maelor's claim with denial and force.
- Powers and limits: Flight, immense strength, blazing eye beams, freezing breath, and near invulnerability. His alien body is dangerously vulnerable to magic, so Maelor's spells can stop his heart and crush his strength.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/caelum-character.png`

## Maelor

- Series continuity: Major antagonist introduced and killed in Issue #8, "The Last Bloodline."
- Role: ageless bloodline murderer and hunter of Lyra.
- Visual identifiers: Lean, imposing man who appears in his late 50s, pale olive skin, sharp cheekbones, deep-set violet eyes, shoulder-length wet-black hair with silver at both temples, trimmed black-and-silver beard, black-green layered sorcerer robes over barnacle-dark scale armor, long hands covered in luminous red-violet family runes, and a cracked-circle bloodline sigil at his throat.
- Behavior: Patient, possessive, and utterly convinced that relatives are reservoirs of magic rather than people. He has spent centuries preparing counters for Lyra's powers and speaks about murder as inheritance.
- Powers and limits: Ageless life, dimensional travel, red-violet binding bands, heart-stopping curses, layered magical wards, shadow shields, force blasts, and stolen bloodline magic accumulated from murdered relatives. Coordinated attacks against his linked wards can briefly expose him.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/maelor-character.png`

## Ardan

- Series continuity: Minor named character introduced and killed in Issue #8, "The Last Bloodline."
- Role: Lyra's grandfather and protector.
- Visual identifiers: Elderly undersea sorcerer with warm olive-brown skin, long white beard, kind amber eyes, flowing white hair, layered sea-blue robes, pearl-and-bronze charms, and pale turquoise magic around his hands.
- Continuity notes: Ardan discovers Maelor's murders, transforms infant Lyra into a superhuman protector, seals her in the crystal vessel, sends her thousands of years forward, and is defeated soon afterward. His sacrifice saves Lyra and keeps part of the bloodline's magic away from Maelor.

## Captain Strategist

- Series continuity: Returning hero from Issue #2, "Crystal Hell."
- Role: Lyra's friend and tactical coordinator of the rescue.
- Visual identifiers: Young team leader with tan skin, close black hair, blue tactical captain coat over lightweight armor, white star-and-chevron chest emblem, red gloves, calm eyes, and a small wrist comm.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/captain-strategist-character.png`

## Laser Light

- Series continuity: Returning hero from Issue #2, "Crystal Hell."
- Role: Lyra's friend whose photokinetic attack breaks Maelor's shadow ward.
- Visual identifiers: Teen-to-young-adult heroine with warm brown skin, braided dark hair pulled back, reflective yellow goggles, white-and-gold jacket over practical light armor, glowing fingertip prisms, and rainbow-white light trails.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/laser-light-character.png`

## Geomancer

- Series continuity: Returning hero from Issue #2, "Crystal Hell."
- Role: Lyra's friend whose earth control traps Maelor and breaks his ground sigils.
- Visual identifiers: Powerful young heroine with dark skin, shaved sides and thick natural curls, green-and-stone-gray armored suit, heavy boots, stone bracers, floating pebbles orbiting her hands, and a grounded stance.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/geomancer-character.png`

## Meridian

- Series continuity: New recurring city introduced in Issue #8, "The Last Bloodline."
- Role: Lyra and Caelum's modern home and the issue's main battlefield.
- Visual identifiers: Optimistic contemporary coastal metropolis with pale-gold glass towers, white stone civic terraces, a broad blue river entering the sea, radial avenues, elevated rail lines, rooftop gardens, rescue beacons, and a distinctive needle-shaped central tower with a circular observation crown.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/meridian-city-setting.png`

## Lyra's Crystal Vessel

- Series continuity: New key item introduced in Issue #8, "The Last Bloodline."
- Role: Magical time capsule that carries infant Lyra beyond Maelor's reach.
- Visual identifiers: Human-length faceted crystal cradle, clear outer shell with pale turquoise light inside, bronze family runes around both pointed ends, suspended pearl-like control stones, and a soft protective field surrounding the sleeping infant.
- Continuity notes: Ardan seals Lyra inside and sends the vessel thousands of years into the future. It eventually opens in Meridian, where Caelum discovers her.
