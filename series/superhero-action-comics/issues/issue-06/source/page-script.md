# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text, title text, and any other readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. Prefer 4-6 clear panels on story pages so the action reads step by step.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Establish Makiko, the shorebound narrator, the Cosmic Seekers, and the starlit sea threat.
- Layout: Dramatic single-image cover with documentary photo-comic texture.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Action Comics". Smaller issue title text: "The Seekers and Makiko".
- Visual beats: Makiko stands at the edge of black surf with her blue-glowing water-worn blade raised, matte-black ninja armor rimmed by starlight. The Shorebound Witness crouches behind her on wet sand, clutching a dead phone. Several translucent Cosmic Seekers breach in a ring of cyan-violet light while a much larger shadow rises beneath liquid-starlight waves.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated directly in the image. No logos or extra unreadable text.

## Page 2: The City Under The Waves

- Story role: Establish the beach, the narrator's curiosity, and the first sign that the lights are dangerous.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "The beach looked peaceful because the danger had not surfaced yet." "Waves of liquid starlight rolled under a sky full of falling stars." "Below them, lights gathered like streets in a drowned city." "I could not swim, but curiosity pulled me closer anyway." "I raised my phone to record proof." "The sea answered first."
- Visual beats: Wide panel of lonely black-sand beach and distant hotel lights; close starlight waves with strange luminous life below; underwater lights suggesting a city grid; the Shorebound Witness stepping near the surf, anxious but fascinated; phone camera view distorting; a burst of cosmic light rising from the deep.

## Page 3: The Seekers Surface

- Story role: Show the phone destroyed and the predators beginning their hypnotic attack.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "The burst killed the phone in my hand." "The screen screamed white, then went black." "The light had called hunters." "Cosmic Seekers breached from the shallows." "Their bodies pulsed in the same rhythm." "My fear slowed into obedience."
- Visual beats: Dead phone smoking faintly in the narrator's hand; his face lit by harsh cyan glare; several dolphin-sized translucent Seekers breaching near shore; their light patterns syncing in rings; the narrator's eyes glazing as the pattern reflects in them; sand pulling under his feet as a massive shape shifts offshore.

## Page 4: Makiko In The Dark

- Story role: Bring Makiko into the fight and explain the core threat.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "Makiko arrived without a footstep." "Her blade cut a blue line through the hypnotic glow." "She had been tracking these attacks for months." "Cosmic Seekers do not only hunt bodies." "Their lights synchronize with minds." "A docile host walks into the sea."
- Visual beats: Makiko materializing from darkness at frame edge; her black armor and mask reflecting starlight; blade unsheathing with a blue line that interrupts light patterns; Makiko stepping between narrator and water; close view of Seekers recoiling from the blade glow; final panel showing a ghostly imagined victim walking calmly into waves under Seeker light.

## Page 5: Use Your Voice

- Story role: Turn the narrator from liability into tactical partner.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "I asked what help I could be." "Makiko saw what I had missed." "My voice had shaken their rhythm." "She went for the larger shadow." "I stayed with the only weapon I owned." "Seekers, leave us be; return to sea."
- Visual beats: The narrator behind Makiko, frightened and honest; Makiko noticing Seeker light stutter when he speaks; close panel of sound waves disrupting cyan-violet light rings; the huge offshore shadow causing a wave to rise; Makiko leaping toward the water with blade glowing; narrator standing alone in shallow foam, speaking the rhyme in a caption box.

## Page 6: The Leviathan Rises

- Story role: Escalate with Makiko fighting the larger creature while the narrator strains to hold the swarm back.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "The swarm scattered when Makiko hit the water." "The deep shape rose like a living reef." "Its crown-lure commanded every smaller light." "Makiko climbed the wave instead of running from it." "My rhyme held the swarm for one breath." "Then fear broke my meter."
- Visual beats: Makiko diving through a cresting cosmic wave; bus-sized Leviathan Seeker emerging under the surface with crown-lure blazing; smaller Seekers turning in perfect formation toward narrator; Makiko running along a collapsing wave face with ninja agility; narrator speaking as light patterns stutter; his expression panicking as one line falters.

## Page 7: No Way Back

- Story role: Show the low point and the narrator's decisive choice.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "I tried to retreat toward the hotel." "The hotel was too far." "One Seeker broke from the circle." "Its light filled my whole world." "I was still afraid." "But I did not go quiet."
- Visual beats: Narrator backing away across wet sand, distant hotel impossibly far; a Seeker accelerating from the surf; Makiko silhouetted in the background battling the leviathan's crown-lure; narrator falling backward as the Seeker looms; close panel of hypnotic light reflecting in his eyes; final panel of him gripping sand and opening his mouth to speak.

## Page 8: Break The Pattern

- Story role: Resolve the crisis through teamwork and leave the survivors changed.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "In cosmic seas you swim and play." "But human minds are not your prey." "Makiko cut the crown-lure." "The pattern shattered." "The Seekers fled to the deep." "We walked back with no proof, only the warning."
- Visual beats: Narrator shouting the completed rhyme while the lunging Seeker's lights fragment; Makiko slashing the leviathan's crown-lure in a burst of blue and cyan spray; every Seeker light pattern breaking out of sync; predators retreating into the starlit sea and the huge shape sinking; Makiko helping the narrator stand on the quiet beach; final panel of the two walking toward distant hotel lights under shooting stars, dead phone left dark in his hand.
