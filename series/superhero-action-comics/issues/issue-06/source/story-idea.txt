Adapted from the provided AI Dungeon gameplay session "The Seekers and Makiko."

On a lonely cosmic beach, a shorebound narrator watches waves of liquid starlight reveal what might be an underwater city. When he tries to record the lights, a burst of cosmic energy kills his phone and draws ancient bioluminescent predators called Cosmic Seekers toward shore. Makiko, his ninja assassin girlfriend, appears from the darkness with a blue-glowing water-worn blade and explains that the Seekers synchronize their lights with humanoid brainwaves to make prey docile. The narrator cannot swim and fears he will only get in the way, but Makiko realizes his calm voice disrupts the creatures' light patterns. As a massive shape rises from the depths, Makiko dives after the larger threat while the narrator uses improvised rhymes to hold the smaller Seekers back. When a Seeker lunges and his courage almost fails, he chooses to stand his ground, completes the rhyme, and helps Makiko break the swarm's synchronization. Together they drive the predators back into the starlit sea and survive the night.
