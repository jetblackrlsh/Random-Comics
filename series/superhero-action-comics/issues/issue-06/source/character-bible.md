# Character And Reference Bible

## Makiko

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #6, "The Seekers and Makiko."
- Role: ninja assassin protector, cosmic-shore investigator, protagonist.
- Visual identifiers: Athletic Japanese woman in her late 20s, sharp dark eyes, black hair tied in a short practical knot, matte-black tactical ninja armor with wet sand and sea-salt scuffs, fitted mask over the lower face, flexible gloves and boots, dark utility wraps, and an obsidian mask and shoulder plates that catch starlight along the edges.
- Behavior: Silent, precise, protective, tactically honest, and willing to dive into impossible danger when the shorebound narrator can hold the smaller swarm at bay.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/makiko-character.png`

## The Shorebound Witness

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #6, "The Seekers and Makiko."
- Role: viewpoint protagonist, Makiko's partner, civilian ally whose voice disrupts Seeker synchronization.
- Visual identifiers: Black man in his early 30s with medium-brown skin, close-cropped hair, worried brown eyes, casual beach vacation clothes with a dark lightweight hoodie over a gray T-shirt, rolled dark pants, bare feet on wet sand, and a cracked dead smartphone in one hand.
- Behavior: Curious, frightened of the water because he cannot swim, honest about feeling useless, but capable of calm speech and improvised rhymes under pressure.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/shorebound-witness-character.png`

## Cosmic Seekers

- Series continuity: New antagonist swarm introduced in Issue #6, "The Seekers and Makiko."
- Role: ancient starlit-depth predators that hunt by synchronized light.
- Visual identifiers: Dolphin-sized translucent deep-sea predators with long undulating bodies, four fin-like limbs, glassy black eyes, trailing tendrils, and intricate cyan, violet, and pearl bioluminescent organs arranged in hypnotic stripe and ring patterns under wet skin.
- Powers and behavior: Rise from the shallows in groups, pulse light patterns together, hypnotize humanoid brainwaves, and become confused when steady rhythmic human speech breaks their synchronization.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/cosmic-seeker-character.png`

## The Leviathan Seeker

- Series continuity: Larger issue-specific predator of the Cosmic Seeker swarm.
- Role: hidden massive threat beneath the waves.
- Visual identifiers: Bus-sized shadow under starlit water, same translucent bioluminescent anatomy as the smaller Seekers but larger, with a crown-like lure of bright cyan-violet organs above its head and enough mass to shove waves onto shore.
- Powers and behavior: Coordinates the smaller Seekers through its crown-lure. If Makiko severs the lure, the swarm loses shared hypnosis and retreats.
- Reference image: Use `../../reference-images/cosmic-seeker-character.png` with page-specific scale notes.

## Starlight Sea Beach

- Series continuity: New setting introduced in Issue #6, "The Seekers and Makiko."
- Role: isolated shore battlefield between land safety and cosmic sea danger.
- Visual identifiers: Lonely black-sand beach outside a distant hotel, cosmic waves glittering like liquid starlight, wet reflective shore, strange bioluminescent life below the surface, a sky crowded with stars and shooting-star streaks, and deep water where shapes can look like a submerged city until they move.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/starlight-sea-beach-setting.png`

## Makiko's Water-Worn Blade

- Series continuity: New key item introduced in Issue #6, "The Seekers and Makiko."
- Role: Makiko's weapon and anti-hypnosis countermeasure.
- Visual identifiers: Sleek short sword with a dark water-smoothed grip, worn metal guard shaped by years of salt exposure, narrow silver blade etched with shallow wave-like grooves, and a faint blue light along the edge when raised near Cosmic Seeker bioluminescence.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/makiko-water-worn-blade-key-item.png`
