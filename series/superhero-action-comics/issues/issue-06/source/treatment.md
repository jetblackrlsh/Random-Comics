# Treatment

## Title

The Seekers and Makiko

## Issue Number

6

## Logline

On a starlit beach where ancient predators hypnotize prey with synchronized bioluminescence, Makiko must fight the monster beneath the waves while her shorebound partner learns that his voice is the only weapon holding the swarm back.

## Central Conflict

The Cosmic Seekers can attack from the water while their light turns minds passive from a distance, and the narrator cannot escape or swim. Makiko can fight them, but the larger Seeker beneath the waves forces her to leave the smaller swarm to the one person who believes he is helpless.

## Theme

Courage is not the absence of fear; it is using the one useful thing you still have when fear tells you it is not enough.

## Moral Message

A hero does not need to match a warrior's skill to matter in a crisis. A clear voice, used at the right moment, can save lives.

## Ending

The narrator keeps speaking even after a Seeker lunges over him, breaking the swarm's rhythm just as Makiko cuts the leviathan's crown-lure. The predators lose their shared hypnosis, retreat to the deep, and the two survivors walk back toward the hotel with proof destroyed but the warning alive.

## Emotional Arc

The Shorebound Witness begins as a curious vacationer who feels exposed and useless because he cannot swim. Makiko begins controlled and protective, convinced she must handle the danger alone. As the Seekers escalate, both learn to trust the narrator's voice as part of the fight. By the end, he is still afraid, but he stands at the waterline and speaks anyway, while Makiko accepts him as a partner rather than a liability.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, cosmic beach discovery, phone destroyed and Seekers surface, Makiko's arrival and explanation, the voice-disruption tactic, the massive leviathan's attack and Makiko's dive, the narrator's failed retreat and decisive rhyme, and the final synchronized counterattack that drives the Seekers back.
