Series Title:
Superhero Action Comics

Series Premise:
This superhero, action-adventure, anthology series follows worlds across the multiverse where superheroes and supervillains fight in the neverending battle of good and evil.

Writing Style:
Captions should be written with clarity in mind, so that the reader has a firm understanding of what is happening in each panel. Each page should have 6 panels minimum.

Issue One Premise:
Mister Titanic crashes through the wall of a burning building and searches for survivors. Smoke blinds him until he reverses the smoke stacks on his back and draws the smoke into his body, clearing the way for civilians to escape. He refuses a little girl's hug because his metal body is superheated, then stays behind to make sure everyone is out. A fire elemental springs from a fireplace, reveals that the disaster was bait for a hero, and battles Mister Titanic through the roof. The villain escapes as the building collapses, leaving Mister Titanic buried. In the ember-dark, Mister Titanic remembers dying as the captain of the Titanic, being rebuilt from his own ship, and accepting a mission to save as many lives as were lost. He refuses to die by fire after once facing death by ice, flashes his floodlight eyes, and is rescued by firefighters.
