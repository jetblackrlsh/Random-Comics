# Character Bible

Use the parent series descriptions and reference images in `series/superhero-action-comics/source/` and `series/superhero-action-comics/reference-images/` as binding continuity for this issue.

## Mister Titanic

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/mister-titanic-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: Towering cyborg man built from riveted dark ocean-liner steel and brass, broad shoulders, heavy segmented metal boots, porthole-like glowing floodlight eyes, weathered captain's coat fragments integrated into armor, two smoke stacks rising from his upper back, scorch marks and old saltwater patina.
- Emotional continuity: Solemn, urgent, protective, and guilty beneath the metal discipline. He never treats rescue as spectacle. By the end he is exhausted, damaged, and determined to live.
- Power continuity: Floodlight eyes can cut through darkness but not always through thick smoke. Back smoke stacks can reverse to draw smoke into his body or vent pressure smoke to rocket him upward for short bursts.

## The Fire Elemental

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/fire-elemental-character.png`
- Visible identifiers: Humanoid monster made of living flame, charcoal-black cracked body plates, furnace-bright orange core, ember eyes, jagged grin, flame plumes around shoulders and head, smoke trailing from limbs.
- Emotional continuity: Cruel, theatrical, excited by danger, and proud that his fire baited a hero.
- Power continuity: Can rise out of ordinary fire, tackle with burning force, fly away as flame and smoke, and survive inside intense heat.

## Survivors

- Role: Ordinary civilians trapped inside the burning building.
- Visual identifiers: Soot-covered adults and children in modern city clothing, coughing, scared, eyes watering, clutching each other, finding their way toward fresh air after the smoke clears.
- Continuity notes: Non-recurring supporting figures. Keep them grounded and human so Mister Titanic's rescue mission stays clear.

## Firefighters

- Role: Emergency responders who pull Mister Titanic free after the collapse.
- Visual identifiers: Modern firefighters in turnout gear, helmet lights, breathing masks, fire hoses, rescue tools, soot and ash on gear.
- Continuity notes: Non-recurring supporting figures. They should look competent and urgent, not decorative.

## Recurring Visual Rules

- Realistic candid photo-comic, documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism feel.
- Every story page uses at least six panels.
- Caption boxes only, no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art.
- Keep Mister Titanic's riveted ship-steel body, porthole floodlight eyes, back smoke stacks, and heavy metal boots consistent in every appearance.
- Keep the fire elemental's cracked charcoal body, flame plumes, ember eyes, and jagged grin consistent after the reveal.
