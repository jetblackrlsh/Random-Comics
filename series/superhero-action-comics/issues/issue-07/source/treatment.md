# Treatment

## Title

Venom in the Machine

## Issue Number

7

## Logline

At a hidden Kenyan port, beetle-android hero Biter fights venomous anti-tech martial artist Green Mamba over stolen Animax brain-transfer equipment, then chooses outside oversight over blind faith in the machine that saved him.

## Central Conflict

Biter sees the stolen Animax system as a rescue technology that can preserve dying minds, while Green Mamba sees it as a prison factory that can turn souls into weapons. Their fight is not good against evil; it is preservation against prevention, and both men have enough truth to be dangerous.

## Theme

Second chances need guardians, not owners.

## Moral Message

Powerful technology can save lives, but saving lives does not excuse secrecy, coercion, or control. A hero protects the future by refusing both destruction for its own sake and unchecked ownership.

## Ending

Biter defeats Green Mamba without killing him, secures the stolen equipment, and sends its location to an independent medical ethics council instead of returning it privately to Project Animax. Green Mamba leaves with a warning that if the machine becomes a prison, he will come back to cut it open.

## Emotional Arc

Biter begins defensive and forceful, treating Green Mamba as an obstacle to the technology that gave him a second life. Green Mamba begins certain that destroying the device is the only moral answer. The battle forces Biter to stop fighting like a machine and show the human fear and hope inside his android body. Green Mamba loses the fight but earns a concession: Biter admits that resurrection without consent can become another weapon.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, dual origin, opposing missions at the hidden port, first clash, stealth reversal, Biter's personal reveal, dust-and-sonic counterattack, and dawn resolution with independent oversight.
