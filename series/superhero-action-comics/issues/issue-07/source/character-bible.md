# Character And Reference Bible

## Biter / Petar Marinoff

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #7, "Venom in the Machine."
- Role: protagonist, beetle-inspired android hero and former Bulgarian Air Force pilot.
- Visual identifiers: Human-sized but powerfully built beetle-android body; glossy black chitinous armor plates with burnt-orange chest and abdominal segments; orange compound-lens eyes; segmented mechanical faceplate with small mandible structures; two black antennae curving back from the helmet; translucent amber beetle wings folded from armored back casings; compact flight thrusters; clawed black mechanical gloves and boots; visible scuffs, sparks, and sliced armor after combat.
- Behavior: Tactical, proud, protective of the technology that saved his mind, still emotionally human beneath the insect-machine body. When stressed, he shifts from direct force to pilot thinking: terrain, timing, wind, visibility, and opponent psychology.
- Powers and limits: Flight, stealth mode, chitinous armor, sensory arrays, mandibles, enhanced strength, short-range sonic pulse, and taser discharge through armor contact. Anti-metal claws can corrode his outer armor and venom strikes can disrupt exposed joints.

## Green Mamba / Edi Kenyatta

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #7, "Venom in the Machine."
- Role: antagonist by mission, martial artist hero, Youdu Clan anti-technology enforcer.
- Visual identifiers: Athletic Kenyan man in a black tactical ninja suit with green snake-scale armor bands across the brow, torso straps, forearms, and shins; magenta-pink eye lenses; green ribbon tails from the mask; cobra-head chest emblem or armored snake chest plate; long anti-metal claws extending from both gloves; compact staff when needed; poised martial arts stance.
- Behavior: Disciplined, quiet, precise, suspicious of body-replacement technology, and willing to destroy a dangerous device before it can enslave living minds.
- Powers and limits: Elite martial arts training, anti-metal claws that dissolve advanced alloys, paralyzing venom delivered through strikes, heightened body awareness, and the ability to read movement from breath, air pressure, and intent. He can be stunned by sonic dust disruption and nonlethal taser contact.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/green-mamba-character.png`

## Hidden Kenyan Port Facility

- Series continuity: New setting introduced in Issue #7, "Venom in the Machine."
- Role: black-market transfer point and central battlefield.
- Visual identifiers: Nighttime container port near the Kenyan coast; stacked shipping containers; wet concrete; sodium harbor lights; cranes; chain-link fences; tarps; illegal medical-tech crates; cracked warehouse monitors; ocean mist; dawn light by the resolution.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/hidden-kenyan-port-facility-setting.png`

## Animax Brain-Transfer Equipment

- Series continuity: New key item introduced in Issue #7, "Venom in the Machine."
- Role: stolen resurrection technology and moral object of the conflict.
- Visual identifiers: Portable but heavy medical-industrial console with a reinforced metal frame, transparent brain-scan cylinder, neural cable bundles, cracked monitor showing an abstract human brain scan, emergency battery packs, shipping-case clamps, sterile white panels scuffed by black-market handling, and amber-blue diagnostic lights.
- Continuity notes: The device can preserve or transfer human minds into artificial bodies. Biter treats it as a second-life technology; Green Mamba fears it can imprison minds in weaponized bodies. By the end, Biter sends its location to an independent medical ethics council.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/animax-brain-transfer-equipment-key-item.png`
