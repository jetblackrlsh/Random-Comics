# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text, title text, and any other readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. Prefer 4-6 clear panels on story pages so the action reads step by step.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Establish Biter, Green Mamba, the stolen Animax device, and the technology-versus-consent conflict.
- Layout: Dramatic single-image cover with documentary photo-comic texture.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Action Comics". Smaller issue title text: "Venom in the Machine".
- Visual beats: Biter drops from smoky harbor darkness with amber beetle wings flared, orange lens eyes bright, one wing already nicked. Green Mamba stands below on wet concrete with anti-metal claws raised, black-and-green snake suit and magenta eye lenses glowing. Between them sits the Animax brain-transfer console in an open shipping crate, its cracked monitor showing a human brain scan. Stacked containers, crane silhouettes, and ocean mist frame the hidden Kenyan port.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated directly in the image. No logos or extra unreadable text.

## Page 2: Two Survivors

- Story role: Establish both heroes' origins and opposing philosophies.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "Petar Marinoff trusted speed, steel, and split-second judgment." "A crash destroyed his body, but Project Animax preserved his mind." "The reborn pilot called himself Biter." "Edi Kenyatta chose discipline over machinery." "The Youdu Clan sharpened him into Green Mamba." "One man owed his second life to a machine; the other feared machines that could steal lives."
- Visual beats: Petar as a Bulgarian pilot beside a jet; wreckage and emergency light implied without gore; Biter awakening in a beetle-android frame; Edi training in a shadowed courtyard; Green Mamba extending anti-metal claws; final split panel comparing Biter's amber mechanical eyes and Green Mamba's magenta mask lenses.
