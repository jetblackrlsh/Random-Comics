**Biter and Green Mamba: Venom in the Machine**

**Story Page 1:** Petar Marinoff was once one of the sharpest pilots in the Bulgarian Air Force, a man who trusted speed, steel, and split-second judgment more than almost anything else in the world. When a catastrophic crash destroyed his body, Project Animax preserved his brain and placed it inside an experimental beetle-inspired android frame built with chitinous armor, sensory arrays, mandibles, flight systems, and stealth technology. The reborn hero called himself Biter, because he had survived death by becoming something smaller, stranger, and harder to crush. Across the world, Edi Kenyatta walked a very different path, training under the Youdu Clan until his body, mind, and spirit became weapons sharpened by discipline rather than machinery. As Green Mamba, he wielded anti-metal claws and paralyzing venom as a martial artist who believed some dangerous technology should be destroyed before it could enslave the living.

**Story Page 2:** Their conflict began when Biter detected an illegal shipment of Animax brain-transfer equipment moving through a hidden port facility near the coast of Kenya. To Biter, the stolen machinery represented a chance to save crash victims, soldiers, and dying innocents the way he had been saved, so he moved to reclaim it before criminals could sell it. Green Mamba reached the same facility first, but his mission was the opposite: the Youdu Clan had learned that the device could trap human minds inside weaponized bodies, and he intended to destroy it before another soul was turned into a tool. When Biter landed in a burst of mechanical wings and demanded that Green Mamba step away from the console, Green Mamba raised his claws and warned him that not every cage looked like a prison. Neither hero was evil, but both believed the other was about to unleash something unforgivable.

**Story Page 3:** Biter attacked first, not with lethal force, but with a flash of speed meant to restrain Green Mamba before the device could be damaged. Green Mamba read the movement like a martial arts form, slipping under Biter's mandibles and raking his anti-metal claws across the android's chest plate. Sparks burst from Biter's armor as the claws began dissolving the outer layer, forcing him to pull back before the damage reached his internal support systems. Biter tried to overpower Green Mamba with direct strength, locking both mandibles around the martial artist's staff and driving him backward through a stack of shipping crates. Green Mamba used the momentum against him, vaulting over Biter's shoulder and delivering a venom-laced strike to the exposed joint beneath his wing casing.

**Story Page 4:** Biter's first strategy failed because he treated Green Mamba like an opponent who could be contained by force. Realizing brute strength would only expose more weak points, Biter shifted into stealth mode and vanished into the dark maze of cargo containers. He circled silently above Green Mamba, using enhanced sensory systems to track his heartbeat, foot pressure, and body heat before diving from above. Green Mamba closed his eyes and listened to the tiny changes in the air, then struck upward with terrifying precision, slicing one of Biter's wing stabilizers and sending him crashing hard across the concrete. Biter's second strategy failed because Green Mamba was not just reacting to technology; he was reading the living intention behind it.

**Story Page 5:** Damaged, cornered, and running out of time, Biter watched Green Mamba leap toward the brain-transfer device with his claws ready to tear it apart. For the first time, Biter stopped thinking like a machine built to win and started thinking like the pilot he used to be, a man who survived by understanding terrain, timing, and the fears of the person across from him. Biter projected his own preserved brain scan onto a cracked monitor and spoke through every speaker in the facility, letting Green Mamba see the truth of his origin rather than just the horror of the technology. "This machine can be a cage," Biter said, "but I am proof that it can also be a second sunrise."

**Story Page 6:** Green Mamba hesitated, and that single breath gave Biter the opening he needed. Instead of charging, Biter used his damaged wing to kick up a storm of dust, then fired a short-range sonic pulse that propelled the dust cloud like a weapon. The artificial storm stunned Green Mamba, giving Biter just enough time to rush towards him. He pinned Green Mamba and released a taser pulse through his armor. Green Mamba was knocked on unconscious by the taser pulse.

**Story Page 7:** With Green Mamba defeated but alive, Biter secured the stolen Animax equipment and transmitted its location to an independent medical ethics council instead of returning it blindly to Project Animax. His goal was achieved: the technology would not be destroyed, but it also would not remain hidden in the hands of criminals or unchecked scientists. Green Mamba, still recovering from the stun, admitted that Biter had chosen a path he had not expected: preservation without surrender, power without ownership. Biter answered that Green Mamba had been right about one thing: a second life should never become another person's weapon. As dawn rose over the port, Green Mamba disappeared into the alleys with a warning that if the machine ever became a prison, he would return to cut it open.
