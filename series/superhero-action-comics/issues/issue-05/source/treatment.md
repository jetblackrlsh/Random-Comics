# Treatment

## Title

The Soulforge Knight

## Issue Number

5

## Logline

After a Far Lands dragon destroys a thousand royal knights and kidnaps the princess, an anxious old wizard raises their molten armor and loyal spirits into a giant super knight who must choose sacrifice over survival.

## Central Conflict

The kingdom needs its princess returned and its king restored, but the dragon has made the normal instruments of royal power useless. The Soulforge Knight is strong enough to challenge the dragon, yet his body is made from souls that can escape and vanish forever.

## Theme

Duty can outlast death when loyalty becomes a choice instead of an order.

## Moral Message

A hero is not defined by whether he survives the battle, but by whether he protects others when survival becomes the price of betrayal.

## Ending

The Soulforge Knight rejects the dragon's offer to save his leaking chest plate. He speaks Wizard Orvan's magic word, releases every knight-soul as one devastating beam, destroys Ashmaw, and dies as the wizard rescues Princess Mirabel and returns her to King Aldren.

## Emotional Arc

King Aldren begins powerful and certain that an army can solve any crisis, then collapses under grief when that army dies. Wizard Orvan begins as a fearful old adviser who can barely breathe through panic, then finds enough calm to make one decisive spell. The Soulforge Knight begins as a resurrected army trying to live again, then accepts that the spirits inside him were already dead and uses their second chance to complete their oath.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, kidnapping and royal charge, dragon's molten moat, the grieving kingdom and wizard's journey, the Soulforge rising, dragon duel and mortal wound, the wizard's magic word and the dragon's bargain, and the soul-beam sacrifice that saves the princess and restores the kingdom.
