# Character And Reference Bible

## King Aldren of Veyr

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #5, "The Soulforge Knight."
- Role: grieving ruler and father of the kidnapped princess.
- Visual identifiers: Elderly medieval king with weathered light skin, silver beard, tired blue eyes, gold circlet, heavy burgundy velvet robe over a chainmail collar, broad shoulders bent by grief, ornate but believable royal hall surroundings.
- Behavior: Begins decisive and commanding, breaks under the loss of his daughter and knights, then regains focus after Princess Mirabel returns.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/king-aldren-character.png`

## Princess Mirabel of Veyr

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #5, "The Soulforge Knight."
- Role: kidnapped princess whose rescue can restore the kingdom.
- Visual identifiers: Young adult princess with warm brown skin, dark curly hair braided with small gold beads, green eyes, practical ivory-and-blue travel dress under a torn blue cloak, small silver pendant, brave but frightened posture.
- Behavior: Captive but alert, survives inside the dragon's stone tower, and returns to her father at the end.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/princess-mirabel-character.png`

## Wizard Orvan

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #5, "The Soulforge Knight."
- Role: anxious old wizard, summoner, and rescuer.
- Visual identifiers: Elderly Black wizard with deep wrinkles, short white beard, worried brown eyes, patched indigo robe, leather belt of glass vials and brass charms, twisted oak staff with a small blue-white crystal, stooped posture that straightens when casting.
- Powers and behavior: Casts old battlefield magic, struggles with fear and racing thoughts, steadies himself with one deep breath, and teaches the Soulforge Knight the word "Asterion" to release the knight-souls as a final beam.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/wizard-orvan-character.png`

## Ashmaw, the Far Lands Dragon

- Series continuity: New antagonist introduced in Issue #5, "The Soulforge Knight."
- Role: dragon kidnapper, territorial monster, and tempter.
- Visual identifiers: Massive four-legged dragon with charcoal-black scales, ember-red cracks along the throat and chest, furnace-orange eyes, tattered bat wings, jagged horns swept back like burnt iron, scarred snout, molten saliva, and firelight reflecting from dark armored plates.
- Powers and behavior: Hears armies from miles away, melts earth and metal with fire breath, fights as a territorial predator, laughs at leaking souls, and offers mercy only to force surrender.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/ashmaw-far-lands-dragon-character.png`

## The Soulforge Knight

- Series continuity: New heroic construct introduced in Issue #5, "The Soulforge Knight."
- Role: giant super knight formed from molten armor and the spirits of the slain knights.
- Visual identifiers: Colossal humanoid knight, thirty stories tall, body made of flowing molten silver-steel and fused medieval armor plates, glowing white-blue knight souls visible through seams, enormous cracked chest plate, crown-shaped helm crest made from spears, many ghostly armored faces faintly reflected inside the metal, heavy heroic stance.
- Powers and behavior: Superhuman strength, giant scale, living armor body, soul light inside the chest, and a final soul energy beam that destroys both himself and Ashmaw.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/soulforge-knight-character.png`

## Royal Knights of Veyr

- Series continuity: Supporting army introduced for Issue #5.
- Role: loyal army whose deaths create the Soulforge Knight.
- Visual identifiers: Thousands of armored medieval knights in polished steel, blue-and-burgundy tabards, lance flags, full helms, war horses and foot soldiers moving as one enormous rescue column.
- Reference image: Use page-specific prompts and the Soulforge Knight reference.

## The Asterion Word

- Series continuity: New spell phrase introduced for Issue #5.
- Role: magic word taught by Wizard Orvan to release the knight-souls as a single beam.
- Visual identifiers: Blue-white glyph light around Wizard Orvan's staff and the Soulforge Knight's broken chest; the readable word "Asterion" appears only in generated caption text or short dialogue caption boxes.
- Reference image: Use Wizard Orvan and Soulforge Knight references.
