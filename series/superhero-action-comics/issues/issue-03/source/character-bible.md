# Character And Reference Bible

## Radiant Vector

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #3, "The God Who Turned Away."
- Role: protagonist, exhausted cosmic-speed superhero.
- Visual identifiers: Black man in his early 30s, close-cropped hair, tired brown eyes, athletic build, cobalt-blue flight suit with white armored ribs, gold sunburst chest emblem, gold gauntlets and boots, faint starfield texture in the fabric, white-gold energy streaks around his hands and feet when moving, solemn protective posture.
- Powers and behavior: Flies, moves at impossible speed, can push velocity hard enough to pierce time, and carries the burden of protecting a planet with too few heroes.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/radiant-vector-character.png`

## The Atlantean Mage Circle

- Series continuity: New antagonist faction introduced in Issue #3, "The God Who Turned Away."
- Role: ancient magical combatants and future vengeance cult.
- Visual identifiers: Mixed group of wizards and witches from ancient Atlantis, sea-bronze armor over linen robes, turquoise and coral jewelry, shell-and-gold staffs, wet windblown hair, glowing blue-white runes on hands and faces, battle-worn expressions. Modern descendants wear darker submerged ceremonial armor with bioluminescent runes and ocean-weathered masks.
- Powers and behavior: Command destructive arcane lightning, ward shields, levitation, and ritual memory magic. By the modern era, rival mage houses have united around the myth that Radiant Vector sank Atlantis.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/atlantean-mage-circle-character.png`

## Ordinary Atlanteans

- Series continuity: New supporting civilian population introduced in Issue #3, "The God Who Turned Away."
- Role: doomed citizens of ancient Atlantis and descendants who inherit the broken myth.
- Visual identifiers: Coastal city civilians in linen, bronze, turquoise beads, work aprons, fisher cloaks, scholar robes, and merchant wraps; frightened families on cracked white-stone streets as the city slides toward the ocean.
- Continuity notes: The people who beg Radiant Vector for rescue become the emotional source of Atlantis's myth of the god who turned away.
- Reference image: Use `../../reference-images/ancient-atlantis-setting.png` and page-specific crowd details.

## The Ocularium

- Series continuity: New key item introduced in Issue #3, "The God Who Turned Away."
- Role: Radiant Vector's alien supercomputer and research oracle.
- Visual identifiers: Floating alien machine made of black glass rings, pearlescent metal ribs, nested lens-eyes, star-map holograms, and translucent data panels showing extinct languages and branching timelines.
- Continuity notes: It reveals the Great Unweaving, the ancient catastrophe that erased public magic from the world.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/ocularium-alien-supercomputer-key-item.png`

## Ancient Atlantis

- Series continuity: New setting introduced in Issue #3, "The God Who Turned Away."
- Role: coastal magical city that becomes the underwater Atlantis.
- Visual identifiers: White limestone terraces, cliffside harbors, bronze sea statues, turquoise tile roofs, canal bridges, mage towers, market streets, stormy blue ocean, and city edges cracking away from the mainland.
- Continuity notes: The city sinks after magical war eats away the ground beneath it. Modern Atlanteans misremember Radiant Vector as the god responsible.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/ancient-atlantis-setting.png`
