Superhero Action Comics
Issue 3

Create Issue 3 of "Superhero Action Comics" based on the following premise:

A tired superhero laments that there are not enough heroes on his world to keep the planet safe. He wishes that there were a way to make everyone super. Using his alien supercomputer, he researches ways to potentially empower humanity. He discovers ancient text describing a catastrophic event that erased magic from the world.

Pushing his speed to its limits, our hero races back in time to stop this event from happening so that all of humanity can be empowered and protected by the mystic arts. When he arrives in the past, he witnesses a massive magical war in a coastal city. Wizards and witches exchange blasts of arcane lightning. Stray bolts eat away at the Earth's edge, causing the city to start drifting away from land and sinking into the ocean.

The superhero wants to swoop in to help them, but he hesitates. He is seeing for the first time just how destructive magic can be. Power in the hands of everyone is fated to destroy them all. He realizes that the reason his future is as safe as it is, is because heroes are the ones with the power.

Citizens of the city see him flying above and call up for him to rescue them, but with a heavy heart he turns his back on them as he allows them to sink into the ocean. Little does he know that this city will become the underwater city of Atlantis. The myth of the god who turned their back on them persists to the modern era. The myth has fragmented until modern Atlanteans believe that he was responsible for the sinking of their city in the first place. The Atlantean mages set aside their differences to train for vengeance. Soon they will rise from the ocean and hunt him down.
