# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. Prefer 3-5 roomy panels on lore-heavy pages so the captions remain readable.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Establish Radiant Vector, the doomed city, and the future vengeance hook.
- Layout: Dramatic single-image cover with documentary photo-comic texture.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Action Comics". Smaller issue title text: "The God Who Turned Away".
- Visual beats: Radiant Vector hovers in a stormy sky above ancient Atlantis as the coastal city breaks loose from the mainland and sinks into a violent ocean. Below him, citizens reach upward from white-stone terraces while blue-white magical lightning tears across the city. In the lower water, ghostly modern Atlantean mages stare up with vengeance.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated directly in the image. No logos or extra unreadable text.

## Page 2: Too Few Heroes

- Story role: Show Radiant Vector's exhaustion and his wish to empower everyone.
- Layout: Four panels.
- Caption boxes: "Radiant Vector saved three cities before sunrise and still heard sirens calling from the horizon." "He was fast enough to be everywhere for a moment, never long enough to stay." "A planet with so few heroes would always be one disaster behind." "That night, he asked the Ocularium how to make humanity powerful enough to protect itself."
- Visual beats: Radiant Vector streaks through smoke over a modern city rescue; he lands exhausted on a rooftop with damaged suit scuffs and distant emergencies behind him; close panel of his tired face looking down at ordinary people below; he enters a hidden observatory where the floating Ocularium opens its alien lens-eyes.

## Page 3: The Lost Door To Magic

- Story role: Explain the research discovery and the temptation.
- Layout: Four panels.
- Caption boxes: "The alien machine searched languages older than nations." "It found the Great Unweaving, a catastrophe that stripped public magic from the world." "Before that day, every human mind could touch the mystic arts." "Radiant Vector saw a future where every citizen could defend the planet, and hope drowned out caution."
- Visual beats: The Ocularium projects extinct alphabets and star-map timelines; holographic ancient text describes the Great Unweaving; Radiant Vector sees a shimmering vision of ordinary modern people raising protective magic shields; he braces in a launching chamber, white-gold speed energy building around him.

## Page 4: Breaking Into Yesterday

- Story role: Send the hero into the past and reveal the magical war.
- Layout: Five panels.
- Caption boxes: "He ran faster than weather, faster than orbit, faster than the calendar could hold." "Time tore open." "He arrived above a coastal city shining with impossible power." "Then the sky split with spells." "Atlantis was not losing magic. Atlantis was drowning in it."
- Visual beats: Radiant Vector becomes a white-gold streak around Earth; fragments of modern city lights smear into ancient stars; he bursts from a time fracture over ancient Atlantis; wide panel of wizards and witches on towers and streets firing arcane lightning; close panel of Radiant Vector's shocked face as magical blasts hit homes and bridges.

## Page 5: The War Of Everyone

- Story role: Show that universal magic is catastrophic.
- Layout: Five panels.
- Caption boxes: "There were no powerless bystanders in the war." "Every family carried a spell. Every argument had artillery." "Shields saved one street and shattered the next." "The city edge began to dissolve under stray lightning." "Radiant Vector finally saw the cost of the miracle he wanted."
- Visual beats: Atlantean mage families and soldiers exchange blue-white and violet arcane bolts; a mother raises a ward while a nearby merchant fires back in panic; magical shields ricochet lightning into a tower; the cliffside under the city turns to glowing dust and falls into the sea; Radiant Vector reaches forward, horrified, about to dive in.

## Page 6: The City Calls Up

- Story role: Force Radiant Vector into the moral choice.
- Layout: Four panels.
- Caption boxes: "He could save them." "He could stop the Unweaving." "He could restore magic to the future, and give every hand a power no world could safely hold." "From the breaking streets below, Atlantis called him a god and begged him to choose them."
- Visual beats: Radiant Vector hovers close enough to see crying citizens reaching from cracked terraces; a child and elder point up at him while the city slides seaward; reflected in his visor, a vision of modern Earth burning under uncontrolled public magic; his fist trembles open as he pulls back from the rescue.

## Page 7: The Turn

- Story role: Resolve Radiant Vector's choice and complete the fall of Atlantis.
- Layout: Four panels.
- Caption boxes: "The future was not safe because power belonged to everyone." "It was safe because the few who carried power had chosen responsibility." "Radiant Vector turned away." "Atlantis sank, and magic went with it."
- Visual beats: Radiant Vector turns his back in the rain, face full of grief; behind him, the city breaks from the mainland like a glowing island; water floods markets, towers, and bridges as blue magic gutters out; final wide panel shows Atlantis descending beneath the ocean while Radiant Vector vanishes into a speed trail.

## Page 8: What The Deep Remembered

- Story role: Reveal the myth and future threat.
- Layout: Five panels.
- Caption boxes: "Centuries changed truth into worship, then worship into accusation." "The people under the sea remembered the sky-god who turned his back." "In time, they decided he had sunk Atlantis himself." "The mage houses ended their old war for one purpose." "Soon the ocean would rise, and Radiant Vector would learn what the deep had been training to avenge."
- Visual beats: Underwater ruins of Atlantis glow with bioluminescent magic; carved murals show a distorted figure like Radiant Vector above a sinking city; modern Atlantean mages in dark ocean armor gather around the mural; rival mages clasp glowing hands over weapons and spellbooks; final panel shows a line of Atlantean mages marching upward from the deep toward moonlit surface water.
