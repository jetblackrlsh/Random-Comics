# Character And Reference Bible

## Atlas

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #4, "Sky Iron Harvest."
- Role: visiting superhero, charity donor, kidnap victim.
- Visual identifiers: Tall extremely muscular white man in his mid-30s, square jaw, short wavy dark-brown hair, blue eyes, clean-shaven, noble but approachable expression. Deep royal-blue fitted superhero suit with brushed silver shoulder panels, silver belt and bracers, white starburst chest emblem, short red cape, reinforced boots, faint rescue scuffs.
- Powers and behavior: Superhumanly strong and normally impervious to damage. Sky Iron is his greatest weakness and causes pain, collapse, and loss of strength when near him.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/atlas-character.png`

## Big Apple

- Series continuity: New character introduced in Issue #4, "Sky Iron Harvest."
- Role: resident New York City superhero, rescuer, tactical protagonist.
- Visual identifiers: Athletic Black man in his early 30s, warm medium-dark skin, close-trimmed beard, focused brown eyes, compact powerful build. Deep forest-green tactical bodysuit with red armor plates at chest and forearms, subtle apple-shaped chest emblem, black utility belt with red apple-shaped gadgets, fitted dark-green cowl with small pointed brow fins, leafy green cape made of overlapping flexible leaf-like panels.
- Tools and behavior: Uses his cowl HUD, The Orchard satellite network, apple-shaped utility tools, the Apple Cart super mobile, and fast practical judgment. Protective of New York civilians and confident under pursuit.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/big-apple-character.png`

## The Sky Iron Android

- Series continuity: New antagonist introduced in Issue #4, "Sky Iron Harvest."
- Role: android villain, body thief, Atlas's captor.
- Visual identifiers: Seven-foot imposing humanoid android with a bulky old-industrial dark gunmetal frame, exposed pistons, copper wiring, angular faceless head, narrow amber slit eyes, heavy squared shoulders, claw-like articulated hands, and orange-brown rust streaks along seams, bolts, knees, and shoulders. Its circular chest hatch opens to reveal a jagged cold blue-violet Sky Iron core in a containment ring.
- Motivation and behavior: Claims its digital mind is immortal but its metal body is doomed to rust. It wants to transfer its mind into Atlas's living body using a helmet device before Big Apple can interfere.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/sky-iron-android-character.png`

## New York City Mayor

- Series continuity: Supporting civilian figure introduced for Issue #4.
- Role: recipient of Atlas's charity check and witness to the attack.
- Visual identifiers: Middle-aged public official in a dark suit and overcoat at a civic podium, composed but shaken after the abduction.
- Reference image: Use page-specific New York charity event details.

## The Apple Cart

- Series continuity: New key item introduced in Issue #4, "Sky Iron Harvest."
- Role: Big Apple's AI super mobile.
- Visual identifiers: Low aggressive armored electric supercar, glossy candy-red body panels, dark forest-green armored fenders, subtle apple-shaped front grille silhouette, reinforced windshield, hidden weapon ports, rugged tires, roof sensor pod, New York street grime and scratches.
- Capabilities: AI autopilot, high-speed pickup without stopping, manual driving mode, ejector launch seat, and apple sauce cannon that can clog weapon systems.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/apple-cart-key-item.png`

## The Orchard

- Series continuity: New key item introduced in Issue #4, "Sky Iron Harvest."
- Role: Big Apple's private satellite network and cowl HUD system.
- Visual identifiers: Transparent augmented-reality city map in Big Apple's cowl, red and green scanning bands, small satellite indicators, and cold blue-violet Sky Iron signal readings over a city grid.
- Capabilities: Scans New York across narrowed frequency bands and pinpoints Sky Iron emissions.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/the-orchard-key-item.png`

## Sky Iron

- Series continuity: New key item introduced in Issue #4, "Sky Iron Harvest."
- Role: Atlas's greatest weakness and the android's anti-Atlas weapon.
- Visual identifiers: Jagged meteorite mineral with dull black-silver crust, cold blue-violet glow from cracks, faint vapor and electromagnetic shimmer, usually held in lead-lined clamps or mounted inside the android's chest ring.
- Effects: Weakens Atlas, makes normally harmless impacts injure him, and allows the android to overpower and kidnap him.
- Reference image: `../../reference-images/sky-iron-key-item.png`
