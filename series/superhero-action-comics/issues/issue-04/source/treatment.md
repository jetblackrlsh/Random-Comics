# Treatment

## Title

Sky Iron Harvest

## Issue Number

4

## Logline

When a rusting android abducts Atlas with a heart of Sky Iron, New York's own Big Apple must track the weakness signal, storm the warehouse, and save the world's strongest hero with a sprinkler pipe and an apple from his belt.

## Central Conflict

Atlas believes his strength makes him the safe public face of recovery after New York's alien invasion, but the android has built itself around the one substance that can hurt him. Big Apple has to prove that local knowledge, fast detection, and practical rescue instincts can beat a villain powerful enough to drop a near-invulnerable hero.

## Theme

Heroism is not only strength; it is noticing the weakness in the room and using the city itself to protect people.

## Moral Message

A hero who understands his home can save even a stronger hero by paying attention, acting fast, and choosing the right tool instead of the biggest one.

## Ending

Big Apple spots rust across the android's metal body and breaks the warehouse sprinkler pipe with a thrown apple. Water shorts the transfer helmet, accelerates the android's corrosion, and frees Atlas before the android can steal his body.

## Emotional Arc

Atlas begins confident and generous, becomes helpless when Sky Iron turns his strength against him, and ends humbled by Big Apple's rescue. Big Apple begins as the city hero standing in Atlas's shadow, then earns New York's trust by treating the abduction as a solvable rescue instead of a spectacle.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, charity ambush, Sky Iron abduction, Big Apple's vow and Orchard scan, android motive and transfer helmet, Apple Cart missile chase, Big Apple's skylight entry and blocked rescue, and the sprinkler-pipe victory.
