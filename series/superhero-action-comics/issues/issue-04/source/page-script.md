# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text, title text, and any other readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. Prefer 4-6 clear panels on story pages so the action reads step by step.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Establish Atlas's capture, Big Apple's rescue mission, the Sky Iron weakness, and the New York setting.
- Layout: Dramatic single-image cover with documentary photo-comic texture.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Action Comics". Smaller issue title text: "Sky Iron Harvest".
- Visual beats: Atlas collapses under cold blue-violet Sky Iron light from the android's open chest. Big Apple launches from the red-and-green Apple Cart below, leafy cape spread, Manhattan towers behind him. The Sky Iron Android looms in a warehouse doorway with rusted metal plates and an open glowing chest.
- Prompt notes: Readable title text generated directly in the image. No logos or extra unreadable text.

- Story role: Resolve the rescue with Big Apple's observation and quick countermeasure.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "Big Apple saw what the android hated most." "Rust." "He pulled one apple from his belt." "The throw split the sprinkler pipe." "Water killed the helmet and ate the metal body alive." "Atlas lived, and New York had found its own champion."
