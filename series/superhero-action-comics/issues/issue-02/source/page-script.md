# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text and profile text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes and profile cards only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. Every story page uses at least six panels.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Hell Queen, the crystal threat, and the friend-versus-friend rescue conflict.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with realistic photo-comic cover energy.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Superhero Action Comics". Smaller issue title text: "Crystal Hell".
- Visual beats: Hell Queen stands in a shattered city plaza, cackling with her left crystal wristband raised, red-white crystallization beams cutting through smoke. Tiny glowing crystals hang from the band, each with a trapped silhouette inside. Her five teammates rush toward her from below, trying to save her rather than kill her.
- Prompt notes: Realistic candid photo-comic cover, not painted poster art. Readable title text generated directly inside the cover image.

## Page 2: Who's Who

- Story role: Introduce the superteam roster immediately after the cover so readers understand each member's appearance, name, power, and team role before the story begins.
- Layout: Six clean profile cards in a 2-by-3 grid, each card containing a clear portrait/action pose and concise readable profile text.
- Profile text: "Captain Strategist" / "Team Leader" / "Clever Thinker"; "Cyber" / "Team Gadgeteer" / "Technomancer"; "Laser Light" / "Team Ranged Combat Specialist" / "Photokinetic"; "Geomancer" / "Team Powerhouse" / "Geokinetic"; "Wild Card" / "Team Wild Card" / "Shapeshifter"; "Inferna" / "Team Mage" / "Spellcasting".
- Visual beats: Captain Strategist in blue tactical captain coat with star-and-chevron emblem, pointing over a battle map; Cyber in gray-blue armor with visor and sonic rifle plus blue holograms; Laser Light in white-and-gold armor and yellow goggles firing rainbow-white light; Geomancer in green-and-stone-gray armor lifting cracked concrete; Wild Card in purple-and-white suit reshaping one arm; Inferna in black-and-red armor with crimson skin, small horns, golden eyes, and restrained red magic.
- Prompt notes: The profile cards should read like an in-universe Who's Who page, with all names and role/power text generated directly in the image art. Use original team designs only.

## Page 3: Monster They Expected

- Story role: Show Hell Queen's current terror and contrast it with Inferna's rejected arrival on Earth.
- Layout: Six panels in clear reading order.
- Caption boxes: "Hell Queen laughed as the city ran." "Heroes, villains, and citizens all fled the beams." "Years earlier, Inferna had crossed to Earth." "She was Satan's daughter, but not his servant." "She came to protect this world." "The world saw horns and called her monster."
- Visual beats: Hell Queen fires crystallization beams in a city plaza; a hero, a villain, and civilians run in different directions; teenage Inferna steps from a fiery portal into an alley; a shadowy demonic father looms behind the portal as she rejects him; teen Inferna stops a falling streetlight to save a pedestrian; frightened crowds recoil from her crimson skin and horns.

## Page 4: The Friends Who Stood Beside Her

- Story role: Show the teenage team defending Inferna and helping the world accept her goodness.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "Other teenagers saw the hero first." "Wild Card stood between her and the mob." "Laser Light showed the cameras what Inferna saved." "Cyber proved the disasters were not her doing." "Captain Strategist gave the team a purpose." "Together, they became defenders."
- Visual beats: Shapeshifter/Wild Card blocks thrown debris with a stretched purple-white arm; Laser Light projects a bright shield around Inferna and rescued civilians; Cyber shows holographic evidence to reporters; Geomancer lifts a broken bridge slab while Inferna guides people clear; Captain Strategist places a steady hand on Inferna's shoulder; final panel shows the six young heroes together as a new team while civilians begin to applaud.

## Page 5: The Crystals Everyone Celebrated

- Story role: Explain the growth of Inferna's powers and how the crystals became praised tools.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "Years passed, and Inferna's power grew." "Her beams could shrink danger into crystal." "Villains no prison could hold were finally confined." "The world called it justice." "She wore the crystals on her left wrist." "Each crystal made her stronger."
- Visual beats: Adult Inferna trains as red-white light forms around her hands; a gigantic armored villain shrinks into a glowing crystal; police and heroes watch in awe as a dangerous captive is secured; crowds cheer while Inferna looks overwhelmed; close panel of her attaching the first crystal to a black wristband; final panel shows many crystals glowing on her wrist as her eyes brighten dangerously.

## Page 6: Too Much Power

- Story role: Show the addiction turning her against everyone and the team refusing execution.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "The strength became intoxicating." "Then addictive." "She stopped choosing targets." "Hero, villain, civilian--all became prizes." "Older heroes reached for lethal answers." "Her team said, No. She is ours to save."
- Visual beats: Hell Queen smiles with glassy golden eyes as the wristband glows; her hand trembles over the crystals like she craves more; she fires beams at an original masked villain and then an original defending hero; a civilian nearly crystallizes while others drag them away; older adult original superheroes raise blades, cannons, and lethal energy toward Hell Queen; Captain Strategist, Laser Light, Geomancer, Wild Card, and Cyber block the older heroes' path.
- Rights-safe visual note: The older heroes in panel 5 must be newly invented for this issue, with no recognizable existing superhero costumes, logos, cape silhouettes, mask shapes, shield symbols, web patterns, bat motifs, lightning-chest motifs, patriotic shield/cowl designs, or color-pattern callbacks.

## Page 7: The Plan Under Fire

- Story role: Show the team testing the crystals and Captain Strategist secretly setting up Shapeshifter's gambit.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "Geomancer struck first." "Her stones could not break magical stone." "Cyber changed the frequency." "The sonic rifle cracked the crystals." "Hell Queen took him before he could finish." "Captain Strategist whispered the real plan."
- Visual beats: Geomancer hurls a ring of concrete and stone at fallen crystals; the stones shatter while the magical crystals remain intact; Cyber braces the sonic rifle and blasts circular vibration waves into the wrist crystals; hairline cracks appear in several glowing crystals; Hell Queen's beam hits Cyber and shrinks him into a new crystal; Laser Light and Geomancer provide cover while Captain Strategist leans close and whispers into Wild Card's ear.

## Page 8: The Crystal That Burned

- Story role: Execute the fake-crystal gambit and break Hell Queen's addictive connection.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "Captain Strategist raised a fallen crystal." "He dared her to stop him." "Hell Queen crossed the battlefield in a blink." "The beam took him too." "She set both prizes on her wrist." "One crystal changed shape."
- Visual beats: Captain Strategist holds up a fallen crystal over broken pavement; Hell Queen sees him and darts through Laser Light's beams; she crystallizes Captain Strategist before he can crush it; she clips his crystal and another crystal onto the left wristband; one crystal secretly reshapes into a simple glowing holy symbol; holy light burns her wrist and face as she screams and tears the band away.

## Page 9: Pure Enough To Return

- Story role: Free the captives, reveal Shapeshifter's disguise, and restore Inferna to her friends.
- Layout: Six panels.
- Caption boxes: "The band hit the ground." "Every crystal shattered." "The captives returned." "Wild Card had been the holy symbol." "Without the crystals, Inferna was herself again." "Hell rejected her, so her friends welcomed her home."
- Visual beats: The torn wristband smashes against pavement; crystals burst open in clean magical light; original heroes, original villains, civilians, Cyber, and Captain Strategist return to full size; Shapeshifter rises from the broken holy-symbol crystal, exhausted but grinning; Inferna, no longer cackling, falls to her knees in shame and opens a red portal to Hell; final panel shows a demonic force throwing her back to Earth where the team catches her in an embrace.
- Rights-safe visual note: All background heroes and villains in panel 3 must be original, non-iconic designs with no recognizable existing superhero costumes, logos, mask shapes, shield symbols, web patterns, bat motifs, lightning-chest motifs, patriotic shield/cowl designs, or color-pattern callbacks.
