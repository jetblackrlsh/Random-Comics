# Treatment

## Title

Crystal Hell

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

After Hell Queen's crystal prison power turns addictive, the teenage superhero team that once helped the world accept her must stop her without killing the friend they still believe can come home.

## Central Conflict

Inferna came to Earth to be a hero, but rejection, fear, and the intoxicating strength of her crystal prisons twist her into Hell Queen. Her former teammates must defeat the crystals without treating her as a monster, even when older heroes want her slain and the city is already running from her beams.

## Theme

Acceptance can save someone once, but friendship must become responsibility when power turns dangerous.

## Moral Message

A person is more than the worst thing their power made possible, and the people who love them may be the only ones patient enough to bring them back.

## Ending

Shapeshifter disguises themself as one of Hell Queen's wrist crystals and transforms into a holy symbol. The burn breaks Hell Queen's addictive connection to the crystals, she throws the wristband down, and every captive is freed. When Inferna tries to exile herself to Hell in shame, her father rejects her because her heart is too pure for the underworld, and her team welcomes her back.

## Emotional Arc

Inferna begins as a rejected teenage demon princess who wants to protect Earth but is feared for her appearance. Her friends help her become accepted, then years later must face the fact that the same friend is harming everyone around her. Captain Strategist chooses a rescue plan over execution, Shapeshifter risks becoming a crystal to save the captives, and Inferna's shame resolves into proof that her goodness survived the addiction.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, Who's Who team profile page, Hell Queen terror and Inferna's rejected arrival, teenage heroes defending her, the rise of crystal prison power, the power becoming addictive, the team attack and secret plan, Shapeshifter's holy-symbol gambit, and the shattered crystals restoring Inferna to her team.
