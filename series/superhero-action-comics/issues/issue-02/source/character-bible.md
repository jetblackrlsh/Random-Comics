# Character Bible

Use the parent series descriptions and reference images in `series/superhero-action-comics/source/` and `series/superhero-action-comics/reference-images/` as binding continuity for this issue.

## Hell Queen / Inferna

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/hell-queen-inferna-character.png`
- Who's Who role: Inferna, Team Mage / Spellcasting.
- Visible identifiers: Adult demonic superheroine with deep crimson skin, small black horns, sharp cheekbones, long black hair with ember-red streaks, golden eyes, black-and-red armored bodysuit with a regal collar, clawed black gauntlets, and a wide left wristband carrying many glowing faceted prisoner crystals.
- Flashback identifiers: As teenage Inferna she has the same crimson skin, small horns, golden eyes, and black hair, but wears a modest dark school-jacket superhero outfit with red trim and looks frightened but determined.
- Emotional continuity: Wants to be a hero and be accepted. As Hell Queen she is euphoric, cackling, and addicted to the power of the crystals. After the wristband breaks, she is ashamed, exhausted, and visibly herself again.
- Power continuity: Fires red-white crystallization beams that shrink and imprison people as small glowing faceted crystals. The more crystals she wears on her left wristband, the stronger and more intoxicated she becomes.

## Shapeshifter / Wild Card

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/shapeshifter-wild-card-character.png`
- Who's Who role: Wild Card, Team Wild Card / Shapeshifter.
- Visible identifiers: Agile nonbinary hero with medium-brown skin, short silver hair, flexible matte purple-and-white suit, white domino mask, asymmetrical utility belt, and a playful but focused expression. Their silhouette can distort into different forms while keeping purple-white color hints.
- Emotional continuity: Loyal, improvisational, brave under pressure, and willing to risk being trapped as a crystal to save the captives.
- Power continuity: Can change body shape, size, texture, and apparent object form, including disguising themself as one of Hell Queen's crystals before becoming a holy symbol.

## Laser Light

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/laser-light-character.png`
- Who's Who role: Laser Light, Team Ranged Combat Specialist / Photokinetic.
- Visible identifiers: Teen-to-young-adult photokinetic heroine with warm brown skin, braided dark hair pulled back, reflective yellow goggles, a white-and-gold jacket over a practical light armor suit, glowing fingertip prisms, and bright rainbow-white light trails.
- Emotional continuity: Protective, fast-thinking, and relentless in providing cover fire without trying to kill Inferna.
- Power continuity: Projects hard-light beams, flashes, and shields.

## Cyber

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/cyber-character.png`
- Who's Who role: Cyber, Team Gadgeteer / Technomancer.
- Visible identifiers: Young gadgeteer hero with light skin, close-cropped dark hair, slim tactical gray-blue armor, augmented reality visor, compact backpack rig, tool pouches, and a chunky sonic rifle with circular emitter rings.
- Emotional continuity: Analytical, nervous under pressure, but brave enough to stand close to the crystals and test the sonic rifle.
- Power continuity: Uses gadgets rather than innate powers. His sonic rifle can crack Hell Queen's crystals with super vibrations but cannot finish the rescue before he is crystallized.

## Captain Strategist

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/captain-strategist-character.png`
- Who's Who role: Captain Strategist, Team Leader / Clever Thinker.
- Visible identifiers: Young team leader with tan skin, close black hair, blue tactical captain coat over lightweight armor, white star-and-chevron chest emblem, red gloves, calm eyes, and a small wrist comm.
- Emotional continuity: Clear, tactical, protective, and unwilling to abandon Inferna to older heroes who want her slain.
- Power continuity: No flashy power required; his strength is battlefield planning, quick deception, and making teammates coordinate under panic.

## Geomancer

- Series source: `../../../source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `../../../reference-images/geomancer-character.png`
- Who's Who role: Geomancer, Team Powerhouse / Geokinetic.
- Visible identifiers: Powerful young heroine with dark skin, shaved sides and thick natural curls, green-and-stone-gray armored suit, heavy boots, stone bracers, floating pebbles orbiting her hands, and a grounded stance.
- Emotional continuity: Direct, protective, angry at the damage but still loyal to Inferna.
- Power continuity: Controls ordinary stone, concrete, and earth. Her powers cannot destroy Hell Queen's magical crystal prisons.

## Older Heroes

- Role: Background superhero faction pressuring the team to kill Hell Queen.
- Visual identifiers: Mixed adult original superheroes in realistic battle-damaged costumes, practical armor, unfamiliar masks, unique color palettes, abstract emblems, and non-iconic weapons, held back by Captain Strategist's team. Avoid recognizable existing superhero designs, logos, cape silhouettes, mask shapes, shield symbols, web patterns, bat motifs, lightning-chest motifs, patriotic shield/cowl designs, or any costume that suggests characters outside this repo.
- Continuity notes: Non-recurring supporting figures for this issue unless later reused.

## Citizens and Captives

- Role: Ordinary people, villains, and heroes endangered by Hell Queen's beams and trapped inside crystals.
- Visual identifiers: Modern city civilians, panicked original masked heroes, dangerous original villains, and tiny silhouettes suspended inside glowing faceted crystals. Background heroes and villains must use original, non-iconic costume designs without recognizable outside superhero symbols, logos, masks, or color-pattern callbacks.
- Continuity notes: Non-recurring supporting figures. Keep them varied so the captions' point is clear: Hell Queen is crystallizing everyone, not only villains.

## Recurring Visual Rules

- Realistic candid photo-comic, documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism feel.
- Every story page uses at least six panels.
- Caption boxes only, no speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art.
- Keep Hell Queen's crimson skin, horns, black-and-red armor, golden eyes, long black hair, and left crystal wristband consistent in every adult appearance.
- Keep the team's costumes and powers consistent with their reference images.
