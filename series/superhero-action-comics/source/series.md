# Superhero Action Comics

## Logline

A superhero action-adventure anthology about worlds across the multiverse where heroes and villains fight the neverending battle between good and evil.

## Premise

Superhero Action Comics follows different worlds, cities, eras, and impossible battlefields where courage is tested by villains who weaponize disaster, fear, power, and spectacle. Each issue can stand alone, but every story belongs to the same larger multiverse of heroic duty and escalating evil.

Issue stories should move quickly, stay clear, and make the action readable. Captions should explain what is happening in each panel without crowding the art. Every story page should use at least six panels when possible so the reader can follow motion, rescue work, tactical reversals, and consequences step by step.

## Tone

High-stakes superhero rescue and combat with a pulpy, direct, emotionally sincere voice. Heroes should solve problems through bravery, sacrifice, and practical judgment, not only through bigger attacks. Villains should feel dangerous because their plans create collateral damage and moral pressure.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Superhero costumes and powers should look physically present in real environments, with smoke, heat, rubble, weather, and impact damage treated as photoreal effects.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
- Action pages should read clearly across multiple panels, with motion broken into visible cause and effect.
- Rescue scenes should show ordinary people, emergency workers, damaged places, and the cost of delay.

## Continuity Notes

- The series is an anthology. Different issues may follow different heroes, villains, and worlds.
- Recurring heroes, villains, settings, or key items introduced by an issue should be recorded in the relevant source file and supported by reference images.
- Issue #1 introduces Mister Titanic, a cyborg hero rebuilt from pieces of the ship he once captained, and a fire elemental villain who engineers a disaster to lure out a hero.
