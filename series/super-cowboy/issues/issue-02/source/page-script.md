# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Signal a western superhero origin issue built around the Light Lasso, lightning, and Super Cowboy's choice to help others.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with realistic photo-comic cover feel.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Super Cowboy". Smaller issue title text: "Lightning Rode With Me".
- Visual beats: Super Cowboy swings high over Dustfall at twilight, blue-white Light Lasso hooked around a tall lamp post, duster flying, white star chest emblem visible, blue eyes glowing. Below him, the main street and canyon-edge sheriff office are visible. Behind the action, storm clouds and a ghostly flashback silhouette of him crawling through muddy desert rain hint at the origin.
- Prompt notes: Realistic candid photo-comic cover, not painted poster art. Readable title text generated directly inside the cover image.

## Page 2: Above Main Street

- Story role: Establish present-day confidence, Dustfall, public trust, and the Light Lasso as joyful motion.
- Layout: Five action panels with vertical motion and wide overhead angles.
- Caption boxes: "By noon, Dustfall no longer looked up in fear when blue light crossed the street." "Super Cowboy swung from lamp post to lamp post as easily as other men crossed a porch." "He ran the rooftops, boots hammering sunbaked planks." "When he flipped over main street, the whole town cheered."
- Visual beats: Super Cowboy launches from one tall wooden lamp post; blue-white lasso loop wrapped around another post; rooftops rush under his boots; townspeople in western clothing cheer from plank sidewalks; wide panel of him flipping over Dustfall main street with the Light Lasso arcing overhead.

## Page 3: The Edge Of Town

- Story role: Bring Super Cowboy to the sheriff office and turn the public triumph into private memory.
- Layout: Four panels moving from exterior landing to introspective close-up.
- Caption boxes: "He landed at the sheriff office where Dustfall ended and Red Mesa Canyon began." "The crowd's cheers faded behind the creak of the porch boards." "In his hands, the Light Lasso hummed like a storm trying to remember its own name." "It had changed his life on the night he thought his life was over."
- Visual beats: Super Cowboy lands heroically on the porch of the weathered sheriff office at the canyon edge; canyon cliffs visible beyond town; close view of his gloved hands holding the glowing Light Lasso; his masked face shifts from confident grin to solemn memory; final panel transitions into dark storm clouds over the desert.

## Page 4: Lost In The Storm

- Story role: Begin the origin flashback and show the lightning strike that reveals the blue light.
- Layout: Four large rain-heavy panels.
- Caption boxes: "Before Dustfall called him Super Cowboy, he was only a lost man on foot in a desert storm." "Rain turned the trail to thick red mud." "Lightning struck so close it knocked him flat on his back." "Then blue light shone from the crater ahead."
- Visual beats: Flashback Super Cowboy struggles alone through heavy desert rain and mud, soaked hat low over his eyes; lightning forks around red-rock terrain; a massive bolt hits just in front of him and throws him backward; from the muddy puddle where it struck, electric blue light glows through rainwater.

## Page 5: The Lasso Wakes

- Story role: Show the Light Lasso forming and bonding with Super Cowboy.
- Layout: Five panels, intimate and supernatural.
- Caption boxes: "The blue light broke apart into floating sparks." "The sparks circled, braided, and became a rope made of lightning." "The Light Lasso rose from the puddle and dropped into his hand." "Power surged through him until his eyes burned blue." "He whispered, \"What are you? What is this power?\""
- Visual beats: Super Cowboy crawls through mud toward the glowing puddle; blue particles rise from sparking rainwater; particles twist into the electric blue-white Light Lasso; the lasso levitates into his gloved hand; blue energy races up his arm and makes his eyes glow as he stares in stunned fear.

## Page 6: Run Or Die

- Story role: Turn wonder into urgency as the storm attacks and the new power reveals superhuman speed.
- Layout: Five panels with increasing speed.
- Caption boxes: "The storm answered before the lasso did." "Bolts began striking faster, each one closer than the last." "He ran because standing still meant dying." "Mud flew behind him as blue particles sparked from every step." "A near strike taught him the first rule of power: focus."
- Visual beats: Lightning bolts blast the ground around him; Super Cowboy starts running through mud clutching the Light Lasso; his stride lengthens impossibly; blue glowing particles trail behind his boots; a bolt narrowly misses from overhead, blasting mud beside him as his amazed expression hardens into focus.

## Page 7: The Canyon Jump

- Story role: Show the decisive escape toward town and connect the origin to his acrobatic present-day movement.
- Layout: Four large kinetic panels.
- Caption boxes: "Town waited beyond the canyon." "He ran toward it faster than fear could catch him." "Lightning hammered the ground in front of every step." "At the canyon mouth, he leaped before the biggest bolt could take him."
- Visual beats: In the stormy distance, the canyon mouth and faint lights of Dustfall are visible beyond red cliffs; Super Cowboy sprints at superhuman speed with blue particle trail; he acrobatically dodges between terrifying lightning strikes; final wide panel shows him leaping across the mouth of the canyon while a massive bolt explodes into the ground just behind him.

## Page 8: I Am On My Way

- Story role: Resolve the origin emotionally and send Super Cowboy toward the next rescue.
- Layout: Four panels in the present-day sheriff office.
- Caption boxes: "The memory ended where the duty began." "At his desk, Super Cowboy looked down at the lasso and said, \"You saved my life, so now I save the lives of others.\"" "A woman burst through the office door: \"Super Cowboy! We need you!\"" "His eyes glowed blue. He raised the Light Lasso and grinned. \"I'm on my way.\""
- Visual beats: Present-day Super Cowboy sits at a wooden sheriff desk in the frontier office, canyon visible through the window; close view of the Light Lasso coiled in his hands; an urgent woman in dusty frontier clothes rushes to the desk; final heroic panel shows Super Cowboy standing, eyes glowing blue, Light Lasso lifted overhead, ready to charge out.
