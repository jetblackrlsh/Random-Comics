## Stable Visual Continuity

Super Cowboy is a broad-shouldered adult white man with a short brown beard, serious eyes behind a blue eye mask, weathered tan cowboy hat with a blue band and white star, long brown leather duster, blue armored suit with red seams, white star chest emblem, utility belt, dusty western boots, leather gloves, and an electric blue-white Light Lasso. Keep his present-day outfit consistent across all pages.

In the origin flashback, he is the same man before fully understanding his power: soaked brown duster, wet tan cowboy hat with blue band and white star, blue armored suit darkened by rain and mud, leather gloves, muddy boots, and frightened but stubborn eyes. The Light Lasso's blue-white glow lights his face and gradually makes his eyes glow electric blue.

Dustfall is a small frontier town with weathered wooden storefronts, plank sidewalks, hitching posts, tall old-fashioned lamp posts along main street, dusty rooftops, cheering townspeople, and desert mountains beyond town.

The Dustfall Sheriff Office sits at the edge of town near Red Mesa Canyon. It is a weathered wooden frontier law office with a plain desk, wanted posters, map wall, gun rack, barred cell, wide front window facing the canyon, and a small porch where Super Cowboy can land.

The Light Lasso is an electric blue-white rope of living light with rope-like coils, sparks, realistic glow on hands and nearby dust or rain, and blue particles that can trail behind Super Cowboy when he moves at superhuman speed.
