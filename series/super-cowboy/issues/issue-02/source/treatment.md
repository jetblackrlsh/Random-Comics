# Treatment

## Title

Lightning Rode With Me

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

After a triumphant Light Lasso swing across Dustfall, Super Cowboy returns to the Dustfall Sheriff Office and remembers the stormy night the lasso found him and turned a doomed desert crossing into the start of his heroic life.

## Central Conflict

Super Cowboy has become a celebrated protector of Dustfall, but the Light Lasso's origin reminds him that his power began as a desperate escape from lethal lightning, mud, and fear.

## Theme

A gift that saves your life becomes a duty when other lives need saving.

## Moral Message

Power is not only what lets a hero survive danger. Power becomes meaningful when the hero chooses to answer danger for someone else.

## Ending

In the present, Super Cowboy sits in the sheriff office and thanks the Light Lasso for saving his life. When a woman races in to ask for help, his eyes glow blue, he raises the lasso, and he proudly answers the call.

## Emotional Arc

Super Cowboy begins joyful and confident, swinging above Dustfall while townspeople cheer. At the sheriff office, that public confidence turns inward as he remembers the night he was frightened, lost, and nearly killed in the storm. The flashback shows panic becoming awe, then awe becoming focus as he uses the Light Lasso's power to survive. Returning to the present, he understands the lasso as a responsibility, not a prize, and moves toward the next rescue without hesitation.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, present-day acrobatics over Dustfall, heroic landing and flashback setup, lightning strike and blue particles, the Light Lasso forms and bonds with him, super-speed escape through the storm, canyon leap back toward town, present-day vow and new emergency.
