Super Cowboy rides through the canyon with urgency on his black horse and reaches town just as Robo Rider breaks out of the bank carrying stolen gold and a laser pistol. Robo Rider claims the gold belongs to his creator and fires at Super Cowboy.

Super Cowboy dodges laser blasts from horseback, twirls his Light Lasso, pulls the laser pistol from Robo Rider's hand, and offers surrender. Robo Rider refuses and threatens a choice: take the gold and let the robot self-destruct with the whole town, or let the robot escape with the gold and save the town.

Super Cowboy reads the bluff, realizing the robot cannot be built to destroy the very gold it was sent to steal. Robo Rider counts down, vents steam, then runs on steam jets. Super Cowboy catches its foot with the Light Lasso but gets dragged through the desert at terrifying speed. He focuses, sends a pulse through the Light Lasso, and disables Robo Rider. Bruised and covered in sand, he recovers the gold and complains that robots keep getting crazier. Deep underground, the mad scientist builds the next Robo Rider.
