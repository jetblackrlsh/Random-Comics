# Treatment

## Title

I Called Your Bluff

## Issue Number

1

## Logline

Super Cowboy must stop a steam-driven robotic outlaw from stealing Dustfall's gold by calling the machine's self-destruct bluff and trusting the Light Lasso under brutal pressure.

## Central Conflict

Super Cowboy wants to save Dustfall and recover the stolen gold, but Robo Rider turns the theft into a moral trap by threatening to destroy the town if Super Cowboy stops him.

## Theme

A bluff only works when fear makes you forget what your opponent really wants.

## Moral Message

Courage is not reckless charging. Courage is staying calm enough to understand the real objective, even when danger is loud and immediate.

## Ending

Super Cowboy catches Robo Rider's foot with the Light Lasso and is dragged violently through the desert. He clears his mind, sends a pulse through the lasso, and disables the robot without surrendering the town or the gold. The final beat reveals the mad scientist underground building another Robo Rider.

## Emotional Arc

Super Cowboy begins in urgent motion, riding hard to intercept a bank robbery. Robo Rider's self-destruct threat tries to turn his confidence into hesitation. Super Cowboy's turning point comes when he chooses calm analysis over panic, reads the robot's objective, and acts on the bluff. By the end he is bruised, exhausted, and irritated, but his confidence is earned because the town is safe and the gold is recovered.

## Story Shape

Eight pages: cover, canyon ride and bank robbery, mounted laser chase, Light Lasso disarm and self-destruct ultimatum, Super Cowboy calls the bluff, Robo Rider's steam-jet escape and lasso catch, Light Lasso pulse and crash, aftermath plus the underground creator reveal.
