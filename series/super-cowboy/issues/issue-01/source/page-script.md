# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Super Cowboy, Robo Rider, the Light Lasso, the stolen gold, and the western science-fantasy series identity.
- Layout: Single dramatic cover image with realistic photo-comic cover feel.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Super Cowboy". Smaller issue title text: "I Called Your Bluff".
- Visual beats: Super Cowboy stands in a dusty frontier street at sunset, brown duster whipping, blue armored suit and white star chest emblem visible, Light Lasso glowing in both hands. Midnight rears behind him. Robo Rider faces him from the bank doorway with skull-like metal face, green eyes, black coat, stolen gold under one arm, and green laser pistol raised. Dustfall's wooden bank and red canyon cliffs frame the showdown.
- Prompt notes: Realistic candid photo-comic cover, not painted poster art. Readable title text generated directly inside the cover image.

## Page 2: The Bank Door Explodes Open

- Story role: Establish urgency, setting, protagonist, and inciting robbery.
- Layout: Four panels: canyon ride, town approach, bank breakout, direct confrontation.
- Caption boxes: "Super Cowboy rode hard through Red Mesa Canyon because trouble had already reached Dustfall." "Midnight hit the main street in a storm of dust." "Robo Rider kicked through the bank doors with gold under one arm." "The machine raised a laser pistol and declared, \"All of this town's gold belongs to my creator.\""
- Visual beats: Super Cowboy gallops on Midnight through red canyon trail; town appears ahead under harsh western sun; Robo Rider bursts out through splintered wooden bank doors holding a gold bar and laser pistol; Super Cowboy leans forward with determined expression, riding straight toward the robot.

## Page 3: Green Fire

- Story role: Show the mounted laser attack and Super Cowboy's physical skill.
- Layout: Five action panels with forward motion.
- Caption boxes: "Robo Rider opened fire." "Green bolts cut the dust around Super Cowboy." "He ducked low, leaned hard, and trusted Midnight's stride." "The Light Lasso began to spin over his head."
- Visual beats: Robo Rider fires green laser bolts down Dustfall's main street; Super Cowboy ducks under one blast while galloping; another blast burns through a water trough beside Midnight; close view of Super Cowboy's focused face and glowing lasso coil; wide panel of him twirling the electric blue-white Light Lasso as green bolts barely miss.

## Page 4: The Choice

- Story role: Disarm Robo Rider and introduce the moral trap.
- Layout: Four panels: lasso throw, pistol yank, surrender offer, ultimatum.
- Caption boxes: "The Light Lasso snapped around the pistol." "One pull tore the weapon from Robo Rider's grip." "Super Cowboy said, \"Surrender while you still can.\"" "Robo Rider answered, \"Take the gold and I self-destruct with the town. Let me go, and save them. The choice is yours.\""
- Visual beats: Lasso loop wraps the green laser pistol; pistol flies from Robo Rider's metal hand; Super Cowboy dismounts near Midnight, still holding the glowing lasso; Robo Rider stands rigid outside the bank with gold bar clamped under its arm as townspeople hide behind windows.

## Page 5: Poker Face

- Story role: Super Cowboy calls the bluff and reveals his tactical read.
- Layout: Four tense panels focused on eye contact and countdown pressure.
- Caption boxes: "Steam poured from Robo Rider's seams as its green eyes began to count down." "Super Cowboy stepped closer, unphased." "\"You have a terrible poker face,\" he said." "\"Your creator wouldn't build you to destroy the very gold you were meant to steal.\""
- Visual beats: Robo Rider's green eyes flash countdown-like light; steam bursts from coat seams and metal joints; Super Cowboy walks toward it through dust, lasso hanging ready, face calm; close stare between Super Cowboy's masked eyes and Robo Rider's skull-like metal face.

## Page 6: Steam Run

- Story role: Turn the bluff into a chase and make Super Cowboy pay physically for catching Robo Rider.
- Layout: Five panels with sudden speed.
- Caption boxes: "The countdown ended with no explosion." "Jets of steam blasted from Robo Rider's feet." "Super Cowboy laughed, \"I called your bluff!\"" "His Light Lasso loop caught Robo Rider's foot." "The machine did not fall."
- Visual beats: Robo Rider's boot soles erupt with steam jets; robot launches away from town at incredible speed with the gold; Super Cowboy twirls the Light Lasso and taunts from the street; lasso loop catches Robo Rider's metal foot; Super Cowboy is yanked off-balance as the line goes tight.

## Page 7: Clear The Chaos

- Story role: Show the low point and decisive Light Lasso pulse.
- Layout: Five panels: dragged like a jetski, focus, energy pulse, collapse, tumble.
- Caption boxes: "Robo Rider dragged him across the desert like a man behind a runaway train." "Sand, speed, and pain tried to fill his mind." "Super Cowboy cleared the chaos and focused on the Light Lasso." "A pulse of blue-white energy raced through the loop." "Robo Rider collapsed mid-stride."
- Visual beats: Super Cowboy skids upright behind Robo Rider, boots plowing through sand, duster tearing; blurred desert racing past; close view of Super Cowboy's bruised face becoming calm; bright pulse travels from his hands through the lasso into Robo Rider's foot; Robo Rider short-circuits and crumples, sending both bodies tumbling through desert sand.

## Page 8: Fifth One This Week

- Story role: Resolve the robbery and reveal the ongoing series antagonist.
- Layout: Four panels ending with underground cliffhanger.
- Caption boxes: "When the dust settled, Super Cowboy was bruised, sand-covered, and furious." "Robo Rider's green eyes had gone black." "The gold was still trapped under its ruined arm." "Super Cowboy muttered, \"God, I hate robots. This is the fifth one this week and they just keep getting crazier.\""
- Final panel caption: "Deep beneath the desert, the creator started on the next Robo Rider."
- Visual beats: Super Cowboy lies in sand with torn duster and scuffed armor beside heavily damaged Robo Rider; close view of Robo Rider's black dead eyes and broken metal body; Super Cowboy rolls the machine enough to reveal the gold bar under its arm; final wide panel shows the mad scientist in an underground tunnel lab tinkering on a half-built Robo Rider amid green tubes, steam pipes, and desert sand leaking from the ceiling.
