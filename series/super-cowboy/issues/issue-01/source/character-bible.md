## Stable Visual Continuity

Super Cowboy is a broad-shouldered adult white man with a short brown beard, serious eyes behind a blue eye mask, weathered tan cowboy hat with a blue band and white star, long brown leather duster, blue armored suit with red seams, white star chest emblem, utility belt, dusty western boots, leather gloves, and an electric blue-white Light Lasso. Keep his outfit consistent across all pages. Damage and dust accumulate after he is dragged by Robo Rider.

Midnight is a large black horse with glossy coat, leather western tack, dark saddle, silver conchos, and dust on the legs.

Robo Rider is a tall robotic outlaw with a black cowboy hat, long black coat, metal gloves and joints, skull-like white metal face, glowing green eyes, steam vents, black western clothing, one arm clamping stolen gold, and a compact green laser pistol until disarmed.

The Mad Scientist is a thin older inventor with wild gray hair, cracked goggles, dusty lab clothes, leather apron, soot-stained gloves, and a frantic underground workshop full of brass tools, green power tubes, steam pipes, and half-built robot frames.

## Text Rules

All readable narration, dialogue, title text, labels, and caption text must be generated directly inside the page art by image_gen. Do not add text later. Use rectangular caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, and no blank boxes.
