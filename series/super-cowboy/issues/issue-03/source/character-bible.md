## Stable Visual Continuity

Super Cowboy is a broad-shouldered adult white man with a short brown beard, serious eyes behind a blue eye mask, weathered tan cowboy hat with a blue band and white star, long brown leather duster, blue armored suit with red seams, white star chest emblem, utility belt, dusty western boots, leather gloves, and an electric blue-white Light Lasso. Inside the stained-glass realm, keep the same outfit and silhouette, but let colored glass light refract across his armor and duster. His shame should read through slumped shoulders and lowered eyes before his focus returns.

The Light Lasso is an electric blue-white rope of living light with rope-like coils, sparks, realistic glow on hands, colored glass, iron bars, and nearby spirits. In this issue it can boost Super Cowboy's speed, act like a wrecking-ball tether against demons, and overload a supernatural lock with a focused pulse.

The Soul-Thief Wizard is a tall gaunt older man with ash-gray skin tone, sharp cheekbones, long white beard, narrow cruel eyes, black wide-brim preacher hat, tattered black frock coat over dusty dark robes, silver occult rings, bone charms, cracked leather boots, and a twisted broom of dark wood with straw bound by copper wire. His magic appears as sickly violet-green soul light.

The stolen girl is about seven years old, with light brown skin, dark curly hair tied with a pale ribbon, a simple white frontier dress, small boots, and a frightened but hopeful expression. Her spirit appears translucent with warm gold light around her hands and face. Keep her coffin closed at the funeral and avoid graphic death imagery.

The angel is an adult figure with calm stern eyes, sunlit white frontier robes, subtle gold armor at the shoulders, broad luminous wings, and a peaceful but testing presence. The angel should feel majestic without looking like a statue or painting.

Demonic spirits of the Damned are shadowy translucent figures with cracked ember eyes, smoky torn silhouettes, clawed hands, and distorted human shapes. Keep them frightening but not gory.

Dustfall Church House is a small weathered frontier church with gray-white wood, simple bell tower, rough plank pews, brass oil lamps, pulpit, mourning wreaths, and tall stained-glass windows. The Stained Glass Prison Window shows Super Cowboy as colored glass with faint moving eyes behind the blue mask and blue lasso motifs glowing in the lead seams.
