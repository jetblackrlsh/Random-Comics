# Super Cowboy

## Logline

A masked frontier hero with a glowing Light Lasso protects desert towns from steam-driven robots, impossible machines, and the hidden creator building them beneath the sand.

## Premise

Super Cowboy rides the canyon trails between isolated frontier towns where old western grit collides with strange machines. He is a pragmatic hero, a sharp reader of bluffs, and a stubborn defender of ordinary people caught between greed and invention.

The series follows Super Cowboy as he confronts increasingly dangerous machines sent by a mad scientist working from underground tunnels below the desert. Each issue should feel like a complete western action story with a clear tactical problem, a clever read of an opponent, and a physical cost that proves Super Cowboy wins through nerve as much as power.

## Tone

Dusty, kinetic, clever western superhero adventure. The action should feel grounded and physical even when the Light Lasso and robots push the story into science-fantasy. Super Cowboy is brave and dryly funny, but not invulnerable.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Wide desert canyons, old wooden frontier towns, sand, harsh sunlight, long shadows, and practical western textures.
- Super Cowboy wears a blue armored suit with red seams and a white star chest emblem under a long brown duster, cowboy hat, blue eye mask, utility belt, dusty boots, and leather gloves.
- The Light Lasso appears as a bright electric blue-white rope of energy that behaves like a real lasso but can pulse energy through whatever it catches.
- Robo Rider machines combine skeletal skull-like metal faces, green glowing eyes, black frontier clothing, metal gloves, steam vents, and violent speed.
- The mad scientist's hidden workspaces should look improvised, dangerous, and buried under the desert.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Continuity Notes

- Super Cowboy has already fought several robots before Issue #1, and he hates them because each one is stranger than the last.
- The Light Lasso responds to focus and intent. It can yank weapons away, hold fast around machinery, and discharge a disabling pulse when Super Cowboy clears his mind.
- Robo Rider is a model or line of machines, not just one outlaw. Destroying or disabling one unit does not end the threat.
- The mad scientist beneath the desert is the creator of Robo Rider and remains active after Issue #1.
