# Character Descriptions

## Character: Super Cowboy

- First appears: Issue #1, "I Called Your Bluff"
- Role: protagonist, masked frontier superhero, defender of desert towns.
- Visual identifiers: Broad-shouldered adult white man with a short brown beard, serious eyes behind a blue eye mask, weathered tan cowboy hat with a blue band and white star, long brown leather duster, blue armored suit with red seams, white star chest emblem, utility belt, dusty western boots, leather gloves, and a glowing electric blue-white Light Lasso.
- Continuity notes: Super Cowboy is brave, tactical, and dryly sarcastic. He reads opponents like poker players and trusts the Light Lasso most when he clears panic from his mind.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/super-cowboy-character.png`

## Character: Midnight

- First appears: Issue #1, "I Called Your Bluff"
- Role: Super Cowboy's black horse and trusted mount.
- Visual identifiers: Large black horse with glossy coat, alert ears, strong build, leather western tack, dark saddle, silver conchos, and dust on the legs from hard canyon riding.
- Continuity notes: Midnight carries Super Cowboy through canyon terrain and town streets at dangerous speed, but Super Cowboy dismounts when the tactical problem requires calm nerve instead of a charge.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/midnight-horse-character.png`

## Character: Robo Rider

- First appears: Issue #1, "I Called Your Bluff"
- Role: antagonist, robotic outlaw, gold thief, machine servant of the mad scientist.
- Visual identifiers: Tall humanoid robot in a black cowboy hat, long black coat, black vest and trousers, metal gloves and joints, skull-like white metal face, glowing green eyes, steam vents in seams and boot soles, one arm clamping stolen gold, and a compact green laser pistol.
- Continuity notes: Robo Rider claims it can self-destruct, but its primary objective is to steal gold for its creator. The Issue #1 unit is disabled by the Light Lasso pulse, while a new unit is already being built underground.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/robo-rider-character.png`

## Character: The Mad Scientist

- First appears: Issue #1, "I Called Your Bluff"
- Role: hidden series antagonist, creator of Robo Rider machines.
- Visual identifiers: Thin older inventor in dusty lab clothes, cracked goggles, wild gray hair, leather apron, soot-stained gloves, and a jittery focus while working among brass tools, green power tubes, steam pipes, and half-built robot parts.
- Continuity notes: The scientist operates beneath the desert sand and keeps building more dangerous Robo Rider units after each failure.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/mad-scientist-character.png`
