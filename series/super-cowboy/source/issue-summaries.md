# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: I Called Your Bluff

- Folder: `series/super-cowboy/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Super Cowboy races into Dustfall to stop Robo Rider from stealing the town bank's gold, then calls the robot's self-destruct bluff and disables it with the Light Lasso.
- Plot summary: Super Cowboy rides Midnight through Red Mesa Canyon and reaches Dustfall just as Robo Rider kicks out of the bank with a gold bar and laser pistol. After dodging laser fire, Super Cowboy pulls the pistol away with the Light Lasso, but Robo Rider threatens to self-destruct unless allowed to escape. Super Cowboy realizes the threat conflicts with the robot's theft objective. When Robo Rider runs on steam jets, Super Cowboy catches its foot, gets dragged across the desert, focuses through the Light Lasso, and sends an energy pulse that collapses the machine. He recovers the gold while, deep underground, the mad scientist begins building another Robo Rider.
- Continuity changes: The first visible Robo Rider unit is incapacitated, Dustfall survives, and the mad scientist's underground operation is revealed.
- New characters: Super Cowboy, Midnight, Robo Rider, The Mad Scientist.
- New settings: Red Mesa Canyon, Dustfall, Underground Robot Tunnel.
- New key items: Light Lasso, Robo Rider Laser Pistol, Stolen Gold Bar.

## Issue #2: Lightning Rode With Me

- Folder: `series/super-cowboy/issues/issue-02/`
- Logline: Super Cowboy swings over Dustfall with the Light Lasso, then remembers the stormy night the lasso formed from blue lightning and saved his life.
- Plot summary: Super Cowboy thrills Dustfall by swinging from lamp post to lamp post, running rooftops, and flipping over main street with the Light Lasso while townspeople cheer. After landing at his sheriff office on the canyon edge, he studies the lasso and flashes back to a night when he was lost on foot in a desert storm. Lightning knocked him into the mud, where blue sparks rose from a crater and formed the Light Lasso. When it levitated into his hand, blue energy surged through him and awakened glowing eyes and superhuman speed. He used that speed to dodge a barrage of lightning and leap toward the canyon route back to town. In the present, he tells the lasso that because it saved his life, he now saves others, then answers a woman's urgent call for help.
- Continuity changes: The Light Lasso's origin is revealed: it formed from blue lightning particles in a storm-struck muddy desert puddle and gave Super Cowboy blue-eyed superhuman speed. Super Cowboy's Dustfall sheriff office is established as his base at the canyon edge.
- New characters: None.
- New settings: Dustfall Sheriff Office.
- New key items: None.
- Reference images added: `dustfall-sheriff-office-setting.png`.

## Issue #3: Stained Glass Souls

- Folder: `series/super-cowboy/issues/issue-03/`
- Logline: A soul-thief wizard traps Super Cowboy inside a stained-glass church window, forcing him to cross a spirit realm and free the stolen souls powering the wizard's magic.
- Plot summary: The Soul-Thief Wizard escapes Dustfall on a broom with a little girl's soul while Super Cowboy is trapped inside a stained-glass church window. At the girl's funeral, her family thanks and condemns Super Cowboy in equal measure, and the town nearly turns on the window while his supporters block a rash attempt to destroy it. Inside the endless Stained Glass World, Super Cowboy realizes the wizard trapped his soul, meaning the window must touch the spirit world. He follows that logic deeper, defeats demonic spirits with the Light Lasso, refuses an angel's offer of heavenly rest, and reaches the wizard's spiritual prison. There he overloads the prison lock with the Light Lasso, frees the captive souls, breaks the wizard's active magic, and returns through the window holding the rescued girl's hand.
- Continuity changes: Super Cowboy learns the Light Lasso can affect spirits and overload supernatural locks when focused through a soul-world connection. The Soul-Thief Wizard's soul prison is opened and his stored magical battery is destroyed, though the exact long-term status of the wizard remains unresolved after his power collapses.
- New characters: Soul-Thief Wizard, stolen girl, angel, demonic spirits of the Damned.
- New settings: Dustfall Church House, Stained Glass World, Wizard's Spiritual Prison.
- New key items: Stained Glass Prison Window.
- Reference images added: `soul-thief-wizard-character.png`, `dustfall-church-house-setting.png`, `stained-glass-world-setting.png`, `wizard-spiritual-prison-setting.png`, `stained-glass-prison-window-key-item.png`.
