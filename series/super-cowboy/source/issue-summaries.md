# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: I Called Your Bluff

- Folder: `series/super-cowboy/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Super Cowboy races into Dustfall to stop Robo Rider from stealing the town bank's gold, then calls the robot's self-destruct bluff and disables it with the Light Lasso.
- Plot summary: Super Cowboy rides Midnight through Red Mesa Canyon and reaches Dustfall just as Robo Rider kicks out of the bank with a gold bar and laser pistol. After dodging laser fire, Super Cowboy pulls the pistol away with the Light Lasso, but Robo Rider threatens to self-destruct unless allowed to escape. Super Cowboy realizes the threat conflicts with the robot's theft objective. When Robo Rider runs on steam jets, Super Cowboy catches its foot, gets dragged across the desert, focuses through the Light Lasso, and sends an energy pulse that collapses the machine. He recovers the gold while, deep underground, the mad scientist begins building another Robo Rider.
- Continuity changes: The first visible Robo Rider unit is incapacitated, Dustfall survives, and the mad scientist's underground operation is revealed.
- New characters: Super Cowboy, Midnight, Robo Rider, The Mad Scientist.
- New settings: Red Mesa Canyon, Dustfall, Underground Robot Tunnel.
- New key items: Light Lasso, Robo Rider Laser Pistol, Stolen Gold Bar.
