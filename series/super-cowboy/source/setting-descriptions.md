# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Red Mesa Canyon

- First appears: Issue #1, "I Called Your Bluff"
- Visual identifiers: Vast red-rock desert canyon, dusty trail, towering sandstone walls, scrub brush, hard sunlight, long shadows, distant mesa silhouettes, and loose sand kicked up by galloping hooves.
- Continuity notes: The canyon is Super Cowboy's main approach route into threatened towns and gives the series a wide western scale.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/red-mesa-canyon-setting.png`

## Setting: Dustfall

- First appears: Issue #1, "I Called Your Bluff"
- Visual identifiers: Small frontier town with a wooden bank, plank sidewalks, hitching posts, water trough, dusty main street, weathered storefronts, swinging doors, tall old-fashioned lamp posts, dusty rooftops, and sunbaked desert mountains in the distance.
- Continuity notes: Dustfall is robbed by Robo Rider in Issue #1. The town survives because Super Cowboy calls the self-destruct bluff instead of letting Robo Rider escape with the gold.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/dustfall-town-setting.png`

## Setting: Dustfall Sheriff Office

- First appears: Issue #2, "Lightning Rode With Me"
- Visual identifiers: Weathered wooden frontier sheriff office at the edge of Dustfall near Red Mesa Canyon, small porch, plain law desk, wanted posters, wall map, gun rack, barred holding cell, wide front window facing canyon cliffs, dusty floorboards, and blue Light Lasso glow reflecting on wood.
- Continuity notes: Super Cowboy uses the sheriff office as his base in Dustfall. It is where he reflects on the Light Lasso's origin and commits to using the power to save others.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/dustfall-sheriff-office-setting.png`

## Setting: Underground Robot Tunnel

- First appears: Issue #1, "I Called Your Bluff"
- Visual identifiers: Secret tunnel deep beneath desert sand, timber supports, cables, steam pipes, green-lit workbenches, brass machinery, rail carts, discarded robot limbs, and a half-built Robo Rider frame.
- Continuity notes: The mad scientist builds the next Robo Rider here after the first visible unit fails.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/underground-robot-tunnel-setting.png`
