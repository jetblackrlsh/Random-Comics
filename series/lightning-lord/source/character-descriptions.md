# Lightning Lord Character Descriptions

## Character: Lightning Lord

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Role: protagonist; young electrokinetic investigator from Duke City.
- Visual identifiers: Black man in his early twenties with medium-dark brown skin, a lean athletic build, an angular oval face, hazel-brown eyes, and short tightly coiled black hair cut into a precise low fade with one subtle copper-dyed diagonal streak above the right temple. He wears a cropped charcoal technical jacket traced with thin copper conductive seams, reinforced shoulder panels, a pale gray crew-neck shirt, dark navy tactical trousers, compact waist capacitors, and insulated gloves clipped to his belt. He has no cape, armor, mask, or glowing chest emblem. A collapsible brushed-steel disc the width of a dinner plate mounts between his shoulder blades.
- Posture and expression: alert, analytical, lightly humorous, and ready to move; often studies equipment before attacking it.
- Continuity notes: Burst Serum gave him electrokinesis, electromagnetic control, energy drainage, and the ability to sense powered systems. Magic Mistress detects no magic in him. His flight disc unfolds into a five-foot circular platform that he levitates electromagnetically.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/lightning-lord-character-and-flight-disc.jpg`

## Character: Magic Mistress

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Role: supporting protagonist; magical scholar, investigator, and master of Mage Manor.
- Visual identifiers: South Asian woman in her early thirties with warm brown skin, a tall slender build, sharp cheekbones, gray-violet eyes, and long wavy black hair with a restrained violet streak near the left side of her face. She wears layered futuristic formalwear: a sleeveless midnight-blue coat falling almost to her ankles and shifting between black and indigo, thin incomplete-constellation patterns sewn in silver thread, a fitted plum tunic, flexible black trousers, and flat black boots. Several small brushed-silver rings engraved with microscopic dead-language symbols float around each wrist without touching her skin.
- Posture and expression: composed and scholarly, with economical gestures; fascination can briefly overcome caution when she encounters unprecedented magic.
- Continuity notes: she can scan for magic, fold space for transportation, create silver bindings and chains, and identify magical traditions. Her modern binding magic can be overridden by ancient Serpent Tongue while its user remains empowered.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/magic-mistress-character-and-rings.jpg`

## Character: Bulk Brute

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark" (named as the off-page organizer of the robbery)
- Role: recurring antagonist; Viper-dependent York York crime lord.
- Visual identifiers: enormous middle-aged man with a barrel-shaped body, deeply weathered olive skin partly replaced by dark moss-green reptilian plates, vertically slit amber pupils, a broken heavy nose, filed lower fangs, and a shaved head crossed by old injector scars. He wears a sleeveless soot-black industrial coat with ceramic impact plates, heavy gray work trousers, steel-toed boots, and a permanent multi-reservoir life-support harness feeding iridescent green Viper through reinforced tubes into ports along his shoulders and spine.
- Posture and expression: massive forward lean, controlled predatory stillness, and the patience of an engineer inspecting a machine.
- Continuity notes: repeated Viper use has left him unable to function without the drug. His organization engineered or acquired a dual-injection rig capable of combining Viper and Burst Serum, but he does not physically appear in Issue #1.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/bulk-brute-character.jpg`
