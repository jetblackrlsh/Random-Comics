# Lightning Lord Setting Descriptions

## Setting: York York

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Visual identifiers: dense retro-futurist metropolis of wet brick, reflective glass, oxidized copper, steam vents, six-lane streets, elevated steel train lines, sleek maglev rails, giant animated billboards, and decades-old neon signs. The city should look unable to choose a century, with modern systems grafted onto stubborn old infrastructure beneath low gray clouds.
- Continuity notes: primary series city; home of Magic Mistress, Mage Manor, Bulk Brute's Viper network, and PowerCore's smaller research campus.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/york-york-setting.jpg`

## Setting: Mage Manor

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Visual identifiers: outwardly narrow pearlescent stone mansion overlooking York York, built from elegant sweeping curves and smoky violet windows. Inside it violates geometry: train-station-sized chambers, floating platforms, luminous symbol-bearing ribbons, rotating doorways, glimpses of impossible destinations, violet ambient light, polished pale stone, and an upside-down library.
- Continuity notes: Magic Mistress's home, library, laboratory, and dimensional transit hub. Its internal size and arrangement can change from day to day.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/mage-manor-setting.jpg`

## Setting: PowerCore York York Research Center

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Visual identifiers: discreet converted telecommunications complex beneath an elevated maglev rail, made of three narrow rectangular mid-century buildings around a concrete courtyard, wedged between a parking structure and an abandoned department store. Branding is limited to one white circle divided by a vertical crimson line. Inside are amber emergency lights, overturned laboratory cabinets, reinforced glass partitions, elevated observation platforms, and a large chemical storage chamber.
- Continuity notes: robbed by Bulk Brute's operatives in Issue #1. The known clean Burst Serum stockpile is stolen and destroyed or contaminated during the pursuit.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/powercore-research-center-setting.jpg`
