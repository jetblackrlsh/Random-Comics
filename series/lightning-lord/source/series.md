# Lightning Lord

## Logline

Electrokinetic investigator Lightning Lord teams with the reality-bending Magic Mistress to uncover why Burst Serum can rewrite not only biology, but humanity's relationship with magic.

## Premise

Years after Burst Serum transformed hundreds of people in Duke City, Lightning Lord still does not know what the substance truly did to him or what other systems it might alter. His search for a clean sample brings him to York York and into partnership with Magic Mistress, a scholar of magical traditions whose methods can test questions that conventional laboratories cannot. Their investigations repeatedly expose engineered collisions between science and sorcery, especially the criminal experiments sponsored by the Viper-dependent crime lord Bulk Brute.

## Tone

Clear, fast-moving superhero mystery with accessible explanations of scientific and magical rules, grounded humor between the leads, photoreal urban spectacle, and an optimistic belief that different disciplines become stronger when their experts cooperate.

## Recurring Visual Language

- York York combines wet brick, glass towers, oxidized copper, elevated trains, maglev rails, old neon, and animated billboards under dramatic storm clouds.
- Lightning Lord is framed through copper circuitry, blue-white electrical arcs, practical technology, and observational close-ups.
- Magic Mistress is framed through silver geometry, violet light, impossible architecture, and floating inscribed rings.
- Burst Serum glows cold blue; Viper glows toxic iridescent green; their unstable magical interaction produces ancient gold symbols.
- The realistic candid photo-comic style should resemble documentary phone photography and handheld photojournalism, with believable materials, natural imperfections, and photoreal visual effects.

## Continuity Notes

- Lightning Lord's own Burst Serum transformation is biological and electrokinetic; Magic Mistress detects no magic in him.
- Burst Serum can temporarily awaken or manufacture a Magi Manis magical affinity when delivered simultaneously with Viper through a powered dual-injection rig.
- The resulting Serpent Tongue magic is exceptionally ancient and can break Magic Mistress's newer binding spell while the rig remains powered.
- Draining the rig's electrical cell stops the Viper delivery, reverses the temporary reptilian and magical mutation, and allows Magic Mistress's bindings to work.
- Bulk Brute's organization has the engineering knowledge and intent to combine Viper with Burst Serum, but the designers and full scope of the project remain unknown.
- Lightning Lord and Magic Mistress become investigative partners at the end of Issue #1.
