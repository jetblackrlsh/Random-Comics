# Lightning Lord Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: Burst Serum

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Visual identifiers: cold blue, faintly self-luminous fluid stored in narrow sealed glass ampoules inside a reinforced vapor-chilled transport case.
- Continuity notes: caused the Duke City catastrophe and permanent biological powers including Lightning Lord's electrokinesis. When combined with Viper in a powered dual-injection rig, it temporarily creates magical affinity and reptilian mutation rather than its usual permanent effect. All known clean York York samples are contaminated or destroyed at the end of Issue #1.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/burst-serum-viper-and-dual-injection-rig-key-items.jpg`

## Key Item: Viper

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Visual identifiers: toxic iridescent green chemical carried in thick transparent harness reservoirs and fed through rugged black tubing.
- Continuity notes: temporarily increases muscle density, reflexes, pain tolerance, and metabolic output while producing progressively reptilian mutations. Users require repeated delivery; shutting down the powered rig reverses a temporary dose.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/burst-serum-viper-and-dual-injection-rig-key-items.jpg`

## Key Item: Dual-Injection Rig

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Visual identifiers: industrial gray back harness with ceramic armor, two transparent cylindrical reservoir ports, separate green and blue tube networks, a central spinal regulator, warning lights, rugged pump housings, and a small shielded lithium power cell.
- Continuity notes: engineered to inject Viper and Burst Serum simultaneously. Its powered regulator is necessary to sustain the temporary reptilian Magi Manis transformation. Its existence proves Bulk Brute's robbery was planned research, not opportunistic theft.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/burst-serum-viper-and-dual-injection-rig-key-items.jpg`

## Key Item: Lightning Lord's Flight Disc

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Visual identifiers: unmarked brushed-steel circular device carried between Lightning Lord's shoulder blades. It unfolds through radial seams into a broad five-foot segmented platform with copper induction channels and a rim lit by blue-white electrical arcs.
- Continuity notes: levitated and propelled by Lightning Lord's electromagnetic power. It can carry Lightning Lord and at least one passenger at high speed but has thermal limits.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/lightning-lord-character-and-flight-disc.jpg`

## Key Item: Magic Mistress's Wrist Rings

- First appears: Issue #1, "Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark"
- Visual identifiers: several small brushed-silver rings orbiting each wrist without contact, each engraved with hundreds of microscopic characters from dead magical languages. They rotate in different axes and glow silver-violet during spellwork.
- Continuity notes: focus Magic Mistress's scanning, transportation, binding, and sealing magic. They sometimes rotate autonomously when they sense unfamiliar magic.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/magic-mistress-character-and-rings.jpg`
