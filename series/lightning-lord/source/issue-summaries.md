# Lightning Lord Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark

- Folder: `series/lightning-lord/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Lightning Lord and Magic Mistress pursue a clean sample of Burst Serum, only to discover that Bulk Brute's gang can combine it with Viper to create temporary reptilian sorcerers.
- Plot summary: Magic Mistress confirms that Lightning Lord's own Burst Serum powers are not magical, so the heroes travel to PowerCore's York York laboratory for a clean sample. They find Bulk Brute's operatives stealing the stockpile and witness one activate a specially engineered dual-injection rig. The combination of Viper and Burst Serum transforms him into a powerful reptilian Magi Manis whose ancient Serpent Tongue magic defeats Magic Mistress's bindings. During the pursuit, Lightning Lord realizes the transformation depends on the rig's electrical power, drains its battery, and allows Magic Mistress to capture the reverted operative. The remaining serum shatters into a muddy gutter, denying the heroes a clean sample, but the engineered rig proves that Bulk Brute is pursuing a planned science-and-sorcery project. Lightning Lord and Magic Mistress become partners to investigate the larger threat.
- Continuity changes: Lightning Lord and Magic Mistress form an investigative partnership. Burst Serum is proven capable of temporarily creating magical affinity when paired with Viper. Bulk Brute's organization is established as the force engineering the combination. The known York York clean samples are lost.
- New characters: Lightning Lord; Magic Mistress; Bulk Brute (named, off-page)
- New settings: York York; Mage Manor; PowerCore York York Research Center
- New key items: Burst Serum; Viper; dual-injection rig; Lightning Lord's flight disc; Magic Mistress's wrist rings
