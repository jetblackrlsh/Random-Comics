## Lightning Lord

Use `../../../reference-images/lightning-lord-character-and-flight-disc.jpg`. Preserve his medium-dark brown skin, lean athletic build, angular oval face, hazel-brown eyes, short tight coils with low fade and subtle copper diagonal hair streak, cropped charcoal technical jacket with copper conductive seams, pale gray shirt, dark navy trousers, waist capacitors, clipped insulated gloves, and unmarked collapsible back disc. Never add a cape, mask, bulky armor, or chest emblem.

## Magic Mistress

Use `../../../reference-images/magic-mistress-character-and-rings.jpg`. Preserve her warm brown skin, tall slender build, sharp cheekbones, gray-violet eyes, long wavy black hair with one violet streak, sleeveless ankle-length midnight-blue coat with silver incomplete-constellation stitching, plum tunic, black trousers, flat boots, and multiple floating engraved silver wrist rings.

## Bulk Brute Operative

Unnamed adult male criminal with a dense powerlifter build, shaved head, gray industrial trousers, steel-toed boots, sleeveless ceramic impact vest, angular bulldog-silhouette respirator, and the dual-injection rig from `../../../reference-images/burst-serum-viper-and-dual-injection-rig-key-items.jpg`. After activation, add thick overlapping moss-green reptilian scales, slit amber pupils, long fangs, a black forked tongue, and fast-moving gold symbols under the skin. These changes recede when Lightning Lord drains the rig.

## Global Page Rules

Every final page is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page. All readable story text is generated directly inside roomy rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Caption boxes contain only the exact reader-facing sentences: no panel labels, caption labels, caption numbers, page numbers, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, subtitles, logos, or prompt scaffolding. Preserve approximately six percent clear outer margin around every complete caption border and every letter.
