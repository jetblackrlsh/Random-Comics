# Treatment

## Title

Lightning Lord and the Serpent Spark

## Issue Number

1

## Publication Date

2026-08-20

## Series

Lightning Lord

## Page Count

8 pages: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

Lightning Lord and Magic Mistress pursue a clean sample of the power-granting Burst Serum, but a Bulk Brute operative combines it with Viper and becomes a temporary reptilian sorcerer whose escape exposes a deliberate plan to fuse science with ancient magic.

## Central Conflict

The heroes must stop a transformed operative from delivering stolen Burst Serum to Bulk Brute while simultaneously understanding why the same chemical that made Lightning Lord an electrokinetic can awaken magic in a different person.

## Theme

No single discipline can explain every mystery. Lightning Lord wins by applying electrical engineering to a magical crisis, while Magic Mistress supplies the magical knowledge that turns a criminal encounter into a larger investigation.

## Emotional Arc

Lightning Lord begins hopeful that Magic Mistress can provide a simple answer about his powers. He is disappointed to learn that he is not magical, then energized by the possibility of testing a clean sample. The PowerCore robbery turns curiosity into urgency, and the ruined serum makes the immediate mission seem like a failure. By the end, he recognizes that the unexpected evidence and a trusted partner are more valuable than the answer he originally sought.

## Ending

With the clean samples lost in a gutter but the transformed operative captured, Lightning Lord and Magic Mistress deduce that someone deliberately engineered a rig to combine Viper and Burst Serum. They shake hands and become partners, committed to stopping Bulk Brute and discovering what Burst Serum truly is.

## Visual Approach

Realistic candid photo-comic pages with documentary urban photography, real skin and fabric texture, natural imperfect framing, believable practical locations, and photoreal electrical and magical effects. Long rectangular caption boxes carry the detailed explanations. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, page numbers, caption labels, or later-added text.
