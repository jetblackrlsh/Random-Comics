# Page Script

All pages are full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Caption-box text, title text, and any other readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, and no later text overlay. Prefer 3-5 roomy panels on story pages so the tactical action and emotional turns remain readable.

## Page 1 - Cover

- Story role: Establish the series identity, the empty throne, the brothers, and the fang-sword tragedy.
- Layout: Dramatic cover composition, not a grid. Damon and Jaco stand on opposite sides of the moonlit throne court, blades drawn, empty obsidian throne looming behind them. Faint portal cracks curl behind Jaco; white path-light trails curl near Damon.
- Caption boxes/title text: Large readable title text generated inside the art: "Fangs of Zarkon". Smaller issue title text: "The Throne Can Wait". No other text.
- Visual beats: Damon and Jaco glare at each other with hatred; the empty throne is centered; two fang swords catch the moonlight; a ghostly suggestion of Zarkon's horned silhouette hangs over the throne.
- Continuity notes: Use Damon, Jaco, throne court, Pathfang Blade, and Reality Splitter descriptions.

## Page 2 - The Empty Throne

- Story role: Establish the missing father, the succession stakes, and Jaco's betrayal.
- Layout: 4 panels. Panel 1: wide shot of the empty throne and the brothers approaching from opposite sides. Panel 2: Damon accusing Jaco, finger near sword hilt. Panel 3: Jaco smirking and lifting the Reality Splitter. Panel 4: close shot of both fang blades angled toward each other.
- Caption boxes: "Demon Emperor Zarkon's throne stood empty because one son had banished him." "Damon came to drag the truth from Jaco." "Jaco smiled because the truth was already a weapon." "Only one brother could rise from the court and call the empire his."
- Visual beats: Hatred, royal absence, moonlit dust, torn banners.

## Page 3 - Reality Splitter

- Story role: Show Jaco's power and make the duel physically dangerous.
- Layout: 5 action panels. Jaco slashes; crescent portal rips tear loose; portals carve through pillars and sand; Damon braces; the last panel shows multiple portal crescents racing toward him.
- Caption boxes: "The Reality Splitter did not merely cut flesh." "Every stroke opened a wound in distance itself." "Crescent portals flew like blades across the throne court." "If Damon misread one angle, the empire would inherit a corpse."
- Visual beats: Photoreal portal crescents, spacetime distortions, cracked pillars, Damon still not moving until the safe path appears.

## Page 4 - The Path Damon Sees

- Story role: Reveal Damon's power and counter Jaco's first attack.
- Layout: 5 panels. Damon's eyes flash white; thin white route-lines appear along the floor; the Pathfang blade tugs forward; Damon slides frictionlessly around portal crescents; he ends beside Jaco without being touched.
- Caption boxes: "Damon's eyes found the one path where death had not arrived." "His fang blade pulled him along it." "For one breath, the floor forgot friction." "Jaco's portals missed by inches because Damon could see where survival waited."
- Visual beats: White eye glow, bone-white light trail, motion blur on feet, portal crescents behind him.

## Page 5 - Moonlight Blindness

- Story role: Jaco finds the weakness in Damon's path-sight.
- Layout: 4 panels. Panel 1: Damon declares Jaco's portal slashes useless. Panel 2: Jaco angles the Reality Splitter toward the moon. Panel 3: reflected moonlight flashes into Damon's eyes. Panel 4: Damon halts mid-step, eyes watering, path-light vanishing.
- Caption boxes: "Damon thought sight made him untouchable." "Jaco did not argue. He borrowed the moon." "White fire filled Damon's eyes." "The path disappeared, and the impossible warrior stopped."
- Visual beats: Blade reflection, harsh moon flare, Damon disoriented, Jaco predatory.

## Page 6 - Blind Instinct

- Story role: Preserve Damon dodging the portal slash while blinded, proving discipline beyond magic sight.
- Layout: 5 panels. Jaco launches a killing portal slash; Damon covers his blinded eyes; the portal ripple distorts dust and banners; Damon remembers Zarkon's training as a small inset-like memory image without dialogue; Damon moves at the last instant and the portal slash tears past his shoulder.
- Caption boxes: "Jaco struck before Damon's sight returned." "Damon could not see the path." "But he felt reality tighten around the incoming wound." "Zarkon had taught him that a warrior's last sense was trust." "Damon moved blind, and death passed close enough to cut his armor."
- Visual beats: No clear eye glow, soundless tension, portal grazing armor, Damon surviving by instinct.

## Page 7 - Dust And Cough

- Story role: Damon turns blindness back on Jaco and wins the duel.
- Layout: 5 panels. Damon cuts across sand-covered ground; dust erupts; Jaco coughs unseen; Damon listens; Damon makes one decisive slice; dust thins to reveal Jaco falling.
- Caption boxes: "Damon answered blindness with blindness." "His blade tore the sand into a storm." "Jaco laughed until dust filled his throat." "One cough gave Damon all the direction he needed." "The succession ended with a single cut."
- Visual beats: Dust cloud, silhouettes, no gore emphasis, Jaco's Reality Splitter falling from his hand.

## Page 8 - The Throne Can Wait

- Story role: Damon chooses father over power.
- Layout: 4 panels. Damon kneels by Jaco's body and picks up the Reality Splitter; the empty throne waits behind him; Damon looks from throne to portal blade; Damon opens a vertical rift with a grief-stricken choice.
- Caption boxes: "The Reality Splitter could open the road to Zarkon." "The throne could be Damon's before the dust settled." "Power waited behind him. His father waited somewhere beyond the wound in space." "Damon chose the father who had made him before the empire that wanted him."
- Visual beats: Jaco dead but not graphic, Damon holding two swords, rift opening, moonlight on empty throne.

## Page 9 - Zarkon's Grief

- Story role: Give Zarkon a full emotional page processing Jaco's corpse.
- Layout: 5 panels. Zarkon steps from the rift relieved; he embraces Damon; he notices Jaco's body; rage flares in his face and claws; rage collapses into grief as he kneels beside Jaco.
- Caption boxes: "Zarkon returned ready to thank the son who found him." "For one heartbeat, Damon was only a child in his father's arms." "Then Zarkon saw the son who would never rise." "His fury searched for someone to punish." "It found only a father who had raised princes into weapons."
- Visual beats: Zarkon enormous and terrifying, hug is sincere, grief shifts through rage, Damon afraid to move.

## Page 10 - Emperor With A Broken Heart

- Story role: End Issue #1 with Damon emperor and the series quest established.
- Layout: 4 panels. Zarkon snatches the Reality Splitter; Damon reaches to stop him; Zarkon opens a rift and rushes through; Damon falls to his knees before the empty throne with both brother and father gone; final panel shows Damon small beneath the throne, crown-shadow over him.
- Caption boxes: "Zarkon took the blade before Damon understood the choice." "He would not rule over the grave his family had made." "The rift closed with Damon reaching for a father who refused to stay saved." "With his brother dead and his father gone, Damon became emperor." "His first command to himself was not to rule. It was to find Zarkon."
- Visual beats: Reality Splitter in Zarkon's claw, portal closing, Damon devastated, empty throne now belongs to him.
