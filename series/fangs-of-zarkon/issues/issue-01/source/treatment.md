# The Throne Can Wait

## Title

The Throne Can Wait

## Issue Number

1

## Logline

Damon wins the demon throne by killing his brother Jaco, but when he chooses family over power, their father Zarkon's grief leaves Damon emperor of a broken empire.

## Central Conflict

Damon and Jaco both claim the right to rule after Jaco banishes Demon Emperor Zarkon. Their duel tests two inherited fang swords against each other: Jaco's Reality Splitter weaponizes portals and misdirection, while Damon's Pathfang blade and glowing eyes make him almost impossible to hit while he can see. The physical fight becomes an emotional test when Damon wins and must decide whether the throne matters more than saving the father who raised him.

## Theme

Power inherited through family violence becomes a curse unless someone chooses love over succession. Damon makes the right choice, but the empire is too damaged for that choice to save everyone.

## Ending

Damon uses the Reality Splitter to bring Zarkon home instead of claiming the throne immediately. Zarkon embraces him, sees Jaco's corpse, and realizes his sons became weapons in the shadow of his rule. He takes the Reality Splitter and vanishes into exile, leaving Damon emperor with a broken heart and a new quest.

## Emotional Arc

Damon begins furious and certain that his brother's betrayal can be answered by strength. During the duel, blindness forces him to move beyond his magical sight and trust his training. After killing Jaco, he chooses loyalty over ambition by rescuing Zarkon. Zarkon's self-exile turns Damon's victory into a wound: he becomes emperor, but the crown now means responsibility, grief, and a promise to find his father.
