The two samurais looked at each other with such glaring hatred. If their feelings could manifest as raw power, they would destroy mountains. Their swords were crafted from the fangs of their father, Demon Emperor Zarkon. Only one of them could rise to rule in their father's absence.

Damon accused Jaco of banishing their father. Jaco admitted the truth and mocked Damon for not knowing which dimension held Zarkon. Their duel began with Jaco's Reality Splitter blade tearing crescent-shaped portal rips through spacetime. Damon used his glowing eyes and Pathfang blade to see safe routes and slide across the ground as if there was no friction.

Jaco reflected moonlight into Damon's eyes, blinding him and breaking the path-sight. Damon still sensed reality rippling around the incoming portal slash, trusted instinct instead of vision, and dodged at the last instant. Damon then cut the sand-covered ground into a dust cloud, blinded Jaco, followed the sound of Jaco's cough, and killed him with one slice.

Damon picked up the Reality Splitter. He could take the throne immediately, but he chose to rescue Zarkon first. Zarkon returned relieved and hugged Damon, then saw Jaco dead. His anger rose, shifted away from both sons, and collapsed into self-blame. Zarkon snatched the Reality Splitter and exiled himself again. Damon became emperor with a broken heart, swearing that one day he would find his father.
