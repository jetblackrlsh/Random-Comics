## Damon Zarkon

Late 20s, stern angular face, medium tan skin, long black hair tied back with loose strands, pale gray eyes that glow white when path-sight activates, athletic swordsman's build, dark charcoal samurai armor with bone-white trim and a weathered crimson sash. He carries the Pathfang blade. His posture is controlled and precise even when furious. After page 5, his eyes are watering and temporarily blinded by reflected moonlight. After page 7, dust and battle scuffs mark his armor.

## Jaco Zarkon

Late 20s to early 30s, sharp cheekbones, medium tan skin, black hair in a high warrior knot, narrow dark eyes, black samurai armor with red lacquer plates and silver fang motifs. He carries the Reality Splitter blade. He moves with arrogant confidence and smiles when he thinks he has found an opponent's weakness. On page 7 he is blinded by dust and killed.

## Demon Emperor Zarkon

Towering horned demon emperor with weathered red-brown skin, heavy brow, bone crown fused with dark metal, long gray-black hair, black imperial armor with gold and bone inlays, clawed hands, scarred face, terrifying presence softened by paternal grief. He appears on pages 8-10 after Damon opens the portal.

## Setting

The Demon Emperor's Throne Court is a moonlit throne court of black volcanic stone, bone-white inlaid floor paths, sand and dust across cracked slabs, massive empty throne carved from obsidian and ivory, torn imperial banners, jagged archways open to a cold moon, weathered gold accents, and ash drifting through the air.

## Key Items

Pathfang Blade: Damon's fang sword, curved ivory katana-like blade with a dark ridged root near the guard, black cord-wrapped handle, bone-white glow, and light trails that pull Damon along safe paths.

Reality Splitter: Jaco's fang sword, serrated ivory katana-like blade with black-red cracks, dark metal guard shaped like broken jaws, and crescent portal light peeling away from each slash.
