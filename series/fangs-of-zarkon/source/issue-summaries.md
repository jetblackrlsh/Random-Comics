# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: The Throne Can Wait

- Folder: `series/fangs-of-zarkon/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Damon defeats his brother Jaco for the empty demon throne, chooses to rescue their father instead of ruling, and becomes emperor only after Zarkon exiles himself in grief.
- Plot summary: Damon and Jaco meet in Zarkon's moonlit throne court after Jaco has banished their father. Jaco attacks with the Reality Splitter's portal slashes, while Damon survives through path-sight and the movement pull of his Pathfang blade. Jaco blinds Damon with reflected moonlight, but Damon senses the portal slash by instinct, then blinds Jaco with dust and kills him by sound. Damon uses the Reality Splitter to retrieve Zarkon, but Zarkon sees Jaco's corpse, realizes his sons destroyed each other in the shadow of his throne, and exiles himself again. Damon is left emperor with a broken heart and a vow to find his father.
- Continuity changes: Damon becomes emperor of the Demon Empire; Jaco dies; Zarkon returns briefly, takes the Reality Splitter, and self-exiles; Damon begins his search for Zarkon.
- New characters: Damon Zarkon, Jaco Zarkon, Demon Emperor Zarkon.
- New settings: Demon Emperor's Throne Court.
- New key items: Pathfang Blade, Reality Splitter.
