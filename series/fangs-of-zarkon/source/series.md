# Fangs of Zarkon

## Logline

After a duel for an empty demon throne leaves one prince dead and one emperor self-exiled, Damon Zarkon rules a broken empire while searching for a way to save the father who cannot forgive himself.

## Premise

The Demon Empire was built by Zarkon, a terrifying ruler whose sons inherited both his power and his emotional damage. Damon, the disciplined warrior prince, carries a fang-forged sword that drags him along paths his glowing eyes can foresee. Jaco, his ambitious brother, carries the Reality Splitter, a fang blade that cuts rifts through space. When Zarkon vanishes into another dimension, the brothers' succession duel turns the imperial throne into a family grave.

The series follows Damon after he wins the crown he no longer wants. He must govern a supernatural empire, uncover where Zarkon has exiled himself, and face the possibility that the empire's cruelty came from the same family loyalty he is trying to preserve.

## Tone

Tragic supernatural martial fantasy with tactical sword fights, demonic imperial politics, emotional consequences, and sincere family grief. Action should be readable and specific: every power has a rule, every rule creates a counter, and every victory costs something personal.

## Recurring Visual Language

- Realistic candid photo-comic style with documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism framing.
- Demonic imperial spaces should feel like physical ancient architecture, not abstract fantasy backdrops: black volcanic stone, bone-white inlays, weathered gold, moonlit courtyards, dust, banners, cracked steps, and human-scale camera angles.
- Damon is identified by white eye glow during precognitive movement, dark samurai armor with bone-white trim, and a fang-forged blade that seems to pull him forward along light paths.
- Jaco is identified by a black-and-red samurai silhouette, a sharper smirk, and crescent-shaped portal wounds torn by the Reality Splitter.
- Zarkon should look regal and monstrous but emotionally readable: horned demon emperor, weathered armor, bone crown, paternal grief visible through fury.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.

## Continuity Notes

- Issue #1 establishes that Zarkon's fang blades have distinct powers: Damon's Pathfang blade guides frictionless movement along seen routes, while Jaco's Reality Splitter opens rifts and portal slashes.
- Jaco banished Zarkon before the issue begins, but he dies during the succession duel.
- Damon chooses to rescue Zarkon instead of immediately taking the throne.
- Zarkon sees Jaco's corpse, processes rage as grief and self-blame, then exiles himself again using the Reality Splitter.
- Damon ends Issue #1 as emperor of the Demon Empire, but his true goal is to find and save Zarkon.
