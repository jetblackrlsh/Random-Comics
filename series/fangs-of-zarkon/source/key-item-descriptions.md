# Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: Pathfang Blade

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Throne Can Wait"
- Visual identifiers: katana-like fang sword with a curved ivory blade grown from a giant demon fang, dark ridged root near the guard, black cord-wrapped handle, bone-white glow when Damon sees a safe path, faint light trails pulling the wielder forward.
- Continuity notes: Damon's inherited blade pairs with his glowing eyes, making him almost impossible to hit while he can see. It cannot guide him when he is blinded, forcing him to rely on instinct.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/pathfang-blade-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Reality Splitter

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Throne Can Wait"
- Visual identifiers: katana-like fang sword with a serrated ivory edge, black-red cracks glowing along the blade, dark metal guard shaped like broken jaws, crescent portal light peeling away from each slash.
- Continuity notes: Jaco used this blade to banish Zarkon and attack Damon. Damon briefly uses it to bring Zarkon back, but Zarkon takes it and uses it for self-exile.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/reality-splitter-key-item.png`
