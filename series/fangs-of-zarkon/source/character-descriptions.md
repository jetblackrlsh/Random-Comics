# Character Descriptions

## Character: Damon Zarkon

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Throne Can Wait"
- Role: protagonist, surviving prince, new emperor.
- Visual identifiers: late 20s, stern angular face, medium tan skin, long black hair tied back with loose strands, pale gray eyes that glow white when his path-sight activates, athletic swordsman's build, dark charcoal samurai armor with bone-white trim and a weathered crimson sash, controlled posture, grief held behind discipline, carries the Pathfang blade.
- Continuity notes: Damon kills Jaco in the succession duel, rescues Zarkon from banishment, then becomes emperor when Zarkon self-exiles. His central ongoing goal is finding his father again.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/damon-zarkon-character.png`

## Character: Jaco Zarkon

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Throne Can Wait"
- Role: antagonist, Damon brother, dead prince.
- Visual identifiers: late 20s to early 30s, sharp cheekbones, medium tan skin, black hair in a high warrior knot, narrow dark eyes, black samurai armor with red lacquer plates and silver fang motifs, predatory smirk, aggressive stance, carries the Reality Splitter blade.
- Continuity notes: Jaco banished Zarkon before Issue #1 and intended to take the throne. Damon kills him during the duel after blinding him with dust.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/jaco-zarkon-character.png`

## Character: Demon Emperor Zarkon

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Throne Can Wait"
- Role: Damon and Jaco's father, former demon emperor, absent ruler.
- Visual identifiers: towering horned demon emperor, weathered red-brown skin, heavy brow, bone crown fused with dark metal, long gray-black hair, black imperial armor with gold and bone inlays, clawed hands, scarred face, terrifying presence softened by paternal grief.
- Continuity notes: Zarkon was banished by Jaco before Issue #1. Damon rescues him, but the sight of Jaco dead breaks him. Zarkon takes the Reality Splitter and exiles himself again.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/zarkon-character.png`
