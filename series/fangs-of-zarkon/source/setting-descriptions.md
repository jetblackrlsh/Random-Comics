# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Demon Emperor's Throne Court

- First appears: Issue #1, "The Throne Can Wait"
- Visual identifiers: vast moonlit throne court of black volcanic stone, bone-white inlaid floor paths, sand and dust across cracked slabs, massive empty throne carved from obsidian and ivory, torn imperial banners, jagged archways open to a cold moon, weathered gold accents, ash drifting through the air.
- Continuity notes: Damon kills Jaco here, opens the portal that returns Zarkon, and inherits the throne after Zarkon's self-exile.
