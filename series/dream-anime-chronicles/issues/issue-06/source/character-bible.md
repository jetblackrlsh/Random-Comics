# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Ash Halfling

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Ash Halfling page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Ash_Halfling`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/ash-halfling-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Young anime woman with long curly orange hair, red eyes, pointed ears, curvy/plump build, plain solid pink T-shirt, pink athletic skirt, white socks, plain pink sneakers, pink round glasses, expressive anxious-to-determined face, and small luminous illusion sparks around her hands.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not make Ash thin, armored, photorealistic, blue-haired, blonde, short-haired, animal-eared, elderly, faceless, or dressed in elaborate robes. Keep the orange curly hair, red eyes, pointed ears, pink glasses, pink T-shirt, pink athletic skirt, white socks, and pink sneakers consistent on every page.
- Powers to show: Small illusions, fleeting light shows, sensing magical presences, and a later synthesis glow where human technology and elfin magic join through the Fusion Bracelet.
- Personality and motivation: Faithful, adventurous, ambitious, determined, loyal to those she loves, and desperate to prove her worth to the elf gods. She starts convinced that becoming a pure elf will make her worthy, then discovers that self-acceptance is the only power that lets the bracelet respond.
- Emotional continuity: Starts hopeful but insecure, grows frantic after rejection, nearly erases herself through forced purity, then becomes calm and fierce when she accepts both her human and elfin heritage.

## Halflight Technorealm

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Ash Halfling page and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/setting-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/halflight-technorealm-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Enchanted fragmented realm where glowing elfin forests, ancient moonstone ruins, and tall shrine gates overlap with sleek floating cities, renewable-energy towers, transparent transit bridges, holographic map altars, and luminous magic-tech circuitry. Use saturated emerald, gold, magenta, cyan, and warm pink light.
- Story use: The realm externalizes Ash's divided identity. Ancient elfin sites judge her as incomplete, while futuristic human spaces help her find tools, maps, and courage that tradition alone denies.

## Fusion Bracelet

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Ash Halfling page and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/key-item-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/fusion-bracelet-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Futuristic bracelet on Ash's wrist, rose-gold and white alloy frame, translucent pink crystal core, tiny emerald elfin runes, cyan holographic circuitry, twin crescent clasps, and a glow that splits into warm human-tech light and green-gold elfin magic before merging.
- Story use: Ash seeks it as a way to become a pure elf. The bracelet initially rejects her because she uses it for self-erasure. It awakens when she chooses synthesis instead of purification.

## Elf Gods

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Ash Halfling page.
- Reference image: None separate; they appear as distant divine silhouettes and luminous shrine reflections, not recurring individually named characters in this issue.
- Stable visible identifiers: Towering abstract elfin divine figures made of emerald-white light, antler-like halos, faceless judgment masks, and long ceremonial silhouettes reflected in moonstone gates.
- Story use: They are the external source of rejection and the symbol of purity Ash thinks she must satisfy. The ending does not make them the source of Ash's worth; it shows that Ash can open a path they could not.
