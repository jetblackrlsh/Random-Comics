Create Dream Anime Chronicles issue #5 as a character introduction issue for Auralite Prism, using the Dream Anime Wiki page at https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Auralite_Prism and the user-provided character reference image.

Auralite Prism is enigmatic, composed, profoundly intuitive, and serene under pressure. They are the Keeper of the Ethereal Essence, guardian of the iridescent cosmic slime that powers magic and holds their post-apocalyptic world in balance. Auralite's aura-based powers allow superhuman leaps and the unusual ability to materialize imaginary characters into the physical realm. Their connection to the cosmic slime gives them deep magic and a bond with the natural energies of their world. They were born amid the ruins of a once-flourishing civilization and created a robot bodyguard to help protect the essence.

This issue should show Auralite failing when they treat guardianship as distance, then force, then command. The defining victory should come when Auralite realizes that imagination must cooperate with the Ethereal Essence instead of using it as a weapon.
