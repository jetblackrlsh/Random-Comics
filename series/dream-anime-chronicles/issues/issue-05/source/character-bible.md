# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Auralite Prism

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Auralite Prism page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Auralite_Prism`, and the user-provided reference image.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/auralite-prism-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Androgynous youthful anime guardian with a smooth delicate face, large luminous rainbow-prism eyes, pale mint/aqua hair with layered sweeping bangs and teal-green shadows, iridescent blue-purple-pink aura markings across the face and neck like living crystal slime, glossy translucent orb accents around the hair and aura, one large circular white-and-black mechanical earpiece/headset on the left side, white collared shirt, dark teal tie, slim white-and-silver futuristic shoulder/bodyguard harness, saturated rainbow glow, serene unreadable expression, and composed upright posture.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not make Auralite look ordinary, gritty, muted, armored like a knight, animal-like, monstrous, elderly, bearded, or photorealistic. Keep the mechanical earpiece on one side, the white shirt and teal tie, the mint/aqua hair, the rainbow eyes, and the iridescent slime-like aura markings consistent.
- Powers to show: Aura-based superhuman leaps, luminous aura shields, deep connection to the cosmic slime, intuitive sensing of natural magical energy, and the ability to materialize imaginary characters into the physical world.
- Personality and motivation: Enigmatic, composed, profoundly intuitive, serene in chaos, peaceful but strong. Auralite is driven to protect the Ethereal Essence, preserve balance in a post-apocalyptic world, and prevent anyone from misusing the world's magic source.
- Emotional continuity: Starts calm but too distant, fails when protection becomes control, then chooses a more honest serenity that listens to the living essence before acting.

## Auralite's Robot Bodyguard

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Auralite Prism page, which notes that Auralite created a robot bodyguard.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/auralite-robot-bodyguard-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Tall sleek anime robot guardian with white ceramic armor, chrome joints, teal and magenta light seams, rounded shoulder pods, a single camera-lens face in a smooth black visor, small floating prism-orb drones around its back, long protective arms, and a calm defensive stance always angled between Auralite and danger.
- Story use: The bodyguard is loyal but literal. It first tries to seal the Heartspring under containment protocols, then learns from Auralite's choice and becomes a bridge for the restored essence.

## Ethereal Essence Heartspring

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Auralite Prism page, which identifies iridescent slime as the source of magic and heart of Auralite's world's power system.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/ethereal-essence-heartspring-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Post-apocalyptic crater-garden surrounded by broken futuristic towers, luminous vines, cracked white ruins, pools and rivers of iridescent rainbow slime, floating translucent bubbles, prismatic flowers, and a central glowing spring that looks alive and cosmic.
- Story use: Auralite's home sanctuary and the living heart of the world's magic. It reacts to Auralite's emotional approach: destabilizing under command, healing when listened to.

## Ethereal Essence

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Auralite Prism page.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/ethereal-essence-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Iridescent cosmic slime that shifts through teal, pink, violet, blue, and gold; glossy liquid surface; tiny stars and prismatic particles suspended inside; can flow like water, bloom like a plant, or rise as magical aura threads.
- Story use: The source of magic and the heart of the world's power system. It is not an inert object; it responds to intuition, balance, imagination, and misuse.

## Imagined Caretakers

- Source: Auralite's power to materialize imaginary characters.
- Reference image: None separate; these are one-issue manifestations of Auralite's defining power.
- Stable visible identifiers: Soft luminous anime figures made from Auralite's aura and Ethereal Essence, with translucent rainbow bodies, gentle faces, gardener-like silhouettes, and glowing hands that repair instead of strike.
- Story use: They contrast with the unstable imagined warriors. Auralite wins by imagining caretakers who cooperate with the essence rather than soldiers who dominate it.
