# Page Script

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean readable caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Cherry Garry as the fierce Champion of Pie at the center of a dessert debate war.
- Layout: Single dramatic anime cover image.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Dream Anime Chronicles". Issue title text: "The Champion of Pie".
- Visual beats: Cherry Garry stands in the Pie-Cake Broadcast Arena with his glossy cherry head, black silhouetted body, sharp white teeth, clawlike hands, and cherry-red sticky laser energy. The arena is split behind him between glowing golden pie crusts and pastel cake towers. Floating cameras, debate lights, and dessert crowds frame him as the central threat.

## Page 2: Born From The Argument

- Story role: Establish Cherry's origin, birthplace, and all-consuming pie loyalty.
- Layout: Four panels: broadcast debate, energy surge, Cherry appearing, first declaration.
- Caption boxes: "The argument began as a television stunt: pie against cake, crust against frosting, devotion against decoration." "Every shout fed the studio lights until dessert loyalty became a storm." "From that storm stepped Cherry Garry, black as a silhouette and red as the fruit at a pie's heart." "He heard one purpose in the noise: pie must win."
- Visual beats: The Pie-Cake Broadcast Arena packed with rival dessert supporters; red-gold pie energy clashes with pastel cake energy; Cherry forms from cherry-red sparks over the debate stage; he grins with sharp white teeth under spotlights.

## Page 3: Lasers Do Not Persuade

- Story role: First failed tactic: Cherry tries to intimidate the arena into declaring pie superior.
- Layout: Five energetic panels with sticky red laser action.
- Caption boxes: "Cherry Garry did not debate softly." "He carved red lines through frosting banners." "He glued cake towers to the ceiling with sticky light." "The crowd shouted pie because they were afraid." "Afraid cheers tasted empty."
- Visual beats: Cherry fires cherry-red sticky lasers from both hands; lasers wrap cake banners and dessert props without killing anyone; audience members recoil; pie loyalists cheer nervously; Cherry's glow flickers despite the noise.

## Page 4: The Weak Point In Every Cake

- Story role: Second failed tactic: Cherry uses debate-sensing instincts to hunt cake arguments, but winning points does not create love.
- Layout: Four panels with argument-energy visualized as glowing threads.
- Caption boxes: "He could feel arguments before they finished forming." "A bad comparison glowed like a target." "A weak claim cracked before his teeth." "But crushing a cake defender's words did not make anyone love pie."
- Visual beats: Cherry stalks between debate desks as red targeting rings appear around trembling cake defenders; he slices through holographic argument diagrams with laser claws; a cake defender lowers their head; the audience falls silent instead of inspired.

## Page 5: Forced Love Fails

- Story role: Third failed tactic: Cherry demands pie chants and loses power because coerced love is false.
- Layout: Four panels, moving from spectacle to collapse.
- Caption boxes: "Cherry demanded the whole arena say pie was best." "The chant grew loud enough to shake the cameras." "For one blazing second, he became enormous." "Then the false love passed through him like air."
- Visual beats: Cherry towers over the arena in a red aura while crowds chant with fearful faces; his sticky lasers swell into huge cherry streams; the energy suddenly thins; Cherry drops to one knee as cake-side frosting towers and pie-side ovens destabilize.

## Page 6: The Reactor Takes A Side Against Everyone

- Story role: Crisis: the dessert reactor overloads, endangering both pie and cake supporters.
- Layout: Four panels ending in a central disaster splash.
- Caption boxes: "The studio had been built to turn dessert passion into spectacle." "Fear made the reactor hungry." "Crust, frosting, cherry light, and camera fire twisted into one collapsing storm." "If Cherry kept fighting the crowd, there would be no one left to love pie."
- Visual beats: The central divided pie-cake stage cracks open; red cherry energy tangles with pink frosting lightning and golden crust shards; floating cameras fall; pie loyalists and cake defenders slide toward a glowing dessert vortex; Cherry sees the danger reflected on his cherry head.

## Page 7: Sticky Light Can Save

- Story role: Decisive choice: Cherry uses his sticky red lasers protectively instead of coercively.
- Layout: Four panels ending with a rescue splash.
- Caption boxes: "Cherry Garry chose the argument he could not lose." "Pie meant warmth, shelter, a slice worth reaching for." "His lasers stopped striking people and started holding the arena together." "For the first time, the crowd cheered because they wanted him to win."
- Visual beats: Cherry grits his sharp teeth and fires adhesive red laser webs around collapsing beams; he catches falling debate desks and dessert stands; he lifts pie loyalists and cake defenders alike from the vortex; final splash shows Cherry anchoring the reactor with sticky red energy shaped like a shining cherry lattice.

## Page 8: A Slice Freely Chosen

- Story role: Resolution: Cherry earns genuine pie love without abandoning his fierce personality.
- Layout: Four panels, hopeful but still combative.
- Caption boxes: "When the storm closed, no command remained." "One cake defender picked up a slice of cherry pie with shaking hands." "They chose it because the Champion of Pie had saved them." "Cherry Garry smiled with every tooth and accepted the victory as real."
- Visual beats: The rescued crowd stands amid repaired glowing crust platforms; the minor cake defender reaches for a cherry pie slice; Cherry watches, still menacing but visibly steadier; red pie-love energy flares around him as the arena lights return.

## Page 9: Cherry Garry Character Poster

- Story role: Final character poster that summarizes the complete issue and reinforces Cherry Garry's defining traits.
- Layout: One full-page poster composition with Cherry in a strong full-body pose beside one large readable caption box.
- Caption box text: "Cherry Garry is the Champion of Pie, born when a television debate over pie and cake became powerful enough to create a fighter. He has a black silhouetted body, a cherry head, sharp white teeth, sticky red laser blasts, and instincts that sense weak points in any argument. His strength grows from real love of pie, not fear or forced applause. Cherry still believes pie is superior, but his first lesson is clear: the best victory is making someone choose the slice freely."
- Visual beats: Cherry Garry stands in a heroic villain-poster pose in the restored Pie-Cake Broadcast Arena, cherry head shining, sharp teeth grinning, black silhouette body crisp, sticky red laser ribbons coiling around his clawlike hands, golden pie light rising behind him, and distant cake defenders cautiously holding cherry pie slices. The large caption box occupies a clean readable side area without covering Cherry's silhouette.
