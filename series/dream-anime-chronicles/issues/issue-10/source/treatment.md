# Treatment

## Title

The Champion of Pie

## Issue Number

10

## Logline

Cherry Garry, the vicious cherry-headed Champion of Pie, tries to win the eternal pie-versus-cake argument with sticky red lasers, but only discovers his true strength when he protects the people whose free love of pie powers him.

## Central Conflict

Cherry Garry wants the Pie-Cake Broadcast Arena to declare pie superior forever. The arena was the place that born him out of a televised dessert argument, so every cake cheer feels like an insult to his existence. He first attacks cake displays, then hunts debate points with his argument-sensing instincts, then drains the crowd's forced pie chants into bigger cherry-red laser blasts. Each tactic fails because fear is not love, and his power weakens when the audience stops choosing pie sincerely. When the arena's dessert reactor overloads and threatens to bury both sides under frosting, crust, and cherry energy, Cherry must decide whether to keep punishing cake defenders or save the crowd so pie can be loved freely.

## Theme

Devotion becomes stronger when it inspires choice instead of demanding obedience.

## Ending

Cherry Garry fires his strongest sticky red laser not at cake fans, but around them, sealing the collapsing pie-crust reactor and carrying civilians to safety on cherry-gloss energy. After the rescue, one frightened cake defender chooses a slice of pie because Cherry proved its greatness without forcing the answer. Cherry is still sharp-toothed, argumentative, and certain pie is superior, but now he understands that his strength rises from genuine love of pie, not coerced slogans.

## Emotional Arc

Cherry begins as a vicious debate-born zealot who thinks any cake praise is a personal attack. His failures make him more dangerous until his forced victory almost empties the very love that powers him. By the end, he remains proudly intense and combative, but his defining identity becomes clearer: the Champion of Pie wins by making pie irresistible enough to choose.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, origin in the broadcast debate, first laser intimidation, failed argument-sensing ambush, forced pie chant and power collapse, dessert-reactor crisis, protective use of sticky red lasers, a freely chosen slice, and a final Cherry Garry poster-summary page.
