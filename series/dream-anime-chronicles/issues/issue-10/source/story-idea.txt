Core lore to preserve: Cherry Garry is the Champion of Pie, born from a television studio debate over whether pie is better than cake. He has a black silhouetted body, a cherry head, sharp white teeth, intense argument energy, cherry-red sticky laser blasts, debate-sensing instincts, and strength tied to love of pie. He despises cake arguments and behaves viciously when he thinks pie is being insulted.

Issue direction: Cherry Garry tries to make the Pie-Cake Broadcast Arena crown pie by force. His lasers, intimidation, and debate-sensing powers all fail because coerced applause does not count as love of pie. When the arena's unstable dessert reactor endangers both pie loyalists and cake defenders, Cherry chooses to protect the pie by protecting the people who might love it freely. He remains sharp, petty, and proudly pro-pie, but the issue defines his real strength as proving pie's worth instead of merely punishing cake.
