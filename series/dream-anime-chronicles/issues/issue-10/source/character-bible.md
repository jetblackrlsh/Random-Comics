# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Cherry Garry

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Cherry Garry page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Cherry_Garry`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/cherry-garry-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Black silhouetted humanoid anime body with glossy highlights, red cherry for a head, short brown cherry stem on top, wide sharp white fanged grin, no ordinary human face, clawlike black hands, sleek shadow-black torso and limbs, cherry-red sticky laser energy around the hands, aggressive debate posture, and pie-red glow that intensifies when people sincerely love pie.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not give Cherry Garry normal human skin, hair, ordinary eyes, a normal human head, a visible nose, a cake-themed costume, a helmet, a robot body, or realistic flesh. Keep the cherry head, black silhouette body, sharp white teeth, and red sticky laser energy consistent on every page.
- Powers to show: Cherry-red sticky laser blasts, adhesive energy strands, debate-sensing instincts that let him locate weak points in an argument, bursts of strength when people truly love pie, and visible weakening when pie praise is forced by fear instead of chosen.
- Personality and motivation: Vicious, petty, argument-hungry, intensely loyal to pie, easily enraged by cake praise, and convinced that being the Champion of Pie gives his existence meaning. He does not become gentle, but he learns that proving pie's superiority works better than terrorizing disagreement.
- Emotional continuity: Starts furious and coercive, becomes desperate when forced loyalty weakens him, then steadies when he protects the crowd and earns one sincere pie choice.

## Pie-Cake Broadcast Arena

- Source: Created from the Cherry Garry wiki backstory that he was born from a television studio debate over whether pie is better than cake.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/pie-cake-broadcast-arena-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Gigantic neon anime television studio split between pie loyalist red-gold crust motifs and cake defender pastel frosting motifs, floating cameras, debate desks, dessert crowd stands, glowing ovens, tiered cake podiums, lattice pie counters, and a central dessert reactor shaped like a glowing divided pie-cake stage.
- Story use: Cherry Garry's birthplace and emotional battlefield. The arena amplifies dessert arguments into real energy, making false applause unstable and sincere love of pie powerful.

## Cake Defender

- Source: New minor named role kept generic so Cherry remains the issue focus.
- Reference image: None for this issue.
- Stable visible identifiers: Young anime dessert-debate contestant wearing pastel frosting-colored jacket, anxious expression, cake-slice badge, and no recurring face detail.
- Story use: A representative cake supporter who fears Cherry at first, then freely chooses a slice of pie after Cherry saves the arena.
