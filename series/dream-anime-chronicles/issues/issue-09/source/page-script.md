# Page Script

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean readable caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Cheun Yi as a heroic, curious shoulder bearer whose purple tornado power is tied to a mysterious book and a recruitment mission.
- Layout: Single dramatic anime cover image.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Dream Anime Chronicles". Issue title text: "The Tornado That Asked Why".
- Visual beats: Cheun Yi spins in the foreground with black bowl-cut hair, shirtless lean torso, brown pants, black belt with rectangular silver buckle, stone shoulder pads and wrist guards glowing through purple cracks. A spiral of bright purple tornado wind wraps around him. The ghoulish gray book floats open behind his shoulder with its living face half-smiling. The Bat-Top Spirit circles above the armor like a tiny winged spinning top, and an unnamed hooded shoulder bearer waits at a realm-crossroads portal in the background.

## Page 2: A Life Waiting To Turn

- Story role: Establish Cheun's ordinary restlessness, personality, and hunger for a larger purpose.
- Layout: Four panels: ordinary village street, Cheun talking, strange wind, book arrival.
- Caption boxes: "Before the armor, Cheun Yi chased every rumor past the edge of the map." "He talked to strangers like every answer was hiding in a new friend." "Then the wind began circling only him." "A gray book with a dead face opened on the road and called his name without using a mouth."
- Visual beats: Bright anime market road at sunset; Cheun animatedly questions travelers with charismatic energy; violet dust begins orbiting his shoulders; the ghoulish gray book lands open in the street, pages fluttering against the wind.

## Page 3: The Shoulders Chose Him

- Story role: Show the book bonding Cheun to the stone shoulder armor and the Bat-Top Spirit.
- Layout: Three large panels, mystical transformation sequence.
- Caption boxes: "The book did not explain destiny." "It fastened destiny to his shoulders." "From the stone came a spirit like a bat taught to spin forever."
- Visual beats: Stone shoulder pads and wrist guards assemble around Cheun with purple light; Cheun stares in thrilled shock as the Bat-Top Spirit emerges from the cracks; final panel shows Cheun lifted in a violet spiral, hair and pants whipping in the wind, grinning despite fear.

## Page 4: The First Storm Was Too Loud

- Story role: First failed tactic: Cheun treats power as exhilaration and loses control.
- Layout: Five kinetic panels with escalating tornado motion.
- Caption boxes: "Cheun spun because the spirit spun." "His wrist became a funnel." "His shoulder became a storm." "The faster he turned, the less he could hear himself think." "Power answered him before wisdom did."
- Visual beats: Cheun rotates one wrist and makes a small purple vortex; he spins his arm and lifts stones; he spins his whole body and creates a growing cyclone; the cyclone tears market banners and pulls debris upward; Cheun lands dizzy as the ghoulish book watches silently.

## Page 5: Questions With Teeth

- Story role: Second failed tactic: Cheun demands answers from the book and gets only cryptic danger.
- Layout: Four panels, eerie and confrontational.
- Caption boxes: "Cheun asked why he had been chosen." "The book showed him other shoulders under other skies." "He asked what would happen when they gathered." "The book answered by letting its margins crawl into monsters."
- Visual beats: Cheun confronts the floating gray book at night; pages flash with silhouettes of distant shoulder bearers; Cheun grabs at the book's mist while purple wind flares; gray-page wraiths peel out of the book margins with torn paper bodies and violet mouths.

## Page 6: The Recruit Who Ran

- Story role: Third failed tactic: Cheun tries to recruit by talking and showing off, frightening the first recruit instead.
- Layout: Four panels at the Shoulder-Bearer Crossroads.
- Caption boxes: "The book pointed him toward another chosen soul." "Cheun arrived with too many words and too much wind." "The recruit saw a stranger, a monster-book, and a storm wearing a smile." "Cheun finally understood that destiny could sound like a threat."
- Visual beats: Cheun steps onto floating stone paths at the violet crossroads; a hooded young shoulder bearer with one glowing stone shoulder pad backs away; Cheun gestures excitedly while purple tornado ribbons swirl too close; the recruit falls near the edge of a broken path as page-wraiths close in.

## Page 7: Tornadoes Can Carry

- Story role: Decisive choice: Cheun stops forcing answers and uses precise wind to protect the recruit.
- Layout: Four panels ending with a protective action splash.
- Caption boxes: "Cheun stopped asking the book for the ending." "He asked the wind for one safe step." "A tornado did not have to destroy." "In Cheun Yi's hands, it could carry."
- Visual beats: Cheun plants his feet and spins only one wrist guard with focused calm; a small purple vortex lifts the falling recruit back onto the path; Cheun spins his shoulders in opposite directions to make a shield-ring; final splash shows controlled purple tornadoes sweeping gray-page wraiths away while Cheun reaches a steady hand toward the recruit.

## Page 8: The Mission Without A Map

- Story role: Resolution: Cheun accepts the recruitment mission without full explanation.
- Layout: Four panels, hopeful and mysterious.
- Caption boxes: "The recruit did not need every answer before taking Cheun's hand." "Neither did Cheun." "The book kept its final purpose locked behind its crooked smile." "Cheun Yi walked on anyway, because the next shoulder bearer might already be calling for wind."
- Visual beats: The hooded recruit accepts Cheun's hand but remains mostly unrevealed; the Bat-Top Spirit hovers proudly over the glowing shoulder armor; the ghoulish gray book closes with one purple eye watching; final panel shows Cheun striding into a portal road of purple wind, determined and smiling, with distant silhouettes of future shoulder bearers ahead.

## Page 9: Cheun Yi Character Poster

- Story role: Final character poster that summarizes the complete issue and reinforces Cheun Yi's defining traits.
- Layout: One full-page poster composition with Cheun in a strong full-body pose beside one large readable caption box.
- Caption box text: "Cheun Yi is a charismatic shoulder bearer chosen by a ghoulish gray book. His stone shoulder pads and wrist guards connect him to a bat-and-spinning-top spirit from another realm. By spinning his body or even one arm, Cheun can create purple tornadoes for combat, rescue, travel, and protection. He wants to know why the book is gathering shoulder bearers, but his first lesson is not an answer. It is a choice: protect the people the mission sends him to find, even while the final purpose stays hidden."
- Visual beats: Cheun stands in a heroic anime poster pose at the Shoulder-Bearer Crossroads, black bowl cut crisp, stone shoulder armor glowing violet, brown pants and black belt visible, one hand controlling a tight purple tornado. The Bat-Top Spirit spirals near his shoulder, the ghoulish gray book floats open behind him, and portal silhouettes of other shoulder bearers glow in the background. The large caption box occupies a clean readable side area without covering Cheun's silhouette.
