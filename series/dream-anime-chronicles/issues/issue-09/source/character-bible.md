# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Cheun Yi

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Cheun Yi page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Cheun_Yi`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/cheun-yi-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Young adult Asian anime man with straight black bowl-cut hair, expressive dark eyes, lean toned build, bare chest, brown pants, black belt with a rectangular silver buckle, stone shoulder pads cracked with purple glow, stone cylindrical wrist guards cracked with purple glow, and a bright violet wind aura that coils around his spinning arms and body.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not make Cheun armored head-to-toe, elderly, blond, blue-eyed, bulky, robotic, reptilian, winged, or photorealistic. Keep the black bowl cut, shirtless torso, brown pants, black belt with silver rectangular buckle, stone shoulder armor, stone wrist guards, and purple crack glow consistent on every page.
- Powers to show: Summoning and controlling purple tornadoes by spinning his whole body, one arm, one wrist guard, or one shoulder. Small vortexes can lift debris, redirect attacks, carry people, or form shields; uncontrolled spins can become dangerous cyclones.
- Personality and motivation: Determined, charismatic, adventure-seeking, heroic, curious, inquisitive, and talkative. Cheun wants to uncover the truth behind the book and recruit other shoulder bearers even though the purpose remains hidden.
- Emotional continuity: Starts exhilarated and impatient for answers, fails when he treats power as spectacle or proof, then becomes steadier when he chooses protection before explanation.

## Bat-Top Spirit

- Source: Cheun Yi lore describing the spirit bonded to his stone shoulder armor.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/bat-top-spirit-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Small supernatural anime spirit shaped like a fusion of a black bat and a spinning top, triangular bat ears, purple glowing eyes, tiny folded wings, tapered spinning-point lower body, circular violet motion rings, and a mischievous but watchful expression.
- Story use: The spirit is the otherworldly presence inside Cheun's armor. It never explains the whole mission, but its movement teaches Cheun that stronger wind comes from centered control rather than frantic speed.

## The First Recruit

- Source: Cheun Yi wiki backstory says Cheun recruits a first ally; this issue keeps the recruit unnamed so Cheun remains the focus.
- Reference image: None for this issue.
- Stable visible identifiers: Hooded young shoulder bearer silhouette with one cracked stone shoulder pad glowing pale violet, frightened posture, and no clear face reveal.
- Story use: The recruit tests whether Cheun is chasing answers for himself or willing to protect another chosen person before he knows the mission.

## Shoulder-Bearer Crossroads

- Source: Created for this issue as the first visible recruitment threshold in Cheun's story.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/shoulder-bearer-crossroads-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Dreamlike anime crossroads suspended between realms, floating stone paths, purple wind spirals, torn pages orbiting like birds, distant portals shaped like shoulder armor, and a sky split between twilight blue and glowing violet storm clouds.
- Story use: The place where the ghoulish gray book points Cheun toward another shoulder bearer and tests whether he can protect a recruit without understanding the full plan.

## Ghoulish Gray Book

- Source: Cheun Yi wiki lore.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/ghoulish-gray-book-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Heavy gray spellbook with a grotesque living face embossed into the cover, small uneven teeth around the cover seam, one cloudy purple eye, cracked stone-like binding, torn page edges, violet mist, and black-purple bookmark tendrils.
- Story use: The book chooses Cheun, bonds him to the armor, gives cryptic directions, and refuses to reveal the final purpose of gathering shoulder bearers.

## Cheun's Stone Shoulder Armor

- Source: Cheun Yi wiki lore.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/cheuns-stone-shoulder-armor-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Pair of rough stone shoulder pads and cylindrical stone wrist guards sized for Cheun, all cracked with inner purple light, weathered gray surfaces, glowing violet seams, and faint bat-wing and spinning-ring motifs.
- Story use: The armor connects Cheun to the Bat-Top Spirit and channels his purple tornado power. It glows brighter when he spins with purpose instead of panic.
