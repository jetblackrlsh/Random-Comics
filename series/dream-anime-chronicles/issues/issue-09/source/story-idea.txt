Create the next issue of Dream Anime Chronicles as a Cheun Yi character introduction issue.

Continuity source: https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Cheun_Yi

Cheun Yi is a charismatic, curious, adventure-seeking shoulder bearer chosen by a ghoulish gray book. The book bonds him with mystical stone shoulder armor connected to a bat-and-spinning-top spirit from another realm. Cheun can summon and control powerful purple tornadoes by spinning his whole body or individual limbs. He is driven to recruit other shoulder bearers even though the book refuses to tell him the final purpose of the mission.
