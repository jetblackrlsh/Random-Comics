# Treatment

## Title

The Tornado That Asked Why

## Issue Number

9

## Logline

Cheun Yi, a curious new shoulder bearer, tries to force answers from the ghoulish gray book that chose him, but only becomes worthy of recruiting others when he uses his purple tornado power to protect a stranger before he understands the mission.

## Central Conflict

Cheun wants the book to explain why he was chosen and why other shoulder bearers must be gathered. The book answers only with grotesque riddles and danger. Cheun first treats his new armor like proof that destiny owes him clarity, then treats his tornado power like spectacle, then tries to recruit another bearer by talking faster than fear can listen. Each attempt fails because he is demanding certainty before responsibility. When gray-page wraiths tear through the crossroads and threaten the first recruit, Cheun must decide whether to keep chasing the book's secret or become the kind of hero who protects people while the larger truth is still hidden.

## Theme

Purpose is not proven by knowing the whole mission. It begins when curiosity becomes protection.

## Ending

Cheun stops spinning to impress, interrogate, or escape confusion. He spins with precision, making small purple tornadoes that shield the first recruit, lift falling stone, and drive the gray-page wraiths back into the book's margins. The book still refuses to explain the final purpose, but Cheun accepts the road anyway. He leaves the crossroads determined to find the other shoulder bearers, not because the book has satisfied him, but because the people it points toward will need someone willing to arrive.

## Emotional Arc

Cheun begins talkative, thrilled, and frustrated that a magical life has started without instructions. His first failures turn curiosity into impatience and power into a storm that endangers the very person he wants to recruit. By the end, Cheun remains adventurous and inquisitive, but his defining identity is clearer: a charismatic seeker who can carry questions without letting them stop him from protecting others.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, ordinary life interrupted, the book and armor bond, first uncontrolled tornado, failed demand for answers, failed recruitment, protective precision, the mission accepted, and a final Cheun Yi poster-summary page.
