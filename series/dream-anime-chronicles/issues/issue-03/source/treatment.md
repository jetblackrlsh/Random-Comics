# Treatment

## Title

Feathers for the Crowd

## Issue Number

3

## Logline

Black Feather, the winged champion of Celebrity Deathmatch Island, nearly loses their crown when fame makes them fight for the cameras instead of the hunt.

## Central Conflict

Black Feather wants the island audience to adore and fear them as the show's unmatched predator. The arena's producers exploit that hunger by flooding the haunted boarding school with spotlights, drones, and a masked challenger who waits for Black Feather to overperform. Every dramatic pose gives the challenger a target, forcing Black Feather to choose between feeding the crowd and surviving the match.

## Theme

Real dominance is not needing every eye in the room. It is knowing when to vanish, listen, and strike with purpose.

## Ending

Black Feather wins by killing the spectacle: they cut the arena lights, fold their wings into the shadows, and use enhanced senses instead of showmanship to outmaneuver the masked challenger. When the crowd goes silent, Black Feather claims the silence as a stronger tribute than applause.

## Emotional Arc

Black Feather begins as a ferocious and charming champion who treats the arena as a stage. Their confidence curdles into arrogance when the crowd cheers every reckless move. The challenger punishes that vanity twice, injuring Black Feather's wing and turning the cameras against them. At the low point, Black Feather realizes they have become trained by applause. They end still proud and dangerous, but sharper: a predator who can command attention by withholding it.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, champion introduction, island-show setup, first showy failure, second aerial failure, low point under the cameras, silent hunting strategy, victory and new rule, and a final Black Feather character poster summarizing the full issue.
