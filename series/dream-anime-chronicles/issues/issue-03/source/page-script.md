# Page Script

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Black Feather as the reigning champion whose wings turn the deathmatch spotlight into a throne.
- Layout: Single dramatic anime cover image.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Dream Anime Chronicles". Issue title text: "Feathers for the Crowd".
- Visual beats: Black Feather crouches on the roof edge of the ruined haunted boarding school arena, huge black wings spread across a red-black sky, clawed hands open, brown slit-pupil eyes glowing under spiked black hair. Camera drones circle like insects. Far below, arena floodlights and jungle fences form a violent stage.

## Page 2: The Champion Everyone Watches

- Story role: Explain Black Feather's identity, powers, personality, and public role.
- Layout: Four panels: broadcast entrance, predatory close-up, aerial dominance, crowd reaction.
- Caption boxes: "Black Feather was the island's impossible champion." "Wings gave them the sky." "Claws gave them the close fight." "The crowd gave them the one thing they hunted harder than victory: attention."
- Visual beats: Black Feather steps through a red-lit school gate as camera drones flare; close-up of pale face, slit brown eyes, sharp black hair, and long dark claws; Black Feather dives between ruined dorm towers with huge black wings; screens show cheering silhouettes while Black Feather smiles like a predator hearing applause.

## Page 3: The Island That Sold Fear

- Story role: Establish Celebrity Deathmatch Island and the producer trap.
- Layout: Three large panels with arena scale.
- Caption boxes: "Celebrity Deathmatch turned a haunted boarding school into a battlefield." "Every corridor watched. Every tree line listened." "Tonight, the producers offered Black Feather a challenger built to punish vanity."
- Visual beats: Wide shot of ruined school buildings swallowed by jungle, red broadcast lights, fences, and giant arena screens; camera drones sweep through cracked hallways and classrooms; the masked challenger waits in shadow with a hooked cable weapon while Black Feather poses beneath a spotlight.

## Page 4: Applause Has A Shape

- Story role: First failed attempt: Black Feather performs instead of hunting.
- Layout: Five action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Black Feather struck first for the cameras." "They let the wings fill the whole screen." "They smiled before the hit landed." "The challenger aimed at the pose." "Applause became a target."
- Visual beats: Black Feather launches in a theatrical wing-spread dive; red lights make the feathers look like a crown; the challenger fires a hooked cable at the exposed wing line; Black Feather twists too late; final panel shows feathers scattering as Black Feather crashes through a classroom wall.

## Page 5: The Sky Learns A Cage

- Story role: Second failed attempt: speed and flight fail because Black Feather still chases spectacle.
- Layout: Five panels emphasizing vertical motion.
- Caption boxes: "So Black Feather chose speed." "They turned the school towers into a black-winged blur." "Every camera followed." "Every camera betrayed the route." "The sky felt smaller when the crowd knew where to look."
- Visual beats: Black Feather rockets upward from broken desks and dust; they slice through floodlight beams with agile turns; drone screens map their flight path in red; the challenger yanks cable anchors between towers; Black Feather slams into the net of cables, one wing bent and trembling.

## Page 6: Trained By The Cheer

- Story role: Low point: Black Feather recognizes the weakness beneath the fame hunger.
- Layout: Four panels, emotional focus.
- Caption boxes: "Black Feather heard the island chanting." "For the first time, it sounded like a leash." "They had not been hunting the crowd." "The crowd had been hunting them."
- Visual beats: Black Feather kneels in a dark hallway, one wing dragging, claws digging into cracked tile; screens in broken classroom windows replay their failures; close-up of their angry eyes reflecting camera lenses; Black Feather folds both wings inward until their silhouette nearly disappears in the hallway dark.

## Page 7: Predator Without A Spotlight

- Story role: Decisive strategy: Black Feather uses silence, senses, and darkness.
- Layout: Five panels moving from disappearance to control.
- Caption boxes: "Black Feather stopped performing." "They cut the lights." "Their eyes found motion." "Their ears found breath." "The match finally belonged to the predator."
- Visual beats: Black Feather claws through a power conduit, plunging the corridor into black-red emergency light; camera drones lose focus and collide; close-up of Black Feather's slit pupils catching tiny movement; feathers drift silently past the masked challenger; Black Feather appears behind the challenger with claws poised but controlled.

## Page 8: The Silence Roars

- Story role: Resolve the fight and redefine Black Feather's fame.
- Layout: Four panels ending with a dominant image.
- Caption boxes: "The challenger swung at empty air." "Black Feather struck from the quiet." "The crowd forgot to cheer." "Black Feather decided silence was also worship."
- Visual beats: The challenger whips the cable through darkness and misses; Black Feather dives from above, wings tight, claws cutting the cable weapon apart; the challenger falls disarmed under shattered red lights; final splash panel shows Black Feather standing over the broken arena screen, wings half-open, calm and terrifying as the crowd is stunned silent.

## Page 9: Black Feather Character Poster

- Story role: Final character poster that summarizes the complete issue and reinforces Black Feather's defining traits.
- Layout: One full-page poster composition with Black Feather in a dynamic full-body pose beside one large readable caption box.
- Caption box text: "Black Feather is a pale human-bird hybrid with black wings, clawed hands, sharp senses, and a hunger for fame. On Celebrity Deathmatch Island, they are the reigning champion because flight, agility, claws, and charisma make them terrifying to watch. But the crowd becomes a weakness when Black Feather performs for applause instead of hunting to survive. After a masked challenger uses cameras and spotlights against them, Black Feather cuts the lights, trusts their senses, and wins from the darkness. Their true power is not just being seen. It is choosing when the world is allowed to see them."
- Visual beats: Black Feather stands in a strong modern anime poster pose on the broken rooftop of the haunted boarding school arena, black wings sweeping behind them, pale face and brown slit-pupil eyes lit by red moonlight, plain black shirt and pants, long clawed fingers relaxed. Drones lie broken around their feet. The large caption box occupies a clean readable side area without covering the silhouette.
