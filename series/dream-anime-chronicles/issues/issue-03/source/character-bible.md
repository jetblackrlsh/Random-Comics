# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Black Feather

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Black Feather page and the user-provided reference image.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/black-feather-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Pale young anime human-bird hybrid with very short spiked black hair, sharp face, brown eyes with slit pupils, intense predatory stare, plain black T-shirt with no graphic, black pants, black sneakers, huge black feathered wings, long dark fingernails, clawed hands, lean agile build, ferocious posture that can become charming and theatrical when the cameras are watching.
- Powers to show: Flight, sharp aerial turns, enhanced eyesight and hearing, claw strikes, bird-like reflexes, and predatory stillness when hiding in darkness.
- Emotional continuity: Starts hungry for applause and camera focus, becomes angry when the show uses that vanity against them, then chooses silence and controlled fear as a stronger kind of fame.

## Masked Challenger

- Source: Issue-specific obstacle.
- Reference image: None; keep the challenger visually secondary and mostly obscured.
- Stable visible identifiers: Anonymous athletic combatant in dark gray arena gear, featureless black mask, red tracking visor, hooked cable weapon, and camera-targeting harness. Never make the challenger more visually iconic than Black Feather.
- Story use: The challenger punishes Black Feather's vanity by attacking when Black Feather poses for the crowd. They are defeated by Black Feather's silent hunting strategy and do not need series continuity beyond this issue.

## Deathmatch Island Haunted Boarding School Arena

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Black Feather page.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/deathmatch-island-school-arena-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Secluded island arena built from a ruined haunted boarding school, broken dormitory windows, cracked school corridors, jungle pressing through fences, red broadcast lights, hovering camera drones, floodlights, survival-show screens, and black-red danger lighting.
- Story use: The island turns survival into entertainment. It tempts Black Feather to perform for the crowd instead of listening to predator instincts.
