Create Dream Anime Chronicles Issue #3 as a character introduction issue for Black Feather.

Use the user-provided Black Feather reference image and the Dream Anime Wiki page:
https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Feather

Core lore to preserve: Black Feather is a pale human-bird hybrid with spiked very short black hair, brown eyes with slit pupils, a plain black T-shirt, black pants, black sneakers, large black feathered wings, long fingernails, and clawed hands. Black Feather is ferocious, confident, charming, attention-seeking, fame-driven, survival-driven, and convinced of their superiority. They are the star contestant and reigning champion on "Celebrity Deathmatch," a violent reality show on a secluded island with a haunted boarding school and wilderness battlegrounds. Their powers include flight, enhanced senses, sharp claws, and extreme agility.

Issue goal: Introduce Black Feather as a dangerous showman whose hunger for adoration almost makes them predictable. The story should show Black Feather failing when they perform for applause, then winning when they use their avian senses, speed, and control of silence to own the battlefield on their own terms.
