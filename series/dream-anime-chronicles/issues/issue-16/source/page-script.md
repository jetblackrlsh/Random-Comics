# Page Script

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean readable caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay. Caption boxes must contain only reader-facing story text, never panel labels or prefixes.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Bato Leo as a living battery trapped between laziness, fear of animals, and a hotel crisis only they can solve.
- Layout: Single dramatic anime cover image.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Dream Anime Chronicles". Issue title text: "The Show That Needed A Battery".
- Visual beats: Bato Leo stands in the interdimensional hotel event ring with metallic silver skin, electric-blue spiked hair, glowing neon-blue eyes, sky-blue chest lightning insignia, dark navy cropped staff jacket, headset, utility belt, and blue-black lower outfit. One hand emits a controlled blue lightning arc. Behind Bato, portal doors flicker, hotel lights dim, and a crystal-maned lion plus small glowing creatures recoil nervously. Bato looks lazy at first glance but determined under pressure.

## Page 2: The Easy Shift

- Story role: Establish Bato, the hotel, the job, the desire for respect, and the lazy avoidance pattern.
- Layout: Four large panels with roomy caption boxes.
- Caption boxes: "Bato Leo worked events at a hotel built between worlds, where every door opened to a different impossible guest." "Bato was a living battery with silver skin, electric-blue hair, neon eyes, and enough stored charge to light the whole lobby." "They wanted respect, not another shift where everyone pointed at them and said, power that." "So Bato kept the headset on, the schedule hidden, and the nearest nap couch memorized."
- Visual beats: Wide shot of interdimensional hotel lobby; Bato slouches near concierge desk with chest lightning glowing; staff gesture toward flickering hotel conduits while Bato looks annoyed; Bato eyes a lounge couch while portal guests pass by.

## Page 3: The Assignment Bato Avoided

- Story role: Reveal the animal-show problem and Bato's fear.
- Layout: Four panels, clear emotional progression.
- Caption boxes: "Then the manager assigned Bato to the Celestial Creature Showcase." "That was the one event Bato always avoided." "Supernatural animals disliked the sharp buzz of Bato's aura, and Bato disliked every claw, antler, fang, and glowing tail in return." "Bato promised to coordinate from a safe distance, which meant from as far away as possible."
- Visual beats: Hotel manager points Bato toward animal-show arena doors; Bato freezes as a crystal-maned lion and comet-deer enter; close-up of Bato's nervous blue eyes and crackling fingers; Bato hides behind an event console with forced casual expression.

## Page 4: The Show Starts Wrong

- Story role: Show Bato's first shortcut failing because the animals sense careless electricity.
- Layout: Five panels with one larger center panel of animal panic.
- Caption boxes: "Bato tried to run the show with remote switches and tiny lightning taps." "The lights obeyed. The portals opened. The schedule looked easy." "But the animals heard the careless current before any guest saw the first trick." "The moon-rabbits bolted, the star serpents twisted through the rafters, and the crystal lion lowered its mane like a warning." "Bato blamed the animals, because blaming them was easier than admitting fear."
- Visual beats: Bato taps console with lazy lightning; show lights flare; small glowing animals startle; crystal lion bristles and star serpents whip overhead; guests gasp while Bato looks defensive.

## Page 5: The Hotel Goes Dark

- Story role: Inciting crisis: portal surge cuts power and raises stakes beyond Bato's comfort.
- Layout: Four dramatic panels.
- Caption boxes: "Then three worlds tried to enter the hotel through the same portal." "The event grid overloaded, the lobby went dark, and every door between worlds began to lock half-open." "Guests were trapped in the wrong thresholds." "The animal show became a stampede because the safest path disappeared."
- Visual beats: Three colored portals collide above arena; blue-white conduits snap dark; guests half-silhouetted in flickering portal doorways; creature herd panics toward unstable light while Bato stares at the blackout.

## Page 6: The Wrong Kind Of Power

- Story role: Bato's first rescue attempt fails because panic lightning scares the animals and worsens the surge.
- Layout: Four action panels with one large failed lightning panel.
- Caption boxes: "Bato finally stopped hiding and did the obvious living-battery thing." "They poured lightning into the dead grid all at once." "The hotel lights screamed back to life for one second." "The animals panicked harder, the portals buckled, and Bato learned that raw power was not coordination."
- Visual beats: Bato leaps onto event platform and channels huge lightning; hotel lights flare too bright; animals recoil violently and portal rims warp; Bato is thrown back by feedback, frightened and embarrassed.

## Page 7: The Fear On Both Sides

- Story role: Low point and insight: Bato understands the animals are afraid of uncontrolled electricity, just as Bato is afraid of them.
- Layout: Four calmer panels with close-ups.
- Caption boxes: "In the emergency silence, Bato saw the crystal lion shaking instead of attacking." "The creatures were not ruining the show." "They were terrified of a battery that only knew how to spark, snap, and run." "Bato was afraid of them too, which meant fear was the first thing they had in common."
- Visual beats: Dim arena; crystal lion crouches protectively over smaller creatures; Bato notices their own hand crackling too sharply; close-up of Bato softening expression; Bato lowers their hand and lets the lightning shrink into a gentle pulse.

## Page 8: The Coordinator

- Story role: Resolution action: Bato uses controlled current and real event coordination to rescue guests and creatures.
- Layout: Five panels with a final wide rescue panel.
- Caption boxes: "Bato stopped blasting and started counting heartbeats, paw steps, portal pulses, and hotel load." "They fed the grid one gentle rhythm at a time." "Blue paths opened across the floor, bright enough to guide the creatures but soft enough not to scare them." "Bato sent guests home by world, creatures home by scent, and power only where it was needed." "For once, everyone followed Bato because Bato was actually leading."
- Visual beats: Bato kneels and sends soft blue pulses through floor conduits; creatures cautiously follow glowing path lines; portal doors stabilize one by one; guests step safely through portals; final wide panel shows Bato calmly directing hotel staff, animals, and portal power with organized electric arcs.

## Page 9: Bato Leo Character Poster

- Story role: Final character poster that summarizes Bato's defining traits and future role.
- Layout: One full-page poster composition with Bato in a strong relaxed full-body pose beside one large readable caption box.
- Caption box text: "Bato Leo is a lazy but capable living battery with metallic silver skin, electric-blue spiked hair, neon-blue eyes, and a sky-blue lightning mark on the chest. Bato works as an event coordinator in an interdimensional hotel and wants respect instead of being treated like a tool. They fear animals, and supernatural animals fear Bato's uncontrolled current. Their real strength is learning to turn electricity into careful coordination, steady rescue, and proof that easygoing does not have to mean useless."
- Visual beats: Bato stands in the restored interdimensional hotel lobby, hotel-staff jacket open over glowing chest insignia, headset and utility belt visible, one hand holding a small calm lightning arc. Behind Bato, portal doors shine steadily and the crystal-maned lion watches from a respectful distance. The large caption box occupies a clean side area without covering Bato's silhouette.
