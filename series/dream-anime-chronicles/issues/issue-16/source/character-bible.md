## Series Style

- Modern colorful anime aesthetic, high saturation, sharp detail, luminous electric and portal effects, dynamic camera angles, clean readable comic-page composition.
- Bato's pages should feel like bright interdimensional hotel chaos: polished silver floors, portal doors, blue-white conduit light, glowing creature-show rings, startled guests, and controlled electricity becoming more orderly as Bato matures.
- Story clarity is the priority. Captions should explain Bato's job, fear of animals, desire for respect, failed shortcuts, the hotel power crisis, and the final choice to coordinate carefully.

## Bato Leo

- Role: protagonist, living battery, lazy event coordinator at an interdimensional hotel.
- Wiki continuity source: `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Bato_Leo`
- Series description: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/bato-leo-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: Slim androgynous anime humanoid with glossy metallic silver skin, elongated limbs, medium-length electric-blue hair styled in jagged energy-like spikes, glowing neon-blue eyes, a neon sky-blue lightning insignia centered on the chest, a sleeveless dark navy cropped hotel-staff jacket, fitted blue-black shorts or leggings, silver utility belt, headset earpiece, and blue-white electric seams that glow when charging or discharging.
- Expression and posture: Relaxed slouch, hands-in-pocket laziness, easygoing half-lidded stare, sudden nervous tension around animals, then focused controlled stance when coordinating the rescue.
- Powers and skills: Stores electricity like a living battery, discharges lightning, powers hotel systems, senses electrical load, and can shape current into gentle pulses when calm.
- Personality and motivation: Lazy and casual, but wants acceptance and respect. Bato fears animals, dislikes animal events, and feels insulted when others treat them only as a tool.
- Defining lesson: Bato earns respect by using power with control and by coordinating frightened creatures instead of hiding from them.

## Interdimensional Hotel

- Role: main setting, workplace, and crisis environment.
- Series description: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/setting-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/interdimensional-hotel-setting.png`
- Visual identifiers: Vast polished anime hotel between worlds, silver floors, glowing blue-white electrical conduit lines, impossible windows full of stars, floating portal doorways with colored rims, central concierge desk, event banners with abstract non-readable symbols, and an attached animal-show arena.
- Story function: The hotel depends on stable portal power. During the Celestial Creature Showcase, a portal surge cuts electricity and threatens to trap guests and creatures between worlds unless Bato becomes a controlled backup battery.

## Celestial Creature Showcase Animals

- Role: frightened event animals and living obstacle.
- Visual identifiers: A small group of bright supernatural anime animals, including a crystal-maned lion, a tiny antlered comet-deer, floating eel-like star serpents, and round moon-rabbits with glowing ears. They are visually distinct but do not need individual names.
- Continuity notes: These creatures dislike Bato's uncontrolled electric aura and panic when Bato uses sharp lightning. They calm only when Bato uses soft rhythmic current, open body language, and safe portal paths.
