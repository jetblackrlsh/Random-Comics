Create the next issue of Dream Anime Chronicles as a character introduction issue for Bato Leo.

Continuity source: https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Bato_Leo

Bato Leo is a living battery with metallic silver skin, electric-blue spiked hair, neon-blue eyes, and a neon sky-blue lightning insignia on the chest. Bato can store and discharge electricity and works as an event coordinator at an interdimensional hotel. Bato is lazy and easygoing, but also wants acceptance and respect. Bato dislikes and fears animals, and supernatural animals tend to dislike Bato. This issue should make Bato's story clear by showing them trying to coast through an interdimensional animal show, failing when their fear and laziness make the animals and hotel power crisis worse, and finally proving they can be respected by using careful electrical control and real coordination to keep everyone safe.
