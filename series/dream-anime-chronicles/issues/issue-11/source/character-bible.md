# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Anima Slayer

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Anima Slayer page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Anima_Slayer`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/anima-slayer-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Androgynous lean athletic anime kaiju slayer with long sleek iridescent blue hair with purple highlights, pale skin with a subtle metallic sheen, electric blue glowing eyes, integrated blue and purple biomechanical armor covering the entire body, matching helmet used in combat scenes, luminescent nuclear-energy lines through the suit, purple armored bat wings that unfold for flight, and a calm studious expression that sharpens into decisive focus.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not give Anima ordinary cloth superhero clothes, a bulky military mech, animal ears, feather wings, red armor, gold armor, fire powers, or a purely human body separated from the armor. The suit should look biomechanically integrated with Anima's body, and the bat wings must be purple armored structures, not soft feathers.
- Powers to show: Nuclear blasts from the hands, flight through armored bat wings, enhanced strength and agility from the integrated suit, precise nuclear-energy control, and strategic kaiju analysis.
- Personality and motivation: Secure in their identity, duty-driven, clever, studious, resilient under discrimination, and determined to protect humanity from kaiju while proving that lower-class origin does not define worth.
- Emotional continuity: Starts under public doubt but internally steady, strains when direct power makes Gate more dangerous, nearly overloads the armor, then wins by trusting observation, knowledge, and precision.

## Gate

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Anima Slayer page, which names Gate as the primary kaiju antagonist whose mouth acts as a portal to another dimension.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/gate-kaiju-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Gigantic anime kaiju with obsidian-violet armored hide, massive jaw shaped like a luminous dimensional portal, concentric blue-purple rings inside the mouth, jagged white teeth around the portal edge, long forelimbs, hunched city-breaking posture, and breach-light leaking from cracks across its chest and throat.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not depict Gate as a normal dragon, dinosaur, robot, wormhole alone, or humanoid villain. Gate must read as a giant kaiju whose mouth is itself a glowing portal.
- Powers to show: Swallowing attacks into the portal-mouth, redirecting force through other dimensional breaches, tearing open blue-purple rifts in the city, and growing more dangerous when hit with brute power.
- Personality and motivation: Gate functions as a monstrous disaster rather than a speaking villain in this issue. Its threat is pattern, appetite, and dimensional redirection.
- Emotional continuity: Gate punishes force without understanding, then becomes vulnerable once Anima reads the timing between its breath, mouth rings, and return portals.

## Gate Breach City

- Source: Built from the Anima Slayer wiki's world description: a futuristic society under constant kaiju threat, with advanced technology, biomechanical engineering, and cities rebuilt after kaiju attacks.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/gate-breach-city-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Bright futuristic anime metropolis rebuilt around kaiju-defense architecture, lower-city neighborhoods under reinforced sky bridges, evacuation towers, glowing blue emergency lanes, biomechanical repair cranes, nuclear-shield pylons, and old kaiju scars visible beneath sleek reconstruction.
- Story use: Anima's home battlefield and class-pressure environment. The lower city remembers Anima's background, while the elevated elite platforms judge whether Anima is worthy of the nuclear wielder title.

## Anima's Biomechanical Nuclear Armor

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Anima Slayer page.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/anima-slayer-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Integrated blue and purple full-body biomechanical armor, matching helmet, purple armored bat wings, luminescent nuclear-energy conduits, hand emitters for nuclear blasts, and visible stress flares when the output is pushed too hard.
- Story use: Anima's greatest asset and vulnerability. The issue clarifies that the armor is strongest when guided by Anima's study rather than used as a blunt proof of worth.
