Create the next issue of Dream Anime Chronicles as a character introduction issue for Anima Slayer, based on the Dream Anime Wiki page at https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Anima_Slayer.

Continuity source facts:
- Anima Slayer has iridescent blue hair with purple highlights, long sleek hair, pale skin with a subtle metallic sheen, electric blue glowing eyes, and an androgynous lean athletic build.
- Anima wears a blue and purple biomechanical armor suit that integrates with their body, includes a matching helmet, purple armored bat wings, and luminescent nuclear-energy lines.
- Anima is a kaiju slayer and one of two nuclear wielders defending humanity from giant kaiju.
- Their armor grants nuclear blasts, flight, enhanced physical ability, and aerial combat mobility, but they rely heavily on the suit.
- Anima is secure in their identity, driven by duty, clever, studious, and deeply interested in learning about monsters.
- Anima came from a lower-class background, faced discrimination, and works to prove their worth through protection and study rather than social approval.
- The world is a futuristic society under constant kaiju threat, with advanced technology, biomechanical engineering, and cities rebuilt after attacks.
- Gate is a kaiju whose mouth acts as a portal to another dimension.
