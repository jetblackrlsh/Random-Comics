# Treatment

## Title

The World That Still Breathed

## Issue Number

4

## Logline

Arma Geddon returns to the planet they once split in half and must learn that controlling catastrophic power means accepting every part of themself before the new life in the cracks is destroyed.

## Central Conflict

Arma wants peace and atonement, but their own fear keeps turning control into suppression. The shattered home world is no longer only a monument to guilt: strange glowing life has grown from the fissures left by Arma's catastrophe. When the planet's stored energy begins to surge, Arma tries to protect the new ecosystem by fleeing, blasting the storm apart, and commanding their summoned demons like tools. Each attempt fails because Arma's panic feeds the same destructive life energy that caused the original disaster.

## Theme

Peace is not found by exiling the frightening parts of yourself. It begins when fear is faced directly and turned toward protection.

## Ending

Arma stops treating the invisible demons as shameful weapons and recognizes them as extensions of their own consciousness. Instead of firing another blast, Arma breathes with the planet's hum, gathers the demons into a controlled orbit, and weaves their white lightning into a protective lattice around the glowing life in the fissures. The planet survives, not healed all at once, but still breathing.

## Emotional Arc

Arma begins terrified that any closeness will cause another disaster. They return to their home world only because the growing life there makes exile feel like cowardice. Their first failures confirm their worst fear: the more they panic, the more destructive they become. At the low point, Arma sees that their demons are not monsters waiting to escape but unacknowledged pieces of their fear and will. By accepting them, Arma becomes less explosive and more precise. They end still burdened, but no longer alone inside themself.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, cosmic wanderer introduction, return to the shattered home world, first failure through isolation, second failure through brute energy, third failure through demon-command tactics, low point and self-recognition, controlled life-energy rescue, and a final Arma Geddon character poster summarizing the full issue.
