Create the next issue of Dream Anime Chronicles as a character introduction issue for Arma Geddon.

Continuity source: https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Arma_Geddon

Arma Geddon is a faceless cosmic wanderer with glowing purple skin, glowing purple dreadlocks, an orange T-shirt, black athletic pants, white socks, white sneakers, and an overpowering aura of white lightning energy. Arma fears losing control because a panic-driven energy outburst once cut their home planet in half. They can fly through space, survive without ordinary biological needs, unleash emotion-amplified life-energy blasts, and summon invisible demons formed from their own energy and consciousness. Arma wants peace, control, atonement, and a way to prevent any further harm.

Issue direction: Arma returns to the shattered home world that they destroyed and finds strange new life thriving in the energy-rich cracks. When a seismic energy storm threatens that new life, Arma tries isolation, brute force, and demon-command tactics before realizing that control does not mean rejecting the destructive parts of themself. Arma accepts the demons as extensions of their consciousness and uses a gentle, disciplined life-energy web to protect the growing world.
