# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Arma Geddon

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Arma Geddon page, retrieved from the wiki API for `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Arma_Geddon`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/arma-geddon-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: One boy / young masculine anime figure with a faceless smooth head, long glowing purple dreadlocks with no beads or ties, glowing purple skin, skinny toned build, plain orange T-shirt, black athletic pants with no lines or drawstring, white socks, plain white sneakers, and an overpowering aura of glowing white lightning energy.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not show white skin, pale skin, a visible face, hair beads, hair ties, ponytail hairstyle, shorts, pants drawstrings, or decorative lines on pants.
- Powers to show: Flight through space, survival in vacuum, emotion-amplified life-energy blasts, white lightning aura, summoning invisible-to-others demons made from Arma's own energy, and perceiving/interacting with those demons as extensions of Arma's consciousness.
- Personality and motivation: Fearful, self-isolating, destructive when panicked, but deeply peace-seeking. Arma wants to master their energy so they can prevent further harm and atone for destroying their home world.
- Emotional continuity: Starts alone and terrified of contact, fails whenever control becomes denial, then chooses acceptance and precision instead of exile or panic.

## Arma's Energy Demons

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Arma Geddon page.
- Reference image: None separate; treat as part of Arma's power and consciousness.
- Stable visible identifiers for readers: In this issue, depict them as translucent white-purple demonic silhouettes made of lightning and life energy, visible to the reader and Arma but ignored by the environment as if ordinary witnesses cannot see them.
- Story use: The demons first look like dangerous external monsters. By the end, they orbit Arma as disciplined extensions of Arma's own will and help shape a protective energy lattice.

## Arma's Shattered Home World

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Arma Geddon page and its WorldSim prompt.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/armas-shattered-home-world-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Vast isolated planet split by deep luminous fissures, remnants of an ancient advanced civilization, half-submerged structures, cracked towers, floating debris, thin breathable atmosphere, strange glowing plant life growing from energy-rich cracks, and a constant visible hum of purple-white energy.
- Story use: The world is both sanctuary and prison for Arma. It proves that the catastrophe did not only leave death: new life now depends on Arma learning control.
