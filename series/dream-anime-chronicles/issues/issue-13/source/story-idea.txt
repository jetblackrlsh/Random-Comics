Create the next issue of Dream Anime Chronicles as a character introduction issue for Alexi Venturas, using the Dream Anime Wiki page at https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Alexi_Venturas as continuity source.

Alexi is an androgynous silver-haired, blue-skinned inventor, gambler, and spirit-factory worker with glowing circuit and card-suit tattoos. They imbue inventions with elemental magic, have uncanny gambling luck influenced by spirits, and want to stop werewolves from accelerating climate change. Their backstory includes a family that honored both ancestor spirits and technology, plus the disappearance of a childhood friend linked to werewolves.
