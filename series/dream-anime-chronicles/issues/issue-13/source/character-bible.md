# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Alexi Venturas

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Alexi Venturas page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Alexi_Venturas`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/alexi-venturas-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Androgynous youthful anime inventor with short vibrant silver hair in an asymmetrical gravity-defying cut, pale luminescent blue skin, deep violet eyes shimmering with elemental magic, slender agile build, fitted dark factory-worker jacket with bright cyan seams, utility belt with cards and tools, fingerless gloves, and glowing circuit-like tattoos running along both arms. Some tattoos resemble playing-card suits and others resemble elemental symbols.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not make Alexi masculine-only, feminine-only, ordinary human skin-toned, long-haired, armored knight, school-uniform student, casino-only gambler, or generic cyberpunk hacker. Keep the silver asymmetrical hair, blue luminescent skin, violet eyes, slender agile build, arm tattoos, card-suit motifs, elemental symbols, inventor tools, and factory-worker context visible whenever possible.
- Powers to show: Imbuing inventions with elemental magic, reading unstable elemental flows, activating circuit tattoos, channeling spirit-influenced luck through gambling decisions, and building devices that counter werewolf elemental absorption.
- Personality and motivation: Highly intelligent, creative, quick-thinking, aloof, mysterious, loyal to friends and family, fiercely dedicated to environmental protection, and driven by the disappearance of a childhood friend linked to werewolf activity.
- Emotional continuity: Starts convinced that a brilliant device or perfect gamble can overpower the werewolves, fails when they treat technology, luck, and spirits as separate shortcuts, then wins by designing a balanced circuit that lets living nature, ancestor spirits, and invention work together.

## Climate Werewolves

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Alexi Venturas page, which describes werewolves as causing environmental threat, absorbing elemental powers, and accelerating climate change.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/climate-werewolves-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Hulking anime werewolves with storm-gray and ash-black fur, ember-orange eyes, frost and flame scars in their coats, clawed hands wrapped in stolen elemental auras, and weather distortions swirling around their bodies as they absorb fire, water, wind, and earth magic.
- Story use: They are the issue's antagonist force. They become stronger whenever Alexi attacks them with isolated elemental force, reckless luck, or unbalanced spirit power. They weaken only when the Elemental Counterdeck redirects stolen elements into balance instead of feeding their absorption.

## Spirit-Loom Factory

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Alexi Venturas page and WorldSim prompt describing factories as battlegrounds where living and spirit realms blur against environmental disasters.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/spirit-loom-factory-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Mystical anime factory where the living and spirits work side by side, with brass-and-cyan machinery, glowing conveyor sigils, translucent ancestor workers, elemental furnaces, hanging card-suit talismans, glass greenhouse walls, storm-warning gauges, and workbenches filled with tools, playing cards, and prototype devices.
- Story use: The factory is Alexi's workplace, laboratory, family inheritance, gambling hall, and battlefield. It becomes unstable whenever one realm is exploited by another.

## Elemental Counterdeck

- Source: Created for Issue #13 from Alexi's wiki-described power to imbue inventions with elemental magic and counter werewolf elemental absorption.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/elemental-counterdeck-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Fan of luminous playing-card-like circuit plates held in a compact mechanical deck frame, with card-suit symbols fused with fire, water, wind, and earth glyphs, cyan circuitry, violet spirit motes, brass hinges, and a balanced four-color glow when activated.
- Story use: The Counterdeck is built from Alexi's failed prototypes. It succeeds because it does not attack the werewolves with more raw elements; it reroutes stolen elemental force into balanced weather, spirit memory, and stable factory machinery.
