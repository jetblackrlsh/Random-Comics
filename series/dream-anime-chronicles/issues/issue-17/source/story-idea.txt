Create the next issue of Dream Anime Chronicles as a character introduction issue for Blue Bird.

Wiki source: https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Bird

Core continuity from the wiki:
- Blue Bird is a male vigilante and renovator in a large corrupt city where illegal weapons move through a powerful syndicate.
- Costume: gray boots, gray gloves, gray belt, shoulder pads made of navy blue feathers, navy blue shirt with a yellow diamond insignia in the center and long navy blue sleeves, navy blue pants, navy blue hood made of feathers with a beak image so the hood resembles a bird's face, and hood shadows so dark they obscure the hero's face.
- Personality: driven, focused, methodical, harsh about taking justice into his own hands, especially when institutions fail to protect innocent people.
- Motivation: despises gun violence and makes it his mission to disarm and destroy illegal weapons in his city.
- Softer side: the bluebird symbol suggests a buried desire for freedom, hope, and peace.
- Occupation: vigilante and renovator. He improves neglected spaces instead of creating from scratch.
- HQ: an abandoned warehouse by the docks, renovated into a high-tech base.
- Key technology: the World Editor supercomputer, used to strategize, hack systems, track enemies, and simulate outcomes.
- Powers/skills: technological mastery, hacking, tracking, agility, bullet-dodging combat skill, stealth, reconnaissance, and tactical planning.
- Backstory: once lived another life, but the details remain hidden even from himself. His connection to blue birds and heroism is subconscious and unexplained.

Issue angle:
Blue Bird tries to stop an illegal weapons shipment by controlling information, then by raiding with stealth and combat, but both fail because the syndicate survives through neglected spaces, frightened witnesses, and civic abandonment. He wins by acting as both vigilante and renovator: he turns his abandoned warehouse HQ and nearby broken buildings into a protective network that exposes the arms route, disarms the weapons, and gives frightened neighbors a safe way to report the next shipment.
