Featured character: Captain Lady Jane.

Source lore: https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Captain_Lady_Jane

Captain Lady Jane source notes: Captain Lady Jane is a cocky, confident, aggressive, arrogant, honorable superhero in training. She has black hair in a ponytail, wears silver knight's armor, black sunglasses, and a white cape. Her enchanted armor is passed down through generations, grants flight, enhanced strength, and durability, and represents a family legacy of warriors who have never known defeat. Her mother taught her honor, courage, and defending the weak, but the undefeated legacy has made Jane overconfident and terrified of public failure.

Issue premise: Captain Lady Jane charges into a supernatural attack on High Harbor City determined to win without ever being knocked down. The villainous crisis punishes that pride by turning every boast into heavier gravity. Jane must fail through brute force and reckless flight before realizing the armor's real honor is not protecting her perfect record; it is protecting people. She wins by deliberately taking the blow, grounding the enemy's power through her family armor, and choosing responsibility over reputation.
