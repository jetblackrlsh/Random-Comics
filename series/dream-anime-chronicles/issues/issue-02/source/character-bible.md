# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Captain Lady Jane

- Source: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/captain-lady-jane-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Young adult anime woman with black hair tied in a high ponytail, black sunglasses, polished silver enchanted knight armor, white cape, athletic superhero stance, confident grin that cracks into pressure and then returns as steadier courage. Use the saved reference image for armor shapes, silver-blue glow, ponytail, sunglasses, and cape continuity.
- Powers to show: Flight from enchanted armor, enhanced strength, high durability, shield-like defensive stance, and power-grounding through the inherited armor when she accepts a blow for others.
- Emotional continuity: Starts cocky and aggressive, becomes furious when brute force fails, visibly shaken by the possibility of breaking the undefeated family legacy, then chooses protective honor over reputation.

## Gravity Gallows

- Source: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/key-item-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/gravity-gallows-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Supernatural black-violet gallows-shaped gravity sigil above the city, chains of dark light, floating stone fragments, clock-bell shapes, heavy downward distortion rings, villainous presence without a human body.
- Story use: The Gallows is the issue's crisis rather than a recurring character. It grows stronger when Jane boasts, turning arrogance into literal weight.

## High Harbor City

- Source: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/setting-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/high-harbor-city-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Bright modern anime coastal city with tall glass towers, harbor bridges, rooftop gardens, blue ocean light, heroic skyline, and silver knight-family banners on public memorials.
- Story use: The city trusts Jane's family legacy but needs Jane to protect people more than protect her image.

## Jane's Enchanted Family Armor

- Source: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/key-item-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/captain-lady-jane-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Polished silver knight armor shaped for aerial superhero action, white cape, inherited crest at the chest, luminous silver-blue seams, scratches and cracks after the low point.
- Story use: The armor grants flight, strength, durability, and the final grounding strategy when Jane chooses to absorb danger for civilians.
