# Page Script

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Captain Lady Jane as a shining armored heir challenged by the weight of her own legacy.
- Layout: Single dramatic anime cover image.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Dream Anime Chronicles". Issue title text: "The Honor of Falling".
- Visual beats: Captain Lady Jane rockets upward over High Harbor City in polished silver armor, black sunglasses, black ponytail, and white cape. Above her, the Gravity Gallows spreads black-violet chains across the sky. Below, tiny civilians look upward from a cracked harbor plaza.

## Page 2: The Undefeated Heir

- Story role: Explain Jane's identity, inherited armor, powers, motivation, and pressure.
- Layout: Four panels: heroic patrol, family legacy memorial, powered flight, private pressure.
- Caption boxes: "Captain Lady Jane wore the armor of an undefeated line." "Her mother taught honor, courage, and defense of the weak." "The armor gave Jane flight, strength, and a city that expected victory." "Jane heard one command louder than all the rest: never be the first to fall."
- Visual beats: Jane posing above a rooftop patrol route; silver knight-family statues and banners; Jane blasting across the skyline with cape streaming; close-up of her sunglasses reflecting ancestor silhouettes.

## Page 3: Gravity Over High Harbor

- Story role: Establish the crisis and tie it to Jane's need to prove herself.
- Layout: Three large panels with city scale.
- Caption boxes: "The Gravity Gallows descended over High Harbor." "Every boast made its chains heavier." "Jane smiled like the city had already been saved."
- Visual beats: Black-violet sigil and chains bending glass towers downward; civilians sliding across a cracking harbor plaza; Jane lands between the crowd and the gravity distortion, silver armor shining too brightly against the threat.

## Page 4: Wrong Power, First Failure

- Story role: First failed attempt: Jane uses direct strength and arrogance.
- Layout: Five action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Jane hit first." "She hit harder." "She promised the Gallows it was beneath her." "The sky answered with weight." "For the first time, the pavement came up faster than her pride."
- Visual beats: Jane punches upward into a gravity chain; silver shockwaves burst; the chain coils around her gauntlet; dark rings press her downward; final panel shows her crashing into the plaza while civilians gasp.

## Page 5: Wrong Power, Second Failure

- Story role: Second failed attempt: Jane tries reckless flight and speed.
- Layout: Five panels emphasizing aerial motion.
- Caption boxes: "So Jane chose speed." "Flight had never failed the family armor." "She turned herself into a silver comet." "The Gallows bent every path back down." "Undefeated began to sound like trapped."
- Visual beats: Jane launches from the crater, cape torn at one edge; she loops through towers with bright silver contrails; gravity chains twist her flight path; she nearly collides with a crowded bridge; she stops the crash with one armored shoulder but drops to one knee.

## Page 6: The Weight Of The Name

- Story role: Low point: Jane confronts the fear beneath her arrogance.
- Layout: Four panels, emotional focus with legacy imagery.
- Caption boxes: "The city did not need a perfect legend." "It needed someone between danger and the street." "Jane remembered her mother's lesson." "Honor was not never falling. Honor was choosing where to fall."
- Visual beats: Jane kneels in cracked armor as ancestor silhouettes loom in the gravity sky; her sunglasses are scuffed but still on; civilians shelter behind her cape; a memory-like panel shows her mother fastening the white cape; close-up of Jane's hand pressing the family crest into the broken pavement.

## Page 7: The Place To Fall

- Story role: Decisive strategy: Jane uses durability and the city leyline anchor instead of pride.
- Layout: Five panels moving from calculation to sacrifice.
- Caption boxes: "Jane stopped performing victory." "She studied the pull." "The harbor anchor could ground the Gallows." "But only if someone carried the weight there." "Jane flew straight into the fall."
- Visual beats: Jane sees glowing lines under the plaza converging at a harbor monument; she lifts civilians behind a barrier of her cape and armor; she charges the Gallows without boasting; black-violet chains slam down; Jane deliberately angles the impact toward the glowing anchor point.

## Page 8: Armor That Stands Back Up

- Story role: Resolve the crisis and redefine Jane's honor.
- Layout: Four panels ending with a vow image.
- Caption boxes: "The impact broke the Gallows open." "The armor held because Jane held for everyone else." "She had fallen." "Then Captain Lady Jane stood taller than the legacy."
- Visual beats: Jane hits the harbor anchor in a burst of silver-blue and black-violet light; gravity chains shatter above the city; Jane rises with cracked armor and torn cape while shielding civilians; final splash panel shows her flying upward again, no boast, one fist raised toward a clear luminous sky.

## Page 9: Captain Lady Jane Character Poster

- Story role: Final character poster that summarizes the complete issue and reinforces Jane's defining traits.
- Layout: One full-page poster composition with Captain Lady Jane in a dynamic full-body pose beside one large readable caption box.
- Caption box text: "Captain Lady Jane is a cocky superhero in training who wears her family's enchanted silver armor, black sunglasses, and white cape. The armor gives her flight, strength, and durability, but the undefeated legacy makes her fear public failure. When the Gravity Gallows attacks High Harbor City, Jane tries brute force and reckless flight, and both fail because her pride feeds the enemy's weight. At her lowest point, she remembers that honor means defending the weak, not protecting a perfect record. Jane chooses where to fall, grounds the Gallows through her armor, saves the city, and stands back up as a truer heir."
- Visual beats: Jane stands in a heroic full-body anime poster pose on a high harbor rooftop, cracked silver armor gleaming, black ponytail and torn white cape blowing in ocean wind, sunglasses reflecting a clear city skyline. Faint broken gravity chains dissolve behind her. The large caption box occupies a clean readable side area without covering her silhouette.
