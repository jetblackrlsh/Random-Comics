# Treatment

## Title

The Honor of Falling

## Issue Number

2

## Logline

Captain Lady Jane faces a gravity-warping siege that turns her own arrogance against her, forcing the undefeated heir to discover that honor means standing back up for the city.

## Central Conflict

Jane believes her family armor requires her to defeat every threat without visible weakness. The Gravity Gallows instead makes pride physically heavy, so every boast and reckless charge drags her closer to the pavement while civilians remain in danger.

## Theme

An honorable warrior is not someone who never falls; an honorable warrior is someone who falls between danger and the people who need protection.

## Ending

Jane wins by letting the Gravity Gallows knock her down in the exact place where the city's leyline anchor can ground its power. She rises with cracked armor, shields the crowd, and claims the victory as her own because she chose duty over an undefeated image.

## Emotional Arc

Jane begins as a swaggering heir who treats the city patrol like a stage for proving she belongs in her mother's legend. Two failures expose how much she fears being the first warrior in her family to lose. At the low point, she remembers that her mother taught her to defend the weak, not to worship a perfect record. By the end, Jane is still bold and aggressive, but her confidence is grounded in responsibility instead of denial.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, origin and legacy, city crisis, first failed brute-force assault, second failed flight tactic, low point under the family legacy, decisive strategy, victory and new oath, and a final Captain Lady Jane character poster summarizing the full issue.
