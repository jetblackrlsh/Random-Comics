# Treatment

## Title

The Bear Who Closed the Door

## Issue Number

12

## Logline

Barrier Bear, a remorseful Superhero Academy student who can open barriers between worlds, enters the spirit world to retrieve the soul lost in their reckless battle, but only succeeds when they stop forcing doors open and learn that atonement begins with choosing which barriers to close.

## Central Conflict

Barrier Bear wants to undo the accidental death caused by their own power. The spirit world offers what looks like a direct path to the lost soul, but every reckless barrier they open creates more danger: shield walls become cages, prisons become lures for guilt-spirits, and shortcuts between planets tear the realm wider. Barrier Bear must learn that their power is not only permission to cross any boundary. It is a responsibility to know when a door should protect, when it should release, and when it should remain closed.

## Theme

Atonement is not erasing consequence. It is protecting the living, honoring the lost, and using power with restraint.

## Ending

Barrier Bear reaches the luminous nexus and finds the lost soul, but the soul does not ask to be stolen back from death. Instead, Barrier Bear closes the unstable barriers threatening both worlds, creates one calm farewell window, and returns to Superhero Academy carrying a new oath: every barrier they open must protect more than their own guilt.

## Emotional Arc

Barrier Bear begins as a wounded but still reckless hero who treats speed as proof of remorse. Their first failures come from trying to smash grief into a solution. By the end, they remain adventurous and curious, but their honor becomes patient. They choose care, restraint, and clear responsibility over the fantasy of undoing everything.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, Superhero Academy guilt and motive, first forced entry into the spirit world, failed shield tactic, failed imprisoning tactic, failed shortcut and low point, listening to the lost soul, closing the dangerous barriers with care, and a final Barrier Bear poster-summary page.
