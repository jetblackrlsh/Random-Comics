# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Barrier Bear

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Barrier Bear page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_Bear`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/barrier-bear-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Young adult anime bear-man superhero with furry yellow body, white belly, white hands, crescent-shaped eyes with tiny black pupils, small black horizontal oval nose, simple W-shaped mouth, black domino mask, red cape with a square gold clasp, sturdy athletic build, and expressive body language that shifts from reckless lunges to careful protective stances.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not make Barrier Bear a realistic animal, teddy bear toy, human in a bear costume, brown bear, panda, polar bear, armored knight, or capeless mascot. Keep the yellow fur, white belly, white hands, black mask, tiny black pupils, W-shaped mouth, and red cape with square gold clasp visible whenever possible.
- Powers to show: Opening glowing barriers between worlds, closing dangerous portals, shaping barrier walls as shields, forming temporary barrier prisons, crossing into the spirit world, and creating a small calm farewell window between life and death.
- Personality and motivation: Reckless, caring, remorseful, honorable, curious, adventurous, and driven by atonement after an accidental bystander death during a rival battle at Superhero Academy.
- Emotional continuity: Starts desperate to undo a tragedy, fails by forcing barriers open or shut without listening, then wins by using restraint, protection, and a respectful farewell rather than trying to steal the soul back.

## The Lost Soul

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Barrier Bear page, which says Barrier Bear seeks redemption by traveling through the spirit world to retrieve the lost soul of the innocent person accidentally killed during a rival battle.
- Stable visible identifiers: Small translucent human-shaped spirit silhouette made of soft white-gold and pale blue light, gentle outline, no detailed face, no horror styling, calm floating posture, surrounded by tiny star motes.
- Story use: The lost soul is not treated as an enemy or object. Their presence teaches Barrier Bear that redemption cannot erase death by force, but it can create honest farewell, protection, and changed conduct.

## Superhero Academy

- Source: Barrier Bear wiki occupation as a dedicated student at Superhero Academy.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/superhero-academy-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Bright modern anime hero school with gold-and-blue academy banners, glass training domes, floating safety barriers, holographic lesson boards, reinforced courtyard tiles, student silhouettes, and colorful power-testing lanes.
- Story use: The issue begins and ends here. It represents the place where Barrier Bear's power caused harm and where their new vow must change future training.

## Spirit World Nexus

- Source: Barrier Bear wiki WorldSim prompt describing the spirit world as outer-space-like, filled with glowing planets, floating starfields, translucent spirits, alien-like beings, emotional reactions, adaptive barriers, and a luminous nexus.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/spirit-world-nexus-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Ethereal outer-space realm with saturated starfields, glowing planets, translucent spirits, alien-spirit silhouettes with shifting forms, radiant barrier pathways, floating door-rings, and a central luminous nexus that shines white, gold, cyan, and violet.
- Story use: The spirit world reacts to Barrier Bear's intentions. Reckless guilt makes barriers crack and multiply, while careful acceptance lets the nexus stabilize into a calm farewell window.
