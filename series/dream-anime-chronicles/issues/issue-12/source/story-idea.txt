Source wiki page: https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_Bear

Barrier Bear is a young adult yellow bear-man superhero student with a white belly, white hands, crescent-shaped eyes with small black pupils, a W-shaped mouth, a small black horizontal oval nose, a black domino mask, and a red cape with a square gold clasp. They are reckless, caring, remorseful, honorable, curious, and adventurous. Their power opens and closes barriers between worlds, including the spirit world. They can use barriers to shield allies, imprison enemies, and travel between planes.

Barrier Bear's defining motivation is atonement. During a battle with a rival at Superhero Academy, they unleashed a barrier too recklessly and caused the accidental death of an innocent bystander. Haunted by that failure, they enter the spirit world, a cosmic expanse of stars, planets, translucent spirits, and alien-like beings, hoping to retrieve the lost soul and understand whether aliens and spirits are one and the same.
