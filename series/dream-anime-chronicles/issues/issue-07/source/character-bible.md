# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Amnie Zia

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Amnie Zia page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Amnie_Zia`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/amnie-zia-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Androgynous lean anime god with long flowing silver hair that looks like strands of light, pale skin with a subtle iridescent sheen, deep cosmic-blue eyes with tiny star-like specks, full body cosmic-blue unitard patterned with glowing star specks, bright silver trim, silver gloves, silver boots, and a silver T-Rex head insignia centered on the chest.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not make Amnie bulky, masculine-bearded, childlike, armored, animal-eared, dark-haired, short-haired, ordinary human, photorealistic, or dressed in robes. Keep the long silver ethereal hair, cosmic-blue starry unitard, silver gloves, silver boots, pale iridescent skin, cosmic-blue starry eyes, and silver T-Rex chest insignia consistent on every page.
- Powers to show: Latent reality manipulation expressed unreliably as dinosaur summoning and dinosaur command; brief fractured memory flashes of greater godhood; luminous star-reality distortions around Amnie when instinct takes over.
- Personality and motivation: Instinctually heroic, protective, drawn to strange crises, frustrated by amnesia, and motivated by the hope that recovering their past will reveal their true identity and full potential.
- Emotional continuity: Starts confused but eager to prove godhood, becomes embarrassed and overwhelmed when spectacle fails and civilians criticize them, nearly chooses a memory fragment over the living crisis, then becomes calm and effective when they protect one concrete person with the power they can trust.

## Bee-Themed Supervillain

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Amnie Zia page, which states Amnie is constantly thwarted by a bee-themed supervillain.
- Reference image: None for this issue; the villain appears as a masked recurring-threat silhouette, swarm drones, honeycomb armor fragments, and taunting projections rather than a fully revealed named character.
- Stable visible identifiers: Tall anime villain silhouette in black-and-gold bee armor, wing-like translucent cape shapes, glowing honeycomb mask, striped gauntlets, hovering mechanical bee drones, amber hexagon energy fields, and theatrical mocking poses.
- Story use: The villain exploits Amnie's uncertainty by turning every oversized dinosaur summon into public chaos, forcing Amnie to stop performing divinity and start solving the immediate rescue.

## Mnemosyne Junction

- Source: Issue #7 original setting built from Amnie's wiki concept of forgotten gods clashing with villains in modern cityscapes and ancient mystical landscapes.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/mnemosyne-junction-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Bright modern anime city intersection layered with ancient divine ruins, cracked marble memory arches, neon crosswalks, floating amber honeycomb barriers, dinosaur-scale streets, old celestial statues, and starry blue-silver energy reflected in glass towers.
- Story use: The city externalizes Amnie's divided condition: ordinary citizens need practical help, ancient ruins pull at divine memory, and the bee-themed attack makes the extraordinary impossible to ignore.

## Amnie's Silver T-Rex Insignia

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Amnie Zia page.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/amnie-silver-t-rex-insignia-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Clean silver T-Rex head emblem centered on Amnie's cosmic-blue chest unitard, metallic reflective edges, star-blue glow from behind, and a sharper shine whenever Amnie commands dinosaurs with focused intent.
- Story use: The insignia is Amnie's most reliable symbol of identity. It cannot restore their memory, but it brightens when they choose protective action over panic or spectacle.
