# Treatment

## Title

The God Who Forgot Tomorrow

## Issue Number

7

## Logline

Amnie Zia, an amnesiac god who can only remember how to summon dinosaurs, tries to save a city from a bee-themed supervillain and the citizens' impossible expectations, but only wins after choosing one ordinary life to protect instead of chasing the full truth of their lost divinity.

## Central Conflict

Amnie knows they were once a god with reality-shaping power, but their memories are missing and their reliable ability has narrowed to dinosaur control. When a bee-themed supervillain turns Mnemosyne Junction into a buzzing crisis maze, Amnie tries to prove their godhood through bigger and stranger dinosaur summons. The villain redirects each spectacle into more danger, while rescued civilians criticize Amnie for ignoring daily needs and never fixing everything. Amnie's true test is not whether they can remember omnipotence, but whether their heroic instinct can make one clear choice under pressure.

## Theme

Heroism does not begin when every answer returns. It begins when someone protects what is in front of them with the power they have now.

## Ending

At the low point, Amnie almost abandons the crisis to chase a memory fragment showing their former place among the gods. Instead, they notice one trapped child, one collapsing street, and one choice that does not require perfect memory. Amnie stops summoning bigger dinosaurs and directs a small coordinated rescue: shield, lift, guide, and protect. The forgotten god does not recover their past, but the silver T-Rex insignia shines as they accept that saving one person honestly is a real beginning.

## Emotional Arc

Amnie begins radiant, confused, and instinctively heroic, convinced that a true god should remember every power and solve every crisis at once. Public criticism and the bee-themed villain's mockery make them feel incompetent and incomplete. Their failures turn spectacle into danger until Amnie realizes the search for their whole identity has become another distraction. By the end, Amnie is still amnesiac, still uncertain, and still drawn to the extraordinary, but their heroic identity becomes clearer: protect first, remember later.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, Amnie's amnesiac arrival, the bee-themed crisis, failed dinosaur spectacle, public criticism, memory temptation, focused rescue choice, coordinated dinosaur victory, and a final Amnie Zia poster-summary page.
