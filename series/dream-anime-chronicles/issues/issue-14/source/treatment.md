# Treatment

## Title

The Gauntlet That Chose Mercy

## Issue Number

14

## Logline

Ameryx Xyr, a serene student chosen by a divine computerized gauntlet after a classroom possession disaster, must save their peers from demonic influence without letting reality-warping power become another kind of erasure.

## Central Conflict

Ameryx wants to cleanse every possessed classmate and stop the demonic breach spreading through converging realities. Their gauntlet can analyze future paths, bend existence, and purge corruption, but the fastest futures all tempt Ameryx toward deleting the possessed, sealing them away, or rewriting the disaster so the victims become collateral damage. The demons feed on that fear by making each possessed peer look beyond rescue. Ameryx must prove that guardianship is not control over reality, but faith in the people still trapped inside the darkness.

## Theme

Power can change reality, but mercy decides what reality is worth saving.

## Ending

Ameryx refuses every future that treats possessed classmates as expendable. They synchronize the gauntlet's divine reality code with their own memory of being possessed and use demonic purge as restoration rather than force. The classroom breach collapses into a luminous convergence sanctuary, the freed students breathe again, and Ameryx accepts the burden of Guardian of Realities because they have learned to save people without erasing who they are.

## Emotional Arc

Ameryx begins calm but shaken by the memory of losing themself to possession. They trust the gauntlet's strategy because fear makes mercy feel too slow. Each failed tactic shows that analysis, reality warping, and temporal foresight can become cruel when separated from empathy. By the end, Ameryx is still serene and tactically brilliant, but their resolve has changed: they will use impossible power only in ways that leave room for redemption.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, ordinary classroom before the disaster, possession and the gauntlet choosing Ameryx, first purge attempt, reality-warping mistake, temporal-awareness temptation, Ameryx choosing mercy, full demonic purge and rescue, and a final Ameryx Xyr poster-summary page.
