Create the next issue of Dream Anime Chronicles as a character introduction issue for Ameryx Xyr, based on the Dream Anime Wiki page at https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Ameryx_Xyr.

Ameryx Xyr is a serene but fiercely determined Guardian of Realities. They were once an ordinary student until a class demonstration opened a demonic possession crisis among their peers. Ameryx was also possessed, but a divine computerized gauntlet chose them, freed them, and gave them powers including divine reality warping, demonic purge, AI-assisted strategy, and temporal awareness. Their defining motivation is to save possessed peers without giving up on redemption.
