# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Ameryx Xyr

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Ameryx Xyr page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Ameryx_Xyr`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/ameryx-xyr-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Slim androgynous anime guardian with short jagged silver hair carrying a holographic rainbow sheen, pale almost translucent skin with an ethereal glow under moonlight, deep violet eyes flecked with gold, a calm but fiercely resolved expression, agile upright posture, and a fused advanced computerized gauntlet covering the right forearm and hand. The gauntlet is covered in esoteric divine symbols, luminous circuit seams, and one large orange LED pulsing with divine energy.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not make Ameryx bulky, ordinary school-uniform-only after the transformation, long-haired, armored knight, demonic-looking, purely robotic, or faceless. Keep the short jagged holographic silver hair, pale translucent glow, violet-gold eyes, slim androgynous build, serene determination, and fused right-arm computerized divine gauntlet visible whenever possible.
- Powers to show: Divine reality warping through glowing geometric edits to space, demonic purge as gold-violet cleansing light, AI-assisted strategy as holographic future paths from the gauntlet, temporal awareness as branching translucent scenes, and empathic resolve that steadies the gauntlet.
- Personality and motivation: Serene, wise beyond their years, empathetic, cautious because of demonic possession, optimistic about redemption, and driven to rescue possessed peers rather than abandon them.
- Emotional continuity: Starts as an ordinary student thrown into demonic possession, is chosen and freed by the gauntlet, almost over-trusts the gauntlet's harsh strategic futures, then becomes Guardian of Realities by choosing a slower merciful purge that restores the victims.

## Possessed Classmates

- Source: Ameryx Xyr wiki backstory describing a class demonstration that caused widespread demonic possession among their peers.
- Stable visible identifiers: Anime students of varied appearances with ordinary school clothes warped by smoky black-violet demonic aura, gold cracks of the original person visible in their eyes or hands, frightened expressions partly hidden under possession marks, and shadows shaped like clawed masks behind them.
- Story use: They are victims, not disposable enemies. Each page should make clear that Ameryx is trying to save the people inside the demonic influence.

## Reality Convergence Ruins

- Source: Ameryx Xyr wiki WorldSim prompt describing a realm where realities converge, with ruined civilizations, sanctuaries, distorted realms, survivors, and possessed beings.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/reality-convergence-ruins-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Bright surreal anime battleground where a ruined classroom overlaps with broken city towers, untouched natural sanctuary fragments, floating reality seams, shattered archways from fallen civilizations, thin luminous cracks in the sky, and black-violet demonic infection pushing against gold-white divine light.
- Story use: The classroom disaster expands into this convergence battlefield. By the ending, it becomes a safer sanctuary threshold rather than a spreading breach.

## Divine Reality Gauntlet

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Ameryx Xyr page.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/divine-reality-gauntlet-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Advanced computerized gauntlet fused to Ameryx's right arm, sleek divine-tech metal, glowing orange LED at the back of the hand or wrist, esoteric symbols etched into plates, violet-gold circuit seams, translucent holographic strategy panels, and reality-warping glyph rings.
- Story use: The gauntlet chooses Ameryx, frees them from possession, analyzes outcomes, enables reality warping and demonic purge, and becomes strongest when Ameryx chooses mercy over erasure.
