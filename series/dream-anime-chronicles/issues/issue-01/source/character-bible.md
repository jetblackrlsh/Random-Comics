# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Armor On

- Source: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/armor-on-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Bald faceless humanoid made of glowing blue energy; smooth featureless head; silver chest armor; incomplete modular silver armor; cyan aura; tall athletic anime silhouette; confident duelist poses that crack into anxious vigilance when the watcher is near.
- Powers to show: Flight, portal creation, armor acquisition attempts, astral perception, and armor bonding.
- Emotional continuity: Proud at first, increasingly rattled by reflections of the Watching Eye, then focused and direct when using astral perception as strategy.

## The Watching Eye

- Source: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/watching-eye-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Giant unblinking eye, gooey black-violet body, wet feathered edges, pale gold iris, shadow tendrils, seen in reflections or sky rifts.
- Story use: The Eye never physically attacks in Issue #1. It watches, pressures, and reveals Armor On's fear.

## Mirror Champion

- Source: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/mirror-champion-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Tall armored warrior, faceless mirrored helmet, crystal shoulder guards, reflective silver-and-rainbow armor, prism lance, angular glass cape, calm defensive stance.
- Story use: The Champion tests Armor On, wins the early exchanges, survives the duel, and loses the Prism Plate honorably.
