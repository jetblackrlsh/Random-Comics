# Treatment

## Title

Armor Against the Eye

## Issue Number

1

## Logline

Armor On invades a floating mirror fortress to claim a legendary armor plate, but the duel forces them to admit that fear, not weakness, has been choosing their battles.

## Central Conflict

Armor On believes the Prism Plate will make them harder to destroy when the hidden watcher finally attacks. The Mirror Bastion instead tests whether Armor On can stop collecting armor as a panic response and use astral perception as a deliberate strategy.

## Theme

The strongest shield is not the one that hides fear, but the one that lets a warrior face it clearly.

## Ending

Armor On defeats the Mirror Champion, gains the Prism Plate, and leaves stronger than before. The Watching Eye remains in the sky, but Armor On finally looks back at it without fleeing.

## Emotional Arc

Armor On begins proud and restless, treating every victory as another step toward invincibility. The Mirror Champion's defenses turn that pride into repeated failure, and the Eye's reflection exposes the paranoia underneath. By the end, Armor On still wants the ultimate armor, but they understand that the power to sense danger can be a weapon, not only a fear alarm.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, origin and motive, arrival at the Mirror Bastion, first failed assault, second failed portal tactic, low point under the Eye's reflection, astral-perception strategy, victory and vow, and a final Armor On character poster summarizing the full issue.
