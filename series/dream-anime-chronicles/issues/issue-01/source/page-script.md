# Page Script

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Armor On's defining image: a proud armor collector challenged by the hidden watcher.
- Layout: Single dramatic anime cover image.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Dream Anime Chronicles". Issue title text: "Armor Against the Eye".
- Visual beats: Armor On flies upward through the Fragmented Digital Sky, blue-energy body blazing, incomplete silver armor shining, one hand reaching toward the floating Prism Plate. The Mirror Bastion glitters below. In the upper sky, the huge feathered Watching Eye opens behind a portal rift.

## Page 2: The Collector Who Feared The Sky

- Story role: Explain Armor On's identity, powers, motivation, and fear.
- Layout: Four panels: astral warrior portrait, portal travel, armor collection, watcher reflection.
- Caption boxes: "Armor On was blue energy wrapped in borrowed steel." "They crossed virtual worlds through portals and flight." "Each defeated warrior gave them another piece of protection." "But every trophy was really for the Eye that watched from nowhere."
- Visual beats: Heroic pose of Armor On with silver chest plate; portal rings between floating worlds; Armor On holding broken armor fragments from past victories; close reflection of the Watching Eye across Armor On's smooth faceless head.

## Page 3: The Mirror Bastion

- Story role: Establish the target and the goal tied to Armor On's motivation.
- Layout: Three large panels with wide fantasy scale.
- Caption boxes: "The Prism Plate waited inside the Mirror Bastion." "Armor On believed it could turn fear into invincibility." "The Mirror Champion stood between them and the next piece."
- Visual beats: The floating crystalline fortress in a blue-violet digital sky; the Prism Plate glowing on a pedestal behind walls of mirrored glass; the Mirror Champion stepping forward with a prism lance while Armor On lands in a battle stance.

## Page 4: Wrong Power, First Failure

- Story role: First failed attempt: Armor On tries to win through direct force and armor acquisition.
- Layout: Five action panels.
- Caption boxes: "Armor On attacked like every past victory could repeat itself." "The Champion reflected each strike." "Armor acquisition found no opening." "The harder Armor On reached, the emptier their hand became."
- Visual beats: Armor On charges with blue energy fists; Mirror Champion parries with lance and mirror shield; Armor On tries to draw armor light from the Champion but the energy rebounds; Armor On is thrown through a prism wall; shattered reflections show Armor On surrounded by missing armor outlines.

## Page 5: Wrong Power, Second Failure

- Story role: Second failed attempt: Armor On tries portals and flight to outmaneuver the Champion.
- Layout: Five panels emphasizing speed and confusion.
- Caption boxes: "So Armor On changed the battlefield." "Portals opened above, below, and behind." "Flight became a storm of blue comets." "The Bastion turned every shortcut into a trap." "The Prism Plate stayed out of reach."
- Visual beats: Armor On, with exactly two arms, opens multiple glowing portals; they dive through one and appear behind the Champion; mirror floors rotate the exit into a lance counterattack; Armor On ricochets between portals and mirror copies; the final panel clearly shows Armor On separated from the Prism Plate by repeating mirror corridors and reflected distance.

## Page 6: The Eye In Every Mirror

- Story role: Low point: Armor On's fear overwhelms them.
- Layout: Four panels, larger captions and emotional focus.
- Caption boxes: "Then the Bastion stopped showing enemies." "It showed the reason Armor On never rested." "The Eye was not in one mirror." "It was in all of them."
- Visual beats: Armor On kneels amid countless mirror panels; every reflection contains the feathered Watching Eye; the Mirror Champion waits silently instead of attacking; Armor On clutches their silver chest plate as blue energy flickers; one close-up shows Armor On's faceless head turned toward the Eye.

## Page 7: The Power That Sees

- Story role: Decisive strategy: Armor On uses astral perception instead of panic.
- Layout: Five panels moving from stillness to tactical reversal.
- Caption boxes: "Armor On stopped chasing armor." "They listened to the feeling of being watched." "Astral perception traced the false reflections." "Only one path was real."
- Visual beats: Armor On closes their stance and lets blue aura become calm; glowing perception lines connect Eye reflections to false mirror paths; Armor On marks the one real blind spot in the Champion's defense; Armor On opens a precise portal inside the lance arc; they pass through with controlled motion instead of panic speed.

## Page 8: Armor Earned

- Story role: Resolve the duel, bond the Prism Plate, and define Armor On's future.
- Layout: Four panels ending with a vow image.
- Caption boxes: "The Champion fell, but did not break." "The Prism Plate chose the warrior who could face the watcher." "Armor On was not invincible." "They were finally looking back."
- Visual beats: Armor On disarms the Mirror Champion without killing them; the Champion kneels and releases the Prism Plate; the plate bonds over Armor On's silver chest armor in rainbow-blue light; final splash panel shows Armor On flying away through the Fragmented Digital Sky, new armor gleaming, staring directly at the Watching Eye in the distance.

## Page 9: Armor On Character Poster

- Story role: Final character poster that summarizes the complete issue and reinforces Armor On's defining traits.
- Layout: One full-page poster composition with Armor On in a dynamic full-body pose beside one large readable caption box.
- Caption box text: "Armor On, a faceless blue-energy warrior, hunts armor across virtual worlds because the Watching Eye makes them afraid to stand still. In the Mirror Bastion, they chase the Prism Plate, convinced it will make them invincible. Direct force, armor stealing, portals, and flight all fail against the Mirror Champion. When every mirror reveals the Eye, Armor On uses astral perception to find the only real path. They win the Prism Plate without destroying the Champion and finally look back."
- Visual beats: Armor On stands in a heroic full-body poster pose in the Fragmented Digital Sky, wearing the Prism Plate over their silver chest armor, blue aura glowing brightly. The Watching Eye appears far behind them in a sky rift, the Mirror Bastion floats below, and the large caption box occupies a clean readable area without covering Armor On's silhouette.
