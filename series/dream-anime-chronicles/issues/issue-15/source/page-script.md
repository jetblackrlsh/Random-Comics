# Page Script

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean readable caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay. Caption boxes must contain only reader-facing story text, never panel labels or prefixes.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Aurum Gold as a brilliant stranded explorer inside a mysterious sandy temple that is both threat and answer.
- Layout: Single dramatic anime cover image.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Dream Anime Chronicles". Issue title text: "The Temple That Answered Back".
- Visual beats: Aurum Gold stands in a dynamic three-quarter explorer pose on a collapsing sandstone bridge, sandy blonde spiky hair, amber eyes, golden-brown skin, ruby tattoo on the right forehead temple, right cheek scar, sleek black unitard with shimmering rainbow gemstone trim. Spell stones orbit one hand in ruby, amber, sapphire, emerald, violet, and clear light. Behind Aurum, a vast sandy temple opens into impossible stairs, star-map carvings, floating slabs, and a distant doorway shaped like a question mark of gold light.

## Page 2: Waking Without A Map

- Story role: Establish Aurum, the temple, the missing memory, and the goal to get home.
- Layout: Four large panels with roomy caption boxes.
- Caption boxes: "Aurum Gold woke in a sea of sand with no map, no memory of arrival, and one impossible temple watching from the dunes." "Their ruby tattoo, cheek scar, and explorer instincts were familiar. Everything else was a question." "The first doorway opened only after Aurum touched the spell stones scattered at its base." "If the temple had brought them here, Aurum would make it explain the way home."
- Visual beats: Aurum wakes half-buried in golden sand beneath a blazing anime sky; close-up of amber eyes, ruby forehead tattoo, right cheek scar; Aurum finds glowing spell stones in sand; temple doorway lights with runes as Aurum steps inside.

## Page 3: The Easy Answer

- Story role: First success that tempts Aurum to rely on power.
- Layout: Four action panels.
- Caption boxes: "The ruby stone answered first, filling Aurum's muscles with heat and speed." "Aurum cleared falling columns, crossed a blade hallway, and smiled at the simple lesson." "Power opened paths." "For one bright minute, the temple seemed like a problem that could be beaten."
- Visual beats: Ruby spell stone ignites around Aurum's hand; Aurum dashes across collapsing tiles with enhanced speed; acrobatic leap through a corridor of glowing blade traps; triumphant Aurum lands before a deeper sealed gate as red light floods the hall.

## Page 4: The Temple Pushes Back

- Story role: First major failure: brute force makes the temple defenses escalate.
- Layout: Four panels, one large impact panel.
- Caption boxes: "At the second gate, Aurum stopped asking and started forcing." "The amber stone turned sand into a giant fist." "The gate did not break. It learned." "Every wall Aurum struck became another wall moving faster than the last."
- Visual beats: Aurum channels amber spell stone into a massive sand fist; the gate absorbs the blow and copies it into shifting walls; temple corridors rotate violently; Aurum barely dodges crushing stone slabs, expression shifting from confidence to alarm.

## Page 5: The Wrong Map

- Story role: Second failure: navigation skill fails when Aurum assumes the maze is fixed.
- Layout: Four clear panels with map imagery.
- Caption boxes: "Aurum tried the next best tool: navigation." "They marked turns, counted steps, and tracked the sun through broken ceilings." "But the temple was not a maze of places." "It was a maze of choices, and every impatient choice redrew the map."
- Visual beats: Aurum scratches route marks into sandstone; overhead view of a glowing maze rearranging behind them; Aurum compares a drawn map to a corridor that has changed; spell stones flicker uncertainly as the walls display new carvings of Aurum's failed strikes.

## Page 6: The Memory Trap

- Story role: Temptation: the temple offers an easy false memory and Aurum almost accepts it.
- Layout: Five panels with one central illusion panel.
- Caption boxes: "Then the violet stone showed the memory Aurum wanted most." "A door home. A hand reaching through light. A life waiting exactly where they left it." "Aurum stepped toward it because longing can look like proof." "But the scar on their cheek burned cold." "The memory had no question inside it, only an order."
- Visual beats: Violet spell stone projects a radiant doorway with a warm home-like silhouette; Aurum reaches toward it, visibly emotional; close-up of right cheek scar glowing cold; the illusion cracks to reveal a trap pit and hooked runes beneath; Aurum pulls back at the last second.

## Page 7: The Question Chamber

- Story role: Low point and reasoning turn: Aurum recognizes the temple's rule.
- Layout: Four calmer puzzle-focused panels.
- Caption boxes: "At the heart of the temple, Aurum found six empty sockets and no enemy to strike." "Every carving showed the same pattern: force, failure, silence, answer." "The spell stones had never been keys by themselves." "They were words, and Aurum had been shouting."
- Visual beats: Aurum enters circular star-map chamber with six empty stone sockets; carvings show past actions as symbolic murals; Aurum kneels, studying sand grains, rune angles, and colored reflections; spell stones float quietly around Aurum, dim but attentive.

## Page 8: Listening Opens The Door

- Story role: Resolution action: Aurum uses curiosity, puzzle-solving, and careful spell-stone arrangement to open the memory chamber.
- Layout: Four panels ending in a large reveal.
- Caption boxes: "Aurum placed the stones as a question: where was I taken from, and what must I learn before I return?" "The sand stopped attacking." "The temple answered in light, not surrender." "A path home existed, but only through the secrets Aurum was brave enough to understand."
- Visual beats: Aurum arranges ruby, amber, sapphire, emerald, violet, and clear spell stones into sockets matching star-map carvings; the chamber calms and sand settles; a golden memory projection shows Aurum being transported from another world by temple light; final large panel reveals a living compass-light floating above Aurum's palm and a deep doorway opening ahead.

## Page 9: Aurum Gold Character Poster

- Story role: Final character poster that summarizes Aurum's defining traits and future role.
- Layout: One full-page poster composition with Aurum in a strong full-body pose beside one large readable caption box.
- Caption box text: "Aurum Gold is a curiosity-driven temple explorer with sandy blonde spiky hair, golden-brown skin, amber eyes, a ruby tattoo on the right temple, a right-cheek scar, and a black unitard trimmed like rainbow gemstones. Transported from their own world into a vast sandy temple, Aurum uses spell stones to enhance their body and shape the environment. Their true gift is not force. It is survival through wonder, puzzle-solving, and the courage to ask the question that opens the way home."
- Visual beats: Aurum stands heroically at the open memory chamber, black unitard and rainbow gemstone trim gleaming, spell stones orbiting like a halo of colored runes, living compass-light in one hand, vast sandy temple pathways glowing behind them. The large caption box occupies a clean side area without covering Aurum's silhouette.
