# Treatment

## Title

The Temple That Answered Back

## Issue Number

15

## Logline

Aurum Gold wakes in a shifting sandy temple and tries to force a way home with spell stones, but only escapes the temple's deadliest chamber after realizing the ruins respond to curiosity, not conquest.

## Central Conflict

Aurum wants to survive the otherworldly temple, recover the memory of how they arrived, and open a path home. The temple offers spell stones that can magnify strength, speed, and environmental control, but every direct use of power turns the temple's defenses smarter and more dangerous. Aurum must decide whether to keep treating the temple as an enemy to overpower or approach it as a mystery that can be understood.

## Theme

Discovery is not the same as taking. The strongest explorer survives by asking the right question.

## Ending

Aurum stops spending spell-stone energy like a weapon and studies how the stones, sand, carvings, and traps answer one another. By arranging the stones as a question instead of a command, Aurum opens the temple's memory chamber, recovers a clear clue about being transported from their own world, and earns a living compass-light that can guide them deeper toward home.

## Emotional Arc

Aurum begins bold, restless, and capable, convinced that enough resourcefulness can turn every obstacle into a solved problem. Repeated failures make them frustrated because the temple seems to punish curiosity with danger. At the low point, Aurum realizes the temple is not punishing questions; it is punishing careless answers. By the end, Aurum remains adventurous and brave, but their defining strength becomes respectful interpretation: they learn to let wonder guide strategy.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, awakening in the sandy temple, first spell-stone power success, brute-force failure, navigation failure, memory temptation, the low-point puzzle chamber, Aurum's defining choice and escape, and a final Aurum Gold poster-summary page.
