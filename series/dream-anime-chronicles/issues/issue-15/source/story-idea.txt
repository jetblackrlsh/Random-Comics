Create the next issue of Dream Anime Chronicles as a character introduction issue for Aurum Gold.

Continuity source: https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Aurum_Gold

Aurum Gold is a sandy-blonde, amber-eyed temple explorer with golden-brown skin, a ruby gemstone tattoo on the right temple of their forehead, a scar on the right cheek from a laser rifle encounter, and a full-body black leather unitard with shimmering rainbow gemstone trim. Aurum is curiosity-driven, adventurous, resourceful, quick-thinking, and skilled at navigation, combat, and puzzle-solving. They were transported from their own world into a vast sandy otherworldly temple complex with no memory of how they arrived. They want to uncover the temple's secrets, survive its defenses, and find a way home. Aurum can harness spell stones to enhance physical abilities and manipulate the environment, but this issue should prove that curiosity and careful interpretation are stronger than treating every mystery like a fight.
