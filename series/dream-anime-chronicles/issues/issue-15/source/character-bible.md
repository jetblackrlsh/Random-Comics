# Character Bible

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean readable caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art by `image_gen`. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay.

## Series Style

- Modern colorful anime aesthetic, high saturation, sharp detail, luminous spell effects, dynamic camera angles, clean readable comic-page composition.
- Aurum's pages should feel like bright adventure anime inside a vast sandy temple: gold light, ruby accents, rainbow gemstone trim, blue-white spell-stone glow, shifting sand, carved stone, and dangerous puzzle architecture.
- Story clarity is the priority. Captions should explain what Aurum wants, why each tactic fails, what the temple is teaching, and how the final choice solves the conflict.

## Aurum Gold

- Role: protagonist, temple explorer, transported survivor, puzzle-solving adventurer.
- Series description: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/aurum-gold-character.png`
- Visual identifiers: Androgynous athletic anime adventurer with golden-brown skin, amber eyes, sandy blonde short spiky messy hair, a small intricate ruby gemstone tattoo on the right temple of the forehead, a scar on the right cheek from a laser rifle encounter, and a sleek full-body black leather unitard with shimmering rainbow colored gemstone-textured trim.
- Expression and posture: Curious alert eyes, restless explorer stance, quick-thinking combat readiness, visible wonder when solving puzzles, frustration only when direct force fails.
- Powers and skills: Harnesses spell stones to enhance physical ability and manipulate the environment; skilled at navigation, hand-to-hand combat, trap reading, and puzzle solving.
- Motivation: Uncover the temple's secrets, survive its defenses, remember how they arrived, and find a way home.
- Defining lesson: Aurum's real strength is not spell-stone force, but curiosity guided by evidence.

- Role: recurring key item, source of power and puzzle language.
- Series description: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/key-item-descriptions.md`
- Reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/spell-stones-key-item.png`
