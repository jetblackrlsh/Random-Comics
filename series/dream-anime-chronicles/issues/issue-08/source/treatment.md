# Treatment

## Title

The Warlord Who Waited

## Issue Number

8

## Logline

Ahrimani Prime, a proud purple reptilian warlord, tries to save the Ahrimani from extinction by crushing the ancient monster hunters head-on, but only becomes truly dangerous after learning to use the purple sand to make time itself part of the invasion.

## Central Conflict

Ahrimani Prime believes domination is the only answer to humiliation. In the ancient world, their pack loses again and again to human monster hunters who study every charge, ambush, and roar. Prime first answers with strength, then stricter discipline, then open terror, but every direct attack teaches the hunters how to cut the Ahrimani down. The legendary purple sand offers a different kind of conquest: not stronger rage, but timing. Prime must choose whether to keep fighting the hunters on the battlefield that is killing their kind or abandon that era entirely and strike a future that no longer remembers the Ahrimani exist.

## Theme

Power becomes most dangerous when patience turns grievance into strategy.

## Ending

Prime stops treating the purple sand as a battlefield trick and uses it as a historical weapon. They lead the surviving Ahrimani through a violet time storm into a complacent future Earth, where their first victory is not a duel but a world caught unprepared. Prime wins the defining conflict, but the ending frames that victory as the birth of a terrifying recurring villain rather than a heroic triumph.

## Emotional Arc

Prime begins furious, proud, and wounded by the ancient hunters' repeated victories. Each failure deepens their fear that the Ahrimani will become a story humans tell after the species is gone. The discovery of purple sand first feeds their aggression, then sharpens their mind. By the end, Prime is still brutal and power-hungry, but their defining identity is clearer: a militant conqueror who can wait, deceive, and move whole wars through time.

## Story Shape

Nine pages: cover, ancient defeat, brute-force failure, purple sand discovery, failed reckless time strike, strategic realization, future invasion, first conquest, and a final Ahrimani Prime poster-summary page.
