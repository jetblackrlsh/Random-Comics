# Page Script

All pages are full modern anime comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. Use highly saturated color, bright glow, sharp detail, dynamic shots and posing, crisp panel borders, and clean readable caption boxes. Caption-box text must be generated directly inside the page art. Use caption boxes only, no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no watermarks, and no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Ahrimani Prime as a purple reptilian conqueror whose brutality becomes more dangerous when time becomes strategy.
- Layout: Single dramatic anime cover image.
- Caption/title text: Cover title text: "Dream Anime Chronicles". Issue title text: "The Warlord Who Waited".
- Visual beats: Ahrimani Prime stands huge in the foreground with violet scaled skin, glowing yellow eyes, purple mohawk crest, tattered dark shirt, rough loincloth, brown wrist and ankle wrappings, brown belt with circular gold buckle, clawed hands raised through a spiral of luminous purple sand. Behind Prime, ancient monster hunters on one side and a ruined future city on the other are split by a violet time rift, while the Ahrimani pack marches from the past into tomorrow.

## Page 2: The Species That Would Not Kneel

- Story role: Introduce Prime's ancient war, pride, pack leadership, and fear of extinction.
- Layout: Four panels: ancient battlefield, Prime close-up, hunter victory, wounded pack.
- Caption boxes: "Ahrimani Prime was born into a war humans thought they could finish." "Every scar on their purple scales became a promise." "The hunters learned the Ahrimani charge, the Ahrimani roar, and the Ahrimani rage." "Prime learned only that survival without conquest was another name for defeat."
- Visual beats: Ancient desert battlefield under a violet-red sky; towering Prime leads purple reptilian warriors; human monster hunters spring trap chains and mirror shields; Prime kneels among wounded pack members, yellow eyes burning with pride and shame.

## Page 3: Strength Was Not Enough

- Story role: First failed tactic: Prime tries to win through raw aggression and discipline, but the hunters adapt.
- Layout: Four action panels moving from command to failure.
- Caption boxes: "Prime answered extinction with strength." "They drilled the pack into a blade." "They led the charge personally." "The hunters had already measured the blade."
- Visual beats: Prime training the Ahrimani pack in strict formations; Prime lunges with enhanced speed and clawed force; monster hunters pivot traps into the formation; final panel shows Prime smashing a shield too late while hunters cut off the pack's escape.

## Page 4: The Sand Beneath The Old War

- Story role: Prime discovers purple sand and its ability to bend time.
- Layout: Three large panels, mystical and ominous.
- Caption boxes: "Below the oldest battlefield, Prime found the color of a different victory." "The purple sand did not promise mercy." "It promised that the hour of defeat could be moved."
- Visual beats: Prime descends into a cracked underground ruin glowing violet; luminous purple sand rises around their claws, making ghost images of past and future Prime overlap; Prime watches a falling spear freeze in midair while their yellow eyes widen with strategic hunger.

## Page 5: The Wrong Future

- Story role: Second failed tactic: Prime uses the sand recklessly for personal attack and nearly loses the pack.
- Layout: Five kinetic panels with unstable time effects.
- Caption boxes: "Prime first used time like another weapon." "A blink became an ambush." "An ambush became a maze." "Every stolen second split the pack thinner." "Power without patience was still a charge."
- Visual beats: Prime teleporting behind hunters in violet sand flashes; multiple afterimages confuse both sides; Ahrimani warriors stumble between overlapping moments; hunters exploit the chaos with anchored sigils; Prime claws through a time ripple, furious that the sand's gift is not simple victory.

## Page 6: To Let The Enemy Forget

- Story role: Defining strategic realization: Prime understands extinction can be faked and the future can be ambushed.
- Layout: Four panels, intense and calculating.
- Caption boxes: "Then Prime stopped asking how to win this battle." "They asked what humans would become after they believed the Ahrimani were gone." "Memory would soften them." "Tomorrow would open its throat."
- Visual beats: Prime stands alone in a violet sandstorm holding grains above a map of eras; ghostly images show ancient hunters aging into legend, then future cities without defenses; Prime orders the surviving pack into silence; final panel shows Prime smiling coldly as the sand forms a huge hourglass gate.

## Page 7: The Pack That Arrived Late

- Story role: Climax: Prime moves the surviving Ahrimani into the future.
- Layout: Four panels with a large time-rift reveal.
- Caption boxes: "The hunters prepared for monsters in front of them." "Prime moved the war out from under their feet." "The Ahrimani vanished from history." "They did not vanish from hunger."
- Visual beats: Ancient hunters storm an empty Ahrimani camp; Prime leads the pack through a towering purple sand portal; silhouettes stretch across centuries; the pack emerges above a bright modern city that has no idea what has returned.

## Page 8: Earth, Unready

- Story role: Resolution: Prime's first future victory defines them as a conqueror.
- Layout: Four panels ending with a dominant conquest splash.
- Caption boxes: "Humanity had forgotten the shape of Prime's shadow." "Its towers were tall." "Its fear was untrained." "Ahrimani Prime did not conquer by arriving stronger. They conquered by arriving late."
- Visual beats: Purple sandstorm rolls through a futuristic skyline; Prime smashes through a defense gate while drones and alarms fail; Ahrimani warriors capture streets as frightened resistance figures flee; final splash shows Prime atop a ruined tower, purple aura blazing, the pack kneeling below, future Earth burning violet behind them.

## Page 9: Ahrimani Prime Character Poster

- Story role: Final character poster that summarizes the complete issue and reinforces Ahrimani Prime's defining traits.
- Layout: One full-page poster composition with Prime in a strong full-body pose beside one large readable caption box.
- Caption box text: "Ahrimani Prime is a towering purple reptilian warlord with yellow eyes, a purple mohawk crest, rough barbarian clothing, and a violet aura of time-bending sand. Ancient monster hunters defeated the Ahrimani again and again, pushing Prime to turn pride into strategy. The purple sand grants strength, speed, and passage across eras, but Prime's true weapon is patience. They let humanity forget the Ahrimani, then led the surviving pack into the future. The warlord who waited became the conqueror Earth never prepared to face."
- Visual beats: Ahrimani Prime stands in a powerful anime poster pose on a ruined future tower, yellow eyes glowing, purple mohawk and reptilian scales crisp, brown wrappings and circular gold belt buckle visible. Purple sand forms an hourglass halo behind them, with ancient hunters fading in one half and conquered future Earth in the other. The large caption box occupies a clean readable side area without covering Prime's silhouette.
