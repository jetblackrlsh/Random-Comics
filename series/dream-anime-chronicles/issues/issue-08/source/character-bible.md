# Character Bible

## Series Style

Use modern anime art with highly saturated color, high detail sharpness, bright glow, crisp linework, dynamic posing, dramatic camera angles, luminous action effects, and clean readable caption boxes. Do not use photorealistic documentary styling for this issue.

## Ahrimani Prime

- Source: Dream Anime Wiki Ahrimani Prime page, `https://dream-anime.fandom.com/wiki/Ahrimani_Prime`, and the series continuity entry in `series/dream-anime-chronicles/source/character-descriptions.md`.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/ahrimani-prime-character.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Towering humanoid purple reptilian anime warlord with violet scaled skin, massive barbarian build, ridged reptile face, glowing yellow eyes, sharp teeth, broad clawed hands, vivid purple mohawk crest, tattered dark shirt, rough loincloth, brown cloth wrappings around wrists and ankles, brown belt with a circular gold buckle, bare clawed feet, and a violent purple aura threaded with sand-like time particles.
- Negative visual constraints: Do not make Ahrimani Prime human, mammalian, dragon-winged, horned, armored like a knight, robotic, slim, small, blue, green, or photorealistic. Keep the purple reptilian skin, yellow eyes, purple mohawk crest, tattered shirt, loincloth, brown wrappings, brown belt, circular gold buckle, muscular barbarian silhouette, and purple sand aura consistent on every page.
- Powers to show: Time manipulation through purple sand, enhanced strength and agility, hardened reptilian scales, warband command, surprise attacks across eras, and tactical use of deception through the illusion that the Ahrimani are extinct.
- Personality and motivation: Determined, militant, strategic, cunning, aggressive, proud, intimidating, and obsessed with conquering humanity so the Ahrimani will never again be hunted toward extinction.
- Emotional continuity: Starts enraged by ancient defeats, becomes more disciplined after brute force fails, briefly overuses the purple sand as another weapon, then becomes coldly triumphant when they understand that the future itself can be ambushed.

## The Ahrimani Pack

- Source: Ahrimani Prime wiki lore describing Prime as leader of the surviving Ahrimani.
- Reference image: None for this issue; the pack appears as supporting warband figures who share Prime's purple reptilian features but remain visually subordinate to Prime.
- Stable visible identifiers: Purple reptilian humanoid soldiers with yellow eyes, rough cloth wraps, primitive blades or spears, sand-streaked war paint, crouched predatory movement, and disciplined formations around Prime.
- Story use: The pack represents the survival stakes behind Prime's conquest. Their fear of extinction lets Prime justify brutality as destiny.

## Ancient Monster Hunters

- Source: Ahrimani Prime wiki backstory.
- Reference image: None for this issue; they appear as unnamed human hunters in ancient armor and cloaks.
- Stable visible identifiers: Human monster hunters with bronze and iron weapons, sealed goggles, layered travel cloaks, sand-scarred shields, trap chains, and bright hunter sigils.
- Story use: They repeatedly defeat Prime's direct attacks, forcing Prime to discover that superior timing can beat superior preparation.

## Ahrimani-Occupied Future Earth

- Source: Ahrimani Prime wiki lore about the pack arriving from the past and overtaking a future that no longer remembers them.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/ahrimani-occupied-future-earth-setting.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Saturated modern anime dystopian Earth under violet skies, ruined skyscrapers, shattered highways, purple sandstorms, glowing time-rift scars, Ahrimani banners, frightened resistance hideouts, and abandoned technology half-buried in violet sand.
- Story use: The future proves Prime's central tactic. Humanity's memory gap becomes the battlefield Prime chooses.

## Purple Sand

- Source: Ahrimani Prime wiki power lore.
- Series reference image: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/reference-images/purple-sand-key-item.png`
- Stable visible identifiers: Luminous violet grains, hourglass-shaped storm trails, glittering particles orbiting claws and weapons, translucent time-ripple rings, fractured ghost images of past and future positions, and deep purple glow whenever Prime bends time.
- Story use: The sand begins as a tempting combat enhancer, then becomes the issue's defining strategic weapon when Prime uses it to move the surviving Ahrimani into the future.
