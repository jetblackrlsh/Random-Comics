# Key Item Descriptions

## Key Item: Prism Plate

- First appears: Issue #1, "Armor Against the Eye"
- Visual identifiers: Angular rainbow-silver armor plate sized for Armor On's chest, glasslike layered metal, bright internal glow, prism facets, luminous blue attachment seams when bonded to Armor On's existing armor.
- Continuity notes: Armor On wins the Prism Plate from the Mirror Champion after learning to use astral perception instead of raw armor absorption. It becomes a visible new layer over their silver chest plate by the end of Issue #1.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/prism-plate-key-item.png`

## Key Item: Armor On's Incomplete Armor Set

- First appears: Issue #1, "Armor Against the Eye"
- Visual identifiers: Modular silver armor over a glowing blue-energy body, initially only a chest plate with partial shoulder and forearm hints, later gaining the Prism Plate as a rainbow-silver overlay.
- Continuity notes: Armor On believes completing this armor set will make them invincible enough to survive the hidden watcher. Each new armor piece should feel like both a trophy and a shield against fear.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/armor-on-character.png`

## Key Item: Jane's Enchanted Family Armor

- First appears: Issue #2, "The Honor of Falling"
- Visual identifiers: Polished silver knight armor shaped for aerial superhero action, white cape, black sunglasses as part of Jane's heroic look, inherited crest at the chest, luminous silver-blue seams, and visible scratches or cracks after heavy impacts.
- Continuity notes: The armor has passed through Captain Lady Jane's family for generations and grants flight, enhanced strength, and durability. In Issue #2 Jane discovers that it can ground a supernatural attack when she accepts the impact to protect civilians.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/captain-lady-jane-character.png`

## Key Item: Gravity Gallows

- First appears: Issue #2, "The Honor of Falling"
- Visual identifiers: Supernatural black-violet gallows-shaped gravity sigil, chains of dark light, floating stone fragments, clock-bell silhouettes, heavy downward distortion rings, and impact shadows that drag the sky toward the ground.
- Continuity notes: The Gravity Gallows attacks High Harbor City by turning boastful pride into literal weight. Jane destroys its manifestation by grounding it through the harbor leyline anchor and her family armor.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/gravity-gallows-key-item.png`
