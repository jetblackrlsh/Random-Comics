# Issue Summaries

## Issue #1: Armor Against the Eye

- Folder: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/issues/issue-01/`
- Logline: Armor On invades the Mirror Bastion to claim the Prism Plate, but only wins after realizing the armor they need most is the power to see fear clearly.
- Plot summary: Armor On races through the Fragmented Digital Sky toward the Mirror Bastion, convinced the Prism Plate will bring them closer to an invincible armor set. The Mirror Champion defeats their first attempts by reflecting Armor On's pride and fear back at them. When the Watching Eye appears in every mirrored surface, Armor On stops chasing raw protection and uses astral perception to identify the real opening, winning the duel and bonding the Prism Plate to their armor.
- Continuity changes: Armor On gains the Prism Plate and learns that astral perception is not only a warning system but a strategic power. The Watching Eye remains unresolved and aware of Armor On's growth.
- New characters: Armor On, The Watching Eye, Mirror Champion.
- New settings: Fragmented Digital Sky, Mirror Bastion.
- New key items: Prism Plate, Armor On's Incomplete Armor Set.
- Reference images added: `armor-on-character.png`, `watching-eye-character.png`, `mirror-champion-character.png`, `fragmented-digital-sky-setting.png`, `mirror-bastion-setting.png`, `prism-plate-key-item.png`.

## Issue #2: The Honor of Falling

- Folder: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/issues/issue-02/`
- Logline: Captain Lady Jane faces a gravity-warping siege that turns her own arrogance against her, forcing the undefeated heir to discover that honor means standing back up for the city.
- Plot summary: Captain Lady Jane patrols High Harbor City as the latest heir to an undefeated line of armored superheroes. When the Gravity Gallows descends over the harbor, Jane tries to prove the family legend through brute strength and reckless flight, but the Gallows turns every boast into crushing weight. After nearly failing the civilians behind her, Jane remembers that her mother taught her to defend the weak, not preserve an image. She deliberately takes the fall at the harbor leyline anchor, grounds the Gallows through her cracked enchanted armor, and rises as a stronger protector.
- Continuity changes: Captain Lady Jane enters the series with a clearer understanding that her family armor's honor is protective rather than performative. High Harbor City, Jane's enchanted family armor, and the Gravity Gallows are introduced.
- New characters: Captain Lady Jane.
- New settings: High Harbor City.
- New key items: Jane's Enchanted Family Armor, Gravity Gallows.
- Reference images added: `captain-lady-jane-character.png`, `high-harbor-city-setting.png`, `gravity-gallows-key-item.png`.

## Issue #3: Feathers for the Crowd

- Folder: `series/dream-anime-chronicles/issues/issue-03/`
- Logline: Black Feather, the winged champion of Celebrity Deathmatch Island, nearly loses their crown when fame makes them fight for the cameras instead of the hunt.
- Plot summary: Black Feather enters the ruined haunted boarding school arena as Celebrity Deathmatch Island's reigning champion, feeding on the crowd's fear and adoration. A masked challenger uses the show's drones, spotlights, and cable traps to punish every theatrical wing spread and camera-facing attack. After Black Feather realizes the applause has trained them into predictable performance, they cut the lights, fold into the shadows, and use enhanced sight, hearing, flight, and claws to win from silence.
- Continuity changes: Black Feather enters the series as a fame-driven human-bird hybrid who learns that controlled silence can command the crowd more powerfully than constant spectacle. Deathmatch Island's haunted boarding school arena is introduced as Black Feather's home battlefield.
- New characters: Black Feather.
- New settings: Deathmatch Island Haunted Boarding School Arena.
- New key items: None.
- Reference images added: `black-feather-character.png`, `deathmatch-island-school-arena-setting.png`.
