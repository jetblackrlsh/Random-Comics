# Setting Descriptions

## Setting: Fragmented Digital Sky

- First appears: Issue #1, "Armor Against the Eye"
- Visual identifiers: Vast saturated blue and violet sky filled with floating islands, glowing circuitry in the clouds, pixel-like star fields, luminous portal rings, drifting ruins, impossible horizon lines, and bright anime energy trails.
- Continuity notes: Armor On travels through layered virtual worlds connected by this sky. It should feel like a dreamlike network of arenas, realms, and floating pathways.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/fragmented-digital-sky-setting.png`

## Setting: Mirror Bastion

- First appears: Issue #1, "Armor Against the Eye"
- Visual identifiers: Floating crystalline fortress suspended in the Fragmented Digital Sky, mirror walls, rainbow prism bridges, reflective battle platforms, glowing blue data runes, and fractured reflections that show different possible versions of a warrior.
- Continuity notes: The Mirror Bastion houses the Prism Plate and tests challengers by reflecting the fear behind their ambition.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/mirror-bastion-setting.png`

## Setting: High Harbor City

- First appears: Issue #2, "The Honor of Falling"
- Visual identifiers: Bright modern anime coastal city with tall glass towers, harbor bridges, rooftop gardens, blue ocean light, heroic skyline, public plazas, and silver knight-family banners on memorial architecture.
- Continuity notes: High Harbor City relies on Captain Lady Jane's family legacy for protection, but its people need Jane's responsibility more than her perfect record.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/high-harbor-city-setting.png`
