# Character Descriptions

## Character: Armor On

- First appears: Issue #1, "Armor Against the Eye"
- Role: protagonist, astral warrior, armor collector, traveler through virtual worlds.
- Visual identifiers: Bald faceless humanoid made of glowing blue energy, smooth featureless head, intense cyan inner light, silver armor chest plate, incomplete modular armor set that can gain new pieces, luminous blue aura, confident forward-leaning battle posture, gestures that shift between proud challenge and anxious vigilance.
- Continuity notes: Armor On flies through virtual realms, opens portals between worlds, senses when the hidden watcher is near, and can absorb armor pieces after defeating warriors. Their pursuit of the ultimate armor is fueled by pride, collector obsession, and fear of the unknown entity that watches them.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/armor-on-character.png`

## Character: The Watching Eye

- First appears: Issue #1, "Armor Against the Eye"
- Role: hidden observer, long-term threat, source of Armor On's fear.
- Visual identifiers: Huge unblinking eye partly hidden in darkness, gooey black-violet surface, wet feathered edges, pale gold iris, impossible scale, tendrils of shadow and drifting feathers, usually seen through reflections, portals, or the sky between virtual worlds.
- Continuity notes: Armor On can sense the entity watching but cannot locate or confront it directly. The Eye's presence pushes Armor On to seek stronger armor, but it may also be testing whether fear controls them.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/watching-eye-character.png`

## Character: Mirror Champion

- First appears: Issue #1, "Armor Against the Eye"
- Role: obstacle warrior guarding the Prism Plate in the Mirror Bastion.
- Visual identifiers: Tall armored anime warrior with faceless mirrored helmet, crystalline shoulder guards, reflective silver-and-rainbow armor, prism lance, cape made of angular glass shards, calm defensive stance.
- Continuity notes: The Mirror Champion is defeated by Armor On in Issue #1 and loses the Prism Plate, but survives the duel. The character may recur as a rival or witness to Armor On's growth.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/mirror-champion-character.png`

## Character: Captain Lady Jane

- First appears: Issue #2, "The Honor of Falling"
- Role: protagonist, superhero in training, armored heir of an undefeated warrior family.
- Visual identifiers: Young adult anime woman with black hair in a high ponytail, black sunglasses, polished silver enchanted knight armor, white cape, athletic build, confident grin, aggressive forward-leaning battle poses, and a family crest glowing at the armor chest.
- Continuity notes: Jane's enchanted family armor grants flight, enhanced strength, and durability. She begins obsessed with preserving her family's undefeated reputation, but Issue #2 teaches her that honor means protecting people even when doing so requires being knocked down in public.
- Reference image: `../reference-images/captain-lady-jane-character.png`
