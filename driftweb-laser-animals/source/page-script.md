# Page Script

Global style for every page: full realistic candid photo-comic page, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, complete page with multiple panels or a splash panel, photoreal documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism look, natural imperfect framing, believable real-world lighting, realistic anthropomorphic animals and practical creature detail, photoreal action VFX, clean rectangular caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, no blank text boxes. All readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art. Do not include page numbers, panel labels, caption numbers, caption labels, or prompt scaffolding in the art. No caption may repeat another caption's wording on the same page. This edition has 8 pages: cover plus 7 story pages.

## Page 01 - Cover

Story role: Present the collision between Driftweb's city-scale heroism, the luminous cloud guardians, and the monster kingdom below.

Layout: Dramatic realistic photo-comic cover splash. Driftweb swings upward beneath his circular silver web parachute through storm clouds. Above him, Aurion, Vela, and Serein stand on the edge of Nimbus Ark with their eyes glowing cyan, violet, and amber. Below, King Hollowcore's enormous drill erupts from a bank as shadow monsters swarm through dust. The transformed All-Beast looms faintly in the storm background as a threatening silhouette. Strong vertical composition linking underground, city, cloud hero, and sky civilization.

Caption boxes/text inside art: Large title text: "DRIFTWEB AND THE LASER ANIMALS". Small caption box: "One accident above the clouds united two kinds of heroes—and divided them over the price of victory."

## Page 02 - The City Above The Clouds

Story role: Establish Driftweb, his web-parachute patrol, and his accidental discovery of Nimbus Ark.

Layout: Five panels. Panel 1: Driftweb launches from a glass tower rooftop at late morning, silver web parachute opening above him. Panel 2: he glides high over the city by shifting body weight beneath the circular canopy. Panel 3: an unexpected updraft tears him into dense white storm clouds. Panel 4: through thinning mist he sees impossible rounded towers and animal silhouettes standing on luminous solid clouds. Panel 5: after his parachute slips away, he fires a silver web line into the cloudstone and pulls himself onto Nimbus Ark as Aurion, Vela, and Serein turn toward him.

Caption boxes inside art: "Driftweb patrolled farther than any web line could swing by letting the wind carry a parachute of his own silk." "One violent updraft lifted him through the cloud ceiling before he could cut free." "The clouds were hiding a city—and the city was solid enough to catch his web."

## Page 03 - The Mistaken Monster

Story role: Turn discovery into conflict, demonstrate Driftweb's danger sense and restraint, and let Aurion stop the misunderstanding.

Layout: Six action panels with clear reading order. Panel 1: Vela, Aurion, and Serein surround Driftweb in a cloud plaza, their eyes igniting. Panel 2: violet, cyan-gold, and amber beams cross where Driftweb stood an instant earlier as he flips away. Panel 3: Driftweb's copper goggles reflect the next beam trajectories just before they fire. Panel 4: he dodges across cloudstone railings and shoots silver webbing over Vela's and Serein's eyes. Panel 5: Driftweb hangs from an arch with open empty hands, visibly refusing to punch or injure anyone. Panel 6: Aurion raises one claw to halt the others while recognizing the visitor's restraint.

Caption boxes inside art: "The Laser Animals had never seen a human reach Nimbus Ark. They had seen monsters wear stranger shapes." "Driftweb's danger sense showed him each shot a heartbeat before it existed." "He answered lethal light with blindfolds of silk, proving escape—not harm—was his goal." "Aurion stopped the attack when he realized their supposed monster was protecting them from himself."

## Page 04 - Two Kinds Of Guardians

Story role: Resolve the misunderstanding, explain the Laser Animals' hidden mission, and launch the shared crisis.

Layout: Four large panels with roomy captions. Panel 1: Aurion removes webbing from Vela's eyes while Driftweb stands cautiously with mask lowered enough to reveal his face and humanity. Panel 2: a prismatic monitor shows Laser Animals descending rainbow bridges to repel shadow monsters from the unaware city below. Panel 3: Driftweb and Aurion clasp forearm to forearm as Vela and Serein relax, recognizing common purpose. Panel 4: the monitor flashes an urgent image of King Hollowcore's drill breaking into a bank vault while monsters loot cash carts; a rainbow bridge ignites beyond the plaza.

Caption boxes inside art: "Aurion admitted that fear had made both sides name the stranger a monster." "For generations, his people had crossed secret rainbow bridges to keep underground beasts away from an unsuspecting humanity." "Driftweb fought different villains, but he understood the same promise: power existed to keep people safe." "Their first conversation ended when King Hollowcore drilled into the largest bank in the city."

## Page 05 - Rainbow Raid

Story role: Unite the heroes in a clear bank battle and trap Hollowcore before he can retreat.

Layout: Six fast panels. Panel 1: Driftweb rides down a vertical rainbow bridge beside the three Laser Animals toward the bank. Panel 2: they crash through dust above the ruptured vault as monsters load money and gold into carts. Panel 3: Vela cuts a falling support beam with precise violet lasers while Serein shields civilians and blasts a stone beast backward. Panel 4: Aurion battles monsters beside Driftweb, who webs several attackers together without injuring them. Panel 5: King Hollowcore orders a retreat and climbs into his black-and-bronze drill vehicle. Panel 6: Driftweb blankets the spiral drill bit in dense silver silk while the Laser Animals surround the immobilized machine.

Caption boxes inside art: "A bridge of hard light carried the new allies from heaven to the vault in seconds." "The Laser Animals broke the monster charge while Driftweb bound every creature he could spare." "Hollowcore fled when the heroes became more than his army could overwhelm." "He could not burrow home after Driftweb turned the drill into a cocoon."

## Page 06 - The All-Beast

Story role: Reverse the apparent victory by transforming Hollowcore and showing why ordinary attacks cannot stop him.

Layout: Five large cinematic panels. Panel 1: Cornered Hollowcore holds up a syringe of swirling green-black monster extract made from his subjects' stolen powers. Panel 2: he injects himself as his burgundy coat tears and his body grows into the huge black-violet All-Beast. Panel 3: Aurion's laser burns through the creature's shoulder, but green luminous tissue regenerates immediately. Panel 4: the All-Beast vanishes into black dust and reappears behind Vela and Serein, hurling them across the ruined vault. Panel 5: Driftweb and the battered Aurion stand alone before the teleporting monster, Driftweb's right shoulder torn and Aurion's cape scorched.

Caption boxes inside art: "Hollowcore had distilled the abilities of his own subjects into one desperate dose." "The king became the All-Beast: every monster he ruled compressed into one body." "Laser wounds healed. Stone claws struck faster than sight. Black dust carried him from place to place." "Only Driftweb's warning sense could feel where the monster would appear next."

## Page 07 - The Opening

Story role: Deliver the climax through teamwork, then make Aurion's lethal choice explicit and emotionally consequential.

Layout: Six panels. Panel 1: close view of Driftweb's copper goggles as multiple likely attack directions reflect across the lenses. Panel 2: instead of aiming at the visible All-Beast, Driftweb fires a thick web net into apparently empty space across the vault. Panel 3: the monster teleports directly into that waiting web and becomes bound for one crucial second. Panel 4: Driftweb turns toward Aurion expecting capture while Aurion's cyan eyes flare to white-hot maximum power. Panel 5: Aurion unleashes a vast cyan-gold beam that engulfs the All-Beast; Driftweb shields his face from the blast. Panel 6: the light clears, leaving scorched stone, drifting ash, Hollowcore's cracked amber goggles, and no body capable of regenerating.

Caption boxes inside art: "Driftweb trusted the danger that had not happened yet." "His web waited at the only place Hollowcore believed was still empty." "The trap created one second for Aurion to end the fight." "Driftweb expected a prison. Aurion chose certainty." "Nothing remained for the All-Beast to rebuild."

## Page 08 - What Victory Leaves Behind

Story role: Resolve the immediate adventure, preserve Driftweb's moral conflict, and reveal the monster heir as a restrained sequel hook.

Layout: Four large panels. Panel 1: At dawn outside the damaged bank, Aurion, Vela, and Serein thank Driftweb before stepping onto a rising rainbow bridge; their armor is damaged but they celebrate the monster war's apparent end. Panel 2: Driftweb stands alone on a rooftop in his ash-covered torn suit, holding a scorched strand of web and watching the rainbow vanish into the clouds. Panel 3: deep underground, surviving monsters kneel in a wet basalt chamber around Morra, whose four red eyes are wet with grief as one claw rests protectively over her pregnant plated abdomen. Panel 4: Morra lifts Hollowcore's broken crown over the unborn heir while green crystal lamps reveal the silent monster army behind her.

Caption boxes inside art: "The Laser Animals returned home believing an ancient war had finally ended." "Driftweb knew the city was safe, yet he could not decide when a person became only a monster." "Far below the celebration, Hollowcore's survivors gathered around Morra and the life she carried." "Their king was dead. His heir—and their hunger for revenge—were only beginning."
