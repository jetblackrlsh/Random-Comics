Source: Jet Black's Plaud recording from August 11, 2026, beginning at 9:25 AM America/Chicago.

An original spider-themed hero patrols by riding the wind beneath a web parachute. A powerful updraft carries him above the clouds, where he discovers a hidden civilization of anthropomorphic animals with laser vision. The animals mistake him for one of the shadow monsters they secretly fight, but his precognitive danger sense and spider-like agility let him evade their eye beams without hurting them. Once the misunderstanding is resolved, both sides recognize that they are heroes protecting the same world.

An alarm reveals that the King of the Underground and his monsters are robbing a bank with a giant drill. The spider hero rides a rainbow bridge down with the laser animals and joins the battle. When the heroes trap the villain's drill, he injects himself with a serum made from the extracted abilities of his monster subjects. He becomes an ultimate monster that can teleport, regenerate, and overwhelm nearly everyone. The spider hero predicts the monster king's next teleport destination and webs the spot before he arrives. The leader of the laser animals uses that brief opening to fire a maximum-power beam that destroys the villain completely.

The laser animals celebrate the end of their war and return to the clouds, but the spider hero is disturbed that their victory required killing a person, even after he became a monster. Beneath the city, the surviving monsters prepare for revenge. One of them carries the underground king's unborn heir, who may one day become the next monster king.
