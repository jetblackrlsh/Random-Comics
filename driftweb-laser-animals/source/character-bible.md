# Character Bible

## Global Photo-Comic Look

Every page is a full realistic candid photo-comic page assembled from documentary, handheld, phone-photo, or photojournalistic images. The city should feel tangible: concrete rooftops, glass towers, storm drains, bank marble, emergency lights, dust, and practical debris. Nimbus Ark should look like a secret physical civilization built on solid luminous clouds, with translucent cloudstone arches and prismatic technology. Superpowers and creatures must look photoreal, as if caught by real cameras with believable composited effects. Use clean rectangular caption boxes only, with all readable story text generated directly inside the page art. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, watermarks, logos, blank text boxes, page numbers, caption labels, or panel labels.

## Driftweb

- Role: Protagonist and spider-themed city hero.
- Identity: Black man, age 24, lean athletic build, dark brown skin, angular oval face, hazel-brown eyes, short neat locs visible above his mask.
- Suit: Graphite-gray close-fitting tactical suit with electric-turquoise seam lines and copper forearm bracers; stylized silver eight-spoke web emblem across the chest; flexible black climbing gloves and boots with tiny gripping pads; copper multi-lens goggles; graphite half-mask covering nose, mouth, and jaw while leaving hair and upper cheeks visible.
- Signature equipment: Twin copper wrist launchers that fire thick silver adhesive silk. His web parachute is a broad translucent silver canopy with dark radial strands, curved like a dandelion seed or circular parasail rather than wings.
- Powers and movement: Wall-crawling, spider-like flexibility and agility, strong adhesive silk, and a precognitive danger sense visualized only through alert posture, rapid head turns, and subtle turquoise flickers reflected in his goggles—not floating symbols or text.
- Personality and posture: Curious, acrobatic, verbally direct, and committed to restraint. He protects first and processes fear afterward. In combat he dodges, binds, redirects, and traps rather than striking to injure.
- Continuity: Keep his dark skin, short locs, copper goggles and bracers, graphite-turquoise suit, silver chest emblem, and circular web parachute consistent. After the bank battle, retain ash on the suit and a small tear at the right shoulder.

## Aurion

- Role: Leader of the Laser Animals and Driftweb's principal ally.
- Species and build: Photoreal anthropomorphic adult male lion, about seven feet tall, powerful but noble build, realistic golden-tawny fur, full darker-gold mane, feline muzzle, rounded ears, large clawed hands, digitigrade legs, expressive cyan eyes.
- Outfit: Sleeveless sky-blue cloudstone cuirass with white-silver edging, short white cape, silver belt, bare furred arms, and a simple circular sun emblem made from inlaid gold metal with no readable text.
- Power: Cyan-gold laser vision. At low intensity it is narrow and precise; at maximum intensity it becomes a wide white-hot beam surrounded by cyan and gold bloom.
- Personality and posture: Calm, protective, commanding, and compassionate toward his people. He sees monsters as an existential threat and accepts lethal force more readily than Driftweb.
- Continuity: Keep golden fur, darker mane, cyan eyes, sky-blue armor, white cape, sun emblem, and large noble silhouette consistent. After fighting the All-Beast, his cape is torn and his armor is scorched.

## Vela

- Role: Fast-moving Laser Animal scout.
- Species and build: Photoreal anthropomorphic adult female hare, five feet eight inches tall, slim athletic build, lavender-gray fur, long upright ears with white inner fur, violet eyes.
- Outfit: White cropped cloudstone jacket over a plum tactical bodysuit, silver wrist guards, no logos or readable text.
- Power: Narrow violet laser vision used for cutting obstacles and precise attacks.
- Personality and posture: Alert, skeptical, fast, and brave. She is the first to attack Driftweb and the first to lower her guard when Aurion recognizes his restraint.
- Continuity: Keep lavender-gray fur, long ears, violet eyes, white jacket, plum suit, and violet eye beams consistent.

## Serein

- Role: Laser Animal defender and heavy rescue specialist.
- Species and build: Photoreal anthropomorphic adult male fox, six feet four inches tall, broad athletic build, turquoise-blue fur with white muzzle and chest, large bushy tail, amber eyes.
- Outfit: Pale-gold shoulder harness, white utility belt, navy trousers, no shirt, no readable text.
- Power: Amber laser vision with heavy concussive impact.
- Personality and posture: Warm, vigilant, physically protective. He shields civilians and carries injured teammates.
- Continuity: Keep turquoise fur, white muzzle and chest, bushy tail, amber eyes, pale-gold harness, navy trousers, and amber eye beams consistent.

## King Hollowcore

- Role: Human underground monarch and primary antagonist.
- Human form: Man in his early 50s, short and broad, pale olive skin, shaved head, heavy gray mustache, deep-set dark eyes behind round amber mining goggles.
- Outfit and equipment: Burgundy military mining coat with brass piping, black armored trousers, heavy boots, bronze drill gauntlet on the right hand, black cane topped with an uncut green crystal. He commands a cylindrical black-and-bronze bank-burrowing vehicle with a massive spiral drill.
- Personality and posture: Proud, theatrical, resentful of the surface, and willing to consume his own subjects' power rather than accept defeat.
- Continuity: Keep shaved head, gray mustache, amber goggles, burgundy coat, bronze drill gauntlet, crystal cane, and compact broad build consistent until transformation.

## The All-Beast

- Role: Hollowcore's transformed state after injecting concentrated monster extract.
- Creature design: Twelve-foot photoreal chimera with Hollowcore's amber goggles fused above deep-set glowing green eyes; massive black-violet scaled torso; two curved ram horns; broad batlike wings; oversized mole claws; a thick reptilian tail; patches of regenerating green bioluminescent tissue; black-dust teleportation distortion around the body.
- Behavior: Teleports in short bursts, regenerates from laser damage, and attacks with overwhelming physical force. Hollowcore's gray mustache remains visible beneath the monstrous muzzle so the person inside is still recognizable.
- Continuity: Keep the same black-violet scales, ram horns, bat wings, mole claws, reptilian tail, green regeneration glow, amber goggles, and gray mustache in every transformed appearance.

## Morra

- Role: Surviving monster and mother of Hollowcore's unborn heir.
- Creature design: Eight-foot photoreal feminine monster with charcoal skin, long ivory horns sweeping backward, muscular arms, four glowing red eyes, long dark braided tendrils, and a visibly pregnant abdomen protected by layered stone-gray plates.
- Outfit and prop: Torn dark-red ceremonial sash and Hollowcore's broken brass crown held in one claw.
- Personality and posture: Grieving, controlled, regal, and vengeful rather than feral.
- Continuity: Keep charcoal skin, four red eyes, backward ivory horns, braided tendrils, pregnant plated abdomen, dark-red sash, and broken crown consistent.

## Nimbus Ark

- Secret cloud civilization made from solid luminous cloudstone above the storm layer. White vapor rolls around transparent bridges, rounded towers, prismatic monitors, and open plazas. A vertical rainbow bridge forms as a physically believable band of refracted hard light connecting the city to the surface.

## Underground Kingdom and Bank

- Hollowcore's monsters inhabit wet basalt tunnels reinforced with rusted mining machinery, green crystal lamps, pipes, and scavenged vault metal. The bank is an urban marble-and-glass building with a deep steel vault, burst flooring, alarms, emergency red light, cash carts, and Hollowcore's black-and-bronze drill vehicle jammed through the vault wall.
