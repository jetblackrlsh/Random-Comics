# Driftweb and the Laser Animals

## Title

Driftweb and the Laser Animals

## Publication Date

2026-08-12

## Logline

When a web-parachuting street hero is blown into a secret kingdom above the clouds, he must survive its laser-eyed animal guardians, earn their trust, and help them stop an underground monster king—only to discover that his new allies define heroism more ruthlessly than he does.

## Central Conflict

Driftweb and the Laser Animals are all protectors, but fear makes each side initially see the other as a monster. Their shared battle against King Hollowcore proves that their abilities complement one another, while Hollowcore's transformation into the All-Beast forces them to choose between restraint and lethal force. Driftweb can create the opening that saves the city, but Aurion decides what to do with it.

## Theme

Seeing the same enemy does not mean sharing the same morality. Cooperation can reveal genuine kinship while also exposing a difference too important to ignore.

## Cast

- Driftweb: A young Black street hero who climbs walls, launches adhesive silk, senses danger seconds before it happens, and patrols by steering a broad web parachute through city wind currents.
- Aurion: A golden anthropomorphic lion and calm commander of the Laser Animals, whose cyan-gold laser vision can escalate from surgical precision to overwhelming force.
- Vela and Serein: A lavender hare and turquoise fox who fight beside Aurion and help defend humanity from monsters while keeping their cloud civilization secret.
- King Hollowcore: A bitter underground monarch who uses a colossal drill and a monster army to rob surface wealth for his hidden kingdom, then consumes his subjects' extracted powers to become the All-Beast.
- Morra: A surviving horned monster who carries Hollowcore's unborn heir and turns the apparent ending of the monster war into the beginning of a future reckoning.

## Emotional Arc

Driftweb begins exhilarated by an accidental discovery that makes his familiar city feel wondrous again. The Laser Animals' attack turns wonder into fear, but his refusal to hurt them earns trust and the joyful possibility of new heroic friends. Fighting beside them validates that hope until Aurion annihilates the transformed Hollowcore. Driftweb ends proud that the city was saved but disturbed by the cost, watching his new allies disappear before he can decide whether he truly belongs beside them.

## Ending

The Laser Animals return to Nimbus Ark believing their ancient monster war is over. Driftweb remains on a rooftop at dawn, holding a scorched thread of web and questioning whether a person stops mattering after becoming monstrous. Far below, Morra places a claw over the life growing inside her as surviving monsters kneel around Hollowcore's broken crown. Their king is dead, but his heir—and the desire for revenge—remain.
