# Page Script

## Global Page Rules

- Every generated image is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel labels, caption labels, logos, or watermarks.
- Every quoted caption below must appear verbatim inside the generated page art, with readable typography and the entire rectangular box comfortably inside a generous safe margin.
- Use three or four large panels per story page so the long captions remain readable.
- Keep action direction and location changes obvious. Do not make the reader infer time jumps or causes.
- Use the two Ruby reference images for every adult Ruby appearance, selecting the state shown on that page.

## Page 01 - Cover

**Story role:** Present Ruby's core conflict: one superpowered woman in front, an entire empowered neighborhood behind her.

**Composition:** Full-page realistic superhero poster photograph. Transformed Ruby stands in the foreground with feet apart, loose fists, huge spiky black hair, vivid pink fur, tail, crimson cropped top, charcoal pants, golden bands, and blazing golden aura. Behind her, ordinary students of many ages stand together outside the community dojo. A faint human-state reflection of Ruby appears in a cracked dojo window, preserving the dual identity without duplicating her physically.

**Readable text:**

- Large exact title: "RUBY SUN"
- Subtitle: "THE POWER WE SHARE"
- Small creator line: "A RANDOM COMICS ONE-SHOT BY NICHOLAS ALEXANDER BENSON"

## Page 02 - Learning to Fall

**Story role:** Establish six-year-old Ruby, her father, the dojo, and the lesson that frames the whole story.

**Layout:** Four large panels in clear top-to-bottom order.

**Caption text:**

1. "When Ruby Sun was six years old, her father taught her how to fall. She hated it."
2. "Every lesson ended with Ruby flat on the dojo floor while he calmly told her, 'Again.'"
3. "He taught punches, kicks, balance, breathing, and restraint. Falling was different because anybody could be knocked down."
4. "What mattered was knowing what to do next. Ruby always stood, brushed herself off, and charged him again."

**Visual beats:** Father throws young Ruby safely; she glares from the floor at wooden beams; he offers a hand; she refuses the easy hand, rolls up, and charges with determined joy.

## Page 03 - One Man Against the Mob

**Story role:** Explain the neighborhood protection racket, the father's refusal, and why skill alone fails when the mob escalates.

**Layout:** Four panels, with the final gunfire aftermath as the largest.

**Caption text:**

1. "The dojo stood in a hardworking neighborhood where the mob demanded protection money from families who already owned little. Ruby's father called it extortion."
2. "When enforcers came to change his mind, he defeated them without cruelty and stood between them and everyone else."
3. "For a few glorious days, Ruby believed one brilliant martial artist had settled everything. Then the mob escalated beyond a fair fight."
4. "Her father did not survive protecting the dojo. Ruby learned that even the strongest guardian could become one terrible point of failure."

**Visual beats:** Exterior neighborhood and mob collection; controlled dojo redirection; warm final memory closing the dojo; adult Ruby at her father's peaceful memorial. The lethal event remains entirely off-panel.

## Page 04 - The Street Becomes a School

**Story role:** Show Ruby's survival, showmanship, first students, found family, and the fear that sends her searching for another answer.

**Layout:** Four panels moving from outdoor markets to a quiet family home.

**Caption text:**

1. "Orphaned Ruby survived by performing the only skill she could sell: impossible kicks, flips, balances, and martial-arts forms that stopped crowded markets."
2. "Coins became meals. Applause became confidence. Then strangers began asking how to escape a grab, face someone larger, or protect a friend."
3. "One student became ten, and a family she trained offered her a room. Belonging returned—along with the terror of losing people again."
4. "The mob was extorting her new family too. Ruby stared at her hands and wondered how fists were supposed to stop bullets."

**Visual beats:** Teen Ruby street performance; informal alley lesson; warm dinner with chosen family; adult Ruby alone after training staring at her open hands.

## Page 05 - The Mountain Answer

**Story role:** Make Ruby's refusal of guns and desperate mountain journey completely explicit.

**Layout:** Three large cinematic panels.

**Caption text:**

1. "Ruby could buy a gun, but the thought made her sick. She wanted an answer stronger than the mob's weapons without becoming someone who worshiped superior firepower."
2. "She remembered the mountain that had named her father's dojo and climbed toward the teacher hidden inside his old stories."
3. "She packed badly, got lost, ran out of food, and collapsed above the clouds—still searching for proof that another kind of strength existed."

**Visual beats:** Gun-shop window reflected across Ruby's conflicted face as she walks away; steep ascent; exhausted collapse near an ancient temple gate.

## Page 06 - Her Father's Master

**Story role:** Reveal the old master, Ruby's lineage, the bands, and the father's refusal.

**Layout:** Four panels inside the mountain temple and courtyard.

**Caption text:**

1. "Ruby woke inside a hidden temple, facing an elderly pink-furred monkey master with golden fire dancing around his fingers."
2. "He had been expecting his old pupil's daughter. Everything Ruby's father knew had begun on this mountain."
3. "The master trained Ruby until every shortcut she had taught herself fell away. Then she told him that guns had killed her father."
4. "He revealed the golden bands her father had refused: a supernatural inheritance that could grant speed, agility, a monkey-like form, and living golden flame."

**Visual beats:** Astonished wake-up; master demonstrating balance and fire; rigorous training montage; bands held solemnly between them.

## Page 07 - Power and Wisdom

**Story role:** State both philosophies, show Ruby challenging the master, and depict her transformation.

**Layout:** Four panels, with transformation as the largest bottom panel.

**Caption text:**

1. "Ruby agreed to wear the bands, but not to depend on them. Martial arts had to remain the foundation beneath supernatural force."
2. "The master warned that weapons and technology would keep evolving. Repeating her father's choices exactly might also repeat his ending."
3. "Ruby answered, 'If your power matters so much, why are you sitting on a mountain while people below are suffering?' The master laughed because her challenge carried real wisdom."
4. "When Ruby accepted the bands, pink fur swept across her body, a tail unfurled, and golden fire erupted around the same impossible smirk she had worn since childhood."

**Visual beats:** Ruby crossing her arms; master pointing toward changing city weapons below; their tense face-off becoming mutual smiles; first transformation.

## Page 08 - A Dojo That Multiplies Power

**Story role:** Show Ruby's return, mass teaching, transformed interventions, and the mob's growing fear.

**Layout:** Four panels across the neighborhood.

**Caption text:**

1. "Ruby returned and founded a dojo that would never pay protection money. She invited anyone willing to learn."
2. "Store owners, restaurant workers, teenagers, parents, and delivery drivers practiced escaping holds, protecting one another, and refusing to stand alone."
3. "When ordinary technique was not enough, mobsters met a pink-furred woman whose golden fire turned their intimidation into humiliating retreat."
4. "Fear had always been the mob's weapon. Ruby gave it back to them—and stories of her golden bands reached their leader, Vincent Vale."

**Visual beats:** Storefront dojo opening; crowded mixed-age class; transformed Ruby disarming armed enforcers without lethal force; Vale studying surveillance photos of the bands.

## Page 09 - The Shortcut

**Story role:** Define Vale's false belief, stage the raid, and show Ruby surrender the bands to save a child.

**Layout:** Four tense panels in Ruby's dojo.

**Caption text:**

1. "Vale had never trained or fought for himself. Other people carried out his threats, suffered his risks, and made his power look effortless."
2. "He saw Ruby's bands as the perfect shortcut. Why spend years building discipline when strength could simply be worn?"
3. "Vale raided the dojo with overwhelming force. While Ruby protected her students, one of his men trapped a young student in the office."
4. "Given the choice between the bands and her student, Ruby did not hesitate. Her fur, tail, and golden fire vanished as she tossed Vale the power he thought made her whole."

**Visual beats:** Vale directing enforcers; surveillance photos of Ruby and the bands; a safe college-age student isolated behind a glass office door; human Ruby removing both bands and throwing them across the room as the student is released.

## Page 10 - Stronger, Faster, Untrained

**Story role:** Show Vale's transformation, why stolen power is not mastery, and Ruby's physical low point.

**Layout:** Four action panels with very clear spatial continuity.

**Caption text:**

1. "The bands transformed Vale. Pink fur, a wild tail, supernatural muscle, and golden flame convinced him that Ruby had never been anything more than borrowed power."
2. "Vale was faster and stronger, but he had no training. Every motion announced itself, every step surrendered balance, and every burst exposed another mistake."
3. "Ruby could read those mistakes, but she could not match his raw force. She prepared to survive by moving instead of overpowering him."
4. "On the mat, Ruby remembered being six years old beneath wooden beams. The lesson that had survived every loss returned: Again."

**Visual beats:** Vale transforms alone; Vale tests uncontrolled power on empty training equipment; Ruby studies a remote security feed with bare wrists; Ruby completes a safe solo roll beneath familiar ceiling beams beside a framed childhood photograph.

## Page 11 - The Dojo Stands Up

**Story role:** Reveal that Ruby's students—not a recovered superpower—change the fight.

**Layout:** Four panels widening from one student to the whole dojo.

**Caption text:**

1. "Ruby stood. She could not defeat Vale alone, but she was no longer alone. One student stepped beside her, then another, then five more."
2. "Across the dojo, hostages used Ruby's lessons: one escaped a grip, two disarmed a gangster, and a shopkeeper pulled a frightened teenager behind cover."
3. "When Vale attacked one person, someone else created an opening. They scattered from his fire together because they had practiced this."
4. "No individual strike was enough. Together, they redirected his momentum, protected the fallen, and never gave fear time to isolate anyone."

**Visual beats:** Ruby rises with first allies; hostage escapes and mutual protection; coordinated dodge from fire; group uses sweeps, redirections, and positioning around Vale.

## Page 12 - The Power We Share

**Story role:** Resolve the fight and state Ruby's mature synthesis with complete clarity.

**Layout:** Four panels, ending on Vale powerless on his back and Ruby addressing the community.

**Caption text:**

1. "Vale shouted that Ruby was nothing without the bands. She looked at frightened people acting despite fear and answered, 'You still think this is about me.'"
2. "Ruby stepped aside. One student redirected Vale's arm, another broke his footing, and Ruby struck his center of balance as three more added their momentum."
3. "Vale crashed onto his back so hard the bands flew free. He became an ordinary man surrounded by people he had spent years convincing were powerless."
4. "Ruby held up the bands and explained: her father was right about skill, her master was right about adaptation, but neither object nor individual was enough. 'This is what makes us powerful.'"

**Visual beats:** Ruby's realization; fully legible combined throw; bands airborne and Vale human on floor; Ruby holding bands but gesturing toward everyone rather than putting them on.

## Page 13 - Again, Together

**Story role:** Show lasting citywide consequence and close on the opening word.

**Layout:** Four panels spanning years, final panel intimate and warm.

**Caption text:**

1. "Years later, Ruby's students had become teachers. Dojos opened in community centers, garages, restaurant basements, and city parks."
2. "The mob could threaten one store only to face ten neighboring stores beside it. Defeating Ruby could no longer return everyone else to helplessness."
3. "Ruby still wore the bands and trained as if she might lose them tomorrow. When danger demanded more, pink fur and golden fire still answered."
4. "But her greatest power lived in people who knew how to stand together. Whenever a new student fell, Ruby offered a hand, smiled, and said, 'Again.'"

**Visual beats:** Montage of decentralized schools; merchants closing ranks beside one targeted store; transformed Ruby flying above as citizens protect one another below; human Ruby offering her hand to a frustrated child on the dojo floor beneath familiar wooden beams.
