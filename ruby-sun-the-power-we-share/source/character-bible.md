# Character and Reference Bible

## Ruby Sun - Adult, Human State

- Asian woman in her mid-twenties with warm medium skin, a strong angular-oval face, dark brown almond-shaped eyes, straight dark brows, and a cocky asymmetric smirk.
- Lean, muscular lifelong martial artist: defined shoulders and arms, powerful legs, compact core, athletic rather than bulky.
- Enormous spiky black hair rising in dramatic flame-like points around and above her head. This is her strongest silhouette marker in both forms.
- Deep crimson cropped martial-arts wrap top, loose charcoal-black baggy fighting pants, black flexible martial-arts shoes.
- Futuristic golden bands encircle both forearms. They are thick, precisely machined, seamless, faintly engraved, and clearly more advanced than jewelry.
- Relaxed fighting posture: feet apart, fists loose at her sides, balanced and ready without a formal guard.
- Expression range: swaggering grin, analytical focus, buried grief, fierce compassion, amused confidence.
- Reference file: `reference-images/ruby-sun-human-reference.png`.

## Ruby Sun - Adult, Transformed State

- Exactly the same woman, face, eyes, enormous spiky black hair, athletic proportions, crimson cropped top, charcoal baggy pants, shoes, and golden forearm bands as human Ruby.
- Vivid short pink fur covers her arms, shoulders, sides, and visible torso without obscuring her human face.
- Long agile pink-furred monkey tail, expressive but anatomically attached and easy to track.
- Shimmering flaming golden aura around her body; golden fire gathers around hands and feet for attacks.
- Transformation amplifies agility and mythic presence but never changes her identity, age, hairstyle, or outfit silhouette.
- Reference file: `reference-images/ruby-sun-transformed-reference.png`.

## Young Ruby

- Six-year-old Asian girl with the same dark brown eyes, strong brows, and unmistakable but smaller wild spiky black hair.
- Red sleeveless training top, loose black practice pants, barefoot in the dojo.
- Defiant, energetic, expressive; frustration quickly turns into another charge.

## Ruby's Father

- Asian man in his early forties, lean and powerfully conditioned, weathered angular face, calm dark eyes, black hair tied into a short practical knot.
- Dark teal traditional gi with rolled sleeves and a black belt; bare feet in the dojo.
- Restrained posture and economical technique. Protective rather than aggressive.

## The Old Master

- Elderly male humanoid monkey, short and wiry, covered in muted rose-pink fur, intelligent amber eyes, long white eyebrows and a neat white chin beard.
- Weathered saffron-and-brown mountain robes, bare feet, wooden prayer beads.
- Moves with effortless balance. Small golden flames curl naturally around his fingers.
- Calm, dryly amused, and formidable; never cartoonish or mascot-like.

## Vincent Vale

- Lean middle-aged man with a narrow clean-shaven face, slicked-back black hair touched with gray at the temples, cold gray eyes, and carefully controlled posture.
- Expensive charcoal suit over a dark burgundy open-collar shirt, polished black shoes, silver signet ring.
- Looks like someone accustomed to ordering violence rather than performing it.
- When transformed by Ruby's bands, he retains the same face, hair, suit, and body identity, but pink fur covers his exposed skin and torso, a pink tail lashes without control, his body swells with supernatural muscle, and wild golden fire surrounds him.
- His transformed movement is visibly untrained: overextended punches, poor balance, uncontrolled tail, and dangerous friendly fire.

## Ruby's Students and Neighborhood

- A visibly diverse cross-section of ordinary city residents: shopkeepers, restaurant workers, teenagers, parents, delivery drivers, elders, and children.
- Practical everyday clothing in grounded colors, sometimes paired with simple training pads or belts.
- They should never look like a uniform superhero army. Their unity is communicated through coordinated movement, mutual protection, and practiced responses.
- Recurring young student in the raid: college-age adult man, about twenty, curly dark hair, red hoodie, jeans, sneakers.

## Key Locations and Props

- Childhood dojo: warm wooden floors, exposed ceiling beams, paper windows, simple training mats; safe and humble before gunfire damages it.
- Mountain temple: old stone and timber above the clouds, worn training courtyard, cold blue daylight, warm golden fire.
- Ruby's city dojo: converted neighborhood storefront with reinforced wood floor, red training mats, wide windows, community notices, and repaired industrial beams.
- Golden bands: identical matching pair, thick seamless futuristic gold, faint geometric engravings, supernatural warm inner glow.

## Continuity Rules

- Ruby's face, hair, outfit, build, and bands must remain consistent on every adult page.
- Pink fur and tail appear only while the bands are actively worn and transformed.
- When Ruby removes the bands, both fur and tail vanish immediately and her golden aura goes dark.
- When Vale wears the bands, he gains the same pink-fur and golden-fire system but remains visually Vincent Vale.
- Every page uses realistic candid photo-comic imagery, not anime, illustration, painterly art, 3D render, or glossy poster art except the cover's more deliberate composition.
- All readable story text must be generated directly in clean rectangular caption boxes. Never add text later.
