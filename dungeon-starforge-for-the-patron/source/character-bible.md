# Character And Reference Bible

## Starter Warlock

- Role: unnamed protagonist; a cautious novice who reaches level two during the run.
- Visible identity: Black man in his late twenties with deep brown skin, lean athletic build, oval face, brown eyes, full short black beard, and long black locs tied into a low bundle with several loose strands around his face.
- Outfit: weathered black leather longcoat with a high collar and wine-red lining, charcoal tunic, dark fitted trousers, scuffed knee-high boots, fingerless gloves, small potion belt, and a tarnished silver patron medallion shaped like a many-pointed star. No readable clothing text.
- Magic: Eldritch Blast is a compact violet-black bolt containing tiny star-like sparks. His spellcasting hand leaves a faint violet afterimage.
- Base posture: alert, slightly cautious, shoulders forward as if listening for threats. Confidence grows after each victory; exhaustion, dust, torn fabric, and bruising accumulate after the Planetar.
- Emotional range: confused curiosity, delighted gratitude, tactical caution, exhilarated pride, mounting anxiety, exhausted doubt, and final defiance.
- Persistent continuity: the Staff of Frost, Philter of Love, healing potions, and Thunderous Greatclub accumulate on his pack and belt. Potion inventory visibly shrinks. After the Planetar, his coat is torn at the left shoulder, his lower lip is split, and he favors his right side. No gore.

## Staff Of Frost

- A five-foot black iron staff with a translucent pale-cyan crystal head shaped like branching ice.
- Frost crawls over the crystal and upper shaft when first discovered.
- It is strapped diagonally across the Warlock's back on later pages.
- On the final page the crystal is dull, dry, and completely inert.

## Thunderous Greatclub

- A heavy dark-oak club reinforced by black iron rings and thin gold lightning runes.
- The runes flicker warmly when first discovered.
- The club hangs awkwardly from a leather harness along the Warlock's pack.
- On the final page every gold rune is dark and inert.

## Philter Of Love

- A small heart-shaped rose-glass bottle filled with luminous pink liquid and sealed with a silver stopper.
- It remains unused on the Warlock's belt throughout the run, emphasizing the inventory's useless abundance at the final battle.

## Healing Potions

- Thumb-length round glass bottles filled with bright ruby liquid.
- A consumed potion leaves an empty bottle at the Warlock's feet or clipped upside-down to his belt.
- His last full potion is consumed during the Planetar battle. No healing potions remain afterward.

## Wayfinder

- Role: solitary guide near the portal.
- Appearance: slender and androgynous adult in a weathered gray travel cloak, layered leather armor, and smooth off-white half-mask with narrow eye openings.
- Prop: crooked iron walking staff hung with three tiny brass bells.
- Demeanor: calm, still, and cryptic; points toward the portal while keeping a safe distance from the monsters.

## Wereboar

- Massive upright boar-humanoid with dark brown bristles, cracked tusks, piggish snout, broad shoulders, torn canvas trousers, and iron scraps tied around its forearms.
- Physical and brutal rather than supernatural-looking. It charges, punches, and slashes with tusks.
- The Warlock initially mistakes its distant silhouette for a Werebear.

## Invisible Stalker

- A roughly humanoid absence revealed through bent torchlight, drifting dust, air ripples, and brief glass-like contours.
- Its attacks distort the environment like a transparent body moving through water.
- Eldritch Blast temporarily outlines it in violet sparks.

## Planetar

- Towering celestial warrior with bronze-gold skin, luminous white eyes, enormous pale-feathered wings, silver-and-blue armor, and a radiant two-handed sword.
- Beautiful, stern, and utterly impersonal, as if generated by the dungeon rather than serving a moral cause.
- Its attacks are clean flashes of white-gold force with no gore.

## Ancient Silver Dragon

- Colossal ancient dragon with mirror-bright silver scales, darker steel ridges, huge torn-edged wings, pale blue-white eyes, long swept horns, and an intelligent, unreadable face.
- Fills the corridor beyond a broken stone arch; its head alone is several times larger than the Warlock's body.
- Remains calm and almost motionless until the final attack, making its overwhelming scale more frightening.
- Defeats the Warlock with one fast foreclaw strike that smashes him against the stone floor without gore.

## Procedural Starforge

- A square-grid dungeon rendered as believable ancient architecture: black basalt blocks, worn flagstones, iron-bound treasure chests, narrow junctions, and repeating corridors that make navigation confusing.
- Cold pinprick stars glow inside cracks in the ceiling and occasionally drift beneath transparent sections of floor, suggesting the dungeon exists somewhere outside ordinary space.
- Torches burn with a mixture of amber flame and faint violet edges.
- The sealed exit is a circular stone-and-metal portal with concentric rings and a central field of dim blue light.

## The Patron Motif

- The Patron never appears as a person, creature, face, eye, silhouette, or voice.
- Use only the many-pointed star on the Warlock's medallion and the violet star-sparks inside Eldritch Blast.
- The visual absence is essential: every claim about the Patron comes from the Warlock.

## Visual Rules

- Every image is a complete 4:5 portrait realistic candid photo-comic page.
- Keep the Starter Warlock's face, skin tone, locs, beard, coat, medallion, equipment, injuries, and potion count consistent.
- Keep the Starforge's basalt corridors, amber-violet torches, cold star cracks, and circular exit portal consistent.
- Use photoreal documentary texture and believable practical lighting; do not use anime, painting, cartoon, or illustrated rendering.
- Caption boxes only. No speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, page numbers, panel labels, caption labels, logos, or watermarks.
- All readable title and story text must be generated directly inside the page art.
