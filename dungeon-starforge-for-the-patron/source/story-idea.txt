Create a Random Comics one-shot from Nicholas Alexander Benson / Jet Black's failed Procedural Starforge dungeon run.

The Starter Warlock awakens in a dungeon with no memory of arriving and decides the maze must be a test from his unseen patron. Treasure chests seem to confirm his faith: a Staff of Frost, a Philter of Love, healing potions, and a Thunderous Greatclub. He avoids monsters until escape demands battle, then survives a Wereboar, an Invisible Stalker, and a Planetar through Eldritch Blast and healing potions. The exit remains sealed. Wounded and out of healing, he discovers an Ancient Silver Dragon, retreats, becomes lost for dozens of turns, and eventually returns because there is no other way forward. At the decisive moment, his Staff of Frost and Thunderous Greatclub are inert. He wonders whether his patron has betrayed him, attacks anyway, misses, and dies from the dragon's first blow.

Preserve the run's earnest journal voice, the Warlock's escalating faith, the dark humor of his confidence, the exact sequence of major discoveries and battles, and the abrupt game-over ending. Do not rescue or revive him.
