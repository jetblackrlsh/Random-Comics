# Page Script

Every page is a full realistic candid photo-comic page in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. All readable title and story text must be generated directly inside clean parchment-colored rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Use caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating subtitles, blank text boxes, later text overlay, page numbers, panel numbers, caption numbers, caption labels, or duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise a dungeon-crawl tragedy built around faith, accumulated treasure, and an overwhelming silver dragon.
- Layout: One dominant cinematic cover composition with three narrow fractured inset panels along the lower edge.
- Exact readable title text: `DUNGEON STARFORGE: FOR THE PATRON!`
- Exact caption text: `He believed every treasure was a blessing. The dungeon never made that promise.`
- Visual beats: The Starter Warlock stands in a black-basalt corridor, battered but defiant, casting a violet-black Eldritch Blast toward the enormous face of an Ancient Silver Dragon emerging through a broken arch. The inert frost staff and thunderous greatclub cross behind his shoulders. Lower insets show an opened treasure chest glowing cyan, a shattered empty healing-potion bottle, and the sealed circular exit portal.

## Page 2: A Test From The Patron

- Story role: Establish the Warlock, the strange dungeon, his interpretation of the trial, and the first apparent blessing.
- Layout: Four large rectangular panels in a clear top-to-bottom reading order.
- Exact caption text:
  - `The Starter Warlock awoke inside the Procedural Starforge with no memory of entering the dungeon.`
  - `He decided the maze was a test from his unseen patron: prove his worth or die trying.`
  - `A nearby treasure chest yielded a Staff of Frost. To him, the gift felt like an answer.`
  - `With arctic power on his back, he entered the corridors certain his patron was watching.`
- Visual beats: Warlock waking on worn flagstones; studying his many-pointed medallion beneath cold star cracks; opening an iron-bound chest filled with cyan glow; delighted close view of the frost staff before he walks into the maze.

## Page 3: Gifts Before Battle

- Story role: Show the inventory accumulating, the Warlock's caution, the monster-filled maze, and his decision to stop avoiding combat.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels, with the final panel larger than the others.
- Exact caption text:
  - `More chests rewarded caution: a Philter of Love, healing potions, and a Thunderous Greatclub.`
  - `He avoided the monsters while mapping the corridors, saving every advantage for the battles ahead.`
  - `But Death Dogs and werecreatures crowded the maze, and the sealed exit demanded every hostile be defeated.`
  - `Lost after seventy turns, he finally chose action. For the patron!`
- Visual beats: montage of rose-glass philter, ruby potion, and lightning-rune club entering his inventory; Warlock peeking around a junction at snarling two-headed Death Dogs; distant Wereboar silhouette that resembles a bear; sealed portal refusing him; final panel of the Warlock stepping toward the Wereboar with violet energy around his hand.

## Page 4: Strength Through Survival

- Story role: Deliver the Wereboar battle, show how healing and Eldritch Blast actually keep him alive, and turn survival into confidence.
- Layout: Five dynamic panels with diagonal movement and one calm closing panel.
- Exact caption text:
  - `A Wereboar nearly crushed him, forcing him to spend his first healing potion.`
  - `Three Eldritch Blasts brought the beast down. The victory strengthened him to level two and restored him completely.`
  - `He had mistaken it for a Werebear, but survival made the error feel trivial.`
  - `He carried on, saving the frost staff and thunderous club for something worse.`
- Visual beats: Wereboar charge and impact; Warlock drinking a ruby potion while dodging; successive violet-black blasts in two narrow panels; Wereboar fallen without gore as starlike level-up light restores the Warlock; confident Warlock walking away with both unused weapons visible.

## Page 5: Courage At The Portal

- Story role: Introduce the Wayfinder, defeat the Invisible Stalker, and reveal that the apparent exit still will not open.
- Layout: Four large panels with clear geography around the portal.
- Exact caption text:
  - `A Wayfinder beside the portal promised that the path listened for footsteps and courage.`
  - `An Invisible Stalker guarded the route. The Warlock saw its distortion and attacked.`
  - `Another healing potion kept him alive until Eldritch Blast tore the unseen predator apart.`
  - `Yet the exit remained sealed. Somewhere in the Starforge, stronger enemies still waited.`
- Visual beats: masked Wayfinder pointing toward circular portal; rippling Invisible Stalker between the Warlock and exit; potion-drinking and violet blast outlining the transparent enemy; Warlock alone before the unresponsive portal while the Wayfinder has vanished.

## Page 6: The Last Potion

- Story role: Stage the Planetar battle, consume the Warlock's final healing resource, and leave him victorious but vulnerable.
- Layout: Five kinetic panels with one wide aftermath panel.
- Exact caption text:
  - `A radiant Planetar blocked the southern halls. The Warlock's blasts pierced its holy armor—but barely.`
  - `His final healing potion bought one last chance.`
  - `The Planetar fell, leaving the Warlock alive, wounded, and completely out of healing.`
  - `He still believed every narrow victory proved the patron was guiding him.`
- Visual beats: Planetar fills the corridor with wings and radiant sword; alternating misses and impacts; Warlock empties his final ruby potion; a strong Eldritch Blast defeats the celestial without gore; torn and bruised Warlock kneels among empty bottles beneath the glowing star medallion.

## Page 7: No Other Way

- Story role: Reveal the Ancient Silver Dragon, show the long retreat and futile search, and bring the Warlock back by deliberate choice.
- Layout: Four large panels, with the first and last acting as mirrored dragon confrontations.
- Exact caption text:
  - `At the far edge of the maze, an Ancient Silver Dragon blocked the path.`
  - `Too wounded to fight, the Warlock retreated and searched every corridor for another enemy, another potion, or another way out.`
  - `For fifty-eight turns he circled the Starforge. The exit never opened. Nothing restored him.`
  - `At last, faith and exhaustion led him back to the dragon. If there was no escape, he would still try.`
- Visual beats: first sight of the colossal dragon through broken arch; Warlock backing away; overhead-like montage of him crossing repeating junctions and returning to the sealed portal while increasingly exhausted; final panel mirrors the first but the Warlock now walks toward the dragon with one hand glowing violet.

## Page 8: The Adventure Ends Here

- Story role: Pay off the inert weapons, break the Warlock's certainty, preserve his final act of courage, and end with the exact failed-run consequence.
- Layout: Four cinematic panels: equipment failure, final attack, dragon counterattack, and quiet aftermath.
- Exact caption text:
  - `At the dragon's feet, the Staff of Frost and Thunderous Greatclub were suddenly inert. The Warlock wondered if his patron had betrayed him.`
  - `Refusing to accept a helpless end, he raised one final Eldritch Blast.`
  - `The spell missed. The Ancient Silver Dragon answered with a single crushing blow.`
  - `He had promised to prove his worth or die trying. The Starforge accepted the second half of the bargain.`
- Visual beats: close panel of dull frost crystal and dark lightning runes as the Warlock's frightened face reflects between them; violet-black blast streaking past the dragon's cheek; one enormous foreclaw striking the Warlock to the stone floor without gore; final wide panel of the motionless Warlock, unused treasures scattered around him, dragon already turning away beneath cold star cracks. End with complete visual finality and no revival.
