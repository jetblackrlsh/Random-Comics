# Treatment

## Title

Dungeon Starforge: For the Patron!

## Format

Standalone Random Comics one-shot, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Publication Date

2026-08-14

## Source

Adapted from Nicholas Alexander Benson / Jet Black's failed `Procedural Starforge` run log. The adaptation condenses 193 turns while preserving the major treasures, battles, journal logic, sealed-exit problem, weapon failure, and final defeat.

## Logline

A young Warlock mistakes every treasure and narrow victory inside the Procedural Starforge for proof that his unseen patron is testing and favoring him—until an Ancient Silver Dragon reveals that faith, preparation, and a full inventory do not guarantee escape.

## Central Conflict

The Starter Warlock believes he must defeat every hostile creature to unlock the dungeon's exit. Caution keeps him alive long enough to gather powerful-looking equipment, but every real victory depends on Eldritch Blast and a shrinking supply of healing potions. When the exit remains sealed and an Ancient Silver Dragon blocks the last unexplored path, he must choose between accepting helplessness and honoring his original vow to try even if it kills him.

## Theme

Faith can supply courage without supplying control. The Warlock's devotion helps him endure impossible odds, but it also encourages him to read random rewards and lucky survivals as a promise that the dungeon owes him victory.

## Ending

After wandering the maze for dozens of turns in search of another route, the wounded Warlock returns to the Ancient Silver Dragon. His Staff of Frost and Thunderous Greatclub are inexplicably inert. He wonders whether his patron has betrayed him, fires one final Eldritch Blast, misses, and falls to the dragon's first blow. The final caption completes his opening vow: the Starforge did not let him prove his worth; it accepted his willingness to die trying.

## Emotional Arc

The Warlock begins disoriented but eagerly devout. Each chest turns uncertainty into delighted confidence. His first brutal battle converts confidence into earned pride, and successive narrow victories make him certain that his patron is guiding him. Running out of healing transforms that certainty into dread. His long retreat from the dragon becomes exhaustion and desperation. When every weapon fails, doubt finally breaks through—but pride and faith still carry him into one last doomed attack.

## Cast

- Starter Warlock: unnamed protagonist, level-two survivor, earnest servant of an unseen patron, and unreliable interpreter of the Starforge's random rewards.
- Wayfinder: a solitary masked guide who offers one cryptic encouragement near the portal.
- Wereboar, Invisible Stalker, and Planetar: escalating enemies that consume the Warlock's health and potions while reinforcing his belief that he is chosen.
- Ancient Silver Dragon: the overwhelming final obstacle and the visual embodiment of a dungeon that never promised fairness.
- The Patron: never appears physically; exists only through the Warlock's interpretation of events and a subtle violet star motif in his magic.

## Art Direction

Full realistic candid photo-comic pages with handheld dungeon-expedition energy: rough practical stone corridors, believable torchlight and cold starlight, natural imperfect framing, photoreal fantasy creatures, and grounded magical VFX. Use black, wine-red, violet, icy cyan, thunder-gold, and silver as the dominant palette. Captions sit inside clean parchment-colored rectangular boxes. No gore, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, page numbers, panel labels, watermarks, or later-added text.
