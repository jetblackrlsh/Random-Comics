# Character And Reference Bible

## Mira Vale

- Role: protagonist and Amberwood guild courier.
- Age range: early twenties.
- Visual identifiers: human woman, medium height, warm brown skin, oval face, focused dark eyes, dark curly hair tied back with a copper clasp, weathered forest-green travel cloak, brown leather vest over an off-white linen shirt, dark trousers, sturdy brown boots, fingerless gloves, and a small brass guild badge shaped like a road winding through leaves.
- Signature magic: hears the emotions of forged metal as faint music. Visually show this as delicate silver-gold sound ripples or threadlike notes around metal tools, especially the stormlight lantern and guild badge.
- Continuity notes: keep her green cloak, brass badge, tied-back curls, and fingerless gloves consistent. She grows from uncertain to quietly brave. Her cloak can become wind-tugged, rain-specked, or dust-covered as climates change.

## Stormlight Lantern

- Role: the key elemental tool Mira carries.
- Visual identifiers: palm-sized but weighty brass-and-blackiron lantern with blue-white stormlight burning inside, small rain-glass panes, etched cloud and lightning patterns, leather carrying loop, and a warm metallic expression through its glow.
- Continuity notes: the lantern is not alive like a face, but Mira's magic reveals its emotions through pulses, silver-gold sound ripples, and changing stormlight. It begins anxious, steadies in Mira's hands, and shines fiercely on the final road.

## Master Elian Rook

- Role: Amberwood guild steward who gives Mira the quest.
- Age range: late fifties to sixties.
- Visual identifiers: human man, dark skin, close-cropped silver hair, trimmed gray beard, round reading spectacles, navy long coat with brass buttons, leather ledger satchel, calm posture, ink-stained fingers.
- Continuity notes: appears mainly in Amberwood guild hall. Practical rather than mystical; he represents the guild's neutral service.

## Tova Granitehorn

- Role: mountain ramfolk element smith and maker of the stormlight lantern.
- Visual identifiers: tall broad ramfolk humanoid with realistic curled horns, pale wool, square muzzle, strong arms, soot on the face, heavy leather apron, iron gauntlets, copper tool belt, standing near a volcanic stone forge with storm sparks in the coals.
- Continuity notes: Tova is powerful but worried. Keep the horns, soot-dark apron, and gauntlets consistent.

## Sahr Of Dunescar

- Role: desert jackalfolk caravan captain whose people need the lantern.
- Visual identifiers: lean jackalfolk humanoid with sandy fur, long ears, amber eyes, narrow muzzle, white headscarf, layered tan and indigo desert robes, cracked water compass hanging from the belt, sand-scuffed boots.
- Continuity notes: Sahr first appears as distant or wary, then relieved. Keep the white headscarf and cracked water compass visible.

## Grand Dale Cultures And Tools

- Amberwood: human homeland, temperate forests, timber guild hall, road maps, neutral quest boards, mixed travelers, brass badges, practical gear.
- Mountain crag kingdom: ramfolk smiths, high altitude forges, stone bridges, stormlight lanterns, ember hammers, sunmetal blades.
- River realm: otterfolk traders, water-forged tools, rain-calling cups, canal markets, wet stone docks.
- Frost kingdom: harefolk farmers, frost-blessed sickles, snowbound fields, heat-charmed hinges.
- Jungle kingdom: lemurfolk or monkeyfolk vine smiths, living vine tools, humid ruins, green light.
- Desert kingdom of Dunescar: jackalfolk caravans, sand roads, water compasses, sun-shelter tents, urgent need for imported tools.

## Visual Rules

- Full realistic candid photo-comic pages in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio.
- Documentary phone-photo or handheld photojournalism look, natural imperfect framing, practical fantasy costumes, believable physical locations, and photoreal elemental effects.
- Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating dialogue, no subtitles.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art.
- Repeat Mira's stable identifiers and the stormlight lantern details in every prompt where they appear.
