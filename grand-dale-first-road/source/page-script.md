# Page Script

Every page is a full realistic candid photo-comic page in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. All readable story text must be generated directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Use caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce Grand Dale, Amberwood, the road between climates, and Mira's first guild quest.
- Layout: One dominant vertical cover image with three small inset panels down the right edge.
- Caption/title text to generate in the page art: Title text: "GRAND DALE: THE FIRST ROAD". Subtitle text: "A Random Comics one-shot." Small caption box: "Every kingdom has a craft. Every person has a secret spark."
- Visual beats: Mira Vale stands at the edge of Amberwood's forest road with her green cloak, brass guild badge, and stormlight lantern glowing blue-white in her hand. The road ahead splits into visible climates: snow fields, volcanic crags, jungle vines, river canals, and a desert sandstorm. Insets show Tova's ramfolk forge, Sahr's jackalfolk caravan, and Mira hearing silver-gold music from the lantern.

## Page 2: Amberwood's Badge

- Story role: Establish Amberwood as the neutral human homeland and Mira as a new guild courier.
- Layout: Four large documentary panels with roomy captions.
- Caption boxes: "Amberwood did not win Grand Dale with one grand spell." "It survived by sending guild members down roads other kingdoms could not walk together." "Humans adapted where frostfolk, desertfolk, marshfolk, and mountainfolk struggled." "Mira Vale received her badge because being useful was its own kind of bravery."
- Visual beats: A temperate timber guild hall under huge trees; Master Elian pins a brass road-and-leaf badge to Mira's green cloak; maps show roads crossing many climates; animalfolk travelers wait uneasily beside human guild members.

## Page 3: Tools That Keep People Alive

- Story role: Explain element smithing as ordinary survival technology, not luxury.
- Layout: Four-panel market montage with practical enchanted tools.
- Caption boxes: "Grand Dale's true wonder was element smithing." "A stormlight lantern could guide a road after sunset." "A frost-blessed sickle could save crops through brutal heat." "No kingdom owned every miracle, so trade kept the world stitched together."
- Visual beats: A river otterfolk merchant displays a rain-calling cup; harefolk farmers use frost-glowing sickles near dry crops; ramfolk smiths test ember hammers and sunmetal blades; a mixed caravan loads crates of practical enchanted tools while guards watch rivals warily.

## Page 4: The Lantern's Song

- Story role: Reveal Mira's signature magic and the urgent quest.
- Layout: Three large panels, intimate and clear.
- Caption boxes: "Every person carried one private magic, small or strange or mighty." "Mira's gift was hearing the emotions of metal." "When the stormlight lantern trembled like a frightened bell, she knew it needed the desert road."
- Visual beats: In Tova Granitehorn's volcanic forge, the ramfolk smith presents the blue-white lantern; silver-gold sound ripples curl from the lantern to Mira's fingertips; Master Elian's quest ledger and a map point toward Dunescar, where a caravan is trapped by a growing sandstorm.

## Page 5: Roads Through Other Peoples' Weather

- Story role: Show why humans are valuable travelers and escalate the difficulty of crossing climates.
- Layout: Five panels with strong climate transitions.
- Caption boxes: "The first road left Amberwood's soft rain behind." "Snow stung the cloak. Jungle heat soaked it. Mountain air thinned every breath." "Mira was not built for one climate, and that became her advantage." "The lantern sang fear whenever borders tightened." "Still, she kept walking."
- Visual beats: Mira crosses from green forest into snow with harefolk struggling under summer heat tools; then humid jungle vines where lemurfolk vine tools coil around bridges; then high mountain stone roads with ramfolk haulers; then wind-scoured desert edge. Keep the lantern visible and glowing, cloak weathered by each climate.

## Page 6: The Cracking Treaty

- Story role: Turn the guide's trade tension into a concrete dispute.
- Layout: Four panels with caravan politics and practical stakes.
- Caption boxes: "At the Dunescar border, distrust had stopped the wagons." "The jackalfolk needed mountain stormlight, but old insults weighed more than need." "Sahr's water compass had cracked. Without light, the sand road would swallow the caravan." "Mira listened until the lantern's metal song answered for everyone."
- Visual beats: Sahr of Dunescar stands with a wary jackalfolk caravan at a checkpoint; ramfolk guards and desert traders argue without speech bubbles; the cracked water compass hangs at Sahr's belt; Mira kneels between both sides, one hand on the lantern, silver-gold music connecting metal tools across the scene.

## Page 7: The Right Tool At The Right Time

- Story role: The decisive action: Mira uses the lantern and her signature magic to open safe passage.
- Layout: Five action-forward panels.
- Caption boxes: "Mira was not the strongest hero on the road." "She had one badge, one cloak, one frightened lantern, and one magic only she could use." "She asked the metal what it needed." "Stormlight answered with thunder." "The safe road appeared."
- Visual beats: Sandstorm walls rise around the caravan; Mira raises the stormlight lantern as blue-white lightning coils inside its panes; silver-gold sound ripples guide her hand to align the cracked water compass beneath the lantern; a luminous road cuts through the storm; jackalfolk and ramfolk shields lower as the caravan begins moving.

## Page 8: Your First Kingdom

- Story role: Resolve the quest and return the guide's daydreaming questions to the reader.
- Layout: Four panels ending with a wide hopeful road image.
- Caption boxes: "By dawn, Dunescar's caravan crossed safely." "The lantern no longer sounded afraid. It sounded useful." "Grand Dale was not held together by perfect trust, but by roads brave people repaired." "So close your eyes: what is your signature magic, what elemental tool do you carry, and which kingdom needs you first?"
- Visual beats: Sahr clasps Mira's forearm in gratitude while Tova's distant ramfolk trade banner is accepted beside the caravan; the stormlight lantern glows calm on a crate of traded tools; Mira looks back toward many climate roads branching from the desert sunrise; final wide panel shows Grand Dale's road network crossing forest, river, snow, mountain, jungle, and desert under a bright sky.
