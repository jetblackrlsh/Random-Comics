## Clifford

- Role: co-protagonist, gentle goliath bard, well-meaning magical musician, and Angora's new friend.
- Apparent age: early thirties.
- Scale and build: approximately eight feet tall, immensely broad and muscular, visibly towering above normal tavern patrons, but with relaxed nonthreatening posture.
- Identity: Black/African goliath man with deep brown skin.
- Face: broad handsome face, warm brown eyes, full shaped black beard, confident eyebrows, expressive smile that shifts to worried embarrassment when his music fails.
- Hair: very long black locs gathered into a high bundled ponytail with several loose locs and small gold cuffs.
- Face and body paint: symmetrical white tribal paint—curving lines and dots across forehead, cheeks, nose, shoulders, and upper arms. Keep this pattern consistent.
- Outfit: layered green and royal-purple bardic garments with gold trim, draped green shoulder mantle, leather belts, gold jewelry, fang necklace, bracers, and ornate circular brooches. No readable clothing text.
- Signature prop: large ornate wooden magical lute with carved gold filigree and curled headstock, proportioned for his giant hands.
- Magic motif: glowing green-gold musical notes and ribbon-like sound trails circling the lute.
- Emotional range: easy warmth, eager helpfulness, startled regret, frantic concentration, embarrassed apology, sincere encouragement.

## Angora

- Role: co-protagonist, disciplined elf fighter seeking a worthy challenge and learning to focus her psychic strength.
- Apparent age: mid-twenties.
- Build and posture: athletic compact build, grounded fighter's stance, direct gaze, shield kept ready.
- Identity: fair-skinned elf woman with lightly freckled cheeks and long sharply pointed ears.
- Face: angular heart-shaped face, green eyes, strong brows, determined expression that softens with empathy around Clifford.
- Hair: vivid deep-red hair in a high practical ponytail, loose strands framing her face. Keep the ponytail and color consistent.
- Outfit: dark gunmetal plate-and-leather armor with dramatic spikes on shoulders and forearms, deep green undersuit and short green cloak accents, sturdy dark boots. Armor may show water and mud during the river fight.
- Signature props: round wooden shield with dark metal rim and central boss; one-handed psychic sword whose solid dark hilt projects a long translucent pink-violet energy blade.
- Psychic motif: bright magenta-violet plasma light, fine electric arcs, and a focused glow in her green eyes.
- Emotional range: competitive confidence, compassion toward harmless creatures, fierce protection, intellectual curiosity, alarm when the sword weakens, intense mental focus, affectionate reassurance.
