# Page Script

Every page is a full realistic candid photo-comic page in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. All readable title and story text must be generated directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Use caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no later text overlay, no page numbers, no panel numbers, no caption labels, and no duplicated caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Present Kaelus, the barely visible assassin, the frozen arena, and the closed-eye conspiracy as one dark-fantasy mystery.
- Layout: Full-page cinematic cover with Kaelus dominating the lower center, one glowing palm raised defensively. Behind him, the Invisible Stalker is suggested by dust and frost curling into a towering humanoid outline around a shattered pillar. A broken silver closed-eye mask lies in the icy foreground.
- Readable title text: "THE LAST WITNESS"
- Visual beats: Kaelus’s midnight-blue coat and silver sleeve runes flare in wind; his pale gray eyes stare toward the revealed storm figure; cold moonlight and cobalt magic cut through the ruined arena; the mask fragment foreshadows the ending.

## Page 2: The Assassin In Empty Air

- Story role: Establish Kaelus’s history, his present mission, the arena, and the exact nature of the unseen threat.
- Layout: Four large rectangular panels in clear top-to-bottom reading order.
- Caption boxes: "Kaelus had once served as a royal battle-mage, until he exposed a conspiracy among the court’s ruling sorcerers and abandoned their palace." "He survived afterward as a wandering protector of people hunted by magical power, knowing the conspirators would eventually answer his defiance." "That answer found him alone inside a shattered arena, where his midnight-blue coat moved in air that should have been still." "An Invisible Stalker circled him: a creature of compressed wind, bound to pursue one target until hunter or quarry was dead."
- Visual beats: Kaelus before a royal court turning away from shadowed masked sorcerers; Kaelus protecting frightened travelers on a road; Kaelus alone at the arena center noticing dust bend around an unseen body; a nearly invisible eight-foot humanoid distortion stalks behind the broken pillar.

## Page 3: Sight And Shield

- Story role: Reveal the assassin and show Kaelus neutralizing its surprise attack.
- Layout: Four action panels, with the final panel the largest.
- Caption boxes: "Kaelus whispered See Invisibility, and pale light sharpened his gray eyes." "Thirty-five feet away, empty air became a towering figure of storm and dust preparing to strike behind a broken pillar." "The Stalker surged forward with fists like battering rams." "Kaelus punched toward it, projecting a blue Shield that stopped both blows and erased the hunter’s greatest advantage."
- Visual beats: close-up of Kaelus’s eyes lighting; his altered sight revealing the wind humanoid; Stalker accelerating across the floor; two huge wind fists explode against a translucent cobalt barrier from Kaelus’s knuckles.

## Page 4: Terrible Tundra

- Story role: Let Kaelus wound the creature, show his compassion, and establish that the assassination was purchased.
- Layout: Three large cinematic panels with room for the frost attack and readable captions.
- Caption boxes: "Kaelus gathered freezing mana between his palms and apologized to the enslaved creature he still had to kill." "TERRIBLE TUNDRA!" "A sixty-foot cone of supernatural cold buried the arena in ice and trapped the Stalker inside a jagged frozen outline." "Badly wounded but still compelled, the elemental rasped, ‘Your death was purchased, Archmage.’"
- Visual beats: Kaelus holds both hands together like a warrior charging a legendary energy blast; vast blue-white cold erupts across floor and pillars; the Stalker remains upright inside cracking translucent ice, its face funnel turned toward Kaelus.

## Page 5: The Last Witness

- Story role: Deliver the central clue and make clear that information cannot release the Stalker from its binding.
- Layout: Four large panels balancing defense, interrogation, memory image, and tragic recommitment.
- Caption boxes: "The binding drove the Stalker forward again, and Kaelus spent another Shield stopping two more attacks." "He promised a quick death if the creature revealed who had issued its command." "The frightened elemental confessed that its unseen summoner spoke through a silver mask engraved with a closed eye—and called Kaelus ‘the Last Witness.’" "The truth proved the old conspiracy still feared him, but confession could not break the command. The Stalker raised its fists again."
- Visual beats: repeated blows slam against a weakening Shield; Kaelus faces the ice-cracked Stalker with grim controlled intensity; vision-like inset of a full silver closed-eye mask delivering the order to a kneeling wind figure; the Stalker reluctantly resumes its stance as Kaelus realizes the implication.

## Page 6: Blizzard Bombardment

- Story role: Show Kaelus using exact distance and action economy to force the faster enemy into a vulnerable position.
- Layout: Four kinetic panels connected by a clear left-to-right spatial line across the arena.
- Caption boxes: "Kaelus shaped a precise sign, vanished into glowing smoke, and Misty Stepped thirty feet backward." "He sprinted another thirty, forcing the Stalker to spend all its supernatural speed crossing the arena without time to strike." "The assassin stopped exactly where Kaelus had planned." "TITANIC TUNDRA: BLIZZARD BOMBARDMENT! Icy spheres and radiant mana bolts cracked its frozen storm-body almost apart."
- Visual beats: Kaelus evaporates and reappears; runs toward far arena wall; Stalker flies fifty feet and closes the last distance with fists raised but no strike; Kaelus opens his palms and fills the lane with a barrage of ice spheres and blue-white bolts.

## Page 7: The Final Shield

- Story role: Give the Stalker one devastating hit, exhaust Kaelus’s defense, and reduce the duel to one last exchange.
- Layout: Four close, violent panels with strong physical continuity.
- Caption boxes: "Still alive, the assassin attacked with desperate fury." "Its first fist broke through and crushed Kaelus across the ribs, nearly throwing him from his feet." "He spun with the impact, turned the motion into a counterpunch, and projected his final Shield into the second blow." "Wind and arcane force exploded between them. Kaelus’s protection was nearly gone, yet the unraveling Stalker remained enslaved to one final charge."
- Visual beats: cracked Stalker lunges; huge wind fist hits Kaelus’s left ribs and tears the coat; Kaelus spins low and punches a last translucent plane into the second fist; both stagger across icy rubble, breathing and swirling hard, facing each other beneath the ceiling.

## Page 8: Rising Glacier

- Story role: Resolve the duel, end the Stalker’s forced mission, reveal physical proof, and turn Kaelus from quarry into hunter.
- Layout: Four panels: low-angle setup, huge diagonal attack, silent aftermath close-up, and wide departure image.
- Caption boxes: "Kaelus dropped to one knee beneath the charging elemental and aimed his palm upward." "DIAGONAL DRAFT: RISING GLACIER!" "A colossal pillar of ice and mana launched the Stalker toward the ceiling, froze its violent winds solid, and shattered its body in a storm of silver light." "A mask fragment bearing the closed eye struck the floor beside Kaelus. He left the arena wounded but alive, carrying proof—and ready to hunt the conspirators who had ordered his death."
- Visual beats: Kaelus braces his forearm at point-blank range; diagonal glacier erupts through the Stalker and suspends it near the ceiling before it explodes into ice; silent close-up of the closed-eye silver fragment in frost beside Kaelus’s hand; Kaelus walks through a broken arena arch into dawn with the fragment clenched, torn coat moving behind him.
