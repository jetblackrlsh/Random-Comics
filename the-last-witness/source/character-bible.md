# Character And Reference Bible

## Kaelus the Archmage

- Role: protagonist, former royal battle-mage, exiled whistleblower, wandering protector, and assassination target.
- Age range: late thirties.
- Build and posture: tall, lean-athletic, disciplined fighter’s stance, controlled gestures, shoulders squared even when wounded.
- Face: angular narrow face, high cheekbones, straight nose, short dark stubble, sharp gray eyes.
- Hair: shoulder-length black hair with a single silver streak at the right temple, usually loose and wind-tossed.
- Skin tone: light olive.
- Outfit: long tailored midnight-blue battle coat reaching below the knees, high collar, fitted black leather trousers, dark boots, black forearm wraps, and silver runes glowing along both sleeves. The coat has no readable lettering.
- Color palette: midnight blue, black, silver, pale blue-white.
- Magic motif: precise martial hand forms; pale light in his gray eyes; translucent cobalt-blue Shield planes projected from his punching hand; dense white frost and blue-white radiant mana for attacks.
- Emotional range: watchful restraint, grim compassion, tactical concentration, pain held under discipline, final renewed resolve.
- Continuity changes: after the first successful Stalker punch, keep bruising and torn cloth at Kaelus’s left ribs through the final page. His coat becomes dusted with frost and stone powder as the duel continues.

## The Invisible Stalker

- Role: antagonist and tragic magically enslaved assassin.
- Scale and body: approximately eight feet tall, broad-shouldered humanoid made from compressed rotating air. When revealed, its form consists of twisting gray-white wind, suspended dust, tiny stone fragments, and pale blue electrical glimmers.
- Face: no human features; only a deep swirling funnel where a face would be, with two faint cold lights suggesting eyes.
- Hands: enormous wind-compressed fists shaped by circling grit.
- Movement: hovers inches above the floor, shoulders forward, attacks with sudden hurricane-force acceleration.
- Damage continuity: normally invisible before See Invisibility; after Terrible Tundra, a jagged shell and outline of frost makes its body visible to the reader. After Blizzard Bombardment, the frozen storm-body is cracked, hollow in places, and leaking ribbons of wind. During the final charge it is barely intact.
- Emotional range: mechanical pursuit, surprise when exposed, fear during interrogation, exhausted resignation, forced final fury.
- Text rule: any spoken words appear only through rectangular caption boxes written as narrated or contextualized quotations, never through speech bubbles.

## The Silver Closed-Eye Mask

- Role: clue linking the assassin to the court conspiracy.
- Design: polished silver ceremonial face mask with sharp cheek contours and a single engraved closed eye across the forehead; no letters, numbers, or readable inscription.
- Story use: the Stalker remembers receiving its order through the complete mask. After the elemental dies, a palm-sized broken fragment bearing the closed-eye engraving falls from its remains.
- Visual continuity: cold silver metal, thin dark engraved line, faint blue magical residue.

## The Shattered Arena

- Role: entire duel location and spatial frame for the tactical movement.
- Design: circular ancient stone arena approximately one hundred feet across, broken spectator tiers, cracked flagstone floor, toppled columns, one large broken pillar near the starting position, high shadowed ceiling with openings to cold moonlight, drifting dust, and scattered chunks of masonry.
- Lighting: cold moonlight from above, faint amber remnants of extinguishing wall braziers, blue-white spell light reflecting on stone.
- Spatial continuity: Kaelus begins in the center. The hidden Stalker begins thirty-five feet away behind the broken pillar. Misty Step and the following sprint carry Kaelus toward the far edge so the Stalker must cross the full arena. The final exchange occurs near the opposite wall beneath the high ceiling.
