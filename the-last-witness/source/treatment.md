# Treatment

## Title

The Last Witness

## Format

Standalone one-shot, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

In a shattered arena, exiled archmage Kaelus survives an Invisible Stalker’s relentless assassination order by exposing the elemental, outmaneuvering it, and turning its dying confession into proof that the royal conspiracy he once revealed still fears him.

## Central Conflict

Kaelus must kill an assassin that has no hatred and no freedom to stop. Every spell he spends brings him closer to exhaustion, while every moment he keeps the Stalker alive offers a chance to learn who purchased his death. The duel therefore tests more than magical power: Kaelus must balance self-defense, tactical restraint, compassion for an enslaved creature, and his need for the truth.

## World Logic

Kaelus was once a royal battle-mage. After he exposed a conspiracy among the court’s ruling sorcerers, he abandoned the palace and became a wandering protector of people hunted by magical authorities. The conspirators now call him “the Last Witness,” implying that everyone else who knew their secret has already been silenced.

An Invisible Stalker is a towering humanoid elemental made from compressed wind, summoned from the Plane of Air. Binding magic gives it one target and compels it to pursue that target until hunter or quarry is dead. It can speak and reveal information, but it cannot disobey the command to kill.

Kaelus’s defensive Shield spells appear as translucent blue planes projected from his punches. See Invisibility gives his gray eyes pale silver-blue light and reveals the Stalker as a twisting storm-and-dust body. His offensive magic combines frost and radiant blue-white mana through disciplined martial hand forms.

## Theme

Defiance becomes purpose when survival produces truth. Kaelus cannot free the bound assassin, but he can end its forced mission, recover evidence, and choose to confront the people who created the violence.

## Ending

Kaelus destroys the Stalker with Diagonal Draft: Rising Glacier. A fragment of the summoner’s silver mask falls from the shattered elemental, bearing the same closed-eye symbol described in its confession. Kaelus leaves the arena wounded but alive, carrying proof that turns him from hunted exile into active investigator.

## Emotional Arc

Kaelus begins watchful and isolated, certain that his past has finally sent an assassin. Seeing the Stalker lets him replace uncertainty with disciplined action. Its confession awakens both compassion and anger because the creature is enslaved by the same hidden power hunting him. After nearly exhausting his defenses, Kaelus accepts that mercy cannot mean surrender. His final victory is grim rather than triumphant, but the mask fragment gives his survival direction and renews his resolve.

## Cast

- Kaelus the Archmage: former royal battle-mage, exposed conspirator, wandering protector, and target known to his enemies as “the Last Witness.”
- The Invisible Stalker: towering air elemental assassin bound to kill Kaelus, capable of speech and fear but unable to abandon its command.
- The hidden conspirators: unseen ruling sorcerers represented by a silver mask engraved with a closed eye.

## Art Direction

Realistic candid photo-comic with dark high-fantasy production design. The shattered arena should feel like a real ancient stone location photographed under cold moonlight and practical dust haze. Magic effects must look physically present: translucent blue Shield planes, frost traveling across masonry, compressed wind carrying grit, radiant mana bolts, ice fragments, and believable impact debris. Use handheld action photography, imperfect low angles, selective motion blur, strong spatial continuity, and clean rectangular caption boxes. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
