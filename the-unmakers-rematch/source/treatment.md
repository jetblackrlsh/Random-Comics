# Treatment

## Title

Fortune's Thread: The Unmaker's Rematch

## Format

Standalone one-shot, eight pages total: one cover and seven story pages.

## Logline

When Jet accidentally disconnects his living golden thread from the cosmic Loom, he refuses erasure by challenging the reality-pruning Unmaker to rock, paper, scissors—and wins freedom, autonomy, and one mysterious rematch.

## Central Conflict

Jet believes that ownership of his own thread should belong to him, while Mara Vey warns that a disconnected choice cannot be reconciled with reality. The debate becomes immediate when the Unmaker identifies Jet as an impossibility to remove. Jet must protect both his life and his autonomy without violence, using the simplest fair agreement imaginable against an incomprehensible cosmic power.

## World Logic

The Loom is the black extradimensional structure beneath ordinary reality. Every person's choices travel along one golden thread, whose vibrations affect countless other threads as reality reconciles consequences. Threadkeepers maintain that network. The Unmaker crawls along the Loom's dark outer boundary and removes contradictions, abandoned possibilities, and anything disconnected from the causal web. Jet's thread is unprecedented because it moves by itself and recreates an impossible knot whenever he holds it.

Fortune's Thread resolves uncertain actions with a genuine random result from one to four: 1 is disaster, 2 is partial success, 3 is success, and 4 is extraordinary success. Jet's pull received 2, opening the Loom at a cost. His challenge received 3, so the wager succeeded exactly as attempted.

## Theme

Autonomy does not require domination. Jet protects ownership of himself through curiosity, consent, and a clear agreement, while the supposedly monstrous Unmaker proves capable of fairness and even play.

## Ending

The Unmaker honors its defeat and releases Jet and his living thread. It then sends him an ivory token marked with rock, paper, scissors and ONE REMATCH. Mara cannot guarantee the token is harmless, but advises Jet to establish friendly stakes before using it. The story closes with Jet holding both his own place in the web of consequence and an invitation from the end of possibility.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins curious and playfully defiant, becomes philosophically firm when Mara asks him to surrender something fundamentally his, and remains composed when cosmic danger points directly at him. His strange wager turns fear into relationship. He ends not triumphant over an enemy, but newly responsible for two unprecedented forms of freedom.

## Cast

- Jet Black / Nicholas Alexander Benson: protagonist, player, and owner of the living golden thread.
- Mara Vey: experienced Threadkeeper, witness, guide, and cautious ally.
- The Unmaker: colossal many-handed custodian at reality's edge, apparent threat, honorable opponent, and possible new friend.
- Jet's golden thread: a living-seeming extension of Jet's causal place in reality that repeatedly ties itself into an impossible knot.

## Art Direction

Full realistic candid photo-comic pages with an intimate phone-camera feeling in Jet's ordinary bedroom, escalating into impossible documentary photography inside the Loom. The supernatural visuals must remain photoreal: millions of luminous gold threads, black endless depth, an ivory segmented body, hundreds of anatomically believable arms, and warm tactile thread-light on faces and fabric. Story pages use large clear panels, realistic handheld angles, and clean rectangular caption boxes. No speech bubbles or thought bubbles.
