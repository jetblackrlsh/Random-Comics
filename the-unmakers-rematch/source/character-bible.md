# Character And Reference Bible

## Jet Black / Nicholas Alexander Benson

- Role: protagonist; owner of the living golden thread; calm nonviolent challenger of the Unmaker.
- Identity reference: `../../series/probably-dreaming/reference-images/jet-black-character.png`.
- Visible identity: African-American man in his late twenties with warm dark-brown skin, brown eyes, very long black dreadlocks extending well past his shoulders, a full dense black beard and mustache, and a friendly expressive face.
- Build and posture: five feet ten inches, slim-to-average athletic build; relaxed posture that becomes steady and deliberate under pressure.
- Outfit: open black zip-up hoodie over a black Dream Anime graphic T-shirt, black athletic pants, white sneakers, and a small black cross-body belt bag. Preserve the look of the reference image. Do not treat clothing text as his name.
- Emotional range: sleepy curiosity, careful skepticism, philosophical resolve, focused playfulness during the wager, relieved wonder afterward.
- Signature objects: glowing golden thread wrapped gently around his left hand; later, a small ivory rematch token held in his right hand.
- Continuity: keep Jet's exact face, complexion, dreadlock length, beard, outfit, body type, and identity consistent on every page. The gold thread remains around his left hand after page 2.

## Mara Vey

- Role: supporting character; Threadkeeper with thirty-seven years of experience; witness and cautious ally.
- Age range and appearance: androgynous adult in their early forties with medium-brown skin, narrow oval face, high cheekbones, dark hazel eyes, and short tightly curled black hair threaded with a few silver strands.
- Outfit: practical charcoal-gray long coat over layered black work clothes, narrow brass clasps, leather forearm wraps, dark boots, and a compact belt holding silver needles, tiny spools, and thread-repair tools. No logos or readable text.
- Color palette: charcoal, black, tarnished silver, tiny warm-gold thread reflections.
- Posture and emotional range: precise careful hands; professional urgency; thoughtful humility when Jet raises the autonomy question; open fear before the Unmaker; stunned disbelief after the game.
- Continuity: keep Mara's face, curls, gray coat, brass clasps, tool belt, and androgynous presentation identical throughout.

## The Unmaker

- Role: obstacle, cosmic custodian, honorable opponent, and possible future friend.
- Scale and anatomy: a colossal ivory-white segmented trunk-like body stretching beyond sight along the outer boundary of the Loom, with hundreds of humanlike arms growing from its segments. Hands vary from ordinary size to building-sized, all anatomically believable, pale, and eerily calm. It has no visible head, mouth, eyes, or face.
- Movement: many hands test, pluck, and occasionally snap threads; when it notices Jet, every hand freezes and points; for the wager, a single huge right hand reaches forward and cleanly forms rock, paper, or scissors.
- Emotional communication: entirely through hand gestures, stillness, posture, and one formal bow made by many arms after losing.
- Continuity: always ivory-white against the black boundary. Never add a face, tentacles, claws, gore, horror wounds, clothing, symbols, or speech.

## Jet's Golden Thread

- Role: key item and living mystery.
- Appearance: impossibly fine luminous gold fiber, warm rather than blinding, with tiny sparks moving inside it. It passes through solid matter without damage until Jet pulls it. Once held, six feet wrap loosely around Jet's left palm and index finger.
- Behavior: twitches toward Jet, curls affectionately, and forms a compact impossible knot shaped like a tiny glowing Möbius tangle.
- Continuity: after page 2, it remains disconnected and physically in Jet's possession. It brightens after the Unmaker honors the wager.

## The Rematch Token

- Role: final artifact and future-story invitation.
- Appearance: palm-sized carved ivory coin or game token attached to a short silver thread. One face bears simple engraved hand symbols for rock, paper, and scissors. The reverse has the clearly readable words `ONE REMATCH`.
- Continuity: first appears on page 7 and remains in Jet's right hand on page 8.

## The Loom

- Role: cosmic setting beneath ordinary reality.
- Appearance: an endless black chamber impossible to fit behind a bedroom wall, filled with millions of taut luminous golden threads crossing in all directions. Threads vibrate like an immense string instrument and disappear into depth. The dark edge contains no stars because it is where reality ends.
- Lighting: golden threads provide warm rim light; Jet's ordinary bedroom retains soft natural morning window light; the Unmaker is lit by cold ivory reflections and gold thread highlights.
- Spatial continuity: the torn bedroom wall is a ragged portal with its construction materials unraveled into thousands of fibers rather than broken debris. Mara begins inside the Loom, then steps into the bedroom beside Jet. The Unmaker remains far beyond the portal until one enormous hand approaches for the game.
