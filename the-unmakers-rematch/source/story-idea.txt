Create a standalone Random Comics one-shot from the complete Fortune's Thread game session “The Unmaker's Rematch.” Jet wakes to knocking inside his bedroom wall, pulls on a living golden thread, and partially succeeds: the wall unravels into the cosmic Loom and his own thread becomes disconnected from reality. Threadkeeper Mara Vey explains that threads reconcile the consequences of choices. Jet's impossible self-knotting thread attracts the Unmaker, a colossal many-handed being at the boundary of reality that removes impossible and disconnected possibilities. Rather than surrender himself, Jet challenges the Unmaker to rock, paper, scissors for the freedom of himself and his thread. The Unmaker accepts, throws scissors, and loses to Jet's rock. It honors the wager, then gives Jet an ivory token marked ONE REMATCH. Mara advises him to define harmless stakes before ever using it. Jet ends the story holding his own living thread and an invitation to play with the end of possibility.
