Create a Random Comics one-shot based on this story, and make sure the comic itself mentions that the story is based on a true story.

Use a different art style from the normal photo art style: colorful, high color saturation, high detail sharpness, bright, dynamic, glowing, and modern anime aesthetic.

Chapter 1: The Descent of the Storm

Driven by a desire to add a sense of wonder and alter how humanity views chaotic weather, a myth was crafted about heroic entities dwelling in the sky. Shortly thereafter, reality bled into legend as a devastating storm swept through the narrator's city, giving rise to a destructive tornado that tore brutally through the neighborhoods below. As devastation loomed and the light of hope began to wane, an extraordinary phenomenon unfolded: two massive, brilliant glowing eyes materialized in the heavens, watching over the unfolding chaos. Concurrently, the horizon transformed into an unprecedented canvas of shifting colors. The narrator went to sleep untroubled and awoke to discover their home completely untouched, their loved ones safe, and the power fully intact, instilling a powerful belief that these cosmic watchers had protected them.

Chapter 2: The Return during the Aurora Borealis

A few days following the initial encounter, the enigmatic phenomenon returned. While watching the vibrant waves of the Aurora Borealis, the narrator and a group of friends witnessed the sky eyes manifesting once more within the northern lights. Their sudden re-emergence leaves behind an aura of mystery, provoking profound questions regarding their true nature, their protective intent, and what these celestial guardians ultimately desire from humanity.
