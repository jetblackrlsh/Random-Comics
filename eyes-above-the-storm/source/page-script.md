# Page Script

Every page is a full modern anime comic page in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. All readable story text must be generated directly inside clean rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Use caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, no thought bubbles, no floating subtitles, no blank text boxes, no later text overlay.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Introduce the true-story basis, the tornado, the cosmic eyes, and the mystery of the sky watchers.
- Layout: Dynamic anime cover with one dominant storm-sky splash image and two small inset panels near the bottom.
- Caption/title text to generate in the page art: Title text: "EYES ABOVE THE STORM". Subtitle text: "Based on a true story." Small caption box: "When the tornado came, the myth looked back."
- Visual beats: A devastated city neighborhood under a roaring tornado; the narrator in a blue hooded jacket stands in the foreground holding a small notebook or phone; above the storm, two enormous brilliant glowing eyes open in the clouds; the horizon burns with saturated cyan, magenta, gold, emerald, and violet. Insets show an intact home glowing warmly and friends under northern lights.

## Page 2: A Myth For Weather

- Story role: Establish that the narrator first created the sky-watcher myth to add wonder to chaotic weather.
- Layout: Four large panels with roomy captions.
- Caption boxes: "This comic is based on a true story." "Before the storm, I wanted weather to feel less empty." "So I imagined heroes living above the clouds." "A myth, I thought, could teach fear to make room for wonder."
- Visual beats: The narrator writes in a notebook or phone beside a rain-streaked window; imagined luminous sky guardians drift above cloud kingdoms; chaotic weather maps and lightning become heroic shapes; the narrator looks up with hopeful curiosity as ordinary storm clouds glow.

## Page 3: The Descent

- Story role: The real storm arrives and turns playful myth into immediate danger.
- Layout: Five-panel page with increasing motion and darker color.
- Caption boxes: "Then the real storm entered my city." "Sirens cut through the rain." "The sky folded into a wall of black wind." "A tornado tore through the neighborhoods below." "Wonder suddenly had to survive fear."
- Visual beats: Storm clouds pile above the city; phones show emergency alerts and radar shapes without relying on legible tiny text; people rush indoors; the tornado funnel drops between streets and rooftops; debris and power flashes streak across saturated rain.

## Page 4: The Eyes Open

- Story role: Reveal the extraordinary glowing eyes and color-shifting horizon at the low point.
- Layout: Three large cinematic panels so the sky event has room to feel monumental.
- Caption boxes: "As hope thinned, the horizon changed colors I had never seen." "Then two brilliant eyes opened in the heavens." "The story I invented was suddenly watching the storm."
- Visual beats: A wide city horizon shifts through impossible bands of cyan, violet, gold, emerald, and rose; the narrator looks up, rain on their hood, while neighborhoods tremble below; the colossal sky watcher eyes form from lightning, cloud breaks, and radiant aurora-like light above the tornado.

## Page 5: Sleeping Under Watch

- Story role: Show the narrator's trust, the night passing, and the impossible relief of waking untouched.
- Layout: Four-panel page moving from night terror to morning calm.
- Caption boxes: "Somehow, I went to sleep untroubled." "The eyes remained with the storm in my mind." "Morning came with an impossible quiet." "My home stood untouched. My loved ones were safe. The power still worked."
- Visual beats: The narrator lies in bed, blue jacket nearby, storm light flickering around the room; a dreamlike panel shows the glowing eyes above clouds like guardians; sunrise reveals an intact home with working lights and devices; loved ones stand safe in warm morning light while distant damage is visible beyond the neighborhood.

## Page 6: After The Passing

- Story role: Process the aftermath and the narrator's growing belief that the watchers protected them.
- Layout: Four panels with contrast between nearby devastation and the spared home.
- Caption boxes: "Outside, the storm had left proof of its teeth." "Around us, whole streets carried the weight of the tornado." "Inside, nothing vital had failed." "I could not prove protection. I could only feel watched over."
- Visual beats: Broken branches, damaged roofs, and emergency lights in nearby blocks; the narrator walks through the neighborhood, humbled and quiet; a clean line of sunlight falls over their intact doorway; the narrator writes the sky eyes again in the notebook, now with reverent seriousness.

## Page 7: The Return

- Story role: A few days later, the Aurora Borealis brings the eyes back in front of friends.
- Layout: Five-panel page with a calm build to wonder.
- Caption boxes: "A few days later, the sky changed again." "Friends gathered to watch the Aurora Borealis ripple over the night." "Green and violet waves crossed the stars." "Then the eyes returned." "This time, I was not alone."
- Visual beats: The narrator and three friends stand in an open yard or field at night; phones are lowered as the aurora intensifies; luminous green, cyan, and violet ribbons fill the sky; the same colossal glowing eyes emerge within the northern lights; friends stare upward in shared silence.

## Page 8: What Do They Want?

- Story role: End on mystery, awe, and the unresolved question of the celestial guardians' intent.
- Layout: Four-panel page ending on a wide final sky image.
- Caption boxes: "Maybe they were only light, weather, and timing." "Maybe they were guardians answering a story." "Maybe the sky wanted us to look up differently." "I still do not know what they want from us. I only know they came back."
- Visual beats: Close-up of the narrator's face lit by aurora and eye glow; the notebook shows a simple sketch of the two eyes without needing extra legible text; friends stand small beneath the immense sky; final wide panel shows the eyes watching from within the Aurora Borealis, bright and unreadable above the quiet city.
