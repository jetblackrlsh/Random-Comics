# Page Script

Every page is a full realistic candid photo-comic page in 4:5 portrait aspect ratio. All readable story text must be generated directly inside roomy rectangular caption boxes in the page art. Use caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel numbers, caption numbers, caption labels, blank text boxes, or later text overlay. Do not repeat any caption wording on the same page.

## Page 1: Cover

- Story role: Promise the train rescue, alien threat, solar stakes, and Jet's cosmic transformation.
- Layout: One dominant cinematic cover image with a narrow inset along the bottom.
- Exact readable text: Title: "LUCID SUN". Caption box: "One city's hero. One stolen star. Eight minutes to save the world."
- Visual beats: The Lucid Vanguard flies above Crownspire City in a sparkling purple aura, reaching toward a train carriage hanging from a shattered elevated track. Below, the black-and-crimson alien stands in a crater with a miniature sun burning in his chest and three drones unfolding behind him. The crimson sky contains a faint colossal image of the radiant Lucid Sun. Bottom inset: the silver-and-purple Crownspire Titan braces itself in the avenue.

## Page 2: Eight Minutes

- Story role: Establish Jet, his powers, the train disaster, the alien, and the extinction-level countdown.
- Layout: Four large rectangular panels in clear reading order.
- Exact caption text:
  - "Jet Black protected Crownspire City as the Lucid Vanguard, shaping sparkling purple Lucid Light with imagination and will."
  - "When a burning object shattered an elevated train line, forty passengers were left hanging over the street."
  - "The armored thief carried a compressed piece of the sun. In eight minutes, Earth would begin to go dark."
- Visual beats: Jet flies between futuristic towers in afternoon light; a burning object smashes through the elevated line; the front carriage hangs over the avenue with terrified passengers visible; the alien rises from the crater with the miniature sun in his chest and three drones beside him while the sky turns crimson.

## Page 3: The Crownspire Titan

- Story role: Show Jet's simultaneous rescue plan, the disruption pulse, and his choice to protect the passengers.
- Layout: Four large action panels, with the final panel taking the lower half of the page.
- Exact caption text:
  - "Jet answered both disasters with one impossible idea: Dreamshift the train into a space-capable rescue robot."
  - "A disruption pulse forced him to preserve either spaceflight or combat stability."
  - "He chose the passengers. The unfinished Crownspire Titan planted its feet in the avenue."
- Visual beats: Jet projects Lucid Light through the endangered train; steel folds into arms and legs while passengers remain secured in glowing seats; the alien fires a red disruption pulse that shreds the forming thrusters; the completed silver-and-purple Titan stands firmly in the avenue with recognizable train-car components and protected passengers.

- Story role: Combine the Titan battle with Jet's cybersecurity analysis and decisive emergency-protocol hack.

- Story role: Explain why Jet must follow and show his consciousness transfer into the alien suit.

  - "Inside the suit, he became its engines, sensors, weapons, and security."

## Page 6: The Dangerous Cargo

- Story role: Neutralize the thief, complete the solar approach, and reveal that returning the core could destroy Earth.

- Visual beats: Blue cryogenic gel floods the alien armor around its trapped wearer; the thief falls still; the armor becomes a small silhouette racing toward the gigantic sun; Jet's violet digital form examines the chest chamber and sees unstable gold magnetic loops aimed toward distant Earth.

- Story role: Resolve both rescue and cosmic transformation, then state Jet's new promise.
