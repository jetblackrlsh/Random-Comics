Create a one-shot based on the following RPG campaign summary:

Jet Black was a dark-skinned Black man with long dreadlocks, a full beard, and the calm, focused demeanor of someone accustomed to solving difficult problems. By day, he worked as a cybersecurity application developer, designing software and studying how complex computer systems could be protected or controlled. As a superhero, he was known as the Lucid Vanguard, and he wielded Lucid Light, a sparkling purple energy generated by his imagination and willpower. Lucid Light allowed him to fly, create force fields, fire energy blasts, and temporarily reshape nearby matter through a power called Dreamshift. One afternoon, while flying above the futuristic towers of Crownspire City, Jet saw the sky suddenly turn crimson as a burning object crashed through an elevated train line.

The collision left the front carriage of the train hanging over the street with forty terrified passengers trapped inside. From the crater rose a towering alien thief dressed in an eight-foot suit of black and crimson armor, with a sealed helmet, glowing red eye-slits, and broad mechanical shoulders. A miniature sun burned inside a transparent chamber in the center of his chest. Three sleek attack drones unfolded from his back and hovered beside him like metal insects. The alien announced that Earth’s sun had been claimed, and Jet immediately understood the true scale of the danger. If the glowing object was part of the sun’s core, then Earth would lose its sunlight after roughly eight minutes, once the last existing rays reached the planet. Saving the falling train mattered, but returning the stolen solar core was necessary to save everyone on Earth.

Jet realized that both emergencies could be addressed at the same time. Using Dreamshift, he attempted to transform the endangered train into a giant, space-capable robot. Purple Lucid Light raced across the carriages as steel folded into armored limbs, wheels became reinforced joints, and the locomotive reshaped itself into a mechanical torso. Inside, the passengers remained secured in glowing protective seats that became command stations. The transformation nearly succeeded, but the alien fired a disruption pulse into the Lucid Light before the process was complete. Jet had enough power to preserve either the robot’s spaceflight system or its combat stability, but not both. After considering the danger to the passengers, he chose combat stability, and the unfinished machine became the Crownspire Titan, a massive silver-and-purple mecha built from the train.

The Crownspire Titan planted its feet firmly in the avenue as the alien’s drones opened fire. Under Jet’s direction, the Titan caught two of the drones in its enormous hands before they could strike the passengers. It seized one by its cannon barrel, trapped the other between its armored fingers, and turned both machines against their owner. The captured drones filled the air with distracting laser fire, forcing the alien thief to raise his arms and defend himself. While the Titan fought, Jet used his professional cybersecurity knowledge to study the wireless signals passing between the drones and the alien armor. Although the technology came from another world, Jet recognized familiar ideas within it: trusted devices, authentication systems, emergency commands, and an autopilot that possessed far more control than it safely should have.

Jet discovered that the drones and the alien suit shared an emergency protocol designed to carry injured soldiers and important cargo back to safety. He hacked that protocol, changed its destination to Earth’s sun, and identified the stolen core as cargo that had to be restored. Purple symbols flashed across the alien’s armor as Jet locked its wearer out of the controls. The furious thief demanded to know what Jet had done, but his suit had already launched into the sky. It carried both him and the solar core toward space while the Crownspire Titan and its passengers remained safely on Earth. The plan had defeated the immediate threat, but Jet knew the alien could still fight the suit’s autopilot or sabotage the core before reaching the sun.

One captured drone remained connected to the fleeing armor, giving Jet a path into its computer systems. He transferred his consciousness through that connection, leaving his body safely seated inside the Crownspire Titan while his mind entered the alien suit. Within its digital architecture, Jet could perceive engines, sensors, weapons, and security processes as if they were parts of a new body. The armored thief felt Jet’s presence and struggled for control, but Jet quickly located the suit’s long-distance stasis system. He classified the alien as a violent and unstable pilot, activated emergency preservation procedures, and filled the inside of the armor with cryogenic gel. The alien’s resistance stopped as he fell into suspended animation, leaving Jet with complete control of the suit during its journey toward the sun.

As the armor approached the star, Jet learned that the glowing core was not a solid object that could simply be put back into place. The thief had compressed part of the sun’s plasma into a containment engine, and releasing it carelessly could create a solar flare powerful enough to burn Earth. Jet chose to surround the core with Lucid Light and stabilize it directly with his consciousness. The risk was enormous: if his control failed, his mind could become trapped inside the sun. Jet accepted that possibility because saving Earth mattered more than returning safely. He entered the core, untangled its unstable magnetic energy, and guided the plasma back toward the layer of the sun from which it had been stolen.

Jet lost control during the final moment of reintegration, but he refused to allow the resulting flare to strike Earth. Instead, he drew the excess solar energy into his own consciousness. The sun returned to full strength, daylight flooded Crownspire City, and the passengers inside the Crownspire Titan celebrated as the alien thief was carried back toward Earth in secure stasis. Jet’s physical body remained alive but dormant within the Titan, while his mind expanded throughout the sun’s plasma and magnetic fields. Reborn as Jet Black, the Lucid Sun, he became a radiant cosmic guardian capable of creating temporary bodies from sunlight, speaking through solar-powered technology, and sensing threats throughout the solar system. His voice reached Earth through radios across the planet as he promised that nothing would threaten the solar system without first passing through him. Jet had begun the day as one city’s superhero, but through courage, intelligence, and sacrifice, he ended it as the Guardian of the Heliosphere.
