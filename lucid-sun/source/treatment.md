# Treatment

## Title

Lucid Sun

## Logline

When an armored alien steals a compressed piece of the sun and leaves forty train passengers hanging above Crownspire City, cybersecurity developer and superhero Jet Black must turn the train into a rescue mecha, hack the thief's armor, and sacrifice his physical future to become the Guardian of the Heliosphere.

## Central Conflict

Jet must save forty people in immediate danger without losing sight of the extinction-level crisis racing eight minutes behind them. Every solution creates a harder choice: protect the Titan's passengers or give it spaceflight, remain safely on Earth or pursue the thief as pure consciousness, and return home or absorb a planet-killing solar flare.

## Theme

Heroism is the disciplined use of imagination, expertise, and sacrifice. Jet does not win through raw force alone; he sees one system connecting every crisis and keeps choosing the lives that depend on him.

## Ending

Jet restores the stolen plasma and prevents the resulting flare from striking Earth by drawing the excess energy into his consciousness. His body survives in the Crownspire Titan, but his mind expands through the sun. Reborn as the Lucid Sun, he becomes the solar system's cosmic guardian.

## Emotional Arc

Jet begins as Crownspire City's calm, capable protector. The train disaster forces him to make a painful limitation explicit, the cyberattack lets his ordinary professional knowledge become heroic power, and the solar crisis asks whether his sense of responsibility extends beyond his own survival. His final sacrifice is not defeat: it transforms one city's hero into a presence vast enough to protect everyone under the sun.

## Cast

- Jet Black / the Lucid Vanguard / the Lucid Sun: a cybersecurity application developer and imaginative superhero who wields sparkling purple Lucid Light.
- The alien solar thief: a towering armored raider who treats Earth's sun as stolen cargo.
- The Crownspire Titan passengers: forty civilians whose endangered train becomes a stable rescue mecha and command sanctuary.
- The Crownspire Titan: a silver-and-purple giant robot Dreamshifted from an elevated train.

## Page Count

Eight pages total: cover plus seven story pages.

## Art Direction

Full realistic candid photo-comic pages with documentary street photography, emergency phone-camera angles, believable futuristic architecture, photoreal Lucid Light and solar effects, and clean rectangular caption boxes. The cover may have polished cinematic energy, but story pages must remain grounded and photographic.
