# Character And Reference Bible

## Jet Black / Lucid Vanguard

- Role: protagonist; cybersecurity application developer; Crownspire City superhero.
- Identity reference: `../../series/probably-dreaming/reference-images/jet-black-character.png`.
- Visible identity: dark-skinned Black man in his 30s with warm dark-brown skin, very long black dreadlocks extending past his shoulders, a full dense black beard and mustache, dark eyes, and a friendly expressive face.
- Build and movement: slim-to-average athletic build; calm, centered posture; moves with deliberate confidence rather than swagger.
- Superhero outfit: fitted matte-black technical suit with a high collar, subtle armored panels, and thin luminous purple seams at the shoulders, forearms, chest, waist, and boots. No cape, helmet, logo, or readable clothing text.
- Signature power: Lucid Light, sparkling translucent purple energy with violet star motes, soft lensing, and geometric dreamlike structures. It creates flight auras, force fields, blasts, Dreamshift transformations, and visible cyber-symbol patterns.
- Emotional continuity: focused during the rescue, analytical during the hack, solemn during the solar approach, and peacefully resolute during his sacrifice.
- Persistence: keep Jet's face, complexion, dreadlock length, beard, body type, and black-purple suit identical on every page until his solar rebirth.

## Jet Black / Lucid Sun

- Role: Jet's final cosmic form, Guardian of the Heliosphere.
- Visible identity: clearly recognizable as the same Jet Black, formed from radiant gold-white sunlight with purple Lucid Light tracing his eyes, dreadlocks, beard, and body outline.
- Scale: may appear as a human-sized temporary body in a ray of sunlight or as a colossal transparent presence within the sun's plasma.
- Continuity: preserve Jet's recognizable face, dreadlocks, and beard. The transformation adds solar radiance; it does not erase his Black identity or redesign his features.

## Alien Solar Thief

- Role: antagonist and thief of compressed solar plasma.
- Visible identifiers: eight-foot black-and-crimson powered armor; broad mechanical shoulders; sealed helmet; narrow glowing red eye-slits; heavy gauntlets; dark scarred metal; no exposed face.
- Solar core: transparent circular chamber in the center of the chest containing a bright miniature sun with roiling gold-white plasma.
- Drones: three sleek black-and-crimson attack drones that unfold from the armor's back and hover like metal insects.
- Persistence: the chest core must remain visible until Jet enters it; cryogenic blue gel fills the sealed armor on the spaceflight page.

## Crownspire Titan

- Role: giant rescue mecha Dreamshifted from the damaged elevated train.
- Visible identifiers: massive silver-and-purple robot assembled from recognizable train carriages; locomotive torso; carriage-panel limbs; wheel hubs at the joints; two enormous hands; incomplete back thrusters that cannot sustain spaceflight.
- Passenger protection: forty civilians remain visible in small groups through glowing protected windows and are secured in purple-lit command seats.
- Persistence: stable feet and heavy legs signal Jet's decision to preserve combat stability; it never flies into space.

## Crownspire City

- Futuristic but believable high-rise city with layered glass-and-steel towers, elevated electric train lines, pedestrian bridges, digital transit displays without readable small text, and a wide central avenue.
- Afternoon daylight turns crimson during the theft, dims during the solar crisis, and returns as brilliant gold on the final page.

## Visual Rules

- Full realistic candid photo-comic pages, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio.
- Use the Jet identity reference for every page where his face or body appears.
- Caption boxes only; no speech bubbles, thought bubbles, floating dialogue, subtitles, page numbers, panel labels, caption labels, logos, or watermarks.
- All readable story text must be generated directly inside the page art.
- Keep all powers, armor, mecha, city, and space effects photoreal rather than illustrated.
