# Rox Rolard and the Animator’s Workshop

## Title

Rox Rolard and the Animator’s Workshop

## Publication Date

2026-08-16

## Logline

Badly wounded by an unstoppable Clay Golem inside a rearranging construct workshop, Dragonborn Warlock Rox Rolard abandons brute force, turns the dungeon’s own machinery against his pursuer, and escapes through a star gate.

## Central Conflict

Rox can destroy the workshop’s Flying Sword, but the Clay Golem outlasts every spell and summoned beast he throws at it. With one clean hit separating him from defeat, he must recognize that surviving the dungeon does not require killing everything inside it.

## Theme

Cleverness is courage expressed through adaptation. Victory belongs not only to the adventurer who defeats a monster, but also to the one who understands the battlefield well enough to move the monster somewhere else.

## Ending

Rox feeds a tracking spell into the workshop, reveals the moving platforms beneath the Clay Golem, and pulls the correct lever. The dungeon carries the undefeated monster away and replaces it with a completed Open Star Gate. Rox escapes through the stars while the golem roars somewhere behind the walls.

## Emotional Arc

Rox begins wary but fascinated, becomes confident after dismantling the Flying Sword, is ground down by the Clay Golem, reaches the edge of defeat, then regains agency by changing the problem instead of escalating the fight. He ends exhausted, injured, and triumphant because he understood the dungeon.
