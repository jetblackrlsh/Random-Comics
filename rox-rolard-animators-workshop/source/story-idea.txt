Rox Rolard, a Dragonborn Warlock, enters the rearranging Animator’s Workshop. He manipulates three enchanted stations, trading lesser animation and animal-speaking magic for Animal Friendship and Conjure Animals while reordering the dangers ahead. In the second room, his Ring of Djinni Summoning destroys an Animated Flying Sword, but a Clay Golem withstands everything Rox throws at it. Acid ruins Rox’s armor; Instant Summons fails; summoned spectral beasts gouge the golem without stopping it. With one clean hit left between him and defeat, Rox feeds Locate Animals or Plants into the workshop’s tracking machinery, exposes the moving platforms, and pulls a lever that carries the undefeated golem away. An Open Star Gate appears because Rox has already completed its hidden trial. He escapes through the stars, proving that the cleverest adventurer may be the one who knows when to move the monster somewhere else.
