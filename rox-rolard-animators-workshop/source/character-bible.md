# Character Bible

## Rox Rolard

- Adult male Dragonborn Warlock with a tall athletic build.
- Deep obsidian-black scales with subtle indigo highlights; angular reptilian face; glowing ember-orange eyes.
- Two thick swept-back onyx horns and small cheek spines; no hair.
- Dark midnight-blue armored warlock coat over black segmented plate, antique-gold trim, violet waist sash, heavy black boots.
- Right hand bears the Ring of Djinni Summoning: a broad antique-gold ring with a large luminous cyan gemstone.
- Leather rune satchel at his left hip; composed, analytical posture even under pressure.
- Pages 5-8 continuity: armor and coat become progressively acid-scorched, smoking, cracked, and ragged; Rox becomes visibly exhausted but remains recognizable.

## Clay Golem

- Twelve-foot humanoid construct of ochre sculptor’s clay.
- Massive torso, blocky limbs, unfinished eyeless face split by a jagged mouth.
- Amber furnace light glows from cracks; corrosive neon-green acid gathers in its mouth and splashes from its fists.
- Missing chunks and deep spectral claw gouges persist after the summoned-animal attack, but it never collapses.

## Animated Flying Sword

- Long polished silver blade with a gold crossguard and a small violet gem in the pommel.
- No wielder; moves like a predatory bird and leaves sharp silver motion trails.
- Shattered pieces remain on the workshop floor after its defeat.

## Workshop

- Vast clockwork animation studio combining ancient machinery and arcane fabrication.
- Bronze mechanical arms hang like sleeping spiders; half-built wooden and metal constructs stand at benches.
- Interlocking circular floor platforms rotate and slide; walls move on giant gears.
- Palette: warm brass and copper machinery, cyan arcane mechanisms, violet warlock magic, neon-green acid, gold-white star gate.
