# Page Script

## Page 1 — Cover

- Full-page heroic composition.
- Rox stands on a rotating bronze platform, Ring of Djinni Summoning glowing cyan, with the Clay Golem looming behind him and the Open Star Gate burning in the distance.
- Visible title: “ROX ROLARD AND THE ANIMATOR’S WORKSHOP”.
- No other readable text.

## Page 2 — The Living Workshop

- Four panels: doors open; Rox enters alone; spider-like machinery and unfinished constructs awaken; rotating floor reveals four stations.
- Captions:
  1. “The doors of the Animator’s Workshop groaned open.”
  2. “Rox Rolard entered alone.”
  3. “Inside, the dungeon rearranged itself.”

## Page 3 — Three Choices

- Five panels: animation sigils exchange; Animal Friendship charm claimed; spectral beasts reveal three coming rooms in a mirror; Rox reorders them; Conjure Animals rune flares in his hand while one untouched station sinks away.
- Captions:
  1. “Three stations. Three choices.”
  2. “Animation yielded to friendship.”
  3. “Beast-speech revealed the halls ahead.”
  4. “A stronger rune answered: Conjure Animals.”

## Page 4 — The Flying Sword

- Five panels: sword launches from rack; Rox dodges; cyan force blast hits the flat; sword misses; second blast shatters it against the wall.
- Captions:
  1. “Room Two opened with a metallic shriek.”
  2. “The Flying Sword struck first.”
  3. “The Ring of Djinni Summoning struck harder.”
  4. “One monster fell.”

## Page 5 — The Clay Golem

- Five panels: golem rises from clay; Rox’s blast misses; acid washes over him; a pale summoning glyph sputters out; golem advances through smoke.
- Captions:
  1. “Then the Clay Golem awakened.”
  2. “Acid ate through armor and scale.”
  3. “Instant Summons failed.”
  4. “The golem did not.”

## Page 6 — Nothing Left

- Five panels: Rox crushes Conjure Animals rune; spectral wolves, panther, and hawk tear chunks from golem; golem swings through fading beasts; final beast misses; Rox kneels in smoking armor as golem closes in.
- Captions:
  1. “Rox shattered the Conjure Animals rune.”
  2. “Spectral claws tore deep.”
  3. “Still, the golem advanced.”
  4. “One more clean hit would end him.”

## Page 7 — Move the Monster

- Five panels: Rox lowers his ring and studies the floor; Locate Animals or Plants enters a dormant tracking device; transparent map reveals moving platforms; Rox pulls a brass lever; floor rotates and walls slide the golem away.
- Captions:
  1. “Rox stopped trying to win the golem’s way.”
  2. “A tracking spell revealed the workshop’s moving heart.”
  3. “He pulled the lever.”
  4. “The dungeon moved the monster.”

## Page 8 — Victory Through the Stars

- Four panels: star gate replaces golem; runes examine Rox and turn gold; wounded Rox dives through as distant golem roars behind moving walls; final wide panel shows Rox collapsed safely in a cosmic field while the portal closes.
- Captions:
  1. “A gate of burning starlight awakened.”
  2. “The trial was already complete.”
  3. “Rox escaped with almost nothing left.”
  4. “Sometimes victory means knowing when to move the monster somewhere else.”
