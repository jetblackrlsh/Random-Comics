# Hero's Journey 17-Stage Page Map

Use this Campbell monomyth order for Monomyth Comics unless the user explicitly provides a different 17-stage list.

## Page Mapping

| Story page | File | Stage | Function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `page-02.png` | Call to Adventure | The protagonist is confronted with a destabilizing summons, threat, need, or impossible opportunity. |
| 2 | `page-03.png` | Refusal of the Call | The protagonist resists, doubts, fears the cost, or sees why accepting the call would destroy their existing life. |
| 3 | `page-04.png` | Supernatural Aid | A mentor, tool, blessing, power, clue, or strange ally gives the protagonist a way to move forward. |
| 4 | `page-05.png` | Crossing the First Threshold | The protagonist commits and enters the dangerous world where old rules no longer protect them. |
| 5 | `page-06.png` | Belly of the Whale | The protagonist is swallowed by the new world, cut off from retreat, and symbolically remade. |
| 6 | `page-07.png` | Road of Trials | The protagonist faces tests that reveal the journey's rules and expose their weakness. |
| 7 | `page-08.png` | Meeting with the Goddess | The protagonist encounters a vision, person, place, or truth that embodies what they most deeply seek. |
| 8 | `page-09.png` | Woman as Temptress | The protagonist is tempted away from the quest by comfort, fear, ego, obedience, despair, or a false purpose. |
| 9 | `page-10.png` | Atonement with the Father | The protagonist confronts the controlling power, creator, authority, origin wound, or law that shaped them. |
| 10 | `page-11.png` | Apotheosis | The protagonist gains a transformed perspective and understands the quest in a new way. |
| 11 | `page-12.png` | The Ultimate Boon | The protagonist obtains the needed cure, weapon, truth, rescue, key, or power to resolve the crisis. |
| 12 | `page-13.png` | Refusal of the Return | The protagonist resists returning to the old world or old duty because the journey has changed them. |
| 13 | `page-14.png` | The Magic Flight | The protagonist escapes with the boon while pursued by consequences, enemies, collapse, or time pressure. |
| 14 | `page-15.png` | Rescue from Without | Outside help, a remembered bond, an unexpected mercy, or a lingering consequence prevents total failure. |
| 15 | `page-16.png` | Crossing the Return Threshold | The protagonist brings the boon back to the boundary between worlds and must translate transformation into action. |
| 16 | `page-17.png` | Master of Two Worlds | The protagonist proves they can hold both the old world and the transformed self at once, or deliberately chooses between them. |
| 17 | `page-18.png` | Freedom to Live | The protagonist claims a future beyond fear, compulsion, or assigned purpose; the final image shows the emotional consequence. |

## Adaptation Notes

- The ordinary world belongs in the opening setup, cover, treatment, and the Call to Adventure page. Do not add an eighteenth story stage for it.
- A stage can be external, internal, or symbolic, but every page must visibly dramatize that stage's function.
- The issue should not read like a worksheet. Stage names guide the script and should not appear as visible page text unless the user asks for labeled stages.
- For morally complicated endings, make the protagonist's final freedom emotionally earned and show the cost clearly.
